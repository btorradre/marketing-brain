## Acceptance

Compare the source and output at the same angle/state, aligned with a uniform transform. Check body width/height, gusset projection, rim-to-handle-apex ratio, four handle roots, felt strip alignment, separate belt ends, cap count and color. Inspect full-size felt/leather boundaries and side detail. Reject a V connection, new zipper/lining/feet, long shoulder strap, glossy knit texture or leather-only body.
