## Construction and angle constraints

The corrected fronts show an arched leather flap, two rolled top handles through shaped cutouts, leather top/band/corner reinforcements, a shallow concave base between the two front corner caps, small gold feet, leather side gussets flaring at upper corners, and a roller buckle/fitting on each side. The matching long thin leather strap clips with gold snap hooks to the top side fittings. Both handles remain present when carried by the long strap.

Keep one upright oval center plate and a short brass turning bar; exact small hardware differs between source/approved color images, so inspect the selected master rather than borrowing a generic Weekender oval. Two large slotted belt-end plates hang low over the canvas in the unfastened state; do not miniaturize or tuck them wholly into the band. Key bell hangs from the handle base. Count only fittings visible at the selected angle; do not invent hidden duplicate hardware.

Open views require actual mechanism evidence: the attached flap folds backward, front leather band remains, and the closure post must not become a second complete closed flap. Earlier supplier-processed open images such as [LC open-angle source](</Users/brooksorradre2/Documents/marketing brain/brands/velantra/products/delphine/real-product/LC-03.png>) do not consistently show the final master hardware. Prefer the confirmed lining macro when no matching open geometry is available. The final August 30 interior shots intentionally stay tight to fabric/zip; no Army Green interior was generated for that set.

## Acceptance and gaps

Reject flat-base substitution, fine felt-like weave, canvas side gussets, erased long-strap fittings, stubby handles, too-small belt plates, wrong center orientation, silver hardware, changed lining or color drift. Compare each output to physical reference and approved target separately. A same-view silhouette match can pass while metric scale stays not verifiable. Obtain a dated ruler/side photo, handle/strap endpoints, material specification, actual capacity test and Army Green interior before making claims that depend on them. Do not reuse stale availability, price, or “phone/sunglasses fit” claims from historic prose.
