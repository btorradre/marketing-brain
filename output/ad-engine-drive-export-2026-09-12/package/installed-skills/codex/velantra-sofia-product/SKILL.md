## Observable identity

- Natural tan woven body with dense repeating rows, braided/laced side edges and slight organic irregularity. Preserve the reference's stiffness, weave pitch and wing shape; neither rigid molded panels nor exaggerated rope slouch are interchangeable.
- Two smooth leather-like handles and one connected fold-over sheet with a broad center lobe, outer tabs and two handle slots. Copy the actual cut and subtly rounded edges rather than inventing floating patches from a textual “three tabs” instruction.
- Two narrow front straps with overlapping free tips. Their exact crossover is variant-dependent in the current references. No added metal buckles, lock, chain or rings; a Kanary request for upgraded metal buckles conflicts with the no-metal photographed product and is not existing hardware evidence.
- Only trim changes color in the reviewed heroes; the woven body remains tan. Current Lady Pink is a softer dusty pink than the rejected magenta v4 render. Caramel is warm tan rather than v4 greige. Caban Black has black leather-like elements, not a black woven body.

The photographed overheads show a fold-back opening and props, but do not identify the device's dimensions or certify a lining, pocket system or laptop capacity. No verified ruler-backed back/base/side series was recovered. Do not generate hidden construction by mirroring the front.
