## Identity and construction

The represented Portico is a compact, softly structured **bucket-shaped top-handle bag** with a narrower rounded rectangular base and flared, slightly undulating open rim. Two broad flat loop handles have rounded stitched roots continuous with the body silhouette. The visible surface has small irregular pebble grain, flexible folds and darker edge finishing. A narrow central vertical strap runs down the front, passes through a small rectangular gold-tone guide/buckle near the rim, and carries a hanging gold-tone padlock lower down. A separate thin leather-like cord from a handle root ends in a matching small bell-shaped key cover. This is not a drawstring, circular-bottom sack or shoulder tote.
