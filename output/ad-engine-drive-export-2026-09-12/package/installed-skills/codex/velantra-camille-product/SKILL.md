## Color and construction routing

Current Camille two-tone colors: **Navy, Emerald Green, Lineman, Lady Pink, Light Grey, Red, Sunny Yellow**. Current fully colored variants: **Solid Navy, Solid Olive Green, Solid Red, Solid Green, Solid Pink, Solid Orange**, each carrying the catalog suffix `(Limited Edition)`.

Draft `boat-tote` and `velantra-boat-tote-1` also list **Solid Yellow (Limited Edition)**. The oldest draft instead lists **Navy, Red, Emerald Green, Lady Pink, Lineman, Faded Blue**. Do not merge Faded Blue with Light Grey, or Sunny Yellow with Solid Yellow.

View [current colors](references/current-color-evidence.jpg) and [alias differences](references/catalog-alias-evidence.jpg). Current two-tone references show cream canvas including the folded top edge, colored flat handles, a narrow front belt with a small gold-tone closure and two keepers, two long front strips, soft lower-corner pleats and a colored lower border. Lineman has its own pale front panel/detail; preserve it instead of recoloring a Navy master.

**The current solid variants are not a simple recolor of that two-tone master.** Their source heroes have fuller colored upper panels and rounder handles. Use each selected solid variant's exact hero. Do not flatten those differences in pursuit of congruent backgrounds.

The older local `colors/<name>/07–13` sets require inspection, not automatic trust:

- `07` is a group composition, not a single-bag hero or a calibrated size comparison.
- Navy `08` shows an open interior with raw fabric edges and seams; `10–13` show closure states/angles. These help only when they match the selected listing's construction.
- Solid Navy `10–13` show a hanging padlock and other details absent from the current solid hero. Do not import those parts into the current product.
- Draft aliases include an embroidered “Sample” image. Never use it to add lettering to the unbranded active Camille.

## Size and materials

Active Camille's **20 W × 14 H × 8 D in**, rounded in catalog to **51 × 36 × 20 cm**, and **10-inch strap/handle drop** are **catalog_only**. The fulfillment sheet copied PDP dimensions; it is not an independent measurement. The exact top/base width endpoints and loading state are unspecified.

The earlier `boat-tote` template lineage has **40 × 30 × 20 cm** with inconsistent W/H inch labels; another older record says **14 × 13 × 8 in**. Those are unresolved historical candidates, not alternate Camille sizes. The exact dimensions of each draft listing remain unknown until tied to a model-specific drawing/sample.

Texture can establish canvas-like body and leather-like trim, not cotton percentage, ounces, cowhide grade, waterproof finish or load rating. Kanary's 16–18 oz canvas, top-grain trim, added feet/base board and new pockets are requested upgrades, not proof of current units. Do not replace a source interior with a fabricated premium lining.

## Acceptance

Check the exact SKU before comparing W/H, gusset projection, handle/body ratio, top band, trim width, closure plate and keepers, seams, color placement and lower pleats. Reject cross-lineage substitutions even when they look more luxurious. Same grid occupancy does not mean equal physical size.

Four representative [R40 comparisons](references/r40-source-comparison.jpg) preserve their supplied current heroes. They cannot prove the 20-inch width or a 10-inch handle drop, and a correct Navy studio image does not validate a Solid Navy interior assembled from a conflicting local set.
