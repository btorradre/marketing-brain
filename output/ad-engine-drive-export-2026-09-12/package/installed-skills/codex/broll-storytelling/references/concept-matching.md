# Match the concept, then source or generate

The workflow is reference inspection → target concept interpretation → treatment → scene choice with an explicit why → editing plan → actual assets → inspection. The user-facing purpose is to explain why these scenes tell this concept's story. Source/generate from that explanation, then verify that the actual asset delivers it. Scale it to the request. A narrow revision updates the relevant existing treatment/rows; it does not need a new full concept exercise.

## 1. Derive the target treatment

Read the brief, reference frame audit, approved script, existing board and selected assets. Describe the target in concrete terms:

- Format and story structure: animated narrative, HeyGen VSL, conversational podcast, documentary, product demo, visual metaphor, photo-led montage or another approach.
- Dominant medium and specific style: flat 2D illustration, textured drawn animation, stylized 3D, realistic 3D, clay-like characters, candid live action, polished photography, editorial collage, or an intentional mix. “Animated” is not specific enough to produce matching scenes.
- Character/product consistency: approved identities, proportions, wardrobe where required, product details and reference assets.
- World and finish: environments, palette, materials, texture, light, depth, lens/composition and motion character.
- Medium-switch rules: where real footage, stills, diagrams and animation may enter and why; distinguish an intentional style change from an accidental mismatch.
- Source transfer: what comes from the reference's visual reasoning and what changes for the target brand, concept and script.

The current target instruction overrides the reference's medium. If adapting a live-action reference into animation, retain useful actions, pacing and story relationships but translate them into the approved animation style. If a presenter VSL uses several media, preserve that deliberate mix instead of forcing uniformity.

Do not label an ad HeyGen-generated, TikTok-sourced or iPhone-shot from appearance alone. “HeyGen-style presenter VSL” can describe the requested format without authenticating a reference's production tool.

## 2. Represent the same beat differently by concept

| Beat / communication goal | Fully animated concept | HeyGen-style VSL with mixed B-roll | Photo-led/editorial concept |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nightmare state: a task is uncomfortable or impossible | Consistent animated character visibly struggles with that task in the established world | Real footage or an appropriate real photo of the relevant human situation; presenter can carry interpretation | Relevant human/context photo with composition and juxtaposition making the limitation clear |
| Discovery: the person finds an explanation | Character explores or connects a legible visual idea in the same style | Real research/task footage, a relevant source image, or presenter reaction | Actual relevant document/photo sequence; avoid fabricated evidence |
| Mechanism education | Matching animated process, diagram or analogy, with clear state/action/change | Scientific animation when explaining an invisible biological process; real demonstration when the process is observable | A legible diagram or a short deliberate animation if the treatment permits it |
| Product/use | Accurate stylized product with matching character/hands and visible use | Real product/hand footage or accurate selected imagery showing the routine | Clear accurate product and use photographs; sequence only what the action needs |
| Desired outcome | Animated character regains the relevant activity in a distinct scene | Real people/photos/videos showing the desired lived state; generated reenactment only when appropriate to the assignment | Specific outcome/activity photo rather than generic happiness |
| CTA | Product/action in the same animated visual language | Presenter invitation, item in hand or a fitting product/gesture insert | Clear product and destination/action composition |

These are illustrative choices, not a fixed template. A scientific diagram might be an inset in one concept and the main scene in another. Actual human imagery can appear throughout a mixed VSL. An animated person's problem or joy is still a human story function without being live-action footage.

## 3. Choose the asset route

| Route | Choose when | Required result |
|---|---|---|
| Existing approved asset | It fits this beat and treatment, preserves identity and does not violate the variety requirement | Inspected file with usable range and beat mapping |
| Source real photo | A single observable state, person, object or evidence image communicates the idea and suits the pace | Saved selected image with source/provenance and fit review |
| Source real video | An authentic behavior, environment or actual demonstration needs motion | Saved selected video with inspected in/out and action; origin and usage status recorded |
| Generate still | The concept requires an original illustration, accurate scene reference, overlay, stylized image or approved photoreal reenactment | GPT Image 2 output inspected against references and treatment |
| Generate video | The concept needs matching animation or a specific motion/scene not available as an approved source | Google Omni output with required style/action/duration, inspected through the motion |

“Source/generate B-roll” authorizes the appropriate asset work after the editing plan; do not return only a proposed shot list when selected assets are expected. A planning-only or skill-building request stops at that phase. Do not create ad assets merely to test this skill.

For sourcing, search the specific observable action, subject/context and style. Inspect candidates; do not select on titles or thumbnails. A still and a video are separate options, not a quality ranking. A static expression, document or product detail may be best as a photo; a change of state generally needs motion or a clear sequence.

For generation, carry the treatment and identity references into every brief. An all-animated project should not quietly receive photoreal stock people. A request for real photographs or authentic footage should not quietly receive generated substitutes. Report the specific sourcing gap, use other matching authorized options where possible, and ask only if changing the required medium/origin is necessary.

## 4. Brief matching animation

Each scene brief includes:

`target style/reference IDs → scene's story job and narration cue → character/product/world constraints → composition → initial state → one dominant action → readable resulting state → duration/aspect → motion character → entry/exit relationship`

Match 2D line/shape/shading or 3D geometry/material/render characteristics, not just colors. Keep the same character and world recognizable while varying scene, angle, action and explanatory scale. A clay-like character scene followed by glossy realistic anatomy is a style change requiring an intentional reason.

Generate/inspect GPT Image 2 scene images in the chosen style before animating them with Google Omni. Where several shots share a character or product, use approved references to preserve identity. Treat a reference screenshot as visual guidance, not automatically as the new production asset. For video prompts specify how that image moves; don't rely on “animate this” to communicate the intended beat.

Scientific animation also needs a correct location/scale and an intelligible process. State what moves, interacts or changes. Visual style does not authorize unsupported biology. Realistic rendering is still illustration, not clinical footage.

Use currently available approved provider tools/workflows. Inspect the supported duration/aspect/model options before making a request that depends on them. If the provider cannot deliver the specified animation style, duration or format, disclose the limitation and do not substitute HyperFrames, Remotion or an unapproved model.

## 5. Select, save and inspect

Maintain an asset manifest with:

`beat ID | exact script cue | why this scene, here | concept treatment | medium/style | route | selected file | source URL/timecodes or generation provenance | identity references | intended action/duration | inspection status | remaining gap`

Verify three fits separately:

1. **Meaning:** the selected image/action communicates the line's job and the correct nightmare/desired state, mechanism, object or evidence.
2. **Concept:** style, identity, materials/light and motion belong in this ad; any change of medium is deliberate.
3. **Sequence:** entry/exit aligns with the narration; action resolves; neighboring shots connect; compositions and actions are varied.

Inspect actual source/generated images and videos, not just job status. Compare the selected set together. Update plan rows and attach actual selected first assets to corresponding Cut Room storyboard cards whenever delivering a storyboard. DaVinci Resolve remains the editor when production editing is authorized.
