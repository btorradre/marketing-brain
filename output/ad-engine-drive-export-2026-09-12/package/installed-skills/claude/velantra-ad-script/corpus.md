## 1. VEL-WEEKENDER greenscreen v3 (THE EXEMPLAR, top performer)

`brands/velantra/products/weekender/concepts/greescreen - 7:10:26/greenscreen v3.mp4`
88 words · 27.9s · 189 wpm · greenscreen VO, word-synced captions, PDP screen rec close

> **[HOOK]** If you're looking for the perfect summer travel bag, I have the one for you.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO]** This is the Velantra Weekender.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's Birkin-inspired, structured, real brass hardware, but actually built for a weekend trip.
> **[PROOF]** This one holds its shape even packed full. I've thrown it in the trunk every weekend this summer, and it still looks brand new.
> **[CAPACITY]** It actually fits everything: two outfits, shoes, your toiletries.
> **[VARIANTS]** It comes in two different colors.
> **[OFFER]** They're having a summer sale right now, and colorways go fast.
> **[CTA]** I left the link below.

**Why it works.** The hook is a qualifying question that hands the viewer the verdict in the same breath. The brand lands at 3.8 seconds. Every attribute is a physical fact of the Weekender and none of them port to another bag. The proof beat is a lived, time-stamped survival claim rather than an adjective. Capacity is three concrete nouns. The offer is a real sale on the real page, shown in a screen recording.

**B-roll sequence:** hero on a bench, hand lifting it by the handles, brass hardware macro on a bed, hands working the belt on a packed bag, sitting in an open car trunk, packed interior open with a sweater and jeans and a pouch, two colorways side by side on a counter, PDP screen rec with price and Add to Cart.

## 2. VEL-SOFIA fall straw

`brands/velantra/products/sofia-woven/concepts/8:2:26 - fall straw/`
~105 words · ~33s · greenscreen VO

> **[HOOK]** Fall is almost here, and everyone I know swaps out their straw bags for suede and leather. I think I found the one that stays out all year.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO]** This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's Birkin-inspired, so it's fully structured, it holds its shape, and the flap folds all the way over the top, so nothing falls out of it.
> **[CAPACITY]** It fits everything: your wallet, a water bottle, a sweater for when it gets cold.
> **[PROOF, styling variant]** And the caramel with boots and a chunky knit, it just works.
> **[OFFER]** They're having a fall sale right now, and the colorways go fast.
> **[CTA]** I left the link below.

**Note the "so" chain in the stack**: each attribute carries its consequence. Structured *so* it holds its shape. Flap folds over *so* nothing falls out. That is the pattern to copy.

**Watch:** this one has no lived proof beat, it substitutes a styling beat. Weaker. Also the read said "Volantra."

## 3. VEL-SOFIA BOF summer winding down

`brands/velantra/products/sofia-woven/concepts/7:27:26 - bof summer winding down (greenscreen)/`
126 words · 38-42s · greenscreen VO, 14 visual slots

> **[HOOK]** Okay, if you've been thinking about the boatkin of summer, this is your sign.
> **[SCARCITY, early]** Summer is winding down and the colors are starting to sell out.
> **[PROOF]** So I've been carrying mine all season and I'm still obsessed.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's hand woven, it's structured so it holds its shape, and the leather is all hand set.
> **[CAPACITY / USE]** You just throw everything in and go. Beach, errands, dinner, whatever.
> **[EXTENSION]** And honestly, it carries straight into fall.
> **[SCARCITY]** Two of the colors are already gone, and I wouldn't wait on the rest.
> **[VARIANTS]** This is the caramel, the one everyone asks me about.
> **[OFFER + CTA]** There's still so much summer left to carry this bag. I've left the link below. The summer sale is still on right now, so go get yours before it's gone.

**This one violates the product intro rule** and is kept as the counter-example. It never says "This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra," it says "the boatkin," so the entire benefit stack accrues to a category word instead of to us. Do not repeat this. Also its stock claim ("two of the colors are already gone") went false: as of 2026-08-21 only Sunny Yellow is sold out.

## 4. VEL-SOFIA-STRUCTURED-01, 30s organic review

`brands/velantra/products/sofia-woven/concepts/8:21:26 - fall all-around (laura_jo review replication)/`
77 words · 29.8s · 163 wpm · one continuous handheld take, captions on thesis beats only

> **[HOOK, doubt + reversal]** I thought this woven tote was gonna fall apart. I was so wrong.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO + ATTRIBUTE STACK]** This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra and I'm obsessed. It's hand woven, no logo on it anywhere, and it fits my sweater, a bottle, my wallet and a book.
> **[PROOF, demonstrated]** And then watch, I set it down and take my hands right off.
> *[hold, 1.5 seconds, nothing said]*
> **[PAYOFF]** Look at that. It's holding all that on its own, not leaning on anything.
> **[VERDICT]** I haven't carried anything else since.

**The product-aware variant.** Doubt and reversal both land in the first five seconds, which opens the loop, and everything after is the answer to "why were you wrong." Capacity folds into the attribute stack to buy runtime. Offer and variants are dropped entirely because the runtime cannot hold them, which is the correct order of sacrifice.

**Demo formats run slower.** 163 wpm here versus 189 on the greenscreen, because hands are doing work and the payoff needs silence over it.
