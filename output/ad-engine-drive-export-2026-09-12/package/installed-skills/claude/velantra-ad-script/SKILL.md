# Velantra Ad Script Formula

Locked by Brooks 2026-08-21 off `greenscreen v3.mp4`, the top performing Weekender ad. Confirmed against three other shipped scripts. **This is how every Velantra ad script gets written.** Deviate only when Brooks names the deviation.

Pair with the product-scale skill (`velantra-weekender`, `velantra-straw-tote`, `velantra-meridian`) for product truth, and load `brands/velantra/` before writing a word.

---

## The formula

| # | Beat | Job | Budget |
|---|------|-----|--------|
| 1 | **HOOK** | Self-ID or doubt, resolved into a verdict. Opens the loop. | 1-2 sentences, 12-20 words, 3-4s |
| 2 | **PRODUCT INTRO** | `This is the [Name] from Velantra.` Say it early, say it plainly. | 5-8 words, 2s |
| 3 | **ATTRIBUTE STACK** | 3-5 specific physical facts of OUR bag, run together in one breath | 13-25 words, 5-7s |
| 4 | **PROOF** | The lived beat. First person, time-stamped, something she watched it survive. | 15-25 words, 5-7s |
| 5 | **CAPACITY** | What actually goes in it, as concrete nouns | 8-14 words, 4s |
| 6 | **VARIANTS** | How many colorways | 5-7 words, 1.5s |
| 7 | **OFFER + SCARCITY** | The sale that is live today, and that colors go | 10-14 words, 3s |
| 8 | **CTA** | `I left the link below.` | 5 words, 1s |

**Total: 85-95 words, 28-30 seconds, ~185 wpm.** That pace is a VO ad. A real-time demo where hands are doing work runs slower, ~160 wpm, so budget ~78 words for 30s there.

Beats 5 and 6 are the first to go when the runtime is tight. Beats 1, 2, 3 and 8 never go.

## Beat rules

### 1. HOOK

Four proven openings. Pick by awareness.

- **Self-ID + verdict** (unaware / problem aware): *"If you're looking for the perfect summer travel bag, I have the one for you."*
- **Observation + verdict** (solution aware): *"Fall is almost here and everyone I know swaps out their straw bags for suede and leather. I think I found the one that stays out all year."*
- **Permission** (solution aware, warm): *"If you've been thinking about the boatkin of summer, this is your sign."*
- **Doubt + reversal** (product aware): *"I thought this woven tote was gonna fall apart. I was so wrong."*

Rules:
- **The reversal or verdict lands inside the hook, never at the end of the ad.** Resolving it immediately is what opens the curiosity loop. Holding it for the ending is a narrative arc and it does not sell.
- **Doubt goes on OUR bag only.** "I thought THIS was gonna fall apart" is her expectation of the thing she bought. "Another floppy straw bag" is a claim about products we do not make and it is banned. Killed on VEL-SOFIA-ONEBAG-01.
- No villain, no problem agitation, no attacking a category. The avatar is already searching, so close her open loop rather than opening a wound.
- She is already mid-thought on the first word. No throat clearing.

### 2. PRODUCT INTRO

**Mandatory, verbatim shape: `This is the [Name] from Velantra.`**

- Always lands by 0:06. Never buried inside a run-on clause, never delayed to build a category case first. An affiliate delays the brand because she is selling the category. We are the brand, so the whole benefit stack has to accrue to us.
- Full product name. "The Sofia Woven Tote," not "this bag."
- Never an epithet. No "the summer one," "the everyday one," "the polished one." Name only.
- One product per ad.

### 3. ATTRIBUTE STACK

Three to five **specific physical facts about our bag**, run together in one breath with commas.

- Facts only, stated flat. Weekender: Birkin-inspired, structured, real brass hardware, full-grain leather over woven canvas. Sofia: hand woven, structured, one-piece leather flap folded over the top, crossed front straps, cream contrast stitching, no logo anywhere.
- **The swap test governs this beat.** Drop a competitor's bag into the script. If it still reads, the stack was generic and you are paying to advertise the category. Rewrite.
- Attach the consequence with "so": *"it's fully structured, it holds its shape, and the flap folds all the way over the top, so nothing falls out of it."*
- **Always include the no-logo fact.** Zero logo on the exterior is a top driver for this avatar and it is a hard verifiable fact about every bag we make.
- These double as the criteria a searching buyer shops by, which is how we become "the best one" without comparing to anything.

### 4. PROOF

One lived beat. First person, past or present perfect, with a time stamp on it.

- *"I've thrown it in the trunk every weekend this summer, and it still looks brand new."*
- *"I've been carrying mine all season and I'm still obsessed."*
- In a demo format this beat becomes the demo itself: she does the thing on camera and says almost nothing over it. **Demonstrate, never describe.** Hold silence on the payoff frame.

### 5. CAPACITY

Concrete nouns, three or four, listed. Never "roomy," never "fits everything" on its own.

- *"two outfits, shoes, your toiletries"* · *"your wallet, a water bottle, a sweater for when it gets cold"*
- "It actually fits everything" is fine only as the lead-in to the list.

### 6. VARIANTS

*"It comes in two different colors."* / *"the caramel, the one everyone asks me about."*
Count must be true on the day. Verify against the live PDP.

### 7. OFFER + SCARCITY

*"They're having a fall sale right now, and the colorways go fast."*

- Must be visibly true on the live page the day it ships. Never invent a countdown, a stock number, or a deadline.
- Scarcity is soft and factual. Colors go fast, not "only 3 left."
- No BNPL, ever.

### 8. CTA

*"I left the link below."* Flat. No urgency performance.

## Hard laws (check every one before delivering)

1. **No em dashes.** Anywhere. Period, comma or parentheses.
2. **No "That's not X. It's Y."** contrastive reveal pairs, or any variant.
3. **No staccato fragment stacks in spoken lines.** Every VO line is a complete sentence and cadence varies. Do not solve a tight timecode by chopping a sentence into fragments, let the line run past the cut.
4. **Never personify the bag.** The product is "it," never "she" or "her." Our bags carry women's names, so "she's holding all that" reads as an actual woman named Sofia. Absolute, every surface, including internal docs.
5. **No competitor comparisons.** No other brand named or on screen, no price comparison, no dupe / replica / alternative / knockoff.
6. **"Birkin-inspired" is legal in VO and captions.** It is our stated mechanism and the live Weekender PDP says it. Never in a generation prompt (the kie claims-grep hard-fails). Never as a comparison, never with a Hermes price, never ranked against anything. Write it as shape plus scale to make it a mechanism rather than a name-drop.
7. **No origin claims.** Ever.
8. **No claim we cannot back**, especially about products we do not make.
9. **No fabricated citations, numbers, studies or reviews.**
10. **No transition angles.** Day-to-night, seasonal switch. A season word is fine as a wrapper on the hook, but if swapping "fall" for "spring" breaks the ad, the positioning was the problem.
11. **The product is on screen essentially the entire ad.** No generic or random-product frames. Dismissals live in VO over OUR product.
12. **Say the brand right.** ElevenLabs and Seedance both render it "Volantra." Catch it on every read.

## Visual format (greenscreen VO, the default)

- 9:16, 1080x1920. Creator cut out on greenscreen, bottom-left, roughly a quarter of frame height. Full-bleed product b-roll behind.
- **New visual every ~3.5s.** Alternate stills and motion, never more than 2 stills or 3 b-roll back to back.
- Word-synced burned captions: white bold rounded sans, thin dark outline, sentence case, no terminal punctuation, 1-2 lines, 4-8 words per card, centered just above the creator.
- Creator jumps bottom-left to bottom-right once, around the 3/4 mark.
- **The last beat is a screen recording of the live PDP** showing the real price, the real strike-through, the swatches and Add to Cart. Clean browser, no extensions. The cart must show the real sale, never an invented graphic.
- One continuous VO take, never per-line splices. VO around -14 LUFS, all generated b-roll audio muted.

For organic review replications the format inverts: one continuous handheld take, no music, no cuts, captions only on thesis beats rather than word-synced. The beat formula does not change.

## Self-audit before delivering

Run all of these. Any failure is a rewrite, not a note.

- [ ] Does the product intro read exactly `This is the [Name] from Velantra.` and land by 0:06?
- [ ] Does the hook resolve into a verdict or reversal inside the hook itself?
- [ ] Swap a competitor's bag into the script. Does it still read? If yes, the attribute stack is generic.
- [ ] Will this angle still work in six months?
- [ ] Is the no-logo fact in there?
- [ ] Is every attribute a physical fact of our bag, verified against the product skill and the live PDP?
- [ ] Is every price, colorway count and stock claim true on the live page today?
- [ ] Zero em dashes. Zero "she/her" for the bag. Zero fragment stacks in spoken lines.
- [ ] Word count and runtime inside budget at the right wpm for the format.
- [ ] Read it out loud. Does it sound like a person talking, or like copy?
