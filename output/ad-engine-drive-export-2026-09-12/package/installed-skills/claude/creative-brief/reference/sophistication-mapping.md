# Hook Sophistication Mapping

When adapting a reference VSL to a new brand, the **hook block changes shape based on the target avatar's awareness level**. Get this wrong and the ad either condescends (talking down to a sophisticated buyer) or confuses (going over a beginner's head).

## The 5 awareness levels (Schwartz)

1. **Unaware** — doesn't know she has a problem
2. **Problem-aware** — knows she has the problem, doesn't know the cause
3. **Solution-aware** — knows the cause + knows solutions exist, doesn't know yours
4. **Product-aware** — knows your product exists, hasn't bought
5. **Most-aware** — knows your product + wants it, needs an offer trigger

Most direct-response ads target levels 2, 3, or 4. Level 1 needs editorial / discovery content; level 5 needs offers, not VSLs.

## Hook shape per level

### Problem-aware hook (e.g. hormonal-belly reference VSL)
- **Pattern:** Symptom callout → "if this is you" → curiosity gap → "I want to show you what I learned"
- **Time budget:** 30+ seconds on symptom inventory before mechanism
- **Visual:** Body shots, mirror selfies, real bodies that look like the viewer
- **Function:** Get her to see herself first; THEN earn the right to teach the cause

### Solution-aware hook (e.g. Motilli GLP-1 VSL)
- **Pattern:** Name the medication/situation + name the symptom + curiosity gap about why nothing has worked
- **Time budget:** ~15 seconds front-loaded, then straight to mechanism
- **Visual:** Bottle graveyard, product montage of what she's already tried
- **Function:** Skip discovery (she already knows). Open the loop: "you've tried X, Y, Z — here's why none of it worked."

### Product-aware hook
- **Pattern:** Name the product + a *new* angle she hasn't considered (new ingredient, new study, new mechanism)
- **Time budget:** ~5–8 seconds — go straight to differentiation
- **Visual:** Product on screen early, near-immediate
- **Function:** Re-engage someone who has heard the pitch before with a fresh hook

## Mapping reference → target

When adapting, compare the awareness levels and adjust:

| Reference | Target | Adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| Problem-aware | Problem-aware | Direct adapt; preserve hook structure |
| Problem-aware | Solution-aware | **Compress hook + vindication blocks by ~50%.** She already knows she has the problem. Skip discovery, go to "here's why your existing efforts haven't worked." |
| Problem-aware | Product-aware | Compress hook AND vindication. Lead with new differentiator. |
| Solution-aware | Problem-aware | **Expand hook block.** She doesn't know she has it yet — need symptom inventory + identification cascade first. |
| Solution-aware | Solution-aware | Direct adapt; swap medications/conditions |

## What stays the same regardless of sophistication

- **Belief-shift count** — if the reference runs 8 shifts, the adaptation runs 8 shifts. Compress *within* blocks, not by cutting shifts.
- **Vindication-before-mechanism** ordering — this earns the right to teach. Required at every sophistication level.
- **Blame-transfer line** — "it's not your fault, the things you tried weren't going to work." Required at every sophistication level.
- **Mechanism block length** — ~70–90 seconds. Doesn't compress with sophistication.
- **Reader-surrogate line** — "I'm reading this thinking…" Required.
- **Hook callback at mechanism resolution** — required.
- **Talking-head/graphic sandwich** in the mechanism block — required.

## Avatar-specific overrides (load from brand memory)

Always check brand memory for explicit voice/objection rules before writing:
- Banned metaphors / mechanism phrasings
- Required mechanism-lead order (e.g. Motilli always leads with apigenin)
- Banned urgency levers (e.g. no gastroparesis fear for GLP-1 avatar)
- Objections to preempt vs objections to *not* preempt (e.g. unsophisticated avatars don't have manufacturing/heavy-metals objections yet)
- Confidence vs evidence calibration (some avatars want one-line dismissals, not deep proof)

These rules override sophistication defaults.
