FAQ

What exactly is Renavita?
A chewable true Ceylon cinnamon gummy (2,000mg Cinnamomum verum) made to support your body's natural insulin response — the cellular step that low thyroid hormone tends to leave behind. Zero sugar, vegan, third-party tested.
Will this really work for me?
If your labs read "normal" but you're still exhausted, foggy, and stuck, you're exactly who we built this for. It doesn't replace your thyroid medication — it supports the downstream step your medication was never designed to reach. And it's backed by a 90-day guarantee, so you can find out with no risk.
I'm on levothyroxine / Synthroid. Is it safe to take together?
Ceylon cinnamon works through a completely separate pathway from levothyroxine — one is thyroid hormone, the other supports glucose uptake at the cell. There are no documented interactions in the research. Keep taking your levothyroxine on an empty stomach as directed; take your Renavita with a meal. As always, run any change past your doctor.
Will this help my blood sugar / A1C?
Renavita supports your body's natural insulin response and healthy blood sugar already in the normal range. It's not a diabetes treatment and it's not a substitute for medical care — it supports the cellular pathway (GLUT4) that a T3 deficit can quietly suppress.
Isn't this just cinnamon? I can buy that at the grocery store.
What's in your cabinet is almost certainly Cassia — a different plant, with up to 250 times the coumarin (hard on the liver) and far less of the MHCP compound that matters. Saying Ceylon is "just cinnamon" is like calling aspirin "just tree bark." The species and the compound are the whole point.
I've tried cinnamon capsules before and they did nothing.
Three reasons that happens: wrong species (Cassia, not Ceylon), wrong dose (most capsules are underdosed), and wrong delivery (a dry capsule swallowed whole gets wrecked by stomach acid before the active is released). You didn't try Ceylon in an absorbed form — you tried a label that said "cinnamon."
My Hashimoto's is complex. Can a gummy really help?
You're right that it's complex, and we'd never claim to fix Hashimoto's. We're specific: low T3 lowers GLUT4, GLUT4 lets glucose into cells, and Renavita supports that one downstream step through a non-thyroid pathway. It's a targeted nudge at one link in a well-documented chain — not a cure, and not a "wellness" catch-all.
What would my doctor say?
Most doctors treat within the protocol they know, and the thyroid-to-blood-sugar connection lags years behind the research in everyday practice. Ceylon cinnamon is a well-tolerated food compound with a strong safety profile. We'd always encourage you to share what you're taking with your doctor — you deserve a care team that's in the loop.
How long until I feel something?
Most women notice the afternoon crash softening in the first week or two, with a clearer shift around weeks 3–4. The deeper changes — steady energy, the scale moving, lab numbers drifting — tend to show across months 2–3. Give it the full bottle.
Are there any side effects?
Ceylon cinnamon is well tolerated at these doses. Because it's gentle on the stomach and sugar-free, most women take it with their morning coffee with no issue. If you're pregnant, nursing, or on medication, check with your doctor first.
What does it taste like?
Warm apple-pie cinnamon, lightly sweetened with stevia and monk fruit. No sugar, no chalky aftertaste. Women tell us it's the first supplement they don't dread taking.
