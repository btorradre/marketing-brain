## Reusable decisions

1. Human experience usually earns human footage; the presenter can fulfill that role.
2. Make a visible verb/action match each beat, not just a noun/category match.
3. Alternate explanation with lived consequence when the story crosses that boundary.
4. Change scientific scale because the explanatory question changed.
5. Distinguish ingredient identity, ingredient action, branded product and actual evidence.
6. Use presenter returns for interpretation, chapter changes, qualification and emotional connection.
7. Let an action resolve; rapid lists and demonstrations need different durations.
8. Make payoff a new scene that answers a specific earlier loss.
9. Let the final images simplify toward the item and action.
10. Treat reference reuse, unsupported visual authority and weak literal matches as observations to evaluate, not formulas to reproduce.
