## Raw emotional action sourcing

Use [TikTok B-roll Sourcing](../../tiktok-broll-sourcing/SKILL.md) when the task calls for raw, organic, text-free TikTok-style action clips with extreme emotion. It is a sourcing specialization within the chosen concept, not a sixth visual format. Every selected clip must pass its EV 5/5, rawness, exact-action and no-text gates. It does not force human footage into a fully animated brief.
