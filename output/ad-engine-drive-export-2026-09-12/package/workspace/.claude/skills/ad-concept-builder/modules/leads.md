# Slot 2 — Lead (the opening)

How line 1 hits. The lead must (a) open ON the problem, (b) no throat-clearing, (c) match the avatar's CURRENT awareness, (d) make the avatar see herself in line 1. Pick ONE.

If a hook already exists from the hook-generation skill, the lead must CONTINUE it (same narrator, loop, temperature) — don't generate a competing opener.

| Code | Lead | Directness | Example | Best awareness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L-A | **Story** | most indirect | "I bought a jacket at Goodwill last Saturday." | any |
| L-B | **Problem-Solution** | medium-direct | "Deep lines around your mouth no cream touches." | problem / solution |
| L-C | **Secret** | medium-indirect | "Your LDL is the LAST thing to change. By the time it's high, the damage is done." | solution |
| L-D | **Proclamation** | indirect | "90% of people on BP meds 20+ yrs die from the exact thing the meds were meant to prevent." | unaware / problem |
| L-E | **Confession** | indirect | "I've been a pharmacist 28 years. I trusted the system for all of them." | solution / product |
| L-F | **Witness** | indirect | "My mother-in-law sat alone at the far end of the table. Nobody told her why." | unaware / problem |
| L-G | **Timestamp** | flexible | "At 2:19 AM I typed 'can atorvastatin cause liver damage' into Google." | problem / solution / product |
| L-H | **Failed-Attempt Catalog** | medium-direct | "Beetroot for 7 months. Every morning. BP didn't move." | solution |
| L-I | **Paradox** | indirect | "He did everything right for 20 years. Marathon shape. Dead at 54." | problem / solution |
| L-J | **Child Truth** | indirect | "'Grandma, why do old people smell funny?' — loud enough for the whole table." | unaware / problem |
| L-K | **Authority Disruption** | flexible | "I'm a cardiologist. A patient walked in with a bag of 11 supplement bottles." | problem / solution / product |
| L-L | **Death/Loss** | indirect | "My husband died at 63. Stroke. They said it was sudden. It wasn't." | problem |
| L-M | **Cost** | medium-direct | "$197 a month on BP supplements. 8 bottles. Numbers hadn't moved in 5 months." | solution / product |

## Awareness → viable leads (the picker constraint)
- **Unaware:** Story, Proclamation, Confession, Witness, Child Truth. *Avoid:* Problem-Solution, Failed-Attempt, Cost.
- **Problem-aware:** Story, Problem-Solution, Confession, Witness, Paradox, Child Truth, Timestamp, Death/Loss. *Avoid:* Failed-Attempt referencing solutions she hasn't tried.
- **Solution-aware:** Secret, Failed-Attempt, Problem-Solution, Timestamp, Paradox, Authority Disruption, Cost. *Avoid:* Proclamation.
- **Product-aware:** Timestamp, Witness, Confession, Authority Disruption, Cost. *Avoid:* Proclamation, Secret.
- **Most-aware:** open on the offer/identity, not discovery — use Confession or Timestamp lightly, then move fast to CTA.

**Throat-clearing test:** delete line 1. If the hook is stronger, line 1 was preamble. Keep deleting until removal makes it weaker.
