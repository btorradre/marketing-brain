## 2. Segment sizes by angle

**SCOPE DECISION (Brooks, 2026-07-02): all four angles target the 48M beachhead only.** We are not pitching or pursuing the broader ~125M weekly pain-relief market — do not present Convenience as an expansion angle or put the 125M in any segment table. (The AHA ~50%-weekly and CHPA 52M stats survive only as citations for the funnel's 46% weekly-OTC filter in the master doc §1.3.)

How the four angles size within the beachhead — they are **doors, not partitions**: one buyer can walk through several (fear and shame co-occur constantly in the VoC; Radhakrishnan found choking history and pill-modification strongly correlated), so the emotional segments overlap and won't sum to 48M. Convenience is different in kind: it isn't a sub-segment but the *universal, low-threat framing* of the format benefit — every beachhead buyer doses weekly by definition and carries the friction with her, so it addresses the full 48M without asking her to confess anything.

| Angle | Sub-avatar | Sizing basis (within the 48M) | Segment size |
|:--|:--|:--|--:|
| **1. Convenience** | Every beachhead buyer, met situationally — no water, on the go | Universal within the beachhead: weekly dosing is the beachhead's defining filter, and no-water moments come with it. No published dry-swallow stat exists — sized structurally, not epidemiologically | **~48M — the full beachhead** |
| **2. Choking Fear** | The phobic — body fights the pill | 32% report gagging (Harris 2003) → 57% of current strugglers have actually choked on medication (Radhakrishnan 2021, OR 7.25) | **~15–27M** · headline **~20M** |
| **3. Adult Shame** | The workaround-runner — kids' medicine, crushing, hiding it | 17% split pills (Harris) → 53% have cut/crushed meds (Radhakrishnan) / 58.8% modified (Schiele 2013, n=1,051); fewer than 1 in 4 have ever told a health professional | **~8–25M** · headline **~17M** |
| **4. Untreated Pain** | The avoider — suffers instead of dosing | 9.4% non-adherent due to swallowing difficulty (Schiele) → 14% delay + 8% skip + 4% quit entirely (Harris); 25.5% skipped pills (Dorman 2019); 23% intentional non-adherence (Marquis 2013) | **~5–12M** · headline **~10M** |

**Headline deck line:** *"Inside the 48M beachhead: ~20M who fear the pill, ~17M who hide a workaround system, ~10M who just don't take it — and one convenience door all 48 million can walk through without confessing anything."*

Keyword-classifying the full VoC database (Reddit/TikTok/YouTube/IG) into the four buckets: 510 comments carry a classifiable emotional/behavioral signal.

*(Multi-label; fear+shame is the dominant overlap — 23 comments hit both.)*

**How to read this honestly:** the scrape targeted "can't swallow pills" content, so it oversamples people *mid-struggle* and undersamples avoiders (who aren't in the comments — they gave up) and situational users (who don't identify with the topic). So the VoC does **not** size Untreated Pain or Convenience — published stats do that (above). What the VoC uniquely gives us:

1. **Fear : Shame ≈ 2.6 : 1 by voice volume.** Fear is the *loudest, most viral* lane — it's what people confess publicly. Shame is quieter but runs deeper (identity), consistent with 3 in 4 sufferers never telling a professional.
2. **Creative-lane weighting for launch:** among the three emotional spears, weight original creative roughly **Fear > Shame > Untreated Pain** for hook volume, while remembering Untreated Pain's ~10M are the *highest-intent* buyers (they're already paying the cost of no solution) — smaller megaphone, hotter conversion.
3. **Convenience earns reach, not depth:** almost no one *emotes* about waterless dosing — which is exactly why it's the broad, low-threat door (§3.2 of the master strategy) rather than a confession lane.

## 4. Full citation list

- Harris Interactive online survey, May–Jun 2003, n=679 (grant: Schwarz Pharma): 40% difficulty; 80% "stuck in throat" sensation; 32% gagging; 17% split pills; 14% delayed / 8% skipped / 4% discontinued; <1 in 4 told a professional. *(Percentages preserved via secondary source (Oralflo); denominator = among those with difficulty.)*
- Harvard Health, "Trouble swallowing your medication?" — "up to 40%" corroboration.
- Radhakrishnan et al. 2021, *Patient Preference and Adherence* (n=152, median age 36, 64% F): 32% current difficulty, 55% lifetime; 57% of strugglers have choked on medication (OR 7.25); 53% of strugglers have cut/crushed meds (OR 3.93). PMC7810703.
- Schiele et al. 2013, *Eur J Clin Pharmacol* (Germany, n=1,051): 37.4% difficulty; 9.4% non-adherent due to difficulty; 58.8% of affected had modified drugs; 70.4% never identified by their GP. PubMed 23052416.
- Marquis et al. 2013, *Int J Clin Pharm* (Swiss pharmacies): 23% intentional non-adherence among affected; difficulty at every dose for 83.7%.
- Dorman et al. 2019 (via Fernandez 2022 review, PMC9805106): 25.5% skipped pills; anxiety 20.6% / depression 28.4% associated.
- Fernandez et al. 2022 narrative review (10 studies, n=2,288): mean pill-aversion prevalence 23.1% (range 11–54%) in adults without physiologic dysphagia.
- CHPA: 23% of US adults (~52M) use acetaminophen-containing medicine weekly.
- American Heart Association poll, Jul 2023 (n≈3,000): ~50% of US adults take pain medication weekly+.
- GM Insights 2024: US acetaminophen ≈ $4.1B; Market.us 2024: North America OTC analgesics ≈ $13.0B.
- Proprietary VoC database v2 (n=4,095, Reddit/TikTok/YouTube/IG), classified 2026-07-02.

**Known soft spots (don't lead with these):** Harris symptom %s survive only via one secondary source; phagophobia "7–9%" has no peer-reviewed anchor (avoid); no published stat exists for adults using children's formulations or for dry-swallow frequency — use VoC verbatims as the evidence for those behaviors instead of a fake percentage.
