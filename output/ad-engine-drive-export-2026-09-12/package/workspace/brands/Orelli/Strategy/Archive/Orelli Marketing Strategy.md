# Orelli — Marketing Strategy

**Product:** Adult-strength acetaminophen (500mg) soft chew. $29.99/bottle — one-time US DTC purchase (reorder when out; **no subscription**).
**Launch window:** Dec 2026 / Jan 2027.
**The angle, in full:** *It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy.*
**The avatar, in full:** A woman 20–38 who heavily struggles to swallow pills.

That is the entire strategy. Everything below is the disciplined execution of those two sentences. We do not broaden the avatar. We do not complicate the angle. Every dollar, every creator, every frame of creative is built for **her**.

---

## 1. The One Woman We Build For

We are not marketing to "people with pain." Pain relief demand already exists — she already buys Tylenol/Advil. We are marketing to a woman with a **specific, lifelong, shameful friction**: she physically cannot swallow pills, and she has never been told an adult alternative exists.

**Her name is Maya. She's 26.** (Use her as the lens for every creative decision.)

- She has struggled to swallow pills **since she was a kid**. She thought she'd grow out of it. She didn't. *("I'm a 29 year old and still struggling 😭")*
- Her body actively fights her: heart races, throat closes, she gags, sometimes vomits. *("My brain literally red flags me as dying. It's horrifying.")*
- She has a **whole humiliating system** to get medicine down — and she's never told anyone the full extent of it:
  - Crushes pills into applesauce/yogurt *("it is absolutely disgusting crushed")*
  - Buys **children's grape liquid** by the half-bottle and does dose math *("the bottle just thinks I'm a chubby 12 year old")*
  - Cuts pills in half, forces them with a specific soda, "mentally prepares" for minutes
  - Or just **suffers the headache** *("I'd rather suffer through pain than risk choking on a pill")*
- Her family has **mocked her** for it. *("All of my family make fun of me.")*
- Her core belief: **"I'm the only adult who still can't do this."** This shame is the lock. It's also the key.

**The conversion moment is not "this tastes good." It's being seen.** The instant a creator says *"wait — you can't swallow pills either?"*, Maya feels recognized for the first time in her life. That recognition is the sale. The product is the relief that follows.

### Who Maya is NOT (guardrails)
- ❌ Not a "wellness enthusiast" browsing supplements. She's not shopping. She's suffering quietly.
- ❌ Not someone we educate about pain or about acetaminophen. She already trusts Tylenol — it's in her cabinet.
- ❌ Not a value-shopper we win on price. We win on **dignity and possibility**, not pennies.
- ❌ Not the "medically captive" Tylenol user (pregnant/CKD/post-op). That's a real, high-LTV Phase-2 segment — **park it.** Phase 1 is Maya only.

## 2. The Emotional Spine (every ad runs this arc)

Every piece of creative — video, static, email, landing page — moves Maya through the same four beats. This is the spine. Memorize it.

| Beat | What it does | Maya's internal line |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **1. Recognition** | Name her exact secret behavior out loud | "Oh my god, that's me." |
| **2. Relief** | Show she's not alone / not broken | "Wait, other adults can't either?" |
| **3. Revelation** | Reveal the product as the obvious answer | "Wait… this *exists*??" |
| **4. Liberation** | Paint the after-state: free, normal, done | "I'm finally free." *(real quote: "I'm almost in tears guys")* |

The whole job of a hook is **Beat 1 in three seconds.** The whole job of the product reveal is **Beat 3.** If an ad skips Recognition and opens on the product, it dies — it becomes "another supplement gummy" and Maya scrolls.

## 3. Positioning & Message Hierarchy

**Category we own:** *The first adult pain reliever you don't have to swallow.* Not a vitamin. Not wellness. Not a supplement. **It's the medicine she already takes — in the only format she can actually take.**

**The message ladder (top = what the ad leads with):**

1. **Identity hook** — "POV: you're a grown adult who still can't swallow a pill." *(Recognition)*
2. **The reveal** — "It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy." *(Revelation — say it this plainly)*
3. **The permission** — "You're not childish. You're not broken. You just needed it made for adults." *(Relief)*
4. **The proof of normalcy** — "We have an entire gummy vitamin industry because nobody can swallow pills. Pain relief just never caught up." *(Reframe — pulled straight from VoC)*
5. **The after-state** — "Headache. Two gummies. Done. No water, no gagging, no system." *(Liberation)*

**The line we protect at all costs:** *"It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy."* Simple, disarming, instantly understood, zero education. Do not dress it up. The plainness IS the persuasion.

## 4. Voice & Language Rules (use her words, not ours)

The VoC database is the brand voice bible. Pull verbatim. Maya trusts language that sounds like her own DMs, not pharma copy.

**Always say:**
- "can't swallow pills" / "gag" / "it gets stuck" / "stays in my mouth" / "dry swallow"
- "I thought I was the only one" / "no more fighting your own throat"
- "it's literally just Tylenol" / "for adults" / "grown adult"
- "no water" / "no system" / "no crushing it into applesauce"

**Never say:**
- "dysphagia," "delivery format," "innovative," "revolutionary," "wellness," "self-care ritual"
- Anything that sounds like a supplement brand or a pharma label
- Efficacy bragging ("works better / faster / stronger") — **we don't fight the chemistry war.** Acetaminophen is what it is; she already accepts it.

**The shame-to-relief mechanic is the most important tonal rule:** name the embarrassing thing *first*, then immediately remove the shame. Never mock — always "me too." The brand is the friend who confesses first.

## 5. Hook Bank (built from real VoC verbatims)

Lead hooks, sorted by mechanism. These are starting points — the creators who genuinely can't swallow pills will improvise better ones. Feed them this list as a brief.

**A. Confessional / Identity ("that's me")**
- "POV: you're 26 and still can't swallow a pill."
- "Things I thought I'd grow out of: swallowing pills. I'm [age]. I did not."
- "My most embarrassing adult secret: I still buy children's medicine."
- "I'm a whole adult and I still gag every single time I try to take a pill."
- "Tell me you can't swallow pills without telling me… I'll go first: I hide them in applesauce."

**B. The Workaround Exposé (name the humiliating system)**
- "Things I do to avoid swallowing a pill: [crush into yogurt / kids' grape Tylenol / cut in half / just suffer]."
- "I've taken half a bottle of children's Tylenol to get one adult dose. Be honest — same?"
- "My headache 'system': cry, mentally prepare for 10 minutes, gag, give up, suffer."

**C. The Vindication / Reframe (you're not broken)**
- "We literally have an entire gummy vitamin industry because nobody can swallow pills. So why is pain relief still a horse pill?"
- "Not being able to swallow pills isn't a 'kid' thing. Up to 40% of adults can't either. You were never the problem — the pill was."
- "You're not childish for needing children's medicine. You were just never given an adult version."

**D. The Reveal ("wait, this exists??")**
- "I just found out adult Tylenol comes in a gummy and I'm genuinely emotional."
- "Nobody told me this existed. It's literally just Tylenol — in a gummy. No water. No gagging."
- "POV: you find out you never have to choke down a pill again."

**E. The Liberation (after-state)**
- "Headache. Two gummies. Done. First time in my life taking medicine didn't ruin my day."
- "I'm 18 and I just took adult medicine for the first time without crying. I'm free."

## 6. Creative Formats & Content Pillars

The format that converts Maya is **confessional, lo-fi, creator-shot UGC** — the exact organic content TikTok already rewards. Not polished ads. The ad IS the confession.

**Four content pillars (rotate; ~70% should be Pillars 1–2):**

1. **The Confession** — creator reveals her own pill-swallowing struggle to camera. Highest performer. Pure Recognition → Relief.
2. **The Workaround Reveal** — creator shows the humiliating system (the applesauce, the kids' bottle, the gagging) then the gummy. Recognition → Revelation.
3. **The "Wait, This Exists" Reaction** — genuine discovery energy. Revelation → Liberation.
4. **The Vindication / Educational** — the "40% of adults can't either" stat, the gummy-vitamin-industry reframe. Removes shame, gives her permission to buy.

**Static / image creative (native-ad style):** comment-screenshot creative pulled straight from the VoC ("I thought I was the only one" / "I'm 29 and still struggling"), with the simple overlay: *It's just Tylenol. In a gummy.* Let the real comments do the work.

**Production note for replication skills:** product must always render accurately via image-to-image from the canonical Orelli reference once shot — never from-scratch. (Same discipline as other brands in the vault.)

## 7. Channel & Creator Strategy

Maya is **already self-selecting on social right now.** "Can't swallow pills" content has millions of views with comment sections full of "OMG me too." Those comment sections are pre-qualified lead lists. We don't interrupt her — we join the conversation she's already in.

**Primary motion: creator seeding (Nano + Micro, 1K–50K).**
- Cast creators who **genuinely cannot swallow pills.** Authenticity is non-negotiable — Maya can smell a script. A real sufferer needs zero brief; she just tells her truth and shows the product.
- Comp: free product + 15–20% affiliate commission. No upfront fees at this stage.
- Brief = the VoC database + the hook bank + one rule: *"Tell your real story. Then show the gummy. Say it's just Tylenol."*
- **Mine comment sections** of existing viral "can't swallow pills" videos → DM those creators and those commenters.

**Platform roles:**

- **Instagram Reels** — validation. She checks our IG before she buys. Keep it full of real testimonials and the confession format.

**Hashtag/SEO surface area she's already tagged in:** #cantswallowpills #pillhack #adultproblems, and the evergreen "how to swallow pills" search demand.

## 8. The Funnel (one step — protect its simplicity)

Maya needs **zero education and has zero objection to pain relief.** Do not build a quiz → email → nurture maze. The funnel is:

**Creator confession → "wait, this exists?" → click → buy.**

- Landing page mirrors the ad's emotional spine 1:1 (Recognition → Relief → Revelation → Liberation). The page is the ad, extended.
- Above the fold: her struggle in her own words + *"It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy."* + buy button. That's the whole pitch.
- FAQ does the objection-handling (Section 9) so the hero stays clean.
- One CTA. **Stock-up offer, not subscription** — buy 2–3 bottles (one for home, one for her bag, one for her desk). Pain hits unpredictably and reactively, so we can't time a recurring charge; the win is she's *never caught without it.*

## 9. Objection Pre-Load (answer these before she asks)

These are the only things standing between Recognition and purchase. Handle each explicitly in FAQ / secondary creative — never in the hook.

| Objection | Where it lives | How we answer |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **"Does it actually taste good — or is it nasty like crushed pills?"** | #1 risk. Taste. | Lead with taste proof in creative (real reaction to first bite). This is a make-or-break product truth — if the chew tastes like chalky acetaminophen, nothing else matters. *Flag to product team: taste-masking 500mg APAP is the single biggest execution dependency.* |
| **"Is it real medicine or a supplement?"** | FAQ hero | "It's real over-the-counter acetaminophen — the same active ingredient as Tylenol, 500mg. Not a supplement. Actual pain relief." |
| **"Will it actually work / acetaminophen is weak"** | Don't pick this fight | We never claim it works *better*. We say: it's the exact medicine she already takes and trusts — just in a form she can take. Lean on menstrual / headache / everyday use where she already accepts it. |
| **"$30 a bottle is a lot for Tylenol"** | FAQ | Reframe from price to what she's escaping: the gagging, the kids'-medicine shame, the suffered headaches, the whole system. Not "cheaper Tylenol" — *takeable* Tylenol. Bundle so per-bottle cost drops on stock-up. |
| **"Is it safe / liver / dosing?"** | FAQ + trust | Our hidden moat: clear single-dose format prevents the accidental double-dipping that causes most APAP harm (45.6% of adults double-dip; format-based dose limits cut liver-failure cases 16% — JAMA). One product, clear dose, no hidden acetaminophen stacking. |

## 10. Offer & Repeat Purchase (one-time model — no subscription)

We are **not** running subscriptions. Pain relief is taken *reactively* — she reaches for it when something hurts, not on a daily schedule — so there's no honest way to predict when she'll need the next bottle. Forcing a recurring charge would fight the actual usage behavior and drive cancellations. Instead we engineer repeat purchase three ways:

- **Stock-up offer as the hero (drives AOV).** Bundle pricing — buy 2 or 3, per-bottle price drops. Framed as **"never get caught without it":** one for home, one for her bag, one for her desk/car. This is the exact behavior the VoC already shows (the Excedrin "emergency bottle everywhere" pattern) — we're naming a habit she already has.
- **Replenishment, not subscription.** When she runs low, a simple reorder reminder (email/SMS) with one-tap reorder — opt-in, helpful, not locked. The trigger is *her* cabinet running empty, not our billing cycle.
- **Retention is real, just not contractual.** Once she switches formats there's no reason to go back — the pill was the only problem and we solved it. The efficacy is undeniable (unlike supplements), so the only job is staying top-of-mind for the next reorder. That's what the stock-up offer + reminder + the customer→affiliate loop do.
- Starter offer: first-order discount or free shipping to clear the trivial trial barrier. The hard part (deciding she wants it) is already done by the creator.

## 11. First 90 Days

1. **Lock the product taste truth.** Get real first-bite reactions on camera. If it tastes good, that becomes hero creative; if it doesn't, fix it before scaling. Everything depends on this.
2. **Seed 50–100 creators** who genuinely can't swallow pills. Brief = VoC + hook bank + "tell your truth."
3. **Mine 20–30 viral "can't swallow pills" videos** → DM creators + top commenters.
4. **Ship the one-step landing page** mirroring the emotional spine.
5. **Build the static comment-screenshot library** from the VoC database.
6. **Target: 5,000 customers in 6 months** to prove unit economics (≈2% conversion on 250K monthly creator reach), with multi-bottle AOV + reorder rate as the LTV levers (not subscription retention).
