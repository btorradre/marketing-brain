# Orelli — Launch Creator & Affiliate Playbook

**Goal:** Go to market through a creator-led distribution machine that finds Maya (woman 20–38 who can't swallow pills) through people who share her exact secret.
**Mechanism:** Five coordinated plays, one shared attribution + comp backbone.
**North-star for launch:** 5,000 subscribers in 6 months (per strategy doc), proven on creator-sourced traffic.

## 1. The Distribution Architecture (how the 5 plays fit)

Think of it as **three layers of trust + one engine + one flywheel.** They are not separate campaigns — they feed each other.

| Play | Tool | Tier | Role in the funnel | Comp model | Target # |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **A. Affiliate engine** | **Katalys** | Nano–Mid (1K–250K) | **Volume + conversion.** The core revenue driver. | Performance (20–25%, lifetime reorder) | **80–100 active** |
| **B. Brand equity** | Manual / talent mgrs | Macro (1M+ IG) | **Credibility + reach halo.** Makes the brand "real." | Flat fee + affiliate kicker | **3–5** |
| **C. Product seeding** | **Aspire** | Nano–Micro (1K–50K) | **Top-of-funnel discovery + UGC volume.** Feeds A. | Free product + affiliate | **continuous, 150–200 gifted** |
| **D. Doctor credibility** | Manual outreach | Authority | **Objection-killer.** Trust layer on LP + ads. | Honorarium / advisory | **5–10** |
| **E. Customer → affiliate** | **Katalys** | Real customers | **Compounding flywheel.** Maya sells Maya. | Same affiliate terms | **Phase 2 (Day 60+)** |

**The flow between them:**
```
Aspire seeding (C) → identifies who posts well + converts
        ↓ promote the winners
Katalys affiliate (A) → put them on performance, scale the 80–100
        ↓ social proof + reach
1M+ equity (B) → halo makes the brand credible, lifts CVR on all of it
Doctors (D) → sit on the LP/FAQ, kill the "is it safe/real" objection for everyone
        ↓ after customers buy
Customer→affiliate (E) → best customers become the next wave of affiliates
```

**Sequencing rule:** Seeding (C) and Affiliate recruiting (A) run in parallel pre-launch. Doctors (D) must be locked *before* launch (they appear on the LP). Equity (B) fires *at* launch for the splash. Customer→affiliate (E) only switches on once you have ~500+ happy customers (Day 60+).

## 2. Workstream A — Katalys Affiliate Engine (80–100 creators)

**This is the core revenue machine.** Everything else supports it.

### A1. Who we recruit (vetting rubric — score 0–2 each, recruit 5+)
1. **Genuine struggle** — she actually can't/couldn't swallow pills. *Non-negotiable. Maya smells a script.* (0–2)
2. **Confessional content history** — already posts relatable/"storytime"/"things nobody tells you" content. (0–2)
3. **Audience match** — 60%+ female, 18–40, US. (0–2)
4. **Engagement, not follower count** — 4%+ ER beats raw reach at this tier. (0–2)
5. **Comment-section signal** — her existing posts have "omg me too" energy. (0–2)

### A2. Sourcing channels (run all four)
- **Comment-mining** (highest intent): pull the top 30 viral "how to swallow pills" / "can't swallow pills" videos on TikTok + YouTube → DM the *creators* AND the top "OMG me too" commenters who themselves create content.
- **Hashtag scrape:** #cantswallowpills #pillhack #adultproblems #pilltok #ChronicallyIll, plus emetophobia/anxiety creators.
- **Aspire database** (Workstream C) flags creators who post well during seeding → graduate them to affiliate.
- **Inbound:** affiliate signup link in every LP footer + email + the brand IG bio.

### A3. The recruiting funnel (target 80–100 active from ~300 outreach)
```
300 DM/email outreach
 → 120 reply (40%)
 → 90 accept + get product (75%)
 → 80–100 post within 21 days (we manage to this)
```
Build the list in a tracker (Airtable/Notion or Aspire CRM) with columns: handle, platform, followers, ER, struggle-verified (Y/N), outreach date, status, Katalys link issued, first post date, sales-to-date.

### A4. Outreach DM (cold, copy-paste)
> Hey [name] — saw your video about [specific struggle she mentioned]. I felt that in my soul, I'm the same way (I genuinely cannot swallow a pill, I've buried this my whole life lol). We just built something for exactly us: **Orelli — it's literally just Tylenol, in a gummy.** Real adult-dose pain relief, no pill, no gagging. I'd love to send you some free, no strings. If you love it and want to share your honest take, we've got a really good affiliate setup (you earn on every order through your link — and on every time those customers reorder, for life). Can I grab an address?

### A5. The brief (keep it loose — authenticity converts)
One page + the VoC hook bank from the strategy doc. The ONLY mandatories:
- **Tell YOUR real story first** (Recognition beat), then show the gummy (Revelation).
- Say the line: **"It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy."**
- **Disclose** (#ad / paid partnership / "affiliate") — required, see §10.
- **Don't** make medical claims beyond "relieves pain / reduces fever." No "cures," no dosing advice.
- Drop her **Katalys link + code** in bio/caption.

### A6. Katalys setup
- Issue each creator a **unique tracking link + branded discount code** (dual attribution: link for click-through, code for "saw it, bought later").
- Commission: **20–25% order value**, with **lifetime / customer-tied attribution** — the creator earns on every *reorder* her referred customers make, not just the first order. (We're not running subscriptions, so this is the no-subscription equivalent of recurring income — and it's exactly what makes creators choose Orelli over one-off CPA brands. Confirm Katalys supports customer-tied lifetime attribution at setup.)
- Payout cadence: net-30 monthly via Katalys.
- **Performance tiering:** auto-bump top 20% of affiliates to 25% + a monthly bonus to keep them loyal and posting repeatedly (repeat posts > one-and-done).

## 3. Workstream B — Brand Equity Play (1M+ IG, 3–5 creators)

**Role:** not direct ROI — *credibility halo + reach.* When Maya sees a creator she already trusts using Orelli, the whole brand becomes "real," and conversion lifts across every other channel. Treat as brand spend, measure on assisted lift + content assets, not last-click ROAS.

### B1. Who
- 1M+ IG women in **lifestyle / relatable / "mom" / health-adjacent** lanes (NOT pure fitness/glam — wrong altitude for Maya).
- Bonus multiplier if she has **personally posted about hating pills / anxiety / medical stuff** — then it's authentic *and* huge.
- 3–5 total. Quality > quantity. One perfectly-matched 2M creator > five mismatched ones.

### B2. Comp & deal
- **Hybrid:** flat fee ($5–15K/post depending on size) **+ 15% affiliate kicker** via Katalys (so they have skin in conversion) **+ usage rights** (we re-run their content as paid social / on the LP — this is half the value).
- Deliverable: 1 in-feed Reel + 2–3 stories with link sticker, whitelisting/usage rights for 90 days minimum.
- Negotiate through their talent manager; budget agency fee (~20%) on top.

### B3. Sequencing
Fire these **at launch (T-0 week)** for the splash. Stagger across 2–3 weeks so reach is sustained, not a one-day spike. Repurpose every asset into paid + LP immediately.

## 4. Workstream C — Aspire Product Seeding (nano/micro discovery)

**Role:** the always-on top-of-funnel that *feeds* the affiliate engine. Cheap, high-volume, low-commitment. Find who posts well + converts, then graduate winners to Katalys affiliate (Workstream A).

### C1. Workflow in Aspire
1. **Discovery:** use Aspire's database + filters (female, 18–40, US, 1K–50K, health/lifestyle/relatable, high ER). Build a 300–400 prospect list.
2. **Gifting campaign:** automated outreach offering free product, no obligation, soft ask for honest post.
3. **CRM tracking:** Aspire tracks who accepted, who posted, content quality, engagement.
4. **Rights:** collect UGC usage rights at gifting (whitelisting clause) so every good post is reusable as paid creative.
5. **Graduation:** anyone who (a) posts something good and (b) drives sales via their gift-code → move to full Katalys affiliate terms. **This is the conveyor belt that fills the 80–100.**

### C2. Volume math
~150–200 units gifted → ~60% post → ~100+ pieces of organic content → ~30–40% of those convert well enough to graduate to affiliate. Refresh continuously; seeding never stops.

### C3. Division of labor: Aspire vs Katalys (avoid overlap)
- **Aspire** = discovery, gifting logistics, CRM, UGC rights, the nano/micro relationship layer.
- **Katalys** = the money layer — links, codes, commission, payouts — for anyone who graduates to performance.
- A creator can start in Aspire (gifted) and end in Katalys (affiliate). Aspire finds them; Katalys pays them.

## 5. Workstream D — Doctor Credibility (5–10)

**Role:** kill the two biggest non-format objections — *"is this real medicine / is it safe?"* — for every other channel at once. Doctors live on the **LP, FAQ, and ads as a trust layer**, not as performance affiliates.

### D1. Who
- **2 anchor MDs** — ideally pharmacist (PharmD), pain/family medicine, OB-GYN, or GI (dysphagia credibility). One with a real social following = 2-for-1 (credibility + reach).
- **3–8 supporting** — PharmDs, NPs, RNs, dentists, pediatric-adjacent — for "reviewed by" volume and quotes.

### D2. Sourcing
- Med/health creators already on TikTok/IG ("doctor explains" lane) — many do paid brand work.
- Platforms/agencies for medical KOLs; LinkedIn outreach; warm intros via the 3PL/manufacturing network.

### D3. Comp & deliverables
- **Honorarium** $1.5–3K each for content rights + advisory; OR **equity/advisory seat** for 1–2 anchors who'll go deeper.
- Deliverables: a "why I recommend an adult chewable acetaminophen" talking-head, a quotable LP testimonial, "medically reviewed by" attribution, and a quote bank for FAQ.

### D4. Compliance (critical — see §10)
- Doctors disclose paid relationship (#ad / "paid partner").
- Claims stay inside OTC monograph: pain/fever relief, dosing accuracy, single-source safety (no accidental APAP stacking). **No** disease-cure claims, **no** "doctor recommends you exceed label," **no** implying clinical superiority over Tylenol (it's the same molecule — that's our whole point, lean into it).
- Their strongest true line is our moat: *"clear single-dose format helps prevent the accidental double-dosing that causes most acetaminophen harm"* — backed by your own studies file (JAMA 16% liver-failure drop; 45.6% double-dip stat).

## 6. Workstream E — Customer → Affiliate Flywheel (Day 60+)

**The compounding engine: Maya converts → Maya recruits the next Maya.** Her testimonial outperforms any creator because it's a real customer with the exact problem. Switch on once you have ~500+ subscribers and proof the product delights.

### E1. Mechanics (all on Katalys, same backbone)
1. **Post-purchase invite:** Day-14 email/SMS — "Love it? Earn on every friend you send. Get your link." One-click enroll into Katalys.
2. **Give-get referral as the on-ramp:** "Give a friend 20% off, get $X" — low-friction entry; the ones who refer repeatedly graduate to full affiliate terms.
3. **Identify hidden creators:** flag customers who already have followings (capture IG handle at checkout / post-purchase survey) → fast-track to affiliate.
4. **UGC engine:** incentivize an unboxing/"first time taking adult medicine without gagging" post → best ones get reposted + recruited.

### E2. Why it's the highest-LTV channel
Zero CAC on the recruiter, infinite authenticity (real customer, real problem), and it turns retention into acquisition. This is the channel that makes the 6-month → 18-month curve bend up.

## 10. Compliance Guardrails (OTC drug — don't skip)

Orelli is an **OTC monograph drug**, not a supplement. Two regimes apply:
1. **FTC endorsement rules:** every paid/gifted/affiliate creator must clearly disclose (#ad, "paid partnership," "affiliate"). Gifted-for-free still requires disclosure. Build it into every brief and the Aspire/Katalys terms.
2. **OTC claims limits:** creators + doctors may only claim what the label supports — **relieves pain, reduces fever.** Prohibited: "cures," dosing advice beyond label, superiority-over-Tylenol claims (it's the same active — our angle is *format*, not potency), any pregnancy/condition claim. Provide an approved-language one-pager + a "never say" list in every brief.
3. **Doctor-specific:** paid relationship disclosed; no implication that a physician endorses off-label use. Keep their claims to format/safety/dosing-clarity — all of which are true and on-brand.

A 10-minute compliance review of any 1M+ or doctor asset before it posts is cheap insurance.

## 11. Master Timeline (T-minus 6 weeks → launch → +90 days)

**T-6 to T-4 (Foundation)**
- Stand up Katalys (links, codes, commission, payout) + Aspire (workspace, discovery filters).
- Build master tracker. Draft briefs, outreach scripts, approved-language + never-say sheets.
- Begin doctor outreach (longest lead time — start now).
- Begin Aspire seeding discovery (300–400 prospect list).

**T-4 to T-2 (Fill the pipeline)**
- Launch Aspire gifting (ship 150–200 units).
- Start affiliate cold outreach (300 DMs) via comment-mining + hashtag scrape.
- Lock 2 anchor doctors + 3–8 supporting; collect their content/quotes.
- Sign 3–5 equity creators; schedule posts for launch window.

**T-2 to T-0 (Pre-launch ramp)**
- Graduate first seeding winners → Katalys affiliates.
- Finalize LP with doctor trust block + UGC from seeding.
- Brief all affiliates; stagger their post calendar (avoid one-day burst).
- Compliance-review equity + doctor assets.

**T-0 (Launch week)**
- Fire 3–5 equity posts (staggered over 2–3 weeks).
- 40–60 affiliates live; push to 80–100 over the following 2 weeks.
- Daily monitoring; repost/whitelist best UGC into paid.

**T+2 to T+6 weeks**
- Hit 80–100 active affiliates. Tier the top 20%. Cut non-posters.
- Continuous seeding refresh → steady affiliate graduation.
- Optimize LP (doctor block A/B, hook A/B from top-performing UGC).

**T+8 to T+12 weeks (switch on the flywheel)**
- ~500+ customers → launch Customer→Affiliate (E): Day-14 invites, give-get, hidden-creator capture.
- Scale winners, formalize repeat-affiliate bonuses, plan Phase-2 segment (medically-captive) only after Maya engine is proven.

## The Plan in One Paragraph

We build one creator machine with five intake valves and a shared money-and-tracking backbone. **Aspire** seeds 150–200 nano/micro women who genuinely can't swallow pills and surfaces the ones who post and convert; those winners graduate onto **Katalys**, where we run 80–100 performance affiliates on 20–25% commission with lifetime reorder attribution (no subscription — they earn on every reorder their customers make) — the core revenue driver. **3–5 1M-follower IG creators** fire at launch as a credibility halo that lifts conversion everywhere, and **5–10 doctors** sit on the landing page killing the "is it real/safe" objection for all of it. Everything is FTC/OTC-compliant by brief. Then, once 500+ delighted customers exist, we flip the flywheel: **Maya recruits the next Maya** through the same Katalys backbone — turning retention into zero-CAC acquisition and bending the growth curve up.
