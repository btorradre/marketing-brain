# Orelli — TikTok Shop Creator Sourcing Playbook

*Created 2026-07-25. The operational how-to for finding, scoring, and recruiting the preorder creator roster. Companion to `00 — Affiliate Program Proposal (Preorder Phase).md`, which sets the terms this playbook recruits against.*

**Target: 900 sourced → 750 contacted → 85 signed → 55 posting → 35 repeat posters, by Oct 4.**

---

## 0. The core insight — why we source *on TikTok Shop* specifically

Ordinary creator sourcing ranks people by **followers**. That metric is nearly worthless for us: a 200K-follower lifestyle creator may have never sold anything to anyone.

TikTok Shop's ecosystem exposes something far better — **per-creator GMV.** Third-party databases index which creators have actually driven purchase behavior, in which categories, at what volume, over what period. That converts creator sourcing from a popularity contest into a performance search.

> **The bar: we are not looking for creators with an audience. We are looking for creators with a *buying* audience, who also genuinely cannot swallow pills.**

That intersection — proven seller × authentic sufferer — is small, and it is the entire prize. Everything below is a method for finding it.

### 0.1 The access constraint (read this before you open Seller Center)

TikTok Shop's native **Affiliate Center creator search and Target Collaboration require an active product listing.** We do not have one — the product isn't shippable until December, and OTC medications additionally require seller category eligibility that hasn't been granted.

**So we do not source inside Seller Center during the preorder window.** We source through:
1. Third-party TikTok Shop databases (paid, precise) — **Lane 1**
2. In-app manual mining (free, high-signal) — **Lanes 2 and 3**
3. Adjacent-SKU creator poaching (paid, high-conversion) — **Lane 4**
4. Partner-hashtag mining (free, pre-vetted) — **Lane 5**

All outreach happens **off-platform** (TikTok DM, Instagram DM, email in bio).

At T-0, when the product is live and listed, Affiliate Center's **Manage Creators** tool accepts an imported off-platform creator list and batch-invites up to **100 creators at a time.** Everything sourced now is built to import cleanly then. **Keep the tracker's schema (§5) intact for exactly this reason.**

## 1. Lane 1 — Database mining (the precision lane)

**Tool: Kalodata Professional (~$100/mo).** Largest creator index, deepest per-creator ROI metrics, contact export. Alternatives: FastMoss (faster data refresh, better creator dashboards), EchoTik ($9–19/mo, GMV- and brand-deal-oriented, thinner insights — a viable budget substitute). Take Kalodata's free 7-day trial first and pull the bulk of the list inside it.

**Target output: 500 scored creators from this lane.**

### 1.1 The filter stack — set these exactly

| Filter | Setting | Reasoning |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Region** | United States | Shipping + FDA jurisdiction |
| **Category** | Health · Beauty & Personal Care · Food & Beverage · Sports & Outdoors | Health is primary; the other three catch gummy-vitamin and wellness sellers |
| **GMV, last 30d** | **$1,000 – $50,000** | ⚠️ The most important filter. See §1.2 |
| **Followers** | 5,000 – **100,000** | Below 5K is unproven; above 100K they ignore a no-sample offer and get expensive (ceiling lowered from 150K by Brooks's SOP — see §3.7) |
| **Avg views/video** | ≥ 3,000 | Reach floor |
| **Female audience** | ≥ 60% | Maya is a woman 25–38 |
| **Audience age 18–44** | ≥ 60% | |
| **Posted in last 14 days** | Yes | Dead accounts waste DMs |
| **Videos, last 30d** | ≥ 8 | Consistency predicts repeat posting |

### 1.2 Why the GMV band is $1K–$50K and not "as high as possible"

This is the judgment call that determines roster quality.

- **Under $1,000/mo:** hasn't proven they can sell. No better than a follower-count pick.
- **$1,000–$50,000/mo:** the sweet spot. Proven conversion ability, still hungry, still answers DMs, and — critically — **still willing to consider an off-platform deal.** Founding Creator status means something to them.
- **Over $50,000/mo:** professionalized. Has a manager, a rate card, and a full TikTok Shop pipeline. Will not send traffic off-platform to a preorder for an unlaunched brand, and if they would, the price is a flat fee outside this budget.

Pull the over-$50K names into a separate **"Launch Wishlist"** tab. Don't burn them now — approach them in December with a live TikTok Shop listing, real reviews, and product in hand. That is a completely different and much easier pitch.

### 1.3 The second pass — the struggle screen

Database filters get you proven sellers. They cannot tell you who genuinely can't swallow pills. Every Lane 1 name goes through a manual pass:

1. Open the profile. Scan the last 30 videos plus any pinned content.
2. Search their content for the signal: pill struggle, health anxiety, emetophobia, medical storytime, "things I can't do as an adult," medication content of any kind.
3. Check the comment sections of their most-viewed videos for *"omg me too"* energy — the audience-recognition signal that predicts our conversion mechanic.
4. Score per §4. **Struggle score ≥1 is non-negotiable.** A proven seller with GMV of $40K who has no relationship to the problem is a *reject*, not a compromise. Maya smells a script, and the FTC cares whether the story is real.

Expect roughly **1 in 5 database names to clear the struggle screen.** That is why the source target is 900 to sign 85.

## 2. Lane 2 — In-app Shop-signal mining (the highest-value intersection)

Free, slower, and produces the best creators in the roster. This lane inverts Lane 1: start from the *authentic struggle*, then filter for *proven seller*.

**Target output: 200 scored creators.**

### 2.1 Method

1. Search TikTok for the strings in §2.2. Sort by both Top and Recent.
2. For each relevant video, open the **creator's profile**.
3. **Look for the orange shopping-bag icon / "Shop" tab on the profile.** That means they are already a TikTok Shop affiliate — they have gone through onboarding, they understand commission, they post shoppable content. Zero education needed on how affiliate work functions.
4. Creator has the struggle content *and* the Shop tab → **priority target, top of the outreach queue.**
5. Cross-check the handle in Kalodata for their actual GMV before contacting. A Shop tab with $0 GMV is just an unused account.

### 2.2 Search strings — work through all of these

**Direct problem:**
`can't swallow pills` · `cant swallow pills` · `how to swallow pills` · `pill swallowing tips` · `struggle swallowing pills` · `gagging on pills` · `pill gets stuck in my throat` · `throat closes pills` · `choking on a pill`

**Identity / shame framing:**
`24 and this is how I swallow pills` · `grown adult can't swallow pills` · `still can't swallow pills` · `things I can't do as an adult` · `adult taking childrens medicine` · `childrens tylenol as an adult`

**Adjacent fear:**
`pill anxiety` · `emetophobia` · `fear of choking` · `medical anxiety` · `health anxiety` · `facing my fear pills`

**Workaround / hack:**
`crushing pills in applesauce` · `pill hack` · `cutting pills in half` · `liquid medicine adult` · `chewable tylenol` · `chewable pain reliever`

### 2.3 Hashtags

`#cantswallowpills` · `#pilltok` · `#pillhack` · `#pillanxiety` · `#emetophobia` · `#medtok` · `#healthanxiety` · `#chewable` · `#tylenol` · `#adultproblems` · `#thingsonlyido` · `#storytime` (filter hard) · `#chronicallyill` · `#momtok` (dosing-adjacent)

### 2.4 Ride the algorithm

Set up a clean TikTok account for sourcing. Watch, like, and rewatch pill-struggle content for two or three days. The FYP will start serving this niche continuously — after that, the algorithm does most of Lane 2's work for you. **Do this on day one of Phase 1**; the account needs a few days to train.

## 3. Lanes 3 & 4 — comment-mining and adjacent-SKU poaching

### 3.1 Lane 3 — comment-mining (highest intent, lowest competition)

**Target output: 150 scored creators.**

Under every viral pill-swallow video sit hundreds of comments from women describing the exact problem in their own words. Many are creators themselves. **They have publicly self-identified as Maya, unprompted, on camera or in writing.** No other channel gives you that.

**Method:**
1. Pull the 30 highest-view videos from the §2.2 searches.
2. Sort comments by Most Liked. Read the top 100 on each.
3. Flag every commenter whose comment is a genuine first-person struggle statement (*"I'm 31 and I still crush mine into yogurt"*), not a joke or a tip.
4. Open their profile. Do they create content? Do they have a Shop tab? Enough followers to clear the 5K floor?
5. **The DM writes itself** — you quote their own comment back to them. This lane has the highest reply rate in the program by a wide margin.

Bonus: many will be under 5K followers and won't qualify as creators. **Capture them anyway in a separate "Customer Waitlist" tab.** They are literally your buyers, and they feed the customer→affiliate flywheel at launch (master strategy §5.5).

### 3.2 Lane 4 — adjacent-SKU poaching

**Target output: 150 scored creators.**

Back in Kalodata, search **products**, not creators. Find SKUs that prove a creator can sell *this category to this audience*:

- Gummy vitamins and supplements (any brand)
- Sleep/melatonin gummies
- Equate Soft Chews and any chewable OTC
- Electrolyte and hydration powders
- Menstrual/cramp relief products
- Digestive and gut-health gummies

For each product, pull the **top 50 creators by GMV**. These people have already demonstrated they can move a chewable wellness product on TikTok. Run them through the §1.3 struggle screen — hit rate here is lower (~1 in 8) but the ones who clear it are the strongest names in the roster.

**A note on menstrual/cramp creators:** acetaminophen is genuinely indicated for menstrual pain, the audience is exactly right, and it's a lane where format frustration is already discussed openly. Weight this segment up.

## 3.5 Lane 5 — partner-hashtag mining

*Added 2026-08-17 (Brooks). Free, fast, and it reads competitors' paid rosters straight off the FYP.*

**Target output: 100 scored creators.** Drawn from the same pool as Lane 4 rather than added on top of the 900 target — the two lanes overlap heavily.

### 3.5.1 The pattern

Disclosed paid partnerships get tagged. In practice the tag is `#<brandname>partner`, and the variants `#<brandname>ambassador`, `#<brandname>creator`, `#<brandname>affiliate`. So `#advilpartner` returns every creator Advil has paid; `#wonderbellypartner` returns Wonderbelly's roster. **The pattern generalizes to any brand name** — the bank below is a starting point, not a limit.

### 3.5.2 Why it's worth the VA's hours

- **Someone else's legal team already vetted her.** She cleared a pharma or OTC brand's compliance review. We're an OTC drug; that matters more here than it would for a supplement.
- **She already says the category words on camera** — "pain relief," "headache," "dose." She won't freeze on our script.
- **She discloses properly.** She knows what `#ad` is for. Under §255.1 that's an asset.
- **It reverse-engineers competitor rosters.** Advil, Midol, and Wonderbelly already spent money finding creators who convert in this category.

### 3.5.3 The hashtag bank

**Tier 1 — OTC pain relief (highest value, always conflict-screen):**
`#advilpartner` · `#tylenolpartner` · `#alevepartner` · `#excedrinpartner` · `#midolpartner` · `#motrinpartner` · `#bayerpartner` · `#salonpaspartner` · `#icyhotpartner` · `#biofreezepartner` · `#voltarenpartner` · plus `#advilambassador`, `#tylenolambassador`, `#alevecreator`

**Tier 2 — chewable / better-medicine DTC (closest format cousins):**
`#wonderbellypartner` · `#tumspartner` · `#peptopartner` · `#gasxpartner` · `#dramaminepartner` · `#zarbeespartner` · `#hiyahealthpartner`

> **Wonderbelly is the single best analogue in this bank.** Chewable antacid, DTC, design-forward, built on the same "why does medicine have to be like this" instinct. Their roster is closer to ours than anyone's. Mine it first and mine it thoroughly.

**Tier 3 — gummy vitamins / supplements (proves she can sell a chew):**
`#ollypartner` · `#golipartner` · `#smartypantspartner` · `#vitafusionpartner` · `#naturemadepartner` · `#bloompartner` · `#ritualpartner` · `#seedpartner` · `#arraepartner` · `#beampartner` · `#nutrafolpartner`

**Tier 4 — women's health / cramp / cycle:**
`#midolpartner` · `#corapartner` · `#raelpartner` · `#flovitaminspartner` · `#thinxpartner` · `#lolapartner` · `#somedayspartner`

**Tier 5 — hydration / recovery / sleep:**
`#liquidivpartner` · `#lmntpartner` · `#hydrantpartner` · `#natrolpartner` · `#zzzquilpartner` · `#beamdreampartner`

**Tier 6 — generic disclosure tags.** Useless alone (millions of results). Always pair with a category term:
`#brandpartner pain relief` · `#paidpartnership gummy` · `#ad chewable` · `#sponsored period cramps` · `#gifted supplement` · `#ad can't swallow pills`

### 3.5.4 Method

1. Search the hashtag **twice** — sorted by Top, then by Recent. Recent surfaces the smaller, still-reachable creators.
2. Open the **creator's profile**, not the video.
3. Check for the orange Shop bag tab.
4. **Cross-check the handle in Kalodata for real GMV.** A partner hashtag proves she gets *paid*. It does not prove she *sells*. Gifted partnerships convert nothing.
5. **Run the §1.3 struggle screen.** The hard gate does not bend here, and it bites harder in this lane than any other — these are professional partners, not women who happened to post about their throat closing.
6. **Run the conflict screen (§3.5.5).**
7. Log as `source_lane = 5-Partner`. In `notes`, record **which brand and the date of the partner post** — both are needed for the conflict check and both improve the DM.

Expect a struggle hit rate closer to Lane 4's ~1 in 8 than Lane 3's. **This lane is for quality and compliance safety, not volume.**

### 3.5.5 The conflict screen

A `#advilpartner` is by definition someone who has promoted a competing analgesic. That's fine and often good. What is *not* fine is contacting someone whose exclusivity clause is still running.

| Partner post is on | Age of post | Action |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Competing analgesic (Tier 1) | <6 months | Flag `Conflict-Risk`. Do not contact. Revisit at 6 months |
| Competing analgesic (Tier 1) | 6–12 months | Contact, ask directly in touch 1 |
| Competing analgesic (Tier 1) | >12 months | Clear |
| Tiers 2–5 | Any | Clear — no analgesic exclusivity applies |

**The question, in touch 1:**

> One quick thing before I say anything else — I saw you worked with [brand] a while back. Is there anything still running on your end that would make a pain relief brand awkward? Totally fine either way, I just would rather ask than put you in a weird spot.

Asking this **raises** reply rate. It signals we're a real company that understands contracts, which is exactly what a professional partner is screening us for.

### 3.5.6 Two hard rules for this lane

- **Never ask her to compare us to the brand she partnered with.** Superiority claims over Tylenol are already banned in `03 — Compliance & Never-Say Sheet`, and asking a former partner to make one is how she ends up in breach of someone else's contract. We're the format, not the better molecule.
- **Never open the DM with the partnership.** "I saw you did an Advil ad" makes her feel like a media buy. Open with her struggle like every other lane; the partnership stays in your notes.

## 3.6 Lane 6 — the rabbit-hole method

*From Brooks's Loom SOP (step 7), 2026-08-17. Highest yield per minute of any lane once you have a good seed, and it's free.*

Every other lane is a cold search. This one **compounds**: one confirmed creator becomes ten more, because TikTok already knows who resembles her.

1. Find one creator who clears the struggle gate. She is the **seed**.
2. On her profile, open **suggested accounts** (the caret next to Follow), and separately open **who she follows**.
3. Women who can't swallow pills follow each other. The emetophobia / chronic-illness / medtok communities are tightly interlinked, so TikTok's suggestions are unusually accurate for us.
4. Score every plausible name. Struggle screen applies unchanged.
5. **Each new confirmed creator becomes a new seed.** Continue until suggestions start repeating names already in the sheet — that pocket is mined out.
6. Reseed from a fresh Lane 2 or Lane 3 find, in a different pocket.

**Rules:**
- **Seed only from confirmed creators.** A weak seed produces a list of weak lookalikes.
- **Log the seed handle** in notes. A seed that keeps producing signers is a pocket worth returning to.
- **Watch for drift.** Three or four hops out you're usually in general wellness or momtok. When the content stops mentioning the problem, stop and reseed.
- **The hook is weaker here** — no comment or video to quote. Use the Rabbit Hole variant (Variant E) in the outreach bank, which opens by *asking* her to confirm the struggle rather than assuming it.

## 4. Scoring rubric — 12 points, recruit at 7+

Score every sourced creator. Anything under 7, or any creator with a struggle score of 0, does not get contacted.

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Genuine struggle** ⚠️ | No evidence | Adjacent (health/medical anxiety, medication content) | Has explicitly said she struggles to swallow pills |
| 2 | **Proven Shop GMV** | <$1K/mo or unknown | $1–5K/mo | $5–50K/mo |
| 3 | **Audience match** | <40% female or wrong age | 40–60% female, 18–44 | ≥60% female, 18–44, US |
| 4 | **Engagement rate** | <2% | 2–4% | >4% |
| 5 | **Confessional history** | Polished/branded only | Some personal content | Regular storytime/"me too" content |
| 6 | **Posting consistency** | <4 posts/mo | 4–12 posts/mo | 12+ posts/mo |

**⚠️ Hard gate: dimension 1 must be ≥1. No exceptions.** A creator with 10/12 and a struggle score of 0 is a reject. This is both the conversion mechanic (Recognition is what sells) and the compliance floor (§255.1 requires honest experience — her *problem* story must be true even though her *product* story is an announcement).

**Priority tiers:**

| Tier | Score | Treatment |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **A — Lane A paid** | 10–12, struggle = 2, GMV ≥$5K | Personal DM from Brooks + **$150 flat content fee** + full Founding terms. Cap at 30. |
| **B — Priority** | 8–9 | Personalized DM, Founding terms, follow up twice |
| **C — Volume** | 7 | Templated-but-personalized DM, Founding terms, follow up once |
| **Waitlist** | <7 but struggle = 2 | Customer waitlist — not a creator, but she's a buyer |
| **Launch Wishlist** | Any score, GMV >$50K | Do not contact now. Approach in December with product in hand. |

## 6. Outreach

### 6.1 Sequencing and volume

| Week | New DMs | Follow-ups | Notes |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| Aug 10–16 | 150 | — | Lane A's 30 go out first, hand-written by Brooks |
| Aug 17–23 | 200 | 150 | Push to hit Gate B (25 signed) before preorder opens |
| Aug 24–30 | 150 | 200 | Preorder live — the pitch gets easier, there's a real page to show |
| Aug 31–Sep 13 | 150/wk | 150/wk | |
| Sep 14–27 | 100/wk | 100/wk | Taper new outreach, shift effort to activating signed creators |

**~150 DMs/week is 3–4 hours a day of real human work.** This is the VA role. Do not plan for Brooks to do it — his time goes to Lane A's 30 personal DMs and to closing.

**Three touches maximum, then stop.** Touch 1 (open), touch 2 at +4 days (new angle), touch 3 at +9 days (soft close). A fourth touch is spam and costs you the launch relationship.

### 6.2 The DMs

Every one leads with **her specific struggle, quoted back to her.** Never open with the brand. Paste from `struggle_evidence`.

> ### ⚠️ Before using these: the `[[FOUNDER LINE]]` placeholder
> Each template contains a bracketed slot for why *we* built this. **Fill it with something true.**
> - **If Brooks genuinely struggles to swallow pills**, say that plainly — it is by far the strongest version and it makes every DM land.
> - **If he does not**, use the market-gap version: *"104 million American adults can't swallow pills and the entire industry has spent forty years tweaking pill coatings instead of just fixing it."* That is true, it is interesting, and it converts fine.
>
> **Do not fabricate a founder struggle.** We are about to make forty creators sign a document promising not to claim experiences they haven't had. Opening our own outreach with a fake one is both indefensible and, under §255.1, the same violation we're screening them for.

**Touch 1 — Lane 3 (comment-mined). Highest reply rate.**

> Hey [name] — I saw your comment on [creator]'s video, the one where you said "[paste her exact words]." I've read a few thousand comments like yours over the last year and that one stuck.
>
> `[[FOUNDER LINE]]` So we built the thing that should have existed already: **Orelli — adult-strength Tylenol, in a gummy.** Real 500mg dose, you just chew it. In production now, ships December.
>
> I'm putting together a small group of founding creators, all people who actually live this. Would you want in? No obligation at all — happy to just send you what it is and you can decide.

**Touch 1 — Lanes 1, 2, 4 (proven sellers).** Lead with the struggle, but name the commercial thing early, because these creators evaluate offers for a living.

> Hey [name] — your video about [specific thing she said] is exactly why I'm reaching out.
>
> `[[FOUNDER LINE]]` We made **Orelli: adult-strength Tylenol, in a gummy.** 500mg, real dose, you chew it. Ships December, preorders open [date].
>
> I'm hand-picking ~30 founding creators and I'd love you to be one — you're one of very few people I've found who has both the actual story and a real track record of moving product on TikTok. Founding terms are 20% on everything, locked at that rate for life (it drops to 15% for everyone who joins after launch), $50 when your first video goes up, and you get free product off the very first production run before it goes on sale.
>
> Want me to send the details?

**Touch 1 — Tier A (Brooks writes each one by hand).** Same content, plus a specific, verifiable observation about her work and the $150 content fee. No template.

**Touch 2 (+4 days) — new information, not a nudge.**

> Hey [name], no pressure at all — just wanted to add one thing I should've led with. The reason I'm only working with people who genuinely can't swallow pills is that I don't want anyone pretending. There's no script and no "review" — you'd just tell your own story and mention the thing exists. That's it. Legally I actually can't have you say you've tried it, since nobody has yet.
>
> If it's not for you that's totally fine, I won't bug you again. If you're curious: [link]

**Touch 3 (+9 days) — soft close, leave the door open.**

> Last note from me, promise. Founding spots close [date] — after that it's the standard 15% instead of 20%-for-life. If the timing's just wrong, no worries at all, I'd still love to send you a bottle in December when they ship. Either way, thanks for talking about the pill thing publicly. It's a bigger deal to people like us than you probably realize.

### 6.3 What kills reply rate

- **Opening with the brand.** She doesn't care yet. She cares that someone read her comment.
- **"Hi babe / Hey love / Dear Creator."** Instant delete.
- **Sending a link in touch 1.** Reads as a bot. Offer, don't link.
- **Asking for a call.** Nano creators don't take calls. Close in DM.
- **Overclaiming.** "This will change your life" — no. Understate; the product is genuinely novel and doesn't need help.
- **Any implication she'll get to try it first.** She won't. Be straight about it in touch 1 or you'll lose her at the agreement.

And the day we go live on TikTok Shop in December, you're first in, at 20%, with product already in your hands.

## 7. From signed to posting — the conversion nobody plans for

Roughly 35% of signed creators never post. That's the biggest leak in the funnel and it is almost entirely fixable with logistics.

**Within 1 hour of signing, they receive one message containing all five:**
1. Their unique **code + link** (already generated — never "I'll send it later")
2. The **Creator Asset Pack** download link (§8)
3. The **Brand Bible** (`02`) and the **Compliance Sheet** (`03`)
4. Their **assigned angle** and 3 hook options written for it
5. A specific **post-by date** — "your $50 activation bounty is for a video live by [date, 10 days out]"

**Then:**
- **Day 3:** "Anything blocking you? Want me to write the first 3 lines?"
- **Day 7:** "Reminder — bounty deadline is [date]."
- **Day 10:** if no post, one final offer of help, then move to Ghosted.

**The single biggest unlock: offer to write the script for them.** Half of the non-posters aren't unwilling, they're stuck on what to say about a product they've never held. A ready-made script that only needs their own story dropped into the first eight seconds converts a meaningful share of that 35%.
