ORELLI — TIKTOK SHOP CREATOR SOURCING PLAYBOOK

Created 2026-07-25. The operational how-to for finding, scoring, and recruiting the preorder creator roster. Companion to the Affiliate Program Proposal (Preorder Phase), which sets the terms this playbook recruits against.

Target: 900 sourced → 750 contacted → 85 signed → 55 posting → 35 repeat posters, by October 4.


0. THE CORE INSIGHT — WHY WE SOURCE ON TIKTOK SHOP SPECIFICALLY

Ordinary creator sourcing ranks people by followers. That metric is nearly worthless for us: a 200K-follower lifestyle creator may have never sold anything to anyone.

TikTok Shop's ecosystem exposes something far better — per-creator GMV. Third-party databases index which creators have actually driven purchase behavior, in which categories, at what volume, over what period. That converts creator sourcing from a popularity contest into a performance search.

The bar: we are not looking for creators with an audience. We are looking for creators with a buying audience, who also genuinely cannot swallow pills.

That intersection — proven seller crossed with authentic sufferer — is small, and it is the entire prize. Everything below is a method for finding it.

0.1 The access constraint — read this before opening Seller Center

TikTok Shop's native Affiliate Center creator search and Target Collaboration both require an active product listing. We do not have one: the product isn't shippable until December, and OTC medications additionally require seller category eligibility that hasn't been granted.

So we do not source inside Seller Center during the preorder window. We source through three channels: third-party TikTok Shop databases, which are paid and precise (Lane 1); in-app manual mining, which is free and high-signal (Lanes 2 and 3); and adjacent-SKU creator poaching, which is paid and high-conversion (Lane 4).

All outreach happens off-platform — TikTok DM, Instagram DM, or the email in her bio.

At T-0, when the product is live and listed, Affiliate Center's Manage Creators tool accepts an imported off-platform creator list and batch-invites up to 100 creators at a time. Everything sourced now is built to import cleanly then. Keep the tracker's schema in Section 5 intact for exactly this reason.


1. LANE 1 — DATABASE MINING, THE PRECISION LANE

Tool: Kalodata Professional, roughly $100/month. Largest creator index, deepest per-creator ROI metrics, contact export. Alternatives: FastMoss offers faster data refresh and better creator dashboards; EchoTik runs $9–19/month and is GMV- and brand-deal-oriented with thinner insights, making it a viable budget substitute. Take Kalodata's free 7-day trial first and pull the bulk of the list inside it.

Target output from this lane: 500 scored creators.

1.1 The filter stack — set these exactly

•  Region: United States. Reasoning: shipping plus FDA jurisdiction.
•  Category: Health, Beauty & Personal Care, Food & Beverage, Sports & Outdoors. Health is primary; the other three catch gummy-vitamin and wellness sellers.
•  GMV, last 30 days: $1,000 to $50,000. This is the most important filter — see Section 1.2.
•  Followers: 5,000 to 150,000. Below 5K is unproven; above 150K they ignore a no-sample offer.
•  Average views per video: 3,000 or more. The reach floor.
•  Female audience: 60% or more. Maya is a woman 25–38.
•  Audience aged 18–44: 60% or more.
•  Posted in the last 14 days: yes. Dead accounts waste DMs.
•  Videos in the last 30 days: 8 or more. Consistency predicts repeat posting.

1.2 Why the GMV band is $1K–$50K and not "as high as possible"

This is the judgment call that determines roster quality.

Under $1,000 a month, she hasn't proven she can sell — no better than a follower-count pick.

Between $1,000 and $50,000 a month is the sweet spot: proven conversion ability, still hungry, still answers DMs, and critically, still willing to consider an off-platform deal. Founding Creator status means something to her.

Over $50,000 a month, she is professionalized. She has a manager, a rate card, and a full TikTok Shop pipeline. She will not send traffic off-platform to a preorder for an unlaunched brand, and if she would, the price sits outside this budget.

Pull the over-$50K names into a separate Launch Wishlist tab. Don't burn them now — approach them in December with a live TikTok Shop listing, real reviews, and product in hand. That is a completely different and much easier pitch.

1.3 The second pass — the struggle screen

Database filters get you proven sellers. They cannot tell you who genuinely can't swallow pills. Every Lane 1 name goes through a manual pass.

1. Open the profile. Scan the last 30 videos plus any pinned content.
2. Search her content for the signal: pill struggle, health anxiety, emetophobia, medical storytime, "things I can't do as an adult," medication content of any kind.
3. Check the comment sections of her most-viewed videos for "omg me too" energy — the audience-recognition signal that predicts our conversion mechanic.
4. Score per Section 4. A struggle score of 1 or higher is non-negotiable. A proven seller with $40K GMV who has no relationship to the problem is a reject, not a compromise. Maya smells a script, and the FTC cares whether the story is real.

Expect roughly 1 in 5 database names to clear the struggle screen. That is why the source target is 900 to sign 85.


2. LANE 2 — IN-APP SHOP-SIGNAL MINING, THE HIGHEST-VALUE INTERSECTION

Free, slower, and it produces the best creators in the roster. This lane inverts Lane 1: start from the authentic struggle, then filter for proven seller.

Target output: 200 scored creators.

2.1 Method

1. Search TikTok for the strings in Section 2.2. Sort by both Top and Recent.
2. For each relevant video, open the creator's profile.
3. Look for the orange shopping-bag icon or Shop tab on the profile. That means she is already a TikTok Shop affiliate — she has gone through onboarding, she understands commission, she posts shoppable content. Zero education needed on how affiliate work functions.
4. A creator with the struggle content and the Shop tab is a priority target, top of the outreach queue.
5. Cross-check the handle in Kalodata for actual GMV before contacting. A Shop tab with $0 GMV is just an unused account.

2.2 Search strings — work through all of these

Direct problem: can't swallow pills · cant swallow pills · how to swallow pills · pill swallowing tips · struggle swallowing pills · gagging on pills · pill gets stuck in my throat · throat closes pills · choking on a pill

Identity and shame framing: 24 and this is how I swallow pills · grown adult can't swallow pills · still can't swallow pills · things I can't do as an adult · adult taking childrens medicine · childrens tylenol as an adult

Adjacent fear: pill anxiety · emetophobia · fear of choking · medical anxiety · health anxiety · facing my fear pills

Workaround and hack: crushing pills in applesauce · pill hack · cutting pills in half · liquid medicine adult · chewable tylenol · chewable pain reliever

2.3 Hashtags

#cantswallowpills · #pilltok · #pillhack · #pillanxiety · #emetophobia · #medtok · #healthanxiety · #chewable · #tylenol · #adultproblems · #thingsonlyido · #storytime (filter hard) · #chronicallyill · #momtok (dosing-adjacent)

2.4 Ride the algorithm

Set up a clean TikTok account for sourcing. Watch, like, and rewatch pill-struggle content for two or three days. The For You page will start serving this niche continuously — after that, the algorithm does most of Lane 2's work for you. Do this on day one of Phase 1; the account needs a few days to train.


3. LANES 3 AND 4 — COMMENT-MINING AND ADJACENT-SKU POACHING

3.1 Lane 3 — comment-mining, the highest intent and lowest competition

Target output: 150 scored creators.

Under every viral pill-swallow video sit hundreds of comments from women describing the exact problem in their own words. Many are creators themselves. They have publicly self-identified as Maya, unprompted, on camera or in writing. No other channel gives you that.

Method:
1. Pull the 30 highest-view videos from the Section 2.2 searches.
2. Sort comments by Most Liked. Read the top 100 on each.
3. Flag every commenter whose comment is a genuine first-person struggle statement — "I'm 31 and I still crush mine into yogurt" — rather than a joke or a tip.
4. Open her profile. Does she create content? Does she have a Shop tab? Enough followers to clear the 5K floor?
5. The DM writes itself, because you quote her own comment back to her. This lane has the highest reply rate in the program by a wide margin.

A bonus: many of these women will be under 5K followers and won't qualify as creators. Capture them anyway in a separate Customer Waitlist tab. They are literally our buyers, and they feed the customer-to-affiliate flywheel at launch (master strategy Section 5.5).

3.2 Lane 4 — adjacent-SKU poaching

Target output: 150 scored creators.

Back in Kalodata, search products rather than creators. Find SKUs that prove a creator can sell this category to this audience: gummy vitamins and supplements of any brand · sleep and melatonin gummies · Equate Soft Chews and any chewable OTC · electrolyte and hydration powders · menstrual and cramp relief products · digestive and gut-health gummies.

For each product, pull the top 50 creators by GMV. These people have already demonstrated they can move a chewable wellness product on TikTok. Run them through the struggle screen in Section 1.3. The hit rate here is lower — roughly 1 in 8 — but the ones who clear it are the strongest names in the roster.

A note on menstrual and cramp creators: acetaminophen is genuinely indicated for menstrual pain, the audience is exactly right, and it is a lane where format frustration is already discussed openly. Weight this segment up.


4. SCORING RUBRIC — 12 POINTS, RECRUIT AT 7 OR ABOVE

Score every sourced creator. Anything under 7, or any creator with a struggle score of 0, does not get contacted.

Dimension 1 — Genuine struggle. 0: no evidence. 1: adjacent, meaning health or medical anxiety, or medication content. 2: she has explicitly said she struggles to swallow pills.
Dimension 2 — Proven Shop GMV. 0: under $1K a month or unknown. 1: $1–5K a month. 2: $5–50K a month.
Dimension 3 — Audience match. 0: under 40% female or wrong age. 1: 40–60% female, 18–44. 2: 60%-plus female, 18–44, US.
Dimension 4 — Engagement rate. 0: under 2%. 1: 2–4%. 2: over 4%.
Dimension 5 — Confessional history. 0: polished or branded content only. 1: some personal content. 2: regular storytime or "me too" content.
Dimension 6 — Posting consistency. 0: under 4 posts a month. 1: 4–12 posts a month. 2: 12-plus posts a month.

Hard gate: dimension 1 must be 1 or higher. No exceptions. A creator scoring 10 out of 12 with a struggle score of 0 is a reject. This is both the conversion mechanic — Recognition is what sells — and the compliance floor, since 255.1 requires honest experience. Her problem story must be true even though her product story is an announcement.

Priority tiers:

Tier A, Lane A paid. Score 10–12, struggle 2, GMV $5K or more. Treatment: a personal DM from Brooks, a $150 flat content fee, and full Founding terms. Cap at 30.
Tier B, Priority. Score 8–9. Treatment: personalized DM, Founding terms, follow up twice.
Tier C, Volume. Score 7. Treatment: templated-but-personalized DM, Founding terms, follow up once.
Waitlist. Under 7 but struggle 2. Treatment: customer waitlist — not a creator, but she's a buyer.
Launch Wishlist. Any score, GMV over $50K. Do not contact now. Approach in December with product in hand.


5. THE MASTER TRACKER — BUILD THIS BEFORE SENDING A SINGLE DM

One Airtable base or Notion database. The schema is designed to export cleanly into TikTok Shop's Manage Creators import at T-0, so do not improvise columns.

Identity fields: handle (text, TikTok handle without the @ — this is the import key at T-0) · creator_name (text, real first name if findable, since DMs that use it convert far better) · source_lane (select: 1-Database, 2-InApp, 3-Comment, 4-Adjacent).

Qualification fields: followers (number) · avg_views (number) · engagement_rate (percent) · gmv_30d (currency, from Kalodata; blank means unverified) · has_shop_tab (checkbox, the Lane 2 signal) · struggle_evidence (long text — paste the actual quote or video link; this is required, and it serves as both the DM hook and the compliance record) · score_1 through score_6 (number) · total_score (formula) · tier (select: A, B, C, Waitlist, Launch-Wishlist).

Outreach fields: contact_method (select: TikTok DM, IG DM, Email) · contact_handle (text) · outreach_1_date, outreach_2_date, outreach_3_date (date) · status (select: Sourced, Contacted, Replied, Negotiating, Signed, Posted, Repeat-Poster, Declined, Ghosted, Removed).

Compliance fields: agreement_signed (checkbox — must be true before a code is issued) · compliance_ack (checkbox, signed the never-say sheet) · first_post_reviewed (checkbox — compliance review before promotion or repurposing).

Performance fields: affiliate_code (text) · affiliate_link (url) · first_post_date (date) · first_post_url (url) · post_count (number) · total_views (number) · clicks, code_uses, orders, revenue (number, from the affiliate app) · epc (formula: revenue divided by clicks — the number one recruiting stat) · bounty_paid (checkbox) · bonus_tier_paid (select) · angle_assigned (select: Convenience, Choking Fear, Adult Shame, Untreated Pain) · notes (long text).

Anti-collision rule: one tracker, one owner. A creator must never receive a DM from two lanes. Dedupe by handle on every import.


6. OUTREACH

6.1 Sequencing and volume

Week of August 10–16: 150 new DMs, no follow-ups. Lane A's 30 go out first, hand-written by Brooks.
Week of August 17–23: 200 new DMs, 150 follow-ups. Push to hit Gate B, 25 signed, before the preorder opens.
Week of August 24–30: 150 new DMs, 200 follow-ups. Preorder is live, so the pitch gets easier — there's a real page to show.
Weeks of August 31 through September 13: 150 new DMs and 150 follow-ups per week.
Weeks of September 14–27: 100 new DMs and 100 follow-ups per week. Taper new outreach and shift effort to activating signed creators.

Roughly 150 DMs a week is three to four hours a day of real human work. This is the VA role. Do not plan for Brooks to do it — his time goes to Lane A's 30 personal DMs and to closing.

Three touches maximum, then stop. Touch 1 opens. Touch 2 lands at plus-4 days with a new angle. Touch 3 lands at plus-9 days as a soft close. A fourth touch is spam and costs us the launch relationship.

6.2 The DMs

Every one leads with her specific struggle, quoted back to her. Never open with the brand. Paste from the struggle_evidence field.

BEFORE USING THESE — the FOUNDER LINE placeholder. Each template contains a bracketed slot for why we built this, and it must be filled with something true. If Brooks genuinely struggles to swallow pills, say that plainly — it is by far the strongest version and it makes every DM land. If he does not, use the market-gap version: "104 million American adults can't swallow pills and the entire industry has spent forty years tweaking pill coatings instead of just fixing it." That is true, it is interesting, and it converts fine. Do not fabricate a founder struggle. We are about to make forty creators sign a document promising not to claim experiences they haven't had; opening our own outreach with a fake one is both indefensible and, under 255.1, the same violation we're screening them for.

Touch 1, Lane 3 (comment-mined). The highest reply rate.

"Hey [name] — I saw your comment on [creator]'s video, the one where you said '[paste her exact words].' I've read a few thousand comments like yours over the last year and that one stuck.

[FOUNDER LINE] So we built the thing that should have existed already: Orelli — adult-strength Tylenol, in a gummy. Real 500mg dose, you just chew it. In production now, ships December.

I'm putting together a small group of founding creators, all people who actually live this. Would you want in? No obligation at all — happy to just send you what it is and you can decide."

Touch 1, Lanes 1, 2, and 4 (proven sellers). Lead with the struggle, but name the commercial thing early, because these creators evaluate offers for a living.

"Hey [name] — your video about [specific thing she said] is exactly why I'm reaching out.

[FOUNDER LINE] We made Orelli: adult-strength Tylenol, in a gummy. 500mg, real dose, you chew it. Ships December, preorders open [date].

I'm hand-picking about 30 founding creators and I'd love you to be one — you're one of very few people I've found who has both the actual story and a real track record of moving product on TikTok. Founding terms are 20% on everything, locked at that rate for life (it drops to 15% for everyone who joins after launch), $50 when your first video goes up, and you get free product off the very first production run before it goes on sale.

Want me to send the details?"

Touch 1, Tier A. Brooks writes each one by hand. Same content, plus a specific verifiable observation about her work and the $150 content fee. No template.

Touch 2, plus-4 days. New information, not a nudge.

"Hey [name], no pressure at all — just wanted to add one thing I should've led with. The reason I'm only working with people who genuinely can't swallow pills is that I don't want anyone pretending. There's no script and no 'review' — you'd just tell your own story and mention the thing exists. That's it. Legally I actually can't have you say you've tried it, since nobody has yet.

If it's not for you that's totally fine, I won't bug you again. If you're curious: [link]"

Touch 3, plus-9 days. Soft close, leave the door open.

"Last note from me, promise. Founding spots close [date] — after that it's the standard 15% instead of 20%-for-life. If the timing's just wrong, no worries at all, I'd still love to send you a bottle in December when they ship. Either way, thanks for talking about the pill thing publicly. It's a bigger deal to people like us than you probably realize."

6.3 What kills reply rate

•  Opening with the brand. She doesn't care yet. She cares that someone read her comment.
•  "Hi babe," "Hey love," "Dear Creator." Instant delete.
•  Sending a link in touch 1. Reads as a bot. Offer, don't link.
•  Asking for a call. Nano creators don't take calls. Close in the DM.
•  Overclaiming. "This will change your life" — no. Understate; the product is genuinely novel and doesn't need help.
•  Any implication she'll get to try it first. She won't. Be straight about it in touch 1 or you'll lose her at the agreement.

6.4 The objection you will hear most

"Why would I send my audience off TikTok instead of just linking a Shop product?"

The honest answer, which works: "Totally fair, and you're right that Shop checkout converts better. We can't be on TikTok Shop yet because the product doesn't ship until December and Shop requires 2-day fulfillment. That's exactly why I'm giving founding creators 20% for life instead of the 15% everyone gets later — you're taking on the harder link, so you get the permanently better rate. And the day we go live on TikTok Shop in December, you're first in, at 20%, with product already in your hands."


7. FROM SIGNED TO POSTING — THE CONVERSION NOBODY PLANS FOR

Roughly 35% of signed creators never post. That is the biggest leak in the funnel and it is almost entirely fixable with logistics.

Within one hour of signing, she receives one message containing all five of the following:
1. Her unique code and link, already generated. Never "I'll send it later."
2. The Creator Asset Pack download link.
3. The Brand Bible and the Compliance Sheet.
4. Her assigned angle and three hook options written for it.
5. A specific post-by date: "your $50 activation bounty is for a video live by [date, 10 days out]."

Then: on day 3, "Anything blocking you? Want me to write the first 3 lines?" On day 7, "Reminder — bounty deadline is [date]." On day 10, if no post, one final offer of help, then move her to Ghosted.

The single biggest unlock is offering to write the script for her. Half of the non-posters aren't unwilling, they're stuck on what to say about a product they've never held. A ready-made script that only needs her own story dropped into the first eight seconds converts a meaningful share of that 35%.

9. WEEKLY SOURCING RHYTHM

Monday: Kalodata pull — 100 new names, filtered and exported. Dedupe against the tracker.
Tuesday: struggle-screen the Monday pull. Score. Assign tiers.
Wednesday: Lane 2 in-app mining for two hours, plus Lane 3 comment-mining for two hours. Score.
Thursday: send the week's new DMs.
Friday: follow-ups, touches 2 and 3. Onboard everyone who signed. Update the weekly scorecard.
Ongoing: reply to inbound DMs within four hours. Reply speed is the strongest predictor of close rate at this tier.


10. THE MIGRATION PLAN — WHAT ALL THIS BECOMES AT T-0

Everything here is scaffolding for December.

1. November: confirm TikTok Shop seller eligibility for OTC medications, started back in Phase 0. This is the gating dependency.
2. Early December: product listed on TikTok Shop with Drug Facts–consistent copy.
3. December: export the tracker, import into Affiliate Center under Manage Creators, and batch-invite in blocks of 100. Every one of these creators already knows the brand, has signed compliance, and is holding free product from the first run.
4. December: open collaboration goes live at 15% standard, with Founding Creators tagged at their locked 20%.
5. December: the first-bite content wave fires. The taste reaction that was legally off-limits all through the preorder is now the launch's hero asset, arriving from 55 creators simultaneously.

That last point is the strategic payoff of the whole constraint. Because nobody could talk about taste in August, nobody has spent it. It arrives all at once, in December, from a roster that is already warm.
