# Orelli — Creator Compliance & Never-Say Sheet

*Creator-facing. This is not an attachment — it is a **signed** part of the Founding Creator agreement. No affiliate code is issued until it's acknowledged.*

---

## Read this part first

Orelli is not a supplement or a wellness product. **It is an over-the-counter drug**, regulated by the FDA. The rules for talking about it are stricter than anything you've done for a skincare or supplement brand, and the penalties are real — not "the brand gets a warning email" real. **Fines for fake or unsubstantiated testimonials run up to $51,744 per violation**, and they can land on both the brand and the creator.

None of this makes your video harder to make. It mostly just means **your video is about you, and the product gets the last eight seconds.** Which is the version that performs better anyway.

Three rules. That's it.

---

## Rule 1 — You have not tried it, so you cannot review it

**This is the big one.**

Federal law (16 CFR §255.1) says:

> *"Endorsements must reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience of the endorser."*
>
> *"When the advertisement represents that the endorser uses the endorsed product, the endorser must have been a bona fide user of it at the time the endorsement was given."*

Orelli does not ship until December. **Nobody on earth has tried it yet — including us.** So no video, caption, comment, or story can say or imply that you have.

**You are making an announcement, not a review.**

| ✅ Completely fine — all true | ❌ Never — creates real liability |
|:--|:--|
| "I can't swallow pills." | "I tried it." / "I've been using this." |
| "I've been buying children's medicine for years." | "It tastes like candy." / "It tastes great." |
| "Someone finally made adult Tylenol as a gummy." | "It actually works." / "It killed my headache." |
| "It's 500mg — same as Extra Strength Tylenol." | "This is my new go-to." |
| "I preordered it." **(only if you actually did)** | "It's so much better than Tylenol." |
| "It ships in December and I'm counting down." | Filming yourself eating or holding a bottle |
| "It's a chewable, so no water needed." | "My doctor recommended it." |

**If someone asks in the comments "does it taste good?"** — the honest answer, and the only allowed one:

> "No idea yet honestly, it doesn't ship till December. I preordered because I finally found something I can actually take."

That answer performs *better* than a fake one. It reads as real, because it is.

**Also: don't fake having the product.** No holding a bottle, no eating a gummy, no unboxing, no "look what came in the mail." Use the product images from the Asset Pack, edited into your video. Everyone understands what a screenshot is.

## Rule 2 — Disclose, every single time

You earn commission, so **there is a material connection** and it has to be disclosed. This is true even though we're not sending you free product.

**Do all three:**

1. **In the caption** — `#ad` or `#affiliate`, in the **first line**, before the "…more" cutoff. Not buried at the end of a hashtag pile.
2. **On screen** — a visible text overlay somewhere in the first few seconds. "#ad" is enough.
3. **Use TikTok's built-in toggle** — mark the post as a paid promotion / branded content in the posting flow.

**Not acceptable:** "sp", "collab", "thanks Orelli", "partner", disclosure only in a comment, or disclosure that requires tapping to see.

Say it out loud if it fits your style — "I'm working with them, obviously" — it costs nothing and audiences respond well to it.

## Rule 3 — Only say what the label says

Orelli's Drug Facts label allows exactly two claims:

> ### ✅ **Temporarily relieves minor aches and pains.**
> ### ✅ **Temporarily reduces fever.**

That's the complete list. You can describe the **format** freely — chewable, no water, adult 500mg dose, tastes like a gummy *(as a description of what it is, not a review of how it tastes)*.

**Never say — no exceptions:**

| ❌ Don't | Why |
|:--|:--|
| "Cures" / "heals" / "treats" anything | Beyond the label |
| "Works faster / better / stronger than Tylenol" | It's the identical molecule. Our angle is **format**, not potency. |
| "Safe for pregnancy" or any pregnancy claim | Requires clinical substantiation we don't have |
| "Safe for kids" / any child dosing | This is a 500mg **adult** dose |
| Any dosing instruction beyond the label | Dosing is the label's job, not yours |
| "Take it with [other medication]" | Interaction advice = practicing medicine |
| "Helps with anxiety / sleep / hangovers / cramps as a cure" | Off-label. *"I get period cramps"* is fine as your story; *"it treats cramps"* is not. |
| "Doctor recommended" / "clinically proven" | Not substantiated |
| "All natural" / "chemical-free" | It's acetaminophen |
| Comparing to a competitor by name, negatively | Legal exposure |

**Saying "Tylenol" is fine and encouraged** — as a factual comparison of the active ingredient. *"It's the same active ingredient as Tylenol"* is true and legal. *"It's better than Tylenol"* is not.

## Rule 4 — Preorder terms must be clear

People are paying $29.99+ for something that arrives in months. Federal rules (16 CFR 435) require the timing to be unmistakable at the point of sale — and we need it in your content too.

**Every video must include, on screen or spoken:**

> **"Ships December 2026."**

**Every caption must include:**

> **"Preorder — ships December 2026. Cancel anytime before shipping for a full refund."**

Never say "get yours now," "in stock," "selling out," "shipping fast," or anything implying immediate delivery. **"Preorder" or "preordering" should appear in the caption.**

Scarcity is fine if it's true — *"founding price ends Friday"* is fine when it does. Made-up urgency is not.

## What happens if a post isn't compliant

Not a punishment system — it's that non-compliant posts create legal exposure for both of us.

1. **We'll message you** and ask for a specific fix (usually adding a caption line — 30 seconds of work).
2. **Fix it and everything continues normally.** This is what happens ~95% of the time. Genuinely, nobody gets in trouble for a missing hashtag they then add.
3. **If it can't be fixed** (e.g. the video says you tried it), we'll ask you to take it down. Commission on that post is withheld.
4. **Repeat issues after being asked** end the partnership.

**Your first post gets reviewed before we promote or repost it.** Send it to us when it's up. Fast turnaround — usually within a couple of hours.
