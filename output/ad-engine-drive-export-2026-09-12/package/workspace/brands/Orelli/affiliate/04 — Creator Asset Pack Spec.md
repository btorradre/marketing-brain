# Orelli — Creator Asset Pack Spec

*Internal build spec. Produced in Phase 0 (Jul 28 – Aug 9). **Gate A does not pass without it.** The pack is what makes a no-sample program possible at all.*

---

## Why this pack is load-bearing

No creator will ever hold the product before they post. Every product visual in every video comes from this pack. If the pack is thin or ugly, forty creators will each improvise their own bad version and the brand will look different in every single video.

**Design target: a creator with basic CapCut skills makes something that looks intentional in under ten minutes.**

Two rules govern everything below:

1. **Everything must be dead simple to drop into CapCut.** Transparent PNGs, vertical video, no layered files, no fonts to install.
2. **Nothing may imply the creator possesses the product.** No hands holding a bottle, no kitchen counters, no "just arrived" staging. Product visuals read as *a picture of a thing that exists*, not *a thing in her house.* This is the §255.1 line and the asset pack is where it's easiest to accidentally cross it.

**Existing source material in the vault:** `bottle_crop.png` (from the pill-swallow UGC set), the website screenshot, the children's-Tylenol and Extra-Strength-Tylenol reference images, and the PolySans brand family. Most of the pack is a production pass on these, not a from-scratch build.

## 2. The comparison shot — highest-value asset in the pack

| # | Asset | Spec | Purpose |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 2.1 | **Children's Tylenol bottle beside Orelli bottle, 9:16** | 1080×1920, clean neutral background | The Adult Shame angle's single strongest visual |
| 2.2 | **Same, transparent PNG pair** | alpha | For creators who want it over their own footage |
| 2.3 | **Extra Strength Tylenol beside Orelli, 9:16** | 1080×1920 | The "same 500mg, different format" beat |

**⚠️ Legal caution on 2.1 and 2.3:** competitor packaging appears here. Permitted as **factual comparison of active ingredient and dose** — this is standard comparative advertising and it is the entire point of the brand. It becomes a problem if paired with a disparaging claim. Pair only with factual overlays like *"Same active ingredient. 500mg."* Never *"Tylenol can't do this"* or anything mocking. **Route these two through legal review with the preorder terms.**

## 4. Typography and text overlays

Delivered as **transparent PNGs**, pre-typed in PolySans, brand cobalt on white and white on cobalt. Creators drag them in — no font installs, no brand drift.

**Hook overlays (one PNG each):**
- "it's literally just tylenol. in a gummy."
- "500mg. same as extra strength."
- "you just chew it."
- "no water. no gagging. no pill."
- "you were never the problem. the pill was."
- "adult dose. gummy format."
- "[age] years old and this is how i swallow pills" *(editable-number variant supplied as two PNGs + a numeral set)*

**Required-disclosure overlays — supplied so nobody has an excuse:**
- "#ad"
- "Ships December 2026"
- "Preorder · ships December 2026"

**Also ship a CapCut-ready text style card** — hex codes, font weights, positioning — for creators who prefer to type their own.

## 6. The Brooks Loom — do not skip this

| # | Asset | Spec |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 6.1 | **Founder video message** | 45–75s, unlisted Loom or MP4, shot on a phone |

Brooks to camera, no script, covering:
- What the product is, in plain language
- Why it exists — the 104 million adults, forty years of coating tweaks and nobody fixing it *(plus his own story **only if it's real** — see the Brand Bible §2 note)*
- Why she specifically was picked
- "You can't say you tried it — nobody has. Just tell your story."
- "DM me directly, any time, I actually answer"

**This single asset does more for activation than every PDF combined.** It converts "a brand emailed me a deck" into "a person asked me to help build something." Deliberately rough — phone camera, real room. A polished corporate video destroys the effect entirely.

## 8. Delivery

**One Google Drive folder, one shareable link, this structure:**

```
Orelli Founding Creator Kit/
├── 📄 START HERE — [Creator Name].pdf     ← her one-pager (7.5)
├── 🎥 Message from Brooks.mp4             ← the Loom (6.1)
├── 📄 Brand Bible.pdf
├── 📄 Compliance Sheet.pdf  (sign & return)
├── 📄 Scripts — pick one.pdf
├── 01 PRODUCT IMAGES/          (transparent PNGs, ready to drop in)
├── 02 SCREEN RECORDINGS/       (MP4, vertical)
├── 03 TEXT OVERLAYS/           (transparent PNGs)
├── 04 COMPARISON SHOTS/
└── 05 LOGO & END CARD/
```

**Rules:**
- Folder link goes out **within one hour of signing.** Not the next day.
- **No ZIPs.** Half of creators work from a phone and can't unzip.
- File names describe contents in plain English — `bottle-front-transparent.png`, not `ORE_PKG_v3_FINAL.png`.
- Never require a login or a form to access.
