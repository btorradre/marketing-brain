Craftsmanship

VALUE IN THE MAKING

The character of the Vivienne begins in the leather.

From the first cut to the final detail, explore the elements that give the bag its shape, texture and quiet presence.

  1. Day 1

    Leather Selection & Cutting

    The leather sets the character of the bag. On the Chocolate Vivienne, a richly grained body meets smoother, warmer trim. Selection and cutting establish that balance: the movement of the leather, the shape of each panel, and the soft silhouette that follows.

  2. Day 4–8

    Thread Selection & Stitching

    Thread and stitching bring the separate elements together. Follow the seams across the front band and the braided detail along the upper edge. Their rhythm gives the Vivienne a distinctive outline, adding definition while leaving room for the leather’s natural folds.

  3. Finishing

    Edge Paint & Finishing

    Look closely at the edges of the handles, trim and corners. The finish traces each curve and gives the darker body and warmer accents their definition. These small details are part of the bag’s character, visible wherever one surface meets another.

  4. Final review

    Final Quality Inspection

    The final review brings the details back into view: the silhouette, the alignment of the front band, the braided edge and the warm gold fittings. Each element should sit naturally with the next, so the complete bag feels balanced and considered.
