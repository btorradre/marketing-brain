# Velantra Google Ads image assets

This folder contains source references from Velantra's product catalog and generated Performance Max creative exports.

Upload-ready files are in `exports/`:

- 4 landscape images at 1200 x 628
- 4 square images at 1200 x 1200
- 2 portrait images at 960 x 1200
- 1 square logo at 1200 x 1200
- 1 horizontal logo at 1200 x 300

The campaign images were generated from Velantra's own Eleanor, Meridian, and Delphine product photography. The two logos are deterministic exports of the existing Velantra text wordmark using the site's New York typeface.
