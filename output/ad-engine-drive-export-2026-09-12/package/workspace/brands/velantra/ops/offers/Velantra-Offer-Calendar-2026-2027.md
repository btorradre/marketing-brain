# Velantra offer calendar · September 2026–August 2027

**Recommendation:** Run a focused fall sale, make Black Friday the strongest broad bag offer in this plan, and use styling gifts, bundles and customer events between seasonal sales. Lead every event with the look and the outfit the customer wants; use the incentive to make that purchase easier.

This is a proposed U.S./USD calendar, prepared September 11, 2026. Dates, discounts, eligibility and thresholds are planning choices, not approved or live promotions. Landed costs, margins, customer acquisition costs, active automatic/code discounts, inventory and fulfillment capacity have not been supplied. No promotions or campaigns have been activated.

## How to use the calendar

- Each month has one recommended event and two alternatives. **Choose one route; do not run all three.** May includes a separately labeled optional Memorial Day event.
- Most event windows last four to seven days. Between them, return to the normal offer and focus on styling, materials, products and customer stories. Avoid rolling countdowns and perpetual sale extensions.
- Proposed percentage discounts apply to the **actual selling price immediately before the event**, not the crossed-out comparison price. Reconfirm that price at launch. Do not raise prices to create a larger advertised discount.
- “Eligible bags” means the explicitly selected SKU/color list that passes cost and stock checks. Preorders, unavailable variants, gift cards and already packaged promotional bundles are excluded unless expressly included. Use “selected bags” in customer copy when exclusions apply.
- One promotional offer per order; exclude stacking with welcome, cart-recovery and other codes. Free shipping follows the existing store policy. Gifts are one per qualifying order, selected from a stated list, with real stock reserved.
- Thresholds are calculated after discounts, before tax and shipping; free gifts do not count toward thresholds. Any exceptions must be written into that event's terms.

## Fall sale execution brief

**Proposed window:** September 24 at 9 a.m. through September 28 at 11:59 p.m., America/Chicago. Five calendar days. Customer messaging must name the timezone. Dates are original planning choices.

**Primary offer:** 15% off a defined list of bags with confirmed availability and acceptable contribution. Preserve explicit dispatch information for any deliberately included preorder. Do not make an unavailable Colette/Vivienne the sale's shoppable hero simply because its aesthetic fits autumn.

**Customer message:** “The Fall Sale. Selected bags, 15% off.” The supporting story is warm colors, textured knits and the bag that completes the outfit. Avoid inventory distress as the main creative argument.

**Preparation:** By September 17, choose the eligible variants, actual baseline prices, margin floor and fallback offer. By September 21, verify stock, discount behavior, shipping threshold behavior and terms. Before any ad asset production, write the concept's editing plan under the workspace SOP; the offer calendar is not a substitute for that plan.

**Communications:** September 22 styling preview; September 24 launch; September 26 product/fit guidance; September 28 truthful final-day reminder. Site, paid ads and email must describe the same offer and end time. SMS only to the appropriate opted-in audience if that channel is in use. Resume normal pricing September 29; do not auto-extend.

**Learning objective:** Establish contribution per visitor and conversion under a 15% incentive. Do not infer that this proves a different November offer will work. Keep product, audience, traffic and promotion changes in the readout.

## Black Friday / Cyber Monday plan

Black Friday is **November 27, 2026**; Cyber Monday is **November 30, 2026**. [Black Friday calendar](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/us/black-friday), [Cyber Monday calendar](https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/us/cyber-monday).

### Recommended offer and rules

**25% off eligible bags + a complimentary Bag Scarf on a discounted eligible bag subtotal of $200+.** One scarf per qualifying order. No accessory discount unless specified. No stacking. Gift selection and stock limit disclosed. A consistent offer runs through VIP access and Cyber Monday; VIP's benefit is earlier access.

The $200 threshold is a proposal designed to encourage a two-bag order at the inspected price points. It must be checked against actual basket distribution and inventory. Qualifying pairs need to be achievable without adding unwanted products. Do not describe this as a verified optimal threshold.

At the inspected prices, Vivienne ($149.99) + Colette ($119.99) would total $269.98 before the promotion and **$202.49 after 25% off**, qualifying for the scarf. This is arithmetic for planning, not evidence either bag will be available. A single $149.99 bag would become **$112.49**. Prices assume ordinary cent rounding; validate actual cart rounding before launch.

If 25% fails the contribution requirement, replace the primary offer with the better-performing viable alternative. Do not assume a gift is automatically cheaper: use its landed cost, extra fulfillment/shipping and any effect on returns. For example, two equally priced bags with 30% off the cheaper bag produce a **15% order discount**, not 30% off the entire order. State that distinction clearly.

## Offer mechanics library

| Mechanic | Best use | Concrete example | What determines whether to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selected-product discount | Fall/spring acquisition events | 15% off selected bags | Product contribution and conversion response |
| Broad eligible-bag discount | Black Friday | 25% off the eligible collection | Strongest cost, stock and fulfillment check |
| Gift with purchase | New season, gifting, customer events | Choose a scarf or charm with one bag | Visible desirability, actual gift cost, reserved quantity |
| Styled set | Increase useful basket size | 15% off a bag + scarf set | Discount must beat the current separate total; choose genuinely complementary pieces |
| Spend-and-save | Encourage a second item | $25 off an eligible $225+ order | Threshold is tested against real baskets and does not create awkward near-misses |
| Second-bag incentive | Collection building or paired gifts | 25% off the lower-priced of two bags | Exact eligible pair logic and allocated refund amounts |
| Archive edit | Clear genuinely retiring colors | 20% off selected archive bags | Actual excess inventory; distinguish from current bestsellers |
| Early access | Confirmed launches/restocks | 48-hour customer preview at normal prices | Real new availability; no invented scarcity |
| Returning-customer offer | Repeat purchase | Complimentary scarf on a customer's next eligible bag | Segment accuracy; exclude recent buyers where repeated pressure is unhelpful |
| Accessory add-on | Small AOV lift | One selected accessory at 30% off with a bag | Explain the discount applies to the accessory, not the bag |

A store-credit offer is a possible later test, not part of the recommended first calendar: for example, spend $200 and receive a $20 credit toward a later $100+ bag order. Define issuance after the return period, eligibility, redemption window and accounting before considering it. It adds operational complexity that the scarf/bundle offers avoid.

## Economics and measurement

For each selected SKU and likely basket, calculate: **net merchandise revenue after discount + shipping collected − landed product and gift costs − payment fees − pick/pack and packaging − outbound shipping − expected return/cancellation cost − attributable acquisition cost.** Compare the result to the contribution floor set by the business. Use actual inputs; no margins or revenue forecasts are invented here.

Evaluate offers using contribution per visitor and contribution per order, supported by conversion rate, average order value, units per order, new/repeat mix, gift redemption, cancellations, returns and on-time dispatch. Higher revenue or AOV alone does not establish a better offer. Where feasible, compare equivalent randomized audiences exposed to one offer each; otherwise label month-to-month comparisons observational and account for seasonality, traffic and product availability.

**Before launch, settle these commercial details:**

1. Actual current offer and automatic/code stacking behavior; confirm the baseline against live checkout.
2. Per-SKU landed cost, acquisition allowance and required contribution floor.
3. Eligible SKU/color list, real available quantities and incoming-stock confidence; reserve promotional gifts.
4. Dispatch and delivery estimates by product/destination. Event end dates are not shipping cutoffs.
5. Exact start/end times, audience, minimum-spend basis, gift choice and limits, exclusions, and treatment of partial returns/gifts under the stated policy.
6. Checkout checks for one bag, two bags, threshold just below/at/above, excluded items, welcome-code combinations, refunds and gift removal.

These are inputs needed to finalize and execute an offer, not a request to pause planning. User selection and the cost/stock check should convert the chosen rows from proposed to ready before production begins.
