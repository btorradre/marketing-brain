# Handbag Shot Language — Pinterest Study (2026-08-01)

Study for the Colette + Margot B-roll library. ~270 pins reviewed across 12 searches (work totes, quiet-luxury structured bags, what's-in-my-bag, packing, cafe still life, car seat, fall totes, wool totes, close-ups, street carry). Contact sheets live in the session scratchpad under `pinterest-study/`.

## The 10 shot families Pinterest actually uses for bags like ours

1. **Face-cropped carry.** The dominant convention. Human present but face never in frame: chest-to-knee crop from the front, shoulder-down crop from the side, or full back view. The outfit and the bag are the whole story. Crook-of-elbow at thigh height for structured bags, shoulder carry for totes.
2. **Back-view walking.** Mid-stride on a sidewalk or crosswalk, bag on the shoulder or in hand, shot from behind at street level. Slightly crooked framing, other pedestrians blurred far away.
3. **Mirror check.** Bedroom or hallway mirror, phone covering the face, bag on shoulder or forearm. Lived-in room visible around the mirror.
4. **Cafe still life.** Bag sitting on a cane bistro chair or beside an espresso cup on a small marble table. Croissant, sunglasses, or lipstick tossed next to it. Window light with real shadows.
5. **Car seat portrait.** Bag standing upright on the passenger seat, seat belt and door visible, golden side light through the glass. Common props: iced latte in the cup holder, flowers, a laptop.
6. **What's-in-my-bag flat lay.** Top-down on a wrinkled duvet or carpet: bag at the top of frame, contents in a loose grid below it. Wallet, phone, AirPods, keys, lip balm, planner, glasses, hand cream, scrunchie.
7. **Open-mouth overhead.** Camera looks down INTO the standing open bag, contents nested inside (laptop against the back wall, notebook, pouch, keys). Shot at 45-70 degrees, often on a bed, lap, or car seat.
8. **Packing action.** One hand mid-motion lowering a laptop / book / pouch into the open bag on a bed or desk. The reach reads instantly. ASMR-adjacent.
9. **Set-down moments.** Bag on park steps, on a wooden bench, leaning against an entryway wall with a magazine sticking out, hanging on a hook or chair back. Life happening around a resting bag.
10. **Macro details.** Hardware clasp, stitching, handle wrap, grain texture in raking window light, very shallow focus, fingers sometimes entering frame with rings visible.

## Light and surface doctrine (what makes them read "real")

- One window or the sky is the only light source. Uneven exposure, blown highlights near the glass, deep soft shadows. Golden hour side light for car and entryway shots.
- Surfaces are lived-in: wrinkled duvets, marble with crumbs, scuffed sidewalks, worn wood benches, car leather with dust in the seams.
- Nothing is centered perfectly. Horizon lines tilt a degree or two. Foreground objects clip the frame edge.
- Real clutter: coffee cups, keys, sunglasses, receipts, a jacket tossed behind the bag. Never a sterile set.
- Fall styling for the Colette: camel coats, chunky knits, leather boots, fallen leaves, grey overcast sidewalks, warm cafe interiors.
- Commute styling for the Margot: blazers, trousers, office doors, desks with monitors, elevator lobbies, morning light.

## Standing rules applied on top (house laws)

- Every prompt carries the anti-CGI preamble + footer (see the pipeline script). Weak "iPhone photo" wording is retired.
- Face-cropped or back-view humans only in this library; when skin is visible: natural texture, no retouching.
- No on-screen text, no other brands' bags or logos anywhere in frame.
- Margot: belts always hang open in their V, silver hardware only, no shoulder strap, never a flap. Colette: open top, belt ends curve outward with gold disc caps, no logos.
- One colorway per generated frame, never a multi-color lineup (colorway-drift law).
- 9:16 vertical, always.
