## ⚠️ Usage law — PLACELESS by design (origin-claims guardrail)

Velantra can never claim or imply where its bags are made. These making-of clips are deliberately placeless: tight crops on hands/tools/material, dark neutral backgrounds, no windows, no faces, no location cues. Keep them that way:
- Cut them against **attribute VO lines** ("Reinforced stitching." / "Straw woven by hand." / "Materials that earn character") — never against place lines ("Founded in Nantucket", coastal imagery VO).
- Never grade/composite them into a coastal or American-workshop context, never add location supers over them, never pair with artisan-origin copy.
