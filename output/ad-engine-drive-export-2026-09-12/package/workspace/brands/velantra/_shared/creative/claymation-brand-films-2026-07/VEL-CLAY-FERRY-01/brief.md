# VEL-CLAY-FERRY-01 — "The Ferry" (Straw Tote)

**Product:** Velantra Straw Tote, caramel. **Format:** claymation brand film, 9:16, ~20s + endcard.
**Stage-5 job:** character-driven charm. The heroine shares her croissant with a cheeky gull on the ferry home. No claims, no demo, no CTA — the tote is her constant companion, never the argument. This film births the campaign mascot (the gull).
**Golden thread:** generosity is chic. The brand feeling = a small kindness on golden water.
**Pipeline:** 4 GPT Image 2 keyframes (refs: `caramel 1.png` + `heroine.png`) → 4 × 5s Seedance chain segments → stitch + shared endcard. Est. ~820cr video.

## Keyframes (scene text; locked STYLE / PRODUCT-NOT-CLAY / TOTE IDENTITY / HEROINE blocks appended by orchestrator)

**K1** Golden afternoon on the open deck of a miniature clay coastal ferry. The clay heroine sits on a white slatted bench mid frame, the straw tote resting on the bench beside her, a golden brown clay croissant peeking out of the top of the tote. Her hair strands and dress hem lift in the wind. Behind the railing, a sculpted felt ocean with tiny white clay wave caps and a distant terracotta roofed harbor town. The clay seagull is landing on the deck railing at the right of frame, wings spread mid flap, eyes locked on the croissant.

**K2** Close two shot standoff: on the left of frame the clay heroine leans slightly over the straw tote on the bench, one eyebrow raised, side eyeing the clay seagull. On the right of frame the seagull perches on the bench armrest, head tilted almost upside down, staring at the golden clay croissant sticking out of the tote between them. The felt ocean soft and out of focus behind them.

**K3** She holds a torn piece of the golden clay croissant out toward the seagull in her open palm, a warm small smile on her face, the straw tote upright on the bench between them with the rest of the croissant tucked inside. The seagull leans forward from the armrest, beak almost touching her palm, one wing lifted in excitement.

**K4** Wide shot from behind the bench: the clay heroine and the clay seagull sit side by side facing the sea, the seagull now perched on her shoulder, the straw tote on the bench beside her glowing in low golden hour light. Ahead of the ferry a tiny clay lighthouse with a warm glowing tip stands on a rocky point, the felt ocean sparkling with sequin glints.

## Segments (motion text; MOTION + FOOTER blocks appended)

**S1 (5s, from K1)** The ferry rocks gently, the felt ocean waves slide past in looping stop motion, her hair strands and dress hem flutter in little jumps. The seagull finishes its landing on the railing, folds its wings in two jerky steps, and swivels its head toward the croissant peeking out of the straw tote. She has not noticed it yet, she gazes at the horizon. Ambient sound: low ferry engine hum, wind, water lapping, one distant ferry horn.

**S2 (5s, from K2)** A silent comic standoff in stop motion: the seagull tilts its head further in three little clicks, she narrows her eyes and slowly slides one protective hand toward the croissant in the tote, the seagull hops one step closer on the armrest, freeze, they stare at each other. The straw tote does not move. Ambient sound: wind, creaking bench wood, one soft questioning seagull chirp.

**S3 (5s, from K3)** She offers the torn piece of croissant in stop motion, the seagull takes it from her palm in one gentle careful peck, tosses its head back and swallows it whole with a happy little body wiggle, then does a tiny stomping dance of joy on the armrest. She laughs silently, shoulders bouncing. Ambient sound: wind, water, one delighted seagull squeak, the soft clay tap of the little dance.

**S4 (5s, from K4)** The ferry glides slowly toward the lighthouse in stop motion, sequin glints on the felt ocean shimmer frame by frame, her hair and dress flutter, the seagull on her shoulder ruffles its feathers once and settles, she leans her head slightly toward it. The straw tote stays perfectly still on the bench. Ambient sound: wind softening, water lapping, one far away ferry horn, gulls in the distance.
