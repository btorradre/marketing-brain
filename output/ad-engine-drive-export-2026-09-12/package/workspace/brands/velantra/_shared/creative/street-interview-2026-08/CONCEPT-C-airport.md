# CONCEPT C — Airport
### Velantra Eleanor Weekender · 28 seconds · 9:16 · five versions

Burned on screen for the first 3 seconds: **"How long are you going for?"**

Same street-interview format, moved into an airport terminal. Someone stops a traveller at the gate and asks how long her trip is. She says four days, and the joke lands immediately: she's holding one bag. The rest is her proving it.

The question does all the work. "How long are you going for?" sets up an expectation of luggage, and the answer is a single bag on her forearm.

---

## The workflow

**Step 1.** Generate her starting frame with GPT Image 2. One frame per woman. Three variants, pick the best.
**Step 2.** Animate that frame with Seedance 2.5 as the first frame. One pass, whole ad, audio included.

Same five women as the Colette concepts, different outfit and location, so these are new frames.

## The script

28 seconds · 6 shots · 5 cuts. **Beats 1 to 5 are identical for all five women.** Beat 6 has two versions — see below. Every line is sized to its beat. Never add words to a beat and never stretch a beat to fit a line.

| Beat | Window | Line | Doing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 00:00–04 | "Four days. And no, I didn't check a bag." | The premise and the joke, in one breath |
| **2** | 00:04–09 | "This is it, this is everything, I'm not even joking." | The reveal — one bag |
| **3** | 00:09–14 | "It's the Eleanor Weekender, it's from Velantra, and it goes right in the overhead." | Name, brand, the practical promise |
| **4** | 00:14–19 | "Three days of clothes, my shoes, my laptop, all in there." | Capacity, itemised |
| **5** | 00:19–23 | "And it still stands up like that when it's full." | Structure — the thing soft duffels can't do |
| **6** | 00:23–28 | **two versions, pick by colorway →** | Close + CTA |

### ⚠️ Beat 6 depends on the colorway. Get this right.

The Weekender is **not** uniformly on pre-order. The live product page says: *"Black is a pre-order. It is all leather with no canvas, made to order in the first run, and expected to ship mid September 2026. Light Chocolate, Army Green and Dark Chocolate are in stock and ship now."*

So a blanket pre-order line would be false in four of the five versions.

| Version | Colorway | Beat 6 line |
|---|---|---|
| **Nora, Priya, Iris, June** | Light Chocolate · Army Green · Dark Chocolate | "I'm never checking a bag again. They ship these out now, get yours below." |
| **Talia** | Black | "I'm never checking a bag again. This one's on pre-order, get yours below." |

Re-check the product page before you generate. If Black sells through its first run or the other colorways go to pre-order, these lines swap.

**Transcribe every pass and check "Velantra."** It has come back as "Volantra" before. If it mispronounces, respell it phonetically in the beat 3 line and regenerate.

## Non-negotiables

- The only brand named anywhere is Velantra. No airline, no luggage brand, nothing else.
- No competitor is named, implied or criticised. Beat 6 is about her own decision, never about anything being worse.
- She never speaks as the brand. "It's from Velantra". Never "our", never "we".
- The interviewer never speaks and is never seen. Only a forearm and a mic at the right edge of frame.
- The question is a burned title card in post, first 3 seconds only. It is never spoken audio.
- **The Weekender is hand or forearm carry only.** The short rolled handles physically cannot reach a shoulder, and if you stage one the model invents a long buckled strap to bridge the impossible pose.
- **Scale is the failure mode on this product.** It renders as a handbag unless you fight it, and dimensions alone never fix it — every beat needs a check against her body. Those are written into the FRAME lines. Don't drop them.
- Seedance cannot spell "Weekender". The name is only ever spoken, never rendered on screen.
- **Only the Black colorway is on pre-order.** Light Chocolate, Army Green and Dark Chocolate are in stock and ship now. The beat 6 CTA is picked by colorway, never applied across the board.
- No music in the generation. No on-screen text in the generation.
- Any frame that reads as CGI, a product render or a catalogue photo is a regenerate, not a ship.

# STEP 2 — Animate it

**Seedance 2.5.** Upload the approved starting frame and set it as `first_frame_url`.

```
model:           bytedance/seedance-2-5
first_frame_url: <the approved starting frame>
duration:        28
aspect_ratio:    9:16
resolution:      720p
generate_audio:  true
```

Three things that silently ruin a pass:
1. The runtime in FORMAT, the beat windows, and `duration` must all be **28**. Mismatch makes it compress and the delivery slides off the timeline.
2. Never write "add", "remove", "change" or "replace" — those reroute the job into edit mode and lock the duration. Write "lifts", "taps", "counts".
3. Never negate an object. "No table" produces a table. Say what IS there.
