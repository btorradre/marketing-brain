# VEL-CLAY-WOVEN-01 — "Woven From the Coast" (Straw Tote)

**Product:** Velantra Straw Tote, caramel. **Format:** claymation brand film, 9:16, ~20s + endcard.
**Stage-5 job:** origin MYTH, not assembly demo. Elements of the coast — straw strands, harbor rope's motion, a ribbon of golden light — weave the tote into being. Kept explicitly fantastical: no artisan hands, no workshop, zero manufacturing/origin implications.
**Golden thread:** the bag as a fairytale object born of the coast itself.
**Pipeline:** 4 GPT Image 2 keyframes → 4 × 5s Seedance chain segments → stitch + endcard. Est. ~820cr video.
K1 refs: `heroine.png` style anchor only ("no people"). K2-K3 refs: `caramel 1.png` (+ heroine sheet for palette). K4 refs: `caramel 1.png` + `heroine.png`.
**BLOCKING ESCALATION (2026-07-18):** S2's on-camera completion beat failed twice with different invented attachments (v1: braids + buckles + saddle flap + wrap straps; v2: rear strap with metal ring + handles threaded through slits in the flap). Per the blocking-escalation law the beat was rewritten: flap + handles now HOVER and descend without ever seating; the light ribbon flares to fill frame; the hard cut to S3's audited frame delivers the completed bag. Never re-introduce an on-camera attachment beat for this product.

**Risk note:** K2 is the only frame in the campaign where the bag is intentionally incomplete — the finished lower two-thirds must still match the reference weave exactly; the identity block rides along with a "finished portion matches the reference exactly" line. QA hard on K2/K3.

## Keyframes

**K1** An empty weathered wooden dock at dawn on a miniature clay coast, pale gold mist over a felt ocean, tall clay sea grass bending along the dock edge, a coil of harbor rope beginning to unwind by itself, single straw strands lifting off the dock into the air like ribbons, a ribbon of warm golden light peeling up off the water surface. The clay seagull watches from a wooden mooring post. No people anywhere in the shot.

**K2** Over the dock planks a swirling spiral of straw strands is weaving itself into the straw tote from the bottom up: the lower two thirds of the bag already complete and perfectly woven in warm sandy caramel with braided cross stitch trim, the top edge still dissolving into flying strands, the taupe leather flap pieces and two rolled leather handles hovering just above in the swirl waiting to land, all lit by the ribbon of golden light circling the spiral. The finished portion of the bag matches the reference bag exactly. The clay seagull leans far forward off its mooring post, mesmerized.

**K3** The finished straw tote stands alone in the center of the weathered dock in first morning sun, complete and perfect, the last three loose straw strands tucking themselves into the weave, tiny straw dust motes settling around it, the felt ocean calm behind, the clay seagull now standing on the dock two steps from the bag, head cocked in wonder.

**K4** The clay heroine walks away down the long dock into the low golden morning sun carrying the straw tote, seen from behind at a slight distance, her dress and hair moving in the sea wind, the clay seagull flying low beside her at shoulder height, the felt ocean sparkling on both sides of the dock.

## Segments

**S1 (5s, from K1)** Stop motion magic begins: the rope coil unwinds in little jumps, a dozen straw strands rise off the dock one by one and start to swirl in a slow spiral over the planks, the ribbon of golden light lifts off the water and joins the spiral, the sea grass leans toward it. The seagull tilts its head watching. Ambient sound: low wind, creaking dock wood, a soft airy shimmer of straw brushing straw.

**S2 (5s, from K2 — v3 blocking, see BLOCKING ESCALATION above)** The weave rises in stop motion: strands lace the top edge row by row while the taupe leather flap, one connected piece with its wide center panel and 2 squared outer tabs, and the two rolled handles hover in the swirl just above the bag, slowly descending but never touching it. The golden light ribbon spirals tighter and tighter around the forming bag until its glow swells and fills the frame with warm golden light in the final moment. Nothing attaches to the bag on camera. Hard cut delivers the completed bag via S3's audited frame. Ambient sound: rapid soft straw whispers, one rising warm shimmer.

**S3 (5s, from K3)** Quiet after the magic: the last strands tuck themselves in with three little pulls, straw dust settles in drifting stop motion motes, the golden light ribbon sinks back into the water, the seagull hops one careful step closer and gently taps the woven side with its beak, then looks up. Ambient sound: morning calm, water lapping, one tiny woody tap, a soft distant gull cry.

**S4 (5s, from K4)** She walks down the dock in unhurried stop motion steps, the tote swinging gently in her hand, the seagull glides beside her with slow jerky wingbeats, sequin glints shimmer on the water, her shadow stretches long over the planks. Ambient sound: wind, dock wood underfoot, wings flapping softly, the morning sea.
