# Congruent B-roll Prompt Pack — non-brand VO lines (2026-07-24)

Purpose: every agitation/observation line in the two films gets a visual that matches WHAT THE LINE SAYS, not a product macro. Brooks generates these himself (Google Omni); prompts are self-contained, text-free, fictional-bags-only, 9:16.

## ⚠️ TOF STRUCTURE LAW (Brooks, 2026-07-24)

These films are TOP OF FUNNEL for UNAWARE audiences. The product is WITHHELD until the brand-entry turn (~60% mark — "So we chose restraint" in F1, "So we build bags that decline the choice" in F2), exactly like the SoaT reference (their shirt/brand enters at 0:34 of 58s) and the founder-brief identity-first law (the ad stays on HER; product = one evidence beat). Everything before the turn uses the product-free prompts in this pack: her world → her identity → the noticing. Product macros/money shot/end card own the final third only.

Variants (swap the age/location clause; each holds something unbranded and beautiful so the line is literally true): "her early fifties at a farmers market stall, holding a paper wrapped bundle of peonies" / "her late thirties at a coffee shop takeout window, plain white cup in hand" / "her late forties seated at a harborside patio table at golden hour, hand resting on a linen napkin".

Standing QA on every generation:
- Fictional bags only: any hardware reading as letters, monograms, or a recognizable turn-lock → regenerate (trade-dress law; the unnamed wink is the move).
- No readable text/digits anywhere (price tags/cards stay blurred) — labels comp in post only.
- Her expression is always composed — noticing, never wounded, never bitter (avatar law: identity first, grievance never).
- Wide/lifestyle shots: no logos on wardrobe or props.
