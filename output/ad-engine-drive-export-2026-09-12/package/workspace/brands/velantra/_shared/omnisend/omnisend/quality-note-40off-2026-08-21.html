Hi there,

I want to address the product quality issues some of you may have experienced recently.

Over the past several weeks, some Velantra bags have reached customers without matching the standard we approved. Some Eleanor Weekenders were produced smaller than they should have been, and some Sofia Woven Totes in Caramel arrived lighter than the color we intended.

If you received one of those bags, I am genuinely sorry.

Velantra is still a young brand, and we are still learning. From the beginning, one of our biggest goals has been to learn as fast as possible, from our customers, from the products we make, and especially from the things we get wrong.

That does not mean accepting mistakes as part of being young. It means recognizing them quickly, taking responsibility for them, and doing everything we can to make sure the same problem does not happen twice.

You trusted us to send you something you would be excited to open, and in these cases, we fell short. Those bags should have been caught before they ever made it to your door.

If you received a bag that does not look or feel right, please reply to this email. We will make it right with a replacement, and you will not be responsible for shipping.

What we're working on

This summer showed us where parts of our production and quality-control process need to be stronger, and we are actively working to improve them.

We are tightening how colors are approved so each dye lot is checked against a physical master swatch before full production moves forward.

We are also building additional measurement and quality checks into production before bags leave the factory, followed by another inspection when inventory reaches our warehouse.

And we are consolidating production with fewer partners so there is clearer ownership over every product we make and much more accountability when something is not right.

We know we are not going to build the company we want by pretending we already have everything figured out.

Our goal is to learn quickly, listen closely, and continuously raise the standard of both the products we make and the service we provide. When a customer points out something we can do better, we want to act on it. When a process breaks, we want to rebuild it. And when we make a mistake, we want to come out of it better than we were before.

That is the kind of company we are trying to build.

And if we miss again

You should never feel like you have to fight with us to get an issue resolved.

If something arrives incorrectly, reach out. Our goal is to make the process simple and take care of you quickly.

Providing a great product is only half of the experience. How we take care of you when something goes wrong matters just as much.

Thank you

A lot of you stayed patient with us through delays this summer. You sent kind messages when you had every reason to be frustrated, gave our team the opportunity to fix problems, and continued supporting a young brand while we learned where we needed to get better.

I do not take that lightly.
