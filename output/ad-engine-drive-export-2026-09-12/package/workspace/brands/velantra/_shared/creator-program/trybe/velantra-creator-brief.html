What We're Looking For

  • Vertical 9:16 video, 15–30 seconds. Shot on your phone. Short and punchy beats long and rambling.
  • Talk like you're texting a close friend. Not a spokesperson, not a script-reader. Real pauses, real reactions, a little laugh — that's the energy that converts.
  • One angle per video. Pick a single angle from this brief and commit to it. Don't try to say everything in one clip.
  • Natural daylight, clean background. Near a window is perfect. Clear the clutter behind you — the bag should be the only product in frame.
  • Show the texture. The weave, the stitching, the leather. Close-ups do a lot of the selling for you.
  • No music in the raw file. Your voice and natural room sound only — trending audio can be added natively in-platform when you post.

How to Sound (Delivery Notes)

  • Hook in the first 2 seconds. Start mid-thought: "Okay this is the most requested bag in my closet right now."
  • Short, plain sentences. Around 30–45 spoken words fits comfortably in a 15-second clip.
  • Let genuine reactions in — a small "mmm," a gasp when you open the box, a laugh under a line. Involuntary beats scripted, every time.
  • Say the bag's name once, naturally: "the Sofia," "the Eleanor weekender," "the Margot."
  • End with an opinion or a nudge, not a hard pitch: "I'm officially obsessed" or "colorways go fast."

The Angles

These are our proven performers. Each one tells you what to make, how to structure it, and gives you an opener to steal. Follow the beat timing loosely — the shape matters more than the exact seconds.

1 · Most Requested

Social proof · continuous hold-to-camera · 15s · any bag
What to make: A hold-to-camera video presenting the bag as the one your audience keeps asking about. Show it front-on, in side profile, then the stitching up close.
0:00–0:05
Hold the bag at chest height, front square to the lens. Deliver the hook with direct eye contact.
0:05–0:10
Turn the bag to a clean side profile on your palm. Sweep a hand along the top line.
0:10–0:15
Lean the handles toward the lens, trace the stitching with a finger, land your verdict.
Steal this opener"Okay this is the most requested bag in my closet right now."

2 · Macro Cold Open

Craftsmanship · bag-only close-up, then cut to you · 15s, one cut · any bag
What to make: A quality-you-can-see video. Open on an extreme close-up of the material and stitching with your voice already talking, then cut to you and close on the price-disbelief line.
0:00–0:05
Extreme close-up of the weave, grain or stitching, slow drift across the front. No face yet.
0:05–0:10
Hard cut to you at chest height. Lift the bag off the table by the handles.
0:10–0:15
Let it settle into the crook of your arm. Close with "it costs nothing like it looks."
Steal this opener"No because look at this stitching up close."

3 · On the Body

Honest review · continuous three-quarter shot · 15s · any bag

0:00–0:05

0:05–0:10
Turn to full side profile so the silhouette reads sharp against your outfit.
0:10–0:15

Steal this opener"Quick honest review of the Velantra Sofia."

Ground Rules

Do

  • Film in daylight near a window
  • Keep the bag's real color and shape — no heavy filters
  • Use your own words on top of our openers
  • Show texture close-ups in every video
  • Disclose the partnership (#ad or platform paid-partnership label)
  • Use your Trybe link/code so your sales track to you

Don't

  • Don't mention where the bags are made
  • Don't name or compare to other designer brands, or call it a "dupe"
  • Don't promise discounts or shipping times we haven't given you
  • Don't add music to the raw file or burn in captions
  • Don't show competitor bags in frame
  • Don't read a script word-for-word — it always shows

"Boatkin" is ours and you're free to use it. It's a made-up word for the structured straw bag trend, not another brand's name — that's exactly why it's safe to say.

Deliverables & Submission

  • Deliver each video as a clean 9:16 file (no watermarks, no burned-in captions) through Trybe.
  • One angle per video. Batch a few in one session — same outfit is fine, vary the angle.
  • Post with your tracked link/code from Trybe and the required disclosure.
  • Strong performers get pulled into paid amplification and are first in line for retainer offers — see the program agreement for details.
