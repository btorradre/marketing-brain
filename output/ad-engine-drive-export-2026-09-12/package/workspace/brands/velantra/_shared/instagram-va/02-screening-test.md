# Instagram VA — Paid Screening Test

Two parts in this doc:

- **PART A** — the test brief you send to shortlisted applicants (copy-paste as-is; external-safe, live links only)
- **PART B** — INTERNAL grading rubric. Never send Part B.

The test is engineered so the AI engines themselves create the trap: every image model splits the Straw Tote's flap into separate pieces by default. Applicants who catch and fix that without being told exactly what to look for have the reference-fidelity instinct this role runs on. Applicants who submit a split flap don't.

Suggested test pay: [$20–40], 48-hour deadline.

## PART A — Test brief (send this)

---

Hi [name] — thanks for applying. Next step is a small paid test ([$__], paid regardless of outcome, 48 hours from when you confirm). This mirrors the actual daily work.

**The product:** Velantra Straw Tote — https://velantrafashion.com/products/the-sofia-woven-tote

Use the photos in that product page gallery as your references. Your generated bag must match them **exactly** — same construction, same details, nothing added, nothing missing. Study the reference photos closely before you generate anything. Matching the reference precisely is the single most important part of this test.

### Deliverable 1 — Lifestyle product shot (no people)

One photorealistic image of the caramel Straw Tote in a summer lifestyle setting of your choice (e.g. on a linen chair by a window, on a boat deck, on a café table). Looks like a real photo taken on a nice camera or iPhone — not an obvious AI render.

- Aspect ratio 4:5
- Filename: `lastname_d1.jpg`

### Deliverable 2 — Model/UGC-style shot (with a person)

One photorealistic image of a woman (30s–50s, polished but natural) carrying the caramel Straw Tote outdoors. The goal: someone scrolling Instagram would believe this is a real customer photo. Watch hands, skin, hair, and light — no plastic AI skin, no warped fingers.

- Aspect ratio 4:5
- Filename: `lastname_d2.jpg`

### Deliverable 3 — Captions

Instagram captions for both images. Brand voice: quiet luxury, understated, confident, no hype words, no emoji spam, minimal hashtags (0–4). Reference the tone of the product page copy. A few sentences each is plenty.

- One .txt or .doc file, filename: `lastname_captions.txt`

### Submission

- For each image, one line stating which AI tool(s) you used and roughly how many attempts it took
- Send all 3 files [via email/Drive link] by [deadline]
- Exact filenames and aspect ratios as specified above

We're grading realism, how precisely the bag matches the reference gallery, caption voice, and how exactly you follow this brief. Good luck!

## PART B — INTERNAL grading rubric (never send)

Score /100. Interview anyone ≥80 with no auto-fails. 65–79 = maybe pile. <65 or any auto-fail = pass.

### Auto-fail (any one of these ends it)

1. **Flap split into separate pieces** — the flap must read as ONE seamless leather sheet folded flat over the front, with the panel-and-two-tabs look as shapes cut into that single sheet. Separate floating tabs, a gap/seam between sections, or anything visible *through* the flap = the exact failure the engines produce by default. Submitting it means they never truly compared against the reference.
2. Metal hardware, buckles, or zippers anywhere on the bag (it has none)
3. Any logo or brand text on the bag (Velantra bags are logo-free)
4. Belt straps wrong: must be exactly 2, crossed in an X on the front, below the flap
5. Front leather detailing duplicated on the back face of the bag
6. Caption mentions another brand (Hermès, Birkin, any competitor) or claims where the bag is made
7. Missed deadline, wrong filenames, or wrong aspect ratio *and* no acknowledgment — the brief-compliance basics

### Scored criteria

**Product accuracy /40** — grade against the PDP gallery:
- Flap: one seamless sheet, folded fully over, lying flat (10)
- Tightly woven straw body + braided cross-stitch edge trim, correct caramel tone (10)
- 2 rolled leather top handles passing through the 2 flap slots; 2 crossed belt straps; white contrast stitching (10)
- Overall silhouette/proportions match; nothing invented (no charms, pockets, trim the reference doesn't have) (10)

**Realism /30:**
- D1: believable light, shadows, surface textures; straw weave doesn't smear into plastic (10)
- D2: human passes the scroll test — natural skin texture, correct hands, hair that isn't painted-on (15)
- Neither image has the AI-glossy oversaturated look (5)

**Brief compliance /20:**
- Exact filenames, 4:5, tool disclosure lines, on time (10)
- Everything asked for, nothing missing, no scope invention (10)

**Caption voice /10:**
- Understated, quiet-luxury register; no "😍🔥 OBSESSED"-tier hype; ≤4 hashtags; reads like the PDP copy's cousin (10)

### What separates a hire from a pass

The winning submission usually comes with a note like "the model kept splitting the flap into three pieces, so I re-prompted until it stayed one piece." That sentence is the job. Someone who noticed the engines fighting them and fought back on product truth will survive our QA standards. Someone who submitted their first pretty output won't.
