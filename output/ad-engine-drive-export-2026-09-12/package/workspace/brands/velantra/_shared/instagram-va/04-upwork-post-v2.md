## A. Job post fields

**Title** (48 chars, Upwork caps at 50):
Instagram Manager (AI Content) for Handbag Brand

**Category:** Sales & Marketing → Social Media Marketing
(alt: Admin Support → Virtual Assistance)

**Skills, up to 10:**
Instagram · Social Media Management · Social Media Content Creation · AI Image Generation · Midjourney · Instagram Reels · Content Creation · Social Media Marketing · Canva · Fashion

**Scope:** Ongoing project · Complex/large · Duration: More than 6 months · Less than 30 hrs/week

**Experience level:** Intermediate

**Budget:** Hourly, $4 to $6/hr, cap 25 hrs/week. Set by Brooks 2026-08-03. Lands at roughly $430 to $650/mo, consistent with the $600 target.

**Job description (paste):**

Velantra is a growing accessible-luxury handbag brand (velantrafashion.com). We are hiring one person to fully own our Instagram: creating and publishing 2 to 3 posts per day, 7 days a week, using AI image and video tools.

This is not a generic social media job. Almost everything we post is AI-generated, and our products have very specific construction details that AI models get wrong by default. The core skill is making AI content that looks completely real, not AI-glossy, and that matches our product reference photos exactly. If you do not naturally zoom in to compare your output against a reference before you submit it, this role is not for you.

You get a full AI suite, paid for by us. You never pay for a credit.

What you'll do:
- Create and publish 2 to 3 posts per day from a batched calendar built and approved in advance
- Short Reels: styling clips, what-fits-inside, colorway reveals, packing and unpacking, simple product motion. Most run 5 to 15 seconds
- Simple statics: product and lifestyle stills, colorway grids, new-arrival and back-in-stock announcements, text cards
- Generate photorealistic AI images from our product photos as references, for both feed stills and Reel frames
- Write captions in our brand voice, quiet and understated with zero hype. Full voice guide provided
- Check every asset against the product reference before it goes anywhere. Our bags have locked design details that must never be wrong
- Light community management: reply to comments, flag important DMs

The rules that are not negotiable:
- The bag in your image must match the reference gallery exactly. Same construction, same hardware, same stitching, nothing added, nothing missing
- Our bags are logo-free. No logo, no brand text, no monogram anywhere on the product
- Never name or compare us to another brand in a caption, a comment, or on-screen text
- Never state or imply where the bags are made
- Nothing that looks like a glossy 3D render. If it does not read as a real photograph, it does not go out

Requirements:
- Hands-on experience with current AI image tools (GPT image models, Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana) and image-to-video tools (Kling, Seedance, Veo, Runway). Show us AI work you personally made
- Obsessive attention to detail. This is the number one thing we are hiring for
- Strong written English. Captions are yours to write
- Native comfort with Instagram: Reels, feed aesthetics, scheduling tools
- Reliable daily availability. Creation is batched, posts run 7 days a week

Structure: paid test task first, then a 2-week paid trial with daily feedback, then ongoing. Roughly 4 hrs/day.

To apply, please answer the screening questions below. Generic proposals will be passed over.

**Screening questions (Upwork allows up to 5):**

1. Start your answer to this question with the word CARAMEL. Then link 3 to 6 examples of realistic AI content you have personally generated, image or video, and name the tool used for each.
2. In 2 or 3 sentences: how do you make AI-generated people and products look real instead of fake? Tell us what you actually do differently.
3. What is your timezone and your daily availability, which hours and which days?
4. Have you managed a brand's Instagram before? Briefly, whose and for how long.

## B. Budget note

Set at $4 to $6/hr with a 25 hrs/week cap, which is roughly $430 to $650/mo. Below Upwork's typical band for AI-content social work, which runs $8 to $15/hr, so expect a proposal pool weighted toward people who can post daily but cannot generate reference-accurate AI imagery.

The screening questions and the paid test carry the whole filter at this rate. Do not skip either, and do not soften the CARAMEL rule. Question 1 is the only cheap way to cut the pile before you spend time on it.

## C. Candidate outreach (manual)

⚠️ Do this by hand through Upwork's "Invite freelancers" on the posted job. Do NOT automate. Bulk automated messaging risks the account.

**How to find candidates:** On the posted job → Invite freelancers → search talent for "AI content Instagram," "Midjourney social media," "AI UGC creator," "fashion social media manager." Filter to intermediate and up, strong Instagram or AI-image history, good English, decent JSS. Shortlist 8 to 12.

**Invite message template** (personalize the first line each time):

> Hi [name], your [specific thing from their profile] is exactly the kind of work we need. We're a luxury handbag brand hiring one person to run our Instagram with AI-generated content, 2 to 3 posts a day. The bar is realism plus matching product references precisely. There's a short paid test task, then a paid 2-week trial. If it sounds interesting, apply to the job and start your answer with the word CARAMEL so I know you read this. Thanks!
