**Budget:** Hourly, [$__–$__ /hr] (or fixed [$__/mo] retainer — pick one; hourly recommended to start so you can gauge speed)

**Job description (paste):**

Velantra is a growing accessible-luxury handbag brand (velantrafashion.com). We're hiring one person to fully own our Instagram: creating and publishing 3 posts per day, 7 days a week, using AI image and video tools.

This is not a generic social media job. Almost all of our content is AI-generated, and our products have very specific construction details that AI models get wrong by default. The core skill is making AI content that (1) looks completely real — not "AI-glossy" — and (2) matches our product reference photos exactly. If you don't naturally zoom in to compare your output against a reference before you hit submit, this role isn't for you.

What you'll do:
- Generate photorealistic AI images and short videos of our handbags (product, lifestyle, and model/UGC-style) working from our product photos as references
- Check every asset against the reference before it's used — our bags have locked design details that must never be wrong
- Write captions in our brand voice (quiet luxury, understated, no hype) — full voice guide provided
- Schedule and publish 3 posts/day (feed + Reels) and keep a simple content calendar
- Light community management: reply to comments, flag important DMs

Requirements:
- Hands-on experience with current AI image tools (GPT image models, Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana) and image-to-video tools (Kling, Seedance, Veo, Runway). Show us AI work you've personally made.
- Obsessive attention to detail — this is the #1 thing we're hiring for
- Strong written English for captions
- Native comfort with Instagram: Reels, feed aesthetics, scheduling tools
- Reliable daily availability (creation is batched; posts run 7 days/week)

Structure: paid test task first, then a 2-week paid trial with daily feedback, then ongoing. ~3–4 hrs/day.

To apply, please answer the screening questions below. Generic proposals will be passed over.

**Screening questions (Upwork lets you add up to 5 — use these):**
1. Start your answer to this question with the word CARAMEL. Then link 3–6 examples of realistic AI content you have personally generated (image or video), and name the tool used for each.
2. In 2–3 sentences: how do you make AI-generated people look real instead of fake?
3. What is your timezone and your daily availability (which hours, which days)?
4. Have you managed a brand's Instagram before? Briefly, whose and for how long.
