---

Welcome to Velantra. This document is your operating manual. Read it fully before your first post. The rules in Sections 4–6 are non-negotiable — they exist because we've watched AI engines break our products in specific, repeatable ways, and every rule below was written after a real failure.

## 1. The role

- **3 posts per day, 7 days a week** on the Velantra Instagram ([@handle])
- Content is **batched and approved in advance** (see Section 8) — never scrambled day-of
- You generate the content with AI tools, check it against product references, write the captions, schedule it, and handle light community management
- Your two core skills, in order: **(1) the bag in every asset matches the real bag exactly, (2) everything looks real, not AI-generated**

## 2. The brand in one page

Velantra is **accessible luxury**: structured, heritage-quality handbags for professional women, $50–$150, no visible logos anywhere, ever. Positioning: "Affordable Luxury Reimagined." Three pillars: durability (two-year warranty), timeless design, understated elegance.

**The customer:** a professional woman, 28–55, who has owned designer bags and outgrown logos. She's impressed by quality she can feel, not status she can flash. Think coastal, polished, quiet confidence — linen, harbors, farm-to-table, rosé with friends. She researches before she buys and she notices details. Write and generate for her.

**Voice:** calm, warm, confident, understated. Short sentences. No hype ("OBSESSED 😍🔥" is a fireable caption). No urgency-spam. 0–4 hashtags per post. Read the product pages on velantrafashion.com until the voice is in your ear — captions should read like the site copy's cousin.

**Founder voice:** founder-perspective posts (brand story, "why I made this," personal notes) are written as **Jessica**, the brand's founder, and signed "— Jessica". No other team names ever appear in public content. If you're unsure whether a post should be founder-voice, it shouldn't be.

## 4. Hard rules — never break these

1. **Never state where the bags are made.** No "made in USA," no "Italian leather," no "European craftsmanship," no atelier-manufacturing claims. Not in captions, not in image text, not in comment replies. If a follower asks, reply: "Our support team can help with product questions — support@velantrafashion.com."
2. **Never mention another brand.** No Hermès, no "Birkin," no competitor names — not in captions, not in hashtags (#birkin is banned), not in on-screen text, not in comparisons ("better than X"). The Weekender is a "travel bag" or "weekend bag," nothing else.
3. **No logos on bags in generated content.** Velantra bags are logo-free. An AI engine inventing embossed text or a logo plate = regenerate.
4. **The bag must match the reference exactly.** Section 5 blocks are pasted into every prompt verbatim. Section 6 checklist runs on every asset before it's scheduled. No exceptions, including "the bag is barely in frame."
5. **Safe claims only.** OK: hand woven (Straw Tote), structured / holds its shape, fits a full day of stuff, seasonal colorways, limited quantities, two-year warranty. Anything not on the live site: don't claim it.
6. **AI people must pass the scroll test.** Natural skin texture, correct hands, real-looking hair and light. Add imperfection cues to prompts (candid framing, slight motion, natural window light, iPhone-photo look). If it looks AI-glossy, it doesn't post.
7. **Never put text into the image with the AI engine.** Engines misspell (they can't even spell "Weekender" reliably). All on-image text is added afterward as an overlay in CapCut/Canva.
8. **In video, never show hands opening or closing a bag's flap, straps, or closure.** Engines reinvent the closure mid-motion every single time. Closed→open changes happen across a hard cut between two separately checked frames, never on camera.

## 5. Locked product-truth blocks (paste verbatim into prompts)

These are exact, tested prompt blocks. Paste them word-for-word — do not paraphrase, shorten, or "improve" them. They exist because every engine we've tested breaks these products in the same ways.

### 5.1 Straw Tote

**Identity block — every Straw Tote prompt** (resolve the color wording for sky-blue or caban-black):

> a structured hand woven straw tote in warm sandy caramel, tightly woven straw body with braided cross stitch trim along the edges, a smooth taupe leather flap folded over the top of the bag from the back: the flap is ONE single seamless piece of leather, its front lower edge cut into the silhouette of a wide center panel with 2 squared outer tabs, the leather fully continuous and unbroken between and above these shapes, with exactly 2 narrow slots through which the handles pass, two rolled taupe leather top handles, two taupe leather belt straps crossed on the front, white contrast stitching on all leather edges, no metal hardware, no logos. The leather flap, tabs and belt straps exist ONLY on the FRONT face of the bag, the back face is plain woven straw, no duplicated front detailing on any other face.

**Flap mechanism block — MANDATORY whenever the bag is carrying things, open, or the flap is visible in motion.** (Why: engines split the flap into separate floating tabs and poke contents through the middle. The truth: the flap is ONE seamless sheet folded all the way over, lying flat; the panel-and-tabs look is a silhouette cut into that one sheet; contents only ever lean out of the mouth BEHIND it.)

> Flap and opening construction: the taupe leather flap is ONE single seamless sheet of leather attached along the top rear edge of the tote and folded all the way forward over the front, lying completely flat. Its front lower edge is cut into the shape of a wide center panel and 2 squared outer tabs, but these are shapes cut into the SAME single sheet, never separate pieces. The leather is continuous and unbroken between the shapes and across the entire top of the bag, including between the two handle slots. The only openings anywhere in the flap are the 2 narrow handle slots. No gap, no seam, no split, no opening exists anywhere else in the flap, and nothing behind or inside the bag is ever visible through the flap. The flap never splits into pieces, never lifts, never stands up, always folded all the way over. When the tote is carrying things, the woven mouth opens BEHIND the flap: the croissant, baguette or flowers lean out of the open mouth at the back of the bag, behind the leather flap, never through the flap. The 2 taupe leather belt straps lie crossed in an X over the front below the flap with rounded ends and white contrast stitching, exactly as on the closed reference bag, never threaded through the flap and never wrapped around the contents. No metal hardware anywhere on the bag.

**Additional line for every VIDEO prompt with the bag in frame:**

> The leather flap stays ONE single seamless sheet folded all the way over, lying completely flat the entire clip: no gap, seam, or split ever opens anywhere in it, nothing behind it ever shows through it, and its cut edge shapes never separate into pieces. Contents lean out of the mouth behind the flap, never through it.

⚠️ Prompt-language law: when writing your own scene text around these blocks, never describe the flap with count language like "3 pieces/elements/sections" — engines literalize counts into separate parts. Say "shapes cut into one sheet."

**Identity block — every Weekender prompt** (resolve the canvas bracket to one colorway):

For full-body / small-in-frame shots (model carry, lookbook), also add:

> the bag in frame is an exact copy of the bag in [the reference image] in silhouette, proportions, materials and details, the two rolled top handles are smooth simple leather tubes with no wrapping, no braiding and no woven texture

**Opening mechanism block — MANDATORY whenever the bag is open, opening, or being packed.** (Why: engines fold the flap back but leave a phantom flap-shaped panel on the front, or strip the leather band entirely. The truth: the leather/canvas two-tone split is identical open or closed; the handles anchor into the wide leather band; the one-piece flap leans back behind the open mouth.)

> Open bag construction: the open bag keeps the exact same two tone split as the closed bag in the reference image. The entire upper section of the bag body, across the front, the back and both sides, is smooth rich cognac brown leather, exactly as deep as the cognac leather upper section on the closed reference bag, and everything below it is [cream ivory woven canvas | deep army green twill canvas]. Folding the flap back does NOT change this split: the line where the leather ends and the canvas begins sits in exactly the same place as on the closed reference bag. The two rolled cognac leather top handles are anchored directly into this wide leather upper band with sturdy leather bases, never into the canvas. Two thin gold posts stand upright on the leather band, and the small gold oval turn lock is mounted on the leather band at the top center of the front. The two cognac leather belt straps hang loose and unfastened down the front with their gold clasp plates. The wide leather band on the front is plain smooth leather and is part of the bag body: no tab sections, no scalloped edges, no pocket shape, no turn lock pocket, it is not a flap. The entire cognac leather flap, one single piece, is folded backward over the top rear edge of the bag and leans back behind the open mouth, clearly visible from the front: the inside face of the flap stands behind the opening showing its two round handle holes, its strap slots and its small gold plate, with the rear rolled handle rising above it. The flap never covers the front of the bag and never splits into pieces. The mouth of the bag is a clean open oval at the top of the leather section, showing the natural cream cotton canvas interior lining and the cognac leather slip pocket on the back interior wall. The bag has NO zipper anywhere, no zipper track, no zipper teeth, no zipper pull along the mouth of the bag, and no embossed text or lettering anywhere on the bag.

## 6. Pre-post audit — every asset, every time

Before anything is scheduled, put the generated asset next to the product-page reference photo and check, zoomed in. For video, scrub to at least 3 points (start / middle / end) — engines break bags mid-clip even from a clean first frame.

**Straw Tote — PASS looks like:** one connected flap lying flat over the front (panel + 2 tabs cut into one sheet), contents leaning out of the mouth *behind* it, 2 belts crossed in an X below it, no metal anywhere, back face plain straw.
**Straw Tote — FAIL, regenerate on sight:** flap split / floating tabs / any gap between sections; flap lifted, half-open, or standing up; anything poking through or between the flap shapes; belts threaded through the flap or wrapped around contents; any metal hardware; front detailing duplicated on the back.

**Weekender — PASS looks like:** same leather/canvas split as the closed reference (wide cognac leather upper band, handles anchored into it), and when open, the one-piece flap leaning back behind the mouth showing its handle holes / slots / gold plate.
**Weekender — FAIL, regenerate on sight:** handles rooted in canvas; leather band missing or shrunk to a thin trim line; a flap-shaped panel (tabs, scallops, turn-lock pocket) on the front of an open bag; malformed or missing flap behind the opening; ANY zipper.

**All products:** no logos or lettering on the bag; no invented hardware, trim, straps, or pockets that aren't in the reference; colorway is a real colorway from the site.

**People:** hands (finger count, natural grip), skin texture, hair edges, eyes, jewelry consistency, garbled background text.

**Caption:** no competitor names, no origin statements, product name matches the site, claims are on the live page, voice check (would the site have written this?), ≤4 hashtags.

If any check fails: regenerate with the relevant Section 5 block pasted in. Three failed attempts → change the composition (different angle/blocking) instead of retrying the same shot, or flag it in the approval doc. **Never post an asset that hasn't passed. A skipped post is recoverable; a wrong-product post is not.**

## 7. Generation guidance

- **Stills:** image-to-image with the product-page photo wired in as the reference, identity block (+ mechanism block when the bag is open/carrying/in motion) pasted verbatim, then your scene description. Mechanism block is required even in stills whenever contents are visible in the bag.
- **Video:** animate from a still that already passed the Section 6 audit — never animate an unchecked frame, and never text-to-video for product shots. Keep clips short; drift accumulates late in clips, so audit end frames hardest. If a clip drifts late, trim to the clean portion or fall back to a slow push-in (Ken Burns) on the audited still — a clean still beats a broken video every time.
- **Realism cues for people:** candid framing, natural window/outdoor light, slight imperfection ("hair moving in the breeze," "photo taken mid-step"), iPhone-photo aesthetic for UGC-style posts, editorial lighting for polished posts.
- **Prompts:** no em dashes or ellipses in generation prompts; plain declarative sentences.

## 8. Weekly workflow

1. **[Day]: batch day.** Generate the next 7 days (21 posts) + captions. Run every asset through Section 6.
2. **Submit for approval:** one doc/folder — each post = final asset + caption + slot + a one-line note if anything needed regenerating and why. During your **first 2 weeks, nothing posts without approval.** After that, approval moves to spot-checks, but the Section 6 audit never goes away.
3. **Schedule** approved posts in [Meta Business Suite / Later / tool].
4. **Daily (15–20 min):** confirm posts went live, reply to comments in brand voice (warm, brief, no claims), flag DMs about orders/complaints/press to [contact] — never handle order issues yourself.
5. **Weekly recap (5 bullets):** top post, worst post, follower delta, anything you want to try next week, anything that blocked you.

## 9. Escalate immediately (don't improvise)

- Any press, influencer, or partnership DM
- Any customer complaint or order issue (route to support@velantrafashion.com)
- Any comment/DM asking where the bags are made (use the Section 4.1 reply, then flag it)
- Anything you're about to post that this doc doesn't clearly cover

When in doubt: don't post, ask. Nobody will ever be annoyed by that question. The reverse is not true.
