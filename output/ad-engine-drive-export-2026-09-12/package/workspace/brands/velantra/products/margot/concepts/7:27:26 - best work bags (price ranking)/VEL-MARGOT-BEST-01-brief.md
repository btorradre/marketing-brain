## 2. Standing-rule status (inherited from VEL-SOFIA-BEST-01, Brooks's direction)

1. `feedback_velantra_no_competitor_comparisons` — **reversed for this format**; real brands named on screen.
2. **Tone law governs everything (Brooks, 7/26):** we are NOT claiming the Margot is the best of a bad field — these are sincerely four of the best work bags on the internet.
