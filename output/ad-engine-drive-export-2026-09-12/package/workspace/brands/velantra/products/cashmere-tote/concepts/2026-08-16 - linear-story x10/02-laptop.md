## The angle

**Golden nugget:** she has already been burned. She bought a bag specifically because it looked big, got it home, and the one thing she bought it for didn't go in. So now she measures before she orders, and the ad is her showing her working.

**Verdict she leads with:** I measured it against my laptop before I ordered it.

**The one physical payoff:** the laptop going in flat, with visible room on both sides.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** the reference lists what fits in passing, inside a sentence about the weather. This one is a due-diligence story. The whole ad is one woman checking a number, and the number is ours.

**Swap test:** passes. 19.7 × 10.2 × 7.1 and a 13" laptop lying flat with room either side is our measurement, not a category truth.
**Six-month test:** passes. Nothing seasonal.

## Edit notes

- Beats 7 and 8 are deliberately short (2.2s and 0.5s). One cut per named object. The edit counts the list for her, so capacity gets proved three times in six seconds instead of asserted once.
- Beat 5 is the payoff. Do not cut into the moment the laptop lands flat.
- Every laptop in this ad must have a **blank lid**. No logo, no sticker, no glowing mark.
- Beats 1, 2 and 10 share one location, one light and one wardrobe. Shoot them as a set.
