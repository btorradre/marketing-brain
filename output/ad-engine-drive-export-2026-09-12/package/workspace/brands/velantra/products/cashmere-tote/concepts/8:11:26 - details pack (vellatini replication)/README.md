## Colette-specific decisions

- **Colorway discipline.** The b-roll library holds both Caramel and Espresso clips. Every clip in
  this cut is **Caramel**. `VEL-COL-007-street-shopfront-pause` and `-004-street-walking-side` were
  cut on sight for espresso trim; intercutting them would read as two different bags.
- **Item order is weighted away from work.** The laptop is one beat in the middle, never the lead,
  and the sequence closes on market flowers. Colette is the everything bag of her week; the Margot
  owns the work slot.
- **Prompt guards that mattered:** the belt is pinned as unbroken and horizontal with both gold disc
  caps visible (Omni has vanished it before), the handles are pinned two-tone felt-with-caramel-top
  (they drift to all-leather or all-felt), and the top edge is pinned PLAIN OATMEAL FELT with no rim
  (the phrase "leather trim along the top edge" grows a binding the canonical does not have).
- Laptop lids and book covers are pinned blank so no logo appears.

## Continuity fix logged

The first build put the already-packed overhead shot (`052`) in act B, so the bag read as packed,
then empty, then packing again. `052` moved to D1 where it is the payoff. Any future cut of this
format must keep every "already full" beat downstream of the pack block.
