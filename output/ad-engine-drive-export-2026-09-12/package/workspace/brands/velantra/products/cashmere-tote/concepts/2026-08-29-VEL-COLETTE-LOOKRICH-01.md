4. The talking-head cutout over a screen recording is our house greenscreen format already.

## 3. Avatar positioning

This runs at **Avatar B, the woman who wants the world and not the logo.** She is the volume buyer at our price points, she does not care about a monogram, and she is shopping for the fall bag right now.

"Without going broke" earns the click, but the brand's language law bans the priced-out and grievance register. So:

- The phrase appears **once, in the hook, and never again.**
- The body runs entirely on **discernment**. She is not settling. She is handed the checklist and shown that one $119.99 bag clears every item on it.
- The price is never framed against another brand. It appears struck through on the real page at the end and does the work by itself.

The whole ad is one promise: *this is what expensive actually looks like, and here is a bag that has every part of it.*

## 6. THE TALKING HEAD

There is no Colette greenscreen creator asset. This is the piece that has to be built, and the reference gives us the exact spec.

### What the reference creator actually is

Woman roughly 35 to 45, hair pulled back in a low bun, **white ribbed tank**, thin gold chain, small gold hoops, a visible tattoo on the forearm, holding a **small handheld content mic** up near her chin. Chest-up framing, slight high angle, warm flat indoor light. She talks continuously with light hand movement, mouth always moving, never still.

The mic is not incidental. It is the single strongest signal in the format: it says "creator recording a voiceover," which is why the ad reads as a recommendation rather than a commercial. **Keep the mic.**

### Production, per the Velantra Avatar SOP

**Step 1. Pinterest reference.** Search `pinterest.com/search/pins/?q=40+year+old+woman+white+tank+top+low+bun+natural+light+selfie`. Pick ONE still, calm photo. Clear face, plain background, not mid-motion, not a public figure. Style and vibe reference only.

**Step 2. Nano Banana Pro, image to image.** Attach the saved photo and run:

> Using the attached photo only as a loose style, age, and vibe reference, do not reproduce this exact person's face or likeness. Generate an ORIGINAL ultra-realistic iPhone front-camera video still of a 40-year-old American woman with dark hair pulled back into a low bun, a few loose strands at the temples, wearing a plain white ribbed cotton tank top, a thin gold chain necklace and small gold hoop earrings, holding a small black handheld content-creator microphone up near her chin in her right hand, framed chest-up at a slight high angle, looking directly into the lens, mouth slightly open mid-sentence as if talking, warm natural indoor window light on her face, realistic skin texture with visible pores and faint fine lines, minimal natural makeup, no beauty filter, no retouching, shot on iPhone look, the entire background is a solid flat chroma-key green (#00B140) with no shadows or gradients, vertical 9:16.

Generate three, pick the one that is not artifacted. Regenerate if it reads too close to the Pinterest face. The identity must be original.

**Step 3. Animate on the finished VO.** Drive the approved still with the full VO track in HeyGen. Natural continuous micro-movement, blinks, small head tilts, light hand-talk on the mic hand. No exaggerated gestures. Key clean, no green fringe.

**Step 4. Comp.** Cut out and place per the shot map. Roughly a quarter of frame height.

### Cutout choreography

The reference moves the creator between nearly every segment rather than the single house jump at the 3/4 mark. **Mirror the reference.** The movement is what keeps a static frame alive, and on a countdown it also marks each new number. Positions are in the shot map, and she drops out entirely for the load-in beats (10 to 13) so the demonstration owns the screen, then returns for the set-down.

### Despill

Neutral garment against green. Despill at 0.5 renders the white tank **pink**. Keep it low and check the tank in pixels, not on the read.

## 9. Standing QA gates

- **Load test.** Would this avatar put that in the bag on this occasion, and does it match what the VO says is inside?
- **Placement test.** Would a real owner set the bag down there? Her own floor, a chair, a bench, a counter, a car seat, yes. A public floor, never.
- **In-point test.** Cut on the frame where the action happens. Never reuse one in-point across a shot list, and verify on the composed timeline, not a source contact sheet.
- **Product truth on every frame:** brushed wool body, leather-wrapped handles, belt across the front and back faces only, each belt end hanging free with its own gold cap, clean wool side gusset, aged gold hardware, no logos, stands upright, open top.
- **Every cutaway is face-free** except the creator layer. A second woman's face breaks the illusion that this is her own footage.
- Zero em dashes, zero "she/her" for the bag, no competitor on screen, product on screen the entire runtime.
- **Kill the pre-order line the day Colette flips to in stock.**
