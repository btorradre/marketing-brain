## The angle

**Golden nugget:** bags are sold on how they look empty and lived in full. She is showing the full version first, because the honest question isn't "is it nice", it's "will all of this actually go in".

**Verdict she leads with:** this is everything I carry on a normal Tuesday.

**The one physical payoff:** the shape not changing once it's loaded.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** it opens on a flat lay of her possessions with **no bag in frame for the first thirteen seconds**. It is the only ad in the family that withholds the product through the entire hook. The bag arrives as the answer to a question the objects have already asked.

**Swap test:** passes. The named object list is generic on purpose, but the payoff ("structured, so once it's full the shape doesn't change") is ours and it is what the objects were set up to test.
**Six-month test:** passes. "A normal Tuesday" is deliberately not a season.

## Edit notes

- **Beats 2 to 8 are seven cuts in ten and a half seconds**, 1.2–2.0s each. This is the fastest sequence in the family and it should feel like a list being counted off. Do not let any of them breathe.
- All seven inserts must be **the same overhead angle, the same bed, the same light**. They are crops and sections of the same shoot. If they don't match, the sequence reads as stock.
- **No bag in frame at all until beat 9.** Do not let a handle creep into the edge of an insert. The withheld product is the hook.
- Every laptop lid and book cover in this ad is **blank**. No logos, no titles, no stickers.
- Beat 13 is the payoff and takes the longest hold. Beat 12 (hand reaching in) must be cut so the bag is already full before 13.

## Self-audit

**Six-month test:** passes. "A normal Tuesday" was chosen specifically so no season, holiday or calendar beat appears anywhere. The object list is year-round.
**Swap test:** passes at the payoff. The object list alone would suit any tote, which is why the ad does not end there; it ends on structure under load, which is ours.
**Compliance:** live price, ship date, decision register, no possession claim ("is going in", present intent, not "has been"). No fibre claim, no lining claim, no competitor, no personification.
