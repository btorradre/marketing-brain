## The angle

**Golden nugget:** she has learned that soft and structured are usually a trade. Soft bags collapse. Structured bags are stiff and cold to the hand. She has been choosing between the two for years without knowing that was the choice she was making, and the brushed wool is the thing that refuses it.

**Verdict she leads with:** the reason I went with this one is that it's both.

**The one physical payoff:** the thumb pressing into the felt and finding give, cut straight against the flat square wall standing on its own.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** the reference says "it's wool" as a reason it goes with clothes. This one makes the wool a structural argument rather than a styling one, which is a different and harder claim.

**Swap test:** passes. Brushed wool with give that still holds a flat wall on all four sides is a Colette-specific combination.
**Six-month test:** passes. Wool is stated as a material property, never as a seasonal one. **No autumn or winter word appears anywhere in this script.**

## Voiceover

> Soft usually means it collapses, and structured usually means it's stiff. The reason I went with this one is that it's both. This is the Colette from Velantra. Leather belt across the front, leather wrapped handles, aged gold hardware, no logo. Nineteen point seven inches across, and it fits a thirteen inch laptop. It's a hundred and nineteen, and they ship the first run in early October.
