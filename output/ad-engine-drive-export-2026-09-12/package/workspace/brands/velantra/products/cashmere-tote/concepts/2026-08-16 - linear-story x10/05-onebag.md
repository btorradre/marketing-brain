## The angle

**Golden nugget:** the morning transfer ritual. Moving the same eight things between three bags every day, and the morning you get to the car without your keys because of it. The bag isn't the problem she's solving. The five minutes and the low-grade dread are.

**Verdict she leads with:** the thing that finally got me was the size.

**The one physical payoff:** she lifts one already-loaded bag off the bench and leaves. No transfer, no checking.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** the reference is about how the bag looks with things. This one is about a routine disappearing. It is the only ad in the family whose payoff is an absence of activity.

**Swap test:** passes. 19.7 across by 7.1 deep, everything at once, open top so she can see it, is our specification.
**Six-month test:** passes. A morning routine is not a season.

## Edit notes

- Beats 5, 6, 7 and 8 are the load-in run, 1.4–2.3s each. Four cuts, four named things. Fast. This is the only place in the ad with any density and it should feel slightly hectic against the calm of beats 1 and 10.
- **Beat 10 is the payoff and it is defined by what does not happen.** No transfer, no double-check, no pat of the pockets. If the edit lets her hesitate, cut a different take.
- Beats 1, 10 and 11 are one location and one continuous morning. Shoot them as a set and keep the light identical.
- Beat 8 shows a child's hand. Keep it to a hand and a forearm only, no face, no second person established.
- The bag is **already loaded in beat 1**. Every "already full" beat must stay downstream of the pack block in any re-cut, or the bag reads full, then empty, then filling.
