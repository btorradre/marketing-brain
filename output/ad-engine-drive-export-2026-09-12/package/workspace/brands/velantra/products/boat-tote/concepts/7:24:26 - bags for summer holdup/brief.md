## Why the reference works
Collection flex format. The opening cluster shot (one hand gripping the whole summer lineup)
is the pattern interrupt; then each bag gets ~1s of screen time, same wall, same hand, hard
cuts. It reads as a personal collection, not a catalog, and the viewer self-selects the color
they would keep.

## Our adaptation
Hook = one hand holding the three Sofia straw totes clustered by their handles (renderable
and auditable; the boat totes join in the singles). Beats 1-6: each bag held up against warm
white wall + cream linen curtain, raw iPhone aesthetic. Overlay "bags for summer" lowercase,
clean sans, top center.
