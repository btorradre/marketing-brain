# Facebook Primary Text, CBS ad. BRAND AWARENESS copy set

**Pairs with:** the CBS founder-to-camera creative in this folder (`Script-Draft-V2-CBS-Creed.md`, V4a current).
**Job:** top-of-funnel brand awareness for Velantra. The subject of this copy is the company and what it believes, not a bag. CBS is third-party credibility, 30,000+ is scale, the collection is the soft landing.
**What is deliberately absent:** product specs, colorways, a named hero product, price, urgency, hard CTA. Every one of those turns this into a product ad.
**Truth locks:** CBS claim is truthful (real KCAL segment, aired, clip on file). Host quote stays paraphrased until the 4:53 ear-check. No origin claims, no competitor names, no BNPL, no personifying the bags.
**Destination:** collection page, not a PDP.

---

## A. The creed (RECOMMENDED)

I'm Brooks. I started Velantra because I grew up around things that were built to last.

Racing boats after school. Canvas, brass and good leather that never stayed new and were never meant to. They got better.

Somewhere along the way bags stopped being made that way, so we started making them that way.

Over 30,000 women carry one now. Last week CBS held one up on the news and said it comes a little weathered, a little worn in, and that is the look.

She got it. That is Velantra.

Brooks, Founder

---

## B. One idea, scaled

Velantra started with one idea. A bag should be worth carrying for twenty years, not one summer.

Over 30,000 bags later, the idea has not changed. Every bag we make is built for one woman with somewhere to be, and by the time it is finished we know her name.

Margot. Camille. Eleanor. Sofia.

Last week CBS News put one of them on television and called the worn-in canvas exactly what we always hoped someone would call it. The look.

Built for decades, not seasons.

---

## C. Three generations, warmest open

I learned what a good bag is worth from three generations of women in my family. My grandmother, my mother, my sister. Not one of them replaced hers every season.

That is the whole reason Velantra exists.

Over 30,000 bags in now, and last week CBS put one on the news. The host said it comes a little weathered, a little worn in, and that is the look.

She got it.

Brooks, Founder

---

## D. Shortest cut, for feed and Reels overlay pairing

30,000 bags. One idea behind all of them. They are supposed to get better the harder you carry them.

Last week CBS said the same thing on the news, about a bag that was a little weathered and a little worn in. That is the look.

That has always been the idea behind Velantra.
