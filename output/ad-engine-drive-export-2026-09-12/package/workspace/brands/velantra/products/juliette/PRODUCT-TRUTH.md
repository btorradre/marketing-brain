## Construction (confirmed from photos)
The bag is **completely unstructured**. It holds no shape, sags under its own weight and
creases. It is handmade and slightly irregular. It is NOT a tidy structured retail tote,
and any frame that reads stiff, symmetrical or catalogue-clean is a reject.

- **Lower two thirds:** suede, in a warm GOLDEN SAND tone. Not orange, not ginger, not
  tobacco. Broad flat front face that sags. Soft rounded bottom corners.
- **Upper third:** a tall, soft, floppy panel of DARK COOL GREY-BROWN leather (dark taupe,
  almost charcoal-brown, NOT warm chocolate) wrapping the whole top edge front and back.
  Its top edge rolls and folds outward under its own weight.
- **The bottom edge of that leather panel is a STRAIGHT HORIZONTAL SEAM** across the suede.
  There is no fringe, zigzag, sawtooth or pointed border. Saying "notches" or "points" in a
  prompt makes the engine render a fringe across the full width. Do not use those words.
- **On the front of the leather panel:** ONE horizontal leather strap running the full
  width with shallow pointed ends, passing through FOUR narrow flat vertical leather keeper
  tabs, TWO of which carry a small flat metal bar. ONE domed rivet at the exact centre of
  the strap. Nothing fastens.
- **Two thin leather pull straps** hang free from beneath the strap, down the front suede
  near the centre, each ending in a cut point.
- **Four leather corner caps** at the base, each with one small metal stud.
- **There are NO studs, snaps or metal anywhere on the side panels or the middle of the
  suede.** An earlier build invented round side snaps. They do not exist.
- **No logo, stamp or plaque anywhere on the bag.**

## Opening mechanism
**The bag does not close.** The leather panel is architectural, not functional: the strap,
the keeper tabs and the rivet are decorative and nothing fastens. The top is a plain open
mouth, and the panel is soft enough to fall open on its own.

Prompt-ready block, paste verbatim into every generation:

> The top of the bag is COMPLETELY OPEN. There is no flap, no zip, no turnlock, no magnet
> and no clasp of any kind. The soft leather panel wraps the top edge and stays open,
> folding outward under its own weight. The horizontal strap and the keeper tabs across the
> front of the panel are decorative only and never fasten to anything.

## Carry truth
Two rolled tubular leather top handles threaded through slots in the collar. Short drop.
**Hand-carry and forearm only.** The handles cannot reach a shoulder.
Never prompt a shoulder or crossbody carry: the engine will invent a strap to bridge it.
