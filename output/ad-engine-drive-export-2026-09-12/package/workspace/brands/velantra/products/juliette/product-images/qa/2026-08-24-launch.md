Judged in house order: photoreal first, then product truth, then composition.

## Sourcing note (DMCA)

`imagery.md` says to seed the hero i2i on the source reference. `laws.md` §5 forbids a
competitor photo as the i2i seed for any asset that ships, and Velantra has a live DMCA
history. Laws is the gate, so the master frame was generated **clean-room** from the
written identity block with NO competitor photo attached, then eyeball-QA'd against their
photos. Every colorway derives from our own approved master. Zero derivation.

## Checks passed on every shipped frame

- **3D-render tell:** none. Real suede nap, real leather pebbling, real creasing, honest
  shadows. No Blender/Octane/Keyshot signature, no plastic surfaces.
- **Opening mechanism:** open top on every frame. No flap, zip, turnlock or clasp invented
  anywhere. The belt strap and tabs stay decorative and unfastened.
- **Carry truth:** hand-carry only in all four lifestyle frames. No invented shoulder or
  crossbody strap, which was the named risk.
- **Scale anchor:** holds. In `lifestyle` the bag runs mid-thigh to below the knee and is
  as wide as the carrier's torso, matching the source photograph of a person carrying it.
- **Metal:** antique brass on every fitting in every frame. Zero silver or nickel, which
  is the deliberate divergence from the source's mixed metals (Brooks, 2026-08-24).
- **Logos:** none anywhere.
- **Colour:** Stone reads cool grey-taupe, not beige or tan. Chocolate reads deep dark
  brown, not caramel. Black reads true black, not charcoal or navy. Explicit negative
  guards were required in each recolour prompt to get there.
- **Recolour fidelity:** in all three lifestyle recolours the model, wardrobe, pose, hand
  position, street and lighting are unchanged. Only the bag moved.
