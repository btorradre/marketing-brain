# Frame QA — The Vivienne

## v4, 2026-08-22 — SHIPPED. Hero approved by Brooks, one defect fixed.

### The defect Brooks caught on the v3 hero

The two round handle cutouts in the flap rendered as **transparent windows showing the dark
green background wall through them**. Physically impossible: the flap hangs in front of the
bag's body, so those holes show the shadowed back-panel leather. Fixed by regenerating from the
approved v3 frame with an explicit occlusion instruction. Everything else about the approved
frame was held.

**New standing rule, now verbatim in the product-scale skill:** every cutout, strap slot and
keyhole shows what is IMMEDIATELY BEHIND it, never the scene. Applies to the flap holes, the
oval plate's keyhole and the end plate's oblong slot.

### Hardware changed to the Weekender's system

Brooks, 2026-08-22: "use the same hardware as the weekender since this bag is shaped for that."
The remaining angles were generated with the Weekender's real-product stills wired as references
(`LC-macro-turnlock-flap.jpg` for the closure macro, `LC-closed-side-strap-detail.jpg` for the
side and gusset, `LC-hand-scale-front.jpg` for scale). The TikTok footage confirms the real
Atorie uses the same family, so this is a standardisation rather than a swap.

### Checked on all 16

- **Occlusion:** every flap cutout, strap slot and keyhole shows shadowed leather. No background
  visible through anything.
- **Structure:** soft and slouchy on every frame. Body bows, sides fold, base spreads.
- **Surface:** matte-to-satin with real pore grain. No crazing, no blotching, no plastic.
- **Hardware:** Weekender system. Oval keyhole plate over the knurled post at centre, two flat
  staples of two parallel bars, strap tips with oblong-slot end plates, keyhole cutouts in the
  flap, leather key bell, side eyelet. Warm brass throughout, no silver.
- **Braided whip-stitch:** discrete interlocking strands, never a soft repeating ripple.
- **Two-tone:** Chocolate has contrast cognac straps. Cognac, Black and Olive are tonal.
- **Black** carries form on grain and soft highlights, brass still reads. **Olive** reads green.
- **Carry truth:** lifestyle is a hand carry on the top handles. No impossible shoulder carry.
- **Scale:** lifestyle sells the 38 cm width against a body.
- **Logos:** none anywhere.
