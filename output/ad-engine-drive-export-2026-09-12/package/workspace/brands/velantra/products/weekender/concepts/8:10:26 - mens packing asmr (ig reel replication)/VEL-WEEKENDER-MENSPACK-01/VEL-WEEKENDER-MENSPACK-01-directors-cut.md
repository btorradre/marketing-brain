## Autopsy (the reference)

* **Angle:** "everything I actually pack fits in this one bag," proven item by item rather than claimed.
* **Messaging:** there is no copy. The claims ladder is physical: capacity (clothes) -> organization (kits and pouches) -> lifestyle taste (headphones, hat, cognac leather goods) -> closure -> pairs with real travel (rides the carry-on) -> walk out.
* **Emotional engine:** ASMR calm and order first (every item nests), then quiet taste signaling (the items are all considered, muted, expensive-looking), then completion satisfaction (it all fits, it closes), then readiness (walk out).
* **Promise:** stated, one bag swallows a real trip's worth of stuff. Unspoken identity upgrade: the man whose things are few, good, and in order.
* **Belief channeling:** viewer believes soft duffels are disorganized pits. New belief installed: a structured bag with real volume makes packing a ritual, not a chore.
* **Golden nugget:** packing as a competence ritual. The pleasure is watching order accumulate; the bag is the container that makes the ritual possible.
* **Beat jobs:** arrival (bag enters the room) -> empty reveal (the volume is the hook) -> accumulation x N (each item is one proof of capacity and taste) -> full-state payoff -> closure -> exit.

**Engine swap note:** the reference's payoff spike is a gadget surprise (it rides the suitcase). Ours is quiet structural confidence, consistent with the timelessness spine and with how MENSLC-01 made the same swap.

**Sameness risk, addressed up front (Brooks's 8/09 rejection of five near-identical packing wides):** this genre is deliberately repetitive, but the repetition is the locked overhead rhythm, not lazy re-framing. Every insert changes the item (navy / cream+charcoal / black leather / cream cotton / steel / matte black / tan / cognac: the palette rotates every cut), the fill state visibly advances every cut, and the run is broken once by the closer slip-pocket insert (S10). If it still reads samey in the edit, inserts drop from the middle (S06/S07/S11 are the candidates) without breaking the accumulation logic.

**Continuity system for the overhead run (the make-or-break of this concept):** nothing chains. Two locked blocks hold the run together across independently authored stills: the OVERHEAD camera-state block (verbatim in every top-down prompt, per framing-is-state) and the CONTENTS map (every already-packed item restated verbatim at its fixed position in every later prompt; items never move once placed). Small settle differences between cuts read as the natural jump-cut grammar of the genre.

**Men's segment rules honored:** no gendering anywhere, no "man bag." No logos on the bag (and the no-lettering negatives ride every prompt). Materials appear as themselves on camera (leather, canvas, brass), no copy to get them wrong. Hand carry only. No competitor luggage in frame at all (the aluminum carry-on stays in the reference).

## Production (the MENSLC-validated stack; no Seedance anywhere)

1. **Stills:** GPT Image 2 i2i on kie (`gpt-image-2-image-to-image`, 9:16, 2K, 10cr), 3 variants per scene, 45 total, sequential with retries. Every scene wires real-product stills from `real-product-2026-08-08/` (open scenes: `LC-open-flap-inner-face-interior.jpg` + `LC-open-interior-slip-pocket.jpg`); S15 adds `creator-ref.jpg` (MENS-01).
2. **Frame-QA subagent pass** on all 45 against the real-product stills + the open/closed calibrations; pick per scene on photoreal, then product truth, then composition; also check the CONTENTS map (right items, right positions) and latent pseudo-text on props.
3. **Animate:** Google Omni i2v from each pick, one action per clip, one re-roll budget; any hands-free scene that drifts (S02, S12, S13) falls back to Ken Burns from the approved still.
4. **Drift QA:** 3-frame drift check per clip; open-bag calibrations; no invented hardware; contents did not teleport inside a clip.
5. **Assemble:** ChatCut, hard cuts on targets above, stock music, clip audio muted, closing card, deliver the finished cut.

**Build tooling:** `_build/` (scenes.py, blocks.py, gen_stills.py, animate_omni.py), inherited from MENSLC-01 with the new OVERHEAD + CONTENTS blocks. `python3 _build/scenes.py S08` prints any assembled prompt.
