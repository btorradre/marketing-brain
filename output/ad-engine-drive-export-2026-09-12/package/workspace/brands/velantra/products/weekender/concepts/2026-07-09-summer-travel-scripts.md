# Velantra Weekender Relaunch — "The Structured Travel Bag, Perfect for the Weekend"
Scripts pack — 2026-07-10 (rev. 5 — competitor-comparison line removed, Velantra-only focus)

## Positioning

Product name stays **the Weekender** — the angle is that it's **a structured travel bag, perfect for the weekend**: the same structured, heritage-luxury silhouette and hardware language Velantra already uses on the Straw Birkin and Inspired Birkin lines, applied to an actual travel bag instead of a tote.

**Standing rule (2026-07-10): Velantra ads never compare to other brands or other products, in any form** — no wound beat built on "other bags," no price comparisons, no competitor mentions, not even generic/unbranded ones. The ad stands entirely on what Velantra itself offers. See `feedback_velantra_no_competitor_comparisons`.

**Every script below now carries the same core facts and wording as the finalized talking-head script** (`2026-07-09-aiugc-talking-head-greenscreen-brief.md`):
- Hook: it's the perfect summer travel bag, a structured top-handle silhouette, real brass hardware, but actually built for a weekend trip.
- Proof: holds its shape even packed full; thrown in the trunk every weekend this summer and it still looks brand new; fits everything — two outfits, shoes, toiletries.
- Product fact: comes in two different colors and three different sizes.
- Close: summer sale right now, colorways go fast.
- CTA: "I left the link below."

**Size for all generation (2026-07-10):** every UGC video below depicts the **Medium** (16in W x 13in H x 6.5in D — "an overnight trip or an oversized everyday carry, slides under an airline seat," per the live PDP). Do not depict Medium Large (18x14.5x7in, "two to three days of clothing") or Large (20x15.5x8in) capacity in these pieces — the "two outfits, shoes, toiletries" packed-open beat should read as an overnight/weekend load in the Medium, not a multi-day pack. The only place all three sizes appear together is the dedicated size-comparison flat lay (2C / B-Roll 6).
