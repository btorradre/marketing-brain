## The mechanism, and why it is a mechanism and not a name-drop

The problem beat states a real bind: the duffel held everything and looked like a gym bag,
the nice bags held nothing. Capacity and shape have been mutually exclusive for her.

"Birkin-inspired shape, scaled up to a travel bag" resolves that bind in one sentence.
It is a structural claim about our own bag, and it explains every downstream benefit
the script already makes: why it holds its shape, why it does not read as luggage, why
people ask if it is designer. The mechanism sits directly between the problem and the
proof stack, which is where a mechanism belongs.

It is never a comparison. Hermes is never named, no Birkin price is quoted, the words
dupe, replica, alternative and knockoff never appear, and the bag is never ranked against
anything. The line describes our silhouette's design lineage and then immediately stacks
our own physical facts, which is the house pattern of leading with a verdict and proving
it with specifics.

252 words. **TTS input feeds the brand as `Vell-Ahn-Trah`**. Plain spelling returns
"Volantra" / "Velen Trail" and kills the take.

## Compliance

| Law | How it is held |
|---|---|
| Creator never speaks as the brand | "they sell it", "they're running a sale", "go look at Velantra". First person only for her own use |
| No competitor comparison | Hermes never named, no Birkin price, no dupe/replica/alternative language, nothing ranked against anything |

| No product-first TOF open | Beat 1 opens on the viewer's situation, not the product |
| Price and colour count | Verified against the live PDP on 8/21 |
| No em dashes | None |
| One-take audio | One continuous ElevenLabs render, lip-sync driven from that file, never chained |

## Production, as built

Three creator variations share one VO, so the bed, the captions and every cut land at the
identical timecode and only the creator changes. That makes it a clean creator-only test.

| Stage | What ran |
|---|---|
| VO | ElevenLabs `Woman Over 30` (`XG3boWHon0IqPdBw55pB`), `eleven_v3` creative preset. Three takes, all STT-gated on word timestamps. Take 2 shipped, 79.20s |
| Avatars | HeyGen `avatar_v` via `POST /v3/videos` with `audio_asset_id`, 9:16, 1080p. Whitney look `f9c4383eea1949c79b81c0ad3af3f8e7`, Brooks `61a57236a8fc4662af2ff35ec81c2a22`, Anna `abb163c6fe0d4880863c30a2323394ca`. About 237 api credits per 79s render |
| Whitney key | She renders on a native green screen. **The green is `0x0AA442`, NOT the `0x007A28` from the DC and BLK builds**, so sweep it every time. Alpha is stable 0.06 to 0.14 with the cliff at 0.18. Shipped `chromakey=0x0AA442:0.10:0.02` plus `despill=type=green:mix=0.15`, measured at 0% fringe with her cream shirt unshifted |
| Brooks and Anna | No green screen (car and hallway), so both were matted by fal `fal-ai/ben/v2/video`. **It returns the subject on BLACK, not with an alpha channel**, despite the output being named .webm (it is really h264 mp4), so the assembler probes the codec rather than trusting the extension |
| Anna key | `colorkey=0x000000:0.01:0.02`. **The threshold must stay this tight because her hair is dark brown**: at 0.10 chunks of hair key out, and by 0.28 her hair and even her eyes are gone. At 0.01 the background is fully removed and the hair survives |

| PiP | **32% width**, bottom-left, bottom-anchored. 44% was inherited from the men's cut and put captions across the creator's face; the reference ad's creator is about 28% |
| Captions | Pillow-drawn cards composited as one VP9 `yuva420p` alpha video. Max 4 words, flushed at terminal punctuation. **This machine's ffmpeg has no libass and no drawtext**, so subtitle filters are not available |
| ⚠️ Caption respell | Burned captions are built from the Scribe transcript, and Scribe spells the brand by ear as "Velentra". Two cards would have shipped a misspelled brand name. A respell map now runs over the STT text before drawing. The audio itself was always correct |
| Offer beat | Headless Chrome at `--window-size=500,3200 --force-device-scale-factor=3`, panned in ffmpeg. **Start the pan below the gallery, at the title**, because the carousel ignores the variant parameter |

## The cold-TOF cut (`-TOF-`), 63.4s

Built 8/21 on Brooks's go-ahead. Four lines trimmed, which drops two beats (the
open-and-packed structure shot and the packed-beside-the-roller shot) and takes the
runtime from 79.2s to **63.4s**, inside the format's normal range.

**What came out, and why none of it was load-bearing:**

| Cut line | Why it survives removal |

## Flagged for Brooks

1. **The Birkin line is already live on our own PDP.** The product description reads
   "The structured, Birkin-inspired silhouette, sized for a weekend away." So the ad is
   not introducing a new claim, it is making the ad congruent with the page it clicks to.
   The standing house law says Birkin stays out of Velantra copy entirely. That law and
   the live PDP already contradict each other, and this ad follows the PDP per your
   direction. Worth resolving the law text so the next build is not re-litigating it.
   Separately: the word Birkin must never enter a **generation prompt**, because the kie runner's
   claims-grep blocks it. It lives in the VO and caption layer only, which this format
   supports cleanly because the b-roll is already shot.

2. **Black is a pre-order.** The PDP says Black ships mid September 2026 and that Light
   Chocolate, Army Green and Dark Chocolate ship now. "Four colors" is true, but if a
   buyer picks Black expecting it to ship, that is a support ticket. Recommend the ad
   copy carries the ship split rather than putting it in her mouth, same call as the
   sale line on BLK-01.

3. **The live PDP description contains an em dash**, in the clause running from "sized for a
   weekend away" into "and made in limited quantities". Minor, but it breaks the house rule on
   our own page.
