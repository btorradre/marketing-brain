## v2 corrects v1

v1 cut from the airport to real product footage shot on a kitchen counter, then to four rapid cuts of four different colorways in four different places. Brooks killed it: it should be one continuous scene of her in the airport.

He is right. Continuity is the entire UGC illusion and v1 traded it away for asset safety. The reference proves the point. Six beats, one woman, one outfit, one bag, one journey: stands at the gate, sits at the gate, opens it on her lap, stows it on the plane, walks the terminal. Not one cutaway.

**The real 4K kitchen footage is out of this ad.** It stays available for statics and PDP. It does not belong in a continuous airport scene.

---

## The three defects, and how v2 fixes them without leaving the scene

**1. Forty percent frozen photo.** Seedance cannot animate an open bag, a hand on hardware, or a walking carry, so v0 froze all of them.
**Fix: Omni, not stills.** Omni is video-native and is the one engine that has held this bag's fold-back mechanism (Brooks-approved 7/11, frame-QA passed). It takes the open bag, the capacity beat, the bin and both walking beats. Seedance keeps only what it is good at: two talking takes with the bag still and front-on.

**2. The bag was mutated in every live clip.** Star medallion where the turn lock should be, straps and key bell gone, wrong colorway in the bin.
**Fix: the mechanism block goes on Omni, which holds it, and the one hardware-heavy talking beat is gated at the keyframe.** The bag has to be large, sharply lit and high-contrast in that first frame or the shot fails no matter how still it is.

**3. The same shot ran twice.** The seated take returned after eighteen seconds of it, and the last 29s was one corridor three times.
**Fix: eight beats, eight distinct moments, moving forward through one journey.** Nothing repeats because the story does not double back.

## Script: 99 words, 35s, ~170 wpm

> If you take a lot of short trips, this is the one I'd get. This is the Eleanor Weekender from Velantra. It's Birkin-inspired, scaled up to a travel bag, structured, real brass hardware, no logo anywhere on it. The whole flap folds back in one piece, so you can see everything at once. Three days of clothes, my laptop, my chargers, all of it. I've flown with it all summer and it goes straight in the overhead bin. It comes in four colors, and they're running a sale right now. The colors go fast. I left the link below.

One continuous VO take across the full 35s, airport ambient under it the whole way. Never spliced per beat.

## Production laws for this build

1. **Continuity lock.** One creator, one outfit (cream knit tee, cream wide-leg trousers, white sneakers, camel coat over the arm), one bag in Light Chocolate, one journey. Every beat generates from the same creator reference and the same product reference. Zero cutaways to any other location, bag or colorway.
2. **Zero frozen stills.** If a beat cannot be produced as motion it gets cut and the VO line goes with it. Never a Ken Burns photo.
3. **No walking carry on Seedance.** Beats 6 and 7 are Omni. That is the shot Seedance destroys: handles vanish, clasp plates elongate into gold spears, straps dangle below the bottom edge.
4. **No open bag on Seedance.** Beats 3 and 4 are Omni, with the opening mechanism block pasted verbatim.
5. **Beat 5 is the highest generation risk.** Three rolls, pick clean, frame-QA against `real-product-2026-08-08/LC-closed-front-unfastened.jpg`. If all three fail, cut the beat and run 30s.
6. **One continuous VO take**, lipsync the two Seedance takes to it.
7. **Single locked 24fps CFR, one encode pass.** The retired files declared five different frame rates with a hitch at every join.
8. **Captions burn from this script, never from ASR.** The transcriber writes "Valentra."
9. **Frame-QA subagent pass** on every generated frame before it animates.
