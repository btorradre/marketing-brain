### Calibration ruling: the front-handle "droop" is REAL
All open-bag variants show the front handle lying forward over the front face. The QA
checklist (mechanism block: "both handles stand upright") called this a disqualifier, but
`real-product-2026-08-08/LC-open-flap-inner-face-interior.jpg` — the wired i2i reference —
shows the physical bag's front handle held DOWN/forward by the hand while open. The rear
handle stands upright on the folded-back flap; the front handle does not self-support.
Footage outranks the written block, so for OPEN stills: front handle resting forward over
the band/upper canvas = PASS. Still FAIL: a handle stretched into a long strap, reaching
the bag's bottom edge, flattened into a strip, or fused without teardrop bases.
(The upright-handles line in the mechanism block was calibrated on the Omni fold-back
look; flag for a skill-file annotation.)
