## 2. Why the reference works

The full teardown is in the Sofia brief and applies identically. The short version, because it drives every choice below:

**The "Ask me a question" sticker is the hook.** It reframes an ad as a reply to a request — she isn't selling, she's answering. Ad-resistance never fires. It borrows social proof without a testimonial (a stranger asked for a full review of this bag by name, so demand is implied rather than claimed), and it qualifies the viewer as already-aware, which is exactly what a retargeting creative wants.

**The B-roll block does the touching for you.** At 6 seconds it cuts from her face to a flat lay and a hand slowly stroking the material. Nobody can feel a bag through a phone, so she feels it on the viewer's behalf.

**The criteria frame is a Trojan horse.** She defines what a good everyday bag *should* be, then the product wins the test she just authored.

**Value-stacking closes it.** One bag doing three jobs is three bags at one price. The ROI math lands without a price ever being spoken. Then it ends on feeling, not a CTA — the Shop Now button carries the ask, and a spoken CTA would break the correspondence frame the sticker built.

## 4. Final script

*Passed ai-copy-blacklist audit 2026-07-24 — no parallel repetition stacks, no false-contrast stacks, no triple short-sentence stacks, no rhetorical transitions, no hedging.*

> Okay so, I have taken this on every trip since March, the Eleanor Weekender from Velantra. The leather goes all the way around the top, and the handles are anchored right into it, so nothing is ever pulling on the canvas. That is the part I did not expect. When you travel enough, you want one bag that fits the overhead bin and still keeps its shape. It is one size, eighteen by fourteen and a half by seven, two to three days of clothes. Every travel bag I owned collapsed when you unpacked it. This one just stands there. It is the flight bag, the gym bag, and the Friday bag. I just love that I stopped packing a suitcase.

**121 words · ~39s at 3.1 words/sec** (the reference's measured delivery rate).

Note on *"Every travel bag I owned collapsed when you unpacked it"* — written as **her own past bags**, lived experience. No brand named or implied, no category wound. The dimension recital is deliberate: reciting a spec from memory is what a real owner does, and it's the authority beat that replaces the reference's "LWG certified" credential.

## 11. QA gates before spend

1. **Pre-animation gate** — every keyframe passes a fresh-context frame-QA audit on the exact crop fed to the engine, BEFORE it is animated. This matters most on S11 and S12.
2. **Per-clip audit** — extract ≥3 frames (start / middle / end) from every clip containing the bag.
3. **Regenerate on sight — open-bag frames:** handles rooted in canvas with small stitched tabs · leather band missing or shrunk to a thin trim line · a flap-shaped panel on the FRONT (tab sections, scalloped edges, turn-lock pocket) · malformed or missing flap behind the opening · a plain featureless fold-back flap (its inner face must show its two round handle holes, strap slots and small gold plate) · **missing front handle** · ANY zipper on the bag · embossed text or lettering.
4. **Regenerate on sight — all frames:** silver hardware (it is gold) · invented braiding, wrapping or woven texture on the handles · buckles, trim or geometry not on the reference · closure state changing mid-clip.
5. Cap 3 attempts per clip. Still failing → change the blocking, then escalate. Do **not** re-roll an open bag through Seedance ref-mode.
6. Voice consistency S01 vs S10 vs S18 back to back — same register, same intensity, no decay into monotone.
7. Runtime lands 37–40s.
