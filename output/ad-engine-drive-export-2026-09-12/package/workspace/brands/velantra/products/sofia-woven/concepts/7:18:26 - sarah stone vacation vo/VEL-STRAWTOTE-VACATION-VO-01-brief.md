## Why the reference works (what we are copying)

1. **First-person ownership arc.** "I bought this bag for one vacation, and now I use it everywhere" reframes a vacation purchase as an everyday staple in the first two seconds. It pre-answers the exact objection that kills straw-bag purchases: "I'll only use it once."
2. **A new visual every ~3 seconds, and every visual proves the line being spoken.** Capacity line → items going in on camera. Versatility line → two locations back to back. Color line → full colorway spread. Nothing is decoration.
3. **Real-people iPhone energy.** Mixed footage sources (beach clips, doorway try-ons, vanity close-ups, one talking-head anchor, a website screen-record) make it read as compiled genuine footage, not a produced ad.
4. **Review-scroll proof beat** right before the CTA — third-party validation at the moment of decision.
5. **Yellow-keyword captions** carry the script for sound-off viewers.

---

## Voiceover script (full, ~125 words, reads at 45s)

Record as ONE continuous take. Warm American female, late 20s to 30s, casual UGC delivery, like she is recommending it to a friend. Slight smile in the voice. Commas and periods only, no dramatic pauses.

> I bought this bag for one vacation, and now I carry it everywhere.
> I wanted a summer bag that didn't look flimsy or feel like a beach toy, and that's when I found the Velantra Straw Tote.
> The hand woven seagrass gives it that vacation feel, but what surprised me was how much it actually holds.
> Phone, sunscreen, my book, even a water bottle, everything goes in and the bag still keeps its shape.
> The leather handles and that belted flap keep everything secure, at the beach or out at dinner.
> And with seven colorways, it somehow works with every outfit I pack.
> It's structured, lightweight, and honestly makes every errand feel a little more like a getaway.
> And judging by the reviews, I'm definitely not the only one loving it.
> Grab the Straw Tote before your colorway sells out.

**Compliance (non-negotiable):**
- NEVER say or show "handmade in Italy," "European," "US-made," or any origin claim. "Hand woven seagrass" and "full-grain leather" are the approved material phrases.
- No competitor names, no comparison to other bags or brands. The Straw Tote is the only bag in the ad.
- Banned words: premium, luxury (as an adjective), affordable luxury, designer-inspired, elevate, treat yourself, exclusive.
- Do not invent review counts or star ratings. Shot 16 must screen-record the real PDP review section as it exists.

## Edit + finishing spec

- **Captions:** Bold white sans-serif, all caps, center-lower third, one to five words per card, key word per card in yellow (match reference). Captions are burned in, word-timed to VO.
- **VO:** One continuous track, no per-segment files. Music: light, sunny acoustic/tropical bed at low volume under the VO, subtle riser into the end card.
- **Transitions:** Hard cuts by default. Whip-pan blur only at shots 2 and 7. No zoom punch-ins longer than 10 percent.
- **Grade:** Bright, warm, slightly lifted shadows. iPhone-real, not cinematic teal/orange.
- **Logo:** VELANTRA wordmark appears only on the end card (shot 17).
- **Pacing check before delivery:** no single visual holds longer than 3.0s. Shots 12 and 14 are double-cut internally.
