## The format change (2026-08-14)

Brooks: *"the very issue you create is too dull and boring. The voice sounds robotic, and it
obviously looks AI-generated."* He sent a viral organic TikTok as the standard
(`@marynicole__/video/7649196782144032014`).

The problem was not the voice model. It was that the ads DESCRIBED and the reference DEMONSTRATES.
Full teardown in the memory note `feedback-ugc-demonstrate-never-describe`. What changed:

- **Every claim now has a physical demo beat.** "Stands up on its own" is proved by her knuckle
  tapping the side. "Fits so much stuff" is proved by holding a water bottle and sunglasses up
  beside the bag for size comparison. The transcriber picks up the diegetic `[tapping]`, which is
  a good sign the action is really happening.
- **The bag never stops moving:** dangled and swung, tapped, rotated to show the deep side wall,
  size-compared, slid onto the forearm with a body turn, pushed close to the lens.
- **Script rewritten in her register** — messy syntax, "literally", opens mid-thought and flat, no
  hype in line one, brand named late and casually, friend-to-friend close.
- **Handheld camera** that drifts and reframes, slightly crooked, bag breaking frame edges, real
  clutter in every room.

## Script (locked, identical across all five)

> Okay so this is my everyday bag and I'm literally obsessed. Look, it actually stands up on its
> own, it doesn't flop over like every other straw bag I've had. And it fits so much stuff. Like my
> water bottle, my sunglasses, all of it. Every single time I wear this out somebody asks me where
> it's from. It's Velantra. The colors are selling out so fast, they're running an end of summer
> sale right now. Please just go get one.

Verified word-for-word in all five transcripts. "Velantra" transcribes as "Volanta"/"Volanter",
the same STT signature the original winning ad produces, so it is a pronunciation match rather
than a defect.

## The flap failure that got caught and fixed

The first demonstration cut had her **loading items into the bag**. Seedance responded by folding
the flap back and opening the tote in A, C and E, which is the exact banned closure state
(the closure-interaction law: never animate hands working the closure, Seedance reinvents it every
time). Rebuilt with the load beat replaced by a **hold-beside-the-bag size comparison** and a hard
"the bag is closed for the entire clip, the inside is never visible" constraint. All five now hold
the flap folded flat across every sampled frame.
