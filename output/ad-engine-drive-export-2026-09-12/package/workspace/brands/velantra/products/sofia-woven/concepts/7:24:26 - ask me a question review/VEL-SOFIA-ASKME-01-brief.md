## 2. Why the reference works

Read this before touching the prompts. Every structural choice below is load-bearing.

**The "Ask me a question" sticker is the whole hook.** Frame 1 carries an Instagram question-sticker reading *"Give me a full review of the Eden bag"*. This does three things at once, in under a second:

1. **It reframes an ad as a reply.** She is not selling. Someone asked. Ad-resistance never gets a chance to fire, because the format says *this is correspondence, not a pitch*.
2. **It borrows social proof without a testimonial.** If a stranger is asking for a full review of a specific bag by name, the bag already has demand. Implied, never claimed — which is why it lands.
3. **It qualifies the viewer as already-aware.** The sticker names the product and assumes you know it. That's a middle-of-funnel signal: this creative is for retargeting people who bounced, not for cold prospecting. The word *"full"* pre-promises objectivity, so the viewer settles in to be convinced rather than braced to be sold.

**The B-roll block does the touching for you.** Six seconds in, it hard-cuts away from her face to a flat lay and a hand slowly stroking the suede. No one can feel the bag through a phone, so she feels it on the viewer's behalf. Tactile transfer is the single highest-leverage move in a handbag ad, and it's why the middle of this ad is silent on benefits and loud on texture.

**The criteria frame is a Trojan horse.** *"When you're looking for an everyday bag that fits into your routine, you want something that's going to have longevity and great quality."* She isn't describing the bag — she's writing the scorecard the viewer should grade every bag against. The bag then trivially wins the test she just authored.

**Capacity is shown, never claimed.** She opens it and pulls things out. Value-stacking follows: one bag worn three ways is three bags at one price, and the ROI math lands without a price ever being spoken.

**No music. No CTA.** Voice-only reads as a friend's story, not a produced spot. The ad ends on feeling — *"I just absolutely love how you can style this handbag"* — and lets the Shop Now button carry the ask. A CTA here would break the correspondence frame the sticker built.

## 4. Final script

*Passed ai-copy-blacklist audit 2026-07-24 — no parallel repetition stacks, no false-contrast stacks, no triple short-sentence stacks, no rhetorical transitions, no hedging.*

> Okay so, this is the bag I wore all summer, the Sofia woven tote from Velantra. Every one is hand woven, so the straw has this tight, dense feel, and it actually holds its shape. I have had straw bags go soft by August. When it is the bag you reach for every day, you want it to still look good in September. It fits so much more than it looks like. Towel, book, sunscreen, and it still sits flat. The handles sit right on your shoulder, so you are not carrying it all day. Market in the morning, dinner that night, same bag. And it still looks new. I just love how much I actually use it.

**118 words · ~38s at 3.1 words/sec** (the reference's measured delivery rate).

Note on *"I have had straw bags go soft by August"* — written as **her own lived experience**, never "other brands" or "most bags". No brand is named or implied. This is the lawful version of the category-contrast the PDP itself makes ("Structured where other straw bags slouch"). If you want zero category contrast at all, cut that one sentence and the script still runs clean at 109 words / ~35s.

## 6. Locked blocks — paste verbatim, never paraphrase

### 6a. IDENTITY BLOCK (every prompt where the bag is in frame)

> a structured hand woven straw tote in warm sandy caramel, tightly woven straw body with braided cross stitch trim along the edges, a smooth taupe leather flap folded over the top of the bag from the back: the flap is ONE single seamless piece of leather, its front lower edge cut into the silhouette of a wide center panel with 2 squared outer tabs, the leather fully continuous and unbroken between and above these shapes, with exactly 2 narrow slots through which the handles pass, two rolled taupe leather top handles, two taupe leather belt straps crossed on the front, white contrast stitching on all leather edges, no metal hardware, no logos. The leather flap, tabs and belt straps exist ONLY on the FRONT face of the bag, the back face is plain woven straw, no duplicated front detailing on any other face.

### 6b. FLAP MECHANISM BLOCK (mandatory on S03–S11, S15, S16 — anything where the flap is visible in motion or the bag is carrying things)

*Resolved token: the contents noun list reads "the towel, book or sunscreen" instead of the locked block's "the croissant, baguette or flowers" — scene resolution only, exactly like the colourway bracket. **Every word of the construction language is verbatim and must stay that way.** Leaving the original nouns in would hallucinate a croissant into a beach shot.*

> Flap and opening construction: the taupe leather flap is ONE single seamless sheet of leather attached along the top rear edge of the tote and folded all the way forward over the front, lying completely flat. Its front lower edge is cut into the shape of a wide center panel and 2 squared outer tabs, but these are shapes cut into the SAME single sheet, never separate pieces. The leather is continuous and unbroken between the shapes and across the entire top of the bag, including between the two handle slots. The only openings anywhere in the flap are the 2 narrow handle slots. No gap, no seam, no split, no opening exists anywhere else in the flap, and nothing behind or inside the bag is ever visible through the flap. The flap never splits into pieces, never lifts, never stands up, always folded all the way over. When the tote is carrying things, the woven mouth opens BEHIND the flap: the towel, book or sunscreen lean out of the open mouth at the back of the bag, behind the leather flap, never through the flap. The 2 taupe leather belt straps lie crossed in an X over the front below the flap with rounded ends and white contrast stitching, exactly as on the closed reference bag, never threaded through the flap and never wrapped around the contents. No metal hardware anywhere on the bag.

### 6c. VIDEO MECHANISM PIN (append to every Seedance prompt with the bag in frame)

> The leather flap stays ONE single seamless sheet folded all the way over, lying completely flat the entire clip: no gap, seam, or split ever opens anywhere in it, nothing behind it ever shows through it, and its cut edge shapes never separate into pieces. Contents lean out of the mouth behind the flap, never through it.

### 6d. ⛔ CLOSURE-INTERACTION LAW — governs this whole board

No clip in this ad shows hands unfastening the belt straps, lifting the flap, or working the closure. Seedance reinvents closure architecture mid-clip every single time it is asked to. **Every open/closed state change happens across a hard cut between two separately-audited frames.** S11 is the only clip where a hand enters the bag, and it touches only the contents, never the flap or the straps.

## 11. QA gates before spend

1. **Pre-animation gate** — every keyframe passes a fresh-context frame-QA audit on the exact crop fed to the engine, BEFORE it is animated. Never animate an unaudited frame.
2. **Per-clip audit** — extract ≥3 frames (start / middle / end) from every clip containing the bag. Seedance breaks the mechanism mid-motion even from a clean first frame.
3. **Regenerate on sight:** flap split in half · separate floating tabs · any gap or seam in the flap · flap lifted or standing up · anything visible through the flap · belts threaded through the flap or wrapped around contents · belt count ≠ 2 · any metal hardware · front detailing duplicated on the back face · invented braided trim, buckles or wrap-around straps.
4. Cap 3 attempts per clip. Still failing → change the blocking, then escalate.
5. Voice consistency S01 vs S09 vs S17 back to back — same register, same intensity, no decay into monotone.
6. Runtime lands 36–39s.
