## 2. The compliance collision, and how it was resolved

**The reference's engine is competitor comparison.** "Giving Saint Laurent Rive Gauche vibes." "Bottega without Bottega prices." "Under a thousand but look like they cost thousands." Every bag is scored against a luxury house, and the price gap is the payoff.

**Velantra bans exactly that** — `feedback_velantra_no_competitor_comparisons` forbids brand comparisons *and* generic ones ("other bags," "cheap bags that fall apart," price comparisons to unnamed luxury brands). `feedback_velantra_designer_inflation_language` additionally bans "affordable luxury" as customer-facing language and any priced-out framing. A literal 1:1 would violate both on the first line.

**Resolution — the comparison moves inside the product line.** She still edits, still ranks, still scores, still crowns a winner. The field of candidates is **the Sofia's own 5 colorways** instead of 5 rival brands. That is precisely the mechanic the reference runs on ("comparing bags against each other, ultimately choosing one best option") with the rival brands removed. Everything else replicates literally:

| Reference device | Kept? | How |
|---|---|---|
| "I edited through all of them" curation authority | ✅ 1:1 | "I own this bag in every single color" |
| Save-bait hook | ✅ 1:1 | "Save this, I am ranking all of them" |
| Desire → *then* price, never the reverse | ✅ 1:1 | Price lands at beat 2 and again at the close, never opening a beat |
| "So you're welcome" price-as-gift | ✅ verbatim | "That one is 119 dollars, so you are welcome" |
| "10 out of 10" scoring | ✅ 1:1 | Applied to stitching instead of hardware (the Sofia has **no metal hardware**) |
| The "little sister" callback beat | ✅ 1:1 | Caramel → Light Chocolate: literally the same weave in a deeper leather |
| Colour emotion ("something about navy gets me") | ✅ 1:1 | Lightning Orange |
| **The apology / rule-break** | ✅ 1:1 in function | Reference apologises for a price over her stated cap; Camille apologises that **Lady Pink is sold out** — a rule-break that is *true* (variant on DENY, qty 0, verified live) |
| Emotional winner, chosen on feeling | ✅ 1:1 | Caramel: "the one I reach for, every time" |
| Engagement CTA + series tease | ✅ 1:1 | "Tell me your favourite" + a sell-out nudge |
| Designer-house name-drops | ❌ **cut** | Brand law. Replaced with weave/structure proof, which is the strongest lawful substitute |
| "Under a thousand but looks like thousands" | ❌ **cut** | Price comparison to unnamed luxury. Replaced by the flat statement "every color is 119 dollars" |

**One structural change, made deliberately.** In the reference the apology bag *is* the winner. Here it cannot be — the apology bag is sold out, and ending an ad on an unbuyable product is bad direct response. The apology therefore sits at beat 5 and the winner at beat 6. The scarcity generated by Lady Pink transfers to the line, and the CTA points at a colorway you can actually add to cart. This is the only place the beat order deviates.

## 4. Final script

*Passed ai-copy-blacklist self-audit 2026-07-25 — no parallel repetition stacks (checked across segment boundaries), no false-contrast stacks, no rhetorical transitions, no hedging. Commas and periods only, no ellipses, no em dashes.*

> Okay so, I own this bag in every single color. Save this, I am ranking all of them for you.
>
> Starting with cream. Hand woven, so the straw feels tight and dense. That one is 119 dollars, so you are welcome.
>
> Black leather on natural straw takes the whole thing into dinner. It fits so much, and the stitching, 10 out of 10.
>
> You have all seen this caramel one. I love her. But meet her little sister. Same weave, deeper leather.
>
> There is something about orange in July that gets me. It holds its shape all day. Mine has never gone soft.
>
> I could not not include this one. Pink is sold out. I am sorry, it went in about a week.
>
> But the one I reach for is caramel, every time. It goes with everything. Browns, blacks, white dresses, you name it.
>
> Every color is 119 dollars. Tell me which one is your favorite, and go before yours sells out like pink did.

**165 words.** Every clip is sized so its line runs at 1.8-2.0 w/s, Seedance's measured native rate, for a total of **87s**. A real creator would read it in 51s — the gap is the engine, not the copy, and per `reference_seedance_askme_run_learnings` the fix is trimming the script, never overriding on-camera audio.

**60-second alternate cut:** drop S09–S10 (Lightning Orange) and S03's second sentence. Structure survives — the reference itself runs 4 bags before the winner in the first 77 seconds.

## 8. Post / captions

- **Captions:** burned-in white, sentence case, thin sans (Helvetica Neue Light), centred lower-third at ~78% height, phrase-by-phrase, no background box, no emoji. Direct match to the reference. **ffmpeg here has no drawtext and no libass** — render every caption as a transparent PIL PNG and overlay it.
- **No music.** Voice only, exactly like the reference. Room tone from the native audio carries the cuts.
- **No hook overlay.** The reference has none; the spoken hook does the work.
- **Stitch:** hard cuts only, no transitions, no crossfades. Trim by speech islands and drop any trailing island under 0.45s — Seedance appends hallucinated stray words at clip ends.
- **End card:** none. The reference ends on her face mid-sentence, which is what makes it feel unproduced.

## 10. QA gates before shipping

1. **Frame-QA subagent pass on every keyframe, before animation** (`velantra-straw-tote` §MANDATORY). Fresh context, never the one that wrote the prompt. Pre-animation gate: never animate an un-audited frame.
2. **Re-audit after every regeneration.** A passed audit belongs to the exact file audited, never its replacement — this is the mistake that shipped a mutated bag on 7/25.
3. **Proportion measurement** on every keyframe and ≥3 frames per clip: W:H 1.18–1.19.
4. **Flap check:** one seamless sheet folded all the way over, lying flat, never lifted, never split, no gap between the cut shapes. Every bag in this ad is closed and empty, which keeps it clear of the open-bag minefield entirely.
5. **Belt check:** exactly 2 straps, matching *that colorway's* reference geometry. No lattice, no third strap.
6. **Colour check** against the live hero — no drift to grey or taupe.
7. **No metal anywhere.** The Sofia has no hardware; Seedance invents turnlocks and grommets when given room.
8. **Transcript check on every clip** — verify the full line delivered, no truncated tail clause, no hallucinated stray word left in.
9. **Energy check on the LAST sentence** of every clip. Regenerate anything that fades flat.
