# VIDEO AD CONCEPT — VEL-FOUNDER-APOLOGY-01 — "A Thousand Apologies"

Format/destination:  UGC-style founder apology, real Brooks on camera (iPhone front camera, one continuous take feel). Runs two ways: (1) retargeting to Straw Tote buyers (Omnisend buyer CSV → Meta custom audience), (2) broad TOF — the apology doubles as social proof.
Avatar:              Straw Tote buyer waiting on her order (primary) / Velantra Woman prospect (secondary)
Awareness/Sophist.:  Buyers = most-aware; prospects = problem→solution-aware / 4–5
Angle (wound):       The delay makes her doubt her own judgment (did this brand take my money and vanish). A real founder with a name and a face restores it: you weren't wrong, you were early.

GOLDEN NUGGET:       "I didn't get fooled — I got there before everyone else did." The apology is really a vindication: the bag is late BECAUSE so many smart women wanted it. Never name the fear (no scam language, ever) — kill it by showing up in person with a real reason and a real date.

---

## HOOK BANK (0–3s, face direct to lens)

- H1: "I owe about a thousand women an apology."
- H2: "If you ordered our straw bag and it still hasn't shown up, this is for you."
- H3: "Our straw bag got way more popular than we planned for. That's not a brag, it's an apology."
- H4: "I'm the founder of Velantra, and I owe you an explanation."

H1 is the lead recommendation — granular-specific, lockable-true (~1,108 unique buyers), and it reads as a confession, not an ad.

## SCRIPT — FULL CUT (~170 words ≈ 47s at 3.6 w/s)

> [HOOK]
>
> I'm Brooks, the founder of Velantra. If you ordered the Straw Tote and you're still waiting on it, I'm sorry. It's late, and that's on me.
>
> Here's what happened. When we launched it, we planned for a strong summer. Instead, over a thousand of you ordered in the first few weeks, and orders came in faster than we could make bags.
>
> So we had a choice. Rush the next round out the door, or slow down and make every single bag the way the first ones were made. We took the slow road.
>
> The whole idea behind Velantra is that the quality has to match the price. That doesn't stop being true just because we're behind.
>
> Your bag is being made right now. Orders start going out in early August, and we're moving as fast as good work allows.
>
> So to everyone who's waiting, thank you. I promise it'll be worth it. And if you're watching this wondering what kind of bag sells out in three weeks, now you know.

## TRUTH-LOCKS (confirm before edit locks)

2. "Orders start going out in early August" — matches the ~early-Aug customer-facing estimate from the 7/6 delay email. Confirm the date still holds before spending on the edit; nothing ages worse than a second missed date in an apology ad.
3. "Sells out in three weeks" (full-cut close) — same lock as the founder-TOF "bags keep selling out" line.
4. Do NOT promise "you'll get an email when it ships" — Dianxiaomi fake-fulfillment arms tracking emails before production (see straw-tote email collision). Only add a ship-notification promise if that pipeline is actually fixed.
