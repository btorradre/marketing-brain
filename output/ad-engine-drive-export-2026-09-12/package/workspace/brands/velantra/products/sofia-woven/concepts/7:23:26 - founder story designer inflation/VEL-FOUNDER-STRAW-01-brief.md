# VEL-FOUNDER-STRAW-01 — "Why It Keeps Selling Out" — Founder Story Ad (Straw Tote)

**Format:** Founder phenomenon-explainer, ~36s, 9:16. Adapted from the Bellaucci Heart Necklace founder ad.
**Reference:** https://app.trendtrack.io/share/ads/bellaucci-R9UlHt (Bellaucci, Meta, 32s — transcript + beat map below)
**Emotional gateway (v2, avatar-corrected 2026-07-23):** DESIGNER INFLATION. Designer prices climbed far past the quality in recent years, the gap keeps growing, and she feels **taken advantage of** by the new price points. Velantra's counter-promise: a quality product that actually matches its price. The identity we sell is the smart-money woman who chooses not to spend frivolously — never a woman who is priced out, bitter, or "tired of" anything.
**Avatar source:** bullets from the ideal customer (Brooks's mom, 2026-07-23): (1) don't focus on what they are tired of, (2) emulate people who aren't frivolously spending on luxury/designer, (3) they feel taken advantage of by the new price points, (4) deliver a quality product that actually matches its price point and counters designer inflation.
**Funnel congruence:** the founder speaks the quality-versus-price argument in first person. Ad → PDP → About = one belief system, one voice.

## 3. Language laws for this concept family (avatar-sourced, 2026-07-23)

- NEVER "scam / scammed / scamming" — the avatar does not feel scammed, she feels **taken advantage of** by the new price points.
- NEVER frame her as priced out, unable to afford, or bitter. She could buy designer, she chooses not to spend frivolously. Identity first, grievance never.
- NEVER a "tired of X" complaint structure in the meaning beat. Observational and identity-forward instead: who she is, what she noticed, what we promise.
- "Affordable luxury" is internal mechanism language only, never spoken in customer-facing copy.
- "Straw Tote" only, never "Strato".
- Designer talk stays category-level: "designer prices", "designer inflation". No brand ever named (the Chanel context stays in strategy, never in copy).
