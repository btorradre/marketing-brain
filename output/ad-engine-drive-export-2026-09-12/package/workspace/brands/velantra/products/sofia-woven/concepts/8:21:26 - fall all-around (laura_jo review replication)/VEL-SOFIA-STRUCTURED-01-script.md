## Staging the stand test (beats 3 to 5)

Seven of the thirty seconds and it is the whole ad. If it looks staged the spot is dead.

- Shoot on a **hard floor she is already sitting on**, same frame throughout. Never cut to a table, never change the light.
- She sets it down and then **lifts both hands into frame and holds them there.** Hands visible and empty is the proof. Hands out of frame reads as a hand propping the bag up.
- **Hold the silence.** A full 1.5 seconds with nothing said and the bag standing. This is the most valuable second and a half in the spot and the instinct will be to talk over it.
- Camera static through the beat. No zoom, no push.
- One take. If she fumbles a word, keep it.

## How she actually talks (pulled off the transcript, these are the rules)

1. **She opens mid-feeling, not mid-topic.** First word is "I", first content is an emotion. "I was so disappointed when I opened this bag last night." No setup, no explaining what the video is.
2. **Clauses chain with and, so, but.** Her first four clauses run together without a full stop doing any work. Short punchy standalone sentences are the tell that a writer wrote it.
3. **She announces a loose structure and then lets it sprawl.** "I realized two things. Number one... But second of all..." and the second runs three times as long as the first.
4. **She interrupts herself to talk straight at you.** "I have to tell you." "I'm hoping I can show you." "See how nicely it moves." "Look at how crisp that detail is."
5. **Softeners and intensifiers all over it.** just, really, honestly, literally, so, again, super.
6. ~~**The bag is "she".**~~ **DO NOT COPY THIS ONE.** She calls the bag "she" throughout ("she's just literally a super simple bag", "she's such a great size"). Velantra bans personifying the product on every surface, and it bites harder for us because our bags carry women's names, so "she's holding all of that" reads as an actual woman named Sofia. Every one of these is "it" in our script. This is the one tic from the reference that does not port.
7. **She says "our".** Our monogram, our stitching, our trim. Possessive and inclusive, like she is walking you around it.
8. **Demo narration is present tense and half finished.** "There we go." "So nice and the color is perfect." She narrates her hands while they move, never before.
9. **Transitions are throwaway one-liners.** "Let's get into the details." "Now, here we are the next day."
10. **She ends personal and slightly exposed.** "I'm in love, though. I might have found the one for me."

At 30 seconds most of rules 2 and 3 cannot fit, because the sprawl is what eats runtime. What survives compression is rule 1 (the mid-feeling open), rule 4 ("Look at that"), and rule 10 (the exposed close). Those three are load-bearing and none of them get cut.

## Production notes

**This wants a real creator with the real bag.** Every beat is a hands-on beat, and the Sofia's flap splits within seconds of motion on Seedance and Kling. Thirty seconds is far more achievable than three minutes, but the load-and-set-down still means hands on the bag for a third of the runtime. Trybe or a paid creator with a real caramel Sofia.

**Delivery.** Flat, unhurried, sitting down, already mid-thought on the first word. No smile in the first line. The laugh at the end is real or it is cut.

## Verify before this is shot

1. **Price and stock.** $119.99 from $149.99 is live, and **Sunny Yellow is the only sold out colorway** as of 2026-08-21, so the older briefs saying two colors are gone are wrong. Neither fact is spoken in the 30s cut, so this only matters if you add the offer beat back.
2. **Dimensions and interior.** 9.5 x 12 x 6 with a four inch drop is on file but not published, and there is no confirmed lining spec. Nothing in this script states either, deliberately.
3. **Brand name.** The 8/2 fall VO said "Volantra." Catch it on the read.
4. **Set dressing.** No other brand on screen. The reference's wall of other-brand bags is her credential and cannot be ours.
5. **Load contents.** Whatever goes in the bag has to be hers and has to look it. No styled props.
