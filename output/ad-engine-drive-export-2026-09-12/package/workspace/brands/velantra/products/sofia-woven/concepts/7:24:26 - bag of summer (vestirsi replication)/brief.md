## Why the reference works

Vestirsi has run this creative for 290 days. It earns that by refusing to sell.

1. **Assertion as proof.** "The bag of the season" is a verdict, not a claim. There's no price, no
   discount, no urgency, no reason-why. The brand simply announces the winner. That posture reads
   as taste authority — the way a magazine names a bag, not the way a store advertises one. A
   shopper argues with a pitch; she doesn't argue with a verdict.
2. **The season is the deadline.** "Season" installs a closing window without a countdown timer.
   It borrows urgency from the calendar instead of manufacturing it, so it never smells like a sale.
3. **The props do the selling.** A pulp coffee-carrier tray, aviators, a takeaway cup. Cheap,
   ordinary, real. That contrast against a luxury bag is what converts a still life into a *life* —
   the viewer projects a morning into it. Remove the props and it's a catalog shot.
4. **Color-matched backdrop.** The wall is keyed to the bag. Nothing in frame competes, and the
   product reads as the source of the whole palette.
5. **Air at the top.** Roughly a third of the frame is empty. Cheap ads fill the frame; expensive
   ads leave room. The negative space is the price signal.

## What we changed, and why

The skeleton is identical — same two-tone color-matched backdrop, same curved plinth, same
lower-left prop cluster, same wide-tracked caps headline in the top third, same total absence of
price or offer. Four deliberate departures:

| Vestirsi | Ours | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| "THE BAG OF THE SEASON" | **"THE BAG OF SUMMER"** | Season is generic; summer is the straw tote's *only* season. Names the window and the category in three words. |
| Soft diffuse autumn studio light | Hard-edged raking sunlight, crisp shadows | Diffuse light reads autumn. Summer light has a hard edge — this single change does most of the seasonal work before the copy is read. |
| Chocolate suede / cool Milan palette | Saturated sun-warmed color fields on bone | Coastal-summer, not autumn-editorial. |
| Coffee tray, perfume, aviators | Iced coffee in a clear takeaway cup, tortoiseshell sunglasses, sunscreen, woven tray | Same structural job — the honest, disposable object that says "a real day" — translated to July. |
| "Handmade in Italy" | Removed | Origin claims are banned for Velantra. Replaced with construction proof: hand woven, holds its shape. |

**ICP calibration.** Our buyer isn't shopping heritage — she wants the bag that looks like it cost
$2,000 and to not feel taken advantage of on the way there. So the image stays pure editorial and
carries *zero* price signal. It builds the two-thousand-dollar impression cleanly. The price then
lands unadorned at the end of the primary text, where it works as a reveal instead of a discount.
No comparisons, no complaint framing, no defending the price.

## Composition (locked across all 8)

- Upper half: flat continuous color field, edge to edge, keyed to that colorway's leather
- Lower half: warm bone-cream surface sweeping in a soft arc from lower-left up to the right
- Tote upright on the bone surface, right of center, ~30° to camera, flap and belts facing viewer
- Lower-left foreground: woven straw tray + iced coffee in a clear domed-lid cup + folded
  tortoiseshell sunglasses + white sunscreen tube
- Sunlight rakes from upper right, hard-edged, clean shadows to the left
- Top third: empty backdrop, reserved for type

**Type (composited locally, never generated):** VELANTRA wordmark in white centered at 13.8% height;
"THE BAG OF SUMMER" in Helvetica Neue Light, white caps, ~660px tracked width, centered at 21.2%.

## Production truth

- Plates: kie.ai GPT Image 2 i2i (`gpt-image-2-image-to-image`), 9:16, 2K, one per colorway,
  each anchored on that colorway's own live PDP "Hand-held front" hero so the leather color is exact
- Verbatim identity + flap mechanism blocks in every prompt (velantra-straw-tote skill)
- Type composited locally in PIL — the model never renders text or the logo
- Scripts: `_production/gen_plates.py`, `_production/overlay.py` (both idempotent)

**QA:** all 8 plates audited before type — flap is one continuous sheet on every plate, handles
through the 2 slots, 2 belt straps, white contrast stitching, no metal hardware, natural tan straw
with only the leather colored, no text or people in any plate.

Three plates were regenerated: **caramel** and **caban-black** (the bone plinth flooded the headline
zone, leaving white type over cream) and **sky-blue** (same backdrop issue). Rejects kept in
`_production/rejected/`.

**Belt-geometry note for future runs:** caban-black and sky-blue render with one belt strap level
and the second angling down across it, rather than the symmetric X seen on the other six. That is
**correct** — those two colorways are photographed that way on the live PDP, and the i2i is matching
its own reference. An earlier prompt revision that forced a symmetric X on all eight was reverted;
anchoring to each colorway's reference beats forcing internal uniformity.
