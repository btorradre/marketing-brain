## What v3 got wrong

v3 was a **category ad with our name dropped into it.** Every reason to believe was generic —
versatile, goes with everything, timeless, makes any outfit look summery. Swap in literally any
woven bag and the script still reads. That's the right script for the creator we swiped: she's an
affiliate linking a dozen options, so selling *the category* is her whole job. We're the brand.
Running her script means paying to advertise raffia totes in general.

**v4 keeps the reel's mechanic and changes what the reasons are about.** The hook is still a
category verdict, because that's what closes an open loop for a woman who doesn't know what to
look for. But from the reveal onward, every reason to believe is a **specific, physical feature of
the Sofia** — the structure, the leather work, the absence of a logo. Those become the criteria she
shops by, and they're facts about our bag, so we're not comparing to anything.

Two other changes:

- **The reveal moved from ~0:20 to ~0:07.** The reel delays the brand because she's an affiliate.
  We want the brand named early so the whole benefit stack lands on the Sofia.
- **The no-logo beat is new.** Per the ICP, "zero logo on the exterior" is one of the strongest
  drivers for this avatar, and it's a hard fact about our bag.

## THE SCRIPT

> **VERDICT**
> If you buy one handbag this summer, make it a woven raffia tote.
>
> **REASSURANCE + REVEAL**
> There are a lot of bags trending right now, but this is the one I reach for all summer. It is the Sofia, from Velantra.
>
> **REASON 1 — the structure**
> And the thing that actually makes it is the shape. It is structured, so it holds its shape. You can set it down and it stands up on its own. Which is why it works for lunch and dinner, not just the beach.
>
> **REASON 2 — the leather work**
> The straw is hand woven, and all the leather on it is real. The flap is one single piece folded over the top, the handles are rolled leather, and there are two leather straps crossed on the front with white stitching. That is what makes it read like a real handbag.
>
> **REASON 3 — no logo**
> And there is no logo on it anywhere. People ask me about it constantly and they have no idea what it is.
>
> **REASON 4 — goes with everything**
> It also goes with everything. Jeans and a white tee, dresses, denim, linen, pretty much your whole summer closet.
>
> **REASON 5 — it holds a real day**
> And it fits a full day. Towel, snacks, sunscreen, everything, and it still looks good at dinner.
>
> **LONGEVITY**
> And a woven tote comes back every single summer, so you will actually wear this one for years.
>
> **CTA**
> It comes in a bunch of colors, mine is the caramel. I will leave the link below.

**225 words → 64.1s.** Rendered at 210 wpm, which is the swipe reel's exact pace.

VO tracks are `VEL-SOFIA-ONEBAG-VO-w30.mp3` (64.1s, **primary**) and `-w40.mp3` (71.9s, slower
alternate). Pace is now pinned with `speed: 1.09` in the ElevenLabs settings — at default speed
this script came back anywhere from 174 to 210 wpm run to run, a ~10s swing in runtime, and the
fast end is the energy the format needs.

## Every claim, and why it stands up

| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| "structured, so it holds its shape" | approved claim |
| "set it down and it stands up on its own" | physical fact, visible in every product shot |
| "the straw is hand woven" | approved claim |
| "the flap is one single piece folded over the top" | the locked product truth — this *is* the Sofia's defining construction |
| "the handles are rolled leather" | approved — leather handle detailing |
| "two leather straps crossed on the front with white stitching" | on the canonical reference bag |
| "no logo on it anywhere" | true, and per the ICP it's a top-3 driver for this buyer |
| "fits a full day" | approved claim |
| "a woven tote comes back every single summer" | category observation, same claim the reel makes |
| "comes in a bunch of colors" | true, and dodges a count while two colorways are sold out |

Nothing claimed about any bag we don't make. No origin, no price, no "Birkin," no brand named.

**One line to watch:** *"which is why it works for lunch and dinner, not just the beach"* is about
the Sofia's own range, not a knock on anything else. If you'd rather have zero ambiguity there,
cut the last four words — it reads fine as "...which is why it works for lunch and dinner too."
