# Delphine image audit vs. the real product asset library
2026-08-17 · audited against `real product asset library/` (4 HEIC stills + 3 4K videos of the real Army Green unit)

**Verdict: the current 18-image set is NOT an accurate adaptation.** The geometry and layout are broadly
right, but the two materials and four hardware/trim details are wrong, and they are wrong in every frame
because they were inherited from the master. Nothing here is a one-frame artifact.

## Method

Device file-staging is still returning HTTP 401, and the VM has no HEIC decoder, so the four stills could
not be read. The three videos were decoded frame-by-frame with ffmpeg on the device and relayed as contact
sheets. Frames used are saved in `realref/`. Findings below come from those frames only; the HEIC stills
remain unread, so treat this as an audit of the video evidence, not of the whole library.

## Correct — leave alone

- 25 × 22 × 14 proportions; nearly square front, deep body.
- Roughly 40 % leather / 60 % canvas split of the body height.
- Two belt straps running to a flat gold kelly-style end plate each, one oblong slot, two dome rivets.
- Two flat gold staples flanking the centre post on the band.
- Three-tab flap folding back as one piece (fixed earlier today).
- Warm brushed brass gold throughout, no silver or chrome.
- No logo, stamp or lettering anywhere.
- Contrast tan topstitching.

## ⚠️ Conflict that needs Brooks to decide

The **supplier's product photo** (`ref-real-supplier.jpg`, the dimensioned shot) clearly shows **a clochette
AND gold base feet**. The **physical unit in the videos has neither**. These are the same bag otherwise.

So one of these is true and I can't tell which from here:
1. The shipped units genuinely have no clochette and no feet — the supplier photo is a dressed sample, and
   the PDP must drop both.
2. The clochette is detachable and was removed for filming, and the feet exist but are not visible at these
   angles.

This decides 18 images. It is also a **claims** issue, not just a rendering one — the live PDP currently
says "gold-tone turn-lock, clasp plates, side buckles **and base feet**". If the units have no feet, that
line is unsupported and has to come off the page.

## Recommendation

Do not regenerate anything until the clochette / feet question is settled. Once it is, the fix is a new
master built to the corrected spec in `prompts/_BAG_BIBLE-v2.txt`, then the same derive-and-recolour route —
about 18 generations. Items 1–4 alone would be worth redoing the set for; they are why the renders still
read as a different, glossier, more expensive-looking bag than the one that will arrive in the box.
