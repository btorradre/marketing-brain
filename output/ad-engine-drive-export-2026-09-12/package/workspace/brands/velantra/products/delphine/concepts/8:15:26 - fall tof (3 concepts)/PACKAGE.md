## 1. The angle, and why it is this one

The seasonal switch is the trigger event. The insight underneath it is the one that survives
cold traffic: **small bags are decoys.** Every woman has bought a cute small bag and then stood
at her door taking things out of it, or carried the bag and held her phone in her hand anyway.

Fall sharpens it, because fall is when she carries *more* — the sunglasses stay, the coat pockets
are useless, the days out get longer. So the fall bag has to be small enough to look right with a
coat and deep enough to work.

**25 × 22 × 14 cm is what earns the claim.** Fourteen centimetres of depth on a 25 cm width is a
genuinely deep small bag. Most minis are flat. That is the whole ad: load it, close it, set it down,
watch it stand.

Frame is never "your other bags." It is **the small bags that lied to you.** Category indictment,
not category claim, so we are the answer rather than another entrant.

**Lane discipline:** the everything-bag position belongs to the Margot and is not available here.
The Delphine's axis is *small that actually holds*, which is the opposite claim. Nothing in this
package says "everything bag," "fits everything," or "goes with everything."

### Product law carried over

- **Closure-interaction law.** No beat has hands opening the flap or working the belt straps.
  Seedance reinvents closure architecture mid-motion every time it is asked to.
- **Re-block, do not reword.** The flap survives at medium distance and disintegrates in macro.
  Every tight framing stays on the canvas, the gold plates or the feet with the flap's top edge
  cropped out.
- **Scale line is mandatory.** The Sofia run rendered the tote too small across all three ads.
  Every prompt here carries a cast-relative size line (wrist to mid-thigh hanging, elbow to wrist
  on the forearm).
- **No shoulder strap.** The long side straps in the spec shots are unconfirmed as detachable.
  Every carry is by hand or forearm.
- Cast from text, not cloned. No origin claims. No product hero in beat 1.

## Engine learnings from this build

**Seedance 2.5 is on Higgsfield** as `seedance_2_5`. `models_explore action:"search"` does NOT
surface it on the query "seedance" — only `seed_audio` returns. Use `action:"list", type:"video"`.

**`generate_video_batch` intercepts with a preset recommendation** and submits nothing. Retry the
same payload with `declined_preset_id` from the error.

**2.5 pre-auths per job and parallel submits race each other.** Firing 5 at once fails most of them
even with a healthy balance. Fire in waves of 2. ~195 credits per 30s ad at 720p.

**NEVER put phonetic hyphens in Seedance dialogue.** "Vel-AN-truh" gets spoken as a word break
("Vel Entrez", "Vel Antru") in ~60% of takes. Put the brand name PLAIN in the spoken line and the
phonetics in a separate directive block with "speak it as ONE unbroken word". Even then the R lands
only ~75% of the time, so **transcribe every take and re-roll the misses**. When one ad fails twice,
change the sentence structure rather than repeating: giving the brand its own phrase
("This is the Delphine. It's from Velantra") fixed the ad that failed the plain fix twice.

**ASR cannot hold the word "Velantra".** Across takes Scribe returned Volanta / Valenta / Volantre /
Volante / Volantra / "Voux Landru". This made me wrongly conclude the pronunciation was fine when
Brooks could hear it was not. **Never adjudicate pronunciation by transcript — it needs a human ear.**
It also means every auto-caption pass on Velantra footage needs a manual brand-name fix; the caption
text was wrong in 5 of 6 timelines, plus "Birkin" came back as "firkin" once.

**Green screen key, measured:** the plate rendered a muted sea green **#358D5B**, not pure green.
`chromakey` (YUV) gave partial alpha across the whole subject and she composited translucent;
`colorkey` (RGB) is the correct filter. ffmpeg similarity is a fraction of the whole colour space,
so 0.30 ate her coat entirely — **0.12 with 0.02 blend** is right; 0.16+ punches holes in dark hair.
**Despill confirmed harmful on this product:** every mix value pulled the cream canvas's green channel
down and widened the R-G gap (mix 0.15 was worst, G -11.5). Ship with no despill and accept a faint
hair fringe. Force the plate's wardrobe to camel/grey with nothing cream or white so a no-despill key
is clean.

# QA PASS + CORRECTIONS — 2026-08-15 (Brooks review)

Full audit of all 30 stills via per-colorway contact sheets. Three corrections.

## 1. B03 load-in was physically impossible (Brooks caught it)

Brooks: *"one of the ones where she opens the bag, the three flaps are still folded over."*

Correct, and worse than a flap glitch. The frame showed the bag **open and closed at the same time**:
the one-piece flap was folded DOWN over the front in its closed position, with all three of its
scalloped tabs and the gold turn-lock plate visible, WHILE the mouth stood open above it with the
wallet and sunglasses case sitting inside. Both states in one frame.

**Root cause:** the prompt described the open state in words but wired only the CLOSED hero as the
image reference, so the model drew the closed front it could see and invented an opening above it.

**Fix:** wire `ref-interior.jpg` (the REAL open-state photo) as the PRIMARY reference and the closed
hero second for colour truth only, then state the consequence explicitly — *because the flap is folded
back, the FRONT shows NO flap: no scalloped tabs, no oval keyhole plate, only the shallow leather band
with the turn-lock and one gold plate either side.* Regenerated clean first try.

**LAW: for any open-bag shot, the open-state photo must be the primary image reference. Describing the
open state while referencing the closed hero produces a both-states-at-once hybrid.**

## 2. B14 two-bags RETIRED

Brooks: *"this random black bag and this random small thing... not relevant to the concept at all."*
Correct on concept, and it was also putting an oversized black tote and a beige mini — reading as
competitor product — inside our own ad. Retired from `picks/` and `clips/` to `_retired/`.
Library is now 28 stills / 20 clips.

## 3. Green screen was built in the wrong format

It was a full-size centred creator composited over B-roll. The format is a **small keyed creator
pinned BOTTOM-LEFT for the entire runtime** with product B-roll full-frame behind her throughout.
Rebuilt: PIP 520x665 at bottom-left, no full-frame creator windows, captions returned to the upper
third since the corner PIP no longer collides with them. Bed is product-only.

# ROUND 2 CORRECTIONS — 2026-08-15 (Brooks review #2)

Three failures. Two were process failures on my side, not model failures.

## 1. Retired assets were left in the timelines

I moved B14 out of `picks/` and `clips/`, reported it retired, and **never removed it from the five
edits it was placed in.** It was still playing in all five, and it was the OPENING SHOT of VO-05.
B13 (a woven straw tote that is not our product) was still the opening shot of VO-01.

**LAW: retiring an asset means removing every timeline item that references it. Moving the file is
not retiring it.** Grep the cut plan for the shot id before claiming a retirement is done.

## 2. The QA pass audited stills, not motion — and the artifact was in the motion

I built contact sheets of all 30 stills, found them clean, and declared the library good. The real
defect was only visible in the ANIMATED clips: **every Omni push-in inflated the bag across the clip.**
B11 opened as a handbag and ended a weekender. B10, B04, B02, B09, B12 all grew. B08 pulled wide
enough to reveal the flap it was specifically framed to crop out.

On a product whose entire mechanism is "it is small", a slow push-in silently destroys the pitch in
10 of 12 clips, and a still-only audit cannot see it.

**LAW: never ask Omni for a push-in, dolly or zoom on a product shot. Camera locked off; motion comes
from the subject. Every motion prompt carries an explicit "the bag occupies the same fraction of the
frame at the end as at the start" clause. QA every clip FIRST-FRAME vs LAST-FRAME, not just stills.**

Over-correcting broke B05: a fully locked camera let her walk out of frame and Omni hallucinated a
camera tripod into the empty street. A tracking PAN is safe (distance never changes); it is only the
push-in/zoom that inflates. Re-rolled with a pan and an explicit "no tripod, camera or crew appears".

## 3. B13 was a bad hook regardless

Opening cold traffic on a woven bag that is not our product gives away the first two seconds.
Every ad now opens on the Delphine.
