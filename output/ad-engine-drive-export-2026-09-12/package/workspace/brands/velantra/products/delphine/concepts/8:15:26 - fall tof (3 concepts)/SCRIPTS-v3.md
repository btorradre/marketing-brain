# The Delphine — Fall TOF scripts v3

**Supersedes v2 (`SCRIPTS-v2-day-to-night.md`) and the v1 claims in `PACKAGE.md` / `FB-SHORT-COPY.md`.**

---

## Why v2 was killed

v2 sold a **transition**: day-to-night, the bag you stop swapping at 5pm. Two problems.

1. **It's a short-life angle.** A transition angle is tied to a moment, so it burns out in about a
   month and takes the whole creative library with it.
2. **It sold a situation, not the bag.** Swap in almost any structured small bag and the v2 script
   still reads fine. That is the exact tell from [[feedback_sell_our_product_not_the_category]] —
   paying to advertise a use case instead of our product.

v2 also carried a villain (the mini you swap out, the tote you're stuck with), which manufactures a
problem for an avatar who is already just looking for a good fall bag.

## The positioning, locked

**Verdict, not argument. The Delphine is simply the good one.**

The hook is a verdict she can accept in one second: *"I think I found the perfect fall bag."*
Everything after it is a stack of **specific, verifiable physical facts about our bag** — which
double as the criteria a searching buyer shops by. That is how we become the best one without
comparing to anything.

**Structure (per [[feedback_tof_no_product_first_seconds]]):**
verdict → brand named early → stacked reasons to believe → the offer → soft CTA.
No problem. No villain. No claim about any bag we don't make.

**"Perfect fall bag" is the seasonal wrapper, not the positioning.** The durable positioning
underneath is *structured, hand-finished, no logo* — swap "fall" for another season and every
reason to believe still stands. That's what makes this library outlive the month.

### The reasons to believe (all physically true, all demonstrable)

- Stands up on its own, holds its shape
- One-piece leather flap folded over the top
- Rolled leather top handles
- Belt straps across the front into a gold roller buckle on each side
- Small gold feet on the base
- Warm gold fittings, **no logo anywhere on it**
- Fully lined inside with a zip pocket
- 25 × 22 × 14 cm — 10 inches across
- Three colors, one production run

**Capacity stays honest:** wallet, cards, keys, a lip color. Never "a whole day," never "everything
you need." Phone and sunglasses stay out until verified on the physical unit.
