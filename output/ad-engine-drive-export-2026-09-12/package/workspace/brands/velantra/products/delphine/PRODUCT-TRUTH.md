## 2b. 🚨 REAL 25 cm SPEC SHEET, 2026-08-15 — SUPERSEDES EVERYTHING BELOW

Brooks sent the supplier's dimensioned product photo of the actual 25 cm bag. **It is not a scaled-down
Eleanor.** Two things I had wrong and one structural difference:

1. **Dimensions are 25 W × 22 H × 14 D cm**, printed on the supplier image. I had derived 25 × 20 × 10 by
   scaling the Eleanor proportionally. Wrong on both height and depth. The real bag is **nearly square from
   the front** (25:22 = 1.14:1, not the Eleanor's 1.24:1) and **much deeper** (14 cm on a 25 cm width, so it
   is a chunky boxy bag rather than a slim one).
2. **The leather section is DEEPER than the Eleanor's, not shallower.** On the supplier photo the leather
   upper reads roughly half the body height. The 40/60 correction was derived from the 46 cm Eleanor and does
   NOT transfer. Re-measure against the supplier photo before regenerating.
3. **The strap architecture is different.** On the real 25 cm the two belt straps run roughly
   HORIZONTALLY across the front, angling slightly down toward the centre, each passing through a gold
   kelly-style plate with an oblong slot, and continuing outward to terminate at a **roller buckle on each
   side gusset**. The Eleanor's straps hang vertically from the band and it has no side buckles.
4. Also present and absent from every render so far: **small gold metal feet along the bottom edge**, a
   **domed / arched flap top profile**, a prominent **centre-front clochette on a long leather tab**, and a
   leather that reads **lightly pebbled/grained reddish chestnut** rather than smooth semi-matte cognac.

**Consequence for copy:** the PDP currently says the fittings are "carried over" from the Eleanor. The
turn-lock, belted straps and corner patches genuinely are shared, so the lineage line survives, but do not
escalate it to "identical hardware" until the physical bag is checked side by side.

**BLOCKER:** the supplier image needs to live at `product-references/REAL-25cm-front.jpg` before any
regeneration. Every frame must then be i2i'd from THAT file, not from the Eleanor stills.

## 3. Construction (inherited from the Eleanor — ⚠️ superseded on the points listed in 2b)

- One-piece leather **flap** hinging from the back panel, folding backward. Three tabs along its lower
  edge: a centre tab carrying the gold oval keyhole plate, and one side tab left and right, each pierced
  with a small vertical oval slot.
- **One** knurled gold mushroom-head turn post at the front centre of the leather band. It is the only
  post on the bag; with the flap down only its head shows through the oval plate's cutout.
- Two gold **staples** on the leather band, each **two parallel flat gold bars** side by side, never one
  solid blade and never a buckle. Each drops through its side tab's oval slot when closed.
- Two leather **belt straps** anchored at the back, over the top edge near each side, under a small leather
  keeper loop on the band, hanging straight down near the side edges. Each tip carries a flat gold rounded
  end plate with an oblong slot and small dome rivets.
- Two short rolled leather **top handles** passing through keyhole cutouts in the flap.
- Leather **corner patches** at the bottom, one small gold **eyelet** high on each side face, a small
  leather **key bell** at the handle base. Back panel is plain.
- **No zipper anywhere.**

## 4. Generation recipe (validated 2026-08-14)

Engine: **GPT Image 2 via the Higgsfield MCP** (`generate_image_batch`, quality high, 2k, 1:1).

**References** in `product-references/` (downscaled from the Eleanor real-product stills):
closed → `ref-LC-closed-front.jpg` + `ref-LC-side-strap.jpg`; open → `ref-LC-open-interior.jpg` +
`ref-LC-open-slippocket.jpg`.

**Two blocks that must be pasted into every closed-bag prompt** — both validated, see
`product-images/gen-2026-08-14/prompts/`:

1. **SMALL-SCALE BLOCK.** The single most important instruction. States 25 × 20 × 10 cm, names it a
   handbag, explicitly rules out travel bag / weekender / duffel / large tote, and adds the two visible
   consequences: handles arch only ~3 in above the top edge, and the gold fittings stay full size so they
   read large against the small front panel.
2. **MANDATORY DETAIL CORRECTIONS** (four defects the model produces on every first roll):
   straps rendered as floating gold plates on bare canvas · a phantom second gold post below the flap ·
   flap side tabs missing their oval slots · leather rendering as suede on one panel.
3. **🔒 TURN-LOCK BLOCK — MANDATORY ON ANY CLOSE CROP (added 2026-08-16).** The lock survives at
   medium distance and shatters in macro, the same distance-dependent failure as the Weekender flap.
   All three `-04-hardware` macros shipped with a mangled lock — two long detached pill-shaped bars
   floating over the oval plate, one of them pale chrome, with a dark void and an exposed post between
   them — while every front and three-quarter frame in the same set was correct. Brooks caught it on
   the live PDP. **The truth: one upright oval brass plate, solid and unbroken, one small domed screw
   near the top and one near the bottom, a short round brass boss at the centre, and ONE short rounded
   brass turning bar across that boss, no longer than about a third of the plate's height.** State the
   negatives too, by name, or the pills come back: no second bar, no duplicated toggle, no long pill or
   capsule shape, no bar floating free of the plate or casting its own shadow, no bar running past the
   plate edge, no hole, slot, keyhole or open gap, no exposed post or thread, and no silver, chrome or
   nickel anywhere. Repair recipe and the correct-lock reference crop:
   `gen-weekender-style/_fix/` (`fix_turnlock.py`, `refs/turnlock-correct.png`, artifacted originals in
   `_fix/ARTIFACTED/`).

Open-bag shots additionally take the Eleanor skill's **opening mechanism block** verbatim. It held
first try at this scale.

**🔒 OPEN-STATE MECHANISM BLOCK — MANDATORY ON EVERY OPEN-BAG FRAME (added 2026-08-20, Brooks:
"the opening mechanisms are messed up… I want the triple flap mechanism shown properly").**
The §4 TURN-LOCK BLOCK above is the CLOSED-state truth only. Pasting it into open-bag prompts
put the oval plate + turning bar on the BAND — wrong: **the oval keyhole plate lives on the
flap's centre tab, not on the band.** The whole packing batch shipped with invented open-state
hardware (oval-plate lock on the band, solid Birkin-style kelly plates as staples, flap as a
rigid upright wall) and Brooks caught it on the board. Ground truth, observed from the real
25 cm photos (`real product asset library/IMG_4190.MOV` frame → the mechanism reference
`IMG_4190-open-front.jpg`, plus IMG_4187/4192/4193):

- The one-piece flap hinges at the BACK panel and folds BACKWARD, standing at a soft backward
  angle behind the open mouth, underside toward the camera — never a rigid vertical wall.
- The folded-back flap shows a row of cutouts: two large EMPTY keyhole-shaped handle cutouts,
  a small vertical oval slot near each end (the side tabs), and the flat gold oval keyhole
  plate on the wider centre tab, seen from behind. **Handles rise IN FRONT of the flap, never
  through it, when open.**
- Front band: ONE small knurled gold **mushroom-head turn post, BARE** — no oval plate around
  it, no turning bar. Either side of the handle bases: ONE **slim upright gold staple** each
  (narrow vertical bar), never a wide solid plate.
- Belt straps UNDONE, hanging loose down the front sides, each tip carrying its flat gold
  rounded end plate with oblong slot resting against the canvas.

**Recipe that fixed it:** add the real open photo as a dedicated MECHANISM REFERENCE image
("copy the opening mechanism from it exactly — never its olive green canvas colour"), plus the
block above written from observation. Never write an open-state prompt from the closed-state
hardware description again.

**🚨🔒 THE ACTUAL LAW — DO NOT RENDER THE FOLDED-BACK FLAP LARGE IN FRAME (Brooks, v5, after
four failed prompt rounds).** The open flap is a MACRO-DISTANCE FAILURE, exactly like the
turn-lock and the Weekender flap above: it renders correctly at medium distance and invents
itself at close range. Inspected at 2x, the v1-v4 open frames each produced a DIFFERENT flap
silhouette and a different number/shape of cutouts (black voids, white slivers, lumpy blob
edges, gold plate floating mid-panel). No amount of prompt hardening fixes this — four rounds
and ~200 credits proved it. **The fix is SHOT DESIGN:**
- **Tight packing inserts:** crop the flap and the top of the bag OUT of frame. Frame only the
  canvas body, the leather band, the mouth, the hands and the contents. State it explicitly:
  "the flap and the top of the bag are CROPPED OFF above the top edge of the frame and are NOT
  VISIBLE ANYWHERE; do not draw a flap." This is also what real packing UGC looks like.
- **Anything showing the whole bag:** shoot it CLOSED at medium distance, where the flap-down
  mechanism (domed edge, straps through the plates, turn lock) renders right every time.
- **Interior hero shots:** go very tight on the black lining + zip pocket; no exterior at all.
Never again write an open-flap block for a close shot. If the flap must be big in frame, the
only reliable route is a real photograph.

**🔒 FLAP PROPORTIONS (Brooks, v4: "still fucked. go look at the actual assets library and
study the opening mechanism") — kept for the medium-distance case only.** Measured from IMG_4187/4188/4190: the folded-back flap is LOW
and RECLINED — it rises only ~HALF the bag body's height above the mouth, tilts well backward,
top edge clearly BELOW the tops of the handles, same width as the bag, smooth soft dome, and
its cutouts are SMALL (two modest keyhole cutouts + centre oval plate + one small slot each
end — a small fraction of the flap's surface). Rounds v2/v3 drew a towering full-height
billboard with flared crown corners and giant cutouts because the block described the flap
without measuring it. **The fix that held first try on all 8: mechanism reference = the SHARP
FRONT-ON real photo (IMG_4187, not a motion-blurred video frame) + "the open bag must match
this photo EXACTLY in every proportion" + the measured proportion block.** Meta-law: never
write a mechanism block from memory or imagination — go to `real product asset library/`,
extract frames, MEASURE the proportions, then write. Pilot 2 frames before rolling a batch.

**⚠️ FLOATING-FLAP COROLLARY (Brooks, same board, v3):** phrasing the open flap as "folded
back BEHIND the bag" makes the model draw it as a SEPARATE panel hovering behind the mouth
with a gap. The wording that holds: "the flap is one CONTINUOUS piece with the back panel,
its base joined to the bag's top back edge along the full width — NO gap, NO background
visible between flap and bag — leaning backward and sagging under its own weight," plus
"copy the flap's position, angle, size and ATTACHMENT exactly from the mechanism reference."
Fixed all 8 open frames first try. QA line item: check the flap-to-bag join for background
bleed on every open frame. (Same run also caught a stretched-handle mutation — handles block:
"SHORT rolled loops, ~3in arch, both ends anchored to the band, never elongated/hanging".)

**Colorway workflow:** author the shot in **Light Chocolate**, then recolour to DC and AG with a

## 6. 🔒 TWO CORRECTIONS FROM BROOKS, 2026-08-14

shorter"). Measured against the real Eleanor closed-bag photos: leather is 42-43% there. The canvas has to read

extend the CANVAS UPWARD, and pin the bottom edge in place. Relational wording alone does not move it.
