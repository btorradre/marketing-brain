# The Delphine — Fall TOF scripts v2, "perfect fall bag" / day-to-night

> **🚨 KILLED 2026-08-16 — see `SCRIPTS-v3.md`.** Brooks: the day-to-night framing is a *transition*

> **The capacity correction below is still valid and carried into v3** — only the angle died.

**Supersedes the capacity claims in `PACKAGE.md` and `FB-SHORT-COPY.md`.** Reason: two independent
TikTok "what's in my bag" videos of the identical 25×22×14cm Birkin silhouette (both self-titled
"day to night" bags) showed real owners carrying one wallet/card pouch + one small cosmetics pouch —
no phone, no sunglasses, no keys loose. "Holds a whole day," "everything I need," and "a normal day
out actually fits" oversell what this footprint actually does. B-roll library (`broll/`), VO tracks
and Seedance clips from the v1 build are still usable footage — only the narration/copy changes below.

**The angle, rewritten:** stop competing on capacity. Compete on not having to swap bags for dinner.
The enemy is no longer "bags too small AND totes too big."
It's **the mini you switch out for dinner** vs. **the tote you're stuck lugging all day**.
The Delphine's job: structured enough to look
finished at night, small enough it's not a decision to carry, and it holds exactly what you actually
reach for — wallet, cards, keys, a lip color. Not a daily workhorse. A day-to-night bag.

"Perfect fall bag" works as a top-of-funnel hook precisely because it doesn't itself promise capacity —
it's a seasonal relevance claim. The proof under it has to stay narrow to survive contact with the
real bag.

**Claims audit v2:** structured / stands on its own ✓ · 25 × 22 × 14 cm ✓ (stated, not oversold) ·
wallet, cards, keys, a lip color ✓ (provable) · day-to-night / no bag-swap ✓ · seasonal ✓.
**Removed:** "phone," "sunglasses," "holds a whole day," "everything I need," "normal day out actually
fits," "the size in between." No origin claim, no competitor comparison, no size-variant claim, no
Eleanor mention, no personification, no shoulder-strap claim, no Birkin/Hermès reference anywhere in
copy (DMCA history — reference research only, never in the ad). Creator never speaks as the brand:
"they're running," never "our sale."

**Open item for Brooks:** confirm on the physical unit whether a phone fits alongside the wallet with
the bag still closing. If yes, "phone" can go back into the load-in line — but only once verified on
the real bag, not assumed from the Birkin reference.
