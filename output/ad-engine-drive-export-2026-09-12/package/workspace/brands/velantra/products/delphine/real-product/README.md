## Why the generated set was scrapped

The real 25 cm bag differs from the Eleanor in ways no amount of prompting would have reached:

- **25 × 22 × 14 cm.** Nearly square from the front, and deep. I had derived 25 × 20 × 10 by scaling
  the Eleanor, which was wrong on height and badly wrong on depth.
- **Domed / arched flap top**, not the Eleanor's flatter profile.
- **Belt straps run outward to a roller BUCKLE on each side gusset.** The Eleanor's hang vertically
  from the band and it has no side buckles.
- **Small gold feet on the base.** The Eleanor has none.
- **Interior is a full fabric lining with a ZIPPED pocket** (black on Light Chocolate, olive on Dark
  Chocolate). It is NOT the Eleanor's caramel leather lining with a slip pocket. Copy corrected.
- Leather reads a warm chestnut with visible grain rather than smooth semi-matte cognac.
