## Evidence needed for exact scale

A supplier sheet needs the exact model/color/size and revision; a physical sample record needs measured endpoints and dated front/side ruler photos. For soft bags, state empty or filled and open or closed. Measure the actual product, not its packaging. Separate usable opening/internal space from external bag dimensions for laptop and packing claims.

The connected fulfillment copy was checked directly: it repeats old PDP measurements and TBC accessory fields. It does not close these gaps. Read [the protocol](SCALE-AND-FIDELITY.md) for how measurement status and image calibration are recorded.
