## Translation rules (Nuamore → Velantra)

| Nuamore | Velantra equivalent | Why it changes |
|---|---|---|
| 4-pillar drumbeat: waterproof / hypoallergenic / tarnish-resistant / 2-yr warranty | **Structured (holds its shape) / Hand-woven seagrass / Full-grain leather trim / 2-year warranty** | Same repetition strategy, claims mapped to the straw-bag category's failure modes |
| "Most gold can't swim" villain | **"Most straw bags don't survive one summer"** — frayed handles, collapsed shape, beach-only | ICP pain points #2 and #5 verbatim |
| Summer-as-deadline | Same, plus **Caban Black extends the season** ("summer bags retire in September — this one doesn't") | We have a season-extension SKU Nuamore doesn't |
| "Rated 4.6/5 by 100,000+" | **Named verified-buyer testimonials only** (Margot R., Eleanor M., Camille D., Sofia L., Anya K.) | We can't claim six-figure volume; ICP trusts "women who look like her" over big numbers anyway |
| "Thought It Was Patek At First!" | **"She assumed I paid four figures for it"** | Anti-Hermès positioning *without naming Hermès* (ICP angle #3) |
| 50% off sale events | **GWP/bundle events only** ("The Summer Set": complimentary Bag Scarf + Horse Charm) | ICP: "aggressive discount codes read as desperation." Steal the 3-email event *structure*, not the discount depth |
| Julia founder letters | **Plain-text letters from Brooks (founder)** + optional CX persona for service-flavored sends | ICP's #1 trust trigger is founder proximity |
| "Sold out 2x" scarcity | **Small-batch scarcity: "made in small numbers" + colorway-level sellouts** | ICP wants scarcity that feels organic, not manufactured — ours genuinely is |
| Oceana Gold Wave = default answer | **Straw Tote in Natural = default answer** | ICP starts with a neutral, then collects |

**Hard rules carried into every email:**
- NO US/EU/Italian origin claims — "full-grain leather," never "Italian leather" ([standing rule](../../../.claude/projects/memory), made in China).
- No invented stats. Sellout counts, review counts, credit amounts marked `[VERIFY]` must be checked against real data before send.
- Banned words (ICP repel list): premium, luxury (as adjective), affordable luxury, designer-inspired, elevate, treat yourself, you deserve it, exclusive drop, curated for you, boss babe.
- Voice: short declarative sentences, em-dashes, "decades, not seasons," quiet confidence. Two registers — peer-speak in hooks, material-specific precision in body.
- Cadence: 3–4x/week, one send time held constant (recommend 11:00 AM ET — mirrors Nuamore's fixed 15:00 UTC). Event days can double up.
- Every email: ONE primary CTA. P.S. either adds the GWP/threshold hook or collapses choice to the Natural Straw Tote.
