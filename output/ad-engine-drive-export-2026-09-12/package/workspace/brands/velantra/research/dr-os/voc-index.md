## Open conversation

### Pain and objection map

Price justification, unfamiliar-brand trust, material skepticism, comfort, fit and availability all appear. Automatic frequencies are available in the report’s evidence screens and are explicitly lexical candidate counts. They are not validated prevalence estimates.

### Failed-solution library

Reports include disappointment with particular materials, an expensive purchase rarely used, problems finding the right travel carrying method, and alleged stock/fulfillment mismatches. These are unverified category reports. Consult each linked post before using an allegation.

### Emotional language

The report preserves “designer bag ick,” anticipation of styling a new purchase, relief about a preferred material, and resistance to approval from a bag-rating creator. They describe different motives; do not merge them into a single grievance.

### Community dialect

Quiet luxury, midluxury, old money, big girl bag and non-logo are contextual terms, not fixed demographic segments. Creator usage is stored separately from audience language.

### Weak signals

Founder trust and bag gifting have thin direct evidence. Male-approval messaging is contested. Industry-wide corruption is not established. Those stay as research hypotheses, with dedicated coverage-gap notes.
