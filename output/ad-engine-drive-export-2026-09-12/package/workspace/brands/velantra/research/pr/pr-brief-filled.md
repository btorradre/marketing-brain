## GETTING STARTED

### Boilerplate (C2)
Velantra is a coastal luxury handbag brand for the woman who lets her taste speak louder than any logo. Led by its hero piece — the Straw Birkin — Velantra designs structured, heritage-quality bags that carry the ease of a seaside summer and the craftsmanship of a bag built to last decades, not seasons. Every piece is made from natural materials — hand-woven straw, full-grain leather, solid brass — with no visible branding, because the details do the talking. Rooted in the quiet-luxury rhythm of the coast — the ferry line, the farmers' market, the harbor-side lunch — Velantra is the bag people stop to ask you about. Backed by a two-year warranty and lifetime repairs, it's designed to be discovered, carried, and passed down.

### Key Media Messaging (C5)
1. **The summer bag of 2026.** The Straw Birkin is the coastal, quiet-luxury successor to last year's viral "Boatkin" moment — the Birkin silhouette in hand-woven straw.
2. **Quiet luxury, no logo.** Heritage materials and construction with zero visible branding — status you feel, not status you advertise (IYKYK).
3. **Built to last decades, not seasons.** Two-year warranty + lifetime repairs. An investment piece, not a trend buy.
4. **The bag people stop to ask you about.** Designed as a conversation piece and word-of-mouth discovery — not a billboard.
5. **Coastal by design.** Made for the harbor, the market, the ferry, the patio — effortless from beach to dinner.

### What Makes Velantra / The Product Unique (C6)
- **The shape everyone wants, without the tax.** The Straw Birkin captures the coveted Birkin silhouette in hand-woven straw with leather trim — no logo, no four-figure price, no waitlist games.
- **Structured straw solves the straw-bag problem.** Most straw bags are flimsy and beach-only; the Straw Birkin holds its shape, works in the city, and carries a laptop + water bottle.
- **Natural materials, no logo.** Hand-woven straw, full-grain leather, solid brass — nothing to advertise the brand for you.
- **A full color run = collectibility.** Buyers rarely stop at one; the color range drives the "which one's mine?" loop.
- **Warranty + lifetime repairs** — rare at this price tier and central to the "decades, not seasons" promise.
- **Direct-to-consumer**, so designer-grade materials at an accessible price.
