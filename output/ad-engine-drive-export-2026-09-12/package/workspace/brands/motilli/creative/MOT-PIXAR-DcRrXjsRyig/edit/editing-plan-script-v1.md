## Voice, captions, music and continuity

Tom is gentle, dryly funny and initially uncertain; he never delivers a medical lecture. Linda begins embarrassed, speaks more openly after the reveal and regains her playful manner. Treat both as adults with a long shared history. Keep the vulnerable admission unhurried. The recurring earrings and interrupted speech connect the opening to the ending.

Final caption timing follows the performed dialogue. Use readable phrase captions, clear contrast and phone-size checks for the older audience. Keep faces, earrings, the hand invitation and pack label unobstructed. Do not rewrite the selected words to force the older90-second framework.

Use a restrained score, a glass catch and footsteps at the opening; reduce the score at the private admission. Bring warmth back at the supportive exchange and a diegetic song at the dance. Preserve dialogue intelligibility; inspect the eventual mix on phone speakers. Do not assign unmeasured source sound settings.

Repeated framing at the table is an intentional callback; mirror detail, doorway two-shot, kitchen conversation and product routine each have different visual jobs. Review actual frames for variety and continuity before generation and export. No unrelated anatomy insertion is required by this script.

## Product and evidence treatment

The drug premise is restricted to semaglutide’s documented delayed stomach emptying and listed GI side effects; the character is fictional. Prescriber contact is separate from product choice and carries no product endorsement. Product copy names the gummy format and her routine preference. It does not say celery restarts the stomach, chlorophyll treats sulfur gas, capsules cannot work, or Motilli treats medication side effects. No dosage or response timeline is specified. The later date scene resolves the relationship; do not add disappearance-of-symptom graphics or an instant before/after claim.

The brand materials contain unresolved formula/efficacy conflicts. See the script evidence notes before expanding this draft into a mechanism-led claim. Review any implied efficacy from the completed edit as well as spoken wording.
