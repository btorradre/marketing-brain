## 💡 1. Concept

### WHY

She is five days out and she has stopped talking about it. The shot is working, the weight is coming off, and she is not giving that up. What is wearing her down is not the constipation itself, it is the private conclusion she has come to after four things failed: that everyone else takes these and everyone else goes, so the problem must be her. Nobody has ever shown her what is actually happening inside her, so she has nothing to blame except herself.

### WHAT

An anatomical animation walks the viewer through five days inside her own gut, in order, hour by hour. One character carries all of it, a translucent glass-skinned skeleton woman with visible organs, in bright ordinary rooms. There is no presenter, no doctor, no clinic, no white coat, and no person who claims to be a customer or claims a result. Nothing is argued. Every beat before the reframe is an event that happens on screen, food arrives, food sits, food ferments, gas rises, water gets pulled out, and she recognises her own week in it before a single claim is made. The named competitor products are real and readable, because the placement argument is the whole ad and the labels are the proof.

### HOW

Open inside the body with the stomach already stalled, then run the clock forward from the first bite through hour six, hour twelve, day two, day three, day four and day five. Teach the stomach slowdown once, at hour one, by showing a healthy squeeze and then breaking it in the identical framing. Withhold Motilli completely until 1:29. At 1:04 the camera pulls back and the three things she swallowed are visible glowing low in her colon while the stomach sits dark six feet above them, and that single shot is the ad. State the criterion at 1:23 before any ingredient is named, then let each of the three ingredients kill one thing she already tried. Hard cuts only, new picture every two to four seconds, no dissolves and no music bed.

### Runtime

2:12 for any selected hook and its matched bridge, followed by the shared body and CTA. 420 spoken words per aired variant, which is 420 divided by 190 words per minute, or 133 seconds. All three hooks run 0:00-0:07 and all three bridges run 0:07-0:13, so every variant hands off to the same shared body line.

## 🎬 3. Editing Notes

- One character for the entire ad. The same glass skeleton woman is on screen or inside her for all 2:12. Never cut to a different figure, a different style, or stock footage. Her rooms change, she does not.
- Build and approve the glass-skeleton base frame before any other shot is generated. Five variants, pick the most ordinary one and not the prettiest. She reads as a museum anatomy model, never a horror prop. Brooks approves her on the Cutroom board first.
- Do not show the Motilli jar, the gummies, or the brand name anywhere before 1:29.
- The product is the gummy jar. Clear jar, white cap, green wrap-around label, 60 vegan gummies, Green Apple. The gummies are dark forest green and heart-shaped, never orange, peach or pink. Motilli never appears as a pouch or a powder. Every product frame is image-to-image off the real jar reference and is never generated from imagination.
- The only powder on screen is the competitor fiber tub at 0:55.
- MiraLAX, magnesium citrate and the fiber powder tub are the real named products with legible labels. This is the exception to our usual generic-competitor rule, because the placement argument is the ad and the labels are the proof.
- Every image lands on its cue word within half a second. Check ferments at 0:30, brick at 0:48, water at 0:48, landed at 1:04, sulfur gas at 1:40, and Motilli at 1:29 individually. No mood footage anywhere.
- Full frame only. No picture-in-picture and no insets. The single split frame is at 1:45.
- Three shot kinds and never blended: an organ isolated on a pale ground, macro inside the tract, and the figure in a room. Do not composite a room behind an isolated organ.
- Captions are burned in, one word per card, heavy geometric sans, white, bold, thick black drop shadow, centred at chest height. The first card sits in a red box, every card after is plain white. No caption over the end card.
- Hard cuts only. No dissolves, no artificial camera shake, no handheld jitter, no zoom drift, no lens flares, no music bed under the voiceover.
- The voice is cloned off the reference speaker and generated as one continuous take. The editor cuts picture only and never cuts audio. No gap exceeds 250 milliseconds.
- Never frame the GLP-1 as the villain. The stomach slowdown is stated as intentional and as how the medication works.
- Runtime gate: the voiceover take is 2:12. If the export is longer than 2:17, there are gaps in it.
- Export 1080x1920, 30fps, H.264, audio at -14 LUFS.
- Verify the Buy 2 Get 1 Free ladder and the 90-day guarantee on the destination page again on launch day before export.
