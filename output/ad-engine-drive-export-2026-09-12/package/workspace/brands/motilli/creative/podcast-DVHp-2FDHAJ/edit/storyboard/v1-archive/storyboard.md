## Editing rules

### Podcast stays central

Host on every question. Female guest delivers explanations. Scientific inserts return to her at the conclusion of each thought. Preserve warm cream studio, boom microphones, consistent eyelines and relaxed conversational delivery.

### Timing and audio

190 delivered words/minute; 0.16s intra-card speaker handoffs; 0.7s end hold. Provisional, not an aligned audio timeline. Final narration 1.1× maximum per user direction; remove dead air first and align cues to actual words. Keep intelligible breaths and short turn-taking pauses. B-roll audio stays muted; speech and continuous room tone bridge cuts. No automatic 1.2× stretch.

### Caption and overlay system

Opening topic strip only during the initial stacked exchange. Then bold white uppercase captions, usually 1–4 words, soft dark shadow, near 60–70% of frame height. Scientific labels stay in the upper third. Keep faces, mouths and bottom platform UI zone unobstructed. Overlays enter on the word they explain.

### Transitions

Hard cuts for speaker changes and B-roll entries/exits. Carry the speaking audio continuously under inserts. Occasional restrained punch-in at a conclusion, approximately 1.08–1.12×. No whip pans, page wipes, noisy whooshes or recurring animated title cards.

### Scientific direction

One clean anatomy style: translucent torso, peach organs, dark teal field, restrained blue for water. Show stomach emptying, intestinal contractions and colon hydration as distinct processes. No stool in stomach, fake studies, product-triggered gut reboot or invented biochemical reactions. Ingredient-role claims remain subject to the evidence limitations in source-notes.md.

### Casting and future pacing

Female gastroenterologist is a casting requirement, not an invented identity. An actual clinician must review and own first-person patient wording. Illustrative lifestyle footage represents desired everyday activities, not documented Motilli outcomes or actual patients.

### Product source correction

The old S27/S28 packshot proxies visibly include stale 5 g / other front-label claims. They are composition references only. Use current approved product artwork for final shots; exactly two green heart-shaped gummies. Verify final physical label reference before media generation.

### Production routing

Storyboard first is the default ad workflow. New stills use GPT Image 2; new video uses Google Omni. Subsequent editing uses the current documented internal editor. Do not start a production timeline from these estimated timecodes; conform after voice and silence cleanup.
