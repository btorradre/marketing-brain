## Conform order

1. Lock the reviewed dialogue and cast.
2. Create the natural conversation audio. Apply the requested 1.1× speed, remove dead air while preserving intelligibility, then align word timestamps. Never stack another speed adjustment on an already accelerated file.
3. Assemble the complete podcast performance from that final audio.
4. Produce only the science and product inserts needed by these cues; use requested duration/aspect ratio directly where the provider supports them.
5. Place every insert on the named spoken cue, maintain continuous dialogue, and return to the correct speaker.
6. Add short captions and ingredient labels, then inspect mouth sync, all speaker handoffs, first product reveal, scientific continuity, current label art and the final offer hold.
