### M07 · 01:04–01:16 · 12s · Mechanism / Medication context

GLP-1 medicines such as Wegovy can slow how quickly your stomach empties. Bloating is also a listed side effect. So this deserves more attention than simply blaming your last meal.

### M08 · 01:16–01:26 · 10s · Mechanism / Explain the movement

Your stomach does more than hold food. Its muscles mix what you eat and gradually move it into your small intestine. That process takes time.

### M09 · 01:26–01:36 · 10s · Mechanism / Connect back to fullness

When your stomach empties more slowly, that lingering fullness can make more sense. The amount on your plate is only one part of the story.

### M10 · 01:36–01:48 · 12s · Mechanism / Separate contributors

### M11 · 01:48–01:59 · 11s · Failed attempts / Why random additions confuse

And adding more to your routine isn't automatically the answer. Some people get more gas with extra fiber. The type, the amount, and your own response all matter.
