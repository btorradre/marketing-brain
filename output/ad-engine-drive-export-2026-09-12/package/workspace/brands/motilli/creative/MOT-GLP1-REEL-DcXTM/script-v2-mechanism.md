## Mechanism logic

The requested structure is three distinct ingredient/action explanations → combined product. It is not a fiber product introduction followed by two ingredient names. The first two intended actions are design requirements from the brand's existing mechanism, not scientific conclusions from this revision.

Interpret the user's “low-soluble” as **low-viscosity soluble** prebiotic fiber, consistent with the existing brand brief. Low solubility means something different. FOS is readily soluble and low viscosity; that does not mean zero gas, zero stool bulk, or no possibility of bloating. Do not give it psyllium's thick-gel water-retention mechanism. The current gummy's FOS identity must still agree with the finalized label/specification.

The fiber dialogue explains the missing causal sequence: resistance to human digestion → reaches colon → bacterial utilization. Its physical distinction from psyllium is viscosity, not that psyllium cannot help constipation. No comparative superiority or GLP-1 clinical relief claim follows from these properties alone.
