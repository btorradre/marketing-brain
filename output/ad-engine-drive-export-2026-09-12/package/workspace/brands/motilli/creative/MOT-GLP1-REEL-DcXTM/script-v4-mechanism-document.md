## Editorial rationale

Each ingredient now has a specific intended job in the brand's three-part approach. Movement precedes odor, which precedes prebiotic/regularity support; the brand appears after those explanations. The sulfur-burp complaint is introduced before chlorophyllin so its intended job answers a stated problem. The earlier fiber attempt opens a natural objection when FOS arrives; viscosity and microbial utilization answer it.

MiraLAX and psyllium retain accurate mechanisms and matter-of-fact delivery. The gut expresses disappointment with incomplete comfort, not hatred of medicines. The ending presents the combined formula; no rapid recovery, abandonment of medication, forced clearance, or guaranteed relief.

## Evidence notes, not spoken copy

- The first two roles are **the brand document's intended formulation rationale**, not verified clinical actions of this finished gummy. “Included for” describes that rationale and must not be represented as proof of an outcome. Source adaptation does not settle claim substantiation.
- Do not upgrade celery's line to “restarts the stomach,” “reverses GLP-1 slowing,” “stimulates contractions,” or an enzyme claim. The reviewed apigenin work is preclinical and concerns colonic physiology, not demonstrated improved gastric emptying from Motilli.
- Chlorophyllin's intended odor role does not establish gas-volume reduction, bloat relief, or complete neutralization of gut hydrogen sulfide at the product dose. Odor research and formulation identity remain distinct from demonstrated efficacy.
- FOS differs from psyllium in viscosity and fermentability. It does not form psyllium's thick gel; this is not a zero-bulk or zero-gas claim. Fermentation may produce gas.
- New primary-source check: Tominaga et al., 1999, `https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bifidus1996/18/1/18_1_49/_article`. At 3g/day FOS, stool-frequency findings depended on baseline frequency and comparator; this supports qualified regularity discussion, not universal efficacy or a GLP-1 Motilli claim.
- Earlier superseded documents disagree about fiber identity, vitamin pillars, colon versus stomach emphasis and doses. They were inspected, not silently adopted. Current draft follows the user's named three ingredients and the canonical section-4 order, using FOS from the current product record rather than importing PHGG evidence.
