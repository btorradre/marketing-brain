# Motilli — “You’re fiber too?”

September 11, 2026. Original character-dialogue adaptation of the supplied SyncNexis reel. Fictional personification, not a customer testimonial. Scope: analysis, writing and an editing plan; no storyboard or production requested.

**GUT:** “MiraLAX? I tried you already.”

**MIRALAX:** “I draw water into your colon to soften your stool.”

**PSYLLIUM FIBER:** “And I hold water and add bulk to help it pass.”

**GUT:** “Then why do I still feel so full?”

**NARRATOR:** “GLP-1s can slow stomach emptying. Helping constipation doesn't necessarily fix that full feeling.”

**MOTILLI:** “I'm Motilli. My prebiotic fiber feeds beneficial gut bacteria.”

**GUT:** “Wait. You're fiber too?”

**MOTILLI:** “Yes. Different fibers have different properties. Mine is FOS, combined with celery juice powder and chlorophyllin in two daily gummies.”

**GUT:** “Okay. Show me what's in it.”

**NARRATOR:** “Meet Motilli. Explore the ingredients at getmotilli.com.”

## Direction and evidence boundary

Gut: tired, skeptical, dryly funny; curiosity replaces disappointment at the end. MiraLAX and psyllium: competent, matter-of-fact helpers. Motilli: straightforward, no superhero promise. The ingredient explanation is the payoff, not a depicted clinical recovery.

Plan approximately 45–50 seconds including reaction pauses and a two-second end hold; this is an estimated read, not generated audio. The reference uses only about 54 words across 28.4 seconds. Two alternatives, a GLP-1 distinction and a product explanation need more time than its one-pill encounter.

The draft deliberately uses the confirmed product format and general FOS function. The current product's human efficacy for GLP-1 delayed gastric emptying, superiority to MiraLAX/psyllium, and gas-neutralizing or stomach-restarting effects are not established by the evidence reviewed. A stronger Motilli-resolves-the-fullness ending needs relevant finished-product evidence. This version does not claim that outcome or direct viewers to stop prescribed care.

MiraLAX and psyllium are differentiated accurately; their properties overlap in retaining/increasing stool water. FOS is not unique to Motilli and may also produce gas. Do not depict it as universally better tolerated, zero-bulk, or gas-free.
