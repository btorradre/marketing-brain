## Updated evidence

Apigenin experiment: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0195502 — mouse model of high-fat-diet obesity, 10mg/kg/day, eight weeks. Reduced colonic inflammatory markers and normalized selected evoked contraction responses; not human GLP-1 gastric-emptying evidence. Chlorophyllin odor trial: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2675439/ — colostomy population and no significant odor advantage over placebo; do not equate odor with gas volume/bloating.
