You feel full for longer. But you still want to go to the bathroom comfortably and get on with your day.

Second, chlorophyllin, a derivative of the green pigment in plants. It's been studied for odor control, with mixed findings. Reducing odor isn't the same thing as removing gas or relieving constipation.
