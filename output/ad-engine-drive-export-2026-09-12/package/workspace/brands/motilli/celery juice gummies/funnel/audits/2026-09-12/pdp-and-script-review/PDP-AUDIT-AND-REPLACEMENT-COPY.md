# Motilli PDP audit and concrete replacement direction

September 12, 2026. Exact live ad destination: https://getmotilli.com/products/motilli-3-bottle-90day-reset . Read current page text and four full-size live carousel assets. Reused same-session mobile viewport, bundle and successful cart-test receipts in the parent folder. No live page/theme/product changes made. This document is a reviewable copy/layout proposal, not a deployed PDP or a claim of conversion improvement.

## What the evidence supports

The PDP is technically usable in the tested mobile Chromium flow. It matches the broad GLP-1 audience and named product. It nevertheless contains a concrete factual inconsistency and several reasons for a skeptical buyer to hesitate. These are observed defects and testable persuasion hypotheses, not demonstrated reasons every visitor abandoned. Earlier campaign snapshot contained only 44 landing-page-view events, not a meaningful estimate of this PDP's long-run conversion rate. No session-level Shopify/Clarity conversion cohort or controlled PDP comparison has been retrieved.

## Page order to test

1. Compact actual product image and one clear supported outcome.
2. Product descriptor and serving; one concise explanation of the relevant difference; readable link to Supplement Facts.
3. Pack choices with total price most prominent, serving duration, shipping and exact CTA price. Keep the guarantee directly under CTA.
4. Short “What's in your daily serving?” panel with type, amount and real role.
5. “What the evidence does—and does not—show” accessible summary or appropriately titled ingredient-research panel, with direct primary study links and ingredient-versus-product distinction.
6. Genuine customer experiences addressing regularity/discomfort from the relevant audience. Do not add clinical outcomes or stock-photo identities to create proof.
7. Actual routine, realistic expectations and medication-related questions.
8. Full guarantee, delivery/returns, contact and company details, plus final purchase block.

Keep the existing brand identity and package accurate. A sticky mobile buying control can make an informed return to cart easier after scrolling; it should show the selected pack/total and respect modal/chat overlays. Its uplift must be tested. A purchase button below a hero is not automatically a design error.

## Proposed hero copy

This is a narrower regularity-support positioning proposal. It deliberately does not claim that Motilli restores medication-slowed stomach emptying. Confirm the finished-product facts and permitted benefit basis before publication.

**Headline:** Support more regular mornings.

**Descriptor:** Motilli Celery Juice Gummies

**Supporting copy:** A daily gummy with FOS prebiotic fiber, celery juice powder and chlorophyllin. Two gummies a day, with no powder to mix.

**Supporting facts:** 60 gummies • 30 daily servings • Green apple flavor

**Evidence link:** See the ingredients and amounts

**Why this direction:** A familiar outcome is easier to understand than “works at the top.” The descriptor tells visitors what it is. The formula sentence and facts make it tangible. This wording offers no unsupported promise of eliminating all GLP-1 side effects. It is a candidate to test after aligning the ad's promise, not a stealth positioning change on the current live page.

## Proof and claims

The strongest scientific source currently located for this formula's listed fiber is [Tominaga et al., 1999](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/bifidus1996/18/1/18_1_49/_article). The study examined 3g/day FOS in healthy female volunteers. Its stratified results differ by initial stool frequency; it cannot substantiate a universal percentage, a Motilli outcome, or reversal of GLP-1-related stomach delay.

The cited [apigenin study](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0195502) concerns a mouse model and colonic inflammation/motor abnormalities. It does not establish human gastric-emptying effects of 50mg celery powder in this product. This is a limit of the inspected evidence, not an exhaustive finding that no other study exists.

Claims that need original documentation before reuse: 91% results, 73% reorder rate, 9,481 review count/source, clinical testing of the finished formula, current manufacturing/testing badges, absence of medication interaction, obligatory ingredient ratio, inevitable 90-day reset and predictable week-specific effects. Do not promote older brand mechanism documents into clinical evidence. The workspace's mechanism-revision-notes.md already records these distinctions.

A scientific explanation may be true for a medication while the proposed supplement solution remains unproven. [Wegovy labeling](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=ee06186f-2aa3-4990-a760-757579d8f77b) supports delayed gastric emptying; it does not establish that Motilli fixes it.

## Copy to retire or substantiate

- Claims that all alternatives work on the wrong organ, that fiber merely accumulates, or that MiraLAX only pushes material out.
- Claims that the exact three ingredients are required in a special ratio or that Motilli is the only combined option, absent evidence.
- A 90-day physiological-reset necessity inferred from a three-bottle offer or guarantee duration.
- Certainty about keeping medication effects unchanged, universal absence of cramps/gas, and promised week-by-week outcomes without matching support.
- An ingredient-research badge presented as a clinical trial of the finished product.

This is also a conversion concern: a buyer who has already tried products has reasons to scrutinize why this one is different. Stronger language cannot repair conflicting facts or missing proof.

## Testing and measurement

First correct factual inconsistencies regardless of any A/B result. Log these corrections so comparisons remain interpretable. Capture product/session totals from first-party analytics, separating campaign traffic and synthetic tests. Verify product_added_to_cart and Meta browser/server AddToCart with matching event IDs where applicable.

Then run separate tests: (A) current page hierarchy versus compact benefit/product-proof layout, pack default unchanged; (B) current three-bottle default versus one-bottle default, copy/layout and prices held fixed. Keep the ad constant in each PDP test. If revising the ad too, run it as a separate creative test or an explicitly planned factorial test rather than attributing a combined lift to the page.

Primary diagnostic: unique eligible sessions adding to cart / eligible product-page sessions. Business outcomes: purchases/session, revenue/session, contribution margin and refund rate. Guardrails: checkout completion, AOV, shipping exposure, actual variant/quantity and event duplication. Hold rate/CTR cannot establish PDP success. Define the baseline and minimum meaningful effect before choosing a sample; no fixed visitor count here is presented as universally adequate.
