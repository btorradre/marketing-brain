**Visual:** Talking head, looking up like she's just realized something. This is the *cognitive synchrony* line — she thinks the viewer's thought one beat after the viewer has it.

## Why this works for a solution-aware GLP-1 avatar (not problem-aware)

### What changed vs. the reference VSL

| Reference (problem-aware) | Motilli (solution-aware) |
|---|---|
| "If your belly looks like this" — discovers the problem | "If you're on Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro" — names the cause she already knows |
| Hook is body-shape callout | Hook is **medication + symptom** callout |
| Spends 33s on symptom discovery | Spends 17s on symptom validation (she already knows them) |
| Vindication needed BEFORE blame transfer (she didn't know it was "real") | Vindication is faster — she already knows it's real, she just needs to be *seen* |
| Mechanism reveal is *what's causing this?* | Mechanism reveal is *why hasn't anything I've tried worked?* |
| Villain reveal = "biofilm" (new word she's never heard) | Villain reveal = "smooth muscle slowdown" (literal anatomy, not a new vocabulary word) |
| Solution = ingredient she's never heard of (bromelain) | Solution = ingredient she's never heard of (apigenin) — same trick |
| Authority = Reddit gut-health practitioner | Authority = Reddit GI nurse in a GLP-1 sub (much closer parasocial match for our avatar) |
| Time on emotional groundwork: 33s | Time on emotional groundwork: 18s — *the solution-aware avatar wants the answer faster* |
| Time on mechanism block: ~75s | Time on mechanism block: ~70s — roughly preserved |

### Why the hook compresses but the mechanism doesn't

A solution-aware avatar doesn't need you to *prove* she has the problem. She lives it. So the front-end (hook + vindication) compresses by half. But she *does* still need the mechanism block at full length — because the entire point of the ad is to install a *new explanation* of why her existing efforts failed. That belief shift takes just as long whether she's problem-aware or solution-aware.

### Where this script is *quieter* than the reference

- **No constructed metaphors.** No "locked door," no "rate-limiter," no "broken loop" branded vocabulary. The mechanism is described in literal anatomy (smooth muscle, water absorption, inflammation). Per memory: this avatar responds to literal mechanism, not copywriter frameworks.
- **Confident competitor dismissals, not deep proof.** "Probiotics and gut tea were never going to be enough on their own" is the *entire* competitor takedown. No proof needed — confidence does the work. Same pattern as the reference's ashwagandha line.
- **No pre-empted objections about manufacturing, extraction, heavy metals, third-party testing.** This avatar hasn't formed those objections yet. Surfacing them would create doubt where there was none.
- **No gastroparesis urgency.** Quality-of-life-now (jeans don't button, looks 5 months pregnant) + fear-of-quitting (the line "was making me consider quitting") do the urgency work. Per memory: only ~2% of GLP-1 users get gastroparesis — not worth the urgency lever.

### Where this script *amplifies* what the reference does

- **The "I love my GLP-1, I'm not going off it" line** at 0:10. This is *bigger* than anything the reference does emotionally. Our avatar's #1 fear is "will I have to quit my GLP-1?" — naming that and resolving it in one sentence is enormous, and the reference VSL doesn't have an equivalent.
- **"Some of it actually made the bloating worse"** at 0:33. This is the avatar's lived experience that every other ad ignores. The viewer who has personally added fiber and gotten *worse* will lock in here.
- **"They're literally right"** at 1:40. This is the line that makes a viewer who has felt crazy for years feel sane in one sentence.

### Pacing-script congruence (mirrors the reference)

| Block | Time | Cut speed | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook stack | 0:00–0:07 | ~1s cuts | Pattern interrupt — three rapid B-roll cuts mirror three triple-callout phrases |
| Possibility | 0:07–0:10 | 1.5s | Emotional pivot — slow enough to feel contrast |
| Vindication + identity | 0:10–0:18 | Hold 6s on talking head | Cognitive reset — "I love my GLP-1" needs to land |
| Symptom stack | 0:18–0:35 | ~2s cuts | Recognition pacing — beat to feel each symptom |
| Act break | 0:35–0:36 | 1s hold | Reset before mechanism |
| Blame transfer | 0:36–0:45 | 2s — held on face | Belief landing pad |
| Authority laundering | 0:45–0:55 | 2.5s cuts (Reddit + study) | Slow enough to read |
| Mechanism reveal | 0:55–1:52 | Talking-head/graphic sandwich, ~2.5s | Prevents science-block dropoff |
| Solution intro | 1:52–2:05 | 3.5s — slows down | Apigenin needs to be absorbed |
| Bridge to offer | 2:05+ | — | Product reveal |

The cut cadence is fastest at emotional peaks and slowest at cognitive peaks — same architecture as the reference.

- Don't show a doctor in a white coat — this avatar doesn't trust them (her doctor didn't catch this)
