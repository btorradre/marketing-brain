## Top-line read

This is a textbook hormonal-belly / gut-health "car-talker" front-end for a perimenopause supplement. The avatar is a 35-55 woman who has tried diets, supplements, and even doctors, and is exhausted by the lack of answers. What's working: the hook stacks three high-specificity body-shape callouts in the first ~3 seconds (belly pooch, hips/cellulite, bloated belly), each with a real body shot rather than a stock image — that's a brutal scroll-stop because the avatar literally sees herself. The before/after at 7-10s is the spine of the entire ad: tear-streaked exhausted "before" matched to smiling, well-lit "after" inside 2 seconds, which earns the right to ask for 30 more seconds of attention. From there it's a clean failed-attempts montage (pants, sausage feeling, bloat, blood draw, thyroid ultrasound) that pre-empties every objection the avatar has been telling herself ("maybe it's my diet, maybe it's my thyroid"), then transitions into a 60-second mechanism reveal anchored on a new villain — *biofilms* — explained with a clean dental-plaque analogy.

What it's doing brilliantly is *blame transfer*. By beat 17-19, every previous failed effort (diets, exercise, ashwagandha, mushroom coffee) is reframed as "never going to work in the first place," which is the single highest-leverage belief shift you can install in this avatar. She doesn't have to admit failure — she gets to be the smart one who finally figured out the system was rigged against her. The Reddit post + scientific study screen-cap at 43-50s does the *I'm not pulling this out of nowhere* job without requiring on-screen credentials.

What I'd fix: the agitation block (beats 8-15) is the longest stretch without a payoff promise being touched — risk of drop-off around the 20-25s mark. The animated 3D anatomy is high-production but uses three slightly different aesthetics (translucent blue body, pink silhouette, photoreal red intestine, cool teal hologram, x-ray skeleton), which subtly signals "stock B-roll" rather than a unified mechanism world. The "estrogen drops → cortisol climbs → bad bacteria multiply → biofilms form → nutrients bounce off" chain is solid copy but it's *five* mechanism steps with no breath in between — at this avatar's sophistication level you can probably cut it to 3 and gain retention.
