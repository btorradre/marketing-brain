Think of digestion as a highway. Food enters at the on-ramp — the stomach. Travels through the middle — the small intestine. Exits at the off-ramp — the colon.

Miralax pulls water into the colon to soften what's sitting there. But nothing was sitting there. The blockage was in her stomach, six feet upstream.

Fiber gummies add bulk to help things pass through the colon. When food can't get out of the stomach, adding bulk is packing more cars onto a highway where the on-ramp is closed.

Dulcolax forces the colon to contract. But you can squeeze an empty tube all day long. Nothing comes out if nothing went in.

Smooth Move tea stimulates the lower intestine. Same problem. You can't flush a pipe from the bottom when the clog is at the top.
