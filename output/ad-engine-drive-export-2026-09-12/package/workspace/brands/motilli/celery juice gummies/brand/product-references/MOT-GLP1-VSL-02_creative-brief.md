## 2. The Visual Format (Locked, copy 1:1)

### Frame layout
- **A-roll (talking head):** Creator sitting in driver's seat of a parked car, seatbelt on, soft natural daylight, warm pastel sweater (the reference uses mint — Motilli should use a warm coral / soft pink / off-white to lean into Motilli's brand palette without copying mint). Fills the **top ~58%** of the 9:16 frame.
- **B-roll PIP:** Bottom **~40%** of the frame. Image or short clip with a **thin 4-6 px solid border** in the exact creator-sweater color so it visually "hangs off" her shirt — the seatbelt and sweater edge naturally transition into the PIP frame.

### B-roll cadence (LOCKED RULES — read before timing anything)
- **There are long stretches with NO PIP.** Bottom half is just the bottom of the car-selfie. These are deliberate breathing moments — intro setup, emotional/reflective lines ("am I broken?", "I'm not selling you anything"), and the build-up before reveals. Some of these stretches run 8–15 seconds with no PIP at all.
- **When PIP IS on, each unique overlay holds for 4–5 seconds before swapping** to the next one. Do NOT cut B-roll to the caption rhythm — captions tick every 1–1.5s, PIPs hold 4–5s. The mismatch is the format.
- **No rapid-fire montages.** Even on listing beats ("I tried everything…"), the PIP underneath is ONE static composite that holds 4–5s. Captions do the rapid-fire work, not the visual.
- Rough target: **21 unique PIP overlays across 2:50** (locked to Avatar_Video.mp4 timing). Cumulative PIP-on time should be roughly 60–65% of runtime; the rest is talking head + caption only.

### Sticker caption style (this is THE signature element)
- **Pill shape:** white rounded rectangle, ~14 px corner radius, slight drop shadow.
- **Font:** Bold sans-serif (SF Pro Rounded Bold / TikTok Sans Bold / Inter ExtraBold). Black, 100% opacity.
- **Word count per sticker:** **2–4 words MAX per line, 1–2 lines max.** Captions advance every ~1.0–1.8s in sync with speech.
- **Position:** Horizontal-centered. Vertically anchored at **~62% down the talking-head crop**, sitting just above the chin/collar — never overlapping the PIP boundary.
- **Always present.** Even when no PIP is showing, captions are on screen.

### Audio
- Single take VO, light room reverb, NO background music for first 8s. After hook lands, low-volume ambient pad (soft pulse) underneath until the close.

## 5. Production Notes for the Editor

### Caption sticker generation
- All caption stickers should be generated as **transparent-bg PNGs** so they can be animated separately. Use the editor's title tool with these exact specs:
  - Background: `#FFFFFF`, corner radius `14px`, padding `18px horizontal / 12px vertical`, drop-shadow `0 2 6 rgba(0,0,0,0.18)`
  - Text: SF Pro Rounded Bold or Inter ExtraBold, `42–48 pt`, `#000000`, line-height `1.05`, center-aligned
- Animation: **0.08s scale-in (95% → 100%)**, hold, **0.05s scale-out** on swap. NO sliding, NO fading — snap in/out feels native.

### PIP frame border
- Border color: match the creator's sweater. For Motilli's coral/peach palette, use `#E8B8A0` or `#F0C7B0`.
- Border width: `5px`, fully rounded `0px` (sharp corners — the reference uses sharp corners).
- PIP transitions: hard-cut in, hard-cut out. No fades.

### Tone of the creator's delivery
- The reference creator delivers in a "venting to a friend in a parking lot" tone. Lots of micro-pauses, eye contact with camera, occasional half-smiles when she's being self-deprecating. Brief our actor explicitly:
  - "You're not selling. You're recapping a 2am rabbit hole you went down. The product reveal should feel like 'oh yeah and then this random thing worked', NOT a pitch."
