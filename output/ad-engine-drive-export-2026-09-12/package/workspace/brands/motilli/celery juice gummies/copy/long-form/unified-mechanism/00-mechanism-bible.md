## The signal molecule — always "butyrate"

## The causal chain (teach it in this exact order)
1. Your colon is a muscle. To move waste, it has to contract. To contract, it needs a signal.
2. That signal is a short-chain fatty acid called butyrate.
3. You don't make butyrate yourself. The bacteria in your colon make it, by fermenting the fiber that reaches them. Bacteria → butyrate → the muscle contracts → things move. Runs quietly your whole life.
4. A GLP-1 works by slowing digestion — slower transit, fuller longer, eat less. That's the point, and it works.
5. But that slowdown reaches all the way to the colon. Less material reaches the bacteria → fewer of them → less butyrate → weaker signal → slower colon → even less reaches the bacteria tomorrow.
6. **It feeds on itself. Every week a little tighter. The medication holds that first slowdown in place, so the loop can't break on its own.**
7. This is why it gets WORSE, not better. You're not failing to adjust — you're in a self-tightening loop.

- Constipation lives in a different system: the colon and its bacteria. The slowed transit starves the butyrate-makers — as a side effect, not the purpose.
- Because they're separate systems, you can fix the gut without touching the weight loss. **You do not have to quit the medication.** This is the single most important belief the copy installs.
