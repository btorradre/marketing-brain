## How to read this
Every line has up to three rows:
- **CAPTION** — the bottom word-pop caption. On every single line. Black text, white rounded boxes, bottom-center.
- **PIP** — the center picture-in-picture insert. Either `YES` (with a GENERATE below) or `NONE — face only`.
- **GENERATE** — the detailed prompt for the visual. Tagged `[GENERATE]` (make it), `[STOCK/UGC]` (source a clip), or `[OWN]` (Motilli product asset).

**Rule:** PiP is ON only when she names a concrete thing you can show. It's OFF (her face + caption) on authority / logic / emotional beats. ~9 of 21 lines are face-only on purpose — don't fill every line.

## Global style
- **Captions:** bold rounded sans (Poppins / Montserrat SemiBold), black on white rounded boxes, bottom-center, phrase-by-phrase pop-in, on 100% of frames.
- **Top banner:** hook only (~first 5s), same box style, top-center, then gone.
- **PiP window:** square / 4:3, centered over her chest, rounded corners, soft shadow, ~55–65% frame width, hard cut in/out.
- **Cards / callouts:** clean white "clinical" cards or white text. **Red X:** bold red over enemy visuals.
