# AD 5A GRADE: "Why Fiber Made It Worse" — Complete Rewrite

---

## SUMMARY

**Copy assessed:** "She added fiber on her doctor's orders and spent six hours learning why it backfired"
**Word count:** ~3,400
**Narrator:** Karen, 57, office manager, skeptic-convert archetype
**Awareness level targeted:** Solution Aware (hostile to fiber)
**Cause-and-effect chain:** GLP-1 slows stomach muscles > food ferments > sulfur gas > nothing moves downstream > fiber adds bulk to stalled system > bloating and constipation worsen. Problem is upstream (stomach), not downstream (colon). Plain cause-and-effect. No named framework detected.

## DIMENSION 1: BELIEF-SHIFT ARCHITECTURE (22/25)

### Belief Shift 1 -- "My suffering is real": 5/5

**Evidence:**
- Bathroom mirror: "my belly was distended and rock hard and when I pressed on it with my fingertips I actually flinched because the pressure inside was that intense"
- Work pants/desk: "sitting at my desk calculating whether I could make it to the bathroom and back without anyone noticing how long I'd been gone, because when it finally did come it came all at once and I could be in there for twenty minutes"
- Sulfur burps in meetings: "I would be on the phone with a vendor and feel it building and have to mute myself and let it out and then unmute and hope they couldn't hear the pause"
- November crisis: "propped up on three pillows with my hand on my stomach...crying. Not the big kind. The exhausted kind."

**Assessment:** At least five distinct mirror moments, all surgically specific to life consequences -- not surface symptoms. The twenty-minute bathroom break calculation, the muting-during-vendor-calls, the lying awake at 2 AM. These are the unspoken internal dialogues of GLP-1 constipation sufferers. The copy earns the full 5 because a reader living this would screenshot these lines.

### Belief Shift 2 -- "My approach is wrong about something": 4/5

**Evidence:**
- "She said it's common on GLP-1s. She said take Metamucil."
- "I called the nurse line. She said give it time. Keep taking the fiber. Your body is adjusting."
- "This is what you tell yourself when the thing your doctor recommended is making you worse. You blame your body, not the recommendation."

**Assessment:** The trust vacuum is built primarily through witnessing -- we watch the doctor give the Metamucil recommendation, watch Karen comply, watch it make everything worse. The nurse line scene reinforces the dismissal. However, the copy loses a point because the narrator DOES editorialize. The line "This is what you tell yourself when the thing your doctor recommended is making you worse" steps outside the scene to comment directly on the reader's psychology. It is a telling line, not a showing line. Similarly, the final close section says outright: "Your doctor is not wrong that you need help. Your doctor is wrong about what kind of help you need." This is direct address telling, not witnessing. The scene work is strong enough to carry a 4, but the editorializing in both the middle and the close prevents the full 5.

### Belief Shift 3 -- "The problem is in the stomach, not the colon": 4/5

**Evidence:**
- Cause-and-effect chain delivered through the Reddit nurse's explanation: "The medication slows the stomach. That's how it works...food sits too long. It starts to ferment. Bacteria break it down and produce sulfur gas."
- Philosophical binary analogy: "I was loading freight onto a train with a dead engine."
- Villain teardowns: "Miralax pulls water into the colon. Downstream. Doesn't touch the stomach." / "Gas-X breaks up gas bubbles in the intestine. Downstream." / "The probiotic adds bacteria to the gut. But the problem isn't bacteria."

**Assessment:** The cause-and-effect chain is clean, delivered in plain language, and arrives through a narrative research scene (Reddit at 2 AM). Villain teardowns are efficient one-line cause applications -- each states which cause the villain misses. No named frameworks. The mechanism section is calm and explanatory, contrasting well with the visceral emotional beats before it. However, the mechanism runs slightly long. The paragraph beginning "More bulk. More weight. More material sitting in a stomach that couldn't push it through" restates what was already established. "Significantly worse. Measurably worse. Every single day." is rhetorical hammering, not forward motion. The fiber-as-freight analogy is used, then the copy keeps going with restatements. This is a minor violation of forward motion within the mechanism section. Additionally, the summary paragraph "Every single thing I tried works on the wrong end of the system. Every one. Including the fiber my doctor told me to take" restates the conclusion already made clear by the individual teardowns. A 4, not a 5, because of the restating.

### Belief Shift 4 -- "The solution needs A+B+C": 5/5

**Evidence:**
- Three separate discovery nights: apigenin (night 1, gastric motility), chlorophyll (night 2, sulfur binding), micro-dose prebiotic fiber (night 3, via another woman's post).
- Existing products fail checklist: "A celery supplement with apigenin but no chlorophyll. A chlorophyll capsule with no prokinetic. A fiber gummy with a full dose that would put me right back where I started. Nothing passed the checklist."
- Product enters reluctantly: "a small company's product page loaded between two other listings I'd already dismissed...I almost scrolled past it...I'd never heard of it."
- Four-day delay: "I did not buy it. I sat on it for four days."
- Confirmation beat: ingredient panel check, Fakespot, BBB, one-star reviews, third-party testing, GMP certification. Pectin vs. gelatin distinction. Concentrated extract vs. generic celery powder.

**Assessment:** This is textbook UMS discovery sequence execution. Each component discovered individually across separate research nights with narrative momentum between each. Existing products explicitly fail the checklist the reader has already built. Product enters through search results, not through pitch. The four-day wait is a strong skepticism beat. The confirmation beat maps product to pre-built criteria without shifting into selling mode. The category differentiators (pectin-based, concentrated extract) feel like what this narrator would actually check. Earns the full 5.

### Belief Shift 5 -- "This works and I keep what I value": 4/5

**Evidence:**
- Progressive timeline: Day 3 (nothing), Day 4 (sulfur burps decrease), Day 10 (burps mostly gone), Week 2 (first normal bowel movement in four months), Week 3 (wall -- bloating comes back), Week 4 (going almost every day), Week 5 (Wednesday morning kitchen scene).
- Skepticism at each stage: "another dud" text to sister, "I'm suspicious of coincidences," Week 3 setback with "of course this was too good to be true."
- Life moments: "I sat there and thought about the fact that this was the first time in almost four months that my body did what it was supposed to do without a laxative"
- Pain-to-payoff callback: Kitchen scene resolves the bathroom mirror scene ("I wasn't holding my stomach. I wasn't pressing on my abdomen to test how distended it was.")
- Social proof: Sister's independent results, wearing regular jeans to church, crying in the bathroom from relief.
- Close: "standing there with my coffee and nothing to manage and nothing to calculate and nothing to hide, that's my morning now."

**Assessment:** Timeline is progressive with skepticism at every stage. The Week 3 setback is a strong anti-AI move -- it introduces mess into the arc. Life moments dominate over metrics. The kitchen scene is a genuine payoff that resolves the bathroom mirror pain scene. Social proof is woven through the sister's parallel story without testimonial formatting. The close ends on a life image. However, the very last two paragraphs lose some power. "I'm telling this because I know there's a woman reading this who just bought a bottle of Metamucil" breaks the fourth wall and shifts from narrative to direct address. The line "You don't load more freight onto a train with a dead engine. You fix the engine" re-teaches the mechanism analogy in the close, which the framework penalizes. The final image ("standing there with my coffee...that's my morning now") recovers, but the close section contains both a direct-address teaching moment and a mechanism re-teach before landing on the image. This prevents the full 5.
