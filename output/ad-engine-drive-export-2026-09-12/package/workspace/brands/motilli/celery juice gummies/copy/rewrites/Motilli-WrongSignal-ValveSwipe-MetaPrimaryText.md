## AWARENESS CALIBRATION (v3, 2026-08-10)

**The rule this file kept breaking:** the moment copy references what she has already tried
(water, fiber, her doctor's advice, laxatives), it is talking to a **solution aware** buyer. She
has to have gone shopping for a fix for that to land. Our avatar mostly has not. She knows she
feels awful and has not connected it to the shot.

**Problem aware opens on the root cause.** The hook promises the explanation, the ⚠️ block is
pure symptom recognition with zero attempted remedies in it, and the entire middle of the ad is
the mechanism. Failed solutions are not the premise and are not in the first half.
