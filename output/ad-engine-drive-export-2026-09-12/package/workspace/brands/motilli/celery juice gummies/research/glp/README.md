## How to Use This Research

### The Research Workflow

**Step 1: Understand the Master Avatar**
- Start with the **00-master-avatar** folder
- Read `master-avatar-voc.md` for the foundational customer voice (600+ quotes)
- Read `master-copywriting-brief.md` for complete strategic positioning, mechanism education, and funnel architecture
- *Time investment:* 60–90 minutes

**Step 2: Select Your Target Sub-Avatar**
- Choose 1–2 sub-avatars matching the pain point you're addressing:
  - **01-constipation:** Highest pain intensity + ER visits (primary market)
  - **02-diarrhea:** Loss of bodily control + public urgency (secondary, high-growth)
  - **03-nausea:** Low-grade daily misery + food anxiety (persistent problem)
  - **04-sulfur-burps:** Social exile + intimacy rupture (identity-level shame)
  - **05-bloating:** Visible weight loss paradox + wardrobe crisis (younger audience hit hardest)
  - **06-hair-loss:** Delayed aging paradox + identity crisis (appearance-focused segment)

**Step 3: Read the Sub-Avatar Profile**
- Open the `sub-avatar-profile.md` in your chosen folder
- Focus on:
  - **SECTION 1:** Deep pain themes (why she suffers, not just what)
  - **SECTION 2:** Emotional expressions + scroll-stop phrases (copy language)
  - **SECTION 3:** Routine + situation discovery (daily friction points + purchase triggers)
  - **SECTION 6:** Angle + concept strategy (3 winning concepts for your sub-avatar)
  - **SECTION 7:** "For Dummies" summary (quick reference)
- *Time investment:* 30–45 minutes per sub-avatar

**Step 4: Reference the Raw Research**
- Open `reddit-data/raw-research.md` for your chosen sub-avatar
- Search for specific pain themes, objections, or copy language
- Use verbatim Reddit quotes to validate your messaging
- *Time investment:* 15–30 minutes (as needed for fact-checking)

**Step 5: Use the Awareness Messaging Matrix (07)**
- Navigate to **07-awareness-level-messaging** to calibrate copy by customer awareness level
- Map your messaging to:
  - **Unaware** (0–5% of market): Introduce problem + mechanism
  - **Problem-Aware** (20–30%): Validate pain + educate on root cause
  - **Solution-Aware** (45–50%): Provide proof + overcome objections
  - **Product-Aware** (15%): Differentiate + close
  - **Most Aware** (5–10%): Retention + subscription messaging

## Cross-Reference Guide

### Multi-Symptom Sufferers (The Overlaps)

Women on GLP-1s often experience **multiple symptoms simultaneously** or **symptom rotation over time**. Use this guide to write copy for women with co-occurring side effects.

#### **Constipation + Bloating (High Frequency Overlap)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Both caused by slowed gastric emptying (upstream problem) + impaired intestinal motility (downstream problem)
- **Behavioral impact:** "I'm backed up AND bloated" — the worst of both worlds
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with the MORE painful/urgent symptom (constipation)
  - Reference bloating as the "secondary prisoner" effect
  - Position Motilli as addressing the ROOT CAUSE (gastric motility) that fixes both
  - Proof angle: "One woman went from 11-day constipation AND pregnancy-belly bloating to normal digestion in 3 weeks"

#### **Sulfur Burps + Constipation (High Frequency Overlap)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Both caused by food fermenting in slow stomach (hydrogen sulfide gas + bacterial overgrowth)
- **Behavioral impact:** "I can't go to the bathroom AND I can't leave the house" (combined isolation)
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with sulfur burps (more socially devastating)
  - Frame constipation as the CAUSE of the burps ("backed-up food = fermentation = smell")
  - Position Motilli as fixing the root cause (stomach motility) that eliminates both
  - Proof angle: "One woman stopped having sulfur burps in week 2, AND restored normal bowel movements by week 3"

#### **Nausea + Constipation (High Frequency Overlap)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Both from slow gastric movement (nausea = food sitting too long; constipation = slow downstream movement)
- **Behavioral impact:** "I feel sick AND can't go" — no relief possible
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with nausea (daily, persistent, food-anxiety inducing)
  - Frame constipation as the "proof" that gut isn't moving (validates nausea complaint)
  - Position Motilli as the prokinetic solution that restores both stomach AND intestinal movement
  - Proof angle: "One woman went from daily nausea + 4-day constipation cycles to eating normally + regular bathroom visits in week 2"

#### **Bloating + Nausea (Moderate Frequency Overlap)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Both from gas accumulation + impaired digestion (bloating visible; nausea = internal pressure feeling)
- **Behavioral impact:** "I feel sick from the pressure and I look pregnant" (internal agony + external humiliation)
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with bloating (visible = more shameful)
  - Frame nausea as the internal experience of the bloating process
  - Position Motilli as restoring stomach function (reduces both)
  - Proof angle: "One woman's pregnancy-belly bloating deflated in days, and her constant nausea disappeared with it"

#### **Diarrhea + Hair Loss (Moderate Frequency Overlap)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Both from metabolic stress (diarrhea = GI distress; hair loss = nutrient loss from accelerated transit)
- **Behavioral impact:** "I can't leave the house AND I'm losing my identity" (behavioral + identity-level crisis)
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with diarrhea (more urgent/embarrassing)
  - Frame hair loss as the sign of nutritional depletion (validates the urgency)
  - Position Motilli as providing targeted nutrition WHILE restoring GI health
  - Proof angle: "One woman stabilized her diarrhea in week 2 AND saw hair regrowth acceleration by week 8"

#### **Constipation + Hair Loss (Lower Frequency But Significant)**
- **Why they co-occur:** Constipation = malnutrition (can't eat enough); hair loss = nutrient deficiency signal
- **Behavioral impact:** "I'm backed up AND falling apart" (physical decline + appearance decline)
- **Messaging strategy:**
  - Lead with constipation (more urgent)
  - Frame hair loss as the visible sign of what constipation is doing (nutrient absorption failure)
  - Position Motilli as restoring both motility AND nutritional support
  - Proof angle: "One woman restored normal bowel function AND stopped hair shedding with targeted nutritional support designed for GLP-1 users"

### When to Use Cross-Reference Guide

- **In copywriting:** If your target mentions 2+ symptoms in her testimonial, use cross-reference to position Motilli as addressing the ROOT CAUSE that fixes all of them
- **In audience targeting:** Look for ads that attract women with specific symptom combinations (e.g., "constipation + bloating" = younger women with visible identity crisis; "constipation + hair loss" = older women with compound aging concerns)
- **In funnel design:** Create different messaging angles for single-symptom vs. multi-symptom sufferers (multi-symptom = more desperate = higher purchase intent + willingness to pay)

## How to Structure Your Copy Using This Research

### Template 1: Single-Symptom Targeting (e.g., Constipation)

1. **Hook:** Use the key scroll-stop phrase from Quick Reference table (verbatim consumer language)
   - Example: "Haven't pooped in 11 days. I am full of sh*t and in 10/10 pain."
2. **Pain Recognition:** Reference the pain theme from SECTION 1 of the sub-avatar profile
   - Example: Validate the physical horror, ER visits, laxative failure
3. **Mechanism Education:** Use the upstream vs. downstream explanation from master copywriting brief
   - Example: "Your problem isn't your colon. It's your stomach. Here's why laxatives failed..."
4. **Solution:** Position Motilli with mechanism-specific language
   - Example: "Celery juice fiber's prokinetic compounds gently wake your stomach up."
5. **Proof:** Use testimonials from raw-research.md with specific results
   - Example: "One woman went from 11-day constipation to normal digestion in week 2."
6. **Close:** Offer relief + identity restoration
   - Example: "You don't have to choose between weight loss and bathroom dignity."

### Template 2: Multi-Symptom Targeting (e.g., Constipation + Bloating)

1. **Hook:** Use the PRIMARY pain (most urgent/shameful from Quick Reference)
   - Example: "Constipation AND a pregnant belly? Neither one should be the price of weight loss."
2. **Pain Stacking:** Validate that BOTH symptoms are real + connected
   - Example: Use Cross-Reference guide to show they share root cause
3. **Mechanism:** Explain how ONE root cause (slow stomach) creates BOTH problems
   - Example: "Slowed gastric emptying = food backs up = bloating visible + constipation physical"
4. **Solution:** Position Motilli as fixing the root (not treating symptoms separately)
   - Example: "One product addresses the real problem: your stomach's motility."
5. **Proof:** Use testimonials showing resolution of BOTH symptoms
   - Example: "One woman's bloating deflated and constipation ended when she restored stomach motility."
6. **Close:** Frame as identity + health restoration
   - Example: "Get your body back. Both ways."

### Template 3: Awareness-Level Targeting (e.g., Problem-Aware Woman)

1. **Lead:** Name the problem she already knows she has
   - Example: "Nausea every morning. Can't eat. Can't function."
2. **Educate:** Explain the mechanism she DOESN'T yet understand
   - Example: "GLP-1 slowed your stomach. Food sits there fermenting, making you sick."
3. **Reframe:** Position the problem as solvable (not just "a side effect you endure")
   - Example: "This isn't something you have to accept. It's something you can FIX."
4. **Solution:** Introduce Motilli as the mechanism-based solution
   - Example: "Prokinetic compounds that wake your stomach up — not harsh laxatives."
5. **Proof:** Use early-stage testimonials (2–3 weeks) from similar avatars
   - Example: "Week 1: Food starts moving. Week 2: Nausea fades. Week 3: You recognize yourself."
6. **Call to Action:** Permission + invitation
   - Example: "You deserve to stay on your medication AND feel good. Here's how."

## Notes on Using This Research

### ✅ DO

- Use verbatim consumer language from research (signals you understand her)
- Reference specific sub-avatars in copy (shows expertise + targeting precision)
- Explain mechanism before offering solution (builds belief + trust)
- Stack multiple pain themes when targeting complex sufferers (constipation + bloating)
- Update research quarterly as GLP-1 user community evolves

### ❌ DON'T

- Attack the GLP-1 medication (she LOVES it; you'll lose her trust instantly)
- Use generic "digestive support" language (she's heard it; it failed her)
- Minimize her suffering ("It's just a side effect; you'll adjust") — she's been dismissed by doctors already
- Make promises of "cures" or "fixes" — frame as support + relief + management
- Forget that her identity is at stake (this isn't just a physical problem; it's a psychological one)
