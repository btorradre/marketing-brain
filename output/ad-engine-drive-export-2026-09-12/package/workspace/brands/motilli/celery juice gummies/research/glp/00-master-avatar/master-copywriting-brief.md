## 4. The Core Mechanism: Upstream vs. Downstream

### Why Everything She's Tried Has Failed

GLP-1s slow down the **STOMACH** (upstream). That's the whole point — slow stomach = feel full = eat less = lose weight.

But when it works too well, stomach muscles essentially "fall asleep." Food sits, ferments, produces hydrogen sulfide gas (sulfur burps), hardens into concrete (constipation), causes nausea and bloating.

**Every remedy she's tried works on the COLON (downstream):**
- Miralax → osmotic laxative (pulls water into colon)
- Dulcolax → stimulates colon muscles
- Fiber → adds bulk to colon
- Enemas → reach the colon
- Stool softeners → work in colon

**The colon is the END of digestion. Her problem is at the BEGINNING.**

It's like trying to unclog a backed-up sink by running more water. You're not fixing the blockage — you're just adding volume to a system that can't move.

### How Motilli Works

Celery contains natural prokinetic compounds (apigenin) that gently stimulate stomach muscles. Like a soft wake-up call for the gut — not harsh, not cramping, just a nudge that says "time to get moving."

**One-sentence mechanism:**
> "GLP-1s put your stomach to sleep; Motilli gently wakes it up so food can move, gas can clear, and nutrients can absorb — without the harshness of laxatives."

**Simplified for ads:**
> "Physical therapy for your gut."

### The Three-Layer Solution
1. **Motility** — Prokinetic compounds wake up stomach muscles (upstream fix)
2. **Odor** — Chlorophyll neutralizes hydrogen sulfide gas (kills sulfur burps at source)
3. **Nutrition** — Vitamins A, C, K, B6, Folate replace what she can't eat enough to get

No competitor addresses all three. Most don't address upstream at all.

## 11. The Kill List: Words & Tones to NEVER Use

### Forbidden Words
- **"Cure" or "Fix"** gastroparesis — we support, we don't cure
- **"Explosive," "Cleanse," "Flush," "Detox"** — terrifies her of losing bowel control
- **"Anti-Aging"** — triggers defiance
- **"Miracle Cure"** — she's too cynical
- **"Easy" or "Effortless"** — dismisses decades of effort
- **"Bioavailable," "Synergy," "Optimize"** — speak like a friend, not a textbook

### Forbidden Tones
- **Attacking the medication** — she LOVES her shot. Never imply she made a bad choice. Only empathy.
- **Condescension about GLP-1 choice** — judgment will backfire instantly
- **Clinical/textbook tone** — she's been gaslit by doctors already; don't sound like one
- **Minimizing her suffering** — this pain is real and she's been dismissed enough
