## 4. THE UMS — Unique Mechanism of the Solution (THREE-PART CHECKLIST)

The solution is taught unbranded first, as what her research kept surfacing. Three things keep showing up:

### 1. MOTILITY SUPPORT (hero, the top)
Something that helps wake the stomach back up and keep food moving into the intestines instead of sitting there. Not a stimulant laxative. Not something that forces contractions in the colon. Something that works where the slowdown actually happens.

### 2. CHLOROPHYLL (the clean-up layer — gas from the ferment)
When food sits in a slowed stomach too long, it doesn't just sit, it ferments, and fermenting food puts off gas. That is the bloat, the pressure, and the rotten-egg burps. Chlorophyll ("the green stuff in plants" in ad voice) neutralizes that gas while the celery enzyme gets food moving again. The pairing line: "moving and clean, instead of sitting and rotting."

### 3. PREBIOTIC FIBER MICRO-DOSE (the feed-without-bulk layer + the competitor kill)
Everybody's first instinct is fiber, and regular fiber is bulk, and bulk into a backed-up colon is more traffic into the jam. A slowed system wants a MICRO-dose of prebiotic fiber, tiny on purpose, enough to feed the good bacteria with zero bulk added. The why-companies-get-it-wrong beat lives here: "almost nobody makes it that way, because tiny doesn't sell. Big fiber numbers on the front of the label sell."
