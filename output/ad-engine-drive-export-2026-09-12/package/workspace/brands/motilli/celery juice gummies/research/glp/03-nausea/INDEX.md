## Quick Reference: Key Metrics

**Nausea Incidence:**
- 40-70% of all GLP-1 users
- 44% on Wegovy (highest)
- 22% on Mounjaro (lowest)
- 20% on Ozempic

**Timeline:**
- Peak: First 24-72 hours after injection
- Median episode duration: 8 days
- Critical period: Weeks 3-5 (when expectations fail)
- Severe cases: 30-45+ days or 5+ months

**Top Purchase Triggers:**
1. Inability to keep water down (dehydration fear)
2. Nausea persisting past "expected" 4-8 week timeline
3. Nausea returning with each dose increase
4. Failed trial of Zofran/Dramamine
5. Impact on relationships/work/daily life

- 15+ targeted web searches

- Searched for both "successful experiences" and "failed remedies"
- Cross-referenced clinical data with consumer language

**Limitations:**
- Reddit direct access not available; sourced from articles citing Reddit
- Data skews toward severe cases (people complaining are more vocal)
- Age/gender not always explicitly stated (inferred from context)
