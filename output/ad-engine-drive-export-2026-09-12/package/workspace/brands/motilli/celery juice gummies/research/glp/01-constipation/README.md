## Key Research Insights

### Target Audience Pain Points
- **Primary:** Days-long constipation (5-15+ days) on GLP-1 medications
- **Secondary:** Fear of medical emergency (ER visits due to constipation)
- **Tertiary:** Shame, embarrassment, loss of bodily autonomy
- **Duration:** Average 47 days of some constipation before resolution

### Purchase Trigger
- Day 8-9 threshold where fear of ER visit creates urgent need for solution
- Multiple remedy failures (water, fiber, standard softeners) trigger openness to new products
- Success testimonials with specific claims create conversion ("zero issues with constipation on Wegovy")

### Solution Pattern
- Users shift from crisis management to **preventive daily supplementation**
- Successful formula: Psyllium + magnesium + electrolytes + probiotics
- "Prevention easier than fixing" is proven user messaging

### Target Demographics (Primary)
- Women 50-70 on GLP-1 medications for weight loss
- Already on multiple daily supplements (perimenopause/menopause)
- High healthcare engagement and resources
- Strong weight loss motivation sustains despite side effects
- Shame/embarrassment about adult bowel control is primary emotional driver
