Eight Days In, Smiling at a Retirement Party

Last fall I was down 26 pounds on Ozempic® — and eight days backed up — standing at my brother-in-law’s retirement party in a dress I chose because it hid the bloat. Not the weight. The weight was finally coming off. The bloat — that by-afternoon “five months pregnant” swelling that shows up no matter what you eat.

I spent that party the way I’d spent every event for months: keeping a polite distance in conversations so nobody caught the sulfur on my breath. Skipping the buffet because food had started sitting in me like wet cement. Leaving early with a “headache.”

In the car my husband asked if I was okay. I said I was fine.

I wasn’t fine. I was tracking bathroom days the way other people track their steps.

When I finally brought it up at my checkup, my doctor didn’t look up from the screen. “Constipation’s very common with these. Stay hydrated. Try Miralax.”

I told her I’d been on Miralax for two months.

She suggested I give it more time. The appointment lasted seven minutes.

I stopped bringing it up after that.
