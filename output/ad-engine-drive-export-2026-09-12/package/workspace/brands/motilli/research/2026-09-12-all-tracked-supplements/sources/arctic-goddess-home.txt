A Clinical Trial to Evaluate the Effects of a Supplement on Symptoms of Menopause and Overall Health and Well‑being
01
Design
Single-group, virtual, 12-week trial. No control or placebo arm — outcomes were measured against each participant's own baseline. 35 female participants (age 40–65) reporting at least four menopause symptoms — night sweats, sleep disturbances, mood changes, fatigue, changes in sexual function, or urinary or vaginal changes — met inclusion criteria and were enrolled.
02
Protocol
One capsule of the Arctic Goddess Proprietary Blend taken twice daily — one capsule in the morning with the first meal of the day, one capsule in the evening with the last meal of the day. Questionnaires completed at baseline, week 4, week 8, and week 12.
03
Measurement
Symptom Questionnaire
11-item menopause symptom questionnaire, 5-point Likert scale (Never–Always), completed at baseline, week 4, week 8, and week 12.
Perception Questionnaire
Up to 30 product-related statements rated Strongly Agree–Strongly Disagree, self-reported at week 4, week 8, and week 12.
04
Statistical Method
Data were checked for normality using the Pearson test. Results were analyzed using a two-way ANOVA with Dunnett's multiple comparisons test in GraphPad Prism 10.5. The significance level was set at 0.05.

What’s inside Arctic Goddess?
Wild Arctic reindeer liver, heart and kidney, beef ovary and uterus, plus Arctic herbs: Rhodiola Rosea, Sea Buckthorn, Nettle and Peppermint. No fillers, no chemicals.
Why reindeer organs instead of beef?
Reindeer survive extreme Arctic conditions, which concentrates far more nutrients in their organs. They also roam far from pollution, so they’re naturally free of the toxins common in farmed cattle.
Is it free of gluten, dairy and soy?
Yes. 100% free from gluten, dairy, soy, GMOs and common allergens. Not vegan, due to the organ ingredients.
Is it third party tested?
Yes. Our product is third party tested for purity, potency and safety, and produced in a GMP certified facility in the USA.
Where are the ingredients sourced?
Timing matters more here than most people realize. Our reindeer organs are harvested in autumn, at the end of the grazing season, when the animals are in the best condition they'll be in all year. They're freeze-dried raw rather than heat-processed, so the nutrient profile stays intact instead of being cooked out. Our herbs are wild-harvested inside their short seasonal window, when their active compounds are at their highest. Every batch is tested for heavy metals, microbials and purity before it ships.
