What's in it?
Three ingredients: magnesium oxide (500mg, providing 300mg elemental magnesium), kiwi fruit extract 10:1 (500mg — equal to a whole kiwifruit), and triphala extract 10:1 (300mg). Vegetarian capsule.
check_box
How fast does it work?
Take 3 capsules with water each night before bed. You’ll notice the first clear changes within 2 weeks: softer, easier, more frequent bowel movements. By week 4, your morning routine is regular, complete, and predictable.
This isn’t an overnight purge. It’s your normal regularity coming back.
check_box
Will it cause cramping or urgency?
No. There are no stimulant laxatives in the formula — nothing that forces your bowel to contract. Magnesium works by drawing water into the colon, which softens things and lets your body do the rest naturally. No emergencies, no racing to a bathroom.
check_box
Can I take it every day?
Yes — it's designed for daily, long-term use. Unlike stimulant laxatives (the "occasional use only" kind), nothing in this formula loses effectiveness over time or creates dependence.
check_box
Will I become dependent on it?
No. Dependence happens with stimulant laxatives because the bowel becomes less responsive and needs higher doses. Nothing in this formula works that way. Stop taking it and your body simply carries on — nothing to withdraw from.
