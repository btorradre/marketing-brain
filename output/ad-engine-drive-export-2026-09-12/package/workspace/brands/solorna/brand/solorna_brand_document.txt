2. BRAND STORY  (Our Story Page)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Solorna was born from a simple frustration -- and a closet full of compromises.
Our founder, a woman in her late thirties, had spent years searching for sandals
that didn't ask her to choose between beauty and comfort, between quality
craftsmanship and a price that made sense. She found luxury brands that looked
exquisite but punished her feet by noon. She found affordable options that fell
apart by summer's end. She found nothing that felt like it was made for the woman
she actually was -- someone who moves through her days with intention, who
values substance over flash, and who believes that what she wears should work
as gracefully as she does.

So she created what she couldn't find. Solorna bridges the space between luxury
craftsmanship and everyday wearability -- sandals designed with the same care
and attention you'd expect from a heritage atelier, offered at a price that
respects the way real women spend. Every pair is thoughtfully constructed with
premium materials, cushioned footbeds, and silhouettes refined enough for a
dinner reservation yet comfortable enough for a full day on your feet.

Rooted in Mediterranean warmth, California ease, and timeless femininity,
Solorna draws inspiration from sun-drenched coastlines, unhurried afternoons,
and the quiet confidence of a woman who knows exactly who she is. We don't
chase trends. We design pieces you'll reach for season after season -- because
the most beautiful things in life are the ones that endure.

4. BRAND VOICE GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------


TONE

Warm. Confident. Understated luxury.

Solorna speaks like a trusted friend with impeccable taste -- never
lecturing, never selling too hard. The voice is intelligent, calm,
and self-assured. It feels like a linen shirt: simple, elevated,
effortlessly put-together.


ALWAYS

  - Approachable and inclusive. Write as though speaking to a friend,
    not a customer.
  - Empowering without being aspirational to the point of exclusion.
    Celebrate who she already is.
  - Quality-focused. Let craftsmanship and materials speak for
    themselves. Specificity builds trust.
  - Sensory and evocative. Paint a feeling -- warmth, ease, confidence
    -- not a sales pitch.
  - Understated. Confidence is quiet. Let the product do the talking.


NEVER

  - Flashy, hyperbolic, or aggressive. No "BEST EVER" or "YOU NEED
    THIS." That is not our woman.
  - Overtly trendy. We do not chase what is momentary. Language should
    feel as timeless as the product.
  - Exclusionary or elitist. Accessible luxury means everyone who
    values quality feels welcome.
  - Overly casual or juvenile. We are warm, not chatty. Refined, not
    stiff.
  - Preachy about empowerment. Show it through design and action, not
    slogans.


VOCABULARY

  Prefer:  crafted, thoughtful, effortless, refined, timeless, ease,
           comfort, intentional, premium, everyday, enduring

  Avoid:   cheap, trendy, sexy, exclusive, obsessed, slay, iconic,
           game-changer, must-have


FORMATTING STYLE

  - Uppercase headings with generous letter-spacing
  - Clean, minimal body copy
  - Short paragraphs. Let white space do the work.
  - Em dashes over commas when the rhythm calls for a pause
