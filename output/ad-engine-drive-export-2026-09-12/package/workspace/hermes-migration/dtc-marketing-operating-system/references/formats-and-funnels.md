# Formats and Funnels

## Asset jobs

### Feed ad

Earn attention from the intended person and create enough conviction or curiosity for the next action. It may complete the sale or open a specific information loop. Judge it by its assigned job, not CTR alone.

### Long-form written ad

Useful when emotional history, failed solutions, a novel explanation, or layered objections require room. Story share is conditional. Criteria precede product evaluation; product timing follows awareness and concept.

### Video ad or VSL

Spoken dialogue carries the causal chain; correlated visuals carry feeling and evidence. Every visual should deepen the lived problem, make the explanation visible, demonstrate proof, or show the restored state. Decorative footage spends attention without moving belief.

Use the current video brief SOP for word-count arithmetic, picture-event density, product presence, one-take audio, prompt specificity, and editor handoff. Do not treat an old long VSL as a length target.

### Advertorial

An ad-driven advertorial is a continuation, not a restart. Confirm the upstream insight briefly, then add new authority, proof, comparison, objection handling, or mechanism depth. Move the reader at least one awareness step deeper.

Valid structures include authority explanation, crisis narrative, system analysis, comparison, discovery/listicle, and evidence-backed investigative framing. Format choice never authorizes fake editorial independence. Disclose sponsorship or advertorial status and use only real people, credentials, publications, and evidence.

### Listicle

Usually a product-aware proof and objection bridge. It works best when the reader already understands the category or mechanism and needs scannable reasons, differentiation, value, trust, guarantee, or practical fit.

Each item should contain a clear benefit, a short reason why, verified proof where available, and an appropriate next action. Do not pad to a number or re-teach a long upstream mechanism. A narrative bridge can preserve tone, but the listicle should remain a closer rather than become a second advertorial.

### PDP or offer page

Sell the transaction without dropping the language and mental model created upstream. Match headline, mechanism terms, visual tone, offer, proof, and CTA. A generic product page after a specific narrative is a congruence break.

### Search ad and landing page

Search intent is the awareness signal. Match the query's job, not only its keywords. Keep the ad promise and landing-page headline tightly aligned. Separate brand and non-brand learning, inspect actual search terms, and treat platform match types, bid rules, API versions, and benchmarks as time-sensitive facts to verify before execution.

## Funnel selection

Ask how much conviction work remains after the ad. Score the situation qualitatively across:

- mechanism complexity;
- education burden;
- familiarity of the product form;
- trust deficit;
- number of solution components;
- offer price and risk;
- audience temperature.

Low remaining burden can go directly to a PDP. Moderate burden may use a listicle or stronger PDP. High burden often needs an advertorial or VSL. These are hypotheses to test, not fixed word-count routes.

## Congruence dimensions

At every transition, evaluate:

1. **Angle continuity:** same specific wound or desire, not merely the same topic.
2. **Mechanism continuity:** confirm and deepen the same causal model; do not swap causes.
3. **Emotional temperature:** the next page opens near the state created upstream.
4. **Credibility continuity:** maintain or deliberately escalate the source of trust.
5. **Promise delivery:** give the clicked-for information within the first meaningful screen.

Natural credibility escalations include peer experience to verified expert confirmation, or one customer story to a real body of social proof. A different narrator is acceptable when the angle and causal thread remain continuous.

## Common funnel patterns

- **Ad → PDP:** ad or existing awareness performs nearly all conviction work; PDP closes the transaction.
- **Ad → advertorial → PDP:** ad earns the click; advertorial builds or deepens conviction; PDP closes.
- **Ad → listicle → PDP:** ad installs most of the explanation; listicle rationalizes and removes remaining objections.
- **Ad → advertorial → listicle → PDP:** use only when each step has a distinct job. More pages are not automatically more persuasive.

## Funnel diagnosis sequence

Diagnose from the top:

1. Delivery context: CPM, audience, spend, time window.
2. Attention and click intent: hook rate, hold, link CTR, CPC.
3. Click-to-page plumbing: landing-page view rate, load and redirect issues.
4. On-page action: scroll, time, CTA click, ATC, lead, or the page's actual job.
5. Checkout and final conversion: ATC-to-purchase, shipping, price, trust, UX, and payment friction.

State what the data shows separately from what it suggests. Use the account's own historical baselines before general ranges. Offer price, vertical, audience temperature, creative format, attribution window, and spend maturity change the interpretation.

Do not diagnose page congruence without the upstream asset. Do not rewrite the landing page when a large share of clicks never load it. Do not call high ATC/low purchase a hook problem.
