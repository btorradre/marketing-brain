At 68, I almost skipped my daughter's wedding photos, not because I was sick, because I couldn't stand looking at my own face anymore, and I didn't want that face in pictures that would live forever. Three years earlier, my skin had completely changed. Loose jawline, gray tone, wrinkles that makeup couldn't hide anymore. People would say, you look tired, but I wasn't tired. I just looked old. Two months before the wedding, my daughter sat me down. Mom, you used to glow. What happened? That sentence broke me, so I finally went to see a dermatologist. She told me something I had never heard before. After menopause, your skin doesn't just wrinkle. The structure actually collapses. Collagen, elastin, everything, and most creams don't rebuild it. She recommended something Korean women have used for years, a peptide formula called Balmy Prime Elixir. Just 60 seconds, morning and night. I didn't expect much, but after two weeks, my daughter noticed first. At the wedding, the photographer stopped me. He said, you have incredible skin. What are you using? And for the first time in years, I didn't avoid the camera.
