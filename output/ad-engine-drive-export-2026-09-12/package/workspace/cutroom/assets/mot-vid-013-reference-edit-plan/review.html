The editing idea to carry over

Let the voice connect a concrete object, an internal explanation and its everyday consequence. Vary the hold length: the reference gives the hook 4.32 seconds, uses some sub-second punctuation, and keeps the final card for 4.36 seconds. Caption changes and movement inside shots maintain activity between cuts.

Our accepted revision keeps anatomy under the digestive explanation and people under waking up, dressing, making plans and feeling like yourself. The plan preserves that balance.

The CapCut plan for Motilli

Planning deliverable. No CapCut project has been changed and no new voice has been cloned. The existing 96.63-second timings below must be replaced with fresh alignment after the new narration is selected.

Voice: two audio analyses describe a brisk, lower-register male American narrator. The current Woman Over 40 take is not established as that voice. Reference cloning needs confirmed voice rights or an authorized existing ElevenLabs voice ID. The source’s rough measured pace is 222 words/minute; preserve our approximately 95-second brief and technical-word clarity.

Edit: mostly straight cuts; brief blur resets at the trap, second ingredient, product reveal and imagined morning. Pair ingredients with their visual function. Use four literal everyday scenes in the close. Finish with the Motilli product, 90-day guarantee and a stable CTA hold.

CapCut: 9.3.0 is installed and plaintext drafts are available. Create a new isolated project; recheck current UI controls before automation. Build one master, then duplicate A/B/C hooks. Captions stay editable above the footage; narration, music and SFX stay on separate tracks.
