## 1. ORDER OF OPERATIONS (never start at the hook)

1. **Load brand + research first.** Sub-avatar dossier (40+ sourced Reddit voices) + canonical mechanism doc. Zero writing from category knowledge.
2. **Name the golden nugget** — the deep frame, not the topic. Constipation is the topic; "she finally got the body and is paying for it with a secret life she'd rather manage alone at 3am than risk losing the shot" is the motive.
3. **Build the belief inventory** — every belief standing between her and buying, sourced to her words, sorted into identity / cause / solution / trust / self.
4. **Pick ONE emotional driver per ad.** Same angle can produce multiple ads with different drivers (WRONGEND = end of the secret; FURIOUS = end of being played). Different driver = genuinely different ad, not a variation.
5. **Choose the awareness level** (solution-aware here = 55% of the avatar and the purchasing segment) and the format that matches.
6. **Write the belief ledger** before any prose: belief → target belief → the SCENE that shifts it → the loop guarding it → where the loop closes.
7. **Write hook and close as a pair** so the same objects open and pay off.
8. **Draft, then audit** against the laws, then naturalize.

## 2. THE BELIEF ARCHITECTURE (what actually converts)

Order matters: each shift makes the next cheaper. Trust → cause → solution.

| Belief she walks in with | What she leaves with | The scene that does it |
|---|---|---|
| "I just haven't found the right product yet" | Every one of them was aimed at the wrong place | The medicine-cabinet walkdown: Miralax→colon, fiber→colon, probiotics→colon |

**The rule underneath:** beliefs shift in emotional moments, never in arguments. Every row above is a SCENE, not a claim.

Every loop is opened with a promise and closed with a payoff. Nothing dangles.

## 4. SIMPLE LANGUAGE — the laws, each born from a failed draft

1. **Lived, not announced.** "It didn't come from my doctor" → "So, of course, the answer didn't come from a doctor's office." Belief rides casual grammar.
2. **Concrete only, no abstractions.** "Planning my mornings around a maybe" → "sitting there every morning waiting on something that never came." Test: can I film it?
3. **Her vocabulary, not ours.** Heard-logic in recommendation-speak ("it's the gentle one," "you're probably not getting enough fiber"), never mechanism words. Her vocabulary upgrades exactly when her beliefs do.
4. **Folk-name ingredients.** "The enzyme in celery," never apigenin — unless the chemical name carries existing demand (berberine does).
5. **Universal anchors.** Produce aisle, not the celery-juice fad. Would EVERY viewer recognize it?
6. **No insider callbacks in hooks.** "Wrong part of your body" means nothing before the reframe teaches it.
7. **No gross imagery.** "A gummy melts," never "already chewed."
8. **Behavior over adjectives.** "Six months pregnant by dinner," never "uncomfortable bloating."

## 5. HOW TRUST IS BUILT (in order of power)

1. **Density of lived detail = truth signal.** The orange tub. Practiced it in the car. Twelve seconds and a refill question. First cup of coffee. Nobody invents these; that's why they read true.
2. **Validate her effort before correcting her.** She did everything right and obeyed her doctor. The mechanism explains her failure without blaming her — absolution is the strongest payoff in health copy.
3. **The messenger has nothing to sell.** Retired gastroenterologist answering questions for free at 10pm. Congruent with the doctor-distrust already installed — the answer CANNOT come from a doctor's office after that scene.
4. **Objections die by TESTING, not stating.** "Water? Miralax? Fiber? Coffee? Ginger? Senna?" — each fails for its own mechanism reason. Third-party authority + illustration in one move. Infinitely more believable than "X doesn't work."
5. **Modest, odd numbers.** Four months, three hundred dollars, day eight, twelve seconds. Small and specific beats big and round.
6. **The anti-sell / skepticism transfer.** "Fully expecting to want my money back," the product-fails-first week-one beat (WRONGEND). Her skepticism gets voiced by the narrator before she can voice it herself.
7. **The med is never attacked.** "The weight was coming off, that part was working." Fighting the thing she loves loses her instantly.

## 6. THE CONGRUENCE CONTRACT

The hook is a check the body must cash. Every object in the hook returns: cabinet of bottles → bottles in the outside trash on a Tuesday. $300 → "my three hundred dollars never had a chance." The counting → "the counting is over." Guarantee (where used) is denominated in the ad's own images, never generic.

## 7. AUDIT BEFORE PRESENTING

Ledger closed · congruence · demo beats · real proof only · messenger congruence · read-aloud simplicity · one awareness level · every behavior true to the avatar's psychology.
