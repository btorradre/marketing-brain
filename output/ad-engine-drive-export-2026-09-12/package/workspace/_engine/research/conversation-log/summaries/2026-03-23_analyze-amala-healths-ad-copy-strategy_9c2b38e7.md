# Analyze Amala Health's ad copy strategy — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-23

**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-23_analyze-amala-healths-ad-copy-strategy_9c2b38e7]]

## What Happened
Analyzed 524 ads across Amala Health (285) and Sculptique (239) to map image-copy congruence patterns. Identified 7 distinct congruence categories. Created swipe file indexes. Attempted Alevia scrape but Chrome crashed on their ~1,200 ad library.

## Key Decisions Made
- Story Prop Images identified as the strongest congruence category — physical object in both image and line 1
- Alevia scrape deferred due to Chrome instability on large ad libraries
- Swipe indexes organized by image filename → copy angle → page name

## Insights & Learnings
- **Story Prop Images are the gold standard for native congruence.** The physical object (Goodwill jacket, wallet, breakfast plate) appearing in both the image AND the first line of copy creates an unbreakable lock that makes the ad indistinguishable from an organic post.
- **Scene Recreation Images** with timestamps (ring camera footage) are the next tier — they look like someone's actual security camera or phone photo from the crisis moment.
- **Mechanism Education Images** (artery cross-sections, plaque grids) are the most versatile because they work across any hook/copy angle without needing to match a specific narrative.
- Alevia runs ~900 of 990 ads as the same copy with different images — extreme image testing model.
