## What Happened
Wrote 5 complete long-form sales letters for Lunessa, replicating Amala Health's proven narrative architecture. Each letter is distinct across narrator archetype, discovery vehicle, emotional engine, lead type, crisis type, and close type. All hit 5 belief shifts with Position 4 product integration.

## Key Decisions Made
- All 5 letters use anti-statin positioning (Rosuvastatin or Atorvastatin as villain)
- Each letter uses a completely different emotional engine: widow grief, betrayal crisis, livelihood destruction, model patient death, genetic unfairness
- Position 4 (Conviction) product integration across all — product appears late after full mechanism education
- CoQ10 depletion mechanism used in "The Mechanic's Hands" as a statin side-effect angle

## Insights & Learnings
- **The "Perfect Compliance → Catastrophic Failure" structure works as a full letter, not just a hook.** "The Woodshop" letter proves this architecture sustains across 1,000+ words.
- **Genetic unfairness is a powerful emotional engine** — "The Fast Food Brother" taps into the specific frustration of doing everything right while someone who does nothing has better numbers. This is a distinct angle from "the system failed me."
- **Multiple discovery vehicles maintain anti-mimicry**: cardiac rehab nurse sister, nurse practitioner daughter, family researcher son, late-night Reddit session, running group friend. None repeat.
