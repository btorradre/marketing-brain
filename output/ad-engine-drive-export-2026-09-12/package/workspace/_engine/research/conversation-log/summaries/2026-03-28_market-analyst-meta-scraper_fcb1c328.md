## Insights & Learnings
- **"Third ovary" mechanism** (Emily Watts / Maple Lane) is the top find: reframes menopause belly fat from "you let yourself go" to "your body is building an emergency estrogen factory to keep you alive." This is brilliant mechanism naming — it takes a stigmatized symptom and gives it medical legitimacy while also creating urgency.
- **GLP-1 naming hijack** is a real trend: Select Nutrients is calling a cinnamon supplement "GLP-1 Burn" — leveraging the massive Ozempic/GLP-1 cultural awareness to sell a natural supplement. This signals how powerful the GLP-1 brand recognition has become in the weight loss space.
- **Eve Baker persona** runs multiple ads for Select Nutrients with different entry narratives but same product funnel — classic multi-hook testing pattern
- All ads discovered today are brand new launches, not proven winners — worth revisiting in 2-3 weeks
