# Investigate Meta ad account performance drop — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-22
**Category:** funnel-work
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-22_investigate-meta-ad-account-performance-drop_c8537c60]]

## What Happened
Investigated Motilli's Meta ad account performance decline. Analyzed 296 ads across 37 ad sets, reviewed top performer copy and creative, compared against competitors (Omyo, Fuel Health), and diagnosed structural and funnel bottlenecks.

## Key Decisions Made
- Two best-performing ad sets are turned off — need to be reactivated
- 37 ad sets must be consolidated to 5-8 for CBO optimization
- Listicle outperforming advertorial (3.68 ROAS vs 2.5-2.8) — test shorter bridge pages
- "Concrete constipation" angle is the current winner — double down
- Consider "Shop now" direct-to-product for retargeting audiences

## Insights & Learnings
- **Double-education funnel is the primary conversion leak.** The long-form ad already does 80% of the belief-shift work. Sending warm prospects to another 2,000-word education piece adds friction that bleeds conversions. The advertorial is redundant, not reinforcing.
- **Listicle > Advertorial for this funnel.** Data proves it: 3.68 ROAS for listicle vs 2.5-2.8 for advertorial. When the ad is already doing the heavy mechanism education, the landing page just needs to close — not re-educate.
- **37 ad sets in one CBO is structural suicide.** $1,161/day split 37 ways = $20-40 per ad set per day = permanent Learning Limited.
- **Competitors (Omyo) running the opposite approach** — short copy, product up front, direct to purchase. No advertorial layer at all. Both approaches work; the question is which matches the audience temperature.
- **"Concrete constipation" is a mechanism-level hook**, not just a symptom callout. It's specific, visual, and visceral enough to function as its own angle.
