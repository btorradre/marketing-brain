## What Happened
Major skill update session. Added 11 enhancements to the long-form copy skill specifically for ultra-long (4,000+ word) direct response copy. Also scraped Rosabella ads from Meta (all 9 were identical content) and began vault restructure work.

## Key Decisions Made
- Five conditions that earn ultra-long copy: cycle mechanisms, generational stakes, deep independence erosion, dual-mechanism solutions, narrator proximity to irreversible consequence
- "None are required. None are structural prescriptions" — the conditions are descriptive, not prescriptive
- Cycle mechanism guidance: "it's not a framework — it's the reader's own recognition"
- Dual-analogy exception to the one-image rule for dual-mechanism solutions
- All 11 additions woven into existing sections (no new sections forced)

## Insights & Learnings
- Cycle mechanisms (where the cause feeds the effect which feeds the cause) naturally justify longer copy because the reader needs to follow the loop
- The accumulation technique (listing individual losses in fast sequence without narrator editorializing) lets cumulative weight build organically
- Extended authority scenes can contain 1,000-2,000 words of mechanism education inside a sustained narrative scene without feeling like a lecture
- Week-by-week results timelines that reverse specific pain scenes from earlier in the copy create satisfying narrative closure
- Rosabella runs 9 identical ad sets (same copy, different targeting) — common scaling pattern
