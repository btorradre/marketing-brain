## What Happened
The market analyst agent completed its Sunday Tier 1 re-scrape rotation, cataloging branded static ads from 4 competitor brands via the Meta Ad Library. Each brand's creative library was systematically documented with ad counts, design archetypes, headline copy, and Library IDs. Catalog entries were created or updated in the vault, and an intel drop summary was filed.

## Key Decisions Made
- Focused on 4 brands for the Sunday rotation: Neurosmile, GLP-1 SOS, Auri Nutrition, and Primal Viking
- Documented visual design archetypes rather than downloading images (Meta CDN protections prevented automated downloads)
- Identified the Neurosmile competitor comparison chart as the top templateable pattern for Lunessa

## Insights & Learnings
- **Neurosmile (~22 statics):** Dominated by competitor comparison charts (Gabapentin vs. their product), bold "burning feet" urgency claims, and clinical authority positioning. The YES/NO checkmark comparison table is their signature format.
- **GLP-1 SOS (~63 statics):** Positioned entirely around constipation relief for GLP-1 users. Key archetypes: product lineup shots, "Relief Like Clockwork" clock imagery, bold claim cards. Clean, benefit-focused creative.
- **Auri Nutrition (~120 statics):** Very active advertiser in nootropics/mushroom gummies. Heavy use of pricing/urgency cards (strikethrough pricing, "$5.99" anchoring), testimonial cards with star ratings, and "After 5 Days" timeline comparisons. Premium dark styling.
- **Primal Viking (~590 statics):** Hyper-masculine testosterone/cortisol positioning. Bold red/black design language. "Your Testosterone Is Locked Up" chain imagery, transformation testimonials, and cortisol-body-composition angles. Massive ad volume suggests aggressive testing.
