## Summary of Activity

Massive competitor analysis session across 524 ads (285 Amala Health, 239 Sculptique). Created a Native Image-Copy Congruence Analysis identifying 7 distinct image-copy congruence categories:

1. **Story Prop Images** — Physical object from the story appears in both image and line 1 of copy (e.g., Goodwill jacket, wallet at Walmart, breakfast plate). Strongest category.
2. **Scene Recreation Images** — Image IS the crisis moment (ring camera footage with timestamp, ER arrival, firefighters)
3. **Narrator Identity Images** — Credential visible in image (nurse in scrubs, pharmacist behind counter, cardiologist selfie)
4. **Mechanism Education Images** — Artery cross-sections, plaque progression grids. Most versatile (works across any cholesterol angle)
5. **Narrator's World** — Setting-based identification
6. **Symptom/Condition** — Visual symptom display
7. **Multi-Panel Collages** — Multiple narrative beats in one image

Also created swipe file indexes mapping every image to its copy angle.

Got through 54 Alevia ads before crash — confirmed 3 page names (Jessica Bennett 44 ads, Dr. Michelle T. Carter 9, Cholesterol Support Group 1), 6 unique copy angles.
