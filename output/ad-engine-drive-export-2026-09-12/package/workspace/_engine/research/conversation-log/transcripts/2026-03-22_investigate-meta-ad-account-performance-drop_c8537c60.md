## Summary of Activity

Deep dive into Motilli's Meta ad account performance. Campaign "motilli 🚀🚀🚀🚀" spent $20,619 over 30 days, 1,106 purchases at $18.64 CPA — down from previous 2.5-3 ROAS.

**Structural problems found:**
1. Two best ad sets (cc img v3 copy 2 at $8,526/466 purchases and cc img v4 at $1,068/66 purchases) are turned OFF
2. 37 ad sets in one CBO campaign — $1,161/day budget fragmented across too many ad sets, most in Learning Limited

**Funnel analysis:**
- "Concrete constipation" angle is the current winner — $15-16 CPA range
- Top of funnel is strong: 5-7% outbound CTR, 30-37% overall CTR, $0.11-$1.08 CPC
- Double-education funnel problem: long-form ad does 80% of belief-shift work, then advertorial repeats same ground = unnecessary friction
- Listicle landing pages are converting at HIGHER ROAS (3.68) than full advertorial pages (2.5-2.8)

**Competitor comparison:**
- Omyo: product-forward, short copy, "Shop now" → direct purchase (opposite approach)
- Dr. Susan Miller / Fuel Health: medium copy, early product mention, 70% OFF, video, direct purchase

**Recommendations:**
1. Turn winners back on
2. Consolidate to 5-8 ad sets
3. Test shorter bridge page (listicle) instead of full advertorial
4. Test "Shop now" direct-to-product for retargeting
5. Double down on "concrete constipation" angle
