# VIDEO Hook Bank — Patterns by Awareness Level

**Built:** 2026-08-20 · **Corpus:** ~90 proven VIDEO ad transcripts (longest-running + most-reused) from 6 scaling health-DR brands tracked in TrendTrack: Nuora, Lymphoria, Sculptique, Resilia, PiPi Tea, Serene Herbs. Days-running and reuse counts noted per example.

**Companion file:** [`HOOK-BANK.md`](HOOK-BANK.md) covers WRITTEN long-form/native copy hooks (Alevia, Eden Labs, Elvera) and carries **the governing law of hooks (Brooks, 2026-08-19): the hook calls out the CONCEPT, not the problem — self-ID timing is the awareness dial, and every hook is a check the body must cash (congruence contract).** That law governs this file too. This file adds the video-native skeletons: what survives when the hook has 2 seconds of audio+visual instead of a headline.

**How to use this bank:** a hook is the CLAIM layer of an angle record (angle = the problem; hook = the sentence that argues about it in the first 2 seconds). Pick the pattern by the awareness level the ad set targets (one ad, one level). `dr-hook-lab` writes brand-specific variants from these skeletons using VoC language; `dr-video-ads` step 7 picks the one that leads the script. Patterns are format skeletons — the words always come from the avatar's own recorded language, never copied from these examples.

**Every pattern here passed the market test (long run times, heavy reuse). House-law filters still apply:** several examples fabricate stats/diagnoses. We port the STRUCTURE and use real proof (Law 2). Compliance notes flagged per pattern.

## UNAWARE → PROBLEM-AWARE

She has the symptom but hasn't named the problem, or blames the wrong thing. The hook's job: make her recognize herself, then reattribute the cause. These hooks CREATE problem-awareness in one sentence.

### U1 — The misattribution flip ("It's not X, it's Y")

**Skeleton:** "Your [thing she blames herself for] isn't [what she thinks it is]. It's [new hidden cause]."

- "Your man boobs aren't fat, brother. They're a layer of alcohol sludge cementing your chest lymph shut." — Lymphoria, 57d
- "It's not fat, your kidneys are crying for help." — Resilia, 130d
- "That's not fat you can diet away, that's fluid trapped." — Lymphoria (Brazil-friend beat, 49d)
- "You don't have menopause weight, you have menopause sludge." — Lymphoria

**Why it works:** the single highest-leverage emotional move in the corpus: ABSOLUTION. It removes self-blame ("you didn't fail, you were fighting the wrong enemy") and simultaneously installs the new mechanism. Shifts an identity belief and a cause belief in one line. The relief is the dopamine. Also explains years of failed effort, which validates instead of shames.
**Belief collided:** "I'm fat / lazy / it's my fault / it's just age."
**Watch-out:** the flip must be TRUE of our mechanism. (Note our own no-transition-angles and swap-test laws still apply.)

### U2 — Symptom-reveal / hidden-diagnosis

**Skeleton:** "If your [ordinary symptom she can verify right now], that's a sign of [hidden internal problem]."

- "If your face is puffy and swollen every morning, that's an indication of early liver failure." — Sculptique, 67d
- "If your pee looks like this, your eyes look puffy like this, your ankles look swollen like this…" — Resilia, 130d, 6x reuse

**Why it works:** a self-test in the first two seconds. She looks at her own pee/face/ankles and self-diagnoses — the sensory-demo principle at hook altitude. Filters hard to sufferers (buyers) and opens a fear-gap loop only the mechanism can close.
**Belief collided:** "this symptom is normal / cosmetic / just aging."
**Compliance:** "early liver failure" is a diagnosis claim — we soften to "a sign your [system] isn't draining/absorbing right" and never name a disease. Fear must be resolvable by the product's real capability.

### U3 — Visible-evidence shock (show the inside)

**Skeleton:** "This is what your [organ/body] looks like when [condition]." / "This is what comes out when [protocol]."

- "This is what your liver looks like if your ALT is above 125. That yellow is fat trapped…" — Sculptique, 45d
- "This is what crawls out of you when you drink African bitter herbs for two weeks straight." — Serene Herbs, 136d, 42x reuse (their #1)

**Why it works:** the invisible internal problem made visual = instant curiosity + disgust loop (disgust is the most under-used scroll-stopper in the corpus). "Day one… day three…" then carries the retention.
**Belief collided:** "nothing is really wrong inside me."

### U4 — The enemy speaks (villain personification)

**Skeleton:** first-person monologue from the problem itself.

- "I am the parasite living inside your gut. And I really hope you keep eating sugar." — Serene Herbs, 108d

**Why it works:** total pattern interrupt (who's talking?!) + installs the villain and the mechanism simultaneously. Every symptom becomes the villain bragging, which unifies the symptom cluster ("I'm the reason you're exhausted… I'm the reason you crave sugar"). The product then enters as the counter-character.
**Belief collided:** "my symptoms are separate problems / my cravings are weakness."

### U5 — Story cold-open (nightmare in medias res)

**Skeleton:** drop into the most emotionally loaded scene of the avatar's life, mid-action, first person. The solution is not mentioned for a long time.

- "I caught my husband cheating with a girl half my age. She was 28, I was 52. And the only reason I didn't crumble was what my sister flew in from Brazil to show me." — Lymphoria, 49d, 133k impressions
- "I only let him touch me with the lights off now. I keep a blanket on my legs." — Lymphoria (cellulite), 49d
- "I was at the pool when she walked past, half my age, flat stomach…" — Lymphoria, 49d

**Why it works:** pure golden-nugget delivery — the hook IS the deep frame (betrayal, shame, invisibility), not the topic. Massive self-ID for the right viewer, massive filter for everyone else. The teased object ("what my sister flew in to show me") is the master loop. This is the full punch-in-the-gut VSL's natural hook.
**Belief collided:** none yet — it earns the RIGHT to shift beliefs by proving the narrator has lived her life.

## PROBLEM-AWARE

She knows the problem, has probably tried things. The hook's job: reframe the cause, escalate the stakes, or validate the failure.

### P1 — Countdown signs / reframe listicle

**Skeleton:** "[N] signs your [problem] is actually [our cause], not [what she was told]." Count DOWN (3→2→1).

- "Three obvious signs your dryness is bacterial, not hormonal." — Nuora, 131d, 7x reuse across variants

**Why it works:** structural open loop (the countdown) + each sign is a mini belief-shift + the reframe in the title does the new-mechanism work before the ad even starts. Self-scoring keeps her watching to check herself against every sign.
**Belief collided:** the category's official cause story.

### P2 — Stages of decline (the escalator)

**Skeleton:** "[Problem] has [N] stages. Most people don't feel a thing until stage [N-2]." End with "comment which stage you're at."

- "Kidney failure has six stages. Most men don't feel a thing until stage four." — Sculptique, 45d, 46x reuse (their most-cloned)

**Why it works:** self-location loop — she reads herself into the map and HAS to find her stage. The silent early stages create urgency without a countdown timer ("the damage is happening now"). The comment CTA feeds the algorithm and the retargeting pool.
**Belief collided:** "if it were serious I'd feel it."
**Compliance:** disease-progression framing is the hottest zone in this bank — port the STAGES structure onto a non-disease progression (buildup, decay of results, compounding) for our brands.

### P3 — Symptom-cluster unification

**Skeleton:** "Your [symptom 1], your [symptom 2], your [symptom 3]… you thought these were separate problems. They're not."

- "Your exhaustion, your bloating, your brain fog, your cravings, your restless sleep. You thought these were separate problems. They weren't. They were all parasites." — Serene Herbs (inside their #1 ad)
- "The stubborn gut… the brain fog… waking up at 3am… joint pain out of nowhere. Sound familiar? It's not just age." — Resilia, 130d

**Why it works:** each named symptom is another self-ID checkpoint (four chances to say "that's me"), and the unification into one cause is itself the mechanism reveal. Very high perceived explanatory power = trust.
**Belief collided:** "I have five unrelated problems" → "I have one fixable problem."

### P4 — Failed-effort empathy (the relatability opener)

**Skeleton:** "I was doing [everything right: specific efforts] and nothing was working. I was still [symptom]."

- "Listen, I was working out, eating clean, walking, running, and nothing was working. I was still puffy." — Lymphoria, 61d, 4x reuse
- "And for years, I followed all the typical health advice under the sun, from strict diets to regular gym sessions and even ashwagandha and mushroom coffees." — Nuora VSL

**Why it works:** meets her exactly where she is (post-failure), validates the effort, and pre-frames the mechanism as the missing variable rather than another thing to try harder at. The trust foundation of every skeptic arc.
**Belief collided:** "I just need more discipline."

### P5 — Authority Q&A mirror

**Skeleton:** open with the exact question she typed into Google, answered by a credentialed voice. "Is [symptom] serious? I get asked this every day. I'm a [practitioner]."

- "Is foamy urine serious? I get asked this every day. I'm a nephrologist, and I will answer the 13 most common questions I get." — Sculptique, 51d

**Why it works:** search-intent mirroring — she is ALREADY asking this. The numbered-questions promise is a structural loop, and answering objections as "questions I get" handles them without selling.
**Compliance:** never invent a practitioner or credentials for our copy (house law). Real expert, real quotes, or reframe as "a nurse explained it to me" story sourced from real research.

## SOLUTION-AWARE

She knows solutions exist, has opinions about them, is comparison shopping or burned out on the category. The hook's job: differentiate via mechanism, co-opt or dismantle what she already knows.

### S1 — Hypothetical future-pace ("This is what would happen…")

**Skeleton:** "This is what would happen to [hyper-specific persona = the viewer] if [she took X] every day for [timeframe]."

- "This is what would happen to a woman in her 40s who drinks wine and wakes up puffy and bloated every single morning if she started taking two drops of lymphoria every day for three weeks." — Lymphoria, 58d
- "This is what would happen to a man who clears his renal lymphatic system with two capsules a day for eight weeks." — Sculptique, 45d, 24x reuse
- Nuora's whole timeline family ("Day one, she takes the gummies…"), 168d

**Why it works:** the workhorse of the entire corpus — every brand runs it. Third-person conditional grammar creates claim distance while delivering full future pacing. The persona line is surgical self-ID (they re-cast the SAME script across personas: wine women / beer men / henny men / women over 50 — persona-swapped format scaling). The timeline is the loop. Beat-by-beat ingredient attribution carries the mechanism education.
**Belief collided:** "nothing works fast enough to bother" + "I can't picture it working for ME."

### S2 — Named-solution takedown

**Skeleton:** "[The thing she's currently taking/believing in] won't fix [problem]. Here's why." / "[Drug] costs you [hidden price]."

- "Milk thistle won't fix liver congestion." — Sculptique, 81d, 16x reuse
- "Lipitor costs you your muscles and your mind. Fish oil barely moves the needle." — Sculptique, 61d
- "If your lymphatic drops are not doing this, get a new one." — Lymphoria, 61d

**Why it works:** collides head-on with the solution-belief she holds RIGHT NOW — the most awake she'll ever be in the first second. The takedown must immediately pivot to the mechanism reason ("protects cells but can't unclog drainage" = respectful, specific, believable).
**Belief collided:** her current solution loyalty.
**Watch-out:** the prescription-drug version co-opts, never trash-talks efficacy ("your number drops on paper, but…"). And for our house: check the no-competitor-comparisons law per brand before using named takedowns.

### S3 — Ingredient personification ("I'm X. Take me if…")

**Skeleton:** each ingredient introduces itself and names who needs it — each "take me if" is a past-failure objection answered.

- "I'm Bromelain. Take me if you've tried probiotics and nothing's worked." — Nuora, 135d, 6x reuse, cloned across every product line
- Inverted version — the FAILED solutions confess first: "Hi, I'm Probiotics, and all I do is add more bacteria on top of the waste already rotting in your gut… Hi, I'm Soursop Bitters, and none of these work until you flush first." — Serene Herbs, 148d

**Why it works:** mechanism education disguised as entertainment; objection handling mapped 1:1 to ingredients; self-filtering ("take me if" = segmentation inside the ad). The Serene inversion is the failed-solutions section of a VSL compressed to 40 seconds.
**Belief collided:** "supplements are all the same" + every specific past failure.

### S4 — Symptom→ingredient matcher

**Skeleton:** "If you have [symptom A], you need [ingredient A]. If [symptom B], you need [B]… But buying them all separately is [expensive/impossible dosing]. That's why [product] exists."

- "If your hoo-ha smells like a fish market, you need bromelain. If you have discharge that won't stop, you need bacillus coagulans…" — Nuora, 167d
- "If you take cleavers, you'll… and if you add bromelain… and when you include all four together…" — Sculptique, 59d, 39x reuse

**Why it works:** stacking self-ID checkpoints AND building the why-this-product case (convenience + dosing) before the product appears. The additive "and if you add…" version builds the formula piece by piece so the bundle feels inevitable.
**Belief collided:** "I can just buy the ingredient on Amazon."

### S5 — Speed-mechanism reveal

**Skeleton:** "Most people don't realize how fast [known ingredient] works for [problem]. Here's what happens inside your body in the first [timeframe]."

- "Most people don't realize how fast berberine works for menno belly." — Nuora, 133d, 3 title-variants

**Why it works:** captures existing ingredient demand (she's heard of berberine) and differentiates on the hidden variable (absorption, form, process). Inside-the-body narration is the loop.
**Belief collided:** "I tried that ingredient and nothing happened" → "you had the wrong form/dose."

### S6 — Reverse psychology ("Don't buy this")

**Skeleton:** "Please don't buy [product]" / "I need to warn you about [product]" — then the complaint is that it works too well / sells out.

- "Please don't buy Sculptique kidney support capsules. They said it would improve my creatinine levels, but they did not warn me how fast it would work." — Sculptique, 84d, 21x reuse

**Why it works:** pattern interrupt via anti-sell; the "complaint" frame lets every claim land as testimony instead of advertising. Cousin of Nuora's apology ad but works at solution-aware, not just retargeting.
**Compliance:** the lab-number claims in the example (creatinine 2.3→1.6) are exactly what we never fabricate. Port the frame, use real results only.

### S7 — Insider ritual / protocol

**Skeleton:** a specific, slightly odd morning ritual demonstrated step by step, framed as cultural or insider knowledge.

- "First thing in the morning… don't brush your teeth. Go straight to the kitchen. Two tablespoons on an empty stomach. It's the African method. Why empty stomach? Because parasites are hungry in the morning." — Serene Herbs, 150d, 14x reuse

**Why it works:** the specificity of the ritual (don't brush your teeth!) is the curiosity hook, and every odd step gets a mechanism reason, which makes the whole system feel like real transmitted knowledge instead of marketing. Also bakes in usage compliance.
**Belief collided:** "I've seen every supplement pitch" — this doesn't pitch, it teaches a ritual.

### S8 — Comment-reply objection hook

**Skeleton:** show a real(-looking) comment carrying the scariest objection, then answer it as a creator reply.

- "'Love this product, but I'm scared of seeing parasites crawl out of me. Anyone else?' — I'm about to blow your mind right now. You're NOT going to…" — Resilia, 130d

**Why it works:** the objection is the hook — the exact fear blocking the purchase, voiced by a peer, answered natively. Retargets fence-sitters at solution/product-aware boundary. Feels like content, not an ad.

## CROSS-CUTTING MECHANICS (why the whole corpus works)

1. **Self-ID through hyper-specific persona casting.** "A woman in her 40s who drinks wine and wakes up puffy" beats "women with bloating" every time. Brands re-cast one winning script across 3–5 personas (wine/beer/henny; moms/grandmas; men/women) — persona swaps are the cheapest format scaling that exists.
2. **The verifiable self-test in second one.** Pee foam, sock marks, morning puffiness, tongue plaque — a check she can run on her own body before the ad finishes. Hooks that let her verify convert belief into felt experience immediately.
3. **Absolution is the strongest emotional payoff in health.** "It was never your fault, you were fighting the wrong enemy" appears in some form in every brand's best ad. The flip from shame to blamelessness is the dopamine spike that buys the next 60 seconds.
4. **Conditional grammar as claim armor.** "This is what WOULD happen…" / "she notices…" — third-person hypothetical delivers full future pacing with legal distance. The whole category writes results this way.
5. **Every hook opens a loop the FORMAT promises to close.** Countdown, stages, day-by-day, N questions, "the last stage will blow your mind." Retention is structural before it's narrative.
6. **Small odd numbers read as true.** 73%, day 5–7, 2.3→1.6, five pineapples, 4,500 feet, 730 days aged. (For us: real numbers only, but keep them small and odd.)
7. **Disgust is underpriced.** The grossest hooks (crawl-out, purge, foam) have the longest runtimes and heaviest reuse in the corpus. Health buyers are morbidly curious; polite ads lose to visceral ones.
8. **The hook names WHO it's for out loud.** "If you're a man over 40 who's lived on fast food…" — explicit filtering is a feature: the algorithm learns from who stays.
9. **The hook GIVES, it never asks (Brooks, 2026-08-20).** A hook has earned nothing yet, so it cannot request anything — no "give me ten minutes," no "stay with me," no "watch till the end." The retention device is always a promised payoff on a short clock: "in the next thirty seconds I'm gonna tell you what I found." An ask is a withdrawal from an empty account; a deadline-stamped promise is a deposit.
10. **No insider callbacks in hooks (Brooks, 2026-08-20).** A hook can never lean on a frame the ad hasn't taught yet — "aimed at the wrong part of your body" means nothing before the reframe exists in her head; it reads as off, not intriguing. Every noun in a hook must already live in HER world (the shot, the Miralax, her appetite, her bathroom) or the tease stays pure curiosity ("one reason all of it keeps failing"). The ad's internal geography (top/bottom, wrong end) is a payoff, never an opener.

## HOUSE-LAW FILTER (before any pattern ships)

- No invented studies, Ns, %, doctors, credentials, or lab numbers — the structure ports, the proof must be ours (Law 2 / no-fabricated-citations).
- No disease-diagnosis claims — reattribute to process/buildup/function, never name a pathology we can't claim.
- Angle first: a hook from this bank must voice an angle from the brand's bank, in the avatar's recorded language, one awareness level per ad.
- AI-tell blacklist (Law 9) applies to every line written from these skeletons. Note: the U1 skeleton ("It's not X, it's Y") is a spoken REFRAME device delivered as a story beat, not the banned written negation-affirmation closer — but write it in natural speech ("that's not fat you can diet away, that's fluid") and never as a punchy paragraph-closing beat.
- Hooks with fear must be resolvable by the product's true capability, and the guarantee window must match the live policy page.
