# The Alevia System: Full Teardown of Their Long-Form Story Ad Library

Studied 2026-08-19 via TrendTrack MCP. ~20 distinct long-form first-person story ads, all selling ONE product (Alevia Amla, standardized extract), all pointing at alevia.com/amla landers, all signed "Feel Like Yourself Again," all using the same "This actually worked, thank you so much." - Emily T. CTA quote. 17 deep-scanned in full plus the flagship Maureen ad and the apology offer ad. Brand audience per TrendTrack: 55-64 and 65+, roughly even gender, GB + US.

## The three-layer funnel

1. **Long-form story ads** (unaware entry): 2,800-5,000 word first-person narratives, image format, LEARN MORE.
2. **Checkmark statics** (problem/solution-aware retargeting): "Amla, the fruit packed with antioxidants..." + 5 benefit checks + 90-day guarantee. These are the account's reach leaders (133+ days running).
3. **Most-aware offer ad**: the "I need to publicly apologize... because I lied" fake-apology pattern interrupt resolving into 50% off / buy-3-get-2 + scarcity.

## The factory model (the single biggest finding)

Alevia is not writing ads. They are running a modular production system:

- **One canonical mechanism core per problem category, reused near-verbatim across ads.** BP: endothelium / nitric oxide / corroding pipe / "treating the number, not the wall" / bucket with a hole. Cholesterol: oxidized LDL / smoke vs fire / delivery trucks / self-feeding loop. Joints + nerves: mitochondria / AMPK / "delivering lumber to a job site with no power" / "the nurse" folklore name. Weight: AMPK as "the switch." The mechanism block is the durable asset; narrative shells are swappable.
- **Templated product-name position.** Both nerve ads name Alevia at EXACTLY 59% of word count. Across the library the brand lands between 55% and 92%, never earlier. Amla (ingredient) is named before Alevia (brand), mechanism before ingredient.
- **Templated beats appear verbatim across ads**: "whatever you're doing, keep doing it" doctor line (3+ ads), the week-1-nothing damaging-admission timeline (all ads), the not-all-Amla standardization block (all ads), the guarantee-verified-by-phone beat, the "Feel Like Yourself Again" signoff.
- **Template bleed tells** (batch production fingerprints to avoid when replicating): two husbands named Wendell; "Patty" is the narrator of one ad and the dead cousin of another; "Dale" dies in one ad and narrates another; Erie PA appears twice; all narrators are 54-63; one ad's hook credential ("13 years as a registered nurse") never appears in its own body copy; one ad contradicts its own dosing (one vs two capsules).
- **Scaling = duplication, not budget on one ID.** Winners get relaunched as 4-12 identical copies (orthopedic surgeon x12, nurse-gabapentin x6, nurse-mother-BP x4). Losers die in 1-6 day test flights. Individual ad IDs churn; the copy is the asset.
- **Winners are translated verbatim internationally** (the Maureen ad runs in French, labradoodle included).
- TrendTrack's estimated spend on these is unreliable (shows single-digit euros on a six-figure ad); use days-running + duplicate count + rank trajectory as the real signals.

## The 16-beat skeleton and what is invariant vs tradeable

Every ad runs the Maureen skeleton. The INVARIANT core (never removed in any of the 17):
hook confession/paradox → stakes teaser → belief-planted narrator identity → agitation before any solution → enemy branded with the system's own word → mechanism reframe that explains ALL past failures at once → delayed product naming with the standardization objection block → damaging-admission results timeline → future pacing that recovers the exact lost territory → 90-day guarantee.

The three beats they trade away most often:
- **Multi-character proof stacking with numbers**: present in Maureen (Elaine + Donna + narrator), thin or absent in nearly every other ad. Maureen is the maximal version.
- **Doctor astonishment**: replaced by witness astonishment (daughter, husband, seamstress) in the identity/grief variants; replaced by "anti-astonishment" honesty ("might never come all the way back") in the observer notebook ad.
- **Messenger separation**: sacrificed in the self-research variants (bridal dress, cortisone, lab-results diary, notebook, register), where an institutional substitute carries trust instead (NIH/"actual papers," failed cheap purchases, guarantee verification).

## The enemy-word refrain (one per ad, always the system's own vocabulary)

watching (Maureen) · managed/controlled (Lisinopril, card list, register) · borderline (Christmas card, CPR) · manage the decline (surgeon) · the merry-go-round (cortisone) · wear and tear (pickles) · willpower (bridal dress) · the number (dad's cuff) · "this is normal at your age" (grandson burn) · attribution bias / "those numbers don't lie" (cardiologist's wife) · "the body is a patient liar" (notebook) · the mute button (gabapentin) · the drawer (CPR, as enemy-object).

## Narrator identity = a planted license for the mechanism

Librarian → "reads too much." Bookkeeper → "reads research like magazines." Nurse → verifies biology in-story. Plumber → pipes mechanism in trade vernacular. Postal worker → "not a person who ignores patterns." Billing supervisor → "does not buy things off the internet on faith." Cafeteria worker → "not a millionaire, doesn't throw money at things." The job is always chosen to make the mechanism digestion or the skeptical purchase in-character.

## Awareness walk (consistent five-stage ladder)

- Unaware → problem aware: always a CONTRADICTION the reader adjudicates ("One of them is lying"; "his LDL was 108 when he died. Controlled."; "the quitter's numbers beat the discipline's").
- Problem → solution aware: always arrives as a question or a search ("why does blood pressure stay high even when medication controls the number"; a 3AM query string quoted verbatim).
- Solution → product aware: ingredient first, then the not-all-Amla moat, then brand.
- Product → most aware: the guarantee, often verified in-story by phone.

## Gradualization toolbox observed

Delayed naming (55-92%); double agitation cycle before any solution; the failed-solutions ladder with exact numbers ("4 points. 8 weeks of discipline for 4 points"; "$3,400 in 5 years"; three failed purchases including CHEAP AMLA, which pre-kills the Amazon objection); micro-agreements; Socratic dialogue delivering the mechanism secondhand; explicit pacing statements ("I want to tell you slowly, because this is the part most women don't get told"); skepticism modeled before purchase; the secret solo trial modeling the reader's own likely behavior; survival spoilers that trade suspense for permission ("He didn't die that day"); dated-diary serialization.

## Trust architecture patterns

- Messenger chains up to FIVE hops from the seller (Walt → doctor → wife → nurse → nurse's father-in-law). The seller never speaks in any story ad.
- Damaging admissions at three levels: product level (week 1 nothing, "I almost stopped"), moral level (the narrator confesses to lying, snooping, secrecy), resolution level ("that's not a revolution, that's a crack"; "he's not 100 percent").
- Guarantee reframes: verified by phone; matched to "the same window the trials used"; empty-bottle acceptance; framed as a persuasion script for a stubborn parent; framed as revealed behavior ("I never sent it back. I just kept buying more").
- Compliance-safe claim language throughout ("associated with," "supports," "has been shown to influence"), narrators stay ON their meds or reduce only under a doctor. The one ad that walked up to drug disparagement (statin depletes CoQ10, register ad) died the same day it launched: the compliance third rail.
- Anti-hype guardrails as trust builders ("It's not a weight loss pill. It doesn't burn fat"; "It doesn't unclog anything. I want to be clear.").

## Devices beyond the Maureen ad (the expansion pack)

- Accusation-bookend hook: the doctor-astonishment payoff moved INTO line 1 as a scandal ("my surgeon asked if I'd been getting black-market stem cells") and re-earned over 3,500 words. This is their most-duplicated ad (x12).
- The covert-dosing blind trial (observer ads): wife hides the capsule in the vitamin organizer, so the husband's improvement is testimony from a subject who doesn't know he's being treated. Placebo objection annihilated in-story. Only an observer narrator can run this.
- Consent theater: the father blesses the ad's publication ("Send it. There's some other 57 year old daughter out there").
- Competitor-hardware recommendation as trust (the messenger recommends a $59.99 Omron cuff before any supplement).
- Intergenerational preview: a parent or sibling as the narrator's living future ("I was becoming my mother"), replacing Pete as the stakes engine.
- The enemy who sleeps in her bed: cardiologist husband as both antagonist and final astonished validator, with an adversarially-validated blind lab test.
- Vindication/revenge engine (Umbria ad); righteous ANGER instead of grief as fuel (cortisone ad); succession stakes (pizza register); eulogy P.S. ("Dale, Tom, Greg, Jim. I'm sorry I didn't know sooner"); deliberate unresolved subplots as authenticity (why Glen never called).
- Observer-framework transform (systematic across all buyer-for-other ads): hook becomes the narrator's complicity confession ("I had to pretend I didn't see it"); crisis becomes a witnessed confession; the loved one's PRIDE becomes the central objection; proof triangulates through household witnesses; the closing checklist targets observable behaviors in someone else (two pairs of socks in July, bracing on the counter, a secret notebook), recruiting the reader as detective.

## What scaled vs what died (performance signals)

- SCALED: insider-authority + accusation bookend (surgeon, x12 duplicates); nurse-gabapentin (x6); nurse-mother BP (x4); Maureen (45+ days, "scaling hard," translated to French).
- SURVIVED LONG: the notebook observer ad (84 days, still live) with covert-dosing + ally-doctor; the lab-results diary (2 months).
- DIED FAST: literary/revenge structure (Umbria, 3 days); grief/identity variants (pickles, bridal dress, ~5 days each despite good reach); statin-disparagement mechanism (same-day kill).
- Confession + peer-messenger (grandson burn) got ~17x the reach of insider-authority + death-proxy (gabapentin) per day of run time, yet gabapentin is what they duplicated: they scale the angle that converts, not the one that reaches.

## Portable lessons (for our brands)

1. Build ONE mechanism block per product-problem pair and treat it as the durable asset; iterate narrative shells around it. This is the angle bank philosophy running at factory scale.
2. The angle is the problem DOOR: same product, same mechanism, 6+ doors (the number, the med's side effects, the treatment fear, the shame scene, the observer's guilt, the mortality artifact).
3. Extract the enemy word from VoC: the dismissive phrase the system actually says to the avatar, then weaponize it as the refrain.
4. Choose the narrator's job to license the mechanism and the purchase.
5. Product name lands at ~60% of word count, ingredient before brand, mechanism before ingredient, standardization moat immediately after brand.
6. The observer/buyer-for-other lane is a separate framework variant with its own devices (complicity hook, covert dosing, behavior checklist, guarantee-as-script). Huge for products bought by wives/daughters.
7. Scale = duplicating proven copy across many ad IDs, not raising budget on one.
8. Compliance third rail: direct med disparagement kills ads same-day. Stay on "the med treats the number, nobody fixed the wall."
