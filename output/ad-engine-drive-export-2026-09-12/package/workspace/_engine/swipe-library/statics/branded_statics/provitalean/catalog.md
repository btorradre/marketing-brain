## Design DNA

### Color & Tone
- Muted warm tones — sepia-leaning, slightly desaturated lifestyle photography
- No designed-graphic overlays — image-only, native-feeling
- Standard FB Ad Library footer chrome (domain, headline, CTA button)

### Photography / Imagery Style
- **Medical/clinical:** Color X-ray torso imagery with red highlight markers — pseudo-scientific overlay (the only "designed" visual in the inventory)
- **Lifestyle UGC:** Bedroom mirror selfies, kitchen scenes, domestic settings — feels like real customer photos
- **Before/after walking:** Two women in neutral activewear walking through a mall/store side by side — composition is subtle, not flagged as comparison

- "My Ozempic results shocked everyone. But the REAL shock 6 months after stopping…"
- Time-anchored disclosure: "Last Tuesday at 11:47 PM, I found my husband sitting on our bathroom floor…"

- Highly personal-narrative, mid-sentence interruptions, ellipses-heavy
- All voiced from female narrator (Sarah Collins, Menopause And Me)
