## Brand Snapshot

Soluma surfaced as one of the most heavily-running new metabolic brands in the May 2 pull. The brand appears to be running a **mid-section / belly-fat angle** with native-looking imagery (close-up shot of a woman's bare midsection holding loose skin / belly fat) paired with the headline **"Reactivate your body."**
