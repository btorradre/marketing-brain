## Design DNA

- **Colors:** Muted clinical (dark reds/browns in tissue close-ups, whites in pill/capsule grids)
- **Photo style:** **Shock-value clinical imagery** — tight medical close-ups of what appears to be arterial/tissue walls; 4-grid product/symptom comparisons with capsule rings; occasional lifestyle shots (couples at dinner) for softer variants
- **Typography:** Zero overlay typography on images — all persuasion lives in the primary text
- **Vibe:** "Clinic-credentialed exposé" — authority-framed, unsettling, editorial rather than polished product ad
- **Key DNA insight:** NVH has **decoupled copy from image**. The image's only job is to stop the scroll; the narrative, mechanism, and close all live in the ad's primary text block.

## Known Ad Creative Approaches

### Archetype: Medical/Clinical Proof (#7) — paired-contrast narrative

**Top-of-funnel hook pattern (image ads, launched Apr 9-10, 2026):**

> "My brother weighs 230 pounds. Hasn't run a mile since high school. Eats drive-through three times a week. His doctor put him on Atorvastatin. His LDL is 132.
>
> My husband rides a bicycle four days a week. Hasn't eaten red meat in four years. Lost twenty-six pounds. Eats salmon, oatmeal, vegetables. Tracks every meal. Rides 80 miles a week..."

The paired-contrast structure is the key mechanic: **Patient A does everything wrong → bad outcome / Patient B does everything right → same bad outcome.** The reader is forced to conclude "lifestyle alone isn't the answer" which primes the mechanism (oxidative damage) and the product (PrimeCell H2).

**Video variant hooks (running alongside the static variant):**
- Authority-defiance: "My doctor said if I refused Lisinopril, she'd document it in my chart as non-compliance against medical advice. I had eleven minutes with her. She'd never met me before."
- Insider-exit: "Dr. Warren's retirement letter came on a Tuesday. Standard form letter. 'After 35 years of practice, I'm stepping [away]...'"

### Design Pattern Confirmation
All 5 new statics observed today **reinforce the 2026-04-10 findings**: NVH continues to run the decoupled-image strategy. Images are deliberately **un-designed** — no typography overlays, no price callouts, no product shots. The persuasion is 100% in the primary text, and images are casting choices (a character or a prop) rather than branded design artifacts.

This makes NVH the **anti-archetype** of polished branded statics. It's a wholly different creative strategy from Bloom, AG1, Onnit, etc.
