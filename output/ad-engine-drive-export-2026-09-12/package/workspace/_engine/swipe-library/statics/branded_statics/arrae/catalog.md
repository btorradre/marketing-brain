## Design DNA

### Color Palette
- **Primary neutrals:** Black (#000000), white (#ffffff), warm beige (#f7f5f0)
- **Body text:** Medium gray (#666666)
- **Button primary:** Near-black (#1b1a1f)
- **Button secondary:** Dark blue-gray (#363f4d)
- **Sale/discount:** Red accent (#e22828)
- **Brand accents:** Warm earth tones — taupe, tan, soft browns (rgba 209,186,170 / rgba 243,233,224)
- **Background tones:** Soft cream and beige (rgba 237,233,230)

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Poppins (weight 300) — elegant, modern, light
- **Display/hero:** Florentino — display serif for major headlines
- **Subheadings:** Kepler Light Italic Display — refined sophistication
- **Body/buttons:** Figtree (weights 300-900) with uppercase button text, 0.1rem letter-spacing
- **Alt heading:** Dallas — lightweight sans-serif with generous letter-spacing (0.2em)

### Photography Style
- Professional, warm-lit product photography
- Minimalist composition with generous whitespace
- Capsule close-ups and lifestyle imagery
- Gallery layouts showcasing product and brand story
- Warm, natural lighting suggesting calm and wellness

### Overall Vibe
Luxury wellness through restraint — sophisticated layouts, generous whitespace, intentional visual hierarchy. Aspirational yet accessible. Premium positioning without clinical coldness.
