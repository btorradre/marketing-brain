## Template Potential for Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra

1. **Gendered challenger messaging** ("not your boyfriend's protein") is a strong formula. Could adapt for women's health: "not your doctor's first recommendation" or similar.
2. **Aspirational beauty-from-within** framing ("GLOW" / "STARTS FROM WITHIN") translates well for skin/beauty supplement positioning.
3. **Retail distribution callouts** build authority — if products are available at retail, this is a high-trust static format.
4. **BOGO / bundle offers** on clean product shots drive conversion — simple and effective for scaling.
5. **Education statics** ("The scoop on Colostrum") are good for newer/unfamiliar product categories.
