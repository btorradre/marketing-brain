## Design DNA — 2026-04-20 update
- **Photo style:** **Predominantly UGC/lifestyle stills**, NOT polished branded statics. Bedroom imagery (couples in bed, woman in red robe), close-up body parts (cracked feet, heel ulcer), hands holding pills/herbs. Strong "clinical case study" or "real-life moment" feel.
- **Branded design layer:** Limited. The most "designed" pieces are bold-claim text overlays on lifestyle photographs (e.g., "70% of heart attack victims had ED first" over an interior shot, "The Silent Killer: Endothelial Dysfunction" over an artwork-style image). Not true branded statics with packaging-forward design — more like "captioned editorial images."
- **Overall vibe:** Native-first / pseudo-editorial. The brand explicitly avoids the polished branded-static look — likely a deliberate choice to look like content, not advertising. Authority-via-persona (Dr. Mike Harris, Mark Zillmann, Rob Moore) rather than authority-via-design.

## Strategic Insight
- **Auri Labs is running an explicit anti-branded-static strategy.** Of ~83 active image ads, fewer than 10 use any meaningful branded design layer. The vast majority are styled to look like organic content, expert posts, or editorial imagery.
- **This is the OPPOSITE of GLP-1 SOS and the new Neurosmile static** observed today, both of which lean into branded design.
- **Implication for our brands:** Auri Labs validates that scaled brands can win with native-first creative when paired with strong persona-driven authority pages and sub-brand fragmentation. But it's a different funnel architecture (lifestyle ad → advertorial → product) and not directly templateable as a "branded static."

## Copy Intelligence
- "If your feet crawl like insects are under your skin, you probably believed your doctor when he said your erectile dysfunction was the same nerve damage spreading up your body."
- "It made perfect sense. First your feet. Then your penis. Next your heart…"
- "Beetroot extract is a scam. Not because beetroot doesn't work. But because 90% of beetroot supplements are formulated to fail."
- "Pharmaceutical companies make $6 billion a year from V*gra. Know what they don't tell you? That failed erections predict heart attacks. There's more money in treating symptoms than curing you."
- "If you're having any erection problems—even mild ones—talk to a cardiologist, not just your primary doctor. Get your arterial health checked. Because your penis might be trying to save your life."
- **Pattern:** Authority-claim openers + named-villain framing (pharma, beetroot industry) + body-system cascade (feet → ED → heart) + "your symptom is a warning sign" reframe.

## Template Potential
- **Bold-text-overlay on lifestyle photograph** is the only template here that translates as a "branded static." The minimalism is the point — copy and image carry the weight, not design.
- **Pattern interrupt headlines** ("X is a scam. Not because…") highly templateable for any brand willing to take a contrarian stance.
- **Body-system cascade narrative** ("First X, then Y, then Z") — strong belief-shift sequence for video and advertorial more than static.
- **For Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra:** This brand is more useful as a **video / advertorial** reference than a static reference. File the design intel but pull copy patterns into the long-form-copy and advertorial skills.
