## Design DNA

### Color & Tone
- Soft natural skin tones dominate the canvas (peach, pink, cream)
- Minimal designed-graphic overlay — image carries the weight
- White footer band with brand domain + headline + Learn more CTA (standard FB Ad Library card chrome)

### Photography / Imagery Style
- **Clinical illustration:** medical-style cross-section of hair follicle (split panel: damaged follicle vs healthy follicle) — editorial-grade rendered illustration, not stock
- **Before/after format:** side-by-side close-up portraits of women, hair-focus framing, neutral backgrounds
- **Subjects:** women 35-55, naturalistic, no celebrity/influencer aesthetic

### Copy / Hook Style
- Pattern: "Please STOP taking supplements for your hair loss on GLP-1 medications…"
- Authority-mom voice — "Sophie" / "I used to be just like you"
- Anti-Nutrafol, anti-biotin, anti-protein-shake counter-positioning
- Personal-first-person construction throughout

## Template Potential

- The split-panel clinical illustration is highly repurposable. Lunessa could run a "hot flash receptor cross-section" variant — same archetypal structure, swap the biology.

- Less direct fit — motility category doesn't map cleanly to follicle illustrations.
