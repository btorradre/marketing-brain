## Template Potential for Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra

1. **Bold Claim + Product format** is highly replicable — simple text overlay on dark/branded background with product shot and single CTA. Works great for mechanism education.
2. **Challenger messaging ("Think X isn't for Y? Think again.")** is a strong pattern for audience-expanding statics. Could adapt: "Think hormones are just a woman's issue? Think again."
3. **Stat-forward statics ("70% of your immune system...")** translate perfectly for clinical proof positioning.
4. **Supp stack integration** angle works for any supplement brand trying to integrate into existing routines.
