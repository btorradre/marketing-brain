## Visual Hierarchy Principles

**For Static Ads:**

1. **Dominant element** (40% of space) - Product or hero image
2. **Headline** (30% of space) - Key benefit or mechanism
3. **Supporting text** (20% of space) - Specific detail or proof
4. **CTA button** (10% of space) - Clear action statement
