## HOW IT SELLS A NOVEL INGREDIENT TO A COLD, SKEPTICAL MARKET

### 1. The criterion line — the single most important sentence in the ad
> **0:44 — "What you need is something that works WITH your slowed gut, not against it."**

Stated BEFORE any ingredient is named. Same move as Nuora's three-step spec. The viewer accepts a
standard while she has no reason to resist it, because nothing is being sold yet. Every ingredient
that follows is then measured against a rule *she already agreed to* — not against her skepticism.

**No ingredient is ever introduced cold. It arrives as the answer to a spec.**

### 2. Every ingredient is introduced by MECHANISM, never by name
Fixed formula: `[ingredient] + [physical verb] + so that + [plain outcome]`

| Ingredient | Line |
|---|---|
| Fermented kombucha | "is full of natural acids that **gently wake your gut's movement back up**" |
| Dandelion root | "**gets your bile flowing, so** food breaks down instead of just sitting there" |
| Slippery elm | "**calms the irritated gut lining, so** things move without the cramps" |
| The probiotic | "tough enough to **survive your stomach acid**, where the regular ones don't" |

No Latin. No "clinically studied." No dosage. No percentages. Just a physical action and a
consequence in her words. **The mechanism IS the proof** — the logic is doing the work a citation
would do, which is exactly the phrasing register Brooks wants.

### 3. Each ingredient is the designated killer of one earlier objection
This is the elegant part — **the ingredient stack IS the objection-handling section.**

| Failed solution killed at 0:35-0:44 | Its designated killer at 0:48-1:06 |
|---|---|
| "Fiber just adds more bulk to the backup" | Kombucha — *wakes movement*, adds nothing |
| "Laxatives force painful contractions" | Slippery elm — "**without the cramps**" |
| "Stool softeners only loosen what's there, but don't push it out" | Dandelion — bile actually *breaks food down* |
| (implied: regular probiotics) | "**where the regular ones don't**" |

Nothing is argued. Every dead alternative has a successor waiting.

### 4. Failed solutions are killed with a FAILURE MODE, not a verdict
Never "it doesn't work." Always *why*: adds bulk · forces contractions · only loosens what's there.
A reason makes the new thing **necessary**. A verdict only makes it optional.

### 5. Pure second person — there is no messenger to distrust
Zero "I." No narrator, no story, no character. "You're about to quit the shot." "You've lost
thirty pounds." "You've tried the fiber." For a cold, skeptical market this does two things:
it removes the person she'd have to trust, and **the ad demonstrating it knows her life IS the
credibility.** She's convinced before a claim is made.

### 6. The promise is deliberately small
Biggest claim in the ad: "go like clockwork, every morning without thinking about it." That's
*normal*, not miraculous. No weight claim. No cure. Skepticism has nothing to grab.

### 7. It never fights the thing she loves
> "you finally get to **stay on the shot you fought so hard to keep**"

The GLP-1 is never the villain — losing it is the stake. Same discipline as the 6-min Nuora VSL.

### 8. Proof is social + risk-reversal, never scientific
Stacked only in the last 12 seconds: "the only brand I trust" · 130,000 women · 4.8 from 9,481
Trustpilot reviews · 60-day money back · one gummy a day. **Zero studies cited.** For a novel
ingredient in a cold market, volume + refund does the job a study would, and nothing is
falsifiable.

### 9. "The only brand I trust"
Five words carrying an entire authority section.

## THE SKEPTICISM → FUNNEL MODEL (Brooks, 2026-08-25)

Market skepticism sets the SHAPE of the ad, not its length.

| | Warm / familiar ingredient (Nuora iron) | Cold / novel ingredient (Jevawell, **and Motilli**) |
|---|---|---|
| Narrator | Authority presenter can carry it | Remove the messenger — pure second person |
| Mechanism | Numbered spec, 3 steps | Criterion line + one mechanism per ingredient |
| Objections | Handled inside the mechanism | **One designated killer per failed solution** |
| Promise | Can be specific (ferritin rises 4-8 wks) | Deliberately modest — "normal" |
| Proof | Practice caseload | Social volume + guarantee |
| Runtime | 2:40 / 509 words | **1:32 / 274 words** |

## FORMAT

Fully 3D-animated — stylised Pixar-ish characters (a friendly kombucha blob, a leaf-headed
slippery elm with a paintbrush, a helmeted probiotic with a shield) intercut with dark cinematic
medical renders of the gut, plus 2-3 domestic scenes of the avatar in a Pixar style. No live
action, no presenter, no product shot until the final card.
Captions: hook in a **red** box (0:00-0:03) then white rounded boxes, black geometric sans, 2-5
words, ~1s per card, centre-frame at chest height. Ends on a static offer card: "Your Gut Back,
One Gummy a Day" · 130,000+ women · Trustpilot 4.8 from 9,481 · green CTA button · 60-day
guarantee badge.
⚠️ Every single ingredient beat is a purpose-built animated character. That is the production
cost of this format — 4 characters + ~8 gut environments. Budget for it.

## PORTING TO MOTILLI (mechanism bible: butyrate loop / apigenin + prebiotic fiber)

The mapping is nearly one-to-one, and our mechanism is *stronger* because it explains why the
problem worsens over time — Jevawell's doesn't.

- **Criterion line** ← "What you need is something that rebuilds the colony, not something that
  pushes what's already stuck."
- **Failure modes** (already written in the bible): fiber = food with no one left to eat it ·
  probiotics = workers with nowhere to live · laxatives = force one movement, rebuild nothing.
- **Designated killers:** apigenin (rebuild the environment — and it *barely absorbs*, so it stays
  down in the colon where the work is) · soluble prebiotic fiber (feeds the butyrate-producers
  specifically, without the coarse bulk that bloats).
- **Never fight the shot** — "two separate systems" is already a REQUIRED belief in the bible.
- **Modest promise** — first normal, non-cramping movement around day five.
- **Proof block** — third-party tested / COA posted · 90-day guarantee (outlasts the rebuild).
- **Target: 274-300 words, ~1:35.**
