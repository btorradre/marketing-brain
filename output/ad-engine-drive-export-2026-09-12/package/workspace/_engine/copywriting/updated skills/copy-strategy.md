# Copy Strategy — The Brief That Drives the Copy

One writing skill cannot serve every avatar. A product-aware reader in a Stage-4 market and a problem-aware reader in a Stage-2 market need almost opposite copy. The writing skill should not be guessing which it faces. This skill decides, hands over a brief, and the writing skill executes it.

**There are no universal ratios, no default story length, no fixed product-discovery rule.** All three are OUTPUTS of awareness + sophistication + concept, computed here.

The system: **copy-strategy** (this, the brief) → **concept-databank** (the twelve vehicles) → **long-form-copy** (line-level execution). This skill pulls concepts from concept-databank by name and never re-lists them. Its output brief maps field-for-field onto the writing skill's locks.

---

## STEP 1 — LOCK THE THREE INPUTS

**Awareness** (where the reader ARRIVES, not where the funnel began): Unaware, Problem-Aware, Solution-Aware, Product-Aware, Most-Aware.

**Sophistication** (how many times this avatar has heard claims in this category) — and what each stage *demands*:

| Stage | Market state | What the copy must do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | First claim, virgin | State the claim plainly. No mechanism needed. |
| 2 | Claim heard, still believed | Enlarge the existing claim — bigger promise, more specificity. Mechanism stays implicit. |
| 3 | Claim tired | Introduce ONE new mechanism that explains why it works this time. Don't pile objections — the market still believes claims. |
| 4 | Mechanisms competing | Enlarge the mechanism AND clear the failure objections. The avatar already tried the category; the dominant job is answering *"why didn't it work for me"* (wrong version, dose, delivery). |
| 5 | Claims exhausted, mocked | Identity and brand. Mechanism is deaf ground — a new mechanism reads as one more tired claim and backfires. Sell who they become, ritual, allegiance. |

Stage 5 is NOT Stage 3 with more story. Stage 3 teaches a mechanism to a market that still believes claims; Stage 5 has stopped believing claims, so it retreats to identity. Misrouting these writes a mechanism ad into a market deaf to mechanisms.

**Concept** — select from concept-databank (the twelve vehicles) plus the tight variant. Lift its metadata into the brief: **engine, awareness lean, discovery default.** Concept selection leans by awareness (a default, not a law):

- Lower awareness (Unaware / Problem) leans to storytelling vehicles — Witness Story, Generational Mirror, Outsider's Mirror, Accidental Beneficiary.
- Higher awareness (Solution / Product) leans to Insider Revelation, Category Intercept, Refusal, Defector.
- Confession/Reversal and Anomaly flex across all levels. Insider can run product-aware (the Renavita nurse). The strategist picks; awareness only sets the gravity.

If any of the three inputs is unknown, stop and resolve it. The brief is wrong if an input is guessed.

---

## STEP 2 — COMPUTE THE BELIEF SET

Five beliefs can shift (1: suffering is real / 2: my approach is wrong / 3: the root cause is X / 4: the solution needs A+B+C / 5: this product is the one). Awareness determines which are ALREADY HELD (confirm in 1–2 sentences, never re-teach) vs. need full work:

- **Unaware:** none held. Belief 1 is the whole opening job. Longest path.
- **Problem-Aware:** 1 held. Heavy work on 2 and 3. Product (5) light.
- **Solution-Aware:** 1–2 held. The fight is 3 and 4 — why everything tried failed, what the real criteria are. The failed-attempt-catalog avatar.
- **Product-Aware:** 1–3 largely held. Collapse the emotional clearing. The work is 4 and 5 — differentiation, objection handling, proof. Do NOT re-prove suffering they already feel.
- **Most-Aware:** 1–4 held. Offer only — price, guarantee, urgency.

Write the belief list explicitly: confirm-fast vs. fully-shift. This is the single step that prevents the most expensive error in the system — re-educating a reader on what they already believe, which reads as padding and kills the sell. It feeds the writing skill's Belief Ladder (Lock 2).

---

## STEP 3 — SET THE PROPORTION (the two-axis rule)

Awareness and sophistication are **independent axes**. Compute both, then size:

- **Awareness sizes the STORY block** (emotional clearing). Lower awareness = thicker story (the reader must care before they will learn). Higher = thinner.
- **Sophistication sizes the MECHANISM + OBJECTION block.** Stage 2 enlarges one claim; Stage 3 teaches one mechanism; Stage 4 teaches the mechanism AND clears 2–4 failure objections; Stage 5 drops mechanism for identity.

They STACK. A problem-aware + Stage-4 piece carries a thick story AND a heavy teardown — that is why it runs long. Length is the sum of these two, never a target. Cap at **3,000 words** as a discipline ceiling. Never strip the story because sophistication is high; never strip the objections because awareness is low.

State the proportion as a lean ("story-dominant" / "mechanism-dominant" / "balanced"), not a word count. The writing skill sizes each beat to its job.

---

## STEP 4 — PULL THE TIMED OBJECTION SET

At Stage 4, objections are not generic — they are the specific failure stories competitors planted. "Wrong species," "underdosed," "doesn't absorb" are objections *because the dominant competitors spent millions installing them.* So the objection list is derived from what the market was taught to worry about, not from the avatar in the abstract.

Pull the live competitor objection set from the swipe library / TrendTrack before writing, so the teardown answers the doubt actually in the reader's head — not one you guessed. Then hand each objection to the writing skill's Objection-Firing Map (Lock 3) with its firing moment:

- "why didn't this work for me" → fires when you claim the ingredient works
- "isn't this just [format]" → fires when the product appears
- "supplements are a scam" → fires during the failed-attempt catalog
- "why didn't my doctor say this" → fires after the mechanism is taught
- "is it actually working" → fires at the first result beat

Skipping this step means the copy argues with a ghost — clearing objections the reader doesn't have while leaving the real one standing.

---

## STEP 5 — SET PRODUCT INTEGRATION + PUSH INTENSITY

Discovery mode comes from the concept's databank metadata, not instinct. It is a **tested variable**, not a virtue:

- Insider Revelation, Category Intercept, Refusal → can name EARLY, sell direct.
- Witness Story, Investigation, Generational Mirror, Outsider's Mirror, Accidental Beneficiary, Defector → discovered LATE (naming early breaks the lived-experience or scene frame).
- Confession/Reversal, Anomaly → either. Test both.

Whatever the mode, the reader must build the evaluation criteria before the product appears (this is what the writing skill's Lock 5 enforces — *earned, not discovered*). Push intensity scales with awareness: most-aware takes a hard offer CTA; problem-aware cannot.

**Rotation rule:** log which discovery mode each live piece uses. Same technique every time burns creative. When the library is lopsided, push this brief toward an untested combination. (Unaware concepts are locked to late discovery and can't be tested both ways — note that in the log.)

---

## STEP 6 — SET THE ENGINE + RUN THE ROTATION CHECK

Lift the engine from the concept's databank metadata (terror / grief-guilt / vindication / curiosity / relief / social-proof / indignation). Then run the check that keeps the library from collapsing into fear:

- **Same-angle test:** could this angle run on a different engine? The kidney angle runs on terror (dialysis) — but also curiosity (Anomaly), vindication (Refusal), or relief. Don't inherit terror by default.
- **Library concentration test:** is one engine carrying more than ~60% of live pieces? If yes, this brief should NOT use it, even if it's the strongest single option. Range beats local maxima.

This matters twice over: creative fatigue (seven flavors of the same fear stop converting) and compliance (terror-stacking on vulnerable health audiences is what gets persona pages flagged). The declared engine feeds the writing skill's engine declaration; the rotation check is mirrored in its Pass 2 governor.

---

## STEP 7 — OUTPUT THE BRIEF

Hand the writing skill exactly this, nothing more. Each field maps to the lock that consumes it:

1. **Awareness + sophistication + concept (+ tight variant)** → narrator (Lock 1, via concept) and structure weighting.
2. **Belief set** — confirm-fast list vs. fully-shift list → Belief Ladder (Lock 2).
3. **Proportion** — story-dominant / mechanism-dominant / balanced → length and structure weighting (Section II).
4. **Timed objection set** — each objection + its firing moment → Objection-Firing Map (Lock 3).
5. **Restored-life-moment for the close** — the one specific scene that resolves the opening pain → Matched Pain↔Payoff (Lock 4).
6. **Discovery mode + push intensity** — named-early/discovered-late, soft/direct/hard, CTA type → Earned-Not-Discovered (Lock 5).
7. **Engine (+ rotation note)** → engine declaration and Pass 2 governor.
8. **Anti-mimicry note** — what's been used across the live library (narrator, guide, engine, metaphor, setting, archetype) so this piece differs.

Then route to the writing skill. It executes the brief; it does not re-decide it.

---

## WHAT THIS SKILL DOES NOT DO

It does not write hooks (hook-generation), pick the emotional wound (the angle, set upstream), list the concepts (concept-databank owns those), or write prose (long-form-copy). It makes the decisions the writing skill was previously making badly because it had no awareness-driven input: belief set, proportion, objection timing, discovery mode, push intensity, and engine. Move those here and the writing skill gets shorter and the copy gets sharper.
