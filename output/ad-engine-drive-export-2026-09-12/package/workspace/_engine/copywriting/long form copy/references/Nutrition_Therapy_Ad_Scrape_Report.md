### 2. Key Structural Elements of the Copywriting Framework
Every single ad template, despite having a different narrative hook, follows a highly consistent **7-step structural framework**:

1. **The Hook (Emotional/Relatable):** Opens with an extreme or highly relatable pain point (e.g., "9 days without a bowel movement," "father took laxatives for 12 years," "stomach swollen like a 6-month pregnancy").
2. **Dismantling the Mainstream Playbook (The Villain):** Attacks standard medical protocols. It positions "eat more fibre," "drink more water," "take laxatives," and "low-FODMAP diets" as useless or actively harmful.
3. **The Pivot to Physiology (The Epiphany):** Introduces a specialist or clinical researcher who reveals the "real" underlying mechanism.
4. **The Core Science (The Mechanism):**
   * **Nitric Oxide:** The primary chemical messenger that signals gut muscles to contract and relax.
   * **Oestrogen Depletion:** Post-menopausal oestrogen drop directly halts Nitric Oxide production.
   * **SIBO (Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth):** Methane-producing bacteria slow motility and damage the gut lining, building an *absorption barrier* that blocks capsules/tablets.
5. **The Solution (The Hero):** Introduces **sublingual L-arginine liquid drops** (Nutrition Therapy's Bowel Support Drops). It explains why drops work where capsules fail: they bypass the damaged gut lining and absorb directly under the tongue.
6. **The Timeline of Recovery:** Outlines a week-by-week progression of what to expect (Weeks 1-2: signal wakes up; Weeks 3-4: bloating eases; Weeks 5-6: daily movements; Weeks 7-8: normal life restored).
7. **The Risk-Free Call to Action (CTA):** Offers a "60-day no-risk trial" with a direct link to their landing pages.
