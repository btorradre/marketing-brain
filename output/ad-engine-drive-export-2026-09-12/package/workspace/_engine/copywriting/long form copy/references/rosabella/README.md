# Rosabella Facebook Ads - Long-Form Sales Letters

## Summary

This directory contains 9 long-form sales letter ads from Rosabella's Facebook Ad Library (page ID: 389183070942458). All ads are active as of March 2026.

## Campaign Theme

All 9 ads feature the same testimonial narrative from a 63-year-old grandmother named Margaret, who shares her personal story about:
- Developing chronic joint pain starting in her mid-fifties
- Gradual loss of mobility and independence
- Weight gain despite eating less
- A health crisis where she falls and cannot get up
- Being told by her daughter about putting her in a care home
- Her discovery of the chronic inflammation cycle and Rosabella Organic Beetroot as a solution
- Her recovery over 4 months

## Key Message Architecture

The sales letter follows this narrative arc:

1. **Hook:** Personal tragedy - daughter tells her she's going to put her in a care home
2. **Problem Identification:** Chronic inflammation driving a destructive cycle
3. **Deep Explanation:** How inflammation leads to joint pain → inactivity → metabolic collapse → weight gain → more inflammation
4. **Social Proof:** Personal health crisis and hospital experience
5. **Expert Validation:** Doctor explains the inflammation cycle in medical terms
6. **Solution:** Rosabella Organic Beetroot (1,300mg cold-processed)
7. **Proof of Results:** Week-by-week recovery over 8 weeks and 4 months total
8. **Call to Action:** Limited pricing offer, 90-day guarantee, urgency (stock levels)
