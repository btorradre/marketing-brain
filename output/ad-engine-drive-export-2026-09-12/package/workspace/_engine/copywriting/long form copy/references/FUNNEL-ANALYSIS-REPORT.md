# Funnel Analysis Report — Landing Pages Behind Winning Long-Form Copy
## March 2026

---

## Key Finding: The Dominant Funnel Pattern

**Long-form FB ad → Angle-matched PDP/Hybrid Advertorial**

The winning brands (especially Alevia) use a single product with a **templatized long-form PDP that gets cloned for different health angles.** Each Facebook ad targets a specific condition, and the landing page copy, educational content, and testimonials are swapped to match. The funnel mechanics (bundles, guarantee, subscription, progress timeline) remain constant across all variants.

## Alevia Funnel Deep Dive (The Template to Study)

### Page Structure (Consistent Across All Angle Variants)
1. Hero with product gallery + 4.8/5.0 rating badge
2. Problem-education section (angle-specific — joints/cholesterol/nerves/cellular)
3. 3-tier bundle pricing with subscription upsell
4. Progress timeline (Weeks 1-2 through 9-12+)
5. Comparison table vs. failed alternatives (turmeric, glucosamine, collagen, etc.)
6. Customer testimonial carousel (verified, with photos/dates)
7. FAQ accordion
8. 90-day money-back guarantee (repeated 3-4x throughout)

### Pricing Architecture
- 1 bottle: $39.99 (33% off)
- Buy 2 Get 1 Free: $79.99 (55% off) — "Most Popular"
- Buy 3 Get 2 Free: $119.99 (59% off) — "Best Results"
- +25% off with subscription (cancel anytime)

### Trust Stack
- 4.8/5.0 rating
- Verified reviews with photos
- 90-day empty-bottle guarantee
- Made in USA
- Third-party tested
- Non-GMO
- Payment security badges

### CRO Elements
- "Limited Time Offer" framing
- Estimated delivery date near CTA
- "Most Popular" anchor on mid-tier bundle
- Problem-agitate-solve copy structure on the page itself
- Progress timeline manages expectations AND reduces refund requests
- Multiple CTA placements throughout scroll depth
- Guarantee mentioned 4+ times

### The Multi-Angle Strategy
Alevia runs ONE product (Amla) through FIVE different landing page angles:
- **/amla/11** — Joint health (osteoarthritis, cartilage)
- **/amla/17** — Cholesterol/heart health (oxidized LDL)
- **/amla-cc** — General cellular health (broadest, top-of-funnel)
- **/amla-28** — Neuropathy/nerve health (most emotional copy)
- **/amla-92** — Joint/mobility variant (guarantee-forward for cold traffic)

Each long-form FB ad angle maps to a matching landing page. The ad says "joints" → lands on /amla/11. The ad says "cholesterol" → lands on /amla/17. **Congruence between ad angle and landing page angle is maintained throughout.**

## Besque (Grandma's Care Ads) Funnel

**Fundamentally different approach:** Premium brand homepage, NOT a direct-response funnel page.

- Lifestyle/aspirational branding over education + urgency
- Trustpilot integration (4.9-5.0 stars)
- 450,000+ customers claimed
- Hero slideshow, collection grid, brand story
- Flagship: Magic Body Oil 100ml at 50 GBP

- No timer/urgency mechanics — brand equity drives conversion

The long-form Facebook ad does ALL the belief-shifting work. The landing page just needs to look credible and close the sale.

## Strategic Implications for Our Copy

1. **The long-form ad must do ALL the belief-shifting work** before the reader lands on the page. The PDP/advertorial reinforces but doesn't introduce new beliefs.

2. **Ad angle → landing page angle congruence is non-negotiable.** If the ad talks about joints, the landing page better talk about joints from line 1.

3. **The progress timeline on the landing page mirrors the results section in the ad.** This is deliberate — the reader sees the same timeline twice (in the ad and on the page), reinforcing the expectation.

4. **The guarantee in the ad copy must match the guarantee on the page.** Alevia uses "90 days" in both. No mismatches.

5. **Multi-angle strategy = one product, many ads, many matching landing pages.** This is how you scale a single SKU to 84+ ads.
