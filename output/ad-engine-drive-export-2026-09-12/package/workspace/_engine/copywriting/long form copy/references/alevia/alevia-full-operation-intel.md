## THE FORMULA (Reverse-Engineered)

All Alevia ads follow this exact template:

1. **Hook:** "[Condition] diagnosed at [age]. I'm [older age] now. Turns out I never actually had [condition]. For [X] years, I 'managed' a condition I didn't actually understand."

2. **Erosion:** Years of decline, failed treatments with specific costs and durations

3. **Generational stakes:** Mother/father followed the same path (optional but frequent)

4. **Guide character:** Physical therapist, functional medicine doctor, or the narrator's own research introduces the AMPK/mitochondria mechanism

5. **Mechanism:** Mitochondria in [relevant cells] are dead → supplements/drugs can't work because cells have no energy → AMPK activation builds new mitochondria → cellular power restored → body can heal itself

6. **Product entry:** Alevia Amla — cold-extracted, therapeutic dose, Himalayan sourced

7. **Differentiator:** "Most Amla is heat-extracted, which destroys the compounds that activate AMPK. Alevia is cold-extracted at therapeutic dose."

8. **Results:** Progressive timeline from days to months

9. **Close:** "Just two capsules every morning. Get yours now before [urgency]."

### Key Insight: ONE Mechanism, MANY Angles
The AMPK/mitochondria mechanism is **universal** — it applies to joints, nerves, cholesterol, cellular aging, parasites. Alevia found ONE scientific mechanism and mapped it to 5+ health conditions, creating 5+ distinct ad angles that all sell the same product. This is the "one mechanism, many doors" strategy at scale.

6. Each page has its own "personality" — authority doctor vs. relatable patient vs. support community
7. Each landing page URL is angle-matched to the ad copy
