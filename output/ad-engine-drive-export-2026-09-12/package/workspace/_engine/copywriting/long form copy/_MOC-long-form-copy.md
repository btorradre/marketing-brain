## How Long-Form Copy Fits Your Funnel

**Top of Funnel**
Long-form narrative ads establish the mechanism and pain point identification. They're used on Facebook, Instagram, and affiliate platforms.

**Mid Funnel**
Long-form copy transitions from education to consideration. Used in email sequences and advertorial formats.

**Bottom of Funnel**
Long-form copy becomes more direct and action-oriented. Used in VSL scripts and sales pages.

## Workflow: From Strategy to Copy

\`\`\`
1. Market Analyst identifies mechanism and pain point
2. Creative Strategist selects narrative angle
3. Copy Chief writes long-form copy using the SKILL
4. Review against comparative examples
5. Test variations and iterate
6. Deploy as ad, email, or advertorial
