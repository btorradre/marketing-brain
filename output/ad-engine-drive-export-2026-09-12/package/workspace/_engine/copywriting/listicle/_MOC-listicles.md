## Listicle Types & Frameworks

### Type 1: The Benefits Listicle
**"5 Benefits of [Solution] and Why [Outcome] Happens"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 benefits → CTA
- Mechanism: Education + desire building
- Best for: Product benefits discovery

### Type 2: The Problem/Solution Listicle
**"5 Reasons Why [Problem] Happens and How [Solution] Fixes It"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 problems → unified solution CTA
- Mechanism: Pain amplification + single solution
- Best for: Positioning against competitors

### Type 3: The Warning Signs Listicle
**"5 Warning Signs You Have [Condition/Problem]"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 warning signs → diagnosis/solution CTA
- Mechanism: Identification + action
- Best for: Condition-based products

### Type 4: The Steps/Process Listicle
**"5 Steps to Achieve [Outcome]"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 steps → implementation CTA
- Mechanism: Clarity + process validation
- Best for: How-to products, education

### Type 5: The Myth-Busting Listicle
**"5 Myths About [Topic] (And What Actually Works)"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 myths + corrections → solution CTA
- Mechanism: Correction + authority
- Best for: Contrarian positioning

### Type 6: The Hidden/Secret Listicle
**"5 Things [Industry] Doesn't Want You To Know"**
- Structure: Intro → 5 secrets → exclusive solution CTA
- Mechanism: Revelation + insider knowledge
- Best for: Alternative solutions, disruption
