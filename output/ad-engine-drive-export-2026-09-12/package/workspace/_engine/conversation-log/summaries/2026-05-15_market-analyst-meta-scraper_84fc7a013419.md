## Insights & Learnings

- **Persona-doctor narrators are scaling in the gut-health niche.** Two of the four candidates use a named doctor character ("Dr. Quintavius Carter," "Dr. Marcus Thompson") as the long-form narrator — same pattern as Motilli's Dr. Rebecca Marsh. This is becoming a dominant pattern in the niche.
- **Niche-bending angles are showing up:** Dr. Quintavius Carter is using a diabetes/gut crossover with Black community targeting. Dr. Marcus Thompson is using a kids' dark-circles → gut connection — both bring gut health into a different awareness frame to escape the saturated mainstream gut-health audience.
- **Pet niche may be using the same long-form story structure.** "Dog Health Tips" opens with "First they eat grass. Then they lick their paws..." — classic cause-chain hook. Worth checking if this is transferable structure for the human gut-health avatar.
- **Dr. Quintavius Carter is running 8 active ads** — suggests this is a scaled long-form copy library worth deep-mining, not a one-off test.
- **Workflow constraint surfaced:** Chrome extension lost tab context twice in this session. The repeated "tab group disappeared" → "recreate and continue" cycle ate ~30% of session time. May need a more resilient capture pattern (export library IDs to a list first, then navigate one-at-a-time with explicit retries).
