# Segment GLP-1 customer avatars by pain points — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Category:** research
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-06_segment-glp1-avatars-pain-points_d54ec8f2]]

## What Happened
Built the complete Motilli sub-avatar research library from Reddit consumer intelligence. 250+ direct consumer quotes collected across 6 GLP-1 side effect pain points, organized into full avatar profiles with awareness-level messaging matrices. This is the consumer intelligence foundation for all Motilli ad copy.

## Key Decisions Made
- Segmented into 6 sub-avatars by primary pain point: Constipation, Diarrhea, Nausea, Sulfur Burps, Bloating, Hair Loss
- Each sub-avatar gets a full profile (pain themes, consumer phrases, daily friction maps, angle strategy) PLUS awareness-level-specific messaging
- The awareness-level messaging matrix covers all 5 Schwartz levels (Unaware → Most Aware) for each sub-avatar
- Cross-reference guide included for multi-symptom sufferers (many GLP-1 users experience multiple side effects)

## Insights & Learnings
- Reddit scraper hit monthly limit — web search agents proved an effective fallback for pulling Reddit data
- GLP-1 users frequently experience MULTIPLE side effects simultaneously — the cross-reference guide is essential for building ads that don't alienate multi-symptom sufferers
- Consumer language varies dramatically by pain point — constipation language is frustrated/desperate, sulfur burps language is embarrassed/isolating, hair loss language is anxious/identity-threatening

- 1 awareness-level messaging playbook (1,676 lines)
