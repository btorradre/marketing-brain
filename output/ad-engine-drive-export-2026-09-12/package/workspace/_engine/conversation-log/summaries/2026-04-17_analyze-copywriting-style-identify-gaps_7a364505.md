# Analyze copywriting style and identify gaps — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-17
**Category:** creative-production
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-17_analyze-copywriting-style-identify-gaps_7a364505]]

## What Happened
Side-by-side analysis of Nutrition Therapy "Bowel Support Drops" (competitor reference, UK menopause/IBS-C, daughter-as-neurogastroenterologist) and the user's adaptation for Motilli (US GLP-1 gut). Identified five mechanical reasons the competitor reads simpler/more conversational, diagnosed five bottlenecks in the user's version, then delivered a full ~1,800-word rewrite that collapses the mechanism to one analogy, swaps the lateral cautionary tale for generational stakes, and sweeps AI-voice patterns.

## Key Decisions Made
- Diagnosed Nutrition Therapy's "simpler" voice as the product of: short sentences at emotional beats / longer at reflection, more interior monologue, kitchen-table anchors, ONE committed analogy, and ~1.5-2x more conjunction openers per 1,000 words
- Identified that user's Motilli draft was stacking THREE competing images (highway, fermentation tank, "six feet upstream") and that "upstream" is river language, not highway language — a covert metaphor break
- Decided the rewrite would commit to ONE analogy (highway only) and ONE UMS criterion (apigenin/prokinetic at the on-ramp), dropping chlorophyllin and soluble fiber from the mechanism teach
- Replaced sister Diane (lateral cautionary tale) with the narrator's own mother (generational inheritance, housebound, recliner) — now closes the emotional circuit at the end
- Stripped two AI-pattern violations: the "No cleanouts / No fiber / No more" parallel stack and the "Stop following / Start supporting" parallel close

## Insights & Learnings
- **Voice "simplicity" is mechanical, not lexical.** Same vocabulary, different rhythm. The five mechanics are reproducible.
- **One-analogy discipline beats multi-analogy stacking even when each individual analogy is good.** The reader holds one image and you let it breathe.
- **The narrator-of-mother frame is structurally stronger than narrator-of-sister or narrator-of-friend** for this avatar. Generational stakes vs lateral cautionary. Existential vs transactional.
- **"That word stayed with me" beats** are a specific named technique — interior-monologue moments that let the reader metabolize teaching while the narrator carries a thought. Track this in voice references.
- **Authority escalation works:** peer narrator (Daughter) confirmed by named credentialed authority (gastroenterologist daughter Sarah / Claire) is a stable Motilli/Nutrition Therapy architecture pattern.
- **Stop/start parallel closes are blacklisted but ALSO present in scaling competitor copy** — flag when seen, judgment call on whether to retain for tonal match.
