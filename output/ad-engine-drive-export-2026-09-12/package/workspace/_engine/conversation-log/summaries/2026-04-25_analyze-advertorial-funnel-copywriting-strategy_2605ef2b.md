# Analyze advertorial funnel copywriting strategy — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Category:** strategy
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-25_analyze-advertorial-funnel-copywriting-strategy_2605ef2b]]

## What Happened
Brooks pushed back hard on a Lunessa advertorial teardown that leaned too heavily on cosmetic critique (em-dashes, bullets, italics), forcing a recalibration. The conversation then evolved into a structural debate: which advertorial pattern should Motilli use — EverNature's deep-narrative 5,000-word architecture or Lunessa's mechanism-first 2,000-word architecture? Final synthesis: build both and split-test, with the longer EverNature-style page favored as the higher-LTV play. A second debate around visual chunking (bullets, pull quotes) for skimmers ended with the diagnosis that chunking is real but third-priority behind missing conversion infrastructure and competitor disqualification.

## Key Decisions Made
- **Don't replace the EverNature-style 2,400-word Motilli advertorial.** Build a Lunessa-style 1,800-2,000-word version alongside it and split-test.
- **Track 60-day reorder and 90-day LTV, not just first-purchase conversion** when comparing the two advertorial patterns. Different funnel lengths produce different customer types.
- **Priority order for fixing the existing Motilli advertorial:** (1) add missing conversion infrastructure — price anchor, guarantee, timeline, FAQ; (2) add competitor disqualification beat; (3) apply visual chunking pass last.
- **Lunessa avatar lock recommendation:** Pick FH-or-strong-family-history-of-premature-cardiac-death and commit. Let general statin-intolerant audience self-recruit through pattern-match.
- **Push-back protocol confirmed:** Brooks called out auto-agreement twice. Norm reset — give unbiased answers, push back when warranted.

## Insights & Learnings

**Two functional advertorial patterns coexist in the supplement category:**
- **EverNature pattern (story-dominant):** 5,000+ words, deep interior narrative, mechanism nested inside emotional arc. Higher LTV, more invested buyers, retention-strong customers. Has scaled 300+ days.
- **Lunessa pattern (mechanism-dominant):** ~2,000 words, mechanism reframe is the spine, personal narrative is wrapper. Faster click-to-purchase but more transactional buyers, weaker retention.

**The "Statins = 1 of 3" mechanism reframe is doing 80% of Lunessa's conversion work.** Visual chunking is delivery vehicle, not conversion driver. Stripping bullets would drop conversion 5-10%, not 50%.

**The skimmer-vs-reader fallacy:** Visual chunking helps skimmers scan further before bouncing — it doesn't convert skimmers into buyers. Buyers are disproportionately full-readers who got emotionally invested. Optimizing chunking before adding conversion infrastructure is optimizing the wrong variable.

**Funnel length isn't a prescription, it's a constraint of testing maturity.** "Lunessa's funnel is shorter so Motilli's should be too" is wrong reasoning. Lunessa hasn't tested longer yet — that doesn't mean longer wouldn't win.

**The CoQ10 reveal is the structural anchor of Lunessa's emotional payload.** "That's not a side effect. That's the MECHANISM." Reframes statin side effects from "rare adverse reaction" to "predictable consequence of how the drug works." This single reframe is what justifies considering an alternative at all.

**Editorial-page conversion infrastructure to standardize across all advertorials:**
1. Price reveal on the page (anchored against failed treatment graveyard)
2. Money-back guarantee with frictionless return (e.g., "you don't have to send the bottles back")
3. Timeline expectation framework (week 4 / week 8 / week 12)
4. FAQ section catching post-close logistics objections
5. Voice-matched CTA ("Here's where I order it" not "CHECK AVAILABILITY")

## Creative Output
No new copy was produced this session — it was strategic. The output is a revised editorial-page architecture standard and a split-test mandate for Motilli (build both EverNature-style and Lunessa-style versions alongside each other).

## Action Items & Next Steps
- **Build Lunessa-style 1,800-2,000-word Motilli advertorial** alongside the existing 2,400-word EverNature-style version
- **Set up split-test infrastructure** that tracks first-purchase conversion + 60-day reorder + 90-day LTV
- **Update existing 2,400-word Motilli advertorial in priority order:**
  1. Add price anchor, 90-day guarantee, week-by-week timeline, 4-question FAQ
  2. Add competitor disqualification beat ("Most GLP-1 supplements on the shelf are...")
  3. Apply visual chunking pass (bullets for trust markers, pull quotes from testimonials, bolded week markers)
- **Update Lunessa advertorial:** Lock FH avatar, deepen the LDL-clearance mechanism (LDL receptor upregulation research), tighten testimonials/comments to feel less staged
- **Consider this conversation when building any future advertorial** — the priority order (mechanism > avatar lock > conversion infrastructure > visual chunking) is the new default

## Notable Quotes / Language

**On mechanism reframes:** "That's not a side effect. That's the MECHANISM." (Lunessa CoQ10 line — reframes statin side effects from bad luck into the cost of admission.)

**On competitor disqualification:** "$19.99. 600mg of red yeast rice with 50mg of CoQ10. That's not a clinical dose. That's marketing dust."

**On the close:** "My mother started taking Lunessa six months ago after 15 years on Lipitor. Last week, she asked me to go for a walk. I almost cried." (Emotional circuit close — resolves the family-history-as-genetic-sentence wound.)

**On pattern recognition:** "EverNature is story-dominant with the mechanism nested inside the narrator's emotional arc. Lunessa is mechanism-dominant with a peer-narrator wrapper." (Two coexisting patterns in the category.)

**On the priority misframe:** "If you're optimizing visual rhythm before you've added the missing conversion infrastructure, you're optimizing the wrong variable. The page without a price anchor or a guarantee is going to underperform regardless of how well-chunked it is."

**On funnel length:** "Lunessa's length is a constraint of where Lunessa is in its testing maturity, not a prescription for what Motilli should do."
