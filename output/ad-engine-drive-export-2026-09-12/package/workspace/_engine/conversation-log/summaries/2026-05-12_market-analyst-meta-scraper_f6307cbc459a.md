## What Happened
The Market Analyst executed its Tuesday menopause-rotation scrape on Meta Ad Library, pulling the keyword "menopause relief" (US/Active/Image, ~650 results). Two high-quality long-form ads were swiped and filed into the references vault, plus a full structural intel drop with mechanism and angle analysis. Two lower-quality candidates were intentionally skipped to stay under the 3-5 quality cap.

## Key Decisions Made

- Limited swipes to 2 ads after deeming them both 9/10 — adhered to "quality over quantity" rule
- Skipped NovaMane (hair growth disguised as menopause), Susan Henderson (neck pain), and Menopause And Me (joint pain crossover) as off-niche or lower priority
- Selected Simply Bloom (vaginal dryness device, Nancy Lem persona) and UsAgain (husband-POV book by James Mitchell, Darnell Brooks persona) as the two cataloged ads
- Closed Chrome cleanly at end of session

## Insights & Learnings

- **UsAgain is a category opener.** First menopause product seen in scrapes that sells to husbands rather than wives. Worth flagging as a structural pattern, not just a one-off ad.
- The "permanent decision in the middle of something temporary" frame (divorcing during the menopause window) is transferable across midlife product categories.
- Simply Bloom's "friction-is-the-enemy" mechanism could port to topical/joint products like Smoothspine.
- Meta Ad Library UX note: the "See ad details" button does NOT expand long-form copy; you must click the truncated "..." indicator inside the body itself. Worth caching in the scraper SOP.
