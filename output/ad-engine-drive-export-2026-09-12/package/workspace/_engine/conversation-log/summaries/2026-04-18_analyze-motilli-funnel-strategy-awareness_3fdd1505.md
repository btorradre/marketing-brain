## What Happened
Continued the Motilli funnel-architecture analysis from a prior session. Pulled live Meta data for the active campaign "motilli images" (`120245281077150653`, $53.07 day-1 spend, 16 active ads across 2 ad sets), scraped both landing-page destinations, ran the 5-dimension `funnel-analysis` congruence audit on both ad-to-page pairs, and appended Part 9 to the existing analysis document with the actionable diagnosis.

## Key Decisions Made
- Reframed the funnel from "failing" to "live-scaling with strong soft metrics" — CTR ~8.2% campaign-level (29-42% ad-set-level), CPC $0.24, elite for cold US supplement traffic.
- Decided the bridge-page opportunity lives on the **Daughter's side** specifically (1,599w emotional narrative → PDP), not the Nurse's side.
- Decided the Nurse → Advertorial path is structurally sound — only the "We Could Sell Out Tomorrow" scarcity close needs replacement.
- Flagged a priority-zero LPV tracking issue (11% rate — 54 link clicks → 6 LPVs) that must be audited before any new creative is built.

## Insights & Learnings
- Motilli is running two different funnel architectures simultaneously inside one campaign — Daughter direct-to-PDP and Nurse-to-advertorial. This means per-ad funnel routing matters more than a single-funnel decision.
- Credibility de-specification is a hidden temperature drop: "Sarah the gastroenterologist" (in ad) → "recommended by gastroenterologists" (on PDP) is a real congruence break even when the angle holds.
- A long emotional peer narrative (1,599 words) dumped onto a PDP loses the mechanism education the ad just built, because PDPs compress mechanism to bullets.
- The Nurse → Dr. Marsh path is a textbook credibility escalation (peer narrator → authority confirmation) and should be the template for the Daughter side too.
- Day-1 funnel data with strong soft metrics but weak conversion data is almost always a tracking issue, not a creative issue. Don't rebuild creative until tracking is verified.
