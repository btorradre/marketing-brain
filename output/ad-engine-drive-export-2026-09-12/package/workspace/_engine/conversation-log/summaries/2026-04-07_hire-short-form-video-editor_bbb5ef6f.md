# Hire short form video editor — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-07
**Category:** General Marketing / Hiring
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-07_hire-short-form-video-editor_bbb5ef6f]]

## What Happened
Created an Upwork job listing for a short form video editor to compile b-roll footage into scroll-stopping videos for the Velantra Boat Tote. The listing went through one revision to emphasize viral format replication and pattern interrupt skills.

## Key Decisions Made
- Pay range set at $10-$15/hr
- Volume: 10+ videos per month, ongoing
- Turnaround expectation: 48hr or less per batch
- Dual-purpose output: organic social AND paid ad creatives
- Platform targets: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Meta
- Application requirement: share examples showing specific viral format replication with reference to what was replicated

## Insights & Learnings
- The emphasis on "this week's trends, not last year's" signals how fast short-form pacing evolves — the editor needs to be actively consuming current content, not working from templates
- Pattern interrupts explicitly called out: jump cuts, zoom shifts, text slaps, audio switches, speed ramps — these are the specific retention mechanics the editor must master
- Frame-for-frame replication was the standard set, not just "inspired by" — this is a high bar that filters for editors who study viral mechanics deeply

## Creative Output
- Upwork job listing (delivered in chat, not saved to file)

## Action Items & Next Steps
- Post the listing to Upwork
- Review applicant portfolios — specifically look for side-by-side viral format recreations
- Once editor is onboarded, establish a content calendar and b-roll delivery pipeline
- Consider creating a reference swipe folder of viral formats for the editor to work from

## Notable Quotes / Language
- "Not just 'inspired by,' but frame-for-frame replication of what's working"
- "Know how to use pattern interrupts to hold attention past the 3-second mark"
- "Tell us which format you were referencing so we can see how closely you matched it"
