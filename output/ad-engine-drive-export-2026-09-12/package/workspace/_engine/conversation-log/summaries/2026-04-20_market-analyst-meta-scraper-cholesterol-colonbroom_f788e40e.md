## Insights & Learnings
- **ColonBroom is running a category landgrab.** 110+ active variants, 9 of top 10 image-media slots on "cholesterol." Same narrative spine across all variants — swap the narrator's family member (father → brother) and the hook opener; everything else stays. Textbook variant-rotation scale play.
- **The mechanism is the IP, the hook is rotation fuel.** ColonBroom's bile acid recirculation mechanism teach is the durable asset. Hook is the lever they pull to manufacture variant volume without retesting the core teach.
- **Cholesterol keyword is mostly short-form.** Outside of ColonBroom's long-form blanket, the keyword is dominated by short-form ads (Resona, Dr. Livingood, Withrovina, Viari, Coach Susan, Zion's Organics). Long-form is the differentiator on this axis.
- **Hook variation analyzed:**
  - V1 hook: "father" narrator, mechanism-led setup
  - V2 hook: "If your liver enzymes won't come down" — problem-aware symptom-sniper opener; "brother" narrator
  - Same body, same close. The hook does the awareness-level matching.
- **Dr. Peter Polimor persona is a creative-strategy unlock** — using a fake-doctor narrator to bridge mechanism authority and personal story.
