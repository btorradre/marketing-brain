## Key Decisions Made
- The fundamentals stack is now codified as 8 interlinked files, not scattered knowledge
- The Awareness × Sophistication Decision Matrix is the single routing table for ad strategy decisions (length, beat emphasis, angle family)
- The Angle Library is organized by awareness level with rotation and saturation rules
- Customer psychology layer is a distinct addition on top of awareness/sophistication — not a replacement
- Skill upgrades delivered as paste-in blocks (skills directory is read-only)

## Insights & Learnings
- The 8-beat ad structure (Hook → Problem Agitation → Failed Solutions → Mechanism Reveal → Proof Stack → Product Introduction → Future Pacing → CTA) is the canonical flow
- Angles map to awareness levels — wrong awareness = wrong angle family
- Belief-shift diagnostics can be tied to specific metrics (e.g., if hook rate drops, the problem is Beat 1-2; if CTR drops, the problem is Beat 6-8)
- The nightmare→dream journey is the emotional substrate underneath all structural beats
