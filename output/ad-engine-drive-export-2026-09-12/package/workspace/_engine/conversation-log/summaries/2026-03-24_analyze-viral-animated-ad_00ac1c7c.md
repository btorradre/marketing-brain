# Analyze viral animated ad for recreation — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-24

## What Happened
Analyzed a viral animated e-commerce ad trend using Gemini's multimodal API for frame-by-frame video analysis. Then created a full animated ad storyboard for Motilli, reimagining the format with the GLP-1 injection itself as an anthropomorphic villain character. Delivered as a professional .docx storyboard document.

## Key Decisions Made
- Used Gemini API directly (via the API key from the .env file) for video analysis since no Gemini MCP server was available
- The GLP-1 shot itself was cast as the villain — a cocky anthropomorphic syringe with sunglasses and a gold chain who brags about weight loss while being oblivious to the digestive damage
- 4-act structure: Hook (shot arrives with swagger) → Damage (shot is present during every suffering scene) → Motilli Enters (shot panics when it sees celery extract) → CTA
- The shot accidentally reveals WHY Motilli works by panicking about specific ingredients — turning villain dismissiveness into mechanism education

## Insights & Learnings
- Animated villain-as-educator is a novel format — the villain's panic reactions serve as mechanism education (it names the ingredients and explains why they threaten its damage)
- The "upstream vs. downstream" mechanism distinction was delivered through the villain's dismissiveness: "Laxatives? Cute. I didn't break your colon. I broke your STOMACH."
- The format works because it's entertaining while educating — viewers stay for the character, absorb the mechanism
