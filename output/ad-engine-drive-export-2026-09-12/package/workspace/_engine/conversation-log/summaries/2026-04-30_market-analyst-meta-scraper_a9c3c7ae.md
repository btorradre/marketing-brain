## Insights & Learnings
- **Inverse-hook discovery:** MyKalm's hook structure inverts the conventional problem-first opener — instead of "I had a problem," it opens with "my numbers improved too fast and the doctor ordered emergency tests." The shock comes from the measurement going too far in the right direction. Highly transferable across cholesterol, A1c, BP, and weight categories.
- **Alevia angle stack:** Alevia is now running cholesterol AND osteoarthritis/orthopedic-nurse angles, indicating a multi-condition rollout strategy worth tracking.
- **Back-pain Meta gap confirmed (second time):** Back-pain supplement narrative format remains absent from Meta — opportunity gap holds.
- **Persona archetype evolution:** "Nurse-narrator with insider mechanism explanation" (Claire / Julie Anderson at Alevia, cardiac nurse at MyKalm) is now the dominant guide-character template across two unrelated brands.
