## What Happened
Brooks wants to scale Velantra's content pipeline by hiring two VAs via Upwork: one for AI b-roll generation (using Kling/ChatGPT/Gemini from TikTok reference clips) and one for short-form video editing (POV-style Reels with ElevenLabs voiceovers). Session produced two complete Upwork job posts with screening questions, pay ranges, and paid test structures.

## Key Decisions Made
- Two separate hires, not one — AI generation and video editing are different skill sets
- B-roll VA: $5-8/hr, 15-25 hrs/week, 100 clips per colorway (all boat tote colors)
- Video editor: $8-15/hr, 10-20 hrs/week, POV Reels for FB/IG
- Content library will live in Google Drive
- Color accuracy is a core QC criterion across all colorways
- Paid test: 5 clips across 2 colorways for b-roll VA
- ElevenLabs voiceover is the audio standard for short-form

## Insights & Learnings
- The content pipeline is: TikTok reference → SnapTik download → AI generation (Kling multi-shot) → QC for artifacts → Google Drive → video editor → POV Reels with voiceover
- Target demo for all content: older women
- Scaling across colorways means matched scene sets — same compositions, different product colors
- Brooks is building toward volume testing: many variations per concept
