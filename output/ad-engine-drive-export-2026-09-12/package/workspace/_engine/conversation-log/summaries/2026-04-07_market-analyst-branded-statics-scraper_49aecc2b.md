## What Happened
Automated branded statics scrape targeting Primal Viking, GleeFull, and Primal Queen. Primal Viking showed a major creative refresh (Apr 5-6), GleeFull had no active FB presence, and Primal Queen runs video-only (zero image ads despite 153K followers). Chrome crashed multiple times against the Meta Ad Library.

## Key Decisions Made
- Documented Primal Viking's creative refresh as a significant competitive signal
- Flagged the AI-generated Viking warrior illustration as a new template format worth replicating
- Confirmed GleeFull and Primal Queen aren't competing in the branded statics space

## Insights & Learnings
- **Primal Viking creative refresh Apr 5-6:** Killed ~12 ads and relaunched with new IDs — this suggests either a creative fatigue response or a strategic pivot. The timing (weekend refresh) is notable.
- **AI-generated mythology illustration** is a new format for Primal Viking — a single Viking warrior illustration is their only true branded static. This format is worth templating: mythology/archetype imagery generated by AI could translate to goddess/botanical imagery for women's health brands.
- **Co-branded influencer pages:** Primal Viking is now running separate FB pages per celebrity partnership — a persona-page scaling strategy similar to what Sculptique does with doctor personas.
- **Discount escalation:** Primal Viking increased from 50% to 60% off — potential sign of conversion pressure or clearing inventory.
- **Primal Queen's video-only strategy** with 153K followers is a deliberate choice — they've decided statics aren't worth the creative investment for their brand.
- **Image extraction still blocked:** Meta Ad Library security prevents all automated image download methods. Manual screenshots remain the only option.
