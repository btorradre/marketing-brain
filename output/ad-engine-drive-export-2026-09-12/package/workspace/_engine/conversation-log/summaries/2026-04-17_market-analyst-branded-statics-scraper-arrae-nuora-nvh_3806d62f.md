## What Happened
Friday rotation across three brands. Arrae and Nuora are running 100% creator-UGC video — no polished branded statics. North Valley Health Clinic is running 5 distinct static ad variations, but in a deliberately "anti-archetype" un-designed style (no typography, no pricing, no product shots). No image files saved to brand folders — sandbox continues to block fbcdn right-click-save. Catalogs updated for all three brands; intel drop filed.

## Key Decisions Made
- Decided NOT to categorize North Valley's statics against the 8-archetype system as clean matches. The "medical lab report" visual is the only one that cleanly maps (clinical-proof archetype). The others are narrative-story statics that don't fit the existing archetypes.
- Proposed a 9th archetype: **"Anti-archetype editorial still"** — scene-based, no brand design language, looks like a news photo.
- Flagged our creative matrix as potentially over-indexed on polished statics.

## Insights & Learnings
- **UGC-video dominance** — two of three Friday brands (feminine wellness + menopause) run zero polished statics. If the category is moving to 100% UGC video, polished-static creative may have lower share-of-voice than we assumed.
- **Anti-archetype visual strategy** — North Valley Health's un-designed scene photos work specifically BECAUSE they don't look like ads. In a feed full of polished statics, an un-designed scene photo is the pattern interrupt.
- **"Circled lab report" visual** — red marker + gloved hand + lab printout is a minimal-production visual that carries high trust signal. Directly adaptable for Lunessa (sleep lab panel) and Motilli (gut biome panel).
- **Gethookd extension pollution** — the browser extension is injecting "Spy Brand" overlays and triggering unexpected popup tabs during Ad Library sessions. Operational risk; not a blocker yet but worth isolating the scraper to a clean Chrome profile.
