## What Happened
Keyword-driven Meta Ad Library scrape on "joint pain relief" and "knee pain supplement." Discovered a new brand — **Joint Health Daily (Kyntra Twist)** — running 16 long-form narrative ads for a flexible resistance-bar device. Extracted 5 unique ad angles in full copy, filed them to the references folder, and wrote a structural intel drop.

## Key Decisions Made
- Added **Joint Health Daily / Kyntra** to the tracked brand roster.
- Used JavaScript DOM extraction rather than page screenshots to pull full untruncated ad copy from Meta Ad Library spans.
- Filed 5 ads spanning 4 distinct avatars (pickleball player, elbow sufferer, carpal-tunnel/hand therapist, women 65+ grip independence, pickleball recovery).
- Skipped already-tracked brands (Alevia / Jessica Bennett) and filtered out product-forward/benefit-list creative (Vahdam USA turmeric).

## Insights & Learnings
- **Long-form native is migrating beyond supplements into physical products.** Kyntra is a device brand running the same editorial narrative structure as supplement long-form — a meaningful format expansion worth flagging.
- The **"itis vs osis" mechanism** from Ad #02 is the most templatable mechanism pattern in the batch. Any niche where the standard medical label causes wrong treatment can steal this one-letter-changes-everything structure.
- The **women 65+ grip-independence angle** (Ad #04) is hitting a massive, underserved demographic with best-in-class emotional architecture. Worth studying for any aging-audience mechanism.
- Meta Ad Library UI consistently truncates body copy — JS span extraction is the reliable capture method going forward.
