## Insights & Learnings
- **"It Was Never Genetic" reframe (Clarae):** Reusable belief-shift architecture for any condition the avatar has internalized as immutable (genetic, age-related, hormonal). Worth cataloging as a structural pattern.
- **Dual-track sister-watching-sister architecture (Peak Form):** Narrator describes another person's transformation in parallel with their own — gives the ad permission to run two case studies inside one story without breaking voice.
- **GLP-1 adjacent supplement category is accelerating:** Vixion's "protect your hair from day one of GLP-1" angle is a leading indicator. Expect a flood of "protect your X from day one" hooks across hair, skin, muscle, and sleep over the next ~60 days. Worth a defensive-positioning play if Velantra/Motilli/Lunessa have any GLP-1 adjacency.
- **Moerie's "expensive pee" angle:** Reframes competitor failure (other supplements pass through unabsorbed) into a vivid concrete villain. Smart-Release Beadlet mechanism gives the product a defensible "why now" for a category that's been around forever.
