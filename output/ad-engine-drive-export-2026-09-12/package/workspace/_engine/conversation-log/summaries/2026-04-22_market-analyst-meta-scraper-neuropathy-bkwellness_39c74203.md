**MAJOR DISCOVERY: Addiction-niche crossover with DR supplement copy.** SK Wellness is selling to **husbands of alcoholic wives**, using a brain-chemistry mechanism (dopamine receptor degeneration + BDNF). 6 concurrent variants launched same day = aggressive testing budget.
