## 4. Mechanism

**The "why" — why does this solution work?**

A mechanism is the structural explanation of how the problem gets solved. It's not what the product does; it's HOW and WHY it works.

### Problem Identification
What is actually broken/causing the pain point?

[Specific, structural description of the root cause]

*Example: "Chronic fatigue in high-performing women is caused by three simultaneous metabolic dysfunctions: (1) energy substrate depletion from sustained stress + inadequate nutritional support, (2) circadian rhythm desynchronization from erratic schedules and work patterns, and (3) dopamine system depletion leading to motivation collapse."*

### The Broken System
What system is malfunctioning?

[Name and describe the system]

*Example: "The energy production and maintenance system: the body's ability to generate and sustain ATP (cellular energy) across the day's demands."*

### The Solution/Reset
What specifically fixes the broken system?

[Describe the fix in structural, specific terms — not product features, but the mechanism itself]

*Example: "A three-phase reset: (1) Energy substrate replenishment through targeted nutrition, (2) Circadian re-anchoring through consistent sleep timing, (3) Neurochemical rebalancing, specifically dopamine system repair through [specific mechanism]. All three phases are required for lasting recovery. A partial reset (e.g., just sleep optimization) doesn't address all three components, so fatigue returns."*

### Role of the Product (or Solution)
How does [Product] or [Solution] fit into this mechanism?

[Describe specifically where in the mechanism the product works]

*Example: "Lunessa specifically targets the energy substrate + neurochemical components (phases 1 and 3) through [specific compounds and dosing]. It does NOT address circadian re-anchoring (that's sleep hygiene), but combined with consistent sleep timing, Lunessa creates the comprehensive reset required for recovery."*

### Complete Mechanism Statement
Write a 4-5 sentence mechanism explanation:

[Your complete mechanism statement]

This statement will guide your writing. Every claim in your copy should trace back to this mechanism.
