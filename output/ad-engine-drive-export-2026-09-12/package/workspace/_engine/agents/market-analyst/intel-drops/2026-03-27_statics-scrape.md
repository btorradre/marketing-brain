## Design Trends Spotted

1. **Warm neutral palettes dominate** — both Arrae and Nuora use cream/beige backgrounds with earth-tone accents rather than clinical whites. Positions supplements as self-care, not medicine.

2. **Serif + sans typography pairing** — both brands use elegant serif headlines paired with clean sans-serif body text. This is the 2025-2026 template for "premium supplement."

3. **Clinical credibility as differentiator** — Nuora leans hard into peer-reviewed research and doctor endorsements. In the supplement space (30% Meta ad rejection rate), brands that lead with clinical proof are gaining edge.

4. **Single images still dominate** — per the Evolut Agency 500-ad analysis, 43.4% of top supplement ads are single static images. Demonstration-style (42.2%) and value-driven (17.4%) are the top creative archetypes.

5. **Novel format experimentation** — Arrae's phone call interface static shows that format innovation (making statics feel interactive) drives engagement. Worth testing for other brands.
