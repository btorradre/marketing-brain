## Key Observations & Trends

1. **Major supplement brands are NOT running branded statics from their official FB pages.** All three Thursday brands (AG1, GOLO, Provitalize) had zero active image ads from their verified brand pages. This is a significant finding.

2. **Native/UGC dominates the supplement ad landscape.** The keyword searches consistently returned ads from individual-named pages (e.g., "Amanda Thompson," "Beatrice Doris," "Growth & Wellness Guide") running long-form native copy with lifestyle images. This suggests these brands may be using network-style advertising through pseudonymous pages rather than branded statics.

3. **The "branded static" archetype may be declining** in the health/supplement vertical on Meta. Brands appear to be investing in:
   - Video ads (which are filtered out by the image-only search)
   - Native/UGC-style image ads run from personal-looking pages
   - Carousel ads (which may not surface in single-image searches)

4. **Ad Library search limitations:** The Meta Ad Library's keyword search only matches ad copy text, not brand names or advertiser pages. Finding brand-specific ads requires the exact Facebook Page ID, and many DTC supplement brands run ads through multiple or unlisted pages.
