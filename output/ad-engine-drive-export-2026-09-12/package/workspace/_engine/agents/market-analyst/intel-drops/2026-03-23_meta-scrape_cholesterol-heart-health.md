## Synthesis & Recommendations

### Angles Worth Testing
- **"Perfect compliance, still died" (from Cholesterol Support Group):** This is the strongest new angle spotted today. The idea that following every medical instruction perfectly still leads to catastrophic failure is a Level 3 sophistication move. Could be adapted for any condition where medication manages symptoms but doesn't address root cause. Works for: blood pressure, diabetes, joint pain, neuropathy.
- **"Wine drinker's belly" (from Dr. Peter Polimor):** Hyper-specific avatar targeting. Instead of "women over 50 with belly fat," this targets "women who drink wine socially and noticed their belly won't go down." The specificity of the behavior + consequence chain is worth testing for any of our product lines where the audience has a guilty pleasure habit contributing to their condition.
- **"Dual-character retired nurse testimonial" (from Tryourplus-H):** The two-person format is unusual and creates built-in social proof (they validate each other). The international authority positioning (Irish nurses from St. James's Hospital) adds credibility without needing a doctor persona.

### Mechanism Innovations Spotted
- **Statin proxy metric failure:** Cholesterol Support Group's core mechanism — LDL is a proxy metric that doesn't measure actual cardiovascular risk — is a sophisticated reframe. Most anti-statin ads argue "statins have side effects." This one argues "statins succeed at their stated goal AND you still die." Much harder to counter.
- **Medication cascade as narrative engine:** Blood Pressure Secrets uses the switch from Lisinopril → Amlodipine → [likely more switches] as a narrative pacing device. Each switch is a micro-arc of hope → failure that builds cumulative reader frustration. This is a structural innovation in pacing.

### Market Signals
- **Blood pressure/cholesterol is EXTREMELY active right now.** The "cholesterol supplement" search returned ~3,200 active results. Blood Pressure Secrets alone has ~410 active ads. This niche is getting crowded fast.
- **Nattokinase is the trending ingredient.** Multiple brands (Dr. Kellerman, Natural Foundation, Healthletic) are all running nattokinase angles. This ingredient is hitting mainstream awareness.
- **Beetroot is the Blood Pressure Secrets play.** Rosabella is positioning beetroot as the natural blood pressure alternative. This is a less crowded ingredient space than nattokinase.
- **Freshness signal:** Most ads found were launched March 20-23, 2026 (0-3 days old). This suggests a wave of new launches in the cholesterol/heart health space, possibly triggered by a seasonal spending cycle or a response to a competitor's success.
- **Patch delivery format emerging:** Tryourplus-H is using a transdermal patch delivery mechanism for their supplement. This is unusual in the supplement space and could represent a differentiation play.
