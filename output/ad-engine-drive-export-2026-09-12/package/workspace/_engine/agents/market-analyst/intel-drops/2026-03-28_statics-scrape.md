1. **Dark-background product hero shots** continue to dominate men's supplement space (Force Factor). Premium positioning through contrast and isolation.

3. **Editorial/native framing** remains dominant in the 55+ senior health market (NativePath) — "your doctor won't tell you" and "health expert urges" patterns.
4. **A/B testing at scale** — both brands running multiple versions of same creative simultaneously with text variations.
