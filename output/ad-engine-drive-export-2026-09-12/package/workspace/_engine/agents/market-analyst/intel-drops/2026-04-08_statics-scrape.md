## Brands Targeted (Wed Rotation)

### 1. Seed (Seed Health / DS-01 Synbiotic)

**Ad Spend Level:** Seven figures/month on Meta — one of the heaviest supplement spenders on the platform.

**Creative Strategy Intel:**

- Typography: Sans-serif, scientific/modern feel

- Vibe: "Science-first wellness" — distinctly not influencer/UGC energy
- Common archetypes: Ingredient-benefit callouts, clinical proof, bold claim + pricing

**Template Potential:** HIGH — Seed's clean, scientific aesthetic maps well to Lunessa (clinical authority) and Velantra (premium positioning).

## Design Trends Spotted (Cross-Brand)

1. **Static images still dominate conversions** — 60-70% of Meta conversions come from statics, not video. Don't over-index on video.
2. **Fewer, more differentiated creatives win** — Seed's shift to ASC testing with less volume but more creative diversity is a signal.
3. **Clean > cluttered** — Both Seed and Bloom favor clean, product-forward layouts over busy designs.
4. **Authenticity positioning** — Bloom explicitly positions against "overproduced" creative. The vibe should feel real, not agency-polished.
5. **Testimonial cards remain a workhorse** — Onnit's Rogan quotes and Bloom's review-based statics continue to perform.
