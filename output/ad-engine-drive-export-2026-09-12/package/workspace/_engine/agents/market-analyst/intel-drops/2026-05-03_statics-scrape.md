Continuing the convention from prior intel drops: image binaries were not saved to disk this run. Same constraints as before — Meta CDN URLs are blocked from JS, save_to_disk on zoom screenshots stores in Chrome extension's local store rather than the workspace. ### 1. PRIMAL QUEEN — STRATEGIC PIVOT FROM BRANDED STATIC TO NATIVE UGC

The single most significant finding of this scrape. Primal Queen ran 2 polished pink/magenta product-lineup statics in April. Today's scrape shows ~85 active image ads — and **none of them use the April design DNA**.

The new format is native UGC: phone-shot couple selfies, gym mirror selfies, candid wedding-dress photos, at-home portraits. No graphic overlays, no brand-color enforcement, no typography. ### 2. PRIMAL VIKING — NEW "BREAKING NEWS" CLINICAL TEMPLATE FAMILY

Ad count continues to compress (~35 → ~23 → ~16 over March/April/May). Brand is consolidating around its winners. The middle-tier of designed bold-claim text-overlay statics (Mark Henry, "Testo Reversal in 30 Seconds") is gone — replaced at the high end by a new editorial/clinical archetype.

**The BREAKING NEWS template:**
1. White or light background
2. Circular cropped microscope/tissue/cellular photograph (any color — pink for fatty-liver, B&W for hair, green for moss/bacteria)
3. Red horizontal bar overlay reading "BREAKING NEWS" in white sans-serif
4. Black headline block with white sans-serif text — declarative, news-style register
5. Optional small body text in lighter weight underneath

Three Library IDs running this template: 2321360358332057 (fatty liver), 938829111545692 (testosterone restore), and a related green/teal variant.

A second editorial template is also new: Library 896220413400170 — black-and-white SEM (scanning electron microscope) close-up of hair follicle damage with red-circle highlight markers. "Men rarely talk about how hair loss affects them. Clinicians are revisiting ancestral nutrition." Quality 8/10. SEM imagery is novel for the supplement category.

Brand has barbelled — either highly-designed editorial statics OR raw UGC lifestyle photos with text-overlay urgency badges (LOW STOCK, ⚠️ SELLING FAST). Nothing in between.

## Cross-Brand Design Trends Spotted

### 1. The "BREAKING NEWS" microscope-photo template family is jumping brands

Primal Viking's Library 2321360358332057 (Feb 12) and Primal Queen's Library 2479754395760248 (Feb 20) appear to use the **same template chassis** — same launch month, same red bar + black headline block construction, same circular microscope crop. Different headlines, different brand colors, but the layout is shared.

**Implication:** Either (a) same design vendor running both brands, (b) one brand white-labeled the winning creative from the other, or (c) third-party template seller distributing to both. Worth flagging — if this template is truly cross-brand it's a high-confidence template to lift for our own brands.

### 2. Pulled-quote testimonial cards have converged across the category

AG1's "Make AG1 Your Morning Routine" pulled quote, Provitalize's testimonial cards, and now GleeFull's "MY OLD JEANS FIT AGAIN" all use variations of the same architecture: studio background + bold serif/sans-serif testimonial headline + product photo. Three of the five top performers in this scrape's brand list are running this format.

### 3. The "Personal-Story Caption + UGC Photo" funnel architecture is becoming category-default for women's health

Primal Queen's pivot is the most dramatic example, but it joins:
- North Valley Health Clinic (decoupled-image strategy since March)
- Provitalize (Lucy Chapman page running the "I almost SLAPPED Dr Smith" personal story)
- GleeFull (Jaimemichelle / Megan co-branded influencer pages)

The polished branded static is no longer the category default for women's supplements. It's been replaced by long-form personal-story primary text + UGC casting decisions for the visual.

**For our brands:** This is a real strategic question. Lunessa specifically — does it lean into the polished "MY OLD JEANS FIT AGAIN" pulled-quote category convergence (high-confidence, follow the herd), or does it counter-position with bolder branded statics where most competitors have abandoned that lane (potential differentiation, higher production cost)?

### 4. Anti-GLP-1 / Ozempic positioning is still an accelerating creative theme

Primal Queen's PQ7 launch is the most explicit yet — direct substitution claim ("GLP-1 Support From Mother Nature, Not a Lab"). Joins Provitalize's anti-Tirzepatide rebound creative documented April 30. The window for category-leadership on this angle is narrowing — many brands now claiming this lane.
