## Design Trends Spotted

### 1. Anti-Pharmaceutical Competitor Positioning is DOMINANT
Neurosmile's entire creative strategy is built around running AGAINST gabapentin — not just selling their own benefits. This "competitor comparison chart" archetype (YES/NO checkmark tables) is the highest-quality template pattern found today. GLP-1 SOS does the same against "harsh laxatives."

**Implication for our brands:** Lunessa could run against statins using this exact template. The comparison chart with checkmarks is visually scannable and immediately communicates superiority.

### 2. Dark/Moody Product Photography is Rising
Auri Nutrition and Primal Viking both use dark backgrounds with dramatic product lighting. This premium aesthetic is replacing the clean white-background product shots that dominated 2024-2025. The dark moody look signals premium positioning.

**Implication for our brands:** Consider dark-background product hero shots for Lunessa and Motilli to signal premium positioning.

### 3. Massive Fresh Launches (100+ Creatives at Once)
Auri launched ~120 statics on a single day (Mar 28). Primal Viking has ~590 active. These brands are running high-volume creative testing at scale — dozens of variants of each archetype to find winners quickly.

**Implication for our brands:** We should be prepared to produce 20-30+ static variants per archetype for proper split testing, not just 3-5.

### 4. "After X Days" Timeline Ads
Auri's "After 5 Days" progressive timeline format is a fresh archetype not widely used yet. Shows daily improvement expectations — sets realistic anchors while building belief.

**Implication for our brands:** Template this for Lunessa ("After 7 Days... After 30 Days... After 90 Days" cholesterol improvement timeline).

### 5. Visual Metaphor Bold Claims
Primal Viking's "YOUR TESTOSTERONE IS LOCKED UP" with chain imagery is the most visually provocative ad found today. The physical metaphor (chains = blocked hormone) creates instant emotional response.

**Implication for our brands:** Develop visual metaphors for our mechanisms. For Lunessa: "Your arteries are being CHOKED" with constriction imagery. For Motilli: "Your gut is FIGHTING you" with battle imagery.

### 6. Reframe Headlines Gaining Traction
"You're NOT Fat. You're Inflamed from High Cortisol" (Primal Viking) is a powerful belief-shift headline format. It reframes the reader's self-perception in one line.

**Implication for our brands:** "You're NOT getting old. Your cholesterol is SUFFOCATING your arteries." / "It's NOT your diet. Your gut bacteria are HIJACKING your cravings."

## Top Template Recommendations

### Priority 1: Competitor Comparison Chart
**Source:** Neurosmile
**Template:** Two-column table with product vs. competitor. Checkmark YES (green) vs X NO (red) for 4-5 benefit rows.
**Adapt for:** Lunessa vs Statins, Motilli vs Probiotics That Don't Work

### Priority 2: Bold Claim with Timeframe
**Source:** Neurosmile ("ELIMINATE IT IN 14 DAYS")
**Template:** Gradient background, bold white all-caps headline with specific day count, product below.
**Adapt for:** All brands. "LOWER YOUR LDL IN 30 DAYS" / "END BLOATING IN 7 DAYS"

### Priority 3: Dark-Background Testimonial Card
**Source:** Auri Nutrition
**Template:** Dark/branded background, gold star ratings, customer quote in large text, product shown.
**Adapt for:** All brands. Premium feel, dense social proof.

### Priority 4: "After X Days" Timeline
**Source:** Auri Nutrition
**Template:** Day-by-day improvement progression. Day 1... Day 3... Day 7... Day 30.
**Adapt for:** Lunessa (cholesterol improvement timeline), Motilli (gut reset timeline)

### Priority 5: Visual Metaphor Bold Claim
**Source:** Primal Viking
**Template:** Dark background, physical metaphor imagery (chains, locks, fire), ALL CAPS provocation.
**Adapt for:** Lunessa ("YOUR ARTERIES ARE BEING CHOKED"), Motilli ("YOUR GUT IS AT WAR")
