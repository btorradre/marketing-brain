## Synthesis & Recommendations

### Angles Worth Testing
- **"Cardiologist delivered bad news" first-person patient narrative** (Rob Moore style): This is a well-executed variant of the authority-disruption hook. The key innovation is putting the reader IN the doctor's office receiving the news, not just hearing about a mechanism. Worth testing for any cardiovascular/cholesterol product.
- **"Your supplement's delivery method is the problem, not the ingredient"** (PURA Sculpt style): The bioavailability/absorption angle is gaining traction. Sublingual vs. capsule is a clean mechanism that's easy to visualize and understand. Could be adapted for any supplement category.

### Mechanism Innovations Spotted
- **Sublingual absorption reframe:** PURA Sculpt is positioning "sublingual liquid" as the mechanism of differentiation — not a new ingredient, but a new delivery system. The "your stomach acid destroys 55% → liver filters 35% → only 10-15% reaches blood" chain is a compelling visual mechanism.

### Market Signals
- **Sculptique scaling hard with persona pages:** ~240+ active ads from one persona page alone, multiple persona pages running simultaneously. This is by far the most aggressive persona-page strategy observed in this niche.
- **April 2026 launch cluster:** Multiple new brands/ads launching in the first week of April — Rob Moore, Evoyra, PURA Sculpt, BetterHealth all launched April 5-6. Could signal a post-Q1 ad spend ramp.
- **Cholesterol niche still active:** ~3,400 active results for "cholesterol supplement" indicates strong competition and ongoing spend in this category.
