# SOP: Market Sophistication Tracking & Schwartz Stage Assessment

## Objective
Track how market sophistication evolves over time using the Schwartz 5-stage awareness model. Identify when avatars migrate between stages (e.g., Problem Aware → Solution Aware but Dissatisfied → Ready for Transformation). Update [[DR-SOP-Obsidian.md|Schwartz stages]] quarterly to inform angle and mechanism strategy adjustments.

---

## The 5 Schwartz Awareness Stages

Reference: [[DR-SOP-Obsidian.md|Schwartz stages in master SOP]]

### Stage 1: Unaware
**Avatar Status:** Doesn't know they have the problem yet.

**Characteristics:**
- No health concern on their radar
- Not searching for solutions
- May have symptoms but attributes them to something else
- Example: Person with early-stage neuropathy who thinks foot numbness is "just getting older"

**Marketing Implication:** Rarely appears in Reddit communities (they're not seeking solutions yet). Appears in cold audiences. Angles must first create awareness ("You might have this problem").

**Example Ad Angle:** "The 3 symptoms doctors miss that show you're developing [condition]"

### Stage 2: Problem Aware
**Avatar Status:** Knows they have the problem; seeking understanding.

**Characteristics:**
- Diagnosed or self-aware of symptoms
- Actively researching: "What is this? What causes it? How serious?"
- Not yet aware of solutions, or exploring initial options
- Likely active in Reddit communities posting initial questions
- High receptiveness to education

**Marketing Implication:** Moderate audience (some avatars in each niche stay here). Angles must educate about the problem ("Here's what's causing this").

**Example Ad Angle:** "Why your doctor can't diagnose the root cause of your [condition]"

### Stage 3: Solution Aware, Dissatisfied
**Avatar Status:** Knows about standard solutions but they're failing.

**Characteristics:**
- Has tried recommended treatments (medication, PT, etc.)
- Frustrated: solutions aren't working or have side effects
- Looking for alternatives but skeptical
- Likely active in Reddit with problem statements ("Nothing works")
- Feeling hopeless ("I've tried everything")
- Ready to question doctor's advice

**Marketing Implication:** Largest audience in pain/health niches. Highest emotional engagement (frustration = motivation). Angles must articulate the failure ("Why solutions fail").

**Example Ad Angle:** "Why gabapentin stops working after 2 years (and what actually helps)"

### Stage 4: Ready to Change, Cautiously Optimistic
**Avatar Status:** Open to alternatives; cautiously trying new approaches.

**Characteristics:**
- Aware that other solutions exist
- Willing to try different mechanisms or approaches
- Skeptical but hopeful ("Maybe this could work?")
- Wants proof or mechanism explanation before committing
- Worried about wasting time/money on another failed solution
- Higher sophistication: understands some health/science concepts

**Marketing Implication:** Moderate-to-large audience (Stage 3 avatars who've reached breaking point). Angles must offer "different approach" ("Here's why THIS mechanism works").

**Example Ad Angle:** "How nerve regeneration actually works (and why your current approach ignores it)"

### Stage 5: Transformation Ready, High Agency
**Avatar Status:** Taking active control; willing to experiment; ready for significant change.

**Characteristics:**
- Self-directed: not waiting for doctor permission
- Willing to adopt new routines, supplement stacks, lifestyle changes
- High health literacy or autodidact researcher
- Motivated by transformation, not just symptom relief
- Asks "What's possible?" not "Will this help my pain?"
- Trust in their own research over doctor authority
- Likely to become advocates/testimonials

**Marketing Implication:** Smaller but high-value audience. Highest LTV (lifetime value). Angles must promise transformation ("Here's what's actually possible").

**Example Ad Angle:** "From numb feet to pain-free hiking: The nerve regeneration protocol doctors won't teach you"

## Stage Assessment Methodology

### Method 1: Reddit Scrape Analysis

**From [[reddit-scraping.md|Reddit scraping SOP]]**, assess Schwartz stage by analyzing:

1. **Question Type**
   - Stage 1-2: "What is this?" / "Should I see a doctor?" → Problem awareness questions
   - Stage 3: "Why didn't this work?" / "Has anyone else failed with [treatment]?" → Solution failure questions
   - Stage 4-5: "What else can I try?" / "How does [mechanism] work?" → Alternative exploration questions

2. **Emotional Language Intensity**
   - Stage 1-2: Confused, curious, mild concern
   - Stage 3: Frustrated, hopeless, angry at system, desperate
   - Stage 4-5: Determined, hopeful, self-directed, experimental

3. **Mentioned Solutions**
   - Stage 1-2: No solutions mentioned; asking what to do
   - Stage 3: Multiple failed solutions mentioned (meds, PT, docs that didn't help)
   - Stage 4-5: Actively researching/trying new solutions; discussing mechanisms

4. **Authority Relationship**
   - Stage 1-3: Trusts doctor but frustrated with results
   - Stage 4: Questions doctor but still respects authority
   - Stage 5: Self-reliant; doctor is consultant, not authority

### Method 2: Competitor Ad Analysis

**From [[competitor-ad-monitoring.md|competitor monitoring SOP]]**, assess stage by analyzing:

1. **What Problem is Ads Addressing?**
   - Ads targeting Stage 2: Focus on problem education ("What causes this?")
   - Ads targeting Stage 3: Focus on solution failure ("Why medication fails")
   - Ads targeting Stage 4-5: Focus on alternative mechanisms ("How this mechanism works")

2. **How Many Competitors Target Each Stage?**
   - If 70% of competitor ads address Stage 3, market is dominated by frustrated avatars
   - If Stage 5 ads rare, opportunity to target change-ready avatars (blue ocean)

3. **What Belief Shifts are Ads Trying to Create?**
   - Stage 3 shift: "Standard solutions aren't the answer"
   - Stage 4 shift: "This alternative mechanism exists and works"
   - Stage 5 shift: "Transformation is possible if I take control"

### Method 3: Quarterly Avatar Survey (Implicit)

**From sales data, testimonials, customer service interactions**, assess:

1. **Customer Questions at Onboarding**
   - If mostly asking "How does this work?", you're attracting Stage 4-5
   - If mostly asking "Will this help my pain?", you're attracting Stage 3
   - If mostly asking "What is this?", you're attracting Stage 2

2. **Customer Testimonials & Results**
   - Stage 3 testimonials: "Finally something that works" (relief focus)
   - Stage 4-5 testimonials: "Changed my life" (transformation focus)

3. **Support Ticket Themes**
   - Stage 3: "When will I feel relief?" (impatient, desperate)
   - Stage 4-5: "How do I optimize this?" (engaged, experimental)

## Stage Migration Signals

### Indicators That Market is Shifting from Stage 3 → Stage 4

**Watch For:**

1. **Reddit Posts Changing Tone**
   - From "Does anything work?" to "I tried [option], it helped but not enough"
   - From hopelessness to cautious optimism
   - From "My doctor says..." to "I'm researching..."

2. **Competitor Ads Changing Positioning**
   - More mechanism-education ads (Stage 4 demand)
   - Less problem-agitation ads (Stage 3 demand shrinking)
   - More "alternative" angles than "standard solution failure" angles

3. **Avatar Language Sophistication**
   - More medical terminology in Reddit posts
   - More questions about mechanism/science
   - Less emotional venting; more tactical problem-solving

4. **Avatar Willingness to Invest**
   - From "cheapest option please" to "best option regardless of price"
   - From skepticism about cost to focus on results
   - From "guarantee or no" to "willing to try if mechanism makes sense"

### Indicators That Market is Shifting from Stage 2 → Stage 3

**Watch For:**

1. **Reduced Problem-Education Content Performance**
   - Stage 2 education ads (explaining the problem) start underperforming
   - Market has moved past "What is this?" into "Why can't I fix it?"

2. **Rise in Frustration Language**
   - Reddit posts shift from questioning to frustrated venting
   - "Nothing works" language increases
   - Doctor-blame language increases

3. **Competitor Shift in Angles**
   - Fewer Stage 2 angles ("Is this normal?")
   - More Stage 3 angles ("Why standard solutions fail")

### Indicators That Market is Shifting from Stage 4 → Stage 5

**Watch For:**

1. **High Adoption of Complex Mechanism Angles**
   - Stage 5 avatars engage with detailed scientific explanations
   - Longer-form educational content performance increases
   - Questions about optimization/stacking/advanced strategies

2. **Emergence of Community/Advocacy**
   - Customer testimonials become more detailed/passionate
   - Organic referrals increase (Stage 5 tells friends)
   - Social proof ads outperform mechanism education ads

3. **Product Evolution Requests**
   - Customers asking for advanced variations, higher doses, combinations
   - Feedback moves from "Does this work?" to "How can I maximize results?"

## Connection to Mechanism Strategy

### Stage 3 Avatars Require Different Mechanisms Than Stage 4-5

**Stage 3:** "Why standard solutions fail"
- Mechanism focus: **Problem with existing solution** (e.g., "Gabapentin causes tolerance; here's why")
- Strategy: Position your approach as fundamentally different from what failed
- Angle: "Why [standard treatment] fails and what actually works"

**Stage 4:** "Alternative mechanism exists"
- Mechanism focus: **How your approach works differently** (e.g., "Unlike gabapentin which numbs sensation, this repairs nerves")
- Strategy: Educate on mechanism; build credibility through science/logic
- Angle: "How [mechanism] works (and why doctors don't recommend it)"

**Stage 5:** "Transformation is possible"
- Mechanism focus: **Pathway to transformation** (e.g., "Complete nerve regeneration possible through 90-day protocol")
- Strategy: Promise bold outcomes; assume avatar will optimize/experiment
- Angle: "From [problem] to [transformation]: The [mechanism] protocol"

**Implication for Creative Strategy:**

If market is in Stage 3, emphasize villain/failure angles.
If market is migrating to Stage 4, introduce mechanism angles.
If market has saturated to Stage 5, introduce transformation/outcome angles.

## Pro Tips for Accurate Stage Assessment

1. **Triangulate Data Sources** — Don't rely on Reddit alone or competitor ads alone. Cross-reference both. Stage 3 avatars show up in Reddit frustration language AND in competitor "failure positioning" ads.

2. **Emotional Language Indicators are Strongest** — Stage 3 = "desperate" language. Stage 4 = "hopeful" language. Stage 5 = "experimental" language. Use emotion as signal.

3. **Watch Answer Patterns, Not Just Questions** — When Stage 2 asks "What is this?" they get answers. When Stage 3 asks "Why doesn't this work?" answers shift to "Try [alternative]." Question-answer patterns reveal stage.

4. **Stage Migration Precedes Market Saturation** — Stage 3 → Stage 4 migration means market is saturating on Stage 3 positioning. Get ahead: develop Stage 4 angles before competitors flood the market.

5. **Smaller Segments at Stages 1, 2, 5** — Most markets are dominated by Stage 3. Opportunities exist at Stages 4-5 (less competitive) and Stage 2 (if you can educate). Stage 1 is too small for most direct response.

---

## Forbidden Patterns in Stage Assessment

- **Conflating Stage with Avatar Demographics** — Stage is about awareness/readiness, not age or income. A 65-year-old can be Stage 3 or Stage 5.
- **Assuming One Angle Fits All Stages** — Stage 3 "failure focus" angle bombs with Stage 5 avatars. Mix angles per stage distribution.
- **Ignoring Emotional Language** — Pure data-driven assessment misses stage shifts. Emotion is the signal.
- **Predicting Stage Shift Without Evidence** — "Market seems like it's advancing" is not data. Use specific signals: Reddit language shift, competitor angle shift, customer sophistication increase.

---

## Notes

Stage assessment informs everything: which angles work, which mechanisms resonate, which villains trigger action, how much education is needed, what beliefs need shifting.

Markets are not static. Stage distributions evolve. Avatars move from Stage 3 (frustrated with standard solutions) toward Stage 4-5 (open to alternatives, seeking transformation) as time passes.

Quarterly assessment keeps creative strategy synchronized with market evolution. When stage distribution shifts, angle strategy must shift accordingly.

The best positioning targets the dominant stage (usually Stage 3) but starts seeding the next stage's angles before the shift is obvious. First-mover advantage in Stage 4 positioning compounds over time.
