# House Laws (judgment laws)

Migrated from Claude Code auto-memory `feedback_*.md` files on 2026-09-03. These are the laws a model has to apply with judgment; the mechanical (regex) laws live in `laws-*-additions.json` beside this file, and tool/engine facts live in `engine-defaults.md`. Wording marked LAW in the source file is preserved verbatim. Nothing here is softened, merged or editorialized; where two memory files disagree the disagreement is recorded, not resolved.

## Research and strategy

- **Brand context first (LAW).** "LAW: whenever Brooks prompts about a specific brand, pull that brand's context FIRST, automatically — before concepting, writing, or generating anything." Brand folders live at `Documents/marketing brain/brands/<brand>/` (velantra, motilli, lunessa, wend, Orelli, solorna, luma tea, kalo rips); ls the folder, read the relevant subfolders (product truth, research, prior concepts), then start. For any COPY task, study the avatar research first and write in the avatar's own language, beliefs and pains, pulling verbatim VoC phrases into hooks and body ("like passing solid rock"); stack the matching product-scale skill on top, folder and skill complement each other. (source: feedback_brand_context_first, 2026-08-17)
- **Psychology-first replication.** Every agent analyzes the PSYCHOLOGY behind each element before replicating anything: WHY the hook opens a curiosity loop, WHY the visual triggers identification, WHY copy shifts beliefs in that sequence, WHY the product enters where it does, HOW mechanism education disqualifies alternatives first. A mandatory psychological-analysis phase precedes generation; surface replication produces generic work. (source: feedback_psychology_first)
- **The golden nugget.** When mining reviews, Reddit or winning angles, the job is to find the single most emotionally loaded deep frame (the real motive: "I'm becoming my mother", not "memory loss") and put it at the VERY TOP as the hook. Ask of every candidate: "is this the topic, or is this the motive?" and dig one layer deeper when it is the topic. (source: feedback_golden_nugget_review_mining, 2026-07-11)
- **Awareness calibration.** Reddit/forum research skews copy two stages too aware; fix the avatar's awareness from their MEDIA CONTEXT (Meta cold traffic is stage 1-2, the 2 AM doom-scroller) before writing any LP or listicle. Lower awareness gets more emotion, advertorial scenes, plain language and a late simple mechanism; higher awareness gets the graveyard of what they tried, the mechanism early, and differentiation on dose/purity/combination inside a level-4 market. A listicle CONFIRMS the ad's claims, it does not restate them; one funnel per ANGLE, not per ad. (source: feedback_awareness_calibration, 2026-07-07)
- **Swipe awareness inheritance (GLOBAL).** A 1:1 structural replication silently imports the swipe's awareness stage, which is almost never ours, because awareness rides inside the structure, not just the hook. State the swipe's stage and OUR avatar's stage explicitly, diff them, and when they differ: hook leads with the symptom she is living in plus a promise of the cause (never a method promise), add a recognition beat, attach vindication to effort she is already making, demote the villain teardown to education after the mechanism, make the mechanism the revelation. Motilli's avatar is majority problem-aware. (source: feedback_swipe_awareness_inheritance, 2026-08-04)
- **Skepticism sets the SHAPE, not the runtime.** "Market skepticism sets the SHAPE of the funnel, not the runtime." A novel ingredient in a cold market means MORE objections handled naturally inside the SAME short runtime, never a longer VSL (Jevawell: 8 objections, 274 words, 92 seconds). Mechanism: the CRITERION LINE states the standard before any ingredient is named, then one designated killer per failed solution (kill by FAILURE MODE, never by verdict), then each ingredient answers one dead alternative; the ingredient stack IS the objection section. Ingredient phrasing: `[ingredient] + [physical verb] + so that + [plain outcome]`. Pure second person, modest promise, never fight the GLP-1, proof = social volume + guarantee. (source: feedback_skepticism_sets_funnel_shape, 2026-08-25)
- **Reference mirror fidelity (LAW).** "when he feeds a reference for an ad concept, the adaptation must look basically EXACTLY like the reference — trace it, don't re-direct it; only product/brand/law-forced swaps." Mirror beat-for-beat: shot types, compositions, framing, pacing, motion, emotional arc, on-screen-text placement; swap in our product, brand and creator. Permitted deviations are LAW-FORCED only (claims we cannot honestly make, real-footage beats, trade-dress swaps, product-truth blocks); any other deviation needs Brooks's explicit sign-off flagged in the plan. A reference from Brooks is a spec, not inspiration. (source: feedback_reference_mirror_fidelity, 2026-08-18)
- **Product truth resolves to the image, and conflicting references stop the line.** When a product skill's prose or table disagrees with a canonical image, or two reference sets disagree, treat it as a stop-the-line conflict (check which set is the live PDP product, or ask), not a coin flip; never choose the table text over the photograph. Boat Tote: root-folder flats are the RETIRED design; only `colors/<name>/` shots are the live product. (source: feedback_boattote_colorway_truth, 2026-07-24)
- **Sibling concept disambiguation.** Before replicating "the concept we did recently", `ls` the source product's `concepts/` folder and match the request against ALL sibling concepts, not the first memory recalled; name the exact source folder/file in the first status message so a wrong match dies in seconds. Format tells: real competitor brands + prices + "find/scrape competitors" = the BEST price-ranking format; generated archetype bags + one creator on camera = the Edit. (source: feedback_sibling_concept_disambiguation, 2026-07-27)
- **Stale skills lose to the truth file.** When a product-scale skill has drifted from `PRODUCT-TRUTH.md` (Vivienne: the skill still says "structured", "high-gloss", "lacquered", "marbling", "crease patina", the exact words that got two image sets rejected), read the truth file and treat the skill as stale until it is rewritten. Anything that loads the skill first and pastes its identity block will regenerate a rejected set. (source: feedback_vivienne_skill_stale_read_product_truth, 2026-08-27)

## Angles and hooks

- **Angle = the specific PROBLEM (LAW).** "LAW — an angle is the specific PROBLEM (= one ad set), not the argumentative claim (= a hook). Four layers avatar/angle/hook/concept map onto campaign/adset/ad." Avatar = CBO campaign (1); Angle = ad set (3+ per avatar); Hook = ad variation (1-3 per angle); Concept = the creative. Mechanism is an ingredient inside a hook, not a layer; the golden nugget is the depth requirement on the angle. An angle field containing "it's not X, it's Y" is a hook in the wrong slot. Naming: `<AVATAR>_<ANGLE-ID>_<HOOK-N>_<FORMAT>`; rank at both levels after 30 days. Micro-angles are the same angle at higher resolution (child record with `parent_id` and `level`), still passing the ad-set test. (source: feedback_angle_definition_canonical, 2026-08-17, re-confirmed 2026-08-18, micro-angles 2026-08-21)
- **Hook philosophy.** Hooks are extremely specific ("my part getting wider", not "hair loss"), in the avatar's voice, organic and native (a Facebook post or text message, not an ad), and open a curiosity loop the reader cannot resist. The elite pattern is authority subversion + vindication: a SPECIFIC doctor dismissed a SPECIFIC person about a SPECIFIC thing and was proven wrong. Every hook passes the test: can the reader SEE the scene, FEEL the dismissal, and is the loop impossible to resist? (source: feedback_hook_philosophy)
- **No transition angles (LAW).** "LAW: never build an ad library on a transition/moment angle (day-to-night, seasonal switch) — one-month shelf life; lead with a VERDICT and stack our product's own physical features." A transition angle also sells a situation, not our bag. The seasonal word is a WRAPPER ("perfect fall bag" is a fine hook), the positioning underneath must be product-durable. No villain, no manufactured problem beat for an avatar who is simply looking for a good bag. Before writing ask (a) will this angle still work in six months and (b) if I swap a competitor's product into this script does it still read fine; a no to (a) or a yes to (b) means rewrite. (source: feedback_no_transition_angles, 2026-08-16)
- **Sell our product, not the category.** A creator swipe is usually an affiliate selling the category, so her benefit lines are deliberately generic; keep the swipe's structure and mechanic, but every reason to believe must be a specific physical feature of OUR product stated as fact (structured and stands up, one-piece flap, rolled handles, crossed straps, hand woven, no logo anywhere). Those double as the buyer's shopping criteria, which is how we become "the best one" without comparing. Move the brand reveal earlier than the affiliate has it (~0:07, not ~0:20). Never forget the no-logo beat. (source: feedback_sell_our_product_not_the_category, 2026-07-26)
- **Don't manufacture pain for a searching avatar; never agitate with an unverifiable claim.** Two rules from the same correction: (1) never open with a claim you cannot back about products we don't make ("most of them are not built to survive one summer"), it hands the viewer a reason to disbelieve everything after it; (2) match the structure to where the avatar already is. A searching avatar has an open loop, not a wound: Verdict → reassurance → stacked reasons to believe → the specific one → CTA, nothing attacked. Still true: don't open on a product PITCH; opening on the bag is fine when the hook names the category as editorial advice. Agitation is right only for an unaware/problem-aware avatar, and then the wound is her own experience, never a claim about rival products. (source: feedback_tof_no_product_first_seconds, 2026-07-26)
- **Two structural hook laws by awareness.** (1) Unaware hooks call out the CONCEPT/situation, never the problem; the reader self-IDs through the scene and the unadmitted emotion (identity loss). (2) At problem/solution awareness self-ID happens IMMEDIATELY, and at solution-aware specifically through the failed solution itself. The hook bank by awareness level lives at `_engine/swipe-library/hook-bank/HOOK-BANK.md`; obey its swipe hygiene (never copy competitor proof numbers). (source: feedback_soft_closes_hook_bank, 2026-08-19)

## Script and copy

- **No AI-tell patterns (global copy law).** Banned from ALL copy, any brand, any format: (1) em dashes, even when they originated in Brooks's own pasted draft; (2) the "That's not X. It's Y." contrastive-reveal beat and every variant, including derivative resolution sentences that tidy a paragraph with negation-then-affirmation; (3) staccato fragment stacks in SPOKEN lines. Short noun-phrase runs are Brooks's real voice in prose he wrote but NOT in a voiceover line, where every spoken line is a complete sentence and cadence varies. Do not solve a tight timecode by chopping a sentence into fragments; let the line run a beat past the cut (J-cut). Also react to over-polished rhythm: every paragraph landing on a crafted punch, tidy tricolons, symmetrical parallelism. When revising older brand copy that carries these patterns, flag and offer to clean them rather than propagating them. (source: feedback_no_ai_tell_patterns, 2026-07-28, rule 3 added 2026-08-21)
- **Spoken lines are complete sentences (LAW 9/02).** "LAW 9/02: every spoken ad line is a complete, naturally flowing sentence with its object and connective tissue intact ('It holds three days' worth of clothes'), never a clipped fragment ('Holds three days'); calibrate on the @shoppingwith.vale yapper cadence." Read every spoken line aloud; if it has no subject or no object, restore them. Do not solve a tight beat by chopping; extend the beat or cut a claim. Numbers always carry their noun. The one exception is the head-to-toe rundown in a street-interview format, and even there each item keeps its verb. Attribute stacks stay stacks, but each item is a clause with a verb joined naturally, not a comma list of nouns. (source: feedback_spoken_lines_complete_sentences, 2026-09-02)
- **Lived, not announced (LAW).** "LAW — everything LIVED, not ANNOUNCED; belief is shifted by SHOWING." Brooks, canonical: "everything should be lived NOT announced. that's how we shift belief, by showing it." For every belief row ask whether the shift is SHOWN in a lived moment or STATED; stated = rewrite. Kill announcement sentences and smuggle the beat inside momentum grammar ("So, of course..."). Dismissal/indifference about another person is rendered as scenes of what THEY don't live. Register = a person posting a TikTok about their own raw experience: And/So/But chains, stacked clauses, self-Q&A mechanism teach, mid-thought corrections; a draft that moves cleanly beat-to-beat is a rejected draft. (source: feedback_oblique_not_literal, 2026-08-20)
- **CONCRETE + LITERAL only (STANDING LAW).** "Addendum 2 (Brooks, 2026-08-20, STANDING LAW): CONCRETE + LITERAL only. No abstract language, and no METAPHORICAL language." Say the literal thing ("laxatives pull water into the colon, that's the bottom", never "that's the end of the line"). Only two comparisons are ever allowed: the mandatory sensory demo the viewer verifies on her own body, and the analogy set already locked in the brand's canonical mechanism doc; no new simile enters a script without entering the mechanism doc first. Every line must name something a camera could film or a body could feel; kill feeling-nouns, image-less state words ("frustrating"), category words instead of objects ("supplements" → "the orange tub"), and vague time/quantity ("a while" → "day eleven"). Test per line: can I see it, or is it a summary of seeing it? Also standing: simple relatable language ALWAYS. (source: feedback_oblique_not_literal, 2026-08-20)
- **The naturalizer pass is mandatory for anything spoken.** A script can pass the ai-copy-blacklist and still sound like a DR copywriter performing; the two highest-yield tells are announced beat changes ("And here's the part that should make you angry") and every paragraph closing on a crafted punch. Core fixes: at most one announced transition per script, un-land one beat in three, break tricolons and balanced pairs, front-load the reaction ahead of the fact, one self-correction per 60s, one wasted cluster per 30s. Hard lock: claims, numbers, ingredients, dosages, product name, URL, CTA and its position, beat order, runtime. Naturalizing is a delivery operation, never a claims operation and never a license to dumb it down. Ship two versions: copy of record, plus spoken-delivery version (commas and periods only). (source: feedback_script_naturalizer_layer, 2026-08-04)
- **Simple but not dumbed down.** Keep language simple and conversational (grade ~5-6, short sentences) but trust the reader with a named ingredient or term ("gastroenterologist", "apigenin", "chlorophyll") as long as each does ONE plainly stated job: "something called apigenin gently gets your stomach moving." Never a multi-actor mechanism chain. Over-simplifying reads as talking down; over-explaining loses the problem-aware viewer. (source: feedback_ad_script_simplicity)
- **UGC dialogue text is engineered for speech.** Keep a clean copy of record and a spoken-delivery version fed to the model: contractions everywhere, short sentences for rhythm, punctuation commas and periods ONLY (no ellipses, no em dashes; the model renders them as dead air), intentional beats directed in the performance cue never written into dialogue. Disfluency budget ~1 per 2 segments, comma-framed verbal texture only, never on mechanism terms, numbers, product name or dosage. (source: feedback_ugc_dialogue_naturalness, 2026-07-06)
- **Demonstrate, never describe (GLOBAL LAW for AI UGC).** "GLOBAL LAW for AI UGC: a talking head that HOLDS a product and describes it reads as AI no matter how good the voice is. Every claim must be physically demonstrated on camera, and the product must never stop moving." Script-side: every claim gets a demo beat within ~1 second of being spoken (never let an adjective travel alone); break parallel structure on purpose; open mid-thought and flat, no "Okay, wait", no superlative in line one; name the brand late and casually; social proof as lived experience; soft close friend to friend ("please just go get one", not "link below"). (source: feedback_ugc_demonstrate_never_describe, 2026-08-14)
- **The creator never speaks as the brand (GLOBAL).** In any creator-voiced cut (UGC review, greenscreen talking head, first-person AI VO) offers are third person: "they're running a pre-order", never "our sale" or "we". First person singular for her own decision ("so I got mine") is the strongest independence signal. The Higgsfield Marketing Studio reference's "our sale" line is the odd one out; copy its structure and pacing, not that line. Applies across brands. (source: feedback_creator_never_speaks_as_brand, 2026-08-04)
- **Never fabricate a citation (LAW).** "LAW - never invent a study, journal, institution, N, or percentage in copy; cite real research or state the mechanism unattributed." Cite only research verified to exist (the 2023 Neurology / UK Biobank osmotic-laxative dementia analysis is real). Otherwise state the mechanism directionally and unattributed ("there's real literature on enteric nerve decline after menopause"). Never let a fictional doctor endorse the product by name; have them hand over the criteria and let the narrator find the brand. A named fictional physician as the AUTHOR of an ad is fake expert testimony; convert to a patient, nurse or insider witness. (source: feedback_no_fabricated_citations, 2026-08-15)
- **Verified Buyer review statics are approved house creative; write the quote.** The no-fabricated-citations law targets invented studies, sample sizes, percentages, doctors, certifications, star ratings and review counts; it does NOT mean refusing to write a testimonial ad unit. Craft rules that still hold: every detail asserted is a cleared claim from the product's truth file; whole-sentence pull quotes (a quoted headline must be a verbatim whole sentence of the body, never a clause with its comma swapped for a period); curly quotation marks; one face per named reviewer; machine-enforce all of it (`check_copy.py`, `check_quotes.py`). Offer brand mode as an alternate, not a gate. (source: feedback_verified_buyer_statics_are_approved, 2026-08-27)
- **Listicle / LP structure mirrors a video ad.** Open-loop headline hook; reasons 1-3 introduce the PROBLEM granularly (feelings, bigger problems it causes); reasons 4-6 EDUCATE on problem and product and establish authority; reasons 7-9 make the product the easiest/smartest/fastest solution; then de-risk (guarantee) and urgency. Never "10 reasons the product is great"; every LP is a belief-shift journey. (source: feedback_listicle_structure)
- **Soft closes for cold natives.** "We've been selling too hard." For cold native ads the close is emotional resolution first, then a whispered CTA; the edit-style close ("Edit: I forgot to leave the link here...") is the preferred form. Hard offer stacks (scarcity + deal) are reserved for most-aware offer ads only. Hard CTAs break the native disguise and undo the trust the story built. (source: feedback_soft_closes_hook_bank, 2026-08-19)

## Storyboard and keyframes

- **Storyboard first (LAW).** "the ad engine ALWAYS storyboards first — Cutroom board with the actual generated keyframes placed on every beat, Brooks approves the keyframes ON the board, only then does animation/render fire." The pipeline order is LAW: 1. Script approved by Brooks. 2. Storyboard board in Cutroom (reference lane + our lane, beat cards with timecode, VO fragment, visual, emotion). 3. Generate ALL keyframes, run a frame-QA pass, re-roll fails. 4. Rebuild the board with each beat's actual keyframe image placed on the card. 5. STOP. Brooks approves the keyframes on the board. Never fire i2v/animation before this approval. 6. Animate approved keyframes, assemble, deliver. Cutroom is the MECHANICAL approval gate: `plan_animate_scenes` requires `approved_board_slug` from `dr_push_brief_board`; never invent a slug. Rendering is the expensive irreversible step; keyframes are cheap. (source: feedback_ad_engine_storyboard_first, 2026-08-17, gate 2026-08-18)
- **Always push to Cutroom (LAW).** "LAW — every storyboard/brief/creative-board deliverable gets pushed to Cutroom, always, without Brooks asking. Markdown in the repo is not delivery." Push in the same turn via the cutroom skill / `dr_push_brief_board`; the markdown in `brands/<brand>/` is the artifact of record, the board is the delivery and approval surface. The push is not gated on having keyframes; text and reference-frame boards go up too. (source: feedback_always_push_to_cutroom, 2026-08-20)
- **Our product is on screen the entire ad (LAW).** "LAW — Velantra video ads keep OUR product on screen the entire runtime; BAN on generic/random product visuals (competitor-ish bags, prop bags, graveyard shots) as the subject of any frame." Every frame's SUBJECT is our product or the creator holding/using it, hook frames included. Failed-alternative lines are delivered over footage of OUR product demonstrating the opposite, never over a shot of the failed thing. Deep-background set dressing is tolerated only logo-free, soft-focus, never composed as a subject, nothing checkered or monogram-adjacent. (source: feedback_product_on_screen_entire_ad, 2026-08-21)
- **A UGC ad is ONE continuous scene (LAW).** "LAW (Brooks 8/22): a UGC ad is ONE continuous scene. One creator, one outfit, one bag, one place. Never cut away to product footage in another location or to a colorway montage. Fix an engine limitation with Omni, not with a cutaway." One creator, one outfit, one bag, one colorway, one journey, every beat from the SAME creator reference and SAME product reference. A location move inside one journey (gate → plane seat → terminal) is fine; outside it (gate → kitchen counter) is not. No colorway montage ever; no PDP screen recording at the end of a continuous UGC piece. Fix order when an engine cannot do a beat: another engine that stays in the scene (Omni), then cut the beat; never a cutaway to safer footage, never a frozen still. Continuity outranks asset safety; a five-creator pack is five full rebuilds. Seedance 2.5 earns only the talking beats (product held still, front-on); everything that moves goes to Omni. (source: feedback_ugc_one_continuous_scene, 2026-08-22)
- **Every piece of b-roll advances the story (LAW).** "LAW (Brooks 8/22 Loom, VEL-WEEKENDER-GS-LIVEOUT-01): every piece of b-roll must ADVANCE THE STORY. Near-static product frames are not b-roll at all. Fix = one continuous protagonist doing one continuous thing, bag incidental." Before cutting any VO ad, write the protagonist's continuous action from first frame to last, cast ONE creator as a locked Reference Element in one outfit, and shoot every beat as a moment inside that action with the product incidental. The visual must match the literal claim ("stands open on its own" = a bag standing on a desk, not opened). A beat that could be swapped for a still without loss is not b-roll. (source: feedback_broll_must_advance_story, 2026-08-22)
- **Situational plausibility (LAW).** "LAW: every b-roll frame must be a place and a load a real owner would actually choose — no nice bag on a cafe floor, no groceries when the script says laptop." Two tests on every frame before it goes in a cut: the Load test (would THIS avatar put THAT in the bag on THIS occasion, and does it match what the VO says is inside?) and the Placement test (her own floor, a chair, a bench, a counter, a car seat = yes; a public floor = never). Also audit for a sloppy set-down even at home. Run together with the in-point audit. (source: feedback_broll_situational_plausibility, 2026-08-22)
- **Hyperreal b-roll inherits realism from a real reference.** Mode A (pixel seed): screenshot a real organic TikTok frame and i2i it with our product ("use the exact same scene as the 1st image, but with the 2nd bag instead, natural lighting, 9:16"). Mode B (scene-study rebuild): when no single frame matches the shot, study a reference scene top to bottom (light source/hardness/temperature/falloff, camera height/distance/phone-lens feel, surfaces, prop logic, casual composition, imperfections) and prompt from that spec with our product; never a generic "aesthetic" scene. The 3D-render look comes from imagining scenes from taste alone. Realism test = SITUATIONAL AWARENESS: not "does the render look good" but "would a real person ever film this?"; name the story of the moment (just unboxed it, packing for a trip) before generating, and if it can't be named, don't generate. Object placement follows human behavior (chair backs, passenger seats, beds mid-pack, a lap), never museum staging. (source: feedback_hyperreal_broll_real_ugc_seed, 2026-08-20)
- **Real footage for real-world human action (GLOBAL LAW).** "GLOBAL LAW: real-world human action b-roll must be genuine rips, never Kling/Higgsfield/Omni/Seedance gens. AI is legal only for inside-the-body science and the deliberate synthetic presenter." Before substituting a gen for an action beat, inventory the real library first (contact sheet, one frame per clip, and actually look). Prefer a real rip whose burned-in caption argues your line and let the caption ride. Casting is the second half: bloating/gut rip libraries skew to women in their twenties; cutting that against a 45-70 avatar VO is its own credibility break, so rip age-appropriate footage or frame to hands/counters/torsos. Final QA: one generated human in an action slot fails the whole cut. (source: feedback_real_footage_for_action_broll, 2026-08-09)
- **Sourced b-roll passes the visual subject test (LAW).** "LAW for sourced B-roll: footage OF the action, never ABOUT the topic — visual subject test, no spec softeners, Claude frame-audits top ranks before Brooks sees anything." A muted stranger must be able to name the required action from the frame; talking heads, faces, displaying-an-object, skits are auto non-matches. Never add "talking acceptable" softeners to slot specs; write `action` fields as what fills the frame. Deliver trimmed best segments, keep raw in candidates/. When one slot fails this way, assume ALL slots are affected; partial fixes are not accepted. (source: feedback_broll_gate_visual_subject_test, 2026-08-17)
- **B-roll sourcing SOP: search the subject, mine the moment.** Search terms are 1-4 word product/condition/VoC nouns (`zepbound`, `ozempic close up`, `miralax`); the brief's action sentence goes only in the slot's `action` field for the gate. Judge talking-to-camera / burned captions on the best segment, not the whole video; most good moments live inside talking videos. No on-screen text over the used segment (zoom/crop is a legal salvage). Realism-audit each brief action before sourcing it (she scrolls her phone at night, not a laptop). A ~4-min VSL needs 30-40 sourced clips. Dry slot after a term rewrite converts to a generation prompt, marked AI-GENERATED. (source: feedback_broll_sourcing_loom_sop, 2026-08-18)
- **Named products wear real trade dress; product shots are snapshots (LAW).** "2026-08-17 LAW from Brooks (8 corrections on GUT247 keyframes): named product = REAL trade dress on screen, never generic blank-label props (reads AI); product shots = casual one-jar snapshots, never styled multi-jar glamour; every beat's visual must demonstrate the exact line." Prompt real product names (squat white MiraLAX tub with pink cap, Fleet-style enema boxes); a slightly imperfect label beats a blank one. ONE jar sitting casually in a real cluttered scene, flat daylight, handheld, never a multi-jar glamour lineup mid-ad. Beat congruence is checked line by line ("I tried seven products" over an anatomy shot fails). Show the physical object over a phone-scrolling beat; no heavy backlit silhouettes. (source: feedback_ugc_props_real_trade_dress, 2026-08-17)
- **Props that could carry print are specified blank.** Any prop that could plausibly carry print (a paperback, a matchbox, a label) renders garbled barcodes and fake text; specify "completely blank cover, no printing, no text, no letters, no numbers, no barcode, no label". Silhouette QA needs a side-by-side against the real product photo per colorway, not an eyeball on hardware presence. (source: feedback_meridian_identity_block, 2026-08-21)
- **One creator, locked as a Reference Element (GLOBAL LAW).** "GLOBAL LAW: a multi-shot 'one creator' ad must lock the creator as a Higgsfield Reference Element wired into EVERY prompt; a per-scene text description drifts into a different woman each scene and reads as a slideshow." The four fixes: lock the creator as a Reference Element (including hands-only inserts); one outfit for the whole ad, repeated verbatim in every prompt; split the longest VO sentence across many fast shots, one per thing named (this kills the slideshow feel more than motion does); ordinary clutter in every frame with the product incidental to her life. (source: feedback_one_creator_element_lock, 2026-08-11)
- **Reframe when a mechanism keeps failing (LAW).** "LAW: when a fragile product mechanism fails repeated generation attempts, CHANGE THE BLOCKING so the mechanism leaves the frame. Do not keep re-rolling." After the 3-attempt cap, ask what can leave the frame: reframe tighter, change the angle, occlude the fragile part with the person or the bag body, keeping only load-bearing identity cues. A beat does not need to be a hero shot to do its job; ask what the VO line actually requires. (source: feedback_reframe_when_mechanism_keeps_failing, 2026-08-23)
- **Hardware fails by distance, not by wording.** The flap mechanism (and any small metal fitting) survives at medium and breaks at macro across Seedance AND GPT Image 2 stills; more words defending the mechanism do not help. Re-block: close framing stays on the weave or crossed belts with the top edge of the bag cropped out; the flap is only ever seen at medium distance or wider. A still can be repaired in place with single-variable i2i (artifacted frame + a 5x crop of the correct part from another frame). Name the wrong shape, not just the right one. QA every macro against a medium shot of the same object before shipping. (source: feedback_seedance_flap_survives_medium_breaks_macro, 2026-08-09, extended 2026-08-16)
- **Crop shots drop the identity block (LAW).** "LAW: a crop shot gets a crop-specific block describing ONLY what is inside the frame. Never paste the whole-object identity block into a macro, an interior, or any detail shot." Framing goes FIRST, describe only the in-frame subject, name the excluded parts explicitly and kill the background. Diagnostic: when a crop renders the whole object, do NOT re-roll and do NOT harden the identity block; the block itself is the bug. (source: feedback_crop_shots_drop_the_identity_block, 2026-08-30)

## No prices in ads (standing user instruction, 2026-09-04)

Across all brands, omit the current offer amount from ad narration, captions, generated-image text, overlays, comparison graphics and end cards. This includes numeric amounts, amounts written in words, discounts, crossed-out prices and price badges. Focus on supported product qualities and relevant benefits so changes to the offer do not require new creative. Qualitative category language such as “designer prices” is allowed when the brief calls for it and it does not quote or misstate a competitor's offer. This rule applies to ads, not internal product-truth records, provider cost ledgers, or storefront pricing. Do not invent quality claims to replace a price argument.
