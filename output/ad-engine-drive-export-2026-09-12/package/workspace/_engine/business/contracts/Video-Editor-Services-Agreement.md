## 1. ENGAGEMENT AND SCOPE OF SERVICES

**1.1 Engagement.** The Company engages the Contractor to provide video editing and post-production services (the "Services") on a full-time, ongoing basis, and the Contractor accepts that engagement, on the terms set out in this Agreement.

**1.2 Brands Covered.** The Services cover all brands owned or operated by the Company, currently including Velantra, Motilli, Lunessa, Orelli, and Solorna, together with any brand the Company launches, acquires, or operates during the Term (collectively, the "Brands"). The Contractor acknowledges that work will be assigned across multiple Brands and that no single Brand assignment is guaranteed.

**1.3 Core Deliverables.** The Services include, without limitation:

- Editing short-form paid social video advertisements (typically 15 to 60 seconds) for Meta, TikTok, and other paid channels
- Editing long-form video sales letters and advertorial-style video content (typically 60 seconds to 5 minutes)
- Assembling supplied AI-generated clips, stock footage, creator UGC, product footage, and B-roll into finished advertisements
- Cutting multiple hook variations, angle variations, and aspect-ratio versions (9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9) from a single master edit
- Adding and styling burned-in captions, on-screen text overlays, lower thirds, and end cards
- Audio work including voiceover syncing, music selection and bedding, level balancing, noise cleanup, and sound effects
- Color correction, color grading, speed ramping, stabilization, and basic motion graphics
- Light retouching and frame cleanup where required
- Organizing, naming, and delivering final files and project files to the Company's designated storage
- Maintaining an organized project archive so any edit can be reopened and revised

**1.4 Assignment of Work.** Work will be assigned by the Company through its designated project channel (Slack, Notion, email, or another system the Company specifies). The Contractor will work from written briefs, scripts, shot lists, and reference material supplied by the Company.

**1.5 Not Included.** Unless separately agreed in writing, the Services do not include scriptwriting, media buying, ad account management, on-camera talent work, or physical production.

**1.6 Standard of Performance.** The Contractor will perform the Services with the skill, care, and diligence reasonably expected of an experienced professional video editor, in a manner consistent with the Company's brand guidelines, reference materials, and written feedback.

## 2. SCHEDULE AND AVAILABILITY

**2.1 Committed Hours.** The Contractor will dedicate **five (5) hours per day, seven (7) days per week**, equal to approximately **thirty-five (35) hours per week** and approximately **150 hours per calendar month**, to the Services. The Parties agree this is a **material term** of this Agreement and a primary reason for the compensation in Section 3.

**2.2 Working Window.** The Contractor's five daily hours must include a minimum **two (2) hour overlap** with **[COMPANY WORKING HOURS AND TIME ZONE, e.g. 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time]** so that briefs, revisions, and approvals can move same-day. Subject to that overlap requirement and to the turnaround standards in Section 4, the Contractor controls when the remaining hours are worked.

**2.3 Time Off.** The Contractor is entitled to **two (2) paid flex days per calendar month**, requested with at least **seventy-two (72) hours** written notice and subject to the Company's approval, which will not be unreasonably withheld. Flex days do not accumulate and do not carry into the following month. Unused flex days are not paid out on termination.

**2.4 Illness and Emergencies.** If the Contractor becomes unable to work due to illness, family emergency, power outage, internet outage, or a natural event, the Contractor will notify the Company as soon as reasonably possible, and in any event before the start of the affected working day where circumstances allow. Absences of up to three (3) consecutive days for these reasons will not reduce the monthly fee. Absences beyond three consecutive days will be handled under Section 3.6.

**2.5 Notice of Extended Absence.** Any planned absence longer than two consecutive days requires at least **fourteen (14) days** written notice and the Company's written approval, and will be treated as unpaid leave under Section 3.6 unless the Company agrees otherwise in writing.

**2.6 Unresponsiveness.** Failure to respond to Company communications for a continuous period of forty-eight (48) hours during committed working days, without prior notice, is a material breach of this Agreement and grounds for termination for cause under Section 12.4.

## 3. COMPENSATION

**3.1 Monthly Fee.** The Company will pay the Contractor a flat fee of **one thousand five hundred United States dollars (USD $1,500.00)** per calendar month for the Services (the "Monthly Fee").

**3.2 Fee Is All-Inclusive.** The Monthly Fee is the Contractor's complete compensation for the Services. It covers all committed hours in Section 2.1 and all deliverables produced within them. No overtime, per-project fee, per-deliverable fee, bonus, commission, royalty, residual, usage fee, or expense reimbursement is owed unless the Company agrees to it in writing in advance. The Monthly Fee is not an hourly wage and does not vary with the number of deliverables produced in a given month.

**3.3 Payment Schedule.** The Monthly Fee is paid **monthly in arrears**. The Company will remit payment within **three (3) business days** after the last day of each calendar month for that completed month.

**3.4 Payment Method.** Payment will be made in USD via **Wise** to the account details the Contractor provides in writing. The Contractor may request an alternative method (PayPal, Payoneer, or direct bank transfer), which the Company may approve at its discretion. The Contractor bears all receiving fees, currency conversion costs, intermediary bank charges, and local transfer fees. The Company's obligation is satisfied when it sends USD $1,500.00 from its account.

**3.5 Partial Months.** If this Agreement starts or ends mid-month, the Monthly Fee for that month is prorated on a daily basis (Monthly Fee divided by the number of days in that calendar month, multiplied by the number of days engaged).

**3.6 Deductions for Unworked Days.** For any approved unpaid leave, or any day beyond the allowances in Sections 2.3 and 2.4 on which the Contractor does not perform the Services, the Company may deduct a prorated daily amount (Monthly Fee divided by the number of days in that calendar month) from the next payment. The Company will itemize any deduction in writing.

**3.7 Rate Review.** The Parties will review the Monthly Fee after **six (6) months** of continuous engagement and annually thereafter. Any increase is at the Company's sole discretion and is effective only when confirmed in writing.

**3.8 No Payment for Rejected Work.** Repeated failure to meet the standards in Section 4 is addressed through the cure and termination provisions in Section 12, not through withholding of the Monthly Fee. The Company will not withhold payment for completed months except where this Agreement expressly allows a deduction.

## 4. PERFORMANCE STANDARDS

**4.1 Turnaround Times.** Unless the brief states otherwise, the Contractor will deliver:

| Deliverable | Turnaround from receipt of complete assets |
|---|---|
| Short-form ad edit (15 to 60 seconds) | Within 24 hours |
| Hook, angle, or aspect-ratio variation of an existing edit | Within 12 hours |
| Long-form VSL or advertorial video (60 seconds to 5 minutes) | Within 48 hours |
| Revision round on an existing edit | Within 12 hours |
| Item flagged "PRIORITY" by the Company | Same working day |

If assets supplied by the Company are incomplete or unusable, the Contractor must flag this within **four (4) hours** of receipt. The turnaround clock pauses until the Company supplies what is missing.

**4.2 Output Target.** Within the committed hours, the Parties anticipate a working target of approximately **two to four (2 to 4) finished short-form deliverables per working day**, adjusted for complexity. This is a planning target used to size briefs, not a guaranteed quota, and the Contractor is not penalized for a shortfall caused by unusually complex assignments or by delays in Company-supplied assets.

**4.3 Revisions.** Each deliverable includes **unlimited revision rounds** against the original brief at no additional cost. A written change to the brief after work has begun (a new script, a new concept, or a new product) is treated as a new deliverable rather than a revision.

**4.4 Quality Standards.** All deliverables must:

- Match the brief, the reference material, and the Brand's stated visual and tonal guidelines
- Be free of technical defects including audio clipping, dropped frames, desynced audio, misspelled on-screen text, black frames, and incorrect aspect ratios
- Use only licensed, Company-supplied, or otherwise cleared music, footage, fonts, and assets
- Be exported to the Company's specified codec, resolution, frame rate, and bitrate
- Follow the Company's file naming and folder conventions (Exhibit A)

**4.5 Communication.** The Contractor will:

- Acknowledge new briefs within **four (4) hours** during the working window
- Respond to Company messages within **four (4) hours** during the working window
- Post a brief written end-of-day summary listing what was delivered, what is in progress, and anything blocking
- Raise any risk to a deadline as soon as it is known, not after the deadline passes

**4.6 Weekly Review.** The Contractor will attend one (1) recurring video call per week of up to sixty (60) minutes, scheduled by the Company within the working window, to review output, priorities, and feedback. This call counts toward the committed hours.

# EXHIBIT A
## DELIVERY SPECIFICATIONS AND FILE CONVENTIONS

These specifications may be updated by the Company in writing from time to time. The current version governs.

### A.1 Export Specifications

| Setting | Standard |
|---|---|
| Container / codec | MP4, H.264 |
| Primary resolution | 1080 x 1920 (9:16) |
| Secondary versions on request | 1080 x 1080 (1:1), 1080 x 1350 (4:5), 1920 x 1080 (16:9) |
| Frame rate | Match source, typically 30 fps |
| Bitrate | 10 to 16 Mbps for 1080p |
| Audio | AAC, 48 kHz, stereo |
| Loudness target | Approximately -14 LUFS integrated, true peak at or below -1 dBTP |
| Maximum file size | Under 500 MB unless otherwise specified |

### A.2 File Naming

Format: `BRAND_PRODUCT_CONCEPT_VERSION_ASPECT.mp4`

Examples:
- `VEL_StrawTote_UnboxingHook_v1_9x16.mp4`
- `MOT_GLP1_WrongOrganNative_v3_4x5.mp4`

Version numbers increment on every revision. Never overwrite a previously delivered file.

### A.3 Delivery Structure

Each project is delivered to the Company's designated storage in a folder named for the concept, containing:

- `/exports` finished files in every requested aspect ratio
- `/project` the editable project file with media relinked
- `/assets` any assets the Contractor sourced or created
- `notes.txt` a short status and decision log

### A.4 Captions

- Burned-in unless the brief states otherwise
- Legible on mobile at arm's length, positioned clear of platform UI safe zones
- Verified against the script for spelling and product-name accuracy before delivery
