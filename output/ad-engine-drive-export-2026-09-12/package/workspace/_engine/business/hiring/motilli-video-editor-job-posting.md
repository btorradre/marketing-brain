## Internal context

- **Filter word is JUNIPER.** SAFFRON is the Velantra editor pile, CARAMEL is the Instagram VA pile (`brands/velantra/_shared/instagram-va/01-job-posting.md`). Three words, three separate piles, no cross-contamination if someone applies to two. Applications missing the word fail the instruction-compliance screen. Delete without reading further.
- **The brand is never named in the post**, and no other brand in the portfolio is mentioned. Same rule as the Velantra posting and the VA posting. The post says "DTC health and wellness brand" and "a supplement."
- **Product truth is referenced but not spelled out.** The post says the jar, cap, gummy shape, and gummy color are non-negotiable without stating what they are, so an applicant cannot reverse-engineer the brand. The actual truth (square jar, white cap, dark forest green heart gummies, never orange or peach) goes out with the trial task, not the posting.
- **Volume target is 3 net new concepts/day plus hook variants**, versus 5/day on the Velantra post. Lower because Motilli formats run longer (15s hook tests through 2 to 3 minute VSLs) where Velantra sits at 10 to 35 seconds. Roughly 15 net new/week plus variant batches. This number is a dial, not a law. Raise it if the applicant pool reads it as light.
- **Hours:** 40/week baseline, same as Velantra. The heavier launch weeks appear once, in Pay and structure, never in the headline fields.
- **Pay is $1,500/mo**, carried over from the Velantra post for consistency. Worth noting that long-form DR supplement editing is a harder skill than short fashion cutdowns, so $1,500 is the floor of what this pulls. If the pool is weak, $1,800 is the next rung.

## What is different from the Velantra posting, and why

1. **Category requirement flipped.** Velantra requires prior fashion/apparel AI work. Motilli requires prior supplement/health/wellness direct response. Same logic, different vertical, and it is stated as required rather than bonus for the same reason.
2. **New section: "Reference work, and how close is close."** Direct answer to the swipe-judgement problem. Copy the structure (beat order, cut rhythm, hook turn, product entry, close stack), rebuild everything else. Screens for people who trace shot for shot without asking why a shot exists.
3. **New section: "Health category rules you have to respect."** No equivalent on the fashion post. Covers script-verbatim VO, no invented claims in captions, no fabricated before/after, no fake doctors, product truth, and the standing no-3D-render law. Doubles as a hard-constraints screen the same way the Velantra trial's auto-fail list does.
4. **Autonomy is called out explicitly** in "You're a good fit if." Written as a plain statement that the editors who did not work out were the ones needing the same five corrections weekly, then names the standard: self-check against the brief before sending, flag problems instead of shipping them. This is the "no hand-holding" requirement in a form that self-selects instead of just sounding demanding.
5. **Technical tooling requirement added.** GitHub, terminal, following a written setup doc. Some Motilli workflows are scripts and API pipelines with no interface, so this is real, not a nice-to-have.
6. **Tool list updated** to HeyGen, ElevenLabs, Google Flow and Veo, Google Omni, Seedance 2, GPT Image 2.
7. **Bonus points now include VSL/long-form and mechanism-animation work**, which is where Motilli's format weight actually sits.
