## About the role

We are a fast-growing DTC fashion brand and we run paid social at volume. We are hiring a full-time video editor who lives in AI tooling and can turn a brief plus a reference ad into a finished, scroll-stopping video.

This is a real editing job, not a "learn AI on the job" job. Almost everything we ship is AI-generated: AI avatars, AI voiceover, AI b-roll, AI product motion. You will be handed a script, a reference ad, and product references, and you are expected to come back with an ad that holds attention from second one to the last frame.

Every AI tool is provided and paid for by us. You never pay for a credit.

## What you'll do

- Ship around 5 net new concepts per day for TikTok, Meta, and YouTube Shorts, working from scripts and briefs we give you. These are short: most land between 10 and 35 seconds
- Generate assets with AI tools: avatars, voiceover, b-roll, product motion, lip sync
- Replicate references. When we send a reference ad plus a brief, you match its pacing, structure, and energy for our product without copying it frame for frame
- Cut for retention: strong first 2 seconds, tight pacing, no dead air, a visual change every few seconds
- Build hooks that stop the scroll, both the visual and the on-screen text
- Add captions, sound design, music, and text overlays that feel native to the platform
- Iterate fast on feedback and keep files organized so the team can find anything

## What the work is actually like

The generation itself is fast and cheap. Our tools do the heavy lifting, the prompts and workflows are already built, and the briefs are detailed. The work is simple and repeatable once you know the system. What we are paying for is speed, taste, and consistency: hitting the daily count without the quality sliding, and knowing which cut holds a viewer and which one loses them.

## You're a good fit if

- You are extremely comfortable with AI creative tools
- You can execute across multiple formats and styles
- You understand pacing, hooks, visual storytelling, and direct-response editing
- You can take a reference and recreate the format without needing your hand held
- You are FAST and can handle consistent creative volume

That last one matters most. Around 5 finished concepts per day is the standard here. The work itself is simple once you know the system, but you have to be able to hold the pace without the quality sliding.

## Requirements

- 2 to 3 years minimum of professional video editing experience (Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, DaVinci, or equivalent)
- Hands-on experience with AI video and audio tools. We use HeyGen, ElevenLabs, Veo 3, Google Omni, and Seedance 2. You should already be working in several of these, and you should be able to pick up the rest quickly
- You have edited AI-generated assets for a fashion or apparel brand before. This is required, not a bonus. Fashion has a specific look, and generic AI product video does not sell handbags
- Direct-response instinct. You know why a viewer drops off at 3 seconds and you know how to fix it. Most of our ads are 10 to 35 seconds, so every single second has to earn its place
- You can build a hook, not just execute one someone handed you
- Comfortable working inside a team with daily communication, shared feedback, and revision rounds
- Reliable full-time availability and strong written English

## Bonus points

- Motion graphics and color grading
- Experience with UGC-style creator ads
