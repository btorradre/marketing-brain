# Angle Library — Expanded from Brooks's Loom Framework

**Purpose:** Operational library of angle families. Each angle is a **psychological point of entry** — the big idea that makes a specific sub-avatar care about watching an ad.

**Source intelligence:** angles named in Brooks's Loom 1 + standard DR angle families, organized by the awareness level they serve best.

---

## What an Angle Actually Is

- An angle is **what you say** — the big emotional/intellectual idea that enters the avatar's mind.
- A concept is **how you say it** — the format (UGC, authority, founder, static, etc.).
- A hook is the **first 3–4 seconds** that delivers the angle.

One avatar has many sub-avatars. Each sub-avatar gets its own angle. Same product, different angles → different ads, different winners.

## Angle Families by Awareness Level

### Problem-Aware Angles

The avatar only knows the problem. The angle has to name the problem in a way they've never heard before, or attach it to an emotional moment they recognize.

| Angle | What It Says | Example Hook Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Doctor Dismissal** | "A trusted authority told you it's fine / it's stress / it's nothing — they were wrong." | "My doctor rolled her eyes when I showed her the research on ___." |
| **Hidden Cause** | "The thing you think is causing this isn't. Here's the real cause." | "It's not your hormones. It's not your diet. It's ___." |
| **Social Shame Moment** | A specific humiliating/anxious moment the avatar has lived. | "The moment I realized my boyfriend could hear me in the bathroom..." |
| **Problem Education** | Edgy, informative reframe of the problem itself. | "Most women over 40 don't know their ___ drops 60% after menopause." |
| **Body Betrayal** | "Your body is doing this *to* you, not because of you." | "Your gut stopped working the day you started ___." |
| **Timeline Shock** | "This has been quietly getting worse for years." | "Three years ago I noticed my part getting wider..." |

---

### Solution-Aware Angles

The avatar has tried things. Failed. The angle has to explain *why* the failures happened — and make that explanation point toward our mechanism.

| Angle | What It Says | Example Hook Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Failed Solutions** | "You've tried everything. Here's why none of it worked." | "I spent $2,000 trying to fix this. Here's what finally did." |
| **Wrong Mechanism** | "Every solution you've tried targets the wrong thing." | "Biotin doesn't fix hair loss. Here's what does." |
| **Category Fraud** | "The entire category is misleading you." | "The supplement industry has been lying about ___." |
| **Stacking Mistake** | "You're doing the right things in the wrong order / combination." | "Taking ___ without ___ is why it's not working." |
| **Diagnosis Reframe** | "What you think you have, you don't." | "This isn't bloating. It's ___." |

---

### Product-Aware Angles

The avatar has tried a product like ours. The angle has to differentiate *us* from the category.

| Angle | What It Says | Example Hook Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Ours vs. Theirs** | Direct comparison, specific dimension. | "Most ___ gummies have 2mg. Ours has 400mg." |
| **Formulation Exposé** | "The version you've been sold is a watered-down fake." | "What's actually in your ___? Mostly filler." |
| **Bioavailability** | "Yours isn't absorbing. Here's why ours does." | "If it's not ___, your body can't use it." |
| **Proof Density** | Stack testimonials/data until skepticism breaks. | "Here are 847 women who tried ___ and what happened." |
| **Founder/Origin Story** | "I built this because the existing options failed me." | "I was the customer before I was the founder." |

---

### Most-Aware Angles

The avatar has bought from us or is actively considering. The angle carries urgency, identity, or community.

| Angle | What It Says | Example Hook Direction |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity Reinforcement** | "People like you use this." | "Women who finally took their health seriously use ___." |
| **Scarcity / Urgency** | Real inventory or time-bound reason to act now. | "We're restocking Monday. Last batch sold out in 6 days." |
| **Offer Stack** | Bundle, free gift, risk reversal. | "180 days to try it. If it doesn't work, full refund." |
| **Community Proof** | "X thousand people are already in." | "Join the 40,000 women who ___." |
| **Loyalty Callback** | For existing customers. | "You loved ___. We just launched ___." |

## Mapping Angle → Beat Structure

Every angle shows up strongest in specific beats of the 8-beat structure:

| Angle Family | Strongest Beats |
|---|---|
| Doctor Dismissal | Hook, Problem Agitation, UMP |
| Hidden Cause | Hook, UMP |
| Failed Solutions | Solution Dismissal, UMS |
| Wrong Mechanism | Solution Dismissal, UMP, UMS |
| Ours vs. Theirs | UMS, Solution Introduction |
| Identity Reinforcement | Close |
| Proof Density | Objection Handling, Close |
| Founder/Origin | Problem Agitation, Solution Introduction |

If you know the angle, you know which beats get the most runtime.

## Rotation Strategy

Don't run one angle forever. Rotate angle families across sub-avatars:

1. Pick 3–5 sub-avatars inside the main avatar.
2. Assign each sub-avatar its dominant angle family.
3. Write 2–3 ad variations per angle (different hooks, same angle, same concept).
4. Scale the winners. Retire the angles that saturate.
5. When an angle fatigues, swap the *concept* before swapping the *angle* — same big idea, new format (UGC → static → founder → authority).

---

## Angle Saturation Warning Signs

- CTR holds but CPA climbs → angle still works, creative fatigued (swap concept)
- CTR drops across all concepts → angle saturated (swap angle)
- Hook hold rate drops but watch time stays → hook fatigued, angle fine (swap hook, same angle)
- Add-to-cart drops but CTR stays → solution dismissal or UMS weakening (rewrite those beats)
