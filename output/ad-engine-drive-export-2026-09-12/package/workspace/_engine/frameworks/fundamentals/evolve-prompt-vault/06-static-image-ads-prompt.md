Start here: You are going to be helping me write an ad brief to create a winning Facebook & Instagram image ad. A winning Facebook & Instagram image ad is defined as a bold and scroll-stopping image with a powerful headline that ultimately allows the company to increase advertising spend profitably, and acquire more new customers.

Please keep in mind that these image ads will need to be focused on specifically attracting NEW CUSTOMERS as ads that can attract and convert new customers are the only ads we are focused on making. So when writing these Facebook & Instagram ads, keep in mind the target avatar we will be focusing on will likely have never heard of our brand and does not care who we are. They only care about themselves. So this image ad has to appeal to them and show them we understand them.

I now need you to ask me the questions below, one at a time, to gather the data prior to writing the brief:

**Research**
1. Please upload your cleaned research document, reviews, and any other resources to better understand this brand

**Avatar**
2. Please upload the target avatar for this ad

**Product**
3. Provide the product name for this ad and a brief description of it (make sure to include unique features, benefits etc.)

**Final Confirmation**
4. Confirm with the user that they are ready to proceed with the static ads.

**Ad Writing Instructions For AI - Step 1**

Using all the data and insights you've gathered so far, you will now write nine headlines for the selected image ad. Ensure you leverage the data in the research document, writing it for the specific avatar, the correct product, and following the tone. Also, make sure the written headlines are short (6-8 words max) and are written with one key objective in mind: **to stop the scroll of our target avatar and grab their attention to learn more and ultimately purchase from our company**

For each ad concept:
- Reference the example ad from below that you used to come up with the headline and how it works for this brand and avatar specifically
- Briefly explain why this headline will stop our potential avatar's scroll
- Provide 2 other iterations of that main headline for me to choose from that follow the same idea.

In order to ensure this ad is a scroll-stopping image ad that the target avatar remembers, and gets them to convert, I will need you to understand what these types of image ads look like.

So please follow the examples below of headlines and image descriptions from world-class winning ads that have been written by the top advertisers in the world, and have been proven to convert.

**Example Image Ad #1: Ryanair**
Core Headline: "The world's cheapest airline."
Bold solid blue background; symmetrical layout. Left: open pizza box, unremarkable pizza, text "PIZZA $19.99". Right: identical box containing a grayscale Leaning Tower of Pisa, text "LONDON → PISA $19.99" with a small Ryanair plane graphic between the cities. Center: bold "vs." Bottom: tagline "The world's cheapest airline" with "cheapest" highlighted in yellow, Ryanair logo. Humor + surprise: a flight costs as much as a pizza.

**Example Image Ad #2: Tesla**
Core Headline: "It takes 3.1 seconds to read this ad."
Clean white background. Top: bold black line "It takes 3.1 seconds to read this ad." with smaller gray line "The same time it takes a Model S to go from 0 to 60 mph." Middle: black Model S side profile, rear motion-blurred to convey acceleration. Bottom: Tesla logo. Clever time comparison ties reading the ad to the car's performance.

**Example Image Ad #3: Nutella**
Core Headline: "This is bread. This is breakfast."
White background, symmetrical. Left: plain toasted bread, "This is bread." Right: same toast with glossy Nutella spread in a heart shape, "This is breakfast." Tagline: "Turning bread into breakfast since 1964." Nutella logo bottom center. Simple transformation contrast.

**Example Image Ad #4: Patrón**
Core Headline: "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take."
White premium background. Headline in bold black serif — double entendre on tequila shots and the motivational quote. Center: Patrón Silver bottle with cork stopper and ribbon, shot glass with ice cube, lime wedge. Bottom: Patrón bee logo. Sophisticated yet playful.

**Example Image Ad #5: Bible App**
Core Headline: "Zero stars. Would not recommend."
Subway interior ad panel, black background, white text. Headline "Zero stars." above a row of five empty outlined stars, then "Would not recommend." attributed to "- Satan". Bottom: Holy Bible icon + "Bible App". Humor via review-culture subversion — even Satan disapproves, implying the app works.

**Example Image Ad #6: Chipotle**
Core Headline: "Usually, when you roll something this good, it's illegal."
Pure white background. Top: tightly foil-wrapped burrito, plump and appetizing. Middle: the headline in casual black hand-drawn font — wordplay likening burrito-rolling to rolling something illicit. Bottom: Chipotle logo on black stamp-like rectangle, "Pass it on. Find yours @ chipotle.com."

**Example Image Ad #7: Corona**
Core Headline: "Good things take lime."
Clear blue sky, beach and ocean background, weathered wooden surface. Golden Corona bottle glistening with water droplets, lime wedge in the neck. Headline in clean white serif — play on "good things take time". Corona logo with gold crown below.

**Example Image Ad #8: Evercool**
Core Headline: "Need better way to stay cool?"
Dim kitchen lit by an open, fully-stocked refrigerator. A young woman lies on the floor, head on a pillow, sleeping next to the open fridge in tank top and shorts — exaggerated, relatable heat desperation. Bottom-right: folded bright blue Evercool™ Cooling Comforter. Headline in bold white to the right; product name bottom left. Humor sets up the product as the sane solution.

**Example Image Ad #9: Daihatsu**
Core Headline: "Picks up five times more women than a Lamborghini."
Plain white background. A boxy silver Daihatsu Hijet MPV loaded with six cheerful passengers — five laughing women, one proud man leaning out the driver's window. Headline in bold black sans-serif; vertical red Daihatsu banner right. Cheeky comparison: practicality beats flash.

**Example Image Ad #10: Surreal**
Core Headline: "We're Dwayne Johnson's favourite cereal."*
Billboard, salmon-pink background, bold black text. Asterisk fine print: *"Dwayne is a bus driver from London." Right: blue "Surreal – Frosted High-Protein Cereal" box with overflowing bowl. Celebrity-endorsement subversion; witty, self-aware.

**Example Image Ad #11: Huel**
Core Headline: "Grab life by the bowls."
Dark black background. Five black bowls of colorful meals in an arc, steam rising; front bowl a creamy mac-and-cheese-like dish. Wordplay on "grab life by the horns". Around the bowls: "Vegan", "27 vitamins & minerals", "25g protein", "Ready in 5 minutes". Huel logo bottom-left.

**Ad Writing Instructions For AI - Step 2**

Now after creating the nine hooks, please ask me to pick my top 3 hooks (or however many I'd like). After confirming, you will then write out the rest of the image ads describing the images and providing additional text for call outs, if needed, to have a complete concept ready to send over to our editors.

You can use the examples above as a reference for how to describe the image ad and how our editors should bring it to life, however, please keep it more condensed than the examples above.

**Final Rules To Consider For Image Ads:**

I want you to consider the following rules prior to writing the headlines and final image ads to ensure they tick all of these boxes:

1. Assume our target avatar does NOT know about us and does not CARE about us.
2. Congruency for imagery suggestions must match and emphasize the headline
3. Make people FEEL something. Make people feel like you understand them.
4. Be Clear and be concise and keep your language simple
