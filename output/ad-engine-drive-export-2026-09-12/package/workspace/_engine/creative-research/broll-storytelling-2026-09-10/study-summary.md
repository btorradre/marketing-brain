## Core decisions encoded

1. Problem recognition/agitation defaults to believable people in ordinary situations: the relevant gesture, interrupted task or contradiction. Source real footage where appropriate or generate a phone-style reenactment. Keep face-only delivery when it is already doing the human job.
2. Scientific visuals explain a specific process with a before-state, visible action and resulting state. Different ideas need different appropriate views, not repeated glowing torsos.
3. Ingredient identity, solution action, product reveal, evidence and CTA are separate visual jobs.
4. Full-frame, inset, split view and staying on the presenter are deliberate choices. Preserve podcast questions/reactions and UGC identity.
5. Cut at semantic cues and allow the action to resolve. Captions changing does not automatically require another scene.
6. Payoff shows regained capability in new footage; compare actual images for variety, not filenames.
