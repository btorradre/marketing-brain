## 1. Weekender greenscreen v3 (THE EXEMPLAR, top performer)

88 words · 27.9s · 189 wpm · greenscreen VO, word-synced captions, PDP screen-recording close

> **[HOOK]** If you're looking for the perfect summer travel bag, I have the one for you.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO]** This is the Velantra Weekender.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's Birkin-inspired, structured, real brass hardware, but actually built for a weekend trip.
> **[PROOF]** This one holds its shape even packed full. I've thrown it in the trunk every weekend this summer, and it still looks brand new.
> **[CAPACITY]** It actually fits everything: two outfits, shoes, your toiletries.
> **[VARIANTS]** It comes in two different colors.
> **[OFFER]** They're having a summer sale right now, and colorways go fast.
> **[CTA]** I left the link below.

**Why it works.** The hook is a qualifying question that hands the viewer the verdict in the same breath. The brand name lands at 3.8 seconds. Every attribute is a physical fact of the Weekender and none of them port to another bag. The proof beat is a lived, time-stamped survival claim rather than an adjective. Capacity is three concrete nouns. The offer is a real sale on the real page, shown in an actual screen recording.

**B-roll sequence used:** hero shot on a bench, a hand lifting it by the handles, brass hardware macro shot on a bed, hands working the belt strap on a packed bag, sitting in an open car trunk, packed interior open showing a sweater/jeans/pouch, two colorways side by side on a counter, then the product-page screen recording with price and Add to Cart visible.

## 2. Sofia Woven Tote — fall straw seasonal angle

~105 words · ~33s · greenscreen VO

> **[HOOK]** Fall is almost here, and everyone I know swaps out their straw bags for suede and leather. I think I found the one that stays out all year.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO]** This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's Birkin-inspired, so it's fully structured, it holds its shape, and the flap folds all the way over the top, so nothing falls out of it.
> **[CAPACITY]** It fits everything: your wallet, a water bottle, a sweater for when it gets cold.
> **[PROOF, styling variant]** And the caramel with boots and a chunky knit, it just works.
> **[OFFER]** They're having a fall sale right now, and the colorways go fast.
> **[CTA]** I left the link below.

**Note the "so" chain in the stack**: each attribute carries its own consequence. Structured *so* it holds its shape. Flap folds over *so* nothing falls out. That is the pattern worth copying.

**Weakness in this example:** it has no genuinely lived proof beat, substituting a styling beat instead — a weaker choice than a true survival/proof claim. The voiceover read also mispronounced the brand name as "Volantra," a reminder to proofread every generated read.

## 3. Sofia Woven Tote — bottom-of-funnel "summer winding down" (a COUNTER-EXAMPLE — what NOT to do)

126 words · 38-42s · greenscreen VO, 14 visual slots

> **[HOOK]** Okay, if you've been thinking about the [nickname] of summer, this is your sign.
> **[SCARCITY, early]** Summer is winding down and the colors are starting to sell out.
> **[PROOF]** So I've been carrying mine all season and I'm still obsessed.
> **[ATTRIBUTE STACK]** It's hand woven, it's structured so it holds its shape, and the leather is all hand set.
> **[CAPACITY / USE]** You just throw everything in and go. Beach, errands, dinner, whatever.
> **[EXTENSION]** And honestly, it carries straight into fall.
> **[SCARCITY]** Two of the colors are already gone, and I wouldn't wait on the rest.
> **[VARIANTS]** This is the caramel, the one everyone asks me about.
> **[OFFER + CTA]** There's still so much summer left to carry this bag. I've left the link below. The summer sale is still on right now, so go get yours before it's gone.

**This script violates the product-intro rule** and is kept specifically as a counter-example. It never actually says "This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra" — it only ever refers to the bag by an informal nickname, so the entire benefit stack accrues to a generic category term instead of to the actual brand. Do not repeat this pattern.

It's also a reminder that scarcity claims must stay true: the stock claim ("two of the colors are already gone") later became factually false once inventory changed — exactly the failure mode the "verify against the live page" rule exists to prevent.

## 4. Sofia Woven Tote — product-aware / demonstrated proof (30s organic review)

77 words · 29.8s · 163 wpm · one continuous handheld take, captions on thesis beats only

> **[HOOK, doubt + reversal]** I thought this woven tote was gonna fall apart. I was so wrong.
> **[PRODUCT INTRO + ATTRIBUTE STACK]** This is the Sofia Woven Tote from Velantra and I'm obsessed. It's hand woven, no logo on it anywhere, and it fits my sweater, a bottle, my wallet and a book.
> **[PROOF, demonstrated]** And then watch, I set it down and take my hands right off.
> *[hold, 1.5 seconds, nothing said]*
> **[PAYOFF]** Look at that. It's holding all that on its own, not leaning on anything.
> **[VERDICT]** I haven't carried anything else since.

**The product-aware variant.** Doubt and its reversal both land in the first five seconds, opening the loop, and everything after functions as the answer to "why were you wrong." Capacity is folded into the attribute stack to save runtime. Offer and variants are dropped entirely because the runtime can't hold them — which is the correct order of sacrifice per the formula (cut beats 5-6 before ever touching 1, 2, 3, or 8).

**Demo formats run slower** — 163 words per minute here versus 189 on a straight-talking greenscreen read, because hands are performing an action on camera and the payoff moment needs a beat of silence over it.
