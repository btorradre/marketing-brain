## Video direction

- Palette and type: warm ivory `bg-primary` frames the photography; ink `text-primary` carries New York display copy; Abel body/labels use `text-secondary`; `accent` is reserved for restrained rules and labels; all colors and roles come directly from `frame.md`.
- Motion grammar: smooth long-tail settles (`power3` default), no bounce. Because there is no voiceover, reveal each piece on a distinct musical phrase or percussion cue and spread those reveals through the back half of each shot. Product pixels stay unchanged; movement comes from crop, position, scale, masks, and layout rather than color filters.
- Rhythm and holds: Frames 2 and 4 are deliberate calm breathers after more active reveals. Frame 5 gives the logo and URL the longest held read. Once each shot resolves, it stays still; subtle jitter is permitted only on one held image layer if the frame otherwise reads dead.
- Cross-format safety: the primary bag, headline, wordmark, and URL remain inside the central safe region and above the bottom 17% keep-out so the 16:9 master can be adapted to 1:1 and 9:16 without losing meaning.
- Negative list: no gradients, rounded cards, drop shadows, third-party logos, fake UI, discounts, prices, unsupported claims, product reshaping, aggressive zooms, motion blur over the leather, lazy breathing, back-half camera drift, slideshow front-load-and-freeze, or screensaver-like independent floating.
