## The actual editing pattern

The ad uses narration to connect unlike images: a familiar object, an internal explanation, then a human consequence. It changes the picture at a new noun, contrast or repeated phrase. The opening and end card are comparatively slow; the escalation and reassurance inserts can be under a second. That variation creates rhythm. Frequent caption replacements and motion inside shots supply additional visual events without requiring a new clip. Engagement effects here are editorial interpretations; no retention or conversion data was supplied.

The reference itself includes substantial people footage. Its useful lesson is the role assigned to each image, not simply an anatomy percentage. Motilli keeps the user-requested heavier scientific balance and reserves people for the four everyday activities.
