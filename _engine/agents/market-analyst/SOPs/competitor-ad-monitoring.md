# SOP: Meta Ad Library Monitoring & Competitor Analysis

## Objective
Systematically monitor competitor brands in the Meta Ad Library (Facebook, Instagram) to track active angles, hooks, mechanisms, villain archetypes, and product integration strategies. Identify angle saturation, mechanism effectiveness, and blue ocean opportunities before competitors saturate them.

## What to Extract from Each Ad

### 1. Ad Metadata
- **Brand:** [From list above]
- **Ad ID:** [Meta Ad Library ID if available]
- **Platform:** [Facebook | Instagram | Both]
- **Format:** [Long-form copy | Carousel | Video | Story | Static image]
- **Visible Run Time:** [Days/weeks/months ad has been running based on library indicators]
- **Targeting Signals:** [Age, gender, geography, interests if visible in library]

### 2. Hook Analysis
**Goal:** Capture the exact opening line and hook mechanism.

Extract:
- **Hook Text:** Exact first line (max 20 words)
- **Hook Type:** [[DR-SOP-Obsidian.md|Hook category]] — Curiosity | Villain reveal | Problem statement | Transformation promise | Social proof | Statistics/data | Question | Benefit statement
- **Emotional Trigger:** What emotion does it activate? (Fear | Desire | Curiosity | Anger | Hope)
- **Specificity Level:** Generic ("Do you have pain?") vs. Specific ("If you can't feel your feet after 10 years...")

Example:
- **Hook Text:** "If your doctor said there's nothing else they can do for neuropathy..."
- **Hook Type:** Villain reveal (doctor helplessness)
- **Emotional Trigger:** Frustration, hope
- **Specificity Level:** High (specific medical situation)

### 3. Angle Identification
**Goal:** Identify the core angle the ad is using.

Extract:
- **Primary Angle:** What is the central argument/promise? (Reference [[angle-saturation-map.md|angle saturation map]] for comparison)
- **Angle Specificity:** What makes this angle distinct from competitors?
- **Angle Fatigue Indicator:** Is this angle oversaturated (running across multiple competitors) or emerging?
- **Blue Ocean Opportunity:** Is there a gap in the angle landscape? (For reporting)

Example angles by niche:
- **Cholesterol:** "Why medications fail," "Food synergy approach," "Doctor-approved alternatives"
- **Neuropathy:** "Why gabapentin stops working," "Nerve regeneration," "Systemic vs. symptomatic"
- **Menopause:** "Hormone balance without HRT," "Thermoregulation mechanism," "Natural alternatives science-backed"
- **Back Pain:** "Why rest doesn't work," "Core activation without pain," "Structural realignment"
- **Skincare:** "Collagen rebuilding mechanism," "Age reversal without surgery," "Cellular renewal"

### 4. Mechanism Analysis
**Goal:** Extract the mechanism of action being claimed/implied.

Extract:
- **Mechanism Type:** How does the product/approach work? (Ingredient | Process | System | Behavioral | Combination)
- **Mechanism Specificity:** Vague ("supports joint health") vs. Specific ("activates collagen cross-linking through [specific ingredient]")
- **Logical Coherence Score:** 1-9 (How well does the mechanism make logical sense? Is it believable?)
  - 1-3: Vague, unclear, seems implausible
  - 4-6: Reasonable but lacks specificity or proof
  - 7-9: Clear, specific, backed by science or obvious logic
- **Market Resonance Score:** 1-9 (How much does this mechanism resonate with target avatar?)
  - 1-3: Not resonant; avatar doesn't care about this mechanism
  - 4-6: Somewhat resonant; interesting but not compelling alone
  - 7-9: Highly resonant; avatar would choose product based on this mechanism
- **Combined Score:** Add Logical Coherence + Market Resonance (target 13-18 for winning ads)
- **Novelty:** Is this mechanism new, emerging, or oversaturated in the market?

Example:
- **Mechanism Type:** Ingredient (specific compound targeting nerve repair)
- **Specificity:** "Alpha-lipoic acid + L-carnitine combination triggers mitochondrial energy in damaged nerve cells"
- **Logical Coherence:** 8 (specific, cited in research, makes biological sense)
- **Market Resonance:** 9 (neuropathy avatars desperate for nerve regeneration, not just symptom relief)
- **Combined Score:** 17 (strong mechanism)
- **Novelty:** Emerging (not yet saturated, few competitors using this combination)

### 5. Villain Identification
**Goal:** Identify what villain/problem the ad is positioning against.

Extract:
- **Primary Villain:** What's the enemy? (Doctor helplessness | Medication failure | System failure | Symptom-only thinking | Age/genetics | Laziness | Willpower)
- **Villain Archetype:** [[DR-SOP-Obsidian.md|Villain types]] — System failure (medical, healthcare) | Villain character (person/entity blamed) | False belief (what avatar wrongly believes)
- **Villain Specificity:** Generic ("doctors don't care") vs. Specific ("doctors only address symptoms, not root cause")
- **Emotional Resonance:** Does this villain trigger the avatar's frustration? (High resonance = stronger ad)
- **Differentiation:** Is this the same villain as competitors or unique?

Example:
- **Primary Villain:** Medication tolerance (gabapentin stops working over time)
- **Archetype:** System failure (medical approach is flawed)
- **Specificity:** "Gabapentin works by numbing sensation, but after 1-2 years your body adapts and it stops working. Actual nerve repair requires a different approach."
- **Emotional Resonance:** High (matches Stage 3 avatar experience exactly)
- **Differentiation:** Unique positioning (most competitors blame doctor ignorance, not medication design)

### 6. Belief Shift Identification
**Goal:** Identify what belief the ad is trying to shift.

Extract:
- **Current Belief (Avatar's):** What does the avatar currently believe? ("Nothing works," "My condition is permanent," "I need to accept this")
- **New Belief (Ad's Goal):** What belief does the ad want them to adopt? ("There's a mechanism that actually works," "Nerve regeneration is possible," "I can reverse this")
- **Belief Shift Mechanism:** How does the ad facilitate the shift? (Data | Testimonial | Mechanism education | Villain revelation | Social proof)
- **Resonance:** Does this belief shift match the avatar's readiness? (Stage 3 avatars = ready for "system is flawed" shift; Stage 4 = ready for "mechanism alternative exists" shift)

Example:
- **Current Belief:** "My neuropathy is permanent and will only get worse"
- **New Belief:** "Nerve regeneration is possible through targeted supplementation; I can reverse this"
- **Shift Mechanism:** Mechanism education (showing how Alpha-lipoic acid + L-carnitine repairs nerve cells) + testimonial (real person recovered function)
- **Stage Resonance:** Stage 4 (ready to try alternative mechanisms)

### 7. Product Integration Analysis
**Goal:** Identify where/how the product is positioned in the funnel.

Extract:
- **Integration Position:** Early (part of awareness/education), Middle (solving the villain), Late (the hero/solution), or Multi-stage (integrated throughout)
- **Integration Style:** Ingredient-focused | Lifestyle-focused | Problem-solving-focused | Outcome-focused
- **Product Reveal Timing:** Early in ad | Mid-ad | Late/CTA only
- **Product Framing:** Cost-benefit framed? Scarcity framed? Guarantees framed? Result-centric? Risk-reversal?
- **Copy Length to Product Reveal:** What percentage of ad copy/video before product name appears?

Example:
- **Integration Position:** Late-stage (ad focuses on problem/villain, product revealed as solution in final 20%)
- **Style:** Problem-solving-focused (how does it solve neuropathy pain specifically?)
- **Reveal Timing:** 80% into ad copy
- **Framing:** Result-centric ("Users report 60% reduction in pain within 6 weeks") + risk-reversal ("30-day money-back guarantee")
- **Copy to Reveal Ratio:** 85/15 (85% problem education, 15% product-focused)

### 8. Awareness Level Assessment
**Goal:** Identify what awareness level the ad targets.

Using [[DR-SOP-Obsidian.md|awareness levels]]:

- **Level 1:** "Unaware" — Target doesn't know they have the problem yet
- **Level 2:** "Problem Aware" — Knows they have pain/issue but not familiar with solutions
- **Level 3:** "Solution Aware, Dissatisfied" — Knows about standard solutions but frustrated they don't work
- **Level 4:** "Product Aware" — Knows your specific product or category exists
- **Level 5:** "Most Aware" — Has used product or similar, needs reminder/new angle

Determine by: Does the ad educate about the problem (Levels 1-2), educate about why solutions fail (Level 3), or educate about why THIS solution works (Levels 4-5)?

## Mechanism Scoring Framework

### Scoring Process (Detailed)

For every ad, score the mechanism on two dimensions:

#### 1. Logical Coherence (1-9)
**Question:** Does the mechanism make logical/scientific sense?

- **1-3: Implausible**
  - Mechanism contradicts basic science
  - Claim is vague or hand-wavy
  - No logical pathway shown
  - Example: "Magical crystals absorb pain"

- **4-6: Plausible but Weak**
  - Mechanism makes sense but lacks specificity
  - General claim without detail
  - Sounds reasonable but not rigorously explained
  - Example: "Supports joint health" (true but vague)

- **7-9: Coherent & Specific**
  - Clear, specific pathway explained
  - Aligns with known science
  - Avatar can understand why it works
  - Ideally backed by data or obvious logic
  - Example: "Alpha-lipoic acid (ALA) is a mitochondrial antioxidant that reduces oxidative stress on damaged nerve cells, proven in [study] to improve pain by 43%"

**Scoring Tip:** Higher scores require specificity. Generic = low score. Specific + plausible = high score.

#### 2. Market Resonance (1-9)
**Question:** How much does this mechanism matter to the target avatar?

- **1-3: Low Resonance**
  - Avatar doesn't care about this mechanism
  - Not addressing their actual pain point
  - Feels like a feature, not a benefit
  - Example: "Contains organic ingredients" (avatars with neuropathy care about pain relief, not organic certification)

- **4-6: Medium Resonance**
  - Avatar thinks this is nice but not essential
  - Credible but not emotionally compelling
  - Addresses part of the problem, not the core
  - Example: "Reduces inflammation" (helpful for joint pain, but avatar really wants mobility back)

- **7-9: High Resonance**
  - Avatar desperately wants this mechanism to work
  - Directly addresses core pain/outcome they desire
  - Emotionally compelling + logical
  - Example: "Triggers nerve regeneration, potentially reversing neuropathy" (avatar's deepest desire: reverse the condition, not just manage pain)

**Scoring Tip:** High resonance comes from mechanisms that promise what avatars actually want, not what science can do.

#### Combined Score
- **13-18:** Winning mechanism (high coherence + high resonance)
- **10-12:** Moderate mechanism (decent coherence, medium resonance or vice versa)
- **7-9:** Weak mechanism (low resonance or incoherent)
- **2-6:** Poor mechanism (avoid or rework)

### Scoring Example: amala_health Ad on Cholesterol

**Ad Hook:** "The cholesterol medication your doctor won't talk about..."

**Mechanism Identified:** "Plant-derived compound (policosanol) inhibits HMG-CoA reductase in liver cells, reducing cholesterol synthesis without statin side effects"

**Logical Coherence: 7**
- Specific and scientific (HMG-CoA reductase is real target)
- Claim is verifiable (statin side effect avoidance is known)
- Minor: Doesn't explain why this is safer than statins at cellular level
- Score: 7 (specific + coherent, but could be more detailed on safety mechanism)

**Market Resonance: 8**
- Avatar pain: "Statins cause side effects (muscle pain, memory issues) but I need to control cholesterol"
- This mechanism directly promises "same benefit, no side effects"
- Highly resonant (avatar's core conflict: manage cholesterol without suffering)
- Score: 8 (not 9 because some avatars are satisfied with statins)

**Combined Score: 15** (Winning mechanism — high on both dimensions)

## Angle Saturation Tracking

### Process
1. **Catalog Angle** — Identify primary angle in each ad (reference [[angle-saturation-map.md|angle saturation map]])
2. **Track Frequency** — How many competitors are using this angle?
   - 1 competitor: Emerging angle (blue ocean)
   - 2-3 competitors: Moderate saturation
   - 4+ competitors: Saturated angle (red ocean)
3. **Assess Dominance** — Is one brand dominating the angle?
   - If one brand runs this angle heavily and wins, others struggle with same angle
   - Indicates angle belongs to that brand
4. **Note Variations** — Are competitors modifying the angle to differentiate?
   - Example: "Why gabapentin stops working" (base angle) → "Why gabapentin stops working and how nerve regeneration works" (variation)

## Blue Ocean Opportunity Identification

### Process
1. **Scan for Gaps** — Which pain points are mentioned in Reddit scrapes but NOT in competitor ads?
   - Example: Reddit mentions "isolation/loneliness with chronic pain" but no competitor ads position around social/emotional impact
2. **Mechanism Gaps** — Which mechanisms are mentioned in [[Mechanism Reference Library_ Top 100 Examples.md|top 100 mechanisms]] but not active in competitor ads?
   - Example: "Circadian rhythm reset" appears in mechanisms library but no competitor uses it for sleep/energy neuropathy pain
3. **Villain Gaps** — Which villain archetypes are underutilized?
   - Most competitors blame "doctor ignorance"; rare to blame "medication design flaws" or "symptom-only thinking"
4. **Demographic Gaps** — Are certain avatars underserved?
   - Example: Most ads target 45-65 age range; what about 65+ avatars?

### Output: Blue Ocean Opportunity Report

```markdown
## Blue Ocean Opportunity — [DATE]

### Opportunity 1: Emotional/Social Impact Angle
- **Gap:** Reddit shows strong isolation/relationship themes, but no competitor ads address emotional recovery
- **Mechanism:** Social re-engagement + pain relief (dual benefit)
- **Target:** Stage 4-5 avatars ready for life transformation
- **Recommendation:** High-priority creative brief for Creative Strategist

### Opportunity 2: Regeneration vs. Management Angle
- **Gap:** All competitors position as "pain relief"; none position as "tissue regeneration"
- **Mechanism:** Nerve/tissue regeneration (neuroplasticity + supplementation)
- **Target:** Stage 5 avatars (transformation-ready)
- **Recommendation:** Differentiation play; medium-priority

```

## Pro Tips for Accurate Monitoring

1. **Mechanism Scoring Consistency** — Always score both dimensions (Coherence + Resonance). One alone is misleading.

2. **Villain Specificity** — Generic villains ("doctors don't care") are everywhere. High-differentiation comes from specific villain positioning ("doctors address symptoms, not root cause").

3. **Angle Saturation Tracking** — Track exact angles, not loose categories. "Natural alternative" is broad. "Plant sterol alternative to statins" is tracked properly.

4. **Run Time Signals** — Meta Ad Library doesn't show exact spend, but run time (weeks/months) indicates success. Ads running 8+ weeks are validated performers.

5. **Blue Ocean Timing** — First mover on blue ocean angles wins. Mark with #urgent-creative-opportunity when you identify gaps before competitors fill them.

6. **Competitor Context** — Understanding WHY a competitor runs an angle helps predict when they'll pivot. If an angle is saturating, they'll move soon.

---

## Forbidden Patterns in Competitor Analysis

- **Copying exact copy** — Track angles/mechanisms/hooks, but never duplicate competitor ad copy verbatim
- **Ignoring Logical Coherence in favor of virality** — Just because an ad has high engagement doesn't mean the mechanism is sound
- **Assuming mechanism score = ad success** — High-scoring mechanism + poor execution = failure. Score mechanism separately from execution
- **Saturation blindness** — Don't dismiss an angle just because 2 competitors use it. Wait until 4+ before calling it saturated

---

## Notes

Competitor monitoring is not about copying. It's about understanding the landscape your creative will enter. What angles are saturated? What mechanisms resonate? What villains does the market respond to? What belief shifts are avatars ready for? Answer these, and you'll create differentiated creative that wins.
