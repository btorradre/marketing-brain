## Design Trends Spotted

### 1. Pain-First, Product-Second (Provitalize pattern)
The dominant trend in supplement statics right now is leading with the PAIN state, not the solution. Provitalize's entire static library is grayscale/muted pain imagery with editorial-style headlines. Product shots are reserved for the landing page.

### 2. Anti-Pharma Positioning
Multiple Provitalize ads position against Ozempic specifically. This is a macro trend — supplement brands are capitalizing on GLP-1 drug fatigue/fear to position natural alternatives.

### 3. Celebrity + Data Overlay (AG1 pattern)
AG1's approach of combining celebrity authority with specific percentage claims in a visual timeline is a premium but effective format. Requires access to survey/clinical data.

### 4. Multi-Page Risk Diversification
Provitalize running from 3+ pages is a sophisticated risk management strategy. If one page gets flagged or shut down, the others continue. Also enables different brand voice testing.

### 5. Page Deletions in Weight Loss
GOLO's page disappearance signals increasing Meta enforcement in the weight loss supplement space. Brands making direct weight loss claims on Meta are at higher risk.
