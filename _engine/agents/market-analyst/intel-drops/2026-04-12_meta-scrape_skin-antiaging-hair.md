## Synthesis & Recommendations

### Angles Worth Testing
1. **"Self-feeding loop" mechanism framing** — Works across niches. The Innerfuel ad shows how to position the problem as a runaway cycle, then position the product as the circuit breaker.
2. **"Thermostat Problem" reframe** (Vono Labs) — Strong contrarian angle for menopause. Could test similar "wrong organ" reframes for other conditions.
3. **Cultural contrast hooks** (Sarah's Wellness Journey) — "Korean women don't get hot flashes" is scroll-stopping. Could test for other conditions where geographic/cultural differences exist.

### Mechanism Innovations Spotted
- **CD38 enzyme as named villain** — Novel mechanism for fibromyalgia/energy niche. Most ads in this space use generic "inflammation" or "mitochondria." CD38 is specific and testable.
- **NAD+ depletion narrative** — Growing scientific basis. Innerfuel is riding the longevity/biohacking wave but applying it to fibromyalgia specifically.

### Market Signals
- **Video dominance:** Most ads in these niches are VIDEO format with shorter primary text hooks, not the 1,000+ word long-form narrative text ads. The format may be shifting.
- **Mechanism-education hooks:** Successful ads leading with mechanism education in the primary text (200-400 words) then driving to advertorial/VSL landing pages.
- **Doctor persona saturation:** Multiple brands using "Dr. X" authority positioning. Still working but getting crowded.
- **Menopause niche heating up:** Vono Labs (28 ads), Sarah's Wellness Journey, and others all competing in menopause space with differentiated mechanism angles.

### Priority Recommendations for Creative Strategist
1. **Test CD38/NAD+ mechanism** for any energy/fatigue/inflammation products — this is a fresh angle with scientific credibility
2. **Consider video-first format** for new ads — the market is moving toward video hooks with shorter primary text
3. **"Wrong problem" reframes** are working across niches — worth developing for our products
