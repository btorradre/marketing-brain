## Synthesis & Observations

### Key Patterns in Neuropathy Niche (March 2026)
1. **Spouse narrators remain dominant.** The Susan Bridgers ads use the classic wife-tells-husband's-story frame. This angle continues to perform — it adds emotional distance and removes the "too good to be true" objection that first-person miracle stories trigger.

2. **$37 price anchor is aggressive.** Carrie Brown and Dr. Robert Thompson are both leading with a specific dollar amount in the hook. This is a product-aware/most-aware play — skipping the narrative entirely and going straight to offer. Suggests they're retargeting or working a warmed audience.

3. **Device brands are in the mix.** EMSense appears to be a TENS/EMS device brand using narrative copy to sell hardware. This is worth watching — device brands in the neuropathy space tend to have different mechanism education needs than supplement brands.

4. **~6,700 active results** for "neuropathy relief" with image/meme filter = a crowded niche. Sophistication stage is likely Stage 4-5 (mechanism/experience saturated). Winning requires either novel mechanisms or exceptional narrative craft.
