## Synthesis & Recommendations

### Angles Worth Testing

1. **"Three things happening right now" scaffolding** — Promises structured education, creates curiosity gap, implicitly positions the narrator as someone with a system. Could be adapted for any niche where the target has tried multiple solutions and is cynical. The structure: "Because there are three things happening right now: One — [hidden mechanism]. Two — [why previous solutions failed]. Three — [the specific reason this keeps happening]."

2. **Extended-qualifier compound hook** — Layering every behavior the target avatar engages in into a single long sentence so they recognize themselves immediately. "If you are [X] and you keep telling yourself [Y], [Z], [A], [B] and still [problem]... I'm about to tell you something nobody in the [industry] world will ever say to you." This compresses avatar-targeting into the first 50 words and earns the read-through.

3. **Targeting the spouse instead of the sufferer** — In niches with a primary caregiver or enabler (addiction, dementia, chronic illness), writing copy to the person next to the patient rather than the patient themselves opens a different emotional wound (helplessness, shame, repeated betrayal) and often a wealthier, more-likely-to-buy audience.

4. **"Mineral Touch Method" curiosity-gap naming** — Naming a mechanism without explaining it, then handing off to a landing page. This works when the short-form ad is part of a longer funnel (advertorial or VSL behind it). Not a standalone long-form technique.

### Mechanism Innovations Spotted

- **Brain-chemistry reframe of addiction:** Positioning addiction as dopamine receptor degeneration + BDNF deficiency rather than a willpower/moral failure. This is a significant sophistication jump for the addiction category, which has historically been dominated by 12-step ("it's a disease of the spirit") and behavioral-therapy ("change your environment") frameworks. A supplement positioned as a BDNF restorer is mechanism-compatible with the reframe.

- **Structural-incapability villain (willpower):** "Her brain is fighting a battle that willpower alone was never equipped to win" is the same villain architecture as Rootofnature's gabapentin positioning — the standard treatment is reframed as the wrong category of intervention, not just insufficient. This is a reusable villain template: identify the target's current solution, then attack it at the mechanism-compatibility level rather than the efficacy level.
