## Design Trends Spotted

1. **Ingredient Education is King:** Neurosmile leads with mechanism education right in the static (NGF, absorption, clinical doses)

2. **Problem-First for GLP-1 Niche:** GLP-1 SOS uses native/UGC imagery showing the problem rather than polished product shots

3. **Lifestyle Staging:** Auri excels at product-in-context photography (kitchen counter, morning routine vibes)

4. **Bold Headlines + Subhead Structure:** Both Neurosmile and Auri use large headline + supporting subhead format

5. **Specific Claims Win:** "10,000mg Lion's Mane Extract", "250mg Magnesium + Ginger Root" - specific dosages build credibility
