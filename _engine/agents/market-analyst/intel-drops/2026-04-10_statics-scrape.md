## Key Observations & Trends

1. **The "branded static" category continues to shrink in supplement advertising.** Across two rotation days (Thursday AG1/GOLO/Provitalize, Friday Arrae/Nuora/NVH), zero brands ran the 8 classic branded-static archetypes (ingredient callouts, bold-claim pricing overlays, competitor comparison charts, testimonial cards). The entire vertical has shifted toward long narrative copy paired with either UGC photos or shock/clinical imagery.

2. **myNuora has cloned its video formula into static image ads** — same Alicia Darling first-person voice, same checklist hooks, same "my doctor/my friend/my daughter" vulnerability opens, now in image format. This is worth capturing as a **native-static swipe category** even though it doesn't qualify as branded-static.

3. **North Valley Health Clinic is the breakout format** — clinical-authority narrative ads with gross-out medical imagery are a distinct archetype worth documenting. This is #7 (Medical/Clinical Proof) executed at a level that's closer to editorial content than a polished product ad. The visual shock does the "stop the scroll" job the way a pricing overlay used to.

4. **All qualifying NVH ads launched Apr 9-10, 2026** — meaning the clinic is in active creative testing right now. Next week's scrape should re-check NVH to see which variants scaled.

5. **Copy + image decoupling is the new branded static.** Instead of text burned onto the image, these brands are putting the persuasion in the primary text (hook + story) and using the image purely for pattern interrupt. The image is no longer the ad — it's the scroll-stop.
