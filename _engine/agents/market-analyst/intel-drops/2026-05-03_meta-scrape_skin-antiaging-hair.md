## Ad-Level Analysis

### Ad: bloom_and_bond_01 — "Your GLP-1 f*cked your hair. And 'just eat more protein' is making it worse."

**Hook Analysis**
- Hook type: contrarian-dismissal + behavior-mirror hybrid. The profanity ("f*cked") signals raw vulnerability; the "just eat more protein is making it worse" line is a direct attack on the dominant well-meaning advice.
- Awareness level: solution-aware → product-aware bridge. Reader already on a GLP-1, has heard "telogen effluvium = wait it out," but doesn't know why it's not working.
- Specificity layers: numeric (Wegovy 14 months, 41 pounds, 100g protein, 10,000 mcg biotin, $85 Nutrafol, 24 weeks 40% hair count, 2,000mg pumpkin seed extract). Behavioral (forced down protein even though appetite was killed; checking the drain; pulling out phone to show side-by-side photos). Temporal (months 1-12 timeline of Michelle's case).
- "See More" cut: lands right after "While you wait patiently for the shedding to stop." Open loop = "wait, why is *waiting* the problem?" Strong.

**Structural Analysis**
- Narrator: doctor (Dr. Patricia Sullivan, dermatologist, 17 years, specializing in hormonal hair loss).
- Narrative posture: calm-after-storm + reluctant-confession ("I need to tell you something about the advice you've probably already been given").
- Emotional temperature: contained-anger (at the medical system that gave incomplete advice) + exhausted-relief.
- Discovery vehicle: doctor's own clinical observation over the last two years — pattern recognition from "the appointments that break my heart."

**Mechanism Analysis**
- Cause-and-effect chain (plain language):
  1. Rapid fat loss on GLP-1 strips estrogen (fat cells produce/store estrogen)
  2. Estrogen shield around follicles disappears
  3. DHT (always present) gains direct access to follicle androgen receptors
  4. DHT miniaturizes follicles silently while telogen effluvium "shedding" makes patient focus elsewhere
  5. Shedding resolves; DHT damage continues; regrowth comes in thin/wispy
- Named framework: "Dual-action DHT protection" (production block + receptor block).
- Villain types: structurally-incapable (the "just wait" advice — not wrong, just incomplete) + outdated-system (doctors trained on telogen effluvium model alone) + fake-industry (Nutrafol $85/mo, Rogaine dependency, spironolactone off-label, repackaged-prostate-supplement saw palmetto).
- Mechanism coherence: 8/9 — the estrogen→DHT→follicle chain is biologically plausible, named, and binds the GLP-1 niche to a discrete supplement formula.
- Market resonance: 8/9 — GLP-1 hair loss is a fresh, fast-growing wound with no dominant solution narrative yet.

**Product Integration**
- Position on spectrum: 4 (Heavy Push) — explicit 2,000mg dose call-out, "this is why I recommend Bloom & Bond to every patient."
- First product appearance: ~80% into the copy. Mechanism education is heavy upfront, then formula reveal.
- UMS: Yes — "concentrated pumpkin seed oil extract (not raw seed powder) + saw palmetto calibrated for women experiencing hormonal disruption from rapid weight loss."

**Overall Assessment**
- Schwartz sophistication: Stage 4 → 5 transition. Saw palmetto and pumpkin seed oil are widely sold; sophistication move is "nobody's combining them at clinical dose specifically for the GLP-1 estrogen-collapse mechanism."
- Belief shifts executed well: (1) "shedding ≠ the real problem" — separates telogen effluvium from DHT damage. (2) "your patience cost you follicles." (3) "natural ingredients don't work because dose/form is wrong, not because they don't work." Three clean structural shifts.
- Key technique worth cataloging: The TWO-PROBLEMS frame ("There are actually TWO separate things happening to your hair at the same time"). This is a clean way to validate prior advice as half-correct without dismissing the reader's two years of effort. Compare to single-villain framing — this is dual-villain framing, which is less attackable.
- Quality score: **8/10** — The mechanism education is sharp, the GLP-1 wedge is timely, and the dermatologist persona keeps it credible. Loses points for slightly long mechanism section before product reveal (could cost some readers at the saw palmetto handoff).
