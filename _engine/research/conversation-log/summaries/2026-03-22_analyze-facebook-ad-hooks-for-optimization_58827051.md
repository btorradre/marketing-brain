## What Happened
Analyzed a swipe file of hooks from Alevia and Amala Health, identified 8 gaps in the current hook-generation skill, then built hook-generation-v2 with all gaps addressed. Added 6 new hook types, 1 new subtype, 1 new specificity layer, and a full templating system.

## Key Decisions Made
- 6 new hook types added: Misdiagnosis Reveal, Professional Insider, Narrative Trojan Horse, Trend-Jack, Mechanism Reframe, Brand Confession
- "Perfect Compliance Catastrophe" added as a named Contrarian Reveal subtype
- Credential Stacking added as 4th specificity layer
- Hook Templating section added — system for adapting winning hook skeletons across 5 axes
- Template Potential added as 6th evaluation dimension

## Insights & Learnings
- **Misdiagnosis Reveal is mechanically different from Contrarian Reveal.** Contradicts the diagnosis itself ("you never had X"), not just the treatment. Creates existential-level curiosity ("then what DO I actually have?").
- **Professional Insider hooks work differently than Borrowed Authority.** The narrator IS the authority breaking ranks — the professional risk IS the credibility signal.
- **Narrative Trojan Horses bypass ad filters entirely.** The Goodwill jacket hook has zero health signal in the first 4 sentences — pure narrative pull.
- **Hook Templating is how these brands scale.** Same skeleton adapted across conditions, medications, demographics. The misdiagnosis hook appears identically for RA, osteoarthritis, and neuropathy.
- **A single-sentence mechanism reframe CAN function as a hook** when it's sharp enough ("Your nerve isn't damaged. It's starving.").
