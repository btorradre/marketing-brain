# Analyze viral animated ad for recreation — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Category:** creative-production
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-25_analyze-viral-animated-ad-for-recreation_0ac1c7c4]]

## What Happened
Analyzed a viral animated e-commerce ad trend, then created a complete storyboard to recreate the concept for Motilli. Used Gemini API for multimodal video analysis. The core concept: the GLP-1 injection itself becomes an anthropomorphic villain character — a cocky syringe with sunglasses and a gold chain who brags about weight loss while dismissing the digestive destruction it caused.

## Key Decisions Made
- The GLP-1 shot is the villain character — personified as charming but oblivious, like "the friend who throws a great party but trashes your house"
- 4-act structure, ~90 seconds total
- Mechanism education delivered through the villain's DISMISSIVENESS — "I didn't break your colon. I broke your STOMACH"
- The Shot panics when it sees Motilli's celery extract, accidentally revealing why Motilli works by explaining its own vulnerability
- Brooks chose the "shot as villain — playful and funny" creative direction

## Insights & Learnings
- Animated villain ads are a viral e-com trend worth tracking
- The villain-as-educator framework is powerful: the antagonist accidentally teaches the mechanism by trying to defend itself
- This format allows mechanism education without feeling like a lecture — the viewer learns through the villain's panic
- Gemini API can analyze video frame-by-frame when called directly with the API key
