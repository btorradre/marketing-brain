# Launch new brand with market research — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-21
**Category:** strategy
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-21_launch-new-brand-with-market-research_a67d9ee5]]

## What Happened
Deep strategic session developing a new gut health supplement brand targeting men aged 50-70 who drink regularly. Covered avatar development (5 segments narrowed to 1 broad avatar), mechanism research (alcohol-biofilm-gut damage), product formulation (8 ingredients), testosterone angle discovery, product format decisions, and Amazon competitive intelligence gathering.

## Key Decisions Made
- **Avatar:** Men 50-70 who drink 3+ days/week — broad enough for TAM, specific enough for resonance
- **Mechanism:** Alcohol → gut biofilm formation → intestinal damage → symptoms (attributed to aging). Secondary layer: biofilm bacteria converting testosterone to estrogen
- **Hero Product:** Capsule format (not gummy) — "The Biofilm Breaker" at $49-59, 60ct, 2/day
- **Core Ingredients:** Bromelain (500mg), NAC (600mg), Serrapeptase (120,000 SPU), Bacillus Coagulans (2B CFU), Bifidobacterium Bifidum (5B CFU), Milk Thistle
- **Positioning:** First brand to draw straight line from alcohol to biofilm to symptoms — nobody else does this
- **Key differentiator:** Bromelain — not in ANY top-selling biofilm disruptor on Amazon (98% biofilm disruption in studies)

## Insights & Learnings
- **Testosterone-gut-alcohol connection** is a powerful mechanism layer: alcohol disrupts gut bacteria that produce testosterone AND increases aromatase (converts T to estrogen). Nobody has connected this to drinking through the gut microbiome
- **Leaky gut and beer gut** are different but connected in a feedback loop — the reframe "Your beer gut isn't a calorie problem, it's a gut lining problem" is a scroll-stopper
- **Amazon competitive gaps:** Nobody combines biofilm disruption + liver support; zero products positioned for drinkers; no bromelain in top biofilm products; volume proves demand (top liver supplement: 20K+ units/month)
- **Milk thistle** included for avatar credibility (men 50-70 know it for liver) not just science
