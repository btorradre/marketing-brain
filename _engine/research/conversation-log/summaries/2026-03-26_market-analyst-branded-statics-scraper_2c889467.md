## What Happened
Scraped Meta Ad Library for branded statics from AG1, GOLO, and Provitalize. AG1 had minimal statics (2 active, Hugh Jackman endorsements). GOLO's page was unpublished/deleted — major competitive signal. Provitalize yielded ~39 active image ads with rich data on pain-angle positioning, anti-Ozempic angles, and multi-page risk diversification.

## Key Decisions Made
- GOLO's disappearance from Meta is a significant market signal — likely policy enforcement or strategic withdrawal due to GLP-1 market disruption
- Provitalize's pain-angle native static format identified as top template recommendation for Lunessa, Motilli, and Velantra
- AG1 is overwhelmingly video-first — low static volume means limited templates to replicate from them

## Insights & Learnings
- **Provitalize runs from 3+ Facebook pages** (Menopause Care And Relief, BB Company, Menopause And Me) — sophisticated multi-page risk diversification strategy
- **Anti-Ozempic positioning is emerging** as a new angle: "Why do women regain weight after Ozempic?" and "Most women are afraid to stop taking Ozempic..."
- **Pain-angle natives dominate** Provitalize's creative: sacroiliac dysfunction, gluteal tendinopathy, lower back pain — all medical condition names used as attention grabbers
- **Coupon stacking** is a pricing angle Provitalize uses: "Double-Dip Coupon Stacking Tip" and "Coupon Stacking Hack"
- **Bold claim archetype** detected: "GLUTEAL TENDINOPATHY KILLER" with red banner — aggressive but attention-getting
