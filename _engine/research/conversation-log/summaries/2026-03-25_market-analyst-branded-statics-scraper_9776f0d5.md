# Standardize Velantra product images — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Category:** creative-production
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-25_standardize-velantra-product-images_e96b9e45]]

## What Happened
Generated a complete set of 98 AI product images for the Velantra boat tote — 14 colors × 7 views each. Maintained remarkable model consistency (same woman, navy top, dark wavy hair across all shots). Fixed the bag's opening mechanism in all 14 interior shots after Brooks flagged the flap was wrong.

## Key Decisions Made
- 14 color variants: 7 standard (cream canvas + colored trim) and 7 solid limited editions
- 7 views per color: hero front, 3/4 angle, side profile, back, hardware detail, interior, model shot
- LL Bean aesthetic for model shots
- Flap-tucks-inside opening mechanism: unlock flap → open flap → tuck inside → bag opens from top

## Insights & Learnings
- AI image generation can maintain surprisingly good model consistency across 98 images when the master prompt is detailed enough
- The opening mechanism detail matters for product authenticity — customers will notice if the bag opens wrong in product photos
- Generation script was saved for re-runs, making it easy to add new colors later

## Creative Output
- 98 product images (133 MB) in Ecommerce/Velantra v2/boat-tote-images/
- Generation script saved for future color additions
- All 14 interior shots regenerated with correct flap mechanism
