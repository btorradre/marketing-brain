## BRAND 1: GLP-1 SOS SUPPLEMENTS

### Overview
GLP-1 SOS runs a multi-page funnel ecosystem on Shopify, with several distinct landing page types all feeding into the same product catalog. **Headline/Hook Strategy:**
- Main headline: "4 Reasons 55,000+ GLP-1 Users Swear By GLP-1 SOS (Hint: It Starts With Finally Going)"

4. Supplement overload — "juggling 5-6 different bottles"

The agitation is highly specific to the GLP-1 user experience, not generic wellness language. This specificity signals deep audience understanding and builds instant trust.

- CTA button text: "Get Relief Now — 40% Off"
- Sticky bottom bar (mobile) with product image, rating, delivery date, and CTA
- CTAs emphasize relief, not purchase

**Offer Structure:**
- $24.99 (down from $42.99) — 40% off
- Time-limited framing with countdown timer
- Delivery date shown ("Arrives by Monday, March 30")

**Urgency/Scarcity:**
- 10-minute countdown timer on the offer card

**Product Introduction Timing:**
Product appears after all 4 reasons are explained (approximately 60-70% down the page). The listicle format educates on problems first, then reveals the product as the unified solution for all 4 issues.

**Guarantee/Risk Reversal:**
Not prominently featured on this page — the CTA card mentions the guarantee elsewhere in the funnel.

**What Makes It Convert:**
- Extremely tight audience specificity — every word speaks to the GLP-1 user

## CROSS-BRAND PATTERN ANALYSIS

### What Both Brands Do Well

1. **Mechanism Differentiation:** Both brands invest heavily in explaining WHY their product works differently from alternatives. GLP-1 SOS explains osmotic vs. stimulant action. Terra explains topical/grounding vs. oral supplements. This is the core conversion driver — giving the reader a NEW mental model.

2. **Problem-Specificity Over Generality:** Neither brand sells "wellness." GLP-1 SOS sells relief from GLP-1 medication side effects. Terra sells grounding for sleep and pain. This specificity enables hyper-targeted ad creative and landing page copy.

3. **Failed-Solution Framing:** Both brands explicitly name and discredit alternatives the reader has already tried. GLP-1 SOS debunks fiber, laxatives, and generic probiotics. Terra debunks supplements, medications, and melatonin. This validates the reader's experience and positions the product as the logical next step.

4. **Social Proof Layering:** Both use multiple types of social proof simultaneously: customer counts, star ratings, testimonials with specifics, media mentions/community references, and review aggregates. No single proof point carries the sale — the stack does.

5. **Generous Guarantees:** 60-day (GLP-1 SOS) and 90-night (Terra) guarantees remove purchase risk and align with product results timelines.

6. **Multiple Page Variants Per Product:** Both brands run 3-4 different landing pages for the same product, each with a different angle/format. This enables ad creative testing at scale — different hooks route to different pages.

### Key Differences

| Element | GLP-1 SOS | Health Journal Now / Terra |
|---------|-----------|---------------------------|
| Price Point | $24.99-$29.99 | $99.95-$109.95 |
| Authority Source | Community/user voice | Medical doctor/historical narrative |
| Mechanism | Scientific/ingredient-based | Historical/conspiracy + science |
| Emotional Lever | Relief from embarrassment + physical discomfort | Fear of chronic pain + sleep deprivation |
| Page Length | Medium-long | Very long |
| Design Style | Clean, modern Shopify | Editorial/medical journal |
| Enemy | Generic supplements/bad advice | Big Pharma/modern lifestyle |
| Urgency Type | Countdown timers + limited-time pricing | Inventory scarcity + sale deadlines |

### Conversion Optimization Recommendations

**For GLP-1 SOS:**
1. Harmonize pricing across pages ($24.99 vs. $29.99 discrepancy)
2. Add video testimonials — the emotional stories (quitting medication, crying with relief) would be more powerful on camera
3. Test a longer guarantee (90 days) to match competitors and reduce friction on first purchase
4. Add a quiz funnel entry point to personalize the experience by primary symptom (constipation vs. burps vs. fog)
5. The 11-reasons-why (hair serum) page needs user testimonials — it currently has zero

**For Health Journal Now / Terra:**
1. Standardize customer count claims across pages (55K vs. 200K is a credibility risk)
2. Align guarantee terms (30-day on PDP vs. 90-night on advertorials)
3. Soften or provide citations for clinical percentage claims — sophisticated buyers will challenge these
4. Update outdated references ("2024 color releases")
5. Add a mid-funnel comparison page specific to knockoff grounding sheets (the comparison table on /1934 hints at this but could be its own page)
6. Test lower price points or payment plans — $100+ is a high impulse-buy threshold from a Facebook ad
