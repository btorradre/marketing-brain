## What Happened
The Tuesday static scraper ran through its rotation (Primal Viking, GleeFull, Primal Queen) plus two substitution attempts (Auri Labs, Bloom Nutrition). Result: zero qualifying branded statics downloaded — but the run produced a major strategic signal: every Tier 1 rotation brand has moved away from designed branded statics toward editorial photography and native/UGC imagery. The intel drop and three brand catalogs were updated to reflect the shift.

## Key Decisions Made

- Did NOT force downloads on non-qualifying creative (lifestyle / editorial photos that lack archetype features)
- Closed Chrome cleanly after each brand to maintain stability
- Pivoted to substitute brands (Auri, Bloom) when rotation brands came up dry — also dry
- Filed the run as a strategy signal rather than treating zero downloads as a failure
- Flagged the Nuvara GLP-1 hair loss before/after ad (seen via Sophie's Natural Health Guide affiliate page) for Friday's Nuora rotation

## Insights & Learnings

- **The branded static may be losing inside the top reference set.** Three of three rotation brands have moved to editorial photography + native CTA copy. Worth a strategy discussion before committing budget to designed-graphic statics for Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra.
- Primal Viking active image ad count has collapsed from ~35 in March to 6 today — and the 6 are all native lifestyle.
- Primal Queen invests in high-end B&W editorial portrait photography. Headlines live in the FB link-preview metadata, NOT inside the image. This is the opposite of a branded static archetype.
- GleeFull may be dark on image ads, or running under a different page name (worth checking).
- Image src URLs are now privacy-blocked from DOM extraction in the Ad Library. Future runs need a screenshot-based catalog approach instead of per-image downloads.
