## What Happened
Sunday-rotation Meta Ad Library scrape against the skin/antiaging/hair niche. Two keywords searched ("hair growth supplement," "anti aging supplement"). Discovered two new brands and filed three full long-form swipes. Session ran cleanly with zero Chrome crashes — strong contrast to the May 2 meta scrape that died on the first ad.

## Key Decisions Made
- Stopped at 3 swipes after one modal mismatch (Jessica Bennett card rendered Lumine ad). Held to the "3-5 quality swipes is the target — don't force 5" rule rather than risking Chrome stability.
- Filed Lumine Organics as a single brand running parallel SKUs (NAD+ for hair, Multi Collagen Complex for skin) under one Becca Fisher persona — recognized as a scalable native template worth documenting as a template card.
- Flagged Based Supplies (reverse-positioned "STOP buying supplements"), Tryllumin (Black women hair), Dr Sheila Washington, and shopgetamalahealth (statin alternative) as priorities for next session.

## Insights & Learnings
- **Lumine Organics is running a beat-for-beat scalable native template across niches.** Same persona, same emotional structure (husband-disconnect beat → public crisis moment → doctor-with-metaphor reveal), same 📢EDIT footer for product reveal. This is a transferable structural unit — the brand effectively built a chassis, then plugged in different products. Worth lifting into Velantra/Motilli playbook.
- Based Supplies running 6 active ads on the same creative with reverse-positioned "Please STOP buying hair growth supplements" hook — anti-supplement framing as a hair-loss angle is unusual and worth a deeper look next session.
- Modal mismatch behavior on Meta Ad Library: clicking "See summary details" on one card sometimes renders a different brand's ad due to multi-version creative ordering. Risk-mitigation: only trust modal contents if brand badge matches the card you clicked.
