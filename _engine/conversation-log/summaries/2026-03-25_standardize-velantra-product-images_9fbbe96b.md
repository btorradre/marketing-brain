# Standardize Velantra product images — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-25
**Category:** creative-production
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-25_standardize-velantra-product-images_9fbbe96b.md]]

## What Happened
Generated a complete set of 98 standardized product images for the Velantra boat tote across 14 colors and 7 views using Gemini API. After initial generation, fixed the interior shot opening mechanism across all 14 colors — the bag has a flap that tucks inside when opened, which wasn't rendering correctly.

## Key Decisions Made
- 14 colors total: 7 standard (cream canvas + colored trim: Navy, Red, Emerald Green, Lady Pink, Light Grey, Sunny Yellow, Lineman) + 7 solid limited editions (Green, Navy, Olive Green, Pink, Red, Orange, Yellow)
- 7 views per color: hero front, 3/4 angle, side profile, back, hardware detail close-up, interior, model shot
- Model consistency maintained across all colors: same woman, navy top, dark wavy hair, LL Bean style
- Interior shots regenerated with corrected flap-tucks-inside opening mechanism after first pass showed the bag closed or with wrong opening style

## Insights & Learnings
- Visual consistency across 98 images was maintained remarkably well — the model, pose, background, and photography style stayed consistent
- The opening mechanism (flap that covers the top, unlocks, tucks inside to open) required very specific prompting to render correctly
- Only 1 out of 98 images failed initial generation (solid-red_detail_hardware) — regenerated successfully on retry
