# Market analyst branded statics scraper — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-10
**Category:** competitor-analysis
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-10_market-analyst-branded-statics-scraper_c0a8289c]]

## What Happened
Friday rotation across Arrae, Nuora (myNuora), and North Valley Health Clinic for branded static ad creative. Two of the three brands either had no qualifying statics or were running pure UGC/personality formats. The session's whole win came from North Valley Health Clinic, where a fresh paired-contrast clinical narrative hook was discovered and catalogued as a high-potential template for Lunessa.

## Key Decisions Made
- Catalogued North Valley Health Clinic as a tracked brand under Archetype #7 (Medical/Clinical Proof).
- Skipped image asset capture this round — same browser→sandbox bridge blocker as prior sessions; documented hook templates and Library IDs in catalog instead.
- Recommended Monday re-scrape for NVH to track which variants scale past "Low impression count."

## Insights & Learnings
- **Paired-contrast clinical narrative hook (NVH):** "Patient A does everything wrong / Patient B does everything right / same bad outcome." This structurally invalidates the user's belief that doing the right things will protect them — high-leverage opener for any health category where compliance ≠ outcomes. Direct fit for Lunessa.
- **Nuora is all-in on UGC personality format** even in static — they're not running the branded-static archetypes at all. Worth knowing for competitive positioning: their entire static presence is "Alicia Darling" personality-driven.
- **Arrae has no branded static presence** — either deprioritized or running pure video. Don't expect to find templates there.
- **Persistent tooling blocker:** browser→sandbox bridge strips base64 blobs and signed fbcdn URLs. Image asset capture isn't possible through this path. Need a different download method or accept that catalog work is text/metadata only.
