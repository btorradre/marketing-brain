## Key Decisions Made
- Scored Cleantra **8.5/10** — above-average mechanism specificity, full capture.
- Scored Berbino **9/10** — exceptionally specific mechanism, specific organism named, strong emotional-death narrator opening.
- Flagged both brands for Tier 1 watchlist.
- Noted the specificity pattern as a 2026 market-stage shift worth cataloging.

## Insights & Learnings
- **The gut/liver niche has moved past vague claims.** "Detox" and "gut bacteria" are dead as proof. Both winning ads name the exact mechanism — *Methanobrevibacter smithii* (Berbino) or hepatic lymphatic drainage (Cleantra).
- **Specificity that can be Googled is the new proof.** Any mechanism a reader can verify in a search bar outperforms vague mechanism language at the same awareness level.
- **Sister-death hook** (Berbino's "My sister died at 42") is a pattern worth tracking — emotional-urgency narrator framing in a category that's been dominated by clinical authority voices.
- **Persona multiplexing** (Berbino runs Elizabeth James + multiple Dr. Cody Miller variants, Cleantra runs Dr. James Thornton) — brands are testing same-mechanism-different-voice at scale.
