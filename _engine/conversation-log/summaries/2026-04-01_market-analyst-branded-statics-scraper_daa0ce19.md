# Apr 1 – Market analyst branded statics scraper — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-01

## What Happened
Wednesday rotation branded statics scrape covering Seed, Bloom Nutrition, and Onnit. ~280 ads reviewed across all three brands with 16 branded statics identified and cataloged by archetype (bold claim, ingredient, clinical, authority comparison, testimonial). Catalog files and an intel drop were created.

## Key Decisions Made
- Classified statics by archetype taxonomy: bold claim, ingredient spotlight, clinical proof, authority comparison, testimonial
- Identified "Bold Challenger Static" format as the dominant replicable pattern across all three brands
- Flagged Chrome CORS restrictions as a blocker for automated image downloads — recommended Apify scraper for future image extraction

## Insights & Learnings
- **Seed's male pivot:** Seed is making a notable strategic shift into male-targeted messaging with copy like "Think probiotics aren't for men?" and "Men don't take probiotics." This is new and worth monitoring — could signal a broader market trend in supplement marketing.
- **Bloom's retail push:** Bloom Nutrition is running massive ad volume with a strong retail distribution play (H-E-B partnership statics, "Available at H-E-B"), plus gendered challenger messaging ("not your boyfriend's protein"). Their aspirational copy ("MADE TO MAKE YOU GLOW", "GLOWING STARTS FROM WITHIN") pairs with clean pink/coral photography.
- **Onnit's low static volume:** Only ~22 ads total. They appear to be shifting budget toward video. Very few branded statics — just product shots and one strong bold claim static ("GET ON-DEMAND BRAIN SUPPORT").
- The **Bold Challenger Static** template (dark background + provocative text challenge + product shot + single CTA) is the most replicable format for Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra.
