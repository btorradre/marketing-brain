# Analyze Aurivita's product marketing copy strategy — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-06

**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-06_analyze-aurivita-copy-strategy_d9fb651a]]

## What Happened
Deep competitive intelligence session on Aurivita (ED supplement). Scraped multiple ad variants and 4 landing pages via Chrome browser automation (Shopify JS-rendered pages). Built a comprehensive funnel architecture analysis documenting their two-track system and the psychology behind why each pairing works.

## Key Decisions Made
- Aurivita runs a **two-track funnel**: Hard Push (full product details in ad) → Listicle, and Soft Push (mechanism-only + P.S. product mention) → Long Advertorial
- The soft-push track is **dominating** — "Irreversible, they said" is the workhorse (65+ days), "The Canary In Your Bedroom" is their newest scaling bet (8+ variants since March 21)
- Hard-push diabetic ads are legacy cash cows still running but NOT being scaled — newer hard-push variants were killed within 15-16 days
- Reverse pairings (soft→listicle, hard→advertorial) were tested and killed within 9 days
- aurilabs.co is a mirror domain of aurivita.co with identical content

## Insights & Learnings
- The P.S.-only product mention in soft-push ads creates a **Zeigarnik Effect** information gap that drives clicks
- Soft-push ads work because they activate **mortality salience** (ED = cardiac emergency) via TMT (Terror Management Theory)
- Hard-push ads use **ELM central route** processing — reader does all analysis in the ad, listicle just provides peripheral validation
- The "Canary in the Coal Mine" metaphor is an evolution of "Irreversible" — same narrator, same mechanism, sharper named metaphor
- **New angle to watch:** "What Diabetes Quietly Took From My Husband" — wife-perspective narrator, different emotional engine, launched March 27
