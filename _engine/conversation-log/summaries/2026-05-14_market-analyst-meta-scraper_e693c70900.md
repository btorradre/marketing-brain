## What Happened
Thursday joint-pain rotation Meta Ad Library scrape. Despite heavy Chrome instability (4+ disconnects), the run uncovered four brands new to the catalog and a significant strategic shift from an already-tracked brand (Alevia). Captured 4 swipes — 2 long-form partial captures, 2 short-form brand-tracking files — plus an intel drop summarizing the mechanism and persona discoveries.

## Key Decisions Made
- Promoted **Provitalize / Better Body Co.** to high priority — large long-form operation (~73 active ads, "Ashley Thomsen" persona) with a named proprietary mechanism ("Hip Endotoxemia").
- Filed Magnali Health and Allura as medium/low priority new brand entries.
- Logged Alevia's expansion as a brand-level event: new "Jessica Bennett" persona (~520 active ads) + new blood-pressure vertical alongside the existing joint-pain operation.
- Committed to filing what was captured rather than forcing more swipes once Chrome dropped repeatedly — followed CHROME STABILITY RULES.

## Insights & Learnings
- **"Symptom-sniper that excludes the common diagnosis" hook pattern** — Provitalize's "lower back pain shoots down your thigh but stops before the knee — probably not sciatica" is a high-specificity hook that simultaneously qualifies the avatar and breaks their existing belief about their condition. Immediately portable across our products.
- **Named-mechanism move** — Provitalize coined "Hip Endotoxemia (HE)" as their villain: gut toxins leaking into the bloodstream causing hip tendon flareups. Naming the villain turns a vague avatar pain into a tracked condition the avatar now "has." Strong belief-shift architecture.
- **Persona ladders inside a single brand** — Alevia is running multiple named personas (Jessica Bennett joining earlier personas), each with their own narrative, while quietly expanding from joints into blood pressure. The persona is the angle. This is a structural pattern we can replicate.
- **Provitalize creative variety** is a swipe goldmine: "Ozempic Melted My Butt," "Cortisone Fixed My Pain Then Destroyed My Hip," "Patience Pays," tendon-deterioration urgency, anger-at-doctors openers — broad emotional surface area on one product.
