# Analyze GLP-1 digestive copy performance — Session Summary

## What Happened
Brooks brought in Metabolae's full listicle as a structural reference and asked for a clean rewrite of the Motilli listicle in that editorial register — shorter sections, less re-education, no narrative flourish. I delivered a V2 Motilli listicle (~700 words across 7 numbered reasons, down ~70% from V1) that matches Metabolae's lean editorial structure: consolidated testimonials at the bottom, 3 CTAs total, no per-section interrupts. Then Brooks asked whether this listicle would beat the Motilli PDP in a CBO test against the Dr. Marsh long-form ad. I argued it probably wouldn't in the first 7-14 days and recommended NOT using CBO for this comparison.

## Key Decisions Made
- Motilli listicle is being rebuilt in Metabolae's structural mold (lean editorial, not narrative). V2 is the working draft.
- Per-section testimonials, per-section CTAs, standalone comparison tables, and standalone scarcity widgets are OUT for this version.
- The page now ASSUMES the highway/on-ramp mechanism metaphor — does not re-teach what Dr. Marsh's letter already installed.
- For Dr. Marsh's ad specifically, the long-form-ad → PDP funnel may be the strongest setup. Brooks should not default to a listicle just because scaling competitors run one.
- Funnel test methodology: separate campaigns with budget caps, NOT a CBO. Measure contribution margin (factoring AOV), not just CPA. Run 7-14 days minimum.

## Insights & Learnings
- **The completeness of the upstream ad determines whether a listicle is additive or redundant.** Dr. Marsh's 1,900-word letter does more persuasion work than most ads in this space, which compresses the listicle's job to "marginal-reader reinforcement" rather than "primary persuasion layer."
- **CBO is a budget-allocation tool, not a learning tool.** Putting listicle-traffic and PDP-traffic in the same CBO will starve the listicle in week one before the deeper contribution-margin signal can emerge.
- **Listicle's structural edge is AOV/bundle uplift + retargeting depth + creative-type pairing flexibility** — not CPA on a complete long-form ad.
- **PDPs over-index on impulse buyers (<60 sec decisions); listicles capture both impulse and considered traffic.** Attribution window (1-day vs 7-day click) heavily skews which one looks like the winner.
- **Competitors run listicles because their specific creative type needs one** — not because listicles are universally superior. This is the trap to avoid.
