## What Happened
Monday meta scrape run targeting the cholesterol / heart-health / statin-alternative axis. Three keywords searched. One new scaling brand discovered: **ColonBroom's "Dr. Peter Polimor" persona funnel** — ~110 active long-form variants. One full 4,700-word native ad was swiped and filed. A second Polimor variant was inspected but noted as a near-duplicate (father → brother narrator swap, hook opener swap) rather than filed. Several other surfaced brands (Resona, Dr. Golden/Novére Red Yeast Rice, Dr. Livingood, Withrovina, Viari, Coach Susan, Zion's Organics) were short-form only and skipped per brief.

## Key Decisions Made
- File **one** Polimor long-form swipe rather than two near-duplicates; document the variant pattern in the intel drop instead.
- Skip Resona and all other brands in the scan window — short-form only, not a fit for the long-form reference library.
- Close Chrome tabs between brand cycles after one crash recovery; prioritize Chrome stability over swipe count.
- Flag ColonBroom for a follow-up funnel-analysis pass on shop.colonbroom.com.

## Insights & Learnings
- **ColonBroom is executing a category landgrab on "cholesterol."** 110+ active variants, occupying 9 of the top 10 image-media slots for the keyword. This is a textbook scale play: one durable mechanism body, rotating hooks + rotating narrator characters (father/brother).
- **Durable IP vs. rotation fuel:** the bile acid recirculation mechanism teach is the locked element; the narrator identity and hook opener are the disposable variant-testing layers. Good model to copy for any mechanism-led long-form program.
- **Tool truncation workaround:** JS tool output caps at ~1K–1.5K chars regardless of input size. For 30K-char ads, build from 1K-char slices saved incrementally. This is now a repeatable pattern for long-form extraction.
- **Short-form noise dominates cholesterol keyword at the viewport top.** Long-form surfaces only below the fold or via direct brand page navigation.
