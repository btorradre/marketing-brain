# Analyze Meta ad funnel performance — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-05-15
**Category:** funnel-work
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-05-15_analyze-meta-ad-funnel-performance_e0184c86baf]]

## What Happened

Deep-dive funnel diagnosis on the Motilli (GLP-1 constipation) Meta ad account. Reviewed delivery, thumb-stop, on-page ATC, and ATC-to-purchase metrics across multiple ad sets (Fiber NO METAPHORS, Nurse v2, Fiber old, Nurse_Listicle, Fiber_Listicle). Concluded the creative is healthy-to-strong and the PDP is the primary bottleneck — the ad-to-PDP transition breaks voice, downgrades credibility, and introduces broken pricing math right at the decision moment. User then surfaced the listicle bridge page (l7.guthealthblog.org) as a hypothesis fix; second pass diagnosed the listicle as structurally far better-suited to the traffic but flagged six specific issues. Session ended with user pivoting the diagnosis again: "I actually think the problem is the offer" — no assistant response captured.

## Key Decisions Made

- PDP is named primary bottleneck. Layers 1-4 (delivery, thumb-stop, click-through, ATC) read healthy; the leak is post-ATC (17-40% close rate vs 50-70% DTC benchmark).
- Listicle architecture is endorsed strategically but the 100%-close-rate signal on Nurse_Listicle (3-for-3 on $34 spend) is called out as statistical theater, not signal.
- A spend-up test was prescribed: $300-500 behind each listicle ad set AND direct-to-PDP ad sets in parallel, evaluated at 20+ ATCs per cohort.
- A campaign-level architecture point was raised: one listicle is serving multiple ad angles (Fiber/Nurse/Daughter's Gastroenterologist/Jevawell), creating credibility-source congruence breaks. Long-term recommendation: one listicle variant per major creative angle.
- User reframed the problem at the end as the offer itself — this is now an open thread.

## Insights & Learnings

- **Ad copy quality can mask PDP weakness.** The "Fiber (old)" metaphor-rich version had a better close rate than the cleaner "NO METAPHORS" version because the slower, deeper read produced deeper-belief readers who closed themselves before reaching the PDP. Stripping ad friction made the PDP weakness visible.
- **Credibility-source continuity is a real funnel mechanic.** The ad ends on Dr. Rebecca Marsh (named gastroenterologist, 47-patient stat, 3,200 patients over 18 years). The PDP replaces her with aggregate review count ("10,247 verified reviews"). That's a credibility *downgrade*, not a transition — the reader gets demoted from "consulting a gastroenterologist" to "reading Amazon reviews."
- **Promise-delivery mismatch:** ad promises "4-8 weeks if early, 3-4 months if late." PDP guarantee reads "Feel the difference in 90 days." Same number, different meaning — and the PDP framing accidentally signals "wait 90 days for anything to happen," which softens urgency.
- **Pricing math integrity matters more for sophisticated avatars.** The "Buy 2 Get 1 Free for $47.98, was $134.97" implies a per-bottle full price ($44.99) that doesn't exist anywhere on the page. Medically literate 30-65 women will spot this in under five seconds. The PDP introduces the exact confusion the ad's villain (fake-savings supplement aisle) was set up to indict.
- **Listicle does the bridge job the PDP can't:** voice continuity (Dr. Marsh survives), mechanism reinforcement without echo (highway on-ramp/off-ramp metaphor vs ad's three-cause model), emotional temperature match (first-person patient narrator), promise-delivery alignment (Days 1-7 / Weeks 2-3 / Weeks 4-6 / Day 90), and villain stack consistency (Miralax, magnesium, fiber gummies, probiotics, Dulcolax).
- **The listicle still routes to a broken PDP.** Fixing the listicle without fixing the PDP just moves more pre-closed readers to the same trust break.
