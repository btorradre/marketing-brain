## What Happened
Massive system-building session. Transcribed and distilled Brooks' two Loom recordings into the foundational DR framework, then built operational tools on top, then added a customer psychology layer. Created 8 new files in `fundamentals/` and updated the Map of Content. This is the session that established the canonical DR framework stack.

## Key Decisions Made
- The entire DR system now rests on a **three-layer stack**: Strategy (awareness/sophistication), Structure (8-beat ad architecture), and Psychology (nightmare→dream journey)
- The **Awareness x Sophistication Decision Matrix** is the primary routing tool — it determines ad length, beat emphasis, and angle family
- **Belief shifts** are diagnosed beat-by-beat with a specific checklist and metrics-to-broken-beat diagnostic table
- Angle families are organized by awareness level with rotation and saturation rules to prevent creative fatigue
- The customer psychology layer (nightmare→dream, reframe pivot, visual-verbal congruence) is now **mandatory** for all copy production

## Insights & Learnings
- Awareness levels and market sophistication are the TWO dials that determine everything about how an ad is written — they're not separate frameworks but a combined decision matrix
- The 8-beat structure (Hook → Close) is the skeleton, but the customer psychology layer is what makes it feel alive — without it, ads are structurally correct but emotionally flat
- The "felt-journey test" is the QA gate: can you read the ad and feel the nightmare→dream arc without knowing what product is being sold?
