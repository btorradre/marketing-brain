# Update product gallery with consistent images — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-15
**Category:** Creative Production
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-15_update-product-gallery-consistent-images_c8f901cc]]

## What Happened
Rebuilt the entire Solid Yellow variant product gallery for the Velantra Boat Tote PDP. Removed inconsistent-shade images (greenish yellows) and a Birkin-lookalike shot, then regenerated 7 consistent golden-yellow canvas images across multiple angles. Also fixed a design error where the bag flap was splitting in half instead of folding over the top, and reordered gallery images per Brooks's direction.

## Key Decisions Made
- Front-facing shot set as the hero/featured image for the Solid Yellow variant
- Group shot of 6 bags moved to last position in gallery
- Greenish-yellow images deleted entirely rather than kept as filler
- High-angle 3/4 view regenerated to fix the split-flap design error

## Insights & Learnings
- Image generation credits can run out mid-session — plan for credit availability before starting batch regeneration jobs
- The AI image generator sometimes produces design inconsistencies (split flap vs. over-the-top flap) that need to be caught by visual QA against existing reference images
- Color consistency across AI-generated product shots requires using a specific reference image to anchor the palette

## Creative Output
- 7 new product gallery images for Solid Yellow Boat Tote variant (all golden-yellow, consistent shade)
- Images uploaded directly to the Velantra PDP

## Action Items & Next Steps
- Monitor how the new gallery performs on the PDP (conversion rate, time on page)
- Consider applying the same consistency treatment to other Boat Tote color variants if they have similar shade mismatches
