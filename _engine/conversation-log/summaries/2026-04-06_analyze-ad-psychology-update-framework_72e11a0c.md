## What Happened
Brooks provided a detailed voice memo explaining the customer psychology principles he wants baked into the system — nightmare→dream state architecture, the power of the reframe, and visual-verbal congruence. He included the full timestamped transcript of a Nour silica/hair-loss video ad as the worked example. Claude created a visual breakdown of the ad and wrote skill upgrade patches for three core DR skills.

## Key Decisions Made
- The **reframe** ("it's not stress — it's silica deficiency") is the single most powerful element in the ad structure — it's the pivot between nightmare and dream states
- Every visual must **correlate** with the script AND with the viewer's current emotional state — not just illustrate what's being said
- The hook must open in the **after state** and juxtapose to the before state (not the other way around)
- Customer psychology layer must be **mandatory** in all copy and analysis skills, not optional

## Insights & Learnings
- The ad's 8-beat structure maps perfectly to a nightmare→dream journey: nightmare deposit (problem agitation), reframe pivot (mechanism), dream withdrawal (transformation), callback close
- Visual-verbal congruence operates on THREE independent layers: what's shown, what's said, and what the viewer FEELS — all three must align at every moment
- The "nightmare-deposit/dream-withdrawal" system means every nightmare moment must be paid back with a corresponding dream moment later
- After menopause, silica production crashes up to 60% — this is the mechanism education that makes the reframe land
