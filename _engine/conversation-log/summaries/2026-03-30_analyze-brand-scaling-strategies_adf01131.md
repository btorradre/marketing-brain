# Analyze brand scaling strategies and copy styles — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-03-30
**Category:** strategy
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-03-30_analyze-brand-scaling-strategies_adf01131.md]]

## What Happened
Scraped Rosabella's Meta Ad Library (beetroot blood pressure supplement) to complete a 4-brand hook-to-lead congruence analysis alongside True Nutra, Nutrition Therapy, and ClearDose. Extracted 6 unique hooks sorted by impressions, analyzed full ad copy, and mapped landing page funnels. Then used the analysis to build two new installable skills (funnel-advisory and hook-congruence) plus a swipe file.

## Key Decisions Made
- Rosabella confirmed as scaling to PDP (product pages), same funnel pattern as True Nutra — no advertorial bridge needed for their awareness level and mechanism complexity
- Four congruence types identified across all 4 brands: Narrative, Identity, Educational, Authority
- Funnel-advisory skill uses a 5-factor scoring matrix (mechanism complexity, education burden, form factor familiarity, trust deficit, solution component count) with a clear decision threshold for PDP vs. advertorial
- Hook-congruence skill designed to work ALONGSIDE existing hook-generation skill, not replace it — adds the congruence layer

## Insights & Learnings
- The "Trojan Horse" congruence pattern (hook promises one thing, lead delivers it while secretly loading the mechanism) appeared consistently across all 4 scaling brands
- Awareness-level matching in hooks is the single biggest differentiator between hooks that convert and hooks that get scrolled past
- The "mirror close" technique — where the close echoes the hook's original promise — appeared in the highest-performing ads across all brands
