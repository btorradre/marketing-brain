# Mar 26 – Market analyst branded statics scraper — Session Summary

## What Happened
The market analyst agent scraped the Meta Ad Library for branded static ads from AG1, Golo, and Provitalize. Chrome crashed multiple times on the Ad Library but the agent adapted, using web research and careful single-tab browsing to gather intel. Provitalize yielded the richest data with ~39 active image ads. Catalog entries and an intel drop were created for all three brands.

## Key Decisions Made
- Adapted from direct image downloads to screenshot-based visual analysis after Chrome instability
- Identified Provitalize's pain-angle native static format as the top template recommendation for our brands
- Flagged Golo's disappearance from Meta as a major competitive signal worth monitoring
- Recommended applying Provitalize's static format to Lunessa (insomnia), Motilli (digestive), and Velantra (fatigue)

## Insights & Learnings
- **AG1** is overwhelmingly video-first with only 2 active branded statics, both using Hugh Jackman celebrity endorsement + stat overlay format. Low static volume signals they don't see statics as a scaling channel.
- **Golo's Facebook page has been unpublished or deleted** — zero active ads. Previously a top-10 weight loss advertiser, now completely gone from Meta. Likely hit by policy enforcement or withdrew due to GLP-1 market disruption. This is a significant market shift signal.
- **Provitalize (BB Company)** is running ~39 active image ads across 3+ Facebook pages ("Menopause Care And Relief," "BB Company," "Menopause And Me"). This multi-page strategy diversifies risk. Their dominant archetype is pain-angle native statics targeting sacroiliac dysfunction and back pain.
- **New anti-Ozempic positioning detected** in Provitalize ads: "Why do women regain weight after Ozempic?" and "Most women are afraid to stop taking Ozempic..." — this is a fresh angle leveraging GLP-1 backlash.
- Provitalize uses a sophisticated mix of archetypes: pain-angle native images, competitor comparison ("Why Over 25k Women Switched From Collagen Powders"), clinical/medical proof (anatomy images with "SACROILIAC JOINT" text), and urgency/pricing ("Coupon Stacking Hack").
