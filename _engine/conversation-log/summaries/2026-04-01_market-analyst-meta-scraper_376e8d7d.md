## Insights & Learnings
- **Rootofnature is running the best neuropathy copy cataloged to date.** Their long-form copy is genuinely strong — the Gerald story (Ad #01) and the 6:15 AM alarm narrative (Ad #02) both scored 8/10.
- **"Wrong molecule" mechanism:** R-ALA vs. racemic ALA differentiation is a reusable mechanism framework. This is the kind of scientific specificity that builds belief — "it's not that the ingredient doesn't work, it's that you've been getting the wrong form of the ingredient." Directly applicable to any supplement niche.
- **Second-person "day narration" technique:** Ad #02 ("Your alarm goes off at 6:15") uses a rare format — writing the reader's day for them in second person. This bypasses testimonial skepticism entirely because the reader isn't evaluating someone else's story, they're recognizing their own routine. Worth studying and replicating.
- Rootofnature has at least 10 distinct copy variants, longest at ~6,000 chars. They're testing aggressively.
