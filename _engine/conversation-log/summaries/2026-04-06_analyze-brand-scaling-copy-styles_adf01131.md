# Analyze brand scaling strategies and copy styles — Session Summary

**Date:** 2026-04-06
**Category:** strategy
**Transcript:** [[conversation-log/transcripts/2026-04-06_analyze-brand-scaling-copy-styles_adf01131]]

## What Happened
Multi-brand competitive analysis across 4 brands (Rosabella, True Nutra, Nutrition Therapy, ClearDose) that produced a hook-to-lead congruence breakdown and led to the creation of two new skills: funnel-advisory and hook-congruence.

## Key Decisions Made
- **Four congruence types** identified: Narrative, Identity, Educational, Authority — these are the ways hooks can connect to leads
- **Funnel advisory** uses a 5-factor scoring matrix (mechanism complexity, education burden, form factor familiarity, trust deficit, solution component count) to determine PDP vs. advertorial
- **Hook-congruence** skill works ALONGSIDE existing hook-generation skill — it adds the congruence layer that was missing
- Rosabella confirmed as scaling to PDP (product pages), same pattern as True Nutra

## Insights & Learnings
- The "mirror close" technique — closing the ad by mirroring the hook's promise — is a pattern across all 4 brands' top performers
- Hook-to-lead congruence is the #1 predictor of whether an ad "falls apart" after the hook — it's not the hook OR the lead, it's the TRANSITION
- Brands that scale to PDP (without advertorial) share specific characteristics: simple mechanism, familiar product form, low education burden
