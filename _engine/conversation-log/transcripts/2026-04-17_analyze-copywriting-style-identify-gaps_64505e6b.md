## How the competitor (Nutrition Therapy) achieves simpler, more conversational language

Five mechanical choices:

1. **Shorter sentences at emotional beats. Longer sentences for thinking.** Competitor fires 4-9 word reaction beats ("I just stared at her." / "I don't want to think about that.") then stretches into 13-14 word reflection sentences. User's version flattens by running reflection at same medium length as reactions.

2. **More interior monologue, less narration of events.** Competitor has narrator thinking on page constantly ("That word stayed with me. Signal."). User's version has reactions but fewer "thought-carrying" beats — reads more like dialogue transcript.

3. **Scene details used as anchors, not decorative props.** Competitor uses 6 specific objects/actions in 2,000 words — laptop, folder, kitchen table, two-hour drive, forehead kiss in driveway, "like I used to kiss hers before school." Each belongs in the scene.

4. **ONE core analogy does the whole job.** Competitor uses factory floor ONLY. User stacks three competing images: highway on-ramp/off-ramp, fermentation tank, "six feet upstream" (which is actually a river metaphor, not highway — highways don't have upstream).

5. **Conjunction-led sentence openers.** Competitor opens ~1.5-2x more sentences with But/So/And/Because. Most reliable "sounds spoken" move in English.

## Bottlenecks in user's version

**Bottleneck 1: Mechanism section is front-loaded and teacherly.** Four analogies/concepts stacked back-to-back. Reader is metabolizing highway while fermentation tank lands on top, then "six feet upstream" on top of that. ~400 uninterrupted words in lecture mode.
- **Fix:** Keep highway. Cut fermentation tank. Replace "six feet upstream" with "six feet in the wrong direction" or repeat on-ramp/off-ramp to reinforce single metaphor.

**Bottleneck 2: Three-ingredient sequence reads like feature list.** Nutrition Therapy sells ONE criterion (sublingual L-arginine) with deep emotional buy-in. User sells THREE (apigenin + chlorophyllin + soluble prebiotic fiber), each getting surface treatment.
- **Fix:** Either lean hardest into apigenin as single criterion, OR keep all three but trim explanations by 30-40% and chain them with cause-and-effect.

**Bottleneck 3: "That word stayed with me" beats are missing.** Competitor's narrator metabolizes the teaching on page. User's narrator receives education then moves to solution.
- **Fix:** Add 2-3 interior-monologue beats inside mechanism section. Example: "On-ramp. I kept repeating it in my head. For eight months I'd been pouring fiber into an off-ramp that wasn't even the problem."

**Bottleneck 4: Sister Diane beat is thinner than the generational equivalent.** Competitor has narrator's OWN mother — housebound at 68, died inside four walls. That's generational inheritance stakes. Diane (sister who quit Wegovy) is lateral cautionary, not existential.
- **Fix:** Biggest lift available. Replace Diane with a mother/aunt/grandmother figure whose body failed her at 65 — narrator watching same thing start in her 50s. Diane can stay but shouldn't be the deepest well.

**Bottleneck 5: AI-pattern violations to sweep.**
- "No cleanouts. No fiber feeding the fermentation. No more treating the wrong organ." — parallel repetition stack. Rewrite required.
- "Stop following the protocol that's failing you. Start supporting the organ that actually needs it." — stop/start parallel stack on blacklist (though competitor uses same pattern).
- "still suffering, still being told, still treating" — borderline.
- Two "That night I called..." beats mirror competitor's ending — fine as template borrowing.
