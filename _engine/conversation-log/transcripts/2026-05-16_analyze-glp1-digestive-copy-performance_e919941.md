4. Two different shopping experiences: PDP signals "buy now," listicle signals "let me explain more first." For most-aware reader (the one who got there because the ad worked), buy-now wins.

WHERE THE LISTICLE WINS:
1. AOV / bundle uplift — listicle's offer block pushes 3-bottle bundle with Most Popular ribbon, free shipping cutoff, bonus guide. PDPs typically let people pick 1 bottle. AOV climb can offset CPA disadvantage.
2. Cold/colder audience expansion — listicle reinforces when upstream ad has any softness. PDP works best when ad is complete.
3. Retargeting layers — listicle browsers are richer retargeting pool than PDP bouncers.
4. Long-tail purchase windows — listicles capture some impulse + considered traffic. PDPs over-index on impulse. On 7-day click attribution, listicle catches up.
5. Different upstream ad pairings — shorter/less-complete creative (30-sec video, image ad, curiosity-driven video) needs a listicle downstream. PDP hurts those.

ACTUAL PREDICTION (CBO, this ad, 7-day click attribution, Purchase event):
- First 3-5 days: PDP wins on CPA, possibly significantly. Meta shifts budget toward PDP.
- Day 7-14 if budget forced back: AOV uplift narrows CPA gap but probably doesn't fully close.
- Day 14-30 sustained: listicle edge shows in retargeting and compounding social proof, not raw acquisition CPA.

If 7-day test on CPA alone → PDP wins. If 30-day on contribution margin → listicle is much closer, may pull ahead with real bundle uplift.

RECOMMENDATION:
DON'T CBO these against each other. CBO is a budget-allocation tool, not a learning tool. It'll kill the listicle in week one before deeper signal can be read.

Phase 1 — Run as separate campaigns, not same CBO, with budget caps. Identical except for destination. 7-14 days at fixed spend. Measure CPA, AOV, contribution margin per click, ATC rate, LPV → Purchase rate.

Phase 2 — Decide on contribution margin, not CPA. Higher AOV with slightly worse CPA can still win.

Phase 3 — Once winner is known for THIS ad, test different ad creative types (shorter video → listicle vs. shorter video → PDP) where listicle has bigger structural edge.
