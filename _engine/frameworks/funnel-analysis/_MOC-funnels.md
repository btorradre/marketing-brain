## Funnel Architecture Framework

### Stage 1: Awareness
**Goal**: Get eyeballs, stop the scroll, create interest
**Best formats**: Long-form ads, video, TikTok, native ads
**Success metric**: CTR (2-5% is good for cold traffic)

### Stage 2: Consideration
**Goal**: Educate, build mechanism understanding, overcome objections
**Best formats**: Email sequences, advertorial content, webinars
**Success metric**: Email open rate, click rate, or content engagement

### Stage 3: Conversion
**Goal**: Clear call to action, reduce friction, drive purchase
**Best formats**: Sales pages, VSL, one-click upsells
**Success metric**: Conversion rate (2-8% depending on traffic quality)

### Stage 4: Retention/Ascension
**Goal**: Fulfill promise, deliver results, build loyalty, sell higher ticket
**Best formats**: Onboarding emails, in-app messaging, community
**Success metric**: Customer satisfaction, retention rate, repeat purchase rate

## Common Funnel Architectures

### 1. Simple Direct Response Funnel
\`\`\`
Cold Traffic → Long-Form Ad → Sales Page/VSL → Order Confirmation
\`\`\`
**Best for**: High-converting mechanisms, viral offers, time-sensitive
**Expected conversion**: 2-5% (direct)

### 2. Email Nurture Funnel
\`\`\`
Cold Traffic → Hook Ad/Content → Email Optin → Email Sequence → Sales Page
\`\`\`
**Best for**: Complex mechanisms, relationship building, high-ticket
**Expected conversion**: 5-15% (from traffic)

### 3. Tripwire Funnel
\`\`\`
Cold Traffic → Low-Cost Offer → Upsell 1 → Upsell 2 → Core Offer
\`\`\`
**Best for**: Building trust, monetizing lookalike audiences, scaling
**Expected conversion**: 30-50% (at least one upsell)

### 4. Webinar Funnel
\`\`\`
Traffic → Webinar Signup → Email Sequence → Live/Replay Webinar → Offer Page
\`\`\`
**Best for**: Premium positioning, expert authority, education-first
**Expected conversion**: 3-8% (attendance to purchase)

### 5. Value Stack Funnel
\`\`\`
Traffic → Content/Hook → Email Value Sequence → Stack Reveal → Limited-Time Offer
\`\`\`
**Best for**: Building perceived value, premium positioning
**Expected conversion**: 10-20% (from initial audience)
