## Core Patterns for Concept Generation

When generating new product concepts, draw upon these underlying patterns:

**1. The "Named Enzyme/Pathway" Pattern**
Mechanisms that name a specific biological pathway (AMPK, 5-alpha-reductase, ATP) inherently sound more authoritative. *Application:* When introducing a new ingredient, always research and name the specific cellular pathway it acts upon.

**2. The "Physical over Chemical" Pattern**
Physical mechanisms (light filtering, temperature cooling, mechanical extraction) often feel more intuitive to consumers than chemical ones. *Application:* Can you solve a chemical problem (like sleep) with a physical mechanism (like a cooling mattress)?

**3. The "Bioavailability" Attack Vector**
Many dominant mechanisms rely on ingredients that are poorly absorbed. *Application:* A highly effective way to position a new product is to attack the market leader's absorption rate, introducing a new delivery mechanism (liposomal, micellar, sublingual) as the true solution.
