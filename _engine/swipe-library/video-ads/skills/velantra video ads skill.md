# Velantra Video Ad Scripts

## Brand Voice Constraints (Apply to ALL Velantra Scripts)

### The Mantra

**Wealth Whispers.** Every word is tested against this. If the copy shouts, it fails. If it tries too hard, it fails. If it explains itself, it fails.

### Language Rules

- No exclamation points (one per 500 words maximum across all brand copy)
- No emojis
- No "boss babe / girl boss / queen"
- No "affordable luxury" or "luxury redefined"
- No "treat yourself" or "you deserve this"
- No "obsessed," "slay," "iconic," "must-have"
- No breathless superlatives

### The Anti-Aspiration Principle

Velantra does NOT sell aspiration ("buy this and become this person"). It sells recognition ("you already are this person, and this was made for you"). The reader should feel FOUND, not sold to.

## UGC / Organic Style Rules

These rules govern scripts meant to feel like organic creator content rather than produced ads.

### 1. Brand Reveal Timing

**WRONG:** Save the brand name for the end as a "close"
**RIGHT:** Introduce brand + product name immediately after the hook

The viewer is looking at a product. They want to know what it is NOW. Saving the reveal is a copywriting trick that feels like an ad.

**Example:**
- ❌ "...It's the Weekender from Velantra. That's the bag."
- ✅ "If you're looking for the perfect weekend travel bag, I have the one for you. This is the Weekender from Velantra."

### 2. Continuous Speech Flow

**WRONG:** Fragment stacking with paragraph breaks
**RIGHT:** Continuous natural speech with connectors

UGC is spoken aloud, not read. Fragments feel like copywriting. Real people use connectors.

**Example:**
- ❌ "Full-grain Italian leather. No logos. Fits under the seat."
- ✅ "It's full-grain Italian leather. No logos, fits under the seat, slides over your carry-on handle..."

### 3. Vernacular Specificity

**WRONG:** Generic specs ("laptop sleeve," "roomy interior")
**RIGHT:** Real specs + how people actually talk about their stuff

**Example:**
- ❌ "Laptop sleeve here."
- ✅ "It's so roomy you can fit anything from a 16 inch MacBook to all your daily drivers."

Key vernacular phrases: "daily drivers," "fishing it off the floor," "without touching everything else"

### 4. Soft Urgency (Product-Aware Only)

**WRONG:** Hard urgency / mic-drop attempts / brand-voice-pure endings
**RIGHT:** Urgency framed as insider tip

For product-aware UGC, urgency works when positioned as a secret the creator is sharing — not a sales push.

**Example:**
- ❌ "That's the bag." (trying to be clever)
- ❌ "Link in bio. Go." (hard CTA)
- ✅ "I'm not supposed to share this but Velantra is having a spring sale and all of these bags are at risk of selling out."

The "I'm not supposed to share this but..." framing positions the creator as giving insider access, not selling.

---

## Anti-Patterns (Document Failures)

1. **Fragment stacking** — staccato sentences that read like copy, not speech
2. **Saving brand for the close** — feels like a reveal/ad trick
3. **Generic specs** — "laptop sleeve" vs. "16 inch MacBook"
4. **Hard urgency** — "limited time" / "selling fast" / "don't miss out"
5. **Clever mic-drops** — "That's the bag." / "You're welcome."

## Swipe File: Approved Scripts

### Weekender — "The Perfect Travel Bag" (Product-Aware)

```
If you're looking for the perfect weekend travel bag, I have the one for you. This is the Weekender from Velantra. It's full-grain Italian leather. No logos, fits under the seat, slides over your carry-on handle, and stands up on its own so you're not fishing it off the floor at security. The inside actually makes sense. It's so roomy you can fit anything from a 16 inch MacBook to all your daily drivers. Pocket for your passport and boarding pass here. Shoes go in the bottom without touching everything else. I've had mine for two years. Fifty-something flights. It looks better now than when I bought it. No broken zippers. No peeling. No hardware that scratches. I'm not supposed to share this but Velantra is having a spring sale and all of these bags are at risk of selling out.
```

**Why it works:**
- Hook → Brand reveal immediate
- Flows as natural speech
- Specific details (16 inch MacBook, fifty-something flights)
- Vernacular language (daily drivers, fishing it off the floor)
- Soft urgency as insider tip

## Script Structure by Awareness Level

### Product-Aware (knows the category, evaluating options)

1. Hook that names what they're looking for
2. Brand + product name (immediate)
3. Key differentiators in natural speech
4. Personal proof / usage details
5. Soft urgency as insider tip

### Most-Aware (knows Velantra, needs reason to buy now)

1. Direct address to the hesitation
2. New information or angle
3. Reinforcement of quality proof
4. Time-sensitive insider information
