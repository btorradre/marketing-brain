## Platform-Specific Video Strategies

### TikTok & Instagram Reels
- 15-30 second format
- Hook must work in first 1-2 seconds
- Text overlays critical
- Fast pacing and pattern matching
- Curiosity-driven mechanics

### Facebook Video Ads
- 15-60 second format
- Can build mechanism more slowly
- Audio often off (subtitles critical)
- Hook still critical but more time to develop
- Story-based mechanisms work well

### VSL (Video Sales Letter)
- 5-15 minute long-form
- Pure education + mechanism demonstration
- No hard sell until end
- Testimonials and proof integrated
- Deep objection handling
