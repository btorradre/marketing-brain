## Design DNA

- **Color palette:** Soft pastel — lavender purple, soft pink, cream/off-white. Feminine, calm, not clinical. The opposite of Primal Viking's aggressive red/black.
- **Typography:** Bold serif for big-claim headlines (e.g., "MY OLD JEANS FIT AGAIN"), clean sans-serif for body. Often paired with hand-script accent fonts.
- **Photo style:** Mix of:
  1. Studio product-bottle group shots on solid pastel backgrounds (the polished branded statics)
  2. UGC mirror selfies of real-looking middle-aged women in everyday clothes
  3. Influencer-shot UGC (Jaimemichelle, Megan, etc.) — at-home, often wearing white/cream loungewear
- **Vibe:** Aspirational-relatable. "I look like your friend's slightly more put-together cousin who figured something out." Not glamorous. Not gritty.
- **Headline pattern:** Specific outcome + emoji ("Hourglass figure back in 21 days 💕✨", "Estrogen Belly Shrank in Weeks!! 🤩", "Double Chin Vanished in Weeks!! 🤩")
