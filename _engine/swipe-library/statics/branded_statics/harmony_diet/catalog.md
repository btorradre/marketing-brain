## Design DNA

- **Color palette:** Bold saturated backgrounds (deep navy/cobalt blue, deep olive-green) with bright food photography popping forward. Warm pinks/raspberry tones in the Menopause Diet Plan logotype.
- **Typography:** Old-style/transitional serif for the "Menopause Diet" wordmark (looks decorative, almost magazine-cover-style). Sans-serif for testimonial body text.
- **Photo style:** Hero-shot food collages arranged to form **anatomical silhouettes** — variant 1 uses berries (raspberries, blueberries, blackberries) shaped into a stylized uterus/ovary form on cobalt; variant 2 uses grapes on olive green doing the same trick.
- **Layout:** Split-style. Top half = wordmark + "3-WEEK" eyebrow. Bottom half = first-person testimonial in two columns ("I wanted to feel lighter, so I started the Menopause Diet Challenge" | "In three months, I went from 180 lbs to 142 lbs").
- **Overall vibe:** Editorial-meets-wellness, magazine-cover composition, **quietly clinical but feminine**. Not screaming sale — implies authority and program legitimacy.

## Template Potential

### For Lunessa (women's hormone/wellness)
- **HIGH FIT.** The anatomical-food-photography concept (berries as uterus, grapes as ovaries, etc.) translates almost 1:1 to a Lunessa cycle/hormone product. The cobalt + warm-food-photography color story matches Lunessa's brand register.
- **Suggested adaptation:** "The Hormone Reset" wordmark in a serif logotype, food photography in the shape of a uterus/ovary, two-column testimonial overlay with weight or symptom-shift numbers.

### For Motilli (gut motility / digestion)
- **MEDIUM FIT.** The food-as-anatomy device works for gut/digestion ("vegetables shaped like an intestine" or "fiber arranged like a colon cross-section"). The bold-claim "TRY NOW" variant is a stronger fit — Motilli could borrow the "Lose X lbs in 3 months" structure → "Stop bloating in X days" with similar CTA design.

### For Velantra (totes/accessories)
- **LOW FIT** for the anatomical concept — but the **editorial typography + two-column testimonial card** layout transfers cleanly. A Velantra version: hero product shot, two-column testimonial overlay ("I packed in 5 minutes flat" / "and didn't lose a single thing"), serif wordmark "The Boat Tote."

## Notes / Why This Is Interesting

1. The **food-as-anatomy creative concept** is rare and visually arresting in the menopause/weight-loss niche, which is dominated by before/after photos and product-shot layouts. This is the brand's main thumb-stop mechanism.
2. Body copy uses **emoji-prefixed bullet points** — common in the menopause niche, signals "wellness" register without sounding clinical.
3. Identical body copy across creative variants = strong signal this is a **scale-stage brand testing visuals only** with a frozen winning copy block. Worth re-monitoring next rotation to see if variants compress to a single winner.
