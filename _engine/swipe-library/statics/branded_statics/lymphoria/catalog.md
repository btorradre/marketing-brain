## Design DNA

- **Color palette:** Native/UGC across the variants observed. Cell-phone-style photos: man's puffy red face in bed, lung CT scan, beach shirtless dad-bod, group of green-scrub doctors.
- **Typography:** No on-image text — all messaging in the body copy + below-image headline strip. Maximum native disguise.
- **Photo style:** **Confronting, ugly-truth photography.** Close-crop facial puffiness + redness, medical-imaging snapshots (CT scans), unflattering beach photo. Designed to make the viewer wince and then read.
- **Layout:** Single image, no overlays, no logos in-image.
- **Overall vibe:** **Aggressive native-emotional bait running on a sophisticated multi-variant funnel.** Lymphoria is one of the cleanest examples of "doctor-persona + native imagery + medical-claim reframe" we've seen in 2026 to date.

## Statics by Archetype

The Lymphoria visual program is **almost entirely native / UGC.** Of the 6+ variants observed, *zero* qualify as polished branded statics in the strict 8-archetype sense. They are intentionally designed to look like Facebook posts, not designed ads.

That said, the brand is the **most strategically interesting find** of this scrape because:

1. The **body-copy architecture** is highly templated and rotated across variants
2. The **doctor-persona shadow page** strategy is professional-grade (multiple doctor specialties: hepatologist, pulmonologist, etc.)
3. The **mechanism positioning** (lymphatic-drainage drops) is novel in the supplement DR space
4. The **gendered variants** (whiskey-male / wine-female) suggest sophisticated audience segmentation
