## Design DNA

### Color Palette
- **Primary accent:** Deep burgundy/red (#D50000)
- **Warm browns:** RGB 163, 111, 90
- **Background:** Cream (#FFF4EF) — warm, inviting
- **Text:** Dark charcoal (#232323)
- **Overall:** Earth tones + clinical whites — premium wellness positioning

### Typography
- **Body/navigation:** Montserrat (sans-serif) — modern, accessible
- **Headlines:** Trirong (serif) — classical sophistication
- **Contrast strategy:** Elegant serif-sans pairing creates premium feel

### Photography Style
- Clean, minimalist product presentation
- Soft-edged product cards (0.6rem border-radius)
- Subtle shadows — no harsh displays
- Professional product framing positioned as sophisticated self-care

### Social Proof Elements
- Judge.me review widgets with verified badges
- Star ratings prominently displayed
- Review carousel for customer testimonials
- Doctor endorsement content (used in editorial/advertorial)

### CTA Design
- Buttons: 4px radius, 4.7rem height (desktop), 16px uppercase text
- Contrasting button-text against solid backgrounds
- Direct, action-oriented

### Overall Vibe
"Premium women's wellness" — refined earth tones, serif headlines, professional product framing. Probiotics positioned as sophisticated self-care, not clinical medicine. Warm but credible.
