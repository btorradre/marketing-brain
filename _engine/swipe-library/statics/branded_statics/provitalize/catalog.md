## Design DNA

- **Primary Colors:** Muted/desaturated tones — grayscale photography, earth tones, occasional green/teal for product shots. Red for urgency banners.
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif body text. Headlines use conversational/editorial style. Occasional bold red/yellow banners for urgency.
- **Photo Style:** Two distinct approaches:
  1. **Pain-Angle Native:** Grayscale or desaturated photos of women (40-60s) holding lower back, hips, or joints. Feels editorial/stock-photo. NOT polished product shots.
  2. **Product-Forward Branded:** Full product bottle shots with bold text overlays, comparison headlines, and pricing callouts.
- **Layout Pattern:** Primarily single-image with editorial headline above. Link preview below with thebeco.com domain showing.
- **Overall Vibe:** Native/editorial disguise. These are designed to look like health articles, not ads. The pain imagery is intentionally unglamorous to match the avatar's lived experience.

## Multi-Page Strategy

BB Company runs Provitalize ads from AT LEAST 3 different Facebook pages:
1. **Menopause Care And Relief** — Primary page for pain-angle + anti-Ozempic content
2. **BB Company** — Corporate page, same pain-angle creative
3. **Menopause And Me** — Community-style page, more personal/first-person stories

This multi-page approach:
- Diversifies risk (if one page gets flagged, others continue)
- Tests different brand voices against the same product
- Allows A/B testing of page-level trust signals
- Fragments competitor analysis (harder to see full picture)

## Template Potential for Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra

### HIGH POTENTIAL Templates:

1. **Pain-Angle Native Static** — The sacroiliac/back pain format is DIRECTLY applicable to Lunessa (sleep disruption), Motilli (digestive discomfort), and Velantra (energy crashes). Replace back pain photos with:
   - Lunessa: Woman lying awake at 3am, muted blue tones
   - Motilli: Woman holding stomach, uncomfortable expression
   - Velantra: Woman exhausted at desk, mid-afternoon crash

2. **Anti-Competitor Comparison** — The anti-Ozempic angle template works for any "natural vs pharmaceutical" positioning. For Lunessa: "Why women are ditching Ambien for this..." For Velantra: "The problem with energy drinks nobody talks about..."

3. **Multi-Page Strategy** — Consider running from 2-3 pages per product to diversify risk and test brand voices.

4. **Coupon Stacking / Pricing Urgency** — The "Double-Dip Coupon" format with 90-day guarantee is a strong conversion-focused template.

5. **First-Person Pain Story** — The "I'm [age], [condition], [vivid symptom]" opening pattern is highly replicable and powerful for building empathy.

### Adaptation Notes:
- Provitalize is running ~39 active IMAGE ads — they're much more static-heavy than AG1
- Their creative is intentionally low-production-value to feel native/editorial
- The multi-page strategy is sophisticated and worth replicating
- The anti-Ozempic angle shows market awareness — they're actively positioning against GLP-1 drugs
- Pain-angle imagery dominates: 70%+ of their statics show physical discomfort, NOT happy product shots
