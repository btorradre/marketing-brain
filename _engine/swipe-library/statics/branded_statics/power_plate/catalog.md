## Brand Snapshot

Power Plate is a **vibration plate device brand** running a "cellular energy" angle into the metabolic-support keyword space. The ad surfaced on May 2 was a polished branded static showcasing the device hero shot with a long-form key-ideas caption — clearly an existing premium hardware brand pivoting to a direct-response supplement-adjacent narrative ("a wave of energy moves up through your body — not once, not occasionally, but 35 times per second").

The device category (vibration plates) typically lives in the ~$1,500–$3,500 price range, and historically has been sold via demonstrations / wellness centers rather than DTC. Seeing them in an Ad-Library metabolic-support pull is meaningful — it suggests the category is being repositioned for a Meta DR audience. Worth tracking as a potential **Velantra-adjacent competitor** in the "gentle daily energy/recovery" promise space.

## Design DNA

- **Color palette:** Clean, neutral studio background. Black/charcoal device against light gray. No brand-color hits in the static itself — the polish comes from product photography quality, not color-coding.
- **Typography:** Headline lives in the Facebook ad-card text strip below the image, not on the image. The image itself is a clean **product-only hero**.
- **Photo style:** **Studio product photography.** Hardware-brand standard — single device, three-quarter angle, even lighting, no human, no lifestyle context. Reads as "this is a serious piece of equipment."
- **Layout:** Hero device, centered, full bleed. Zero on-image text. The story is told in the long-form caption above the image, not on the image.
- **Overall vibe:** **Premium hardware brand running a direct-response play.** Reads more like an Apple product launch image than a typical supplement static. Differentiated from the rest of the metabolic-support set, which is heavily UGC/native.
