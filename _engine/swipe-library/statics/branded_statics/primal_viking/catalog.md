## Brand DNA

- **Colors:** Dark black/charcoal backgrounds, red/crimson accents for urgency, gold/amber for premium. Occasional deep navy. Very masculine, aggressive color palette.
- **Typography:** Extra-bold condensed sans-serif for headlines, often ALL CAPS. Impact-style urgency fonts. Some ads use stencil/military-style fonts.
- **Photo Style:** Dark, moody product shots. Male physique imagery (silhouettes, transformations). Viking/Norse aesthetic elements. Before/after body composition photos. Chain/lock imagery for "testosterone locked up" concept.
- **Overall Vibe:** Hyper-masculine men's health brand targeting testosterone, cortisol, body composition, and male vitality. Aggressive direct response with fear-based copy (cortisol making men soft, testosterone being "locked up"). Multiple sub-brands (Primal Viking, Primal Edge, Better Performance) running under the same umbrella.
