## Design DNA

- **Color palette:** Muted, lifestyle-photo-driven. Earth tones — wedding scenes, garden settings, older women in soft natural light. Not branded color system; relies on photo emotion.
- **Typography:** Default Facebook ad-card font. **No on-image text overlay** in most variants — the persuasion lives entirely in the body copy + below-image headline.
- **Photo style:** Stock-feeling lifestyle photography. Wedding shot, two older women in a garden, older mother + adult daughter sitting together. **Designed to feel like a personal Facebook post, not an ad.**
- **Layout:** Single-image, no overlays, no logos, no product shots in the hero image. Product/brand only appears in the small below-image headline + descriptor strip.
- **Overall vibe:** **Native/UGC-style emotional bait.** Built to slip under banner-blindness as if it were an organic post a friend shared.
