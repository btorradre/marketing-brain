## Design DNA

- **Primary Colors:** Deep green (#1B5E20), dark navy/charcoal, white text, bright green accent highlights on stats
- **Typography:** Bold serif for headlines ("Results you *can* feel"), clean sans-serif for stat callouts
- **Photo Style:** Professional studio photography of Hugh Jackman in casual athletic wear (blue t-shirt), holding AG1 product. Clean, aspirational, premium feel.
- **Layout Pattern:** Split layout — left side text/stats, right side celebrity + product. Stats use a timeline/progression visual (2 weeks → 1 month → 2 months).
- **Overall Vibe:** Premium, authority-driven, clinical-but-approachable. Leans heavily on celebrity endorsement + data-backed claims.

### New Design DNA Observations (April 2026)

- **Pulled-quote testimonial card is now AG1's dominant static archetype.** Six of seven captured statics use the same template: photo of subject (athlete / professional / older woman) + italic pulled-quote overlay + green coin badge + AG1 wordmark.
- **The "FREE" green coin overlay** appears in multiple statics — recurring branding device that signals welcome-kit/free-bottle offer without screaming "limited time."
- **Subject diversity expanded** from Hugh-Jackman-only (March) to: athlete (tennis), older woman, young professional, "early riser" lifestyle. They are casting against the "for people like you" mirror principle — every demographic gets a face.
- **Two new utility templates added:** the retail-distribution announcement ("New at Target") and the welcome-kit pricing urgency ("$72 Free"). Both lean on hard offers rather than pure brand storytelling.
- **No celebrity-only statics observed in this scrape.** The Hugh Jackman creatives appear to have rotated to video. Statics now lean heavier on real-people testimonial cards.
