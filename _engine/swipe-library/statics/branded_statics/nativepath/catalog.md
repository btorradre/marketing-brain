# NativePath — Branded Statics Catalog

## Brand Info
- **Brand:** NativePath
- **Facebook Page:** @nativepath
- **Followers:** 75.6K (Facebook), 15.5K (Instagram)
- **Category:** Vitamins/supplements — Collagen, CoQ10, skin health, heart health
- **Active Ads:** ~180 total (~26 image-only) (as of 2026-03-28)
- **Discovery Date:** 2026-03-28 (Saturday Discovery Day)

## Design DNA
- **Primary Colors:** Warm neutrals, soft whites, light earth tones — clean wellness palette
- **Typography:** Clean serif/sans-serif mix; editorial headline style
- **Photo Style:** Lifestyle photography (hands holding supplements, glasses of water with capsules); stock-feeling but polished. Some use clinical/body imagery (blue glowing body, brain scans)
- **Overall Vibe:** Editorial wellness — feels more like a health article than an ad. Native/editorial positioning. Softer and more trust-building than hard-sell supplement brands.

## Statics by Archetype

### Medical/Clinical Proof (Primary Archetype)
- **"Your 'normal' blood work is lying to you"** — Multiple versions running with different imagery: red CoQ10 capsules close-up, blue glowing human body figure, anatomical/tissue imagery. All paired with same headline. Clinical/authority framing. CTA: "Learn More". Running since Mar 4-5, 2026.
- **"Important Alert for Statin Users"** — Alert-style banner creative with warning tone. Urgency framing for statin users. Text-heavy editorial approach. CTA: "Learn More". Running since Mar 5, 2026.

### Ingredient-Benefit Callout (Secondary Archetype)
- **CoQ10 Product Shots** — Red capsules against clean backgrounds. Product-forward but in lifestyle context (hand pouring into glass, capsules next to water). Ingredient education focus.

### Authority/Editorial
- **Health Expert Urges Seniors** — Third-person authority framing ("Health Expert urges seniors with dark skin spots and thinning skin to add THIS unique protein to their diet"). Editorial headline structure.
- **Skin Elasticity Focus** — "Forget Expensive Creams: Seniors Are Turning to This Simple Daily Habit to Improve Skin Elasticity Instead" — editorial/listicle framing.

## Key Observations
- Massive ad volume (~180 active) with heavy text-variation testing
- MOST ads are native/editorial style — very few true "branded statics" with designed layouts
- Strong focus on two health angles: heart health (CoQ10/statins) and skin aging (collagen/elasticity)
- Targeting: Seniors 55+ concerned about aging, heart health, skin deterioration
- Ad copy uses fear/urgency language: "lying to you", "alert", "poison", "urgent"
- Multiple ads have "multiple versions" tag — aggressive A/B testing of copy + image combos
- Images are more stock-photo-adjacent than designed branded statics

## Template Potential (Lunessa/Motilli/Velantra)
- **Editorial/authority framing** ("Health Expert urges...") is a strong pattern for feminine health — swap the health angle to match product
- **"Forget [Expensive Solution]" format** translates directly — "Forget Expensive Creams" → "Forget Expensive Hormone Treatments"
- **Medical imagery + clinical language** builds trust — could apply to hormone/menopause products
- **WARNING: Most NativePath statics are NOT polished branded designs** — they're native/editorial images. Less useful as visual templates, more useful as copy/framing templates.
