## Design DNA
- **Primary Colors:** Black, deep red, dark tones — aggressive masculine palette
- **Typography:** Bold condensed sans-serif for headlines; all-caps for CTAs ("PERFORM LIKE A MAN IN HIS PRIME")
- **Photo Style:** Product bottle hero shots on dark/moody backgrounds; muscular male talent for testimonial cards
- **Overall Vibe:** Premium men's performance — clean but aggressive. High contrast. Dark backgrounds make product bottles pop. Red accents for urgency and energy.
