## Ad Architecture Summary

**Narrator:** Rheumatologist (authority disruption — 22 years of practice, now questioning textbooks)
**Architecture:** Story-dominant with authority narrator
**Hook Type:** Authority disruption + patient surprise result
**Length:** ~3,500+ words (~17,184 characters)
**Headline:** Feel Like Yourself Again

### Structural Breakdown:

1. **Hook/Open:** Doctor's patient asks to stop Meloxicam — not giving up, but because imaging showed unprecedented results

2. **Authority Grounding:** 22 years treating osteoarthritis, "half of what walks through my door"

3. **Patient Story (Susan):** 4-year decline, progressive cartilage loss every 6 months, waking at 2-3am from knee pain, hands too stiff to make a fist for first 30 minutes

4. **Failed Treatment Cascade:**
   - Ibuprofen → stomach ulcer
   - Meloxicam → raised blood pressure
   - Cortisone injections → brief relief then accelerated damage
   - Turmeric → stomach issues, no results
   - Glucosamine/chondroitin → 8 months, nothing
   - Collagen supplements → $60/bottle, 3 months, nothing

5. **Generational Stakes:** Susan's mother followed same path: hands → knee → hip → walker → wheelchair → housebound

6. **Mechanism (AMPK/Mitochondria):**
   - Joint supplements fail because mitochondria in joint cells are already dead
   - No cellular energy = can't use nutrients
   - Analogy: "delivering lumber to a construction site with no power"
   - Amla activates AMPK → builds new mitochondria → restores cellular power

7. **Product Entry:** Patient mentioned "something a woman in my Facebook group told me about" — doctor skeptical

8. **Results Timeline:**
   - Week 1: Morning stiffness shorter
   - Week 2: Knee doesn't grind as much, can make fist without wincing
   - Week 3: Walked to mailbox without gripping railing
   - Month 2: Sat through granddaughter's school play
   - Month 3: Stopped reaching for Meloxicam
   - Month 5: Imaging showed cartilage REGROWTH in bone-on-bone areas

9. **Differentiator:** Most Amla is "dead powder" (dried, high-heat processed). Alevia = Himalayan sourced, standardized to active compounds, wildcrafted, third-party tested

10. **Close:** 90-day money-back guarantee, small batch urgency, instruction close

## Key Strategic Insights

1. **One copy, 84+ creatives** — They found one winning narrative and scaled it across dozens of image variations (doctor in scrubs, team photos, surgical settings, hand/joint close-ups)

2. **Authority narrator eliminates skepticism early** — A rheumatologist questioning their own field creates instant credibility

3. **The mechanism (AMPK) is reused across multiple conditions** — Same core mechanism (mitochondrial energy) applied to arthritis, cholesterol, parasites, neuropathy. One mechanism framework, multiple angle entries.

4. **PDP funnel** — The long-form copy does ALL the belief-shifting work. The landing page just needs to close the sale.

5. **Massive scaling velocity** — 0 to 84 ads in one month suggests high confidence in the copy + rapid creative testing
