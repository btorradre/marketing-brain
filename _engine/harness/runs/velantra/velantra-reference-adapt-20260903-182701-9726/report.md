The reference turned out to be structurally different from what its own label suggests.

The reference product is a soft synthetic duffel built around zippers, a trolley sleeve,
a padded laptop pocket and a sealed bottom compartment. Ours is a structured
leather-over-canvas bag with no zipper anywhere. Tracing was never an option, so the
redirect is the whole job: the beat functions, cut rhythm, energy curve and script flow
moves were kept exactly, and every claim in the ladder was rebuilt from our own
construction facts.

## Decisions and assumptions

**Live product truth beat the skill in three places.** The live page was fetched before
any claim was written.
1. Colorways are now four (Light Chocolate, Army Green, Dark Chocolate, and Black on
   pre-order for mid September 2026). The product skill records three.
2. The live page reads "Packs 2 to 3 day trips." The script says 3 days of clothes and
   goes no higher.
3. **Interior lining conflict, unresolved and flagged.** The live page says "Natural
   cotton-canvas lining with a leather slip pocket." Brooks's own 2026-08-08 footage of
   the physical bag shows a smooth caramel leather lining, and the product record notes
   that the cotton-canvas description was already caught and corrected once as an error.
   The live page wins by the workflow rule, but here the live page contradicts the
   physical object. Rather than assert either, the script claims only the leather slip
   pocket, which both sources agree on, and never names the lining material. The page
   copy should be corrected. This is on the board as an open conflict.

**No colorway count in the voiceover.** The fourth colorway is on pre-order, and the
house law requires a pre-order claim to carry a ship date. Naming a count would either
break that law or force a ship date into a creator's mouth. The count is dropped and
three shipping colorways appear on screen instead.

**Three reference claims could not be ported and were replaced, not softened.**
The personal-item and baggage-fee claim is false for an 18 inch wide bag and would become
a support ticket, so the overhead bin claim (live PDP copy) takes that slot. The padded
laptop pocket does not exist, so the beat shows a laptop going in flat against the leather
slip pocket, which keeps the demonstration and shrinks the claim to what is true. The
shoulder-carry relief line is impossible on our short rolled handles, so the forearm carry
with hands free takes that slot.

**The superlative slot was the load-bearing swap.** The reference reserves its longest
hold and its only slowdown for "my favorite feature is the bottom compartment." That slot
now carries the one-piece flap folding all the way back, which is the strongest fact we
own and the one thing on the board that no competitor can copy.

**Angle sourcing is honest about a gap.** There is no women's Weekender VoC in the folder.
VEL-A-002 rests on three real verbatims (the size objection and the slouch objection from
the 8/31 men's comment pull, the no-logo reward from the ICP), and the compromise-bag
framing on top of them is labelled [INFERRED] in the bank with its reasoning shown. It was
not promoted to a quote. A dr-voc-mining pass on women's weekender language is the next
research job and is recorded as the bank's biggest gap.

**One instruction conflict, resolved in favour of the workflow spec.** The dr-os pipeline
guide carries a mirror-fidelity law that says reference adaptations should trace the
reference. The workflow spec for this run says never trace and redirect instead. The
workflow spec governed. In this case the two would have collided regardless, because a
one-to-one copy of a zippered duffel is not producible with our bag.

**Runtime.** The reference runs 81.15 seconds at roughly 190 words per minute. Our script
is about 280 words, which lands near 85 seconds at the same pace. Cut count and rhythm
match the reference (28 cuts, mean 3.0 seconds against their 2.90, nothing over 4 seconds).

**Self-audit.** Swap test passes: the reference's own bag fails five of the six
construction lines. Six-month test passes: no season, no holiday, no calendar beat, and
the only dated line is the offer. Every claim has a demonstration beat on its own card.
The bag is on screen in all 20 scenes. The creator says "they," never "our." No em dashes,
no negation-then-affirmation beat, no competitor mention, no origin claim, no
personification, no invented spec, no Birkin or Hermes anywhere in script, prompts or
cards. Law gate clean on the script and on all three saved artifacts.
