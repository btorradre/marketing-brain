## About the role

We are a DTC health and wellness brand running paid acquisition at volume on Meta, TikTok, and YouTube. We are hiring a full-time video editor who already lives inside AI tooling and can turn a script plus a reference ad into a finished, direct-response video that sells.

This is a real editing job, not a "learn AI on the job" job. Almost everything we ship is AI-generated: AI avatars, AI voiceover, AI b-roll, AI mechanism animation, AI product motion. You will be handed a script, a reference ad, product references, and a brief, and you are expected to come back with an ad that holds attention from second one to the last frame.

Every AI tool is provided and paid for by us. You never pay for a credit.

## What you'll do

- Ship around 3 net new concepts per day from scripts and briefs we give you, plus hook and intro variants off whatever is already working. Lengths run from 15 second hook tests to 2 and 3 minute VSLs
- Generate assets with AI tools: talking-head avatars, voiceover, lifestyle b-roll, mechanism and "inside the body" animation, product motion, lip sync
- Replicate references one to one. We send a reference ad plus a brief and you match its structure, pacing, and energy for our product, rebuilding every frame rather than tracing it
- Decide which clip goes where. We hand you the script and the assets. The judgement we cannot hand you is knowing which visual belongs under which line so the story builds instead of just playing. When the voiceover names a problem, the viewer sees that problem. When it names the mechanism, they see the mechanism. One idea per shot, and the shot proves the line
- Cut for retention on long-form. Direct response supplement ads live or die on the first 3 seconds and then on whether the viewer makes it past the 20 second mark
- Build hooks that stop the scroll and speak directly to our customer. A hook that gets random views is worthless. The first seconds have to make our exact buyer feel "this is about me," which means you need to actually understand who she is, what she has already tried, and what she is worried about, and you will get a full avatar breakdown from us to work from. You batch hook variants on winning bodies
- Add captions, sound design, music, b-roll layering, and text overlays that feel native to the platform, not like a template
- Iterate fast on feedback and keep files organized so anyone on the team can find a clip six weeks later

## What the work is actually like

The generation itself is fast and cheap. Our tools do the heavy lifting, the prompts and workflows are already built, and the briefs are detailed. The work is simple and repeatable once you know the system. What we pay for is speed, taste, and consistency: hitting the daily count without the quality sliding, and knowing which cut holds a viewer and which one loses them.

## Reference work, and how close is close

A large part of this role is swipe work. We will send you an ad that is spending real money and ask you to rebuild it for our product. The skill we are testing is judgement about what to copy and what to leave behind.

You copy the structure: beat order, cut rhythm, where the hook turns, when the product enters, how the close is stacked. You do not copy the claim, the product, the footage, or the brand's specific story. Every frame gets rebuilt from our assets and our product truth.

If you can watch a reference and tell us in plain language what is actually doing the work in it, you will do well here. If your instinct is to match it shot for shot without asking why a shot exists, you will not.

## Health category rules you have to respect

We sell a supplement, which means the copy is regulated and the script is not a suggestion.

- The voiceover matches the approved script word for word. You never rewrite a line to make it flow better, and you never add a claim in an on-screen caption that is not already in the script
- No cure language, no disease claims, no fabricated before and after imagery, no invented doctor endorsements, no fake clinical results on screen
- Product truth is written down and it is non-negotiable: the jar, the cap, the gummy shape, the gummy color. Getting these wrong is an auto-fail, not a note
- Anything that looks like a glossy 3D render instead of real footage gets rejected and regenerated

None of this is hard once you have read the rules. It just requires actually reading them.
