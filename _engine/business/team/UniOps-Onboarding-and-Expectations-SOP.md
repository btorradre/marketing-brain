# PART 1: UniOps

## 1.1 What UniOps is

UniOps is the app we use to give you access to our paid tools without ever sending you a password.

Here is the problem it solves. We pay for a stack of AI and creative tools: Higgsfield, Canva, ChatGPT, Claude, ElevenLabs, Gmail, Kalodata, and more get added regularly. In the old way of working, a new hire gets passwords pasted into a chat, and then every time they log in from a new place the owner has to hand over a 2FA code at 2 in the morning. That is bad for everyone.

With UniOps, you open a tool through the UniOps desktop app and you are simply logged in. You never see a password. You never need a 2FA code. You never pay for a credit or a subscription out of your own pocket, ever.

**In one line:** UniOps is your front door to every tool we pay for.

It also measures how much time you spend working inside those tools, which is how we track the 40 hours. Section 5 explains exactly what it records and what it does not. Read that section carefully so there are no surprises.

## 1.2 What you need before you start

- A computer running macOS (Apple Silicon or Intel) or Windows
- The email address you gave us during hiring
- A phone with an authenticator app installed (Google Authenticator, Authy, or 1Password all work)
- A stable internet connection

## 2. Setup (do this on day one, takes about 15 minutes)

### Step 1: Accept your invite and verify your email

- [ ] You will receive an invite email from **team@uniops.app**. Click the link inside.
- [ ] Check your spam or junk folder if it has not arrived within 5 minutes.
- [ ] Click the verification link in the email.

**If the link says "invalid token" or "expired":** verification links expire after 24 hours. Go to the sign-in page and click **Resend verification email**, then use the fresh link.

**If nothing arrives after 10 minutes:** email team@uniops.app, and message us in `[TEAM CHAT]` so we know you are blocked.

### Step 2: Set up 2FA on your own account

- [ ] Follow the prompts to add UniOps to your authenticator app.
- [ ] **Save your backup codes somewhere safe.** Not in your Downloads folder. A password manager or a printed note.

This 2FA is for your UniOps account only. You will never need 2FA codes for the tools themselves, because UniOps handles that.

**If you ever lose access to your authenticator:** email team@uniops.app from your registered address with the subject line **2FA reset request**. They verify your identity and reset it within one business day. Tell us in `[TEAM CHAT]` too, because you will be locked out until it is done.

### Step 3: Download and install the desktop app

- [ ] Go to **https://uniops.app** and click the download button for your operating system.
  - Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 through M4): the standard Mac download
  - Mac (Intel): use the "Intel Mac? Download here" link
  - Windows: the Windows download
- [ ] Install it the normal way for your system.
- [ ] Open the app and sign in with your UniOps email, password, and 2FA code.

**The desktop app is required.** Our tools are only accessible through it. You cannot do this job from a normal browser tab.

### Step 4: Set your tracked applications

This step matters, especially for editors. Please do not skip it.

UniOps can measure the time you spend in desktop applications outside of it (your video editor, for example). It does this from a **tracked apps list that you control yourself in Settings**. For accounts created from August 2026 onward, that list starts **empty**. Nothing is tracked unless you put it there.

- [ ] **Editors:** open Settings and add the editing applications you actually work in. CapCut, Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, whichever you use.
- [ ] **VAs:** add any desktop application you spend real working time in outside of the UniOps browser.

**Why you want to do this:** if your editing app is not on the list, the hours you spend cutting in it do not show up as worked time. Adding it protects you, not us. If you do the work, we want it counted.

UniOps only checks two things about an app on that list: is it running, and is it the window in front. It cannot read window titles and it cannot see anything inside the app.

### Step 5: Confirm you are in

- [ ] Open one assigned tool through UniOps to confirm access works.
- [ ] Post in `[TEAM CHAT]`: "UniOps setup done, tools open fine."
- [ ] Tell us immediately if a tool you need is missing from your list.

## 3. Using UniOps every day

### 3.1 Opening a tool

Open the UniOps desktop app, find the tool you need, and open it. You are logged in. That is the whole flow.

### 3.2 Sessions: one person at a time

A "tool" in UniOps is a shared account. When you open it, you take a **session** on it, and while you hold that session nobody else on the team can use that tool. Everyone with access can see your name against it.

This has practical consequences:

- **Release the session when you are done.** Do not hold ChatGPT for four hours because you might need it later. Someone else is waiting.
- **If a tool shows as "pending" or busy,** the person holding it may have walked away. Message `[TEAM CHAT]` and ask them to release it. An admin can also end a stuck session from Dashboard, then Sessions.
- **Plan around it.** If you know you need a specific tool for a long block, say so in chat so nobody is blocked without warning.

Holding sessions you are not using is the single fastest way to annoy the rest of the team. Take it, use it, release it.

### 3.3 Start of shift

- [ ] Open the UniOps desktop app and sign in
- [ ] Post "in" in `[TEAM CHAT]` with what you are working on today
- [ ] Check `[BRIEF BOARD]` (editors) or `[HELPDESK]` (VAs) for what is queued

### 3.4 End of shift

- [ ] Release every tool session you are holding
- [ ] Post your end of day summary in `[TEAM CHAT]` (see Section 6.4)
- [ ] **Quit the UniOps desktop app completely.** Not minimize. Quit.

**Why quitting matters:** the app keeps measuring while it is open. It does not stop at the end of your working hours and it does not stop when you sign out. Leaving it running overnight or over a weekend keeps the clock going, and that time gets credited to whoever was last signed in on that computer. Quitting the app is what stops it. Do not leave it open.

### 3.5 If your session drops

Sessions can drop for three reasons: your internet cut out and the connection timed out after about 2 minutes, an admin ended the session, or you were idle past the workspace timeout. Reopen the tool and carry on. If "Connection lost" keeps appearing, restart the desktop app. If it keeps happening after that, tell us and email team@uniops.app.

## 4. The rules

These are not suggestions. Breaking them is grounds for removal from the team.

1. **Never log into our tools outside of UniOps.** Not with a browser extension, not on your phone, not on a second device. UniOps is the only door.
2. **Never share your UniOps account.** One human per account. Not a friend, not a family member, not a second freelancer you have quietly subcontracted to. Every session is logged with a name against it.
3. **Never upgrade a plan, buy credits, change a payment method, or touch billing on any tool.** If you run out of credits or need a higher tier, message `[YOUR EMAIL]` and we will handle it the same day. Unauthorized upgrades hit our card and they are treated seriously.
4. **Never connect your own personal accounts** to a workspace tool, and never store credentials in UniOps that you do not have the right to store.
5. **Never use our tools for anything outside our work.** No personal projects, no client work for anyone else, no reselling access.
6. **Quit the desktop app at the end of every shift.** See 3.4.
7. **Do not falsify hours.** Leaving the app open to accumulate time, or running an app in the foreground while doing nothing, is fraud, not a workaround. We look at output alongside hours, and the two have to make sense together.
8. **Customer data never leaves the helpdesk.** No screenshots of customer information into personal chats, no sharing one customer's details with another, no exporting lists. (VAs specifically.)

## 5. What UniOps tracks, in plain language

We would rather you hear this from us clearly than discover it later and feel misled. Here is exactly what the system records.

### It DOES record:

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Presence and active time** | When you are signed in and active, and how much of that time is spent inside a connected tool versus elsewhere in the app. Measurement stops while your machine is asleep or locked. Time counts while a UniOps window is the one in front, whether or not you are touching the keyboard. |
| **Away-from-keyboard stretches** | While you have a tool open, it records any continuous stretch of **12 minutes or more** where UniOps was not the window in front, or your machine was locked or asleep, or you were not touching the keyboard or mouse. It records a start time and an end time for that stretch and nothing else. |
| **Tool usage** | Which tools you open and for how long, with a row per session showing when you opened it and when you released it. |
| **Desktop application usage** | For the applications on your own tracked apps list only (Step 4). It checks whether each one is running and whether it is the window in front. Nothing else. |
| **Presence status** | Active, away, or offline is visible to other members of the workspace. Whoever is holding a tool session is shown by name to everyone with access to that tool. |

### It does NOT record:

- **No screenshots.** Ever.
- **No keystroke logging.**
- **No page titles, no message contents, no file contents.**
- **No browsing history.** The desktop app keeps a local history file for its own address bar suggestions, and that never leaves your computer.
- **Nothing at all about what you do outside the app** beyond the tracked apps list you built yourself.

### What this means for you

Nobody is reading over your shoulder. We cannot see what you type, what you write, or what you look at. What we can see is: were you signed in, were you in a tool, for how long, and which tool.

On the away-from-keyboard stretches: whether those get subtracted from your worked hours is a setting on the platform, and **it is currently off**. We are not going to dock you for standing up to make coffee or for a 20 minute lunch. What we do watch for is a pattern, for example eight hours logged with six hours of away stretches inside it. If we see that, we will talk to you about it rather than silently deducting anything.

Practical note: thinking time, reading a brief, and watching reference footage all count as work, and if you are doing them inside UniOps or inside a tracked app they get measured correctly. This is another reason to complete Step 4 properly.

# PART 2: What we expect from you

## 6. Expectations for everyone

### 6.1 Hours

**40 hours per week.** This is a full time role and we treat it as one.

- 5 working days per week, roughly 8 hours per day
- Your working window is `[SHIFT WINDOW]`
- Hours are measured through UniOps. If you are working, you should be signed in and working inside the tools.
- Editors: heavy concept weeks can run longer, up to 50 or 60 hours. Those weeks are the exception, not the standard, and we will tell you in advance when one is coming.

If you cannot hit 40 hours in a given week for any reason, tell us **before** the week ends, not after. A heads up is fine. A silent short week is not.

### 6.2 Availability and time off

- Planned time off: at least **7 days notice** in `[TEAM CHAT]`
- Sick or emergency: message us as early as you can that morning
- Public holidays in your country: send us your list in your first week so we can plan around it
- Disappearing without a message for a full working day is treated as abandoning the role

### 6.3 Communication

- Reply to messages from the team within **30 minutes** during your working window
- If you are stuck for more than 30 minutes on anything, ask. Do not spend three hours quietly blocked.
- Ask questions in `[TEAM CHAT]` rather than direct messages where possible, so answers are useful to everyone
- Write in full sentences. Clear written English is part of the job.
- Weekly check-in call: `[CHECK-IN]`. Attendance expected.

### 6.4 End of day summary

Every working day, before you quit the app, post this in `[TEAM CHAT]`:

```
EOD [date]
Done: [what you finished, with links]
In progress: [what is half done and where it stands]
Blocked: [anything waiting on us, or "nothing"]
Tomorrow: [what you are picking up next]
```

It takes two minutes and it removes the need for us to chase anyone.

### 6.5 Pay

- Paid `[PAY DAY]`
- All tools, software, and credits are provided by us. You never pay for a credit. If you are ever about to spend your own money on something for this job, stop and message us first.

## 7. Expectations: Video Editors

### 7.1 Output standard

**Around 5 net new concepts per day.** That is the bar, roughly 25 per week.

- Most finished ads land between **10 and 35 seconds**
- You will be handed a script, a brief, a reference ad, and product references. You come back with a finished ad.
- Revision notes get turned around **the same working day** wherever the note is a cut, a timing change, a caption fix, or a swap. Bigger reworks get a timeline from you within the hour.

If a concept is going to take much longer than usual, say so early. What we cannot work with is finding out at 6 PM that the day produced one ad.

### 7.2 Quality bar

- **Hook first.** The first 2 seconds decide everything. Both the visual and the on-screen text have to stop the scroll.
- **A visual change every few seconds.** No dead air, no static holds, no shot that outstays its purpose.
- **No 3D render look.** If a frame looks like a 3D model or a video game asset instead of something filmed, it gets regenerated. This is a hard rule across every brand we run, no exceptions.
- **No AI-glossy people.** Real skin texture, real hands, real hair, natural light. If it reads as AI on a phone screen at arm's length, it does not ship.
- **Match the reference without copying it.** When we send a reference ad, we want its pacing, structure, and energy applied to our product. We do not want a frame by frame trace.
- **Product accuracy is absolute.** Our products have locked construction details, and the AI engines get them wrong by default in specific repeatable ways. You will get a product truth document per product. Compare your output against the reference photos before you submit, every time. An ad with a wrong product detail is a dead ad no matter how good the edit is.
- **Never let an AI engine put text into the image.** The engines misspell constantly. All on-screen text is added afterward as an overlay in CapCut or Canva.

### 7.3 Files and delivery

- Everything goes to `[DELIVERY FOLDER]`, organized so anyone on the team can find it without asking you
- Keep your project files, raw generations, and exports separate and labeled
- Never delete raw assets after export. We reuse them constantly.

### 7.4 Tools

Everything is provided through UniOps. If you need a tool we have not given you access to, ask. Do not sign up for it yourself, and do not use a personal account for our work.

## 8. Expectations: Customer Support VAs

### 8.1 The role

**40 hours per week on customer support.** You are the voice of the brand to every customer who contacts us. Your job is to resolve their problem fast, keep them happy, and keep them from disputing a charge.

You will work out of `[HELPDESK]`. Each brand has its own support SOP covering products, policies, refund flows, and response templates. Read the SOP for your assigned brand fully before you answer a single ticket, and keep it open while you work.

### 8.2 Response time SLA

Every incoming message gets classified and answered within these windows. This is the core measurement of the role.

| Priority | Respond within | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 Critical** | **1 hour** | Chargeback threat, allergic reaction, any health concern |
| **P2 High** | **4 hours** | Refund request, missing or damaged order, payment issue |
| **P3 Standard** | **12 hours** | Order status, tracking, general question |
| **P4 Low** | **24 hours** | Product question, subscription management |

**Zero tickets are left unanswered at the end of your shift.** If a ticket cannot be resolved, it still gets a human reply telling the customer where things stand and when they will hear back.

### 8.3 Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Average first response time | Under 4 hours |
| Resolved on first contact | Above 80% |
| Refund requests saved through retention | Above 40% |
| Customer satisfaction | Above 4.5 out of 5 |
| Tickets left unanswered at end of shift | Zero |

### 8.4 Escalate immediately, do not improvise

Send these straight to `[YOUR EMAIL]` and flag them in `[TEAM CHAT]` the moment they appear:

- Any chargeback threat ("dispute", "call my bank", "fraud")
- Any health or safety issue (allergic reaction, hospital, illness)
- Any legal threat, lawyer, or regulator
- Any press or influencer inquiry
- Anything involving a customer's data being exposed
- Anything you are not sure about

Escalating something that turned out to be routine is never a problem. Guessing on something that turned out to be serious is.

### 8.5 Absolute rules for support

1. **Verify the order number and email before sharing any account detail.** Always.
2. **Never make a medical claim or a medical recommendation.** For any health question the answer is to consult their healthcare provider.
3. **Never process a refund before running the retention flow** in the brand SOP.
4. **Never ignore a chargeback threat.** That is P1, always, immediately.
5. **Never share one customer's data with another customer or any third party.**
6. **Log every single interaction** in `[TICKET LOG]` with: category, priority, resolution, retention attempted (yes or no), escalated (yes or no), and outcome. A ticket that is not logged did not happen.

### 8.6 Tone

Warm, human, and on the customer's side. Acknowledge the frustration before you jump to the solution. Never clinical, never corporate, never condescending. Use the customer's own words back to them. Be honest about timelines and never overpromise, because an overpromise today is a chargeback in two weeks.

### 8.7 Weekly

Every Friday, send a short summary covering:

- Ticket volume and breakdown by category
- SLA compliance (any tickets that missed their window, and why)
- Refunds and chargebacks, with the reason for each
- Any pattern you noticed: a product complaint repeating, a shipping route going slow, a page confusing people. You see the customers before we do. Tell us what you see.

## 9. Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Verification email never arrived | Check spam. Wait 5 minutes. Click "Resend verification email" on the sign-in page. Still nothing after 10 minutes, email team@uniops.app. |
| Verification link expired or "invalid token" | Links expire after 24 hours. Request a fresh one from the verify-email page. |
| "Email not verified" on login | Your account exists but verification was not completed. Resend and click the link first. |
| Lost your 2FA / authenticator | Email team@uniops.app from your registered address, subject **2FA reset request**. Reset within one business day. |
| A tool session is stuck as "pending" | The holder may be idle. Ask in `[TEAM CHAT]`. An admin can end it from Dashboard, then Sessions. |
| Session dropped unexpectedly | Usually a network drop (auto-releases after about 2 minutes), an admin ending it, or idle timeout. Check Dashboard, then Sessions, and reopen. |
| "Connection lost" keeps appearing | Brief network interruption. The app retries on its own. If it persists, restart the app. |
| Desktop app will not open or shows a blank screen | Quit and relaunch, then check for an update in the system tray. If it persists, email team@uniops.app with your OS version and app version. |
| "Forbidden" or 403 on the dashboard | Sign out and back in. If it continues, your role may not have that permission. Tell us. |
| "Something went wrong" / crash | Note the reference code shown on the error screen and email it to team@uniops.app. |
| A tool you need is missing from your list | Message `[YOUR EMAIL]`. Do not sign up for it yourself. |

**UniOps support:** team@uniops.app, or the **Help** button in the bottom right of the dashboard. They respond within one business day (Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM ET). For a full lockout, put **URGENT** in the subject line and they prioritize it.

**Always tell us in `[TEAM CHAT]` too** when you are blocked, so we know why you are not working and can unblock you from our side.

## 10. When you leave

Whenever the role ends, whether that is your choice or ours, access to every tool is revoked in a single click from the UniOps dashboard. There is nothing for you to uninstall, no passwords to change, and no accounts left in your name.

What we do ask on the way out: hand over your in progress work and your file structure, and leave the `[DELIVERY FOLDER]` or `[TICKET LOG]` in a state the next person can pick up.
