# YIXBhp — visual storytelling reference audit

Reference: https://app.trendtrack.io/share/ads/resilia-YIXBhp  
Audit date: September 10, 2026. Original downloaded to `source.mp4`; source URL/hash in `provenance.json`.

## What this concept is doing

This is an **inside-the-body guided demonstration**, carried by a recurring male commentator. Its primary visual material is endoscope-like CGI: a camera seems to enter the body, encounter an antagonist, watch its actions, then watch a two-stage intervention. The human phone-style inserts make selected symptoms and habits recognizable. The final act shifts toward ingredients, provenance, product, claimed demand and a click instruction.

This reference is useful precisely because it does **not** open on a person displaying discomfort. It opens on an unusual internal view plus a guide promising to show something hidden. Human agitation enters once the internal story has a consequence the viewer can recognize. The transferable decision is to choose footage for the current narrative job: hidden mechanism gets a legible process image; felt symptom gets a particular human action; synthesis gets the guide; ingredients get physical material; the close gets the actual item and action to take.

All mechanism/efficacy statements below describe **the advertiser's story**, not established medical facts. The images do not establish parasite presence, explain a person's symptoms, verify ingredient effects, or substantiate dosage/time promises. CGI is an illustration technique, not observed clinical evidence. The user called the supplied references high-converting; no conversion or retention data was available, so this audit makes no causal performance claim.

## Strong reusable rules supported by the visuals

1. **Match the image to the verb, not merely the topic.** Nutrient interception, egg persistence, nerve signaling and shield opening have different actions. The symptom montage similarly uses tugging a waistband, holding a forehead, lying on a sofa, eating, waking, rubbing a knee and showing skin. A generic unhappy person would erase those distinctions.
2. **Move between explanation and lived consequence.** The ad repeatedly leaves internal views at the instant the story names a felt symptom or everyday behavior, then returns to the proposed source. A useful planning pattern is mechanism → recognizable consequence → mechanism interpretation.
3. **Use the presenter for pointing, chapter changes and synthesis.** The keyed face appears when the viewer is told to look somewhere. It vanishes when a demonstration needs room. Full-frame face passages tend to summarize or bridge, especially after ingredients and treatment stages.
4. **Change scientific scale for a reason.** At 49.600–59.300, the visual sequence moves local nerve → torso/brain → local cluster → microscopic signal → whole-system diagram. This is materially more explanatory than endlessly revisiting one translucent torso.
5. **Treat montage lists differently from demonstrations.** Seven symptom inserts across 7.134 seconds work as recognition flashes; the 6.200-second black-fluid shot provides setup, action and visible outcome within one view. Average cutting speed alone would be the wrong rule.
6. **Give sequential mechanisms distinct visual identities.** Gold first-stage fluid and black second-stage fluid distinguish roles and preserve order. Any new ad should use an accurate, evidence-supported mechanism; copy the clarity of sequencing, not these unsupported biological claims.
7. **Make relief behavioral.** Laptop work, discarding a sweet and restful sleep show changed life activities. They are more specific than a string of smiling portraits. Do not label unrelated actors as the same customer's transformation.
8. **Converge at the close.** Origin detail becomes two inputs, then a single capsule, then the package and exact click gesture. B-roll stops expanding the story and helps the viewer identify the item and next step.

## Pacing, captions and transitions

There are 93 mapped visual segments, averaging 1.966 seconds, median 1.733 seconds. This includes an overlay-only change, so it is not a count of 93 unique underlying clips. Most entries are hard cuts. The conspicuous early exception is a zoom-blur transition at 6.400 seconds. Shot movement mostly comes from camera drift/forward travel, moving liquid/particles, natural human gestures or hand-held product display, rather than ornamental transitions.

Captions are small white phrases with a dark outline around the middle/lower-middle of the portrait image. The opening adds a fixed red/yellow headline; it disappears with the 6.400-second transition. Captions often replace within a shot. Cuts tend to follow new visual propositions or list items, but a new noun does not demand a new clip: four nutrient labels remain in one colored-object view at 38.700–41.833. `shot-map.json` contains timestamped ASR overlap for checking line/shot alignment; timing is source evidence, not prescribed timing for a future narration.

## Variety audit and limitations to avoid copying

The human symptom montage is notably varied in person, room, scale and action. The scientific sections are much less varied: numerous shots repeat the same circular tunnel aesthetic and similarly colored coated blobs. Their actions differ, but a new skill should not treat different filenames or tiny framing changes as sufficient variety. Use distinct scientifically appropriate views when a new mechanism idea genuinely changes the explanatory question.

Repeated commentator coverage is an intentional continuity anchor. The late split landscapes visibly reuse the geographic images just shown separately; that repetition functions as a two-origin recap. The later golden-fluid flush resembles the earlier worm-tunnel composition, but original component media are unavailable, so exact source-clip reuse is **not certified**. Finished-frame captions and overlays also prevent a simple pixel-hash audit from proving clip uniqueness.

Human inserts look compatible with phone/social footage, but the original capture device, TikTok source, stock library and generation model cannot be established from this finished ad. Do not claim these were found on TikTok or shot on an iPhone. Likewise, the leafy plant, mountain, soil, lab and people imagery are not provenance, laboratory or testimonial documentation.
