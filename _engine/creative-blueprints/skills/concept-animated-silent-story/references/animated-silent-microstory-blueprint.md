## Concept and script architecture

A short visual cause-and-effect story sets up desire, hides one fact, reveals it and resolves through a readable reaction.

Visual action script: want → request/response → consequence → withheld information revealed → confirming action → final twist/reaction. No invented narration or mandatory explanatory captions.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Stable characters/prop, legible cause and effect, protected reveal time and a payoff understandable with sound muted.

**Adaptable:** Premise, characters, hidden information, setting, prop and emotional/comic ending. Source joke is not a customer or relationship claim.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 — Immediate want | Character points/reaches/looks toward a desirable product; partner response visible. **A** | Primary two-shot; action in first frame → object of desire understood. | 1.5–3s. | Eyeline, gesture and object establish the premise without speech. |
| S2 — Consequence | A clear transaction or response confirms the request had an effect. **A** | Cut/brief overlap after request → hold result of action. | 1.5–3s; no disconnected montage. | The next state follows causally from the first. |
| S3 — Protect hidden information | Character enters new setting and performs the action that will reveal the secret. **A** | Hard cut on phase change; keep revealing area concealed until intended action. | 3–6s; allow setup gesture to complete. | Anticipation requires the viewer to see enough cause before the payoff. |
| S4 — Reveal and recognition | Camera/door/character move exposes a distinct surprising context. **A** | Reveal trigger → hold until hidden fact can be recognized. | 1.5–3s reveal/recognition; may continue S3’s same base shot. | Recognition time is necessary; cutting immediately would lose the joke. |
| S5 — Confirm meaning | Character places or uses the same prop in the revealed situation. **D** | Closer angle after reveal → complete physical contact/result. | 1.5–3s. | The specific prop ties the earlier action to the new information. |
| S6 — Twist and reaction | A new request or changed response reframes the situation; finish on reaction. **A** | Time/location change → readable twist → response resolves. | 2–4s; optional brief target tag after payoff. | The ending gives the sequence a clear emotional/comic conclusion. |

## Required asset roles

Action script; character/wardrobe/prop/world references; reveal geography; each scene’s first/last states; cue-based sound plan. No dialogue is required.

## Instantiate and hand off
