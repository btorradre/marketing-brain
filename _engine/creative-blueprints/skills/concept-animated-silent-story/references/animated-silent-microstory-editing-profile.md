## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame causal action. Use two-shot for request, closer action for consequence, wider reveal and reaction framing. Covering details remain in the same animated world.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut after the causal action can be understood, not when a fixed duration expires. Protect the first moment the secret is readable and the final reaction. Do not break a revealing door/hand move mid-cause.

## Transitions and handles

Hard cut for new story phase. Proposed brief time-compression overlap 0.10–0.25s only where both compositions remain legible; count overlap once and preserve handles. Never dissolve away the reveal itself.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Default no external B-roll. A prop close-up is motivated confirmation: enter after the reveal, hold through placement/contact and return to the character for payoff. Unrelated reaction stock would break the world.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Use eyelines and physical reveal before extra effects. Track both characters for request, widen for hidden context, settle for recognition. Camera must not expose the secret prematurely.

## Captions and graphic events

Default no editorial captions. Diegetic phone/sign content must remain legible if it carries the joke. Optional brand tag is separate; do not use explanatory paragraphs to rescue unclear staging.

## Voice, music and sound design

Design original action accents and music around request, consequence, reveal and reaction. A pause or held sound can protect anticipation. Source no-speech finding is model-assisted; exact source sound timing is not certified.

## Image finish and time treatment

Consistent stylized materials, character age/body, wardrobe and prop size. No accidental morphing as transition; no unnecessary speed ramps that shorten the readable action.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Resolve the twist and reaction before optional CTA. A loop may reconnect desire only if understandable; do not crop away the punchline to make it loop.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by a simpler premise/fewer causal steps, not by removing recognition. Lengthen only for added meaningful action or reaction. Test silent comprehension before adding audio.

## Reject and verify

Reject age/wardrobe drift, wrong carried prop, extra limbs, incorrect door geography, unreadable phone, premature reveal and incomplete reaction. Inspect every transition frame.
