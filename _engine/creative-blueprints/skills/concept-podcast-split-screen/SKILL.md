# Podcast-style split-screen listicle

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md), [R02 audit](../../research/R02/analysis.md), and the existing broll-podcast skill when producing inserts. R02 is a single speaker with podcast visual cues, not verified footage from an actual interview. Its AI provenance is unknown.

## Argument and delivery

Choose this format when the viewer needs an unfolding argument and a stable human anchor. R02 presents five desirable benefits as ironic reasons to avoid the product: longevity, attention, concentration, label/status and overpaying. The speaker's dry warning conflicts playfully with the desirability of the outcome. That rhetorical inversion is the reusable device; source claims and exact phrasing are not.

For a target, map the current copy SOP to the actual argument before polishing delivery. Keep UMP before UMS if mechanisms are discussed. Each reason needs a distinct supported proposition; do not manufacture a numbered list with five versions of the same benefit. Ensure irony is understandable without misleading the viewer about risks or facts. Use authentic VOC and documented claims, not invented testimonial counts or laboratory history.

## Picture-to-word roles

| Function | Insert and reason |
|---|---|
| Physical action or product use | Show the actual object being handled at the action cue. |
| Sensory description | A distinct texture/ingredient metaphor can evoke the sensation; label it internally as metaphor, not ingredient evidence. R02 uses sugar/caramel imagery for scent. |
| Mechanism | Show an appropriate diagram/physical process only if substantiated; a molecular animation does not prove efficacy. |
| Emotional/social outcome | Use a relevant reaction or clearly hypothetical scene. Stock/generated people are not verified customers. |
| Numbers, price, guarantee | Provide readable verified information at the cue; unrelated romantic footage cannot substantiate a policy. |
| Sarcastic turn or chapter introduction | Return to the speaker so expression and phrasing land. |

Keep speaker identity, wardrobe, microphone and room consistent. Use the lower panel for genuinely different scenes, not repeated reaction clips. R02's stable upper half permits rapid visual changes without losing the voice; full-frame resets at numbered reasons restore hierarchy. The product close-up near 82s is motivated by introduction, not a random periodic zoom.

## Edit and sound

Plan the reveal timing deliberately. R02 names the product late; that is optional source structure, not a rule for all targets. Enter an insert on the relevant clause and leave before it contradicts the next thought. Full-speaker moments may span a sentence. Do not cover every syllable or use a fixed cut interval.

Keep phrase captions in a readable zone near the panel seam without blocking the product or mouth. Caption replacements, lower-panel cuts and presenter cuts are separate events. Preserve intelligibility and the ironic pause; music/SFX must not obscure the rhetorical reversal. The research does not establish source compressor settings, beat sync or voice synthesis.

Deliver the argument map, approved narration, speaker/reset plan, distinct insert list with semantic roles and evidence status, and exact target timing after voice alignment. QA irony comprehension, honest claims, product geometry, lip sync if generated, uninterrupted caption meaning and meaningful B-roll variety. Do not turn a staged podcast scene into fictitious independent expert testimony.
