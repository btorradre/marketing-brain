## Concept and script architecture

A stable speaker frames desirable benefits as ironic reasons to avoid the product; changing illustrations make the argument concrete.

Provocative exclusion/hook → distinct numbered reasons → interpretation/product introduction → sensory or use detail → verified value/offer. The target copy SOP still governs new argument development; irony cannot invent proof or invert real risks misleadingly.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** One speaker anchor, distinct propositions, full-speaker rhetorical resets, semantically timed lower-panel inserts and a deliberate product reveal.

**Adaptable:** Reason count, duration, reveal point, panel proportion and types of evidence. Microphone styling does not establish a real independent podcast.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 — Ironic hook | Speaker plus an immediately legible product/outcome illustration. **H** | Split layout; hook referent → turn introducing first reason. | 3–6s hook module; cut to full speaker on numbered reset. | A visual promise makes the rhetorical inversion understandable. |
| L2 — Reason/chapter | Full speaker delivers reason title or sarcastic turn. **P** | Remove lower insert at reason opening → restore only when a concrete illustration is named. | 1–3s reset, or whole qualifying sentence. | Expression carries the joke and organizes the list. |
| L3 — Illustrate supported benefit | Distinct action, sensory metaphor or substantiated explanatory view. **B** | Lower panel enters on operative clause → exits before a different proposition → P. | 1.5–4s simple insert; 3–6s actual process. | Picture translates this particular proposition; its proof limits remain explicit. |
| L4 — Interpret or qualify | Return full face for qualification, punchline or next reason. **P** | Leave B before the interpretive clause; continuous narrator audio. | 1.5–4s; repeat L2–L4 for genuinely different reasons. | A speaker return tells the viewer how to read the previous illustration. |
| L5 — Name and reveal product | Speaker holds accurate item; brief close product emphasis. **H** | Enter product view at named introduction → retain through recognition → split only for new useful detail. | 2–5s recognition. | The argued benefits resolve into a purchasable object. |
| L6 — Specific sensory/use detail | New texture/action or ingredient-like metaphor with accurate status. **B** | Insert on exact descriptor → leave when voice changes to value/policy. | 1.5–3.5s per distinct idea. | Sensory imagery evokes the described experience without pretending to prove composition. |
| L7 — Value/offer and action | Product with speaker or verified offer graphic. **H** | Full speaker/product at CTA → hold complete action and readable facts. | 3–6s closing module. | The product and truthful instruction stay identifiable at decision time. |

## Required asset roles

Stable presenter performance; distinct beat-linked inserts; approved product views; supported claim/offer ledger; accurate captions; final aligned narration. No reference adoption/stock statistics transfer automatically.

## Instantiate and hand off

Map every approved line or silent action to these slots; assign actual selected asset IDs or explicit gaps. Resolve the entry/exit/return cues and all separate picture, overlay, caption and audio events.
