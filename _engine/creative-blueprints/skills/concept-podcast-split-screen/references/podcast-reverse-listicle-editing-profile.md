## Coverage and layer structure

Proposed vertical split starts near half frame, adjustable for face and evidence. Full speaker is a separate state, not merely an empty lower panel. Keep captions near a stable seam/clear region without obscuring product or mouth.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Enter a reason on speaker, cut the lower panel on semantic action change, return on qualification or punchline. Do not cut the upper speaker whenever lower footage changes. Preserve ironic pauses and complete demonstrations.

## Transitions and handles

Zero-overlap cuts between lower-panel inserts and for rhetorical resets. Proposed product punch is one discrete closer framing at introduction, held through recognition. No continuous zoom bounce or mandatory wipes. Any overlap belongs only to a justified within-insert process.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Enter on the clause it explains, not at the top of an unrelated sentence. Leave before price/policy/interpretation unless the image directly serves it. Return to P at every reason transition where expression matters. Metaphor, actual demonstration and evidentiary footage each have explicit status.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Stable speaker; varied meaningful action inside inserts. A macro may move slowly to expose texture; explanatory animation follows the actual causal interaction. Avoid repeating generic reactions or a single science view.

## Captions and graphic events

Short uppercase phrase captions in a consistent high-contrast treatment are an available source-matched direction. Keep reason numbers as structural text, claims/offer as verified labels, captions as exact speech. Separate their event timing.

## Voice, music and sound design

Voice anchors all visual changes; direct dry delivery with intelligible pauses for irony. Music subordinate; minimal accents at a real rhetorical turn. Do not invent audio cuts from the visual edit; align real final voice first.

## Image finish and time treatment

Presenter world remains consistent; insert media may differ deliberately by semantic job. Normalize readability without making documentary-like clips into glossy product film. No unapproved narration speed changes.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Reveal timing follows the brief, not source percentage. Finish with visible exact product and truthful offer/action. Generic romantic imagery cannot support a guarantee or customer count.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer supported reasons or simpler illustrations within authorized copy scope. Lengthen via new proof or explanation, not more filler panels. Preserve each proposition’s speaker → illustration → interpretation logic.

## Reject and verify

Reject fictitious expert/customer endorsement, repeated source clips, unsupported molecular proof, irony that reverses factual meaning and pictures that lag their clause. Verify every insert exit/return.
