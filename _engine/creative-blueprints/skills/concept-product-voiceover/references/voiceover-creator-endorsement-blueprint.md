## Concept and script architecture

An offscreen narrator gives a credible product/styling argument while an authorized wearer handles and models the exact item.

Source: claimed social response → named garment → texture → elevated look → occasions → fit context → style identity → availability. Target testimony, fit facts and stock history need actual evidence; recorded or synthetic voice is a deliberate production choice.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Continuous voice, line-specific physical actions, detail-to-outfit returns and accurate product identity. Testimonial words cannot be assigned to a fictional customer.

**Adaptable:** Argument/benefits, wearer/setting, duration, caption presence, reveal and CTA. Follow new-copy SOP or preserve the exact approved script.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| V1 — Desired look/hook | Strong styled torso/full look or distinctive texture action from first frame. **W** | Primary montage; hook clause → product name or concrete descriptor. | 1–3s opening views. | The appearance visibly motivates interest; modeling does not prove compliments. |
| V2 — Named product | Reveal clear garment and complete a putting-on/adjusting action. **A** | Enter on product noun → action completes → detail. | 1–3s per action; direct cut on meaningful phase. | The named item becomes tangible and identifiable. |
| V3 — Material/detail | Show a different meaningful texture, seam, cuff or drape action. **D** | Exact detail cue → enough inspection → wider outfit at styling clause. | 1–2.5s simple detail. | Close scale contributes information unavailable in a full-body shot. |
| V4 — Styling/occasion | Distinct useful outfit/context or getting-ready action. **W** | Enter on desired social look → show complete silhouette/result. | 2–4s; avoid repeated chest rubs. | The benefit becomes an imaginable way of dressing. |
| V5 — Fit context | Readable actual wearer front/side/back, one approved size fact if relevant. **W** | Fit cue → finish informative turn → next benefit. | 2–4s per necessary view. | Coverage helps assess fit but cannot certify physical dimensions without evidence. |
| V6 — Payoff and truthful action | Best resolved look with current offer/availability instruction if supported. **W** | CTA clause → hold recognizable exact product and complete instruction. | 2–5s finish. | The item being requested remains visible at decision time. |

## Required asset roles

Exact approved narration and evidence ledger; authorized wearer/product; distinct action/detail/outfit views; final voice alignment; optional captions/offer graphics. No random extra lifestyle footage needed.

## Instantiate and hand off

Map every approved line or silent action to these slots; assign actual selected asset IDs or explicit gaps. Resolve the entry/exit/return cues and all separate picture, overlay, caption and audio events. Save the target editing plan before production; apply the shared contract for Cut Room and current model/editor rules.
