## Concept and script architecture

A short benefit contrast is made visible through dressing, full look, material and fit before a clear truthful value/action finish.

Source: comfortable feel versus polished appearance → named fabric → relaxed/light fit → styled versatility → price/value → shop and brand. New copy follows current structure/evidence rules; never invent a mechanism to fill a short format.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Few dense useful propositions; each gets a visible demonstration or clearly tagged claim; typography remains restrained; a legible finish resolves the sequence.

**Adaptable:** Product, exact benefit contrast, outfit options, verified value comparison, duration and card count. Preserve approved script when only visuals change.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 — Benefit contrast | Present exact item, then begin putting it on. **A** | Comfort/feel cue → dressing action → finished-look clause. | 1–2s views within 3–4s hook module. | The physical item and dressed result explain the contrast. |
| Q2 — Finished appearance | Full silhouette with deliberate styling and simple confident pose. **W** | Enter on appearance payoff → leave for concrete product/material. | 1.5–3s. | Viewer sees what polished actually looks like. |
| Q3 — Material and construction | Hanger/cloth/detail makes texture and garment identity readable. **D** | Named material cue → inspection → wider fit. | 1–2.5s; labels only from evidence. | A tactile view supports understanding but does not prove composition. |
| Q4 — Fit and versatility | Distinct front/side/back or second meaningful outfit. **W** | Fit/use phrase → complete turn/result → next proposition. | 1.5–3s per useful view. | The wearable difference is more informative than repeated modeling poses. |
| Q5 — Truthful value | Clear product remains visible while verified value is spoken/shown. **W** | Value cue → enough time for numbers if present → action. | 2–4s depending on reading load. | The claim remains attached to the exact item. |
| Q6 — Action and brand | Resolve wearer/product view, then simple shop/brand card if requested. **G** | CTA words → readable action → brand finish. | 1.5–3s per card; combine if short and readable. | The viewer finishes with a clear instruction and identity. |

## Required asset roles

Exact product/wearer, hanger/dressing/whole/detail/fit views; verified material/price basis; concise approved narration; accurate phrase captions; current logo/action assets.

## Instantiate and hand off

Map every approved line or silent action to these slots; assign actual selected asset IDs or explicit gaps. Resolve the entry/exit/return cues and all separate picture, overlay, caption and audio events. Save the target editing plan before production; apply the shared contract for Cut Room and current model/editor rules.
