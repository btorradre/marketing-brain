## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame product demonstration with continuous offscreen voice; sparse independent speech captions. No presenter panel or extra lifestyle montage required.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut on the contrast, named feature, fit state or new outfit; a caption may span multiple picture shots. Preserve the completion of dressing and the readable full-look result.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts, zero overlap. Use clean action state changes and clear card cuts. Do not blend garment colors or morph wardrobe to show a real variant.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

All demonstration footage can cover VO. Material insert starts at its noun/descriptor, ends when the voice turns to fit, and returns to a readable body view. Keep price/value on exact product or clean graphic; no unsupported proof insert.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Use actual dressing/turn/cloth action; keep camera straightforward. Front/back/close views must answer different questions. Avoid gratuitous push-ins during small caption reading.

## Captions and graphic events

Restrained serif phrases are source-matched; one or two readable lines in clear space, with contrast checked on pale fabric. Price comparison, speech and CTA are separate events. Never assume white text works on a white shirt.

## Voice, music and sound design

Concise conversational VO; do not rush the contrast or value numbers. Music supports but cannot mask endings of phrases. Align captions and cuts to final voice; source timestamps are approximate.

## Image finish and time treatment

Soft coherent home light, true fabric color, stable identity and modest grade. No automatic speed change. Use source PTS at fractional rates rather than treating 23.976 as exact 24.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Keep the spoken action complete before/through the action card, then readable brand. A single combined card is an adaptation if the target is shorter; document it.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Reduce redundant views or simplify authorized offer copy before shortening recognition. Longer versions add concrete fit/use/evidence, not repeated cards. Preserve all approved words and real number-reading time.

## Reject and verify

Reject unverifiable material/price comparison, near-identical poses, unreadable serif captions and changing garment construction. Check exact price basis, final word alignment and finish.
