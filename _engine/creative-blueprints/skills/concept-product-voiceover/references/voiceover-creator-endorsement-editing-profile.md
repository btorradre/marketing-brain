## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame product/wearer montage under offscreen voice. A visible model does not automatically mean lip-synced presenter or actual testimonial speaker. Alternate useful detail and whole appearance.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut when an action phase or idea changes; preserve dressing completion and fit recognition. Fast detail cuts are possible, but do not turn every new phrase into a nearly identical torso shot.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts, zero overlap. Match garment/hand states where useful; an intentional getting-dressed jump must still make sense. No routine dissolves, zoom transitions or speed ramps.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Most pictures cover continuous VO. Enter a detail at the named descriptor, exit when it becomes a styling claim, and return to whole look. A spoken social/stock claim does not justify fabricated customers or stock footage as evidence.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Use genuine sleeve adjustment, turn, drape, closure or new outfit action. Post crops alone do not supply variety. A motivated closer detail should settle; preserve enough field for fit assessment.

## Captions and graphic events

Source has no added captions; target captions are explicit direction. Use restrained accurate phrases when needed, outside the relevant garment detail. Fit/price labels require verified facts and separate timing.

## Voice, music and sound design

Natural conversational voice with clear emphasis and breaths; optional music underneath. Preserve approved voice speed. Picture cuts do not require voice cuts; perform actual word alignment before locking.

## Image finish and time treatment

Consistent skin, material color and credible everyday texture; maintain wardrobe between nonchronological shots. No heavy retouching that changes fit or finish. Treat intentional outfit variants explicitly.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Hold the strongest resolved look through the actual CTA. Optional branded card is a target addition and must not consume needed product recognition time. No copied scarcity claim.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorter: fewer complete arguments/actions within scope. Longer: distinct styling contexts or useful evidence. If a clause is long, use a meaningfully new view or purposeful hold, not repeated gestures.

## Reject and verify

Reject invented compliments/fit data, synthetic personal experience presented as real, repeated source reuse and picture/claim mismatch. Preserve narration and caption timing in visual-only revisions.
