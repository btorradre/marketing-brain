## Compare the editing treatments

These are generalized target defaults informed by the sources, not mandatory source settings. Each linked profile gives exceptions and adaptation rules.

| Variant | Main cut trigger / protected hold | B-roll or visual treatment | Transition and pace character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal wardrobe interview | Topic or needed viewing scale; protect personal memory and closing reaction | Face/outfit coverage; mostly in-shot accessory reveals | Few motivated hard changes, conversational holds |
| Event outfit roundup | New wearer or useful detail reset; complete each reveal | Distinct looks; live tilt/approach to named accessory, usually no separate insert | Hard encounter boundaries, varied answer lengths |
| Cultural conversation | Informative speaker emphasis, listener reaction or chapter | Two-shot and faces; default no literal stock footage of named places | Direct changes, longer personal-story holds |
| Greenscreen guide | New comparison/criterion; clear clutter for guide interpretation | Product pairs, distinct diagrams, readable interface over/behind presenter | Separate overlay entrances and base cuts; quick examples then slower explanation |
| Podcast reverse listicle | New proposition; full-speaker reason/punchline reset | Lower-panel human action, sensory metaphor or supported mechanism by clause | Direct panel cuts; voice continuous; protected rhetorical pauses |
| Craft-to-carry film | New physical operation/scale; complete function and carry payoff | Tactile process, actual object handling, styled ownership | Quick direct process cuts, slower whole/carry/brand finish |
| Animated discovery musical | Causal story phase or meaningful viewpoint; protect emotion/reveal | One animation world, purposeful product/use inserts and refrain | Longer character holds, selective short blends, distinct final chapter |
| Silent animated microstory | Completed cause/action; protect hidden-information recognition | Primary animated story and matching prop detail | Few phase changes, optional brief time compression, held payoff |
| Countdown catalogue | Actual next item and aligned count; recognition per item | Stable comparison setting with genuinely distinct products | Direct rhythmic replacement, no identity-blending morphs |
| Creator product voiceover | New descriptor/action/fit idea; finish the gesture | Native-looking try-on, tactile detail and complete styling | Continuous voice over distinct useful actions; avoid repeated poses |
| Concise value voiceover | Benefit contrast, feature, fit or value; readable finish | Hanger/dressing/whole/detail/second styling; restrained captions | Dense direct demonstration sequence and clear action/brand hold |
| Urban fashion film | Useful step/turn, detail reveal or musical phrase | Editorial looks, accessories, portraits and city context | Direct cuts with occasional directional pan punctuation |
| Fashion mood montage | A new meaningful material/color/life association | Distinct fashion, still life, interiors and human moments | Still holds and clean cuts; no obligatory camera animation |
| Kinetic product details | New feature/physical angle; resolve macros into the whole | Accurate distinct stills of shape, texture, interior and hardware | Rapid hard replacement; avoid copied repeat cycles |

## What each pair specifies

- Script/visual communication architecture, defining grammar and flexible variables.
- Each beat’s action, visual style, placement, entry/exit/return cue, proposed dwell, handoff and editorial reason.
- Primary coverage versus true inserts, reactions, overlays, stills and camera movement.
- Cut triggers, protected holds, transition types/handles, subject/camera/post movement and layer behavior.
- B-roll treatment, permitted source/generation routes, semantic role and evidence limits.
- Captions/graphics, voice/music/SFX, image finish, speed treatment, product reveal and CTA/end hold.
- Short/long adaptation, timing conflicts, asset requirements and rejection/QA criteria.

## Use the library

Read the [application contract](profile-contract.md). Map exact approved words/actions into the [target editing-plan template](../assets/concept-editing-plan-template.md), choose actual assets and align final voice before locking cuts. Production still follows the [current production contract](production-contract.md). A reference may require no separate B-roll; do not cover meaningful primary action simply to satisfy a ratio.
