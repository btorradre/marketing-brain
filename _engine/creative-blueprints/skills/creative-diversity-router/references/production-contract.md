## Copy and claims

Analyze reference structure as observed. For new or explicitly revised ad copy follow the [included copywriting SOP](../../../support/Copywriting-Structure-and-Specificity-SOP.md) (or its newer authoritative host version): Hook → Problem → Brief problem → Agitation → Solution/category discussion → Dismissal → UMP → UMS → Solution intro → Future pacing using social proof. UMP precedes UMS. Use actual customer language and specific supported evidence; keep the earlier solution/category discussion distinct from product introduction.

Do not silently rewrite approved narration. A short visual-only brand film, authentic interview or approved short script does not authorize fabricated mechanism, testimonial, chronology or benefit claims to fill missing stages. Document how the requested format and approved scope fit the SOP; if the task truly requires an incompatible copy decision, surface that specific missing decision while progressing independent work.

Fashion leads with styling, silhouette, materials and outfit aspiration. Function supports the desire. Use an immediately striking, relevant opening; fashion impact is desirable appearance rather than exaggerated distress. A reveal delay is concept-specific, not universal. Never copy reference prices, adoption counts, sellout claims, credentials or guarantees into a target without current evidence.

## Plan and production

Save the plan in the concept's `edit/` folder before generating images, video or voiceover, assembling or exporting. In the originating vault, product concepts live at `brands/<brand>/products/<product>/concepts/<concept>/`; a standalone harness uses its designated concept workspace. Follow the [included editing-plan SOP](../../../support/Ad-Editing-Plan-First-SOP.md) or its newer authoritative host version. Include exact approved line/beat; provisional in/out and duration; selected asset or gap; visible action; incoming/outgoing transition; cut cue; camera/subject movement; overlays and captions; voice and sound direction; CTA/end hold; and delivery/QA. State separately why the scene belongs, what the viewer should understand/feel, why its style fits and why the edit changes at that cue. Re-align after final voice selection. Silent formats use visual beats instead of invented speech.

For requested storyboards read `.claude/skills/cutroom/SKILL.md`, use its `cutroom/board_builder.py` workflow, and deliver the working board URL in the correct brand project. Include the first generated asset set, presenter when applicable, every distinct insert/overlay and first frame for every video scene. Inspect and attach actual selected images to cards; reference frames belong in a separate lane. Text-only boards are interim unless explicitly requested. Analysis evidence sheets are not storyboards.

Use GPT Image 2 for image production and Google Omni for video generation. Inspect approved product/presenter references and outputs. For Velantra resolve product/color/size via `brands/velantra/product-skills/registry.json`, read the dedicated product skill and SCALE-AND-FIDELITY.md. Do not certify physical dimensions from uncalibrated images.

Use DaVinci Resolve for editing; check the current connection, preserve existing projects and isolate new work. HyperFrames, its HTML/GSAP composition route, and Remotion are banned. Simple requested hero clips are generated directly at requested duration/aspect; retain provider originals when no edit is requested. Report unsupported formats/durations instead of silently trimming or substituting. Do not claim editor operations without successful tool receipts.

## QA and evidence

Audit actual images for repeated sources and near-identical composition before production and export. Distinct filenames/crops/mirrors are not distinct scenes. Presenter continuity and a catalogue's comparison background may be intentional; each inserted B-roll beat still needs distinct meaningful content. Preserve approved speed, word timing and captions during visual-only revisions.

Before export inspect picture continuity, product geometry, exact narration/caption alignment, safe areas, intelligibility, music rights/mix, offer accuracy and finish/loop. If TikTok action sourcing is requested, use its dedicated skill and EV 5/5 plus no-text interval verification; this does not license replacing requested footage with generated content.

The research used every decoded frame at thumbnail scale with selected enlarged checks. Sound descriptions are model-assisted, not directly auditioned; exact sync, instruments, loudness, source editor settings, AI provenance, rights and performance are unverified unless specifically documented. Distinguish visual observation, speech/caption evidence, model inference, editorial hypothesis and measured result. Source assets are study evidence, not cleared commercial reuse.
