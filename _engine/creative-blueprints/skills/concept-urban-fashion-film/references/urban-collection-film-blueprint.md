## Concept and script architecture

A collection becomes a desirable way of moving through a city; whole outfits, details and attitude build a visual story without explanatory narration.

Visual sequence: immediate style/world → complete look → meaningful accessory/material detail → movement/attitude → distinct next look → resolved fashion finish. Music organizes energy but does not replace visual meaning.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Coherent city/style world, distinct looks, whole/detail/movement alternation, purposeful camera/subject motion and clear product/outfit readability.

**Adaptable:** City, casting, styling, look count, pan punctuation, music and optional brand ending. Location must support the clothing.
