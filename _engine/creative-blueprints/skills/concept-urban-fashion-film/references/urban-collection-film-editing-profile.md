## Visual styles and where they belong

| Style ID / role | Observable treatment and source route | Placement and allowed purpose |
|---|---|---|
| W | Editorial city fashion: coherent styled models, natural architectural light, confident walking/turning, street depth and varied useful camera scale. | Primary looks and attitude, using authorized footage or coherent generated scenes. |

## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame fashion footage; no fixed presenter. Detail shots serve the visual style argument. The city is context, not the main subject.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Change scale when it adds styling information; cut on a useful step, turn or gesture phase or a chosen musical phrase after listening. Vary holds by recognition, not uniform beat count.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts dominate. A short directional pan/blur is an actual planned bridge with start, travel, incoming direction and settle; source preset unknown. Do not add a whip between every look. No forced morph between people.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Fashion details enter after a whole-look anchor or immediately readable hook, then return to full look/face/movement. City inserts are optional punctuation and must not postpone clothing recognition. Distinct looks need distinct actions.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Tracking can reveal drape, approach can emphasize attitude, tilt can show accessory relationship. Define subject anchor and settle. Separate generated/captured movement from post reframing; avoid identical Ken Burns motion.

## Captions and graphic events

Default no speech captions. Optional collection title/brand stays sparse and does not cover silhouette. If text becomes explanatory, allocate real reading time and acknowledge a format adaptation.

## Voice, music and sound design

Licensed/original music is the timing spine; choose actual phrase changes and accents after listening. Optional footsteps/city sound can ground the setting. Exact source beat phase and instrumentation are unverified.

## Image finish and time treatment

Coherent editorial palette, authentic material colors and consistent skin tones. Any grain/contrast is target direction, not recovered source settings. No routine slow motion or ramps; use only when action and capture support them.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Resolve the final look/gesture; tag/CTA is optional to the brief. A pure collection film need not become a last-second sales paragraph.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer complete looks; retain whole/detail recognition. Lengthen with new styling, movement and locations that share the world. Do not fill with repetitive city pans.

## Reject and verify

Reject generic travel filler, repeated walk composition, mismatched accessories, broken gait/straps, premature detail before recognizable object and unreadable fast full looks. Verify full-speed rhythm with actual music.
