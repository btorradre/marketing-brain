## Visual styles and where they belong

| Style ID / role | Observable treatment and source route | Placement and allowed purpose |
|---|---|---|
| A | Presenter with visibly distinct products, neutral uncluttered room/light, natural small gesture and stable camera. | Abundance/selection hook. |
| I | Actual individual product presented on the same comparison axis with clear silhouette, unobscured hardware and consistent apparent scale. | Catalogue primary coverage; not generic B-roll. |

## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame comparison display with same presenter/background. Keep item silhouette in a consistent readable area. No split-screen or supplementary B-roll required.

## Cut rules and protected holds

One direct cut for each new actual item. Preserve count-to-item agreement and stable recognition. Do not add a second pose cut within a sub-second item hold.

## Transitions and handles

Hard cuts, zero overlap. Avoid product morphs/dissolves that blend two identities or imply transformation. Align presentation axis where useful without faking equivalence of scale.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Default separate B-roll zero. A functional detail cannot be inserted into a rapid count without providing extra time and explicitly changing the catalogue structure. If used, return to the same item before advancing count.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Small lift/turn only if shape stays recognizable; settled image is valid. Stable capture makes differences legible. Do not apply the same digital zoom to every item.

## Captions and graphic events

Short introductory promise; item numbers/names only if the target needs them and reading time supports them. All labels follow actual order. Avoid simultaneous long captions, price list and rapid countdown.

## Voice, music and sound design

Optional count provides progression; align final number onset to item change. Music supports momentum. Check intelligibility and real beat alignment; source model estimates are not edit markers.

## Image finish and time treatment

Match exposure/background and honest color. Do not resize products into false physical equivalence. No speed manipulation that makes products unreadable; stills can hold unchanged.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Finish the last count and item. CTA or loop is a target choice; a full-set return should be deliberate, not a second repeated catalogue cycle.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Total roughly equals intro plus N recognition holds plus optional ending. If over duration, reduce item count only within brief scope or simplify labels; do not silently omit promised items.

## Reject and verify

Reject wrong count, duplicated models, unsubstantiated ranking, hidden handles, unreadable labels and mismatched spoken numbers. Compare real silhouettes, not filenames.
