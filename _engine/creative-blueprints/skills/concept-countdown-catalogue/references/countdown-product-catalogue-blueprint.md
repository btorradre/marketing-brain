## Concept and script architecture

A finite selection of attractive products changes rapidly against a stable comparison background, making silhouette differences the main information.

Honest selection promise/count → one distinct item per beat → final item or clear target finish. Do not imply objective rank or sales popularity without evidence.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Exact count, genuinely distinct items, consistent comparison conditions, useful order contrast and enough recognition time.

**Adaptable:** Item count, category, presenter, numbers/titles, audio countdown and whether to add a CTA. Count and labels must agree with selected products.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 — Range hook | Presenter already lifts/arranges a striking selection or abundance. **A** | Full primary; title/count promise → first individual item ready. | 1–2s; hard cut into catalogue. | The viewer understands the scope before comparing items. |
| K2 — First clear item | Hold item so shape, handles and material are readable. **I** | First count/name cue → steady recognition → next actual item. | 0.7–1.2s for simple unlabeled silhouette. | Stable display establishes comparison conditions. |
| K3 — Contrasting next item | New actual product changes size, structure, texture or shape. **I** | Each aligned count → matching item; exit after recognition. | 0.7–1.2s each; more with text or complex detail. | Difference, not novelty effects, supplies the sequence’s interest. |
| K4 — Remaining selection | Complete remaining distinct items with small useful presentation gestures. **I** | Repeat K3 with accurate count and order; no duplicate filler. | Duration derives from actual remaining items. | A finite promise must be fulfilled exactly. |
| K5 — Final selection/finish | Conclude on the last item or a deliberate complete-set return. **I** | Last count → readable item → finish/tag if requested. | 1–2s optional final hold; 1.5–3s optional CTA. | The ending confirms completion rather than cutting the last item short. |

## Required asset roles

Verified ordered product IDs and images/footage; distinct trait per item; presenter/background continuity; exact count/audio if used; optional valid destination.

## Instantiate and hand off

Map every approved line or silent action to these slots; assign actual selected asset IDs or explicit gaps. Resolve the entry/exit/return cues and all separate picture, overlay, caption and audio events. Save the target editing plan before production; apply the shared contract for Cut Room and current model/editor rules.
