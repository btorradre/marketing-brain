# Street-style interview

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md). Evidence: [R01 personal profile](../../research/R01/analysis.md) and [R11 event roundup](../../research/R11/analysis.md), including enlarged frames. Do not assume an apparently informal encounter was spontaneous.

## Build the conversation

Choose a single-person profile when personal history matters; choose an event roundup when distinct looks and short discoveries supply range. Open on a desirable complete look already turning, walking or holding a distinctive accessory. The first question should orient the viewer quickly, then invite concrete item details.

Prepare question prompts, not invented answers: what drew the wearer to this look; what an unusual item is; how it was acquired; why it matters; how they put the outfit together. Let a real memory interrupt the item list. R01's family connection changes the function from inventory to personal meaning. Close on the wearer's smile, reaction or useful detail. A target sales handoff is a deliberate adaptation, not something present in these sources.

For generated/staged versions, write them as dramatized fashion conversations. Do not present synthetic speakers as actual customers or invent provenance, endorsements or inherited items. Record actual speaker permissions and source meaning when editing real footage.

## Map the words to camera attention

| Cue | Coverage and reason |
|---|---|
| Identity, taste, personal memory | Stay on the face; expression is the evidence of the conversation. |
| Whole outfit or large garment | Widen enough to read silhouette and proportions. R01 changes framing for the dress at 22.33s. |
| Belt, bag, ring, shoe | Follow the named object with a tilt/approach or motivated cut. Wait for the wearer to expose it; a bag turn or raised hand can be the reveal. |
| Object provenance | Allow detail then return to face; naming a gift has interpersonal meaning beyond its hardware. |
| Joke or closing answer | Hold the response and smile; do not cover it with generic lifestyle footage. |

R11 often goes full look → belt/bag → jewelry → face **inside one continuous take**. Log camera events separately from cuts. A sequence of tilts is not a sequence of B-roll inserts. Keep hand orientation and accessory visibility readable rather than inventing crop zooms for every noun.

## Edit and sound

Cut at topic changes, useful performance resets or the next wearer. Keep pauses that express thought; remove only genuinely redundant delay. In roundups let a brief answer stay brief and a valuable story run longer. Maintain environmental continuity without imposing matching interview lengths.

R01 uses white serif phrase captions; R11 has no added captions. Decide captions explicitly for the target and check contrast over clothing. Avoid conflating text changes with picture cuts. Prioritize intelligible questions and answers, preserve useful street ambience and keep music subordinate if used. Source music/mix details are not verified.

Deliver an editing plan with question/actual answer spans, cue-to-detail movements, preserved reactions, actual asset provenance and a rationale for each wearer. QA the full outfit, readable detail, honest answer ordering, no invented customer history and meaningful differences between looks. Do not replace a conversational format with an unrelated rapid product montage.
