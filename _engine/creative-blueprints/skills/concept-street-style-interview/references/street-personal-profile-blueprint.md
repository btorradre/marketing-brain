## Concept and script architecture

An appealing wearer introduces a look, then gives its objects personal meaning. Curiosity about a person sustains the sequence.

Source architecture: identity/place → item questions → personal provenance → complete outfit/accessories → playful human close. Prepare questions and preserve actual answers. New ad copy and any sales handoff follow the current copy SOP and evidence rules.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** One recognizable wearer; questions motivate attention; memory stays with the speaker; whole outfit resolves the details.

**Adaptable:** Number of items, location, interview length, caption treatment and an explicitly added target CTA. The personal story must be authentic or transparently dramatized.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 — Style hook and orientation | Wearer already turns or gestures within a striking complete look. **A** | Primary coverage; enter immediately → hold through identifying answer. | 3–7s opening; direct cut only if orientation improves. | Desirable styling earns the interview; face and outfit establish who we are following. |

| P4 — Personal meaning | Hold face during a real memory; preserve pause and reaction. **A** | Primary face; provenance cue → thought/reaction resolves. | 5–15s if warranted; no decorative cover. | The person supplies the meaning; stock family imagery would imply unsupported history. |
| P5 — Small item inspection | Wearer raises bag, extends foot or rotates jewelry for a clear view. **D** | Prefer in-shot movement; if separate insert, enter on item → leave after gesture → return face. | 1.5–4s detail plus movement; direct cut if separate. | The selected orientation answers the item question and then restores rapport. |

## Required asset roles

One authorized wearer/look and question/answer source; full silhouette and face coverage; distinct detail views only for selected items; caption transcript if used; optional verified target CTA.
