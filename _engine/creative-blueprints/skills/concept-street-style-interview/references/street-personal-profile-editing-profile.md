## Coverage and layer structure

One full-frame wearer. Keep face, garment and microphone clear; no permanent split-screen. Detail coverage is usually a motivated in-shot reframe.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut at a question/topic reset, a needed scale change or the closing reaction. Preserve complete memory, foot/bag gesture and joke response. No periodic punch-in timer.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts, zero overlap. A continuous tilt/approach is camera movement, not a dissolve. No default wipes or flashes; any target brand fade is a separate optional ending event.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Default separate B-roll is zero. If an item cannot be read in the primary frame, insert its actual detail on the named item, hold through orientation, and return to the same answer/face after recognition. Keep interview audio continuous.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Move medium → wider for garment; follow the wearer’s reveal; settle before a small detail needs reading. Avoid post zoom during the emotional answer. Proposed crop changes depend on source resolution, not guessed reference keyframes.

## Captions and graphic events

Optional restrained serif phrase captions over a low-detail torso region; generally one or two lines. Move the caption region when it would cover the named item. Replace with actual phrases, independently of picture cuts.

## Voice, music and sound design

Prioritize question/answer and useful ambient continuity. Preserve thoughtful pauses and laughter. Any music stays subordinate; no fabricated sentimental SFX during personal history. Exact source mix is unverified.

## Image finish and time treatment

Natural skin/material color and coherent daylight; match exposures between real takes. No routine speed changes, freezes, fake handheld shake or aggressive glamour filter.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Return to the face for the final human response. A target offer/tag is an explicit addition, with readable hold and verified facts; do not overwrite the actual answer.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by selecting fewer complete item modules, not clipping the meaningful memory. Lengthen through specific follow-ups and new details, not extra crops. Protect approved words and reaction time.

## Reject and verify

Reject invented provenance, covered emotional answers, unreadable accessories, false brand authentication and repeated detail footage. Check exact answer meaning and caption sync.
