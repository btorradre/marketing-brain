## Coverage and layer structure

One wearer fills the frame at a time. Establish full silhouette, then follow actual accessories and return to face. Do not treat each downward tilt as another shot.

## Cut rules and protected holds

New wearer at resolved answer/thanks; within-encounter jump only for a useful detail or redundancy. Preserve different encounter lengths; no mandatory per-person shot count.

## Transitions and handles

Direct hard cuts, zero overlap. Weather/background changes may remain if they mark a new encounter. Avoid dissolving two faces together or adding a wipe for each item.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Default zero separate B-roll. A needed insert must show that wearer’s actual item at its question cue, preserve the answering audio and return before the next personal question. No generic brand stock under provenance.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Follow extended hand, turning bag, raised foot or exposed earring. Settle long enough to inspect. Return upward intentionally; do not whip between unrelated body parts or manufacture digital shake.

## Captions and graphic events

Source uses no added captions. Target captions are an explicit accessibility/delivery choice: short accurate phrases with moving safe placement away from the item. Item labels need verified names; omit uncertain labels.

## Voice, music and sound design

Keep enthusiastic but intelligible questions, answers and event ambience. Smooth discontinuous room tone only where needed. Optional music supports energy without covering a quiet respondent.

## Image finish and time treatment

Preserve real event texture and diverse light; correct exposure/color for readability without erasing the environment. No routine slow motion or speed ramps over speech.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Allow the final wearer’s reply/smile to resolve. A brand handoff is an explicit target adaptation, never a claim the reference participants endorse that product.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer distinct encounters or fewer questions; lengthen through new looks and meaningful answers. Never make seven speakers recite the same benefit to fill time.

## Reject and verify

Reject near-identical looks passed off as diversity, wrong accessory at a cue, clipped answers and invented brand names. Check all camera reveals and each encounter’s completion.
