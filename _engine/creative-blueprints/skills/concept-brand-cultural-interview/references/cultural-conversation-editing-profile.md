## Coverage and layer structure

Two-shot is the relational anchor; guest/host/listener faces are primary conversational coverage, not generic B-roll. Keep consistent eyelines and readable hands/mics.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut on informative emphasis, a new answer, a revealing response or a chapter. Preserve the reaction after humor and the full emotional thought. No alternating close/wide pattern merely to hit a timer.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts and restrained reframing. Location chapter changes can be hard cuts with continuous conversational context. Proposed J/L cuts require actual audio alignment; they are not certified source settings.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Default external inserts are zero. Add one only if a necessary detail cannot be understood from speech/gestures; enter on that referent, leave before the relational punchline and return to speaker/listener. Do not automatically cover named restaurants.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Walking scenes may track backward while keeping both faces legible. A discrete crop can emphasize a speaker; hold the new scale rather than bounce each sentence. Keep text fixed in frame during any post transform.

## Captions and graphic events

Separate persistent topic title from phrase captions. Proposed vertical layout: topic near upper clear space, speech in lower middle above interface exclusion. Change the title only at chapter cues; preserve short complete phrases.

## Voice, music and sound design

Conversation, laughter and pauses lead. Bridge ambient discontinuities without implying continuous location when it changed. Optional music stays under both voices; maintain natural listener timing.

## Image finish and time treatment

Natural street/editorial color and consistent skin tone; wardrobe supplies palette. No unsupported slow-motion or freeze treatment over a real response.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Resolve the shared thought and goodbye. A target brand tag is separate from the conversation and must not create fictitious endorsement.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer chapters while keeping question/answer/reaction. Lengthen by deeper anecdotes and meaningful follow-ups, not more crop changes. Protect the personal section’s slower pace.

## Reject and verify

Reject stock food covering a joke, reaction shots that reverse meaning, fabricated spontaneity and topic labels that overstate expertise. Verify actual speakers/captions and timing.
