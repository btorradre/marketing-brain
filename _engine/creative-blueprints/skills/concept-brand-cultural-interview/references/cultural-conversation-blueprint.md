## Concept and script architecture

Host and guest reveal taste and personality through a shared cultural interest; their relationship is the main visual subject.

Accessible recommendation questions → specific anecdotes/banter → more personal belief or experience → shared close. Prepare a question ladder; preserve actual answer meaning. The source is not a direct product VSL.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Speaker/listener relationship, topic chapters and meaningful two-shot holds. Expressions can be more useful than literal pictures of named places.

**Adaptable:** Topics, number of chapters, locations, participants and a separately justified brand ending.

## Required asset roles

Authorized speakers and actual/staged dialogue; two-shot plus meaningful faces; chapter locations; accurate transcript/topic labels. External B-roll only for a demonstrated information gap.
