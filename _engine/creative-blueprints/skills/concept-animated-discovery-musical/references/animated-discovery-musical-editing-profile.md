## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame character narrative, selective product beauty and final graphic. Captions may progress several times within one base shot; overlays are not extra scenes. Tag imagined uses explicitly in the plan.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut on story phase, meaningful viewpoint or completed action. Hold a reaction until the narration’s emotion is visible. Do not default long-form animation to two-second clips.

## Transitions and handles

Hard cut for clear location/phase changes. Proposed related-time dissolve 0.15–0.35s, with handles and one shared transition ID. Environment blends/morphs only for intentional imagination/time compression; inspect every affected frame and do not duplicate characters accidentally.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Covering scenes must share the established animation language. Product/imagined-use insert enters on its referent, ends after a visible result, then returns to the character if narration resumes personal interpretation. No generic realistic stock inside the animated world.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Gentle approach can support longing; widen to reveal context; approach exact product for examination. Set a start/end frame and settle before text needs reading. Watch reflections, hands, eye lines and overlapping body instances.

## Captions and graphic events

Short legible phrase captions plus separate verified price/feature overlays. Enter numbers on the actual number cue, not later. Keep offer typography stable and large enough at the real delivery size.

## Voice, music and sound design

Explicitly choose target spoken, sung and refrain sections before timing visuals. Use original/authorized words/music and align final performance. Do not adopt unreliable source timestamps or assume exact instrumentation.

## Image finish and time treatment

Coherent render style, materials, character scale and palette per location. A photoreal product insert is a declared medium change. Avoid time warping character action to force a lyric fit; revise schedule within scope.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Product reveal is tied to the target named introduction and brief. After payoff, keep refrain only as long as distinct meaning warrants, then hold verified offer. No invented adoption count or animated friend presented as authentic testimonial.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by removing redundant journey/catalogue modules within authorized script scope. Lengthen by meaningful action/evidence, not repeating compositions. If approved voice cannot fit, state the actual timing conflict.

## Reject and verify

Reject expression/line mismatch, late prices, identity drift, duplicated protagonist, impossible reflection/watch geometry, unexplained chronology and repetitive refrain footage. Audio boundaries need actual target alignment.
