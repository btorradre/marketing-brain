# Animated discovery story with musical treatment

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md), [R04 full audit](../../research/R04/analysis.md), and broll-animated-ads when producing coverage. The source is about 180 seconds. Generated-looking 3D is observed; the actual model and exact spoken/sung boundaries are not verified.

## Story architecture

Write a coherent desire and causal journey before prompts. R04 moves from saving for travel → coveted luxury watch → price/wait barrier → imagined ownership worry → return home → product discovery around 91.9s → imagined use → price comparison → receipt and try-on → admiration → musical catalogue → offer. This is a specific source sequence, not evidence that a 90-second delay is optimal.

Choose reveal timing from the target brief and attention burden. Fashion desire should lead: an attractive object, anticipated styling or an appealing life moment. Do not amplify distress just to create a problem section. Follow the current copy SOP for target copy, with truthful UMP/UMS and no invented cause, testimony, material or performance claim. Explicitly label a fictional protagonist's experience as dramatization; first-person delivery does not make it authentic social proof.

## Scene logic

| Story function | Picture and reason |
|---|---|
| Establish desire | Give the character a visible purposeful action and an object of attention. Looking through glass can place the viewer beside the character's aspiration. |
| Encounter a barrier | Show a readable real price/wait fact or clearly fictional obstacle with a matching reaction. Do not leave a smiling expression under a worry line. |
| Carry unresolved desire | A new location can preserve the same emotional objective; maintain prop and wardrobe continuity through travel. |
| Discover the product | Reveal exact target design when named. A floating object is an illustrative effect, not a demonstrated feature. |
| Imagine ownership | Use distinct meaningful life contexts and mark them as future pacing if they precede purchase. Do not confuse imagined use with documented chronology. |
| Receive and try on | Finish the physical action. Mirror, wrist and hands need geometry checks; a reflection must match the character. |
| Emotional payoff | Return to the desired styling and expression. Generated friends admiring the item are illustrative, not verified reviews. |
| Brand refrain/offer | Change visual task deliberately, then provide a readable truthful ending. Every catalogue insert must add a new view or use. |

## Continuity, editing and music

Create an identity/world sheet: character face/age/body, wardrobe by story time, product dimensions/reference, hand carrying side, environment, color/light and intended expression. Keep covering scenes in the same animation language unless a product macro deliberately changes medium. Track imagined versus actual story events.

Use holds long enough for performance; captions may progress several times within one shot. Hard cuts can change place, a dissolve can compress time, and an impossible morph can express imagination. State which and why. R04's beach-to-café section blends environments, then reveals a seated duplicate as the walking figure passes; distinguish that transition and continued camera move from separate use-case cuts. Avoid duplicating the protagonist or changing product geometry during transformations.

Plan spoken sections, sung sections and refrain as separate target decisions. The source has a musical advertising treatment, but automated audio analyses disagreed on boundaries; do not copy their timestamps as a score. Use original/authorized music and words. Record actual voice alignment before locking cuts, captions or end hold. Do not pad duration with repeated silk/product compositions as the source does.

Deliver a narrative/editing plan with reveal ledger, scene rationale, audio mode per section, continuity sheet, claim evidence and readable CTA. QA the full journey for causal clarity, expression-to-line match, accurate overlays, distinct scenes and purposeful duration. Flag story or audio uncertainty rather than presenting a model summary as verified synchronization.
