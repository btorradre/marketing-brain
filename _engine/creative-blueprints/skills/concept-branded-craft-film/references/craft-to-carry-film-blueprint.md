## Concept and script architecture

Tactile material and purposeful making/handling lead to a desirable finished object and styled ownership.

Visual sequence rather than spoken VSL: material/action → construction variation → finished form → worn/carry confidence → brand. Sparse chapter copy may carry the thought; do not invent documentary manufacturing claims.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Distinct visible actions, tactile scale, purposeful macro-to-whole progression, slower ownership payoff and readable identity finish.

**Adaptable:** Product-specific operations, number of details, color variants, sparse text, optional recorded material sound and end-card duration.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.
