## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame details and wider payoff; no presenter or inset architecture required. Image hierarchy changes from contact/action to complete object to wearer.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut when a new operation, scale or ownership phase adds information. Complete the visible function even if a musical tick arrives early. Lengthen the payoff after quicker construction details.

## Transitions and handles

Direct cuts, zero overlap, are the base grammar. Match direction or contact when useful but do not fake continuous manufacture across different products. Optional final fade is target direction, not observed source necessity.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

The film is primary visual storytelling; process/details can serve as B-roll in a larger hybrid only at a matching craft/function cue. In that case exit after action completion and return to the parent speaker or whole product, not an unrelated stock scene.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Subject/tool action leads. Controlled following movement can track carry; a macro camera move is justified only if it reveals a surface or part. Avoid automatically pushing into every still.

## Captions and graphic events

Sparse chapter copy, consistent clear zone and modest scale. Give each phrase enough uninterrupted time; one chapter may span several shots. Logo is a separate ending event.

## Voice, music and sound design

Plan atmospheric/original music and selected tactile contact/closure/footstep sounds when appropriate. Use actual sound or transparently designed effects; exact source effects were not verified. No obligatory voice track.

## Image finish and time treatment

Warm materials, controlled neutrals and coherent contrast. Preserve actual material color. No unverified speed ramps; slow footage only when authorized and physically convincing. Avoid heavy grain masking product detail.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Whole/carry recognition precedes brand. CTA is optional to the target brief and cannot replace the entire ownership payoff.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer distinct operations while preserving whole product and carry. Lengthen through meaningful craft/use details, not longer logo padding or repeats. Do not imply a process the brand cannot substantiate.

## Reject and verify

Reject misrepresented workshop provenance, impossible tool contact, changing hardware/material, cropped silhouette and identical actions in different files. Verify that styling remains the main desired outcome.
