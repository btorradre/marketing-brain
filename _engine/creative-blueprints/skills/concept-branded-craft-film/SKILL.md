# Branded craft film

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md) and [R03 evidence](../../research/R03/analysis.md). Source: a 17.43s 4:5 film with 12 visual segments, process macros, finished black briefcase, longer carry shot and black brand card. Preserve source aspect information; audit padding is not a creative letterbox choice.

## Visual script

Define the desired ownership feeling and the actual construction details that support it. Build material → purposeful making/handling → finished form → worn/carry payoff → brand memory. Use short chapter copy only when it adds a thought the images cannot carry. Do not invent a voiceover simply because other ads use one.

Hook with an immediately tactile action: material bending under a hand, precise tool contact or a striking finished detail. Choose the most desirable, legible action already underway. Alternate shot scale and direction so successive scenes reveal different facts. A needle entering leather, edge treatment and a fastener operation are distinct actions; three nearly identical finger strokes are not.

## Why each scene belongs

- Material macro introduces grain, finish and suppleness as visible qualities. It cannot independently prove fiber/leather composition or durability.
- Real making footage supports process credibility only when tied to the actual product and manufacturer. Generated artisans may illustrate a look but must not be represented as documentary production evidence.
- Closure, handle and interior details translate attention to construction into ownership relevance. Allow the action to complete so the part's use is comprehensible.
- A full object resolves the preceding fragments. Keep exact color and product continuity or identify a deliberate variant change; do not imply all differently colored scenes show one uninterrupted object's manufacture.
- Styled carrying supplies fashion payoff: posture, silhouette and outfit relationship show why one would want it. R03 holds this about 3.67s after faster detail shots.
- A restrained end hold gives the brand time to register. R03's roughly 3.77s card is source-specific, not a universal allocation.

## Edit, sound and QA

Use direct cuts when actions or angles change. Let motion within the frame carry rhythm; do not add dissolves or camera moves by default. Tighter early details can accelerate attention, followed by a calmer payoff and finish. Typography should share a stable position and be sparse enough to read over texture. No voiceover is established in R03.

Plan tactile sound only from actual recording or an explicitly designed sound layer. Needle, leather, zipper and footsteps can distinguish actions, but do not assert the source contains an exact effect without listening evidence. Choose music for the desired pace and leave space for material sounds; verify the final mix by listening.

Deliver a compact editing plan with each process/action, its source provenance, the corresponding ownership payoff, distinct compositions, text chapters and end hold. QA product construction, hand/tool physics, real process attribution, material continuity, readable typography and source-specific durations. This format conveys care and desire; it does not establish measured quality or sales performance on its own.
