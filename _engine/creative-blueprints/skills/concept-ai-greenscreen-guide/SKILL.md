# AI or recorded greenscreen guide

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md) and [R05 audit](../../research/R05/analysis.md). Observed: seated presenter, rapidly replaced comparison overlays, then a cutout presenter over a website. Actual capture, keying and AI provenance are unknown.

## Script and information progression

Define the viewer's decision and the guide's legitimate role. Use concrete comparisons to demonstrate useful judgment before describing a service. In R05, six premium/alternative fashion pairs precede an introduction to personalized styling, four explanatory concepts, the website and a profile-link CTA. The pictures evolve from examples → framework → interface → action.

For new ad copy follow the copy SOP through the shared contract. Avoid inventing credentials, equivalent quality or savings. A similar silhouette is a visual comparison, not proof of identical materials, manufacture or value. Check current prices and comparison basis if used. Keep aspiration in the finished look, with cost or convenience supporting it.

## Layer plan

| Spoken job | Visible layer and editorial reason |
|---|---|
| Compare two alternatives | Show both clearly with persistent left/right or upper/lower mapping; names/prices appear at the corresponding noun. The viewer can inspect the difference without remembering a previous shot. |
| Introduce the guide/persona | Clear competing products and return attention to the face. A visual pause changes the task from comparison to relationship. |
| Explain body shape, color, contrast or style | Give each idea a distinct appropriate diagram/image. Do not recycle one generic chart for all concepts. |
| Show how a service works | Use a readable authentic interface or explicitly illustrative mockup; shrink/reposition the cutout only when it reveals the relevant area. |
| Tell the viewer where to act | Show the actual current destination and cue the relevant control; avoid a tiny unreadable profile screenshot. |

Treat the presenter track, comparison assets, text and background as separate event tracks. R05's early product changes include body resets; later diagrams stack over the same room view. Count picture cuts separately from overlay entrances. Keep product cutouts from covering eyes, lips and the informative hand gesture. A visually dominant product can overlap the torso if that hierarchy is deliberate.

## Timing, performance and production

Front-load immediately legible examples, then slow for concepts that require reading. Do not copy the six-pair count or approximately 1.5–2-second comparison pace when the target needs longer recognition. Stage entrances on the spoken item, clear obsolete prices, and keep the service section visually different from the comparison section.

Direct confident conversational delivery with pauses for the viewer to compare. An AI presenter route uses the approved identity and current allowed generation tools; an authorized recorded route uses the actual footage. Resolve handles the layered edit. Check hair/glasses edges, lighting mismatch, eye contact, stable size and overlap throughout. Source masking technique and exact animation curves are not known.

Use captions for the actual spoken content, with emphasis selectively tied to meaning. Sound accents may mark a genuine comparison or action; do not add a whoosh to every word. Deliver an editing plan with layer order, phrase-to-asset map, verified comparison facts, presenter holds, interface legibility and CTA destination. QA the full sequence for both comprehension and distinct visuals.
