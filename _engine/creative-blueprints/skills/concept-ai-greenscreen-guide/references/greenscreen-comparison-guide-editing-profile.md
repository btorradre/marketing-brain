## Coverage and layer structure

Presenter is base; product/diagram cards are overlays, then interface becomes background with smaller keyed presenter. Keep the mouth/eyes visible and the active evidence area unobstructed.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Change comparisons on each new pair, not each caption word. Clear overlays for persona/interpretation. Reset body footage at a clean phrase seam; hidden cuts beneath cards remain separate from card events.

## Transitions and handles

Direct base cuts. Proposed graphic entrances: 0.15–0.30s short slide or scale from the assigned side, then settle; do not animate prices while reading. Remove outgoing pair before unrelated one overlaps. Interface handoff is one layout event, not a chain of wipes.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Product and criterion assets annotate the guide rather than cover the whole screen by default. Website coverage begins on process language and ends when the guide interprets the benefit. Each insert needs a named exit and return state.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Keep camera stable. Direct guide gesture toward the active evidence region. Scroll only enough to show the relevant function; avoid uncontrolled full-page movement. Inspect mask edges, glasses and hand occlusion.

## Captions and graphic events

Separate comparison labels/prices, criterion names, speech captions and CTA arrow. Proposed safe layout reserves face/mouth zone and a lower caption band; adjust for actual aspect/subject. Verify all numbers and labels.

## Voice, music and sound design

Brisk rhythmic pair naming can ease into explanatory speech. Keep voice continuous through graphics; optional soft comparison accents only where they clarify a change. Align after final voice, not model timestamps.

## Image finish and time treatment

Consistent room light and clean edges. Match asset scale for fair visual comparison, not misleading real-size equivalence. No speed-ramped presenter speech; interface movement can be edited only without misrepresenting function.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Use actual current destination and one clear action. Circle/arrow starts when named and leaves enough unobstructed reading time. Optional benefit line returns to presenter after the destination is understood.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by fewer pairs/criteria, not sub-readable prices. Lengthen with meaningful worked examples or actual process actions. Resolve dense diagram time conflicts before generation.

## Reject and verify

Reject face-covering products, stale prices, false identical-quality claims, duplicated criterion graphics, illegible scrolling and fake credentialed personas. Check every layer independently.
