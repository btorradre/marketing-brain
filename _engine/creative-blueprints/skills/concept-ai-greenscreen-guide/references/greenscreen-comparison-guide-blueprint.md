## Concept and script architecture

A visible guide earns attention through concrete comparisons, then explains a method/service and shows the action destination.

Rapid paired examples → guide identity/promise → distinct selection criteria → process/interface → desired outcome and destination. Verify prices, criteria and credentials; AI or recorded presenter is a target production choice.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** Stable guide; predictable comparison layout; different diagrams for different concepts; intentional switch to interface demonstration.

**Adaptable:** Number/categories of comparisons, criterion count, graphics layout, service and CTA. Reduce examples before making prices unreadable.

## Required asset roles

Approved presenter; two accurate cutouts per comparison; verified comparison facts; distinct criterion graphics; actual destination/interface; transcript and mask-safe compositions.
