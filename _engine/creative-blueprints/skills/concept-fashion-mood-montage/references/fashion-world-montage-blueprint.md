## Concept and script architecture

Distinct looks, materials and lived spaces share a precise palette and life context, creating an aspirational world through association.

Visual associative script: desired outfit → material/season → lived setting → human activity/relationship → fashion return → tactile detail → resolved look. There is no established spoken sales script to force into every image.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** A specific coherent taste world, genuinely distinct image roles and a return from abstract associations to wearable fashion.

**Adaptable:** Season, palette, number of images, imagery roles, music/vocal character and optional sparse theme/brand. Source archive imagery is not a cleared asset library.

## Beat blueprint

Cues are semantic placeholders. Replace them with exact approved words/actions in the target plan. Dwell ranges are proposed seconds for the named shot/module, not source measurements or a total-runtime promise.

| Slot / communication job | Visible action and style | Placement; enter → leave / return | Proposed dwell; handoff | Why this scene and edit belong |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 — Desired way of dressing | Compelling complete outfit or paired styled figures. **F** | Full-frame still; first image → silhouette/theme understood. | 0.5–1.1s simple image. | Fashion establishes the aspiration before lifestyle associations expand it. |
| M2 — Tactile/seasonal association | Distinct knit/leather/objects/seasonal still life sharing a precise palette. **O** | Direct cut from look → material/shape link readable. | 0.5–1.1s. | The world becomes tangible beyond the model. |

| M4 — Human life moment | Meaningful activity or relationship within the same world. **L** | Human gesture/context → next fashion return. | 0.6–1.3s. | Aspiration includes living and relating, not only possessions. |
| M5 — Fashion contrast and detail | New silhouette, then a genuinely new texture/object or styled view. **F** | Return to wearable look; optional repeat M2–M4 only with new content. | 0.5–1.1s each. | Alternation preserves fashion relevance and visual variety. |
| M6 — Resolved look | Final outfit embodies the palette/material world. **F** | Last association → readable wearable conclusion; optional tag after. | 0.8–1.8s final image. | The sequence ends with a concrete desired appearance. |

## Required asset roles

Original/owned/licensed stills or GPT Image 2 outputs with identity constraints; explicit palette/world statement; image-role and sequence rationale per image; music rights; optional theme text.

## Instantiate and hand off

Resolve the entry/exit/return cues and all separate picture, overlay, caption and audio events.
