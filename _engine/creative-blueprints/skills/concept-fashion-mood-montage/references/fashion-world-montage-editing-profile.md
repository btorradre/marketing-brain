## Visual styles and where they belong

| Style ID / role | Observable treatment and source route | Placement and allowed purpose |
|---|---|---|
| F | Editorial fashion stills: coherent tailoring, season/light and materials; varied bodies/angles/scale, original or authorized sources. | Primary style proposition and return. |
| O | Tactile still life of meaningful materials/objects; composed light, palette and texture rather than arbitrary luxury props. | Material association; not performance or ingredient proof. |
| L | Distinct inhabited interiors, outdoor activities and human moments that fit the same life context and color world. | Associative expansion; no implied real customer story without evidence. |

## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame distinct stills; their sequence is the primary visual story. Do not label all interiors as generic B-roll or make a separate talking-head layer.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Cut when the next image advances a specific color/material/life association. Regular rhythm is optional calibration; a complex image may deserve longer. Preserve a wearable fashion return between abstract clusters.

## Transitions and handles

Clean zero-overlap cuts are the default. Do not add dissolves or zoom effects merely because assets are still. A slow target dissolve is a deliberate different mood, not source-matched necessity.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

Associative images belong only if they connect to the desired world and neighboring images. Enter material/life imagery after a fashion anchor, then return to a distinct look. No arbitrary mansion/landscape filler or false testimony.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

Still hold is valid. If the target needs motion, plan an informative movement separately instead of animating every crop identically. Do not count a pan over the same source as a new image.

## Captions and graphic events

Optional one short stable theme in a consistent clear zone. Existing editorial marks inside source photos are separate from added text. Avoid tiny theme text as the only way to understand the sequence.

## Voice, music and sound design

Choose music for the intended world; verify the edit against its real phrases. Vocals are not necessarily a narrator. Do not transcribe/copy source song lyrics or assert beat-perfect source synchronization.

## Image finish and time treatment

Curate palette/light/texture first, then modest grade to cohere sources without falsifying product color. No routine noise/grain preset. A shared palette can connect strongly different subjects.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Finish on a desirable look or product grounded in the world. Optional CTA must have separate recognition time; do not flash a dense offer in a half-second image slot.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by preserving one clear fashion → world → fashion arc with fewer images. Lengthen with distinct related roles, not repeated archive loops or the same image recolored.

## Reject and verify

Reject random attractive images, repeated near-identical compositions, unlicensed reference reuse, illegible added text and a world that no longer relates to the product/brief. Verify each image’s specific connection.
