# Fashion worldbuilding montage

Read the [shared contract](../creative-diversity-router/references/production-contract.md) and [R12 audit](../../research/R12/analysis.md). The 7.6s source uses 15 still-image holds, approximately half a second each, under a small persistent seasonal theme. Music has vocals according to model-assisted analysis; there is no established spoken ad script.

## Define the world before selecting images

Write one specific desired-life proposition: how the wearer looks, what materials surround them and what ordinary moments express that taste. Translate it into a palette, season/light, tailoring language, texture set and appropriate locations. Do not substitute a vague adjective such as luxury for these decisions.

Choose a purposeful sequence across image roles. R12 alternates worn tailoring, knit/fruit still life, interiors, horse/country imagery, family, runway and leather/knit objects. Brown, green, cream, burgundy, wood and tactile fabrics make the worlds feel related. The meaning comes from association, not a causal claim.

| Image role | Why it belongs |
|---|---|
| Complete outfit | Establish or re-establish how the viewer might dress. |
| Material/object detail | Make the palette tactile and tie the world to the product. |
| Inhabited interior | Show the kind of atmosphere associated with the taste, not an unrelated expensive room. |
| Human relationship/activity | Give the aspiration a life context beyond possessions. |
| Contrasting fashion silhouette | Refresh attention while preserving the world's visual logic. |
| Closing look | Return abstract associations to a wearable outcome. |

State a specific link from each image to the previous and next: material, color, gesture, subject or life context. Reject an image that is attractive but adds no relevant association. Keep compositions and subjects distinct; a common palette does not require repeated near-identical outfits.

## Edit and rights

Stillness is a valid choice. Use clean cuts when the image itself contains sufficient interest. R12 does not require invented camera animation to explain its rhythm. Choose target holds according to image complexity and intended feeling; do not mechanically apply half-second cuts to every brand or duration.

A short stable theme line can unify the sequence. Preserve hierarchy so it does not compete with faces or small objects. Existing editorial/brand marks inside reference imagery are source content, not permission to reuse them as target assets. Build with original, owned or appropriately licensed imagery; GPT Image 2 handles new image production under the workspace rules.

Select music for the intended emotional world and verify synchronization in the target. Do not transcribe or reuse song lyrics from the reference. If a target needs a sales CTA, make the transition from mood to action explicit and preserve enough recognition time for the product.

Deliver the compact editing plan with the world definition, every image's role/sequence rationale, actual source or generation need, timing, text and music direction. QA visual coherence, genuine variety, product identity, source rights and a closing frame that resolves the fashion desire. Do not claim the montage demonstrates function or verified performance.
