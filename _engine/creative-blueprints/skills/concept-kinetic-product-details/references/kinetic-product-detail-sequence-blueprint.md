## Concept and script architecture

Rapid distinct views make one product’s form, surfaces, hardware and interior register as an attractive coherent object.

Visual inventory: dramatic whole/open shape → signature surface/hardware → interior or underside → carry/function detail → resolved whole. No narration required; still replacement does not establish stop-motion capture.

## Keep and vary

**Defining grammar:** One accurate product, genuinely different informative compositions, coherent light/background, tactile-to-whole comprehension and purposeful rhythm.

**Adaptable:** Feature inventory, shot count, hold times, music and ending. Source repeated cycles are observed; target should not copy them as filler.

## Required asset roles

Exact product/color/size references; distinct verified feature views; coherent lighting/background plan; ordered still inventory; music rights; optional brand/CTA. No video generation required for intentionally still holds.
