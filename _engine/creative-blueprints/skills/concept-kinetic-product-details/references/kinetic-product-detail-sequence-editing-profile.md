## Coverage and layer structure

Full-frame still images of one product. No presenter layer or mandatory explanatory text. The rapid sequence itself supplies motion impression.

## Cut rules and protected holds

Direct replacement on deliberate rhythm with recognition checks. Alternate whole, signature and construction scale rather than adjacent near-identical crops. Return whole after a detail cluster.

## Transitions and handles

Zero-overlap hard cuts. No morphs, simulated product transformation or automatic crossfades. Loop handoff is a planned visual return only if authorized by the brief, not repeated multi-cycle filler.

## B-roll entry, exit and return

These are primary visual product views; in a narrated hybrid insert a relevant detail only on its descriptor, leave after recognition and return to the parent product/speaker state. Do not reuse this rapid rhythm for complex mechanisms.

## Camera, reframing and subject action

No within-still motion required. A new physical angle adds information; digital crop/zoom does not establish a new scene. If a real action is needed, change the asset plan rather than pretending still replacement demonstrates it.

## Captions and graphic events

Default no captions. Any feature name/price must receive real reading time and exact evidence. A three-tenths-second slot is unsuitable for an offer paragraph.

## Voice, music and sound design

Music may guide image rhythm; listen to actual track and choose cut accents. No assumed source BPM or exact synchronization. Optional subtle effects must not overwhelm tactile recognition.

## Image finish and time treatment

Consistent neutral backdrop, material hue, grain/finish and hardware. Light may emphasize different surfaces while product identity stays fixed. No unnecessary speed manipulation of stills.

## Reveal, CTA and ending

Resolve into full silhouette before optional identity/action. A single purposeful sequence is preferred under visual-variety rules; a loop needs a clear brief-specific role.

## Shorter, longer and timing conflicts

Shorten by selecting fewer distinct features while keeping whole/detail/whole comprehension. Lengthen by genuinely new physical views or a different useful action, not repeat cycles. Complex construction gets longer holds.

## Reject and verify

Reject invented compartments/hardware, inconsistent strap attachment, falsely certified material/scale, duplicated source crops and rapid abstract flashes that obscure the object. Verify every still against approved product references.
