Use a brand-cultural-interview skill when the audience is invited into a taste world through a real or transparently staged conversation. Prepare topics and follow-up opportunities; preserve actual answers, listening and meaningful pauses. Choose coverage by who carries information or reaction. Use location resets as chapters and retain two-shots when the relationship is the point. Do not automatically replace anecdotes with stock footage or bolt on an unsupported sales VSL.
