# Source study: 14 references

Study date: September 16, 2026. This is the portable evidence subset for the [engine library](../README.md). The original research log remains in the originating vault; production does not require its temporary jobs or local download paths.

**Coverage:** 21,504 decoded frames over 745.674 seconds. Every frame was inspected in chronological contact sheets at approximately 180px width; selected boundaries and details were inspected at larger scale. This is exhaustive thumbnail coverage with selected enlarged checks, not full-resolution review of every pixel. Frame intervals are zero-based and normally start-inclusive/end-exclusive; preserve source PTS for fractional frame rates.

**Audio:** model-assisted interpretation, not direct audition. Approximate speech maps are not word-aligned subtitles. R04's exact spoken/sung boundaries remain unresolved. Exact sound synchronization, instruments, mix settings and performance are unverified.
