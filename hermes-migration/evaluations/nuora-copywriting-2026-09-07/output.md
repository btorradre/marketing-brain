This is a paid demonstration of LoopDock. I put each cable in its own channel and fasten the reusable strap to hold it coiled. Now I lift the organizer out and move it to another bag. The rigid base keeps the organizer from folding as I transfer it. LoopDock is twenty-nine dollars. If you switch bags, take a look at how these features keep your cables organized for the move.
