A woman at the pharmacy stopped me while I was paying. She looked at my face and said, sorry, can I ask you something? I said, of course. She said, how old are you? I laughed and told her. Because a year earlier, no one would ask me that. My skin looked tired all the time. About a year ago, I changed one thing. A dermatologist friend told me I was using the wrong type of skin care. She said mature skin needs support, not just moisture. At first, my skin felt better. Then it looked more even. Then the deep lines softened. It was not fast, but it was real. My neighbor noticed first. Then my hairdresser said I looked different. People started asking questions. Back at the pharmacy, she smiled and said, I hope I look like that at your age. I thanked her. And for the first time in years, I believed it.
