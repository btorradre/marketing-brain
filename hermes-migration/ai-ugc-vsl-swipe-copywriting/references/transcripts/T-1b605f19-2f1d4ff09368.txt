My aesthetician paused my facial and said something she claimed she's never said in 19 years of doing skincare. She leaned back, stared at my face and said, Linda, this doesn't make sense. She grabbed my chart and pointed to notes from last year. Elastity loss, deep dehydration, collagen decline. I remember that visit clearly because after menopause my skin had changed so quickly it scared me. But about a year ago I started doing something different. A dermatologist friend told me most products hydrate the surface. She said mature skin needs ingredients that actually rebuild structure underneath. At first my skin just felt more hydrated. Then the dull gray look faded. Then the deep lines around my mouth slowly softened. I noticed it but I thought maybe I was imagining things. Then other people started noticing. My daughter asked if I had done something to my face. My hairdresser said my skin looked brighter. Even my neighbor told me I looked 10 years younger. My aesthetician turned the lamp back on and studied my skin again. Then she leaned back and shook your head. Linda, she said, I've seen thousands of faces under this lamp and 70 year old skin doesn't usually improve like this.
