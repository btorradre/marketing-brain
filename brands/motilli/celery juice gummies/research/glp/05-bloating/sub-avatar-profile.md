## SECTION 3: ROUTINE & SITUATION DISCOVERY (20%)

### Daily Friction Map

**Morning (6-8 AM):**
- Wakes up after interrupted sleep (1-2 hour intervals due to gas)
- Checks mirror: assesses bloating severity for the day
- Selects outfit based on bloating visibility (elastic waistbands, loose fabrics)
- Pain assessment: Is today manageable or severe?
- Decision: Can I socialize/go out, or do I need to stay home?

**Mid-Morning (9 AM-12 PM):**
- Food anxiety: What can I eat that won't trigger bloating/pain?
- Avoids carbonated drinks, high-FODMAP foods, high-fat meals
- Eating smaller portions despite minimal appetite suppression from GLP-1
- Gas management: burping, belching in private
- Bloating peaks post-eating

**Afternoon (1-4 PM):**
- Physical management attempts: walking, stomach rubbing, positional changes
- Social friction: canceling plans if bloating is severe
- Photo avoidance: refuses to take pictures, controls camera angles in group settings
- Clothing friction: pants feel tighter, waistbands uncomfortable

**Evening (5-8 PM):**
- Anticipatory anxiety about nighttime gas/sleep disruption
- Dinner decisions: eat early to allow digestion time? Skip dinner?
- Spouse/partner communication: "Be prepared for the burping tonight"
- Begins nighttime bloating phase

**Night (9 PM-6 AM):**
- Interrupted sleep cycles (1-2 hour intervals)
- Active gas management: squatting, stomach massage, lying on stomach
- Spouse may assist or sleep separately to avoid disturbance
- Frustration accumulates as night progresses
- By morning, exhausted AND bloated
