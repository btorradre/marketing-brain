## 4. THE TWO TRAPS (why what she does RIGHT NOW keeps her stuck)

The Nivara wrong-version model, now with two live villains. Each trap is a thing she is doing today, tied to a symptom she feels today.

### Trap 1: The osmotic laxative trap (THE FEAR BEAT)
Her maintenance routine is daily MiraLAX, magnesium citrate on bad weeks. VOC shows daily osmotic use normalized as "prevention."

- **Wrong end:** osmotics pull water into the COLON. The signal died upstream. She is flooding the exit of a conveyor belt whose motor is off.
- **The treadmill:** it forces an evacuation, restores nothing, and day one of the next backup starts immediately. That is why she has been on it for months with zero progress. Forcing is not fixing.
- **The fear escalation (use with the exact sourced wording):** long-term regular laxative use has been linked in published research to significantly higher dementia risk, with the strongest association found for osmotic laxatives specifically (UK Biobank cohort, Neurology, 2023; roughly 50 to 60 percent higher risk among regular users). Plus the label-level realities: cramping, urgency, electrolyte disruption, dependence on it just to go at all.
- **Compliance envelope (hard rule):** ALWAYS "linked to" / "associated with," NEVER "causes." Cite "a major 2023 study" or "research in the journal Neurology." Do not say laxatives damage the colon and do not use "cathartic colon." The defensible fear is (a) the published association and (b) the treadmill: months of daily use, still constipated, now dependent on forcing it. That second one needs no study and she feels it already.

### Trap 2: The protein-fiber trap (the fiber beat, rebuilt)
- **Setup (vindication):** high-protein eating is CORRECT on a GLP-1. She needs it for muscle. But protein has no fiber, so the fix everyone gives her is "eat more fiber, 25 to 35 grams a day."
- **The backfire:** bulk fiber works by absorbing water and swelling into mass. On a normal gut that mass stretches the wall and triggers movement. On a signal-quieted gut nothing responds to the stretch. She just parked more freight on a stopped belt. That is the worse bloat, the gas, the brick feeling after Metamucil. Even the doctors admit it: raising fiber fast on a slowed gut makes bloating and gas worse.
- **The double dehydration:** bulk fiber also DEMANDS water to swell. Slow transit is already over-drying her stool. Bulk fiber on a slow gut competes for the water her stool needed. Bigger, drier mass. Harder exit.
- **The right version:** the answer was never zero fiber and never bulk fiber, it is the LOW-VISCOSITY SOLUBLE kind: dissolves fully, adds no bulk, forms a soft water-holding gel that keeps moisture IN the stool instead of letting the slow colon strip it out, and feeds the gut bacteria that support the motility rhythm. Fiber that works WITH a slowed gut instead of against it.
- This trap is the direct parallel to FerraVital's "85 percent of your iron sits in your gut" beat: the responsible thing she is already doing is producing the symptom she hates.
