## 3. THE UMP — Unique Mechanism of the Problem

### The root cause (upstream)
GLP-1 medications slow the **stomach** — that's the point (slow stomach → feel full → eat less → lose weight). But when it works too well, the stomach muscles essentially **fall asleep**. Food sits, stalls, and ferments. That single upstream failure fans out into the whole cluster:

- **Ferments → hydrogen sulfide gas → sulfur burps** (the rotten-egg smell).
- **Sits and hardens → constipation, bloating, "cement stomach," nausea.**
- **She can barely eat → under-nutrition** (missing vitamins → fatigue, brain fog, "Ozempic face").

### Why everything she tried failed (LEAD WITH ABSOLUTION — NEVER BLAME)
**Every remedy she's tried works on the COLON (downstream). Her problem is at the BEGINNING of digestion, not the end.**
- **Osmotic laxatives (Miralax/PEG, magnesium):** pull water into the *colon*. Doesn't touch the stalled stomach.
- **Stimulant laxatives (Dulcolax, senna):** crack a whip at *colon* muscles. Same miss, plus cramping/urgency.
- **Bulk fiber powders (Metamucil, fiber gummies):** add mass to the *colon* — and for someone whose stomach is already too full and too slow, **volume makes the fullness and nausea worse.** This is why "just eat more fiber" backfires for her specifically.
- **Gas-X / simethicone:** breaks up gas bubbles but does nothing about the hydrogen sulfide *odor* — so the smell stays.
- **Probiotics:** drop in bacteria downstream; mixed results, and again never address the sleeping stomach.

> **Compliance guardrail:** Frame laxatives/fiber as *targeting the wrong location (downstream)*, NOT as "addictive," "damaging," or "cathartic colon" (ACG/AGA consider that a myth — scientifically wrong and FTC-risky). The defensible villain is **downstream vs. upstream**, not laxative harm.

### The fiber-backfire beat (handles her objection inside the problem)
Coarse or high-dose bulk fiber packs *more volume* into a stomach that's already too full and too slow — that's the bloat and nausea. The fix isn't more bulk; it's getting the stomach moving. (This is why Motilli's fiber is a **gentle, low-volume prebiotic micro-dose** — feed good bacteria *without* adding the bulk that makes her worse.)

## 9. THE NIVARA-STYLE MECHANISM MODEL (how to keep the UMS dead simple)

Modeled on **Nivara / FerraVital** (iron for GLP-1 hair loss) — a proven scaler (~0→101K impressions in 6 days, Jul 2026). Its UMS lands because it's **one clean "aha," not a stack of claims.** The pattern:

**The "WRONG VERSION" mechanism.** Don't argue she needs a *new* thing (she's tried everything). Argue the thing she already reached for was the **wrong kind / wrong end** — AND that wrong version is itself *causing a symptom she's feeling right now*. Then the solution is simply the *right* version.

Nivara's build, beat by beat:
1. **Hook = vindication + reframe + villain:** "losing hair on a GLP-1, and the iron you bought is wrecking your stomach — you bought the *wrong kind*."
2. **Authority:** "I'm a doctor who prescribes these."
3. **Vindicate her instinct:** "You were right that it's your iron." (never make her feel dumb)
4. **Real mechanism, one simple pathway:** GLP-1 kills appetite → ferritin crashes → hair starves. ("Everyone says it's the weight loss. It's not. It's the iron.")
5. **Why the shelf fix fails — tied to a symptom she feels:** ferrous sulfate absorbs ~15%, the other **85% sits in your gut = your nausea, cramping, constipation.** ← the villain-form IS the secondary pain.
6. **Kill the obvious objection:** "you can't just eat more iron — the whole point of the med is you eat less."
7. **UMS = the right version + helpers:** iron bisglycinate (absorbs ~4× better, gentle) + cofactors (C, B6, B12, folate).
8. **Soft product reveal:** "you could take these separately, but easier is one called FerraVital, all in one capsule."
9. **Timeline proof:** energy in a few weeks, shedding slows by wk 6–8.
10. **Resolution/identity:** "stay on the GLP-1, keep losing weight — with your hair and energy intact."
11. **Risk reversal + rally close:** 90-day guarantee · "don't let your iron be the thing that makes you quit."
