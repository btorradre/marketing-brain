# SECTION 1: CROSS-AVATAR MESSAGING RULES

## Universal Dos

**DO: Center her identity, not just symptoms**
- Every message should speak to who she wants to be, not just what she wants to avoid
- Example: "Keep your medication AND your dignity" vs. "Fix your constipation"
- Why: Symptom relief feels temporary; identity restoration feels permanent

**DO: Acknowledge the impossible choice**
- She's chosen weight loss over comfort; every message should validate this trade-off without shame
- Example: "You love your medication. We get it. Let's just remove the cost."
- Why: She's tired of being dismissed or judged; validation is more persuasive than pressure

**DO: Use her exact language**
- Pull phrases directly from Reddit, health forums, and VoC research
- "Brick in my stomach," "concrete," "sulfur burps," "the pregnancy paradox," "stolen my dating life"
- Why: Insider language signals "I understand your specific pain, not a generic one"

**DO: Educate on mechanism before pushing product**
- Before saying "try Motilli," explain why her past solutions failed
- Upstream vs. downstream (constipation), symptom vs. cause (diarrhea), stomach-speed vs. nausea-signal (nausea)
- Why: Sophisticated buyers want to understand the "why"; education builds belief

**DO: Lead with peer testimonials**
- She trusts women like her more than doctors or celebrities
- Testimonials should include: specific problem → what she tried → why it failed → turning point → result
- Why: Peer proof removes skepticism faster than any other proof mechanism

**DO: Separate product messaging from medication messaging**
- Motilli = GLP-1 SUPPORT, not GLP-1 replacement or criticism
- Every message should reinforce: "Your medication is working. We're just making it livable."
- Why: She LOVES her medication; attacking it kills the entire sale

**DO: Make the connection between suffering and identity loss**
- Don't just address constipation; address what it costs her (sex life, work confidence, dating)
- Don't just address bloating; address what it costs her (visibility of success, pride in weight loss)
- Why: Emotional stakes are higher than physical stakes; motivation to buy increases

**DO: Offer permission, not pressure**
- "You don't have to suffer in silence" / "You deserve relief" / "It's not selfish to want your life back"
- Why: She's already made the hard choice (taking GLP-1); easy choices feel manipulative

---

## Universal Don'ts

**DON'T: Attack the medication**
- Never imply she made a bad choice taking GLP-1
- Never suggest "natural alternatives" to weight loss drugs
- Never frame GLP-1 as the problem (you're the problem, we're the solution)
- Why: Instant credibility destruction; she LOVES the medication

**DON'T: Use clinical/textbook tone**
- Avoid: "gastroparesis," "receptor agonist," "prokinetic compounds" without context
- Why: She's been gaslit by doctors; clinical language feels dismissive

**DON'T: Promise miracles or timeline guarantees**
- Avoid: "Cured in 3 days!" / "Gone forever!" / "100% relief guaranteed"
- Better: "Most women see improvement in week 1-2" / "Relief comes faster than waiting for adjustment"
- Why: False promises kill credibility; realistic timelines build trust

**DON'T: Use shame-based language**
- Avoid: "Disgusting," "embarrassing," "weak," "broken," "can't control yourself"
- Better: "Your body's having a predictable reaction to your medication" / "You're not alone in this"
- Why: She already feels shame; adding more kills the sale

**DON'T: Minimize the suffering**
- Avoid: "Just take a gummy!" / "It's just a side effect" / "Many people have it worse"
- Why: Dismissal signals you don't understand the severity; she'll scroll past

**DON'T: Assume all women have all symptoms**
- Each sub-avatar has distinct pain; constipation messaging won't work for hair loss sufferer
- Create separate messaging tracks by symptom type
- Why: Irrelevant messaging = no scroll stop

**DON'T: Lead with price or bundles**
- Save pricing/offers for mid-funnel or checkout
- Lead with transformation, not transaction
- Why: Price before belief = bargain hunting, not committed buying

**DON'T: Show celebrity endorsements or influencer marketing**
- She actively distrusts celebrity supplements (Lemme = eye-rolls)
- Why: Signals marketing gimmick, not medical legitimacy

## Multi-Symptom Sufferers (The Most Common Profile)

**Key Insight:** Most women aged 50-70 on GLP-1 experience 2-3 symptoms simultaneously (constipation + nausea + hair loss, or bloating + sulfur burps + nausea). Single-symptom isolation is rare.

### Strategy for Multi-Symptom Messaging

**1. Lead with the symptom that creates most identity-level pain (highest impact)**

| If she has... | Lead with... | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Constipation + Nausea | Constipation (ER risk, physical crisis) | Urgency + physical pain = higher emotional charge |
| Bloating + Sulfur Burps | Sulfur Burps (social exile) | Social identity threat > physical appearance threat |
| Hair Loss + Bloating | Hair Loss (visible aging) | Looking older directly contradicts weight loss benefit |
| Nausea + Hair Loss | Hair Loss (appearance) | Nausea is persistent but private; hair loss is public |
| Diarrhea + Bloating | Diarrhea (loss of control) | Loss of bodily autonomy > appearance crisis |
| Constipation + Bloating | Constipation (medical emergency risk) | Medical emergency overrides appearance concern |

**2. Acknowledge the "symptom cluster" in secondary messaging**

Don't ignore the other symptoms. Validate them:
- "Constipation, AND you're bloated, AND you feel nauseous? That's the GLP-1 trifecta."
- "Sulfur burps AND constipation are hitting you at the same time — you're living the nightmare."

**3. Position Motilli as addressing the ROOT CAUSE that cascades to multiple symptoms**

Example: "When your stomach can't move food properly (one upstream problem), you get constipation, nausea, gas buildup, and bloating. Fix the root (motility), and the cascade resolves."

**4. Use "symptom relief timeline" to show multi-symptom improvement**

- Week 1: Constipation easing (most physical symptom resolves fastest)
- Week 2: Nausea improving (as food moves through, gas/nausea decrease)
- Week 3: Bloating reducing (as gas clears)
- Week 4+: Hair loss slowing (as nutrition absorption improves)

**5. Show the multi-symptom benefits in testimonials**

Real testimonials should mention: "I was dealing with constipation, bloating, AND nausea. After 3 weeks on Motilli, I can poop regularly, the bloating is gone, and I can actually keep food down. It's not just one problem solved; it's like I got my whole system back."

## The Master Narrative (Applies to All Sub-Avatars)

**The Single Unifying Story Across All 6 Symptom Types:**

She found the medication that silenced the constant food noise in her head. For 40+ years, food was an obsession. Now, it's quiet. Weight loss is happening. She's finally winning.

**She now believes:** "The weight loss is real, but the cost is too high. Did I make a terrible mistake?"

---

### ACT III: The False Solutions Fail
She tries standard remedies. The internet promised them. Her doctor suggested them. Nothing works.

- **OTC laxatives** don't touch constipation (wrong mechanism)
- **Imodium** provides temporary relief but doesn't address diarrhea's root cause
- **Zofran** fails her nausea (she's tried it; it didn't work)
- **Gas-X** and **probiotics** don't stop sulfur burps
- **Fiber supplements** make her bloating worse
- **Biotin and collagen** supplements don't stop hair from falling out

**She now believes:** "Nothing works. I'm broken. I have to choose between the weight loss I want and my quality of life."

---

### ACT IV: The Solution Reframe (Motilli's Positioning)
Someone says, "You've been treating the SYMPTOM. Fix the ROOT CAUSE, and the symptoms resolve."

**The Root Cause:** GLP-1 slows stomach motility (intentionally, for appetite control). Everything she's suffering from traces back to this ONE problem: food isn't moving through her system properly.

- Food backs up → constipation (it's stuck)
- Stomach empties too fast in some people → diarrhea (it's rushing)
- Food sitting longer → fermentation → gas → nausea/sulfur burps/bloating
- Poor nutrient absorption → hair loss (malnutrition)

**The Reframe:** "You don't have a constipation problem, a nausea problem, or a hair loss problem. You have a STOMACH MOTILITY problem. One upstream fix addresses all the downstream chaos."

**Motilli's mechanism:** Celery juice contains apigenin (prokinetic compound) that gently wakes up stomach muscles. Not harsh. Not a laxative. Just a gentle "hey, time to move food through."

**She now believes:** "Oh. THIS is why nothing else worked. They were all treating the wrong problem. Motilli addresses the actual root."

---

### ACT V: The Identity Restoration
Within weeks, she notices: food moves through her system. Digestion works. Gas clears. The cascade of symptoms resolves.

But more importantly: she gets her IDENTITY back.

- She can date again (no more sulfur burp terror)
- She can sleep in the same bed as her husband (no more intimacy rupture)
- She can go to work meetings without planning bathroom access
- She can eat a meal without gagging or panicking
- She can see herself in the mirror and recognize herself (no more gaunt, aged appearance)
- She can wear regular clothes (no more maternity pants)

**She now believes:** "I kept my medication AND got my life back. This is how it was supposed to work all along."

# SECTION 2: CONSTIPATION SUFFERER — SCHWARTZ 5 LEVELS

## Level 1: UNAWARE
**Definition:** Doesn't know GLP-1 constipation is solvable. Thinks it's just "the price you pay" and will go away on its own.

**What stops the scroll:** Personal story of someone like her who discovered constipation wasn't inevitable — it was solvable.

**Hook example:** "I spent 2 weeks thinking I'd ruined my body with Ozempic. Turns out, I just needed to know the right solution existed."

### Lead Angle
"It's not permanent. And you don't have to white-knuckle through it."

The entire lead should position constipation as **temporary + solvable** rather than **permanent + inevitable**.

### Key Belief Shift Needed
**FROM:** "This is just what happens on GLP-1. Everyone suffers through it for a month or two."
**TO:** "This is a specific problem with a specific solution. I don't have to suffer."

### Example Opening Line (2-3 sentences, consumer voice)
"I hit day 9 without going and thought I'd broken something inside me. My friend told me she fixed the same thing in 2 weeks with one supplement she found on Reddit. I was skeptical, but I was desperate enough to try. That conversation changed everything."

### The "Big No"
**NEVER say:** "Constipation is just a normal side effect."
**Why it kills the sale:** She already knows it's normal. Telling her it's normal without offering solutions signals dismissal. She'll scroll past.

### Mechanism Bridge
At Level 1, she doesn't yet understand that **laxatives address the colon (downstream), but GLP-1 constipation is a stomach problem (upstream)**.

Bridge statement: "You've probably tried MiraLax or fiber, right? Those work on your colon — which is the END of digestion. But GLP-1 slows down the beginning. That's why nothing's working. That's also why this specific approach works."

# FINAL GUIDANCE FOR COPYWRITERS

## Universal Rules (Apply These to Every Ad)

1. **Lead with identity, not symptom.** Use her name + her fear + her desired self, not just the side effect.
2. **Educate before selling.** Explain the mechanism BEFORE you mention the product name.
3. **Use peer proof > authority.** Testimonials from women like her > doctor endorsements > celebrity.
4. **Speak her language.** Use Reddit phrases, insider terminology, exact pain descriptors from her community.
5. **Validate > minimize.** Never tell her it's "normal" or "temporary" as a solution. Validate first, then offer solutions.
6. **Offer permission.** She's already made the hard choice. Make the easy choice feel safe, not manipulative.
7. **Separate medication from solution.** Motilli supports GLP-1, not replaces or criticizes it.
8. **Show the timeline.** Week 1, Week 2, Week 3 — specific progression matters for belief-building.
9. **Use mechanism as proof.** Understanding WHY this works converts better than "just trust me."
10. **End with identity restoration, not symptom relief.** The benefit isn't "no more constipation." It's "I got my life back."
