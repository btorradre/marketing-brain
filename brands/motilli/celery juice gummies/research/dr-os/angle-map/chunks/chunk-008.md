When she realizes it has been 5+ days without a bowel movement. Panic sets in. She Googles "when to go to ER for constipation." [Source: Medical articles on dose-dependent side effects; GoodRx, Mounjaro clinical data]

### 3. "The Public Incident"
Trapped gas in a meeting. Bloating so severe she looks visibly different. Or worse — the constipation-to-diarrhea swing catching her at work or a social event. This is the moment she feels her body has betrayed her. She may have been part of the self-described "sh*t the bed club."
[Source: Reddit accounts via Health and Me, Business Insider nurse story]

## Purchase Trigger Event
The most common purchase trigger is a **convergence moment**: Day 3-5 of constipation + visible bloating + a failed attempt with a previous remedy (Miralax did not work, or caused cramping). She opens her phone and searches "fiber gummies for Ozempic constipation" or "gentle constipation relief GLP-1" or sees a TikTok/Instagram ad at exactly the right moment.

[Source: Reddit remedy sharing patterns, GLP1effect.com aggregation, Amazon review patterns]

## Objection Threads

### Objection 1: "Does this actually work or is it just marketing?"
- GLP-1 users are skeptical of supplements generally — "But do they work and are they safe? Or are they just marketing hype?" [Source: Geisinger health article]
- They have already tried multiple products that failed
- They need specificity: HOW does it work differently from the Metamucil that didn't help?
- **Overcome by:** Mechanism explanation + social proof from other GLP-1 users specifically

### Objection 2: "Gummies never have enough fiber — you'd need to eat the whole bag"
- Educated users know that fiber gummies typically deliver 2-5g per serving vs. the 25-30g daily need
- "Gummies rarely provide a meaningful fiber dose without taking several per day" [Source: Casa de Sante / medical articles]
- They worry about sugar alcohols causing additional GI issues
- **Overcome by:** Differentiating mechanism (celery juice compounds that support motility, not just fiber bulk) + transparency about dose

### Objection 3: "I don't want to be dependent on ANOTHER product"
- Already taking a weekly injection, possibly Miralax daily, maybe magnesium
- Adding another supplement feels like admitting defeat
- Fear of creating a new dependency on top of GLP-1
- **Overcome by:** Positioning as working WITH the body's natural process, not forcing it; temporary support while body adjusts

### Objection 4: "Will this interact with my medication?"
- Legitimate medical concern about supplement-drug interactions
- GLP-1 medications already slow gastric emptying — will fiber make it worse?
- "Adequate hydration is critical to prevent paradoxical worsening of constipation" from fiber [Source: Fella Health, medical sources]
- **Overcome by:** Natural food-based ingredients (celery juice), doctor-friendly ingredient list, no stimulant laxatives

### Objection 5: "I've already tried fiber supplements and they made my bloating worse"
- Fiber without adequate water worsens constipation — many users learn this the hard way
- Psyllium husk caused gas/bloating for some users
- They associate "fiber supplement" with previous bad experiences
- **Overcome by:** Differentiating the celery juice mechanism (apigenin/luteolin supporting motility + soluble fiber) from bulk-only approaches. This works differently.

They trade tips and stacks, but the underlying belief is that they are stuck with this problem for as long as they are on the medication. No one has given them a framework that suggests their gut can actually ADAPT and function normally again with the right support.
