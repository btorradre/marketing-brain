Day 9 was the one that broke me.

I sat on the edge of the tub at 2 am doing math on my phone. Counting back to the last time I had gone.

My name is Dana. I am 52. I started Mounjaro® last March and I have lost 40 pounds since.

That night I would have given a lot of it back for one normal morning.

It Started Small. Then It Was 9 Days.

Bloating after dinner. Pants that would not button by 4 pm.

Then it got bad. 6 days without going. Then 9.

My stomach felt like wet cement. Hard, heavy, stuck.

Then came the burps. They tasted like rotten eggs.

I burped in the car with my daughter. She rolled down her window and did not say anything. She did not have to.

I bought everything. Miralax. Fiber gummies. Senna tea. Magnesium. Prune juice.

I hid the prune juice behind the orange juice so my husband would not ask.

Some of it worked once. None of it worked twice.

My Doctor Told Me To Drink More Water

He said to eat more fiber. He said it was a common side effect.

I was already drinking 80 ounces a day. I was already eating fiber.

So I started saying no to things. Dinner with friends. My son’s game. A trip we had planned for a year.

I was thinner than I had been in 20 years. And I was hiding in my house.

The Nurse Who Said We Were All Treating The Wrong Organ

One night I could not sleep. I was on my phone at 1 am reading about all of it again.

I found a long post from a nurse who works with people on these medications. It stopped me cold.

Here is how she explained it.

Think of digestion like a highway. Food gets on at your stomach. It gets off at your colon.

These medications do not slow down your colon. They slow down your stomach.

So food sits up top. For hours. Sometimes days.

It sits and ferments. That is where the rotten egg smell comes from.

It dries out and hardens. That is the cement feeling.

By the time anything reaches your colon, it is already a brick. Most of the time nothing gets there at all.

That was the part that got me. There was nothing in my colon to push out. The jam was way up top.

Then I Understood My Medicine Cabinet

  • Miralax pulls water into the colon. Nothing was in my colon.
  • Fiber adds bulk to move things through the colon. I was adding cars to a road that was already closed.
  • Senna squeezes the colon. You can squeeze an empty tube all day.

Every single thing I bought was aimed at the wrong end.

The 3 Things That Actually Work On The Stomach

So I looked up what actually works on the stomach. Three things kept coming up.

  • Apigenin. It is in celery. It supports the nerve that tells your stomach to push food along.
  • Chlorophyll. It grabs the sulfur gas and cancels it out before it comes up.
  • A soft, low bulk fiber. It softens what is stuck without adding more bulk.

Wake the stomach up. Handle the gas. Soften what is sitting behind it.

I went looking for one thing with all 3. That took me a while.

Most stomach products had nothing for the gas. Chlorophyll drops did nothing for movement. Most fiber was the bulky kind that made me worse.

Then I Found Motilli

It is a gummy made for people on these medications.

Celery juice concentrate for the apigenin. Chlorophyll. The soft low bulk fiber. All 3, in the right forms.

I ordered it that night.
