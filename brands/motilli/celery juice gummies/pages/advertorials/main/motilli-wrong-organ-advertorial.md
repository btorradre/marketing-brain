## The "Wrong Organ" Problem Nobody Is Talking About

Here's what I need you to understand — and what your doctor probably hasn't explained:

**GLP-1 medications work by slowing your stomach.**

That's the mechanism. That's how semaglutide and tirzepatide reduce appetite. They tell the muscles in your stomach wall to contract less frequently. Food stays longer. You feel full. You eat less. You lose weight.

**But when food sits in a slowed stomach, three things happen:**

**1. Fermentation.** Bacteria begin breaking down food that should have moved through hours ago. This produces hydrogen sulfide gas — rotten egg sulfur. That gas rises. Those are your sulfur burps.

**2. Distension.** The gas expands. Your stomach inflates. That's the bloating that makes you look pregnant by 4 PM and has you changing into elastic waistbands every evening.

**3. Transit failure.** The stomach muscles aren't pushing food down. Nothing arrives in your intestines on time. That's the constipation — not because your colon isn't working, but because your stomach isn't sending anything to it.

**One organ. Three symptoms. One root cause.**

## Why Everything You've Tried Has Failed

This isn't complicated once you see it.

**Gas-X (Simethicone)** breaks up gas bubbles in the intestine. Your sulfur gas is forming in the stomach. By the time Gas-X reaches where it works, the damage is done.

**Charcoal capsules** absorb gas in the lower GI tract. Same problem. The hydrogen sulfide has already been produced and released before charcoal can touch it.

**Miralax** pulls water into the colon to soften stool. Your problem isn't a dry colon — it's a stomach that isn't sending anything down. You're adding water to an empty pipe.

**Fiber supplements (Metamucil, psyllium)** add bulk to the colon. But on a GLP-1, your stomach is already struggling with volume. Adding bulk makes the fullness worse. Many of my patients tell me fiber made their symptoms *more* severe — and they're right. It does. Because it adds mass to a system that can't process the mass it already has.

**Probiotics** colonize the large intestine. They're useful for certain conditions. GLP-1 gastric slowdown isn't one of them. The fermenting bacteria causing your sulfur burps are in the stomach, not the colon.

The failure isn't you. The failure is a mismatch between where these products work and where your problem is.
