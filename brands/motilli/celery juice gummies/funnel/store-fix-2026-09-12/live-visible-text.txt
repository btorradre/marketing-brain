THE FIBER FROM THE AD

What does FOS actually do?

FOS is a prebiotic fiber. It feeds the good bacteria already living in your gut and can help support regular bowel movements. So when smaller meals mean less fiber, Motilli adds some of that fiber back into your day.

01
FOS prebiotic fiber

Also called fructooligosaccharides. This is the fiber we explain in the ad for daily regularity support.

02
Celery juice powder

Celery juice powder is included alongside the FOS fiber in Motilli's daily gummy formula.

03
Chlorophyllin

The formula also contains sodium copper chlorophyllin, a chlorophyll-derived ingredient.
