## The Core Mechanism (One-Paragraph Version)

GLP-1 medications slow down the stomach — that's how they kill appetite and cause weight loss. But when the stomach slows too much, food sits, ferments (causing sulfur burps), and hardens (causing constipation). Every standard remedy (Miralax, Dulcolax, fiber, enemas) works on the COLON (downstream), but the problem is in the STOMACH (upstream). Motilli's celery-derived prokinetic compounds gently wake up stomach muscles so food can move again — upstream fix for an upstream problem. Chlorophyll neutralizes the sulfur gas. Vitamins replace what she can't eat enough to get.

## Target Market

**Primary Avatar:** "The GLP-1 Survivor" — Women 50–70, peri/post-menopausal, on semaglutide or tirzepatide, middle-to-upper-middle income ($75K–$150K+), 40+ years of diet history. She LOVES her shot and won't stop, but the digestive side effects are ruining her quality of life.

**Secondary Avatar:** The Diarrhea Subavatar — same demographics, but experiencing recurring diarrhea/urgency rather than constipation. Unoccupied territory — no product specifically targets this.

**Market Size:** ~30M US GLP-1 users × 40–50% GI side effects = 12–15M potential customers. 3–5M actively seeking solutions.

**Blue Ocean:** GLP-1 side effect support is a Level 2 sophistication market — young, undersaturated, no dominant competitor. This is the window.

**Primary Angle:** "The Silent Tax of Skinny" — Mechanism + Enemy Hybrid. Validates her choice, names the enemy (side effects, not the drug), introduces upstream vs. downstream mechanism.

**Core Promise:** "Keep the weight loss. Ditch the digestive nightmare."

**Positioning:** The GLP-1 Companion — purpose-built for her specific lifestyle, not a repurposed fiber supplement.
