Every remedy you have been handed — the Miralax, the stool softeners, the fiber gummies, the prune juice — works on your colon. The downstream end.

It is a prokinetic — something that physically helps your stomach move food forward instead of letting it sit and ferment. Not a laxative. Not a bulk-forming agent. An upstream fix for an upstream problem.
