# Foliara — Brand Guide

*Botanical balance, bottled.*

Foliara is a pumpkin seed oil gummy supplement crafted for women aged 50–70 navigating hypothyroid, hormonal, and hair-thinning concerns. The brand sits at the intersection of **editorial wellness** (Moon Juice, Goop) and **modern apothecary** (Nuora, Olipop) — sophisticated, gentle, and trustworthy, with a mature sensibility one notch more refined than its younger-skewing peers.

## 2. Typography

- **Display / Wordmark:** *Trirong* or *Cormorant Garamond* — high-contrast serifs with a magazine-masthead cadence. Used for H1, hero, logo.
- **Editorial body:** *Tenor Sans* or *Montserrat Light* — wide-tracked uppercase for labels and microcopy.
- **Running body:** *Inter* 400 / 500 for paragraphs and UI at 16–18px, line-height 1.6.

**Hierarchy**
- H1 — Trirong 52–64px, tracking -1%
- H2 — Trirong 32–40px
- Eyebrow — Montserrat 11px, uppercase, +180 tracking
- Body — Inter 17px, Ink Bark on cream
- Button — Montserrat 13px, uppercase, +120 tracking, Terracotta fill

---

## 3. Voice & Tone

Foliara speaks like a knowledgeable older sister who has already walked this road. Warm, composed, specific, never alarmist. We don't "fix" women — we meet them where they are.

- **Is:** reassuring, botanical, specific, grounded, lightly poetic
- **Isn't:** clinical, girly, bro-science, miracle-cure, emoji-heavy, cutesy

**Vocabulary we reach for:** balance, nourish, quietly, gentle, ritual, thyroid, roots, rhythm, everyday, steady, morning.
**Vocabulary we avoid:** boost, hack, cure, slay, crush, snatched, game-changer.

**Tagline candidates**
- *Botanical balance, bottled.*
- *Roots of quiet strength.*
- *For the thyroid. For the hair. For the whole you.*
- *A gentler ritual for changing seasons.*

## 4. Logo Usage

- **Primary logo** (`logo/logo-primary.png`) — square stacked lockup. Use as main identity mark on cream or blush surfaces.
- **Horizontal lockup** (`logo/logo-horizontal.png`) — website headers, email banners, footers.
- **Clear space:** minimum padding equal to the cap-height of the "F" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 120px wide on screen, 25mm wide in print.
- **Color rules:** terracotta on cream is canonical. Sage on cream allowed for secondary/botanical contexts. Reversed (cream on terracotta) allowed for single-color moments. **Never** on pure white, pure black, or any hue not in the palette. **Never** stretch, skew, recolor outside palette, or add drop shadow.

---

## 5. Photography Direction

- **Light:** soft directional morning light, warm color temperature (3200–4000K feel)
- **Backdrops:** warm cream, blush peach, weathered marble, cream linen, terracotta tile as accent
- **Props:** whole raw pumpkin seeds, small pumpkins, olive/sage sprigs, brass spoons, cream linen
- **Models:** real women 50–65, silver-streaked or natural hair, visible skin texture, minimal makeup, calm/confident expressions. No AI-smoothed skin, no stock-photo vibes.
- **Forbidden:** clinical white backgrounds, harsh studio flash, neon colors, lab/syringe imagery, before/after body-shaming tropes.

---

## 6. Bottle & Label Layout

- **Bottle body:** warm sandy cream `#F0E4D1`, matte finish
- **Cap:** terracotta `#B8604A`, matte, low-profile
- **Label:** cream paper stock, centered composition
  1. Tiny sage leaf glyph (top, centered)
  2. `FOLIARA` wordmark — Trirong, terracotta, centered
  3. Thin hairline rule
  4. `PUMPKIN SEED OIL GUMMIES` — 10pt, Montserrat, uppercase, tracking +180, Ink Bark
  5. Bottom: small `60 GUMMIES · THYROID + HAIR SUPPORT` microcopy in taupe
