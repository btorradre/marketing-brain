How to Measure Your Ring Size:
1. Use a Paper Strip
Cut a 10 cm long, narrow paper strip.
Wrap it around the finger where you'll wear the ring.
Mark where it overlaps and measure the length in millimeters.
This measurement is your ring size. For example: 54 mm = Size 54.
3. Measure an Existing Ring
Measure the inner diameter of a ring you already have.
Multiply that number by 3.14 — the result is your ring size.
4. Visit a Local Jeweler
You can also visit a nearby jeweler — they usually offer sizing free of charge.
Tip:
Is your ring size between two sizes? Choose the larger one.
