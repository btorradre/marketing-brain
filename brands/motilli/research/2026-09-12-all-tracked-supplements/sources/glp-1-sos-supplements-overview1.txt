Here's What Your Doctor Never Explained
Here's what nobody tells you when you start these medications:
GLP-1s don't just slow your appetite. They slow your entire digestive system.
That's how they work — they slow gastric emptying by 50%+. But it also means everything gets stuck.*
Stimulant laxatives
(Dulcolax, Senna, Ex-Lax) work by triggering muscle contractions in your intestines. But your GLP-1 is actively suppressing those contractions. You're fighting against your own medication. Result: painful cramping, unpredictable urgency, or nothing at all.
Fiber supplements
(Metamucil, Benefiber) work by adding bulk to stimulate movement. But bulk requires movement to work — and your GLP-1 has slowed movement. Adding more bulk to a system that barely moves just means more bloating. Many people report fiber makes them feel worse.
Miralax
draws water into the colon — can partially help. But it doesn't address the root cause (slowed gastric emptying). Doesn't help with sulfur burps. Many need higher and higher doses for diminishing results.
