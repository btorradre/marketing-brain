Everything You Need to Know
We believe choosing tea should be simple. Find quick answers to the most common questions about our blends, sourcing, and shipping — so you can shop with confidence.
What are the health benefits?
PiPi Tea is naturally rich in minerals and antioxidants that help support balance, focus, and calm. It can aid digestion, improve mental clarity, and promote overall well-being — all while offering a clean, chemical-free source of hydration.
What is the best time of the day to take PiPi Tea?
You can enjoy PiPi Tea any time of day. Many customers love it in the morning for gentle energy and clarity, or in the evening to unwind and restore balance. Since it’s naturally smooth and low in caffeine, it fits perfectly into any daily ritual.
How many servings are in a bag?
Each bag contains approximately 20 servings, depending on your preferred strength. Our premium loose leaves can often be re-steeped for a second, equally delicious cup.
Where is it sourced from?
All of our tea is sourced directly from the
Puer Mountains in Yunnan, China
, a region famous for its high-altitude soil, clean air, and centuries-old tea-growing traditions. Every leaf is grown with care — 100% organic, pesticide-free, and sustainably harvested.
