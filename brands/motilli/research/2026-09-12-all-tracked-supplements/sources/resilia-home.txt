That's a track record no synthetic compound can match. We only work with herbs that traditional wisdom trusted first, and modern research has since studied. Everything else is just noise.

Each Resilia® softgel is built around one ancient herb and one specific job:

Aged Odorless Garlic

Are Resilia® supplements third-party tested?
Yes. Every batch of Resilia® is third-party tested for purity, potency, and contaminants before it leaves the facility. What's on the label is what's in the softgel — no proprietary blends, no fillers, no hidden ingredients. If it isn't clean enough to test openly, it isn't clean enough to sell.
Can I take Resilia® with other supplements or medications? ‡
Resilia® is made from food-grade botanical ingredients that are generally well-tolerated alongside most daily supplement routines. That said, if you're currently taking medication, are pregnant or nursing, or have a medical condition, we recommend talking to your healthcare provider before starting any new supplement.‡
