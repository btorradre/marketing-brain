    How is this different from the iron pills I already tried?

    No pill to swallow and no ferrous sulfate. The raspberry strip melts on your tongue in about 30 seconds and is built to be gentle enough to take every single day. Your body absorbs iron best in small daily amounts, so the dose you keep taking is the dose that counts.

    Is 19mg really enough to move my ferritin?

    Yes. Push an iron dose too high and it can trigger a signal that tells your gut to absorb less the next day. 19mg every single day beats a big pill you skip because it hurts your stomach.

    I have the MTHFR gene variant. Can I take CAVAÉ?

    Yes. The CDC says people with MTHFR variants can still use folic acid, and 400mcg DFE is the daily amount they recommend for adults. That's exactly what each strip contains. See the CDC guidance. If you have concerns, talk with your healthcare provider.

    How long until my ferritin moves?

    We won't promise a number or a date. Iron stores rebuild over months, and customers who track their labs usually retest at 8 to 12 weeks. That's why every order gets 60 days to prove itself on your own bloodwork.

    Will it upset my stomach like other iron?

    It's built not to. There's no pill to swallow and no ferrous sulfate. If iron pills have burned you before, this format exists for exactly that reason.

    What if it doesn't work for me? And how fast does it ship?

    Every order is backed by our 60-day promise. If the number didn't move, message us and we'll take care of you. We fulfill orders in 1 to 3 business days and ship USPS, which typically takes 3 to 5 days from there. Free shipping on 3+ month supplies.
