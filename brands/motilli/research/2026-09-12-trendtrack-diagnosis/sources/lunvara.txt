How does it actually work?
Your colon doesn't move because of what's in it. It moves because something tells it to. Bacteria in your colon make an acid called
acetate
— the signal that tells the colon wall to squeeze. Antibiotics, low-fiber years, and the hormonal shift in your 40s and 50s thin those bacteria out, and the signal goes quiet.
Laxatives make it worse: a flush strips bacteria out along with everything else, so the dose keeps climbing. And probiotics alone can't fix it — most die before they reach the colon, and the few that arrive have nowhere to take hold in a gut that isn't moving yet.
Lunvara does it in the right order. The finished kombucha acids get you moving
now
. Then spore-form bacteria that survive the trip — including the strain that makes acetate — plus the inulin they feed on rebuild the thing that makes the signal. So over time your gut does more of the work itself.
