- **Motion:** pill travel, organ facial acting, background microbe movement and the clearing action keep held shots active. R02 has a noticeable following/reframing move; the finished render does not establish whether it was a generated camera move or an editorial crop.
- **Handoffs:** cuts mark arrival in a new location, exposure of stakes, onset of action, the survivor reveal and the lesson. No dissolves or stylized transitions are visible at the verified boundaries.

## Psychological devices

These are editorial interpretations, not measured retention findings or statements of creator intent.

1. **Immediate incongruity:** an organ responds emotionally to an ordinary medication. Recognizable object plus impossible social situation creates a quick reason to look.
2. **Anthropomorphism:** eye direction and facial expression make internal biology understandable through social intentions: concern, confidence, warning, surprise and smugness.
3. **In-medias-res entrance:** the pill is already arriving. There is no greeting or topical preamble; the missing context is part of the hook.
4. **Journey anticipation:** following the pill through successive locations turns an abstract process into an unfolding event.
5. **Threat to valued helpers:** the helpful-bacteria warning gives the action emotional stakes. The viewer can anticipate trouble before the threatened event occurs.
6. **Competence reversal:** apparent confidence is followed by an admission of imperfect selectivity, then a survivor. Each turn revises the viewer's model of the pill.
7. **Action payoff:** the promised attack happens on screen, rewarding waiting before the next question is introduced.
8. **Surprise plus humor:** the surviving bacterium answers an apparent victory with defiance. It is a compact status reversal between characters.
9. **Low verbal load:** roughly 54 spoken words; face, movement and caption updates do much of the explanatory work. This is not a narrator-led VSL.
10. **Educational reward:** the final lesson reframes the entertainment as useful knowledge. A share/save motive is plausible; no sharing or retention data was supplied.

## Motilli transfer

Retain the organ-as-viewer, medicine-as-character, short exchanges, specific bodily actions, reaction pauses and a late reversal. Adapt the aggressive pill into two well-meaning attempted solutions. The emotional sequence becomes hope → recognition of the familiar approach → disappointment → an explanation → skepticism about the new arrival → transparent ingredient answer.

The user corrected the villain framing. The desired opening thought is that the usual approach has not delivered the comfort someone wanted, not that medicine is evil. Do not borrow the antibiotic attack, microbial destruction or survivor biology for MiraLAX or fiber.

Motilli contains prebiotic fiber. The proposed late reversal is the gut discovering that the new arrival also contains fiber. Explain type and role without claiming all other fiber is ineffective, FOS is unique, or Motilli fixes delayed gastric emptying. The draft's payoff is informed product evaluation. A depicted clinical rescue would require evidence currently absent.
