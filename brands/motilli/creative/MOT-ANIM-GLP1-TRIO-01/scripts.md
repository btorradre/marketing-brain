## Problem awareness — inside a GLP-1 gut

341 words; approximately 135 seconds including hold.

Watch the stomach. Food moves through it with muscular contractions. A GLP-1 can slow how quickly that food leaves. See how it stays there longer instead of passing through at the same pace?

You feel full for longer. But you still want to go to the bathroom comfortably and get on with your day.

Now watch what happens when you reach for MiraLAX or fiber. MiraLAX holds water in your stool, helping it soften and pass. A bulking fiber like psyllium absorbs water and helps form stool. Both can help you go.
