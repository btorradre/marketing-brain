## Evidence-dependent UMS slots

E1: specify a supported celery/apigenin action on the relevant digestive process and its viewer-relevant consequence. Existing material does not establish human GLP-1 stomach-emptying benefit. Do not fill with “restarts the stomach,” “celery enzyme,” or softened support phrasing carrying the same unsupported claim.
E2: specify a supported chlorophyllin action relevant to gas/bloating/odor, keeping those endpoints separate. Existing evidence does not establish GLP-1 gas neutralization or less bloating at the listed serving.
E3: state the supported combined benefit connecting all three roles to the UMP. This depends on E1/E2 and matching formulation evidence; co-presence does not demonstrate synergy. FOS feeding beneficial gut microorganisms is supported generally, not proof it corrects gastric emptying or that this product treats GLP-1 constipation.

These slots are explicit copy gaps, not authorization questions. All independent structural, hook, demonstration, supported physiology, product placement and editorial changes are completed now. Final factual mechanism copy cannot be completed from the reviewed mechanism document alone. No repeat request to the user is introduced.
