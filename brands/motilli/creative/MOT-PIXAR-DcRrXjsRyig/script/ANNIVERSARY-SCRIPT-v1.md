# The Anniversary He Thought She Forgot

**Motilli · Script draft 1 · Fictional animated short**

Tom and Linda, early 60s. Estimated110–125seconds with conversational pauses and visual holds. 202 spoken words. Timing is provisional; no audio has been generated.

*A candlelit anniversary dinner at home. Tom raises his glass. Linda suddenly pushes back her chair, nearly tipping hers. He catches it.*

**TOM:** Thirty-two years, and you still—

**LINDA:** Tom. I’m sorry. I can’t do this.

*She disappears down the hall. Tom lowers his glass.*

**TOM:** I hadn’t even started the speech.

*He stops outside the closed bathroom door.*

**TOM:** Linda? Have I done something?

**LINDA:** No.

**TOM:** Then why do you keep leaving?

*Inside, Linda sits on the closed toilet lid in her anniversary dress. She presses a tissue beneath one eye.*

**LINDA:** Because I’m bloated all the time. I can barely go to the bathroom. Since I started the shot, every dinner feels like a gamble.

**TOM:** Why didn’t you tell me?

*She looks at herself in the mirror. The earrings he gave her catch the light.*

**LINDA:** I wanted you to look at me in this dress. Not picture me stuck in here.

**TOM:** I thought you didn’t want our evenings anymore.

*Linda opens the door. Tom’s expression softens.*

**LINDA:** I wanted to lose the weight. I didn’t want to lose our evenings.

**TOM:** Then let’s stop pretending you’re fine.

*The next morning. Linda finishes a call to her prescriber and joins Tom at the kitchen table.*

**LINDA:** I finally called. My medication can slow how fast food leaves my stomach. Bloating and constipation can be side effects.

**TOM:** And you thought you just had to live with it?

**LINDA:** I thought I wasn’t supposed to complain.

*He slides her coffee closer and sits beside her.*

**TOM:** You can be happy about your progress and still ask for help.

*Later, during her morning routine. Linda sets a Motilli bottle beside her water glass.*

**TOM:** What’s that?

**LINDA:** Motilli. Celery juice gummies. I wanted something simple to add to my daily routine.

**TOM:** Gummies?

**LINDA:** You know how I feel about swallowing pills.

*A later date night. The same earrings, the same table. Linda gets up. Tom looks worried for a moment.*

**TOM:** Linda?

**LINDA:** I’m turning the music up.

**TOM:** I still have a speech.

**LINDA:** You can tell me while we dance.

*They dance, a little awkwardly, in the kitchen. End on the Motilli pack.*
