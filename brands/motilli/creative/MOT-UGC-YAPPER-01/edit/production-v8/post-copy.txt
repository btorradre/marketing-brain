Okay, long post, but I wanted to share because I know I'm not the only one dealing with this.

I've been on Ozempic for about a year. The weight was coming off, but I was backed up for months. It was so frustrating to have the shot working and still feel uncomfortable.

What I ended up trying was a blend of three ingredients: apigenin from celery juice, chlorophyllin, and a soluble prebiotic fiber. I'd tried them separately before. This was the first time I felt like anything had actually moved the needle.

People at my gym have been asking what I'm doing differently, so I figured I'd share here too. That's just my experience, but I wish someone had talked about this part sooner.
