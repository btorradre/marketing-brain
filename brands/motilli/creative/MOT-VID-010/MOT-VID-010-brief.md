## 💡 1. Concept

### WHY

She started the shot and the weight came off. Then things stopped moving, and she started buying. Miralax, stool softeners, magnesium, a fiber powder she mixes and does not finish. Four or five boxes are in that cabinet, half used, and none of them worked for long. What she has quietly concluded is that her body is the thing that is broken, because everyone else takes these and everyone else goes.

### WHAT

An AI-generated woman walks the viewer through the boxes already in the cabinet and reads the one line on each that says where it works. The comparison is location, never quality, and never safety. She never claims to be a customer and never claims a personal result. She names the real products by name and shows real packaging, because the proof of this ad is the trade dress: the boxes themselves say colon and bowel, and the slowdown starts at the stomach. No doctor, no clinic, no white coat.

### HOW

Open on the cabinet, put the real boxes on the counter, teach the GLP-1 stomach slowdown one time at 0:30, then read the labels back and let them do the work. Build a three-line checklist on screen: where it works, what is in it, how you take it. Withhold the Motilli bottle completely until 1:18, then hold the label beside the checklist. New B-roll every two to four seconds so the reading sequence never stalls, and finish on a handheld packshot.

### Runtime

1:48 for any selected hook and its matched bridge, followed by the shared body and CTA. 333 spoken words per aired variant, which is 105 seconds at a natural 190 words per minute plus about three seconds of air across the label inserts. All three hooks run 0:00-0:08 and all three bridges run 0:08-0:19, so every variant hands off to the same shared body.

## 🎬 3. Editing Notes

- Keep a small, unobtrusive `AI-GENERATED PRESENTER` disclosure on screen for the entire ad.
- New B-roll every two to four seconds through the reading sequence. Simple hard cuts, no transitions.
- Do not show the Motilli bottle, gummies, or any part of the label before 1:18.
- Competitor products are real, named, and legible. Show the actual printed directions line. Never deface, blur, cross out, or put a red X over a competitor product.
- Location only. Nothing on screen or in VO may call another product unsafe, harmful, habit-forming, or something you become dependent on. The claim is that it works somewhere else, and nothing more.
- No doctor, nurse, clinic, white coat, stethoscope, or medical office anywhere in the ad. No on-screen study card, percentage, review count, or patient number.
- Presenter never says she uses it, never reports a result of her own, never says "I."
- Every Motilli product frame is image-to-image from the real product reference pack. Never generate the bottle or label from imagination.
- Verify Buy 2 Get 1 Free and the 90-day money-back guarantee on the destination page again on launch day before export.
