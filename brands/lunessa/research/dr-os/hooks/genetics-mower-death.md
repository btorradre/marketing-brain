# Golden nugget

"My brother died doing everything right, and I am next in line." The topic is inherited cholesterol; the motive is surviving-sibling dread plus the injustice of a disciplined man dying anyway.

# HOOK 1 — The Sound That Stayed
My brother's lawn mower kept running for twenty minutes after his heart stopped. His wife found him because the sound stayed in one place.
- Pattern: crisis-scene opener (Alevia CPR/copper-elbow structure); trauma memory fixating on the wrong detail = truth certificate.

# HOOK 2 — The Half-Mowed Lawn ✅ CHOSEN
Half of my brother's backyard was mowed and half wasn't, and it stayed that way for three weeks, because nobody in the family could bring themselves to finish it.
Then one morning his neighbor did it before sunrise and never said a word about it. That half-finished lawn is the reason I'm writing this.
- Pattern: grief relic (pickle-jar structure). The lawn doubles as the ad's mechanism metaphor (front strip = diet, back half = liver production nobody mentioned), so hook, reframe, and ring-closure are one image.

# HOOK 3 — The Arithmetic
The first thing I felt when Beth called to tell me my brother was dead was arithmetic. Grief came second. He was 54. I'm 49. We come from the same blood.
I have never told anyone that until right now.
- Pattern: forbidden-thought confession (burned-grandson structure); the shameful first reaction every surviving sibling has and nobody admits.

# HOOK 4 — Dying Healthy
My brother finished a 10K eight weeks before his heart stopped. There's a photo of him at the finish line, grinning, holding a banana.
The men in my family die healthy. I need you to understand what that sentence does to the people they leave behind.
- Pattern: paradox hook (good-days-lie structure); collapses fitness=safety in two words; most surgically aimed at the fit-but-sick avatar.

# HOOK 5 — Forty Dollars
My sister-in-law sold my brother's lawn mower last month for forty dollars. She watched a stranger load it into his truck, walked back inside, called me, and cried for an hour.
It still ran fine. That was the hard part. It ran twenty minutes longer than he did.
- Pattern: artifact-with-afterlife (returned-Christmas-card structure); biggest pure scroll-stopper of the set.
