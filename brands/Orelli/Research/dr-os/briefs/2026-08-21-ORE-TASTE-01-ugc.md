# THE BRIEF

## OVERVIEW

This is a reply to the one question people keep asking: does it taste good? You are not going to answer it, because you have not tried it and neither has anyone else. You are going to say that out loud, and then tell them the thing you actually do know, which is what you have been swallowing, chewing and crushing for the last twenty years.

She is 25 to 38, she cannot swallow pills, and she has been quietly buying children's medicine as an adult. The conversion trigger for her is being seen. Play it like you are settling a question, not selling anything.

## WHAT YOU CANNOT SAY OR SHOW (read twice)

Orelli is an over-the-counter drug, not a supplement. Nobody has tried it yet, including the founders. The rules are stricter than anything you have shot for a wellness brand and the fines run to $51,744 per violation.

**Never:**
- Anything that says or implies you have tried it. No "it tastes like candy," no "it works," no "my new go-to."
- **No shot of you holding, eating, opening or receiving Orelli.** No unboxing, no "look what came." There is no Orelli product on your set.
- Any claim past the two on the label: temporarily relieves minor aches and pains, temporarily reduces fever. This script deliberately makes neither, which is cleaner still.
- "Better than," "stronger than," "faster than" anything. The angle is format, never potency.
- Nothing about pregnancy, nothing about kids' dosing, no dosing advice, no drug interactions, no doctor recommendations.
- "Get yours now," "in stock," "selling out," "shipping fast." It is a preorder.

**Always:**
- `#ad` in the **first line** of the caption, before the "more" cutoff, and visible on screen in the first few seconds.
- TikTok's branded-content toggle on.
- "Ships December 2026" spoken or on screen. It is spoken in line 8 and carded below, so you are covered twice.
- Your link and code in the **pinned** comment, and actually pinned.

**If someone asks in the comments whether it tastes good,** the only allowed answer, and the one that performs best:
> "No idea yet honestly, it doesn't ship till December. I preordered because I finally found something I can actually take."
