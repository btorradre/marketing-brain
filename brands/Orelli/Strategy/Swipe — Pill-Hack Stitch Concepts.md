## Which are STITCH targets vs ORIGINAL-content templates

**Two different jobs — don't mix them up.**

### → STITCH targets (the "shake head no → Orelli" concept): #1, #4, #5
The **hack / technique** videos are the right fodder. The whole persuasion is **authority subversion**: every one of these — even the doctor's — is an elaborate coping ritual for a problem that *shouldn't have to exist.* The head-shake "no" reframes the entire premise: *you're tilting your head like a baby bird… or you could just not swallow a pill at all.*
- **Best fodder = the absurd-effort hacks (#1, #4 head-tilt floats).** The visual contrast with "just chew a gummy" is sharpest. Dunk-ability is high (warmly).
- **#5 is weaker fodder** ("use enough water, center of mouth") — less absurd, harder contrast. Lower priority.
- **Tone rule:** never insult the doctor or the advice — we *have* doctors on our side (Workstream D). Play it warm: *"Great tip. Also tried it 100 times. Here's what finally worked."* Respect → reframe → reveal.

### → ORIGINAL-content templates (affiliates make their OWN): #2, #3
The **confessional** videos are NOT stitch targets — stitching your own avatar with a "no" mocks the very woman you're selling to. Instead these are the **template every seeded affiliate copies** to make original content:
- **#3 is a plug-and-play viral format:** **"[age] years old and this is how I swallow pills"** + grimace + jump-cuts. Hand this to affiliates verbatim. Ends on the Orelli reveal instead of resignation.
- **#2 is the emotional ceiling:** real-time panic, racing heart, "fear of choking." Authenticity no product shot can fake. The Recognition beat in its purest form.

## Concept refinement — the stitch ad, beat by beat

```
0:00–0:03  STITCH: the pill-hack plays (head-tilt float). Just long enough to register "oh, the pill hack."
0:03–0:05  CUT to affiliate. Slow head shake. "No. Stop."
0:05–0:12  "Cute hack. I've tried every one of these. You know what's easier than
            tilting your head like a baby bird every time you have a headache?
            Not swallowing a pill at all."
0:12–0:18  REVEAL Orelli gummy. "It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy.
            Real adult dose. No water, no gagging, no head tricks."
0:18–0:22  "I'm 26. I'm done doing the bird thing." [chews] [link in bio]
```

**Why it works (maps to the emotional spine):** the hack = Recognition ("I've tried that"), the "no" = pattern interrupt + vindication ("you were never the problem, the pill was"), the reveal = Revelation ("wait, this exists"), the chew = Liberation.

**Guardrails:** affiliate must disclose (#ad / affiliate). Claims limited to "relieves pain / reduces fever" — no "works better/faster than Tylenol" (same molecule; the angle is *format*). See playbook §10.

## Format intelligence (applies to ALL Orelli creative)
- Vertical, lo-fi, face-to-camera, shot on a phone in a real home. Zero production polish — polish reads as "ad," confession reads as "true."
- Bold on-screen text, top-center, hook in the first frame. The hook is readable with sound off.
- Proven viral templates to reuse: **"[age] and this is how I [struggle]"**, **"facing my fear"**, **"hack nobody taught me"** (subverted), **"POV: you just found out…"**.
- The grimace/struggle face and real-time panic are the highest-trust assets in the whole category. Cast for it.
