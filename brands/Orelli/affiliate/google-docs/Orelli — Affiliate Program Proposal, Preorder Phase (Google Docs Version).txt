ORELLI — AFFILIATE PROGRAM PROPOSAL: PREORDER PHASE

Created 2026-07-25. Governs the August–October 2026 preorder window. Supplements — does not replace — the Orelli Marketing Strategy & Launch Plan, which remains the master for the December/January launch. Where this document and the master conflict on the preorder window, this document wins.

Objective: generate paid preorders and brand awareness through a TikTok-Shop-sourced creator army, at near-zero fixed cost, to produce demand proof for the raise.
Window: preorder live August 24, 2026 through October 4, 2026 (6 weeks), extendable to November 1.
Mechanic: creator content on TikTok → off-platform link and code → Shopify preorder at $29.99 and up → fulfills December 2026 / January 2027.
The constraint that shapes everything: no product is shipped to creators. They have never tasted it and never will before launch.


1. WHAT CHANGED FROM THE MASTER STRATEGY — AND WHY

Seeding. Master strategy: Statusphere seeding, 150–200 units gifted. Preorder phase: zero seeding, zero samples. Why: the product doesn't exist in shippable form yet, and Brooks locked zero product cost.

Money layer. Master strategy: Katalys. Preorder phase: Shopify plus a lightweight affiliate app. Why: Katalys is a launch-scale tool; the preorder window needs codes and links live in two weeks, not a platform integration.

Discovery. Master strategy: creators discovered via the Statusphere network. Preorder phase: TikTok Shop creator databases, ranked by GMV. Why: this sources creators who have provably converted TikTok viewers into buyers — a far higher bar than follower count.

Creative. Master strategy: confessional testimonial ("I tried it"). Preorder phase: announcement, never testimonial. Why: the FTC bona-fide-user rule, covered in Section 4. This is not optional.

Program cost. Master strategy: $97K over 6 months. Preorder phase: roughly $22K, of which $5K is unconditional. Why: no samples, no seeding platform, no equity creators in this window.

Goal. Master strategy: 5,000 customers and unit-economics proof. Preorder phase: demand signal, machine metrics, and a creator roster. Why: the preorder is a proof exercise, not the revenue engine.

What does not change: Maya is still the avatar. The four angles still hold. The emotional spine — Recognition, Relief, Revelation, Liberation — is still the arc. The one line is still "It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy."


2. THE HONEST READ ON WHAT THIS WINDOW CAN PRODUCE

The number belongs on the table before the plan, because it determines how this gets framed to investors.

The median post is unimpressive. Start there: 8,000 views (the nano/micro median) × 1.0% click-through to an off-platform link = 80 clicks × 1.5% preorder conversion rate = roughly 1.2 preorders per post.

A preorder page for an unproven OTC drug arriving in four months converts worse than the 3%-or-better the master strategy targets for a live landing page. Assume 1.5%, not 3%.

But medians are the wrong unit for TikTok. View distribution is violently right-skewed — a roster's mean views per post typically runs 2–3x its median, because a handful of posts break containment and the rest don't. Total preorders is driven by that tail, not by the typical post. So the scenarios below hold click-through and conversion constant at 1.0% and 1.5%, and vary only how the views land.

Floor case — nothing breaks out: 20 active creators · 60 posts · 8,000 mean views per post · 0.5M total views · roughly 75 preorders · roughly $3,100.
Base case — normal skew: 35 active creators · 130 posts · 20,000 mean views per post · 2.6M total views · roughly 390 preorders · roughly $16,000.
Hit case — one video clears 500K: 35 active creators · 130 posts · 60,000 mean views per post · 7.8M total views · roughly 1,170 preorders · roughly $48,000.

(Revenue figures assume an average order value of roughly $41.)

The strategic point: this window is hit-driven, not volume-driven. "Can't swallow pills" is a proven high-engagement topic with millions of cumulative views and a comment section that reads like group therapy. The plan is to buy as many lottery tickets as possible on a topic the algorithm already rewards — which is why roster size and post count are the metrics to manage weekly, not any individual creator's performance.

Plan against the base case (roughly 390). Be solvent at the floor (roughly 75).

2.1 Therefore: reframe what the raise slide actually is

Do not lead the deck with preorder revenue. $16K is not an impressive revenue number and it invites the wrong comparison. Lead with the four things this window does prove, none of which require a big dollar figure.

1. Cost per acquired customer of $7.50–8.20, measured on real cohorts, against a $15–25 paid-social benchmark. This is the whole thesis, and it will be measured rather than projected.
2. Preorder conversion rate on cold creator traffic. People paid $30 for a product that does not exist yet, four months early, with no reviews. That is the single strongest demand signal available to a pre-revenue CPG brand.
3. A recruited, briefed, contractually-locked roster of 35–60 creators with proven TikTok Shop GMV, ready to fire on day one of launch. That is a distribution asset with a dollar value.
4. A content library of 100-plus pieces of rights-cleared UGC and a hook bank ranked by real performance data.

Frame it as: we spent $22K and proved we can acquire a customer for $8 before the product existed.


3. THE OFFER — REBUILT BUNDLE-FORWARD, BECAUSE 20% DOESN'T SURVIVE A SINGLE UNIT

$29.99 with a 20% commission and a discount code leaves almost nothing. Shown plainly, for a single bottle with a 20% commission and a $5 code: price $29.99 · creator code −$5.00 · net revenue $24.99 · commission at 20% of net −$5.00 · processing at 3% −$0.75 · COGS at fulfillment −$9.50 · fulfillment and shipping −$7.00 · contribution $2.74, or 11%.

That does not work. The fix is the same one the master strategy already identified — fixed per-order costs hit once, so bundles amortize them — applied harder at the preorder.

3.1 Recommended preorder offer architecture

1 bottle: $29.99 ($29.99 per bottle). Role: entry, de-emphasized.
2 bottles: $49.00 ($24.50 per bottle). Role: HERO — default-selected on the page.
3 bottles: $65.00 ($21.67 per bottle). Role: upsell — one for home, one for her bag, one for her desk.

Creator code: flat $5 off, one per customer, no stacking with bundle promos. Flat beats a percentage here — it caps exposure and reads bigger on a single unit ("$5 off" beats "10% off" at this price).

Contribution at the hero SKU, the 2-bottle at $49: net after the $5 code $44.00 · commission at 20% −$8.80 · processing at 3% −$1.32 · COGS −$19.00 · fulfillment −$7.60 · contribution $7.28, or 17%.

17% is thin, and there is no point pretending otherwise. It is acceptable for this window only, because the window's purpose is proof and roster-building rather than profit, and the cohort is capped. It is not a steady state. At launch, commission reverts to 15% standard and 20% top tier, and the code goes away, which restores the master strategy's 31% blended contribution.

Action required: push bundle take rate above 55%. Every creator brief, the page default, and the code terms all point at the 2-pack.


4. THE COMPLIANCE SPINE — THE ONE THING THAT CAN ACTUALLY SINK THIS

Because no creator will have tasted the product, the program lives or dies on one rule.

16 CFR 255.1: "Endorsements must reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience of the endorser." And: "When the advertisement represents that the endorser uses the endorsed product, the endorser must have been a bona fide user of it at the time the endorsement was given."

The FTC's Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective October 21, 2024) makes testimonials from people without genuine experience directly penalizable — up to $51,744 per violation. Per violation. With 40 creators posting, that is not a theoretical number.

The design response: every piece of preorder creative is an ANNOUNCEMENT, not a TESTIMONIAL.

Truthful and allowed, paired against what creates liability:
•  "I've never been able to swallow pills" (her real experience) — never "I tried it and it's amazing."
•  "Someone finally made adult Tylenol as a gummy" — never "It tastes like candy."
•  "I preordered it" (if she actually did) — never "This works so well for my headaches."
•  "It comes out in January and I'm counting down" — never holding or eating a bottle she doesn't have.
•  "Real 500mg adult dose, you chew it" (a label fact) — never "better than Tylenol," since it is the same molecule.

This constraint is a gift, not a tax. It forces every video to spend its runtime on the Recognition beat — her real story, which is the actual conversion trigger per master strategy Section 2 — and hands the product a clean Revelation beat at the end. It also protects the taste gate: no creator can burn the taste story before it's locked, so first-bite reactions stay a fresh, unspent asset for launch.

Two more disclosure obligations, both non-negotiable.

Material connection. Every creator discloses #ad or #affiliate, in-video and in caption. Affiliate commission alone triggers this even with no free product.

Preorder terms, under the FTC Mail Order Rule (16 CFR 435). The expected ship date must be clearly disclosed at point of sale, in the confirmation email, and in every creator caption. If the date slips, we must notify and offer a full refund. Ship-date language: "Ships December 2026. Cancel anytime before shipping for a full refund."

The full creator-facing version is the Creator Compliance & Never-Say Sheet.


5. COMP — HOW YOU RECRUIT WITH NO FREE PRODUCT

We are asking a creator to accept four negatives at once: no sample, an unproven landing page, an off-platform link that TikTok suppresses, and a product that ships in four months. That has to be paid back.

The instinct is a higher commission rate. Resist it — a permanently elevated rate mortgages margin that is already thin. Pay instead in things that are scarce, capped, and mostly free.

5.1 The Founding Creator offer

•  Commission: 20% of order subtotal on all preorder sales. Exposure: variable.
•  Locked top-tier rate for life: stays at 20% post-launch, when the standard rate drops to 15%. Exposure: variable, capped by roster size.
•  Activation bounty: $50 on first qualified post. Exposure: $5,000, first 100 creators only.
•  Milestone ladder: 5 orders → $50 · 25 orders → $250 · 50 orders → $750 · 100 orders → $2,000. Exposure: $15,000 pool cap.
•  First product off the line: free bottles shipped to every Founding Creator at launch, before customers. Exposure: roughly $2,000.
•  Founding Creator status: named on the site, first access, private group, direct line to Brooks. Exposure: $0.

Total fixed exposure is roughly $22,000, of which only the $5,000 activation bounty is unconditional. Against the master strategy's $97K program budget, the preorder window is about 4.5x cheaper because there is no seeding, no platform fee, and no equity creators.

The "first product off the line" line does double duty: it costs roughly $2,000 and it buys the entire first-bite content wave at launch, from creators who are already briefed, already have an audience primed by their preorder post, and already have skin in the game. That is the highest-leverage $2,000 in the plan.

5.2 Commission payout schedule, and why it's split

Preorder cash is a liability until we ship. Paying full commission in September on an order that could be refunded in December is how you end up chasing clawbacks from 40 creators.

Recommended: 50% of commission paid net-30 from the order date, and 50% paid within 30 days of fulfillment. Disclosed in the creator agreement up front, plainly. Creators accept this readily when it is explained; what kills trust is discovering it later.

5.3 The barbell: don't only spray

Cold-DMing 800 nano creators for six weeks is a grind with a mediocre hit rate. Run it, but pair it with a concentrated bet.

Lane A — paid proven sellers, 30 creators. Hand-picked from Kalodata by actual TikTok Shop GMV, offered a flat $150 content fee plus full Founding Creator terms. Cost: $4,500. These are people who have demonstrably converted TikTok viewers into buyers. Thirty of them will very likely outperform 400 cold nano DMs, and they are the roster we want at launch anyway.

Lane B — volume cold outreach, 750–900 DMs. Commission-only Founding terms. This is where the breakout lottery ticket comes from, because you cannot predict which nano creator's confessional goes to 800K.

Lane A is added budget beyond the $22K above. Recommended total program: roughly $26,500.


6. ARCHITECTURE — WHERE EACH PIECE LIVES

TikTok Shop creator databases (Kalodata, FastMoss, EchoTik), filtered by GMV, category, and audience, surface proven sellers. Three more lanes feed the same funnel: in-app mining of pill-struggle content cross-filtered for orange-cart profiles; comment-mining of the "omg me too" commenters who are themselves creators; and adjacent-SKU poaching of whoever currently sells gummy vitamins on TikTok Shop.

A note on Katalys: hold it for launch. It is the right long-run money layer per the master strategy, but standing it up is a multi-week integration and the preorder window needs codes live in fourteen days. Use a Shopify affiliate app now and migrate at T-0.

9. RISKS, RANKED, WITH THE ACTUAL MITIGATION

Risk 1 — the taste gate has not passed, and creators are already telling their audiences. This is the top risk and it is structural: forty creators announce a product in September, and the formulation fails in October.

Mitigation: the announcement-not-testimonial rule in Section 4 means no creator has made a claim about how it tastes or works, so there is nothing to retract about the product experience. Preorder cash is held in a separate account and not spent before the gate passes. Hard deadline: the taste gate passes by October 15, or preorders are refunded in full with a personal note from Brooks. Cap the preorder at 2,000 units — a number we can refund without insolvency.

Risk 2 — payment processor reserve or account hold. Charging cards in September for December delivery is exactly the pattern that triggers Shopify Payments and Stripe risk review. They can impose a rolling reserve and hold the cash, which defeats the purpose of preorder cash funding the production run.

Mitigation: call the processor before Gate A, not after. Disclose the preorder model and the ship date proactively. Have a backup processor approved. Budget for the possibility that 100% of preorder cash is held until fulfillment — meaning the raise, not the preorder, funds the $148,200 production run.

Risk 3 — creators won't send traffic off-platform. TikTok suppresses off-platform links, and a TikTok Shop creator's bio link is their monetization real estate. This is the core friction of sourcing on TikTok Shop but selling off it.

Mitigation: a dual CTA — a pinned comment with a short branded link, plus "just search Orelli." Branded search is algorithm-neutral, costs the creator nothing, and filters for high intent. Do not require a bio-link change; make it optional and pay a small bonus for it. Attribution leans on codes over links, which also captures the delayed conversion that confessional content produces.

Risk 4 — preorder conversion comes in under 1%. Then a $30 purchase four months early is too big an ask cold.

Mitigation: Gate C on September 6 exists precisely for this. If conversion is under 1%, do not add creators — fix the page first. The fallback lever is to add a free email-capture waitlist path alongside the paid preorder, so the traffic isn't wasted, and convert the list at launch.

Risk 5 — a creator makes a non-compliant claim. One creator says "I tried it, it's delicious," and we have a 255.1 problem plus fake-testimonial exposure.

Mitigation: the compliance sheet is a signed part of the agreement, not an attachment. Every first post is reviewed before it is promoted or repurposed. There is a takedown clause in the agreement. A non-compliant post means commission withheld and the creator removed. Budget 20 minutes per creator for first-post review — the cheapest insurance in the plan.

Risk 6 — regulatory: TikTok Shop OTC eligibility at launch. TikTok Shop restricts OTC medication listings to sellers who meet eligibility requirements, and health claims must match the Drug Facts "Uses" label exactly.

Mitigation: start the TikTok Shop seller application and the OTC category eligibility conversation now, in Phase 0, even though we won't list until December. Lead time is unknown and it is the gating dependency for the entire T-0 migration. If eligibility is denied, the launch affiliate program stays off-platform permanently — survivable, but it changes the launch plan, so we want that answer in August rather than November.
