ORELLI — CREATOR ASSET PACK SPEC

Internal build spec. Produced in Phase 0, July 28 through August 9. Gate A does not pass without it. The pack is what makes a no-sample program possible at all.


0. WHY THIS PACK IS LOAD-BEARING

No creator will ever hold the product before she posts. Every product visual in every video comes from this pack. If the pack is thin or ugly, forty creators will each improvise their own bad version and the brand will look different in every single video.

Design target: a creator with basic CapCut skills makes something that looks intentional in under ten minutes.

Two rules govern everything below.

1. Everything must be dead simple to drop into CapCut. Transparent PNGs, vertical video, no layered files, no fonts to install.
2. Nothing may imply the creator possesses the product. No hands holding a bottle, no kitchen counters, no "just arrived" staging. Product visuals read as a picture of a thing that exists, not a thing in her house. This is the 255.1 line, and the asset pack is where it's easiest to accidentally cross it.

Existing source material in the vault: the cropped bottle image from the pill-swallow UGC set, the website screenshot, the children's Tylenol and Extra Strength Tylenol reference images, and the PolySans brand family. Most of the pack is a production pass on these, not a from-scratch build.


1. PRODUCT STILLS

1.1 Bottle, front, transparent PNG. 2000px tall, alpha channel, no shadow. Purpose: the default product cut-in, used in roughly 80% of videos.
1.2 Bottle, three-quarter angle, transparent PNG. 2000px tall, alpha. Purpose: variety, so 40 videos don't share one frame.
1.3 Bottle with soft drop shadow, PNG. 2000px tall, alpha. Purpose: for creators who cut it onto a plain background.
1.4 Gummy hero, single gummy, transparent PNG. 1500px, alpha. Purpose: the "you just chew it" beat.
1.5 Gummies, small scatter, transparent PNG. 2000px wide, alpha. Purpose: texture and appetite shot.
1.6 Bottle on brand background, 9:16. 1080 by 1920. Purpose: drop-in full-screen product card, zero editing.
1.7 Bottle plus gummies, 9:16. 1080 by 1920. Purpose: same, alternate.

Note: if final packaging isn't photographable yet, these are renders. That is fine and normal for a preorder, but the render must match what actually ships. Every asset carries the label and dose exactly as it will appear, because these images are effectively advertising claims about the product.


2. THE COMPARISON SHOT — HIGHEST-VALUE ASSET IN THE PACK

2.1 Children's Tylenol bottle beside the Orelli bottle, 9:16. 1080 by 1920, clean neutral background. Purpose: the Adult Shame angle's single strongest visual.
2.2 The same pair as transparent PNGs, alpha. Purpose: for creators who want it over their own footage.
2.3 Extra Strength Tylenol beside Orelli, 9:16. 1080 by 1920. Purpose: the "same 500mg, different format" beat.

LEGAL CAUTION on 2.1 and 2.3: competitor packaging appears in these. It is permitted as a factual comparison of active ingredient and dose — standard comparative advertising, and the entire point of the brand. It becomes a problem if paired with a disparaging claim. Pair only with factual overlays such as "Same active ingredient. 500mg." Never "Tylenol can't do this" or anything mocking. Route these two through legal review alongside the preorder terms.


3. SCREEN RECORDINGS

3.1 Site scroll, vertical. 1080 by 1920, 6 to 8 seconds, MP4, no audio. Purpose: the "look, it's real" beat, which outperforms a static image for legitimacy.
3.2 Preorder page, showing price with the ship date visible. 1080 by 1920, 5 seconds. Purpose: doubles as the ship-date disclosure.
3.3 Add-to-cart tap-through. 1080 by 1920, 6 seconds. Purpose: for "I preordered it" — shows the action without faking possession.
3.4 Clean preorder page screenshot. 1080 by 1920 PNG. Purpose: static alternative.

Asset 3.2 is the compliance workhorse. A creator who drops it in has satisfied the ship-date disclosure visually, whether or not she says it out loud.


4. TYPOGRAPHY AND TEXT OVERLAYS

Delivered as transparent PNGs, pre-typed in PolySans, brand cobalt on white and white on cobalt. Creators drag them in — no font installs, no brand drift.

Hook overlays, one PNG each:
•  "it's literally just tylenol. in a gummy."
•  "500mg. same as extra strength."
•  "you just chew it."
•  "no water. no gagging. no pill."
•  "you were never the problem. the pill was."
•  "adult dose. gummy format."
•  "[age] years old and this is how i swallow pills" — supplied as an editable-number variant, two PNGs plus a numeral set.

Required-disclosure overlays, supplied so nobody has an excuse:
•  "#ad"
•  "Ships December 2026"
•  "Preorder · ships December 2026"

Also ship a CapCut-ready text style card — hex codes, font weights, positioning — for creators who prefer to type their own.


5. BRAND ELEMENTS

5.1 Logo, white, transparent PNG.
5.2 Logo, cobalt, transparent PNG.
5.3 Logo lockup with "It's literally just Tylenol. In a gummy."
5.4 Brand color chips with hex codes.
5.5 End card, 9:16 — logo, tagline, "Ships December 2026," and "link in comments."

Asset 5.5 matters more than it looks. A consistent two-second end card across 130 videos is what makes a scattered creator roster read as one brand campaign.


6. THE BROOKS LOOM — DO NOT SKIP THIS

6.1 Founder video message. 45 to 75 seconds, unlisted Loom or MP4, shot on a phone.

Brooks to camera, no script, covering: what the product is, in plain language · why it exists — the 104 million adults, forty years of coating tweaks and nobody fixing it, plus his own story only if it's real (see the Brand Bible, Section 2 note) · why she specifically was picked · "You can't say you tried it — nobody has. Just tell your story." · "DM me directly, any time, I actually answer."

This single asset does more for activation than every PDF combined. It converts "a brand emailed me a deck" into "a person asked me to help build something." Keep it deliberately rough — phone camera, real room. A polished corporate video destroys the effect entirely.


7. WRITTEN ASSETS BUNDLED IN

7.1 Creator Brand Bible.
7.2 Compliance & Never-Say Sheet.
7.3 Three full scripts, from Brand Bible Section 8.
7.4 Hook bank sorted by angle, from Brand Bible Section 5.
7.5 Her personal one-pager, auto-generated: code, link, assigned angle, three hooks for that angle, post-by date, and payout summary.

Asset 7.5 is per-creator and must be generated at signing, not later. A creator who receives one page with her name, her code, and her three hooks posts materially more often than one who receives a folder and is told to find her own.


8. DELIVERY

One Google Drive folder, one shareable link, structured like this:

Orelli Founding Creator Kit
   START HERE — [Creator Name].pdf  (her one-pager, asset 7.5)
   Message from Brooks.mp4  (the Loom, asset 6.1)
   Brand Bible.pdf
   Compliance Sheet.pdf  (sign and return)
   Scripts — pick one.pdf
   01 PRODUCT IMAGES  (transparent PNGs, ready to drop in)
   02 SCREEN RECORDINGS  (MP4, vertical)
   03 TEXT OVERLAYS  (transparent PNGs)
   04 COMPARISON SHOTS
   05 LOGO & END CARD

Rules:
•  The folder link goes out within one hour of signing. Not the next day.
•  No ZIPs. Half of creators work from a phone and can't unzip.
•  File names describe contents in plain English — bottle-front-transparent.png, not ORE_PKG_v3_FINAL.png.
•  Never require a login or a form to access.
