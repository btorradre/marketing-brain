# Orelli — Affiliate Program Proposal: Preorder Phase

*Created 2026-07-25. Governs the Aug–Oct 2026 preorder window. Supplements — does not replace — `Strategy/Orelli — Marketing Strategy & Launch Plan.md`, which remains the master for the Dec/Jan launch. Where this doc and the master conflict on the preorder window, this doc wins.*

**Objective:** Generate paid preorders and brand awareness through a TikTok-Shop-sourced creator army, at near-zero fixed cost, to produce demand proof for the raise.
**Window:** Preorder live **Aug 24, 2026** → **Oct 4, 2026** (6 weeks), extendable to Nov 1.
**Mechanic:** Creator content on TikTok → off-platform link/code → Shopify preorder at $29.99+ → fulfills Dec 2026/Jan 2027.
**Constraint that shapes everything:** no product is shipped to creators. They have never tasted it and never will before launch.

## 2. The honest read on what this window can produce

I want the number on the table before the plan, because it determines how you frame this to investors.

**The median post is unimpressive. Start there:**

```
8,000 views (nano/micro median)
  × 1.0% click-through to an off-platform link   →  80 clicks
  × 1.5% preorder CVR (no reviews, no taste proof, 4-month wait)
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ≈ 1.2 preorders per post
```

A preorder page for an unproven OTC drug arriving in four months converts *worse* than the ≥3% the master strategy targets for a live LP. Assume **1.5%, not 3%.**

**But medians are the wrong unit for TikTok.** View distribution is violently right-skewed — a roster's *mean* views per post typically runs 2–3× its median, because a handful of posts break containment and the rest don't. Total preorders is driven by that tail, not by the typical post. So the scenarios below hold CTR and CVR constant at 1.0% / 1.5% and vary only **how the views land**:

| Case | Active creators | Posts | Mean views/post | Total views | Preorders | Revenue (@ ~$41 AOV) |
|:--|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| **Floor** — nothing breaks out | 20 | 60 | 8,000 | 0.5M | **~75** | ~$3,100 |
| **Base** — normal skew | 35 | 130 | 20,000 | 2.6M | **~390** | ~$16,000 |
| **Hit** — one video clears 500K | 35 | 130 | 60,000 | 7.8M | **~1,170** | ~$48,000 |

**The strategic point: this window is hit-driven, not volume-driven.** "Can't swallow pills" is a proven high-engagement topic with millions of cumulative views and a comment section that reads like group therapy. The plan is to buy as many lottery tickets as possible on a topic the algorithm already rewards — which is why roster size and post *count* are the metrics to manage weekly, not any individual creator's performance.

**Plan against Base (~390). Be solvent at Floor (~75).**

### 2.1 Therefore: reframe what the raise slide actually is

**Do not lead the deck with preorder revenue.** $16K is not an impressive revenue number and it invites the wrong comparison. Lead with the four things this window *does* prove, none of which require a big dollar figure:

1. **Cost per acquired customer = $7.50–8.20**, measured on real cohorts, against a $15–25 paid-social benchmark. This is the whole thesis, and it will be *measured*, not projected.
2. **Preorder conversion rate on cold creator traffic** — people paid $30 for a product that does not exist yet, four months early, with no reviews. That is the single strongest demand signal available to a pre-revenue CPG brand.
3. **A recruited, briefed, contractually-locked roster of 35–60 creators** with proven TikTok Shop GMV, ready to fire on day one of launch. That is a distribution asset with a dollar value.
4. **A content library** of 100+ pieces of rights-cleared UGC and a hook bank ranked by real performance data.

Frame it as: *"We spent $22K and proved we can acquire a customer for $8 before the product existed."*

## 3. The offer — rebuilt bundle-forward, because 20% doesn't survive a single unit

$29.99 with a 20% commission and a discount code leaves almost nothing. Shown plainly:

**Single bottle, 20% commission, $5 code:**

| Line | |
|:--|--:|
| Price | $29.99 |
| Creator code | −$5.00 |
| **Net revenue** | **$24.99** |
| Commission (20% of net) | −$5.00 |
| Processing (3%) | −$0.75 |
| COGS (at fulfillment) | −$9.50 |
| Fulfillment + shipping | −$7.00 |
| **Contribution** | **$2.74 (11%)** |

That does not work. The fix is the same one the master strategy already identified — **fixed per-order costs hit once, so bundles amortize them** — applied harder at the preorder.

### 3.1 Recommended preorder offer architecture

| SKU | Price | Per bottle | Role |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| 1 bottle | $29.99 | $29.99 | Entry, de-emphasized |
| **2 bottles** | **$49.00** | **$24.50** | **HERO — default-selected on the page** |
| 3 bottles | $65.00 | $21.67 | Upsell, "one for home, bag, desk" |

**Creator code: flat $5 off, one per customer, no stacking with bundle promos.** Flat beats a percentage here — it caps exposure and reads bigger on a single unit ("$5 off" > "10% off" at this price).

**Contribution at the hero SKU:**

| Line | 2-bottle @ $49 |
|:--|--:|
| Net after $5 code | $44.00 |
| Commission (20%) | −$8.80 |
| Processing (3%) | −$1.32 |
| COGS | −$19.00 |
| Fulfillment | −$7.60 |
| **Contribution** | **$7.28 (17%)** |

**17% is thin, and I am not going to pretend otherwise.** It is acceptable *for this window only*, because the window's purpose is proof and roster-building, not profit, and the cohort is capped. It is not a steady state. At launch, commission reverts to 15% standard / 20% top tier and the code goes away, which restores the master strategy's 31% blended contribution.

**Action required:** push bundle take rate above 55%. Every creator brief, the page default, and the code terms all point at the 2-pack.

## 4. The compliance spine — the one thing that can actually sink this

Because no creator will have tasted the product, the program lives or dies on one rule.

> **16 CFR §255.1:** *"Endorsements must reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience of the endorser."* And: *"When the advertisement represents that the endorser uses the endorsed product, the endorser must have been a bona fide user of it at the time the endorsement was given."*

The FTC's Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465, effective Oct 21 2024) makes testimonials from people without genuine experience directly penalizable — **up to $51,744 per violation.** Per violation. With 40 creators posting, that is not a theoretical number.

**The design response: every piece of preorder creative is an ANNOUNCEMENT, not a TESTIMONIAL.**

| ✅ Truthful and allowed | ❌ Creates liability |
|:--|:--|
| "I've never been able to swallow pills." *(her real experience)* | "I tried it and it's amazing." |
| "Someone finally made adult Tylenol as a gummy." | "It tastes like candy." |
| "I preordered it." *(if she actually did)* | "This works so well for my headaches." |
| "It comes out in January and I'm counting down." | Holding/eating a bottle she doesn't have |
| "Real 500mg adult dose, you chew it." *(label fact)* | "Better than Tylenol" *(same molecule)* |

**This constraint is a gift, not a tax.** It forces every video to spend its runtime on the Recognition beat — her real story, which is the actual conversion trigger per the master strategy §2 — and hands the product a clean Revelation beat at the end. It also *protects the taste gate*: no creator can burn the taste story before you've locked it, so first-bite reactions stay a fresh, unspent asset for launch.

Two more disclosure obligations, both non-negotiable:

- **Material connection.** Every creator discloses `#ad` or `#affiliate`, in-video and in caption. Affiliate commission alone triggers this even with no free product.
- **Preorder terms (FTC Mail Order Rule, 16 CFR 435).** The expected ship date must be clearly disclosed at point of sale, in the confirmation email, and in every creator caption. If the date slips you must notify and offer a full refund. Ship-date language: **"Ships December 2026. Cancel anytime before shipping for a full refund."**

## 5. Comp — how you recruit with no free product

You are asking a creator to accept four negatives at once: no sample, an unproven landing page, an off-platform link that TikTok suppresses, and a product that ships in four months. You have to pay that back.

The instinct is a higher commission rate. Resist it — a permanently elevated rate mortgages margin you already saw is thin. Pay instead in things that are **scarce, capped, and mostly free.**

### 5.1 The Founding Creator offer

| Component | Terms | Exposure |
|:--|:--|--:|
| **Commission** | 20% of order subtotal, all preorder sales | Variable |
| **Locked top-tier rate for life** | Stays at 20% post-launch, when standard drops to 15% | Variable, capped by roster size |
| **Activation bounty** | $50 on first qualified post | **$5,000** (first 100 only) |
| **Milestone ladder** | 5 orders → $50 · 25 → $250 · 50 → $750 · 100 → $2,000 | **$15,000** pool cap |
| **First product off the line** | Free bottles shipped to every Founding Creator at launch, before customers | ~$2,000 |
| **Founding Creator status** | Named on the site, first access, private group, direct line to Brooks | $0 |

**Total fixed exposure: ~$22,000, of which only the $5,000 activation bounty is unconditional.** Against the master strategy's $97K program budget, the preorder window is ~4.5× cheaper because there is no seeding, no platform fee, and no equity creators.

The "first product off the line" line does double duty: it costs ~$2,000 and it buys you the **entire first-bite content wave at launch**, from creators who are already briefed, already have an audience primed by their preorder post, and already have skin in the game. That is the highest-leverage $2,000 in the plan.

### 5.2 Commission payout schedule (and why it's split)

Preorder cash is a liability until you ship. Paying full commission in September on an order that could be refunded in December is how you end up chasing clawbacks from 40 creators.

**Recommended: 50% of commission paid net-30 from the order date. 50% paid within 30 days of fulfillment.** Disclosed in the creator agreement up front, plainly. Creators accept this readily when it is explained — what kills trust is discovering it later.

### 5.3 The barbell: don't only spray

Cold-DMing 800 nano creators for 6 weeks is a grind with a mediocre hit rate. Run it, but pair it with a concentrated bet:

- **Lane A — Paid proven sellers (30 creators):** hand-picked from Kalodata by *actual TikTok Shop GMV*, offered a **flat $150 content fee + full Founding Creator terms**. Cost: **$4,500.** These are people who have demonstrably converted TikTok viewers into buyers. Thirty of them will very likely outperform 400 cold nano DMs, and they are the roster you want at launch anyway.
- **Lane B — Volume cold outreach (750–900 DMs):** commission-only Founding terms. This is where the breakout lottery ticket comes from, because you cannot predict which nano creator's confessional goes to 800K.

Lane A is added budget beyond the $22K above. **Recommended total program: ~$26,500.**

## 6. Architecture — where each piece lives

```
TikTok Shop creator databases (Kalodata / FastMoss / EchoTik)
   │  filter by GMV, category, audience  →  proven sellers
   ├── In-app mining: pill-struggle content × orange-cart profiles
   ├── Comment-mining: "omg me too" commenters who are creators
   └── Adjacent-SKU poaching: who sells gummy vitamins on TT Shop
        │
        ▼
   Outreach (off-platform DM/email) → Founding Creator agreement
        │
        ▼
   Creator Education Kit  ──▶  Brand Bible · Compliance Sheet · Asset Pack · Hook Bank
        │
        ▼
   Content posted on TikTok (announcement-framed, never testimonial)
        │  CTA: pinned-comment link  +  "just search Orelli"
        ▼
   Shopify preorder page  ──  2-pack default  ·  ship-date disclosed  ·  code + link attribution
        │
        ▼
   Demand proof: CVR, CAC, roster, content library  ──▶  the raise
        │
        ▼
   T-0 (Dec/Jan): migrate the entire roster into a live TikTok Shop listing
```

**The migration is the point.** Everything built here is designed to be portable. At launch, TikTok Shop's Affiliate Center "Manage Creators" tool lets you import an off-platform creator list and batch-invite up to 100 creators at a time. The roster you recruit in August becomes your day-one TikTok Shop affiliate army in December — already educated, already compliant, already holding your product.

## 9. Risks, ranked, with the actual mitigation

**1 — The taste gate has not passed, and creators are already telling their audiences.**
This is the top risk and it is structural. Forty creators announce a product in September; the formulation fails in October.
> **Mitigation:** the announcement-not-testimonial rule (§4) means no creator has made a claim about how it tastes or works — there is nothing to retract about the product experience. Preorder cash is held in a **separate account and not spent before the gate passes.** Hard deadline: **taste gate passes by Oct 15 or preorders are refunded in full with a personal note from Brooks.** Cap the preorder at **2,000 units** — a number you can refund without insolvency.

**2 — Payment processor reserve or account hold.**
Charging cards in September for December delivery is exactly the pattern that triggers Shopify Payments/Stripe risk review. They can impose a rolling reserve and hold your cash — which defeats the purpose of preorder cash funding the production run.
> **Mitigation:** **call the processor before Gate A, not after.** Disclose the preorder model and the ship date proactively. Have a backup processor approved. Budget for the possibility that 100% of preorder cash is held until fulfillment — meaning the raise, not the preorder, funds the $148.2K production run.

**3 — Creators won't send traffic off-platform.**
TikTok suppresses off-platform links, and a TikTok Shop creator's bio link is their monetization real estate. This is the core friction of "source on TikTok Shop, sell off TikTok Shop."
> **Mitigation:** dual CTA — **pinned comment with a short branded link + "just search Orelli."** Branded search is algorithm-neutral, costs the creator nothing, and filters for high intent. Do not require a bio-link change; make it optional and pay a small bonus for it. Attribution leans on **codes over links**, which also captures the delayed conversion confessional content produces.

**4 — Preorder CVR comes in under 1%.**
Then a $30 four-month-out purchase is too big an ask cold.
> **Mitigation:** Gate C on Sep 6 exists precisely for this. If CVR < 1%, do not add creators — fix the page first. Fallback lever: add a free email-capture waitlist path alongside the paid preorder so the traffic isn't wasted, and convert the list at launch.

**5 — Creator makes a non-compliant claim.**
One creator says "I tried it, it's delicious," and you have a §255.1 problem plus a fake-testimonial exposure.
> **Mitigation:** compliance sheet is a **signed** part of the agreement, not an attachment. Every first post is reviewed before it's promoted or repurposed. Takedown clause in the agreement. Non-compliant post = commission withheld and creator removed. Budget 20 min/creator for first-post review — it is the cheapest insurance in the plan.

**6 — Regulatory: TikTok Shop OTC eligibility at launch.**
TikTok Shop restricts OTC medication listings to sellers who meet eligibility requirements, and health claims must match the Drug Facts "Uses" label exactly.
> **Mitigation:** start the TikTok Shop seller application and OTC category eligibility conversation **now**, in Phase 0, even though you won't list until December. Lead time is unknown and it is the gating dependency for the entire T-0 migration. If eligibility is denied, the launch affiliate program stays off-platform permanently — which is survivable but changes the launch plan, so you want that answer in August, not November.
