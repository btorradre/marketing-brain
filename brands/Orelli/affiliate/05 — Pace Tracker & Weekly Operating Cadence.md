## 3. The Friday meeting — 30 minutes, same five questions

Every Friday, 30 minutes, Brooks + whoever runs sourcing. Do not let it become a status update.

1. **Are we on pace?** Fill the column. Circle every red cell.
2. **What's the bottleneck this week?** Exactly one. Run it through §4.
3. **Which angle is winning?** Rank the four by preorders-per-post. Reallocate next week's briefs toward the winner.
4. **Who are our top 3 and bottom 10 creators?** Send the top 3 a personal message from Brooks. Nudge or retire the bottom 10.
5. **What decision is due?** Check §5 gates.

**Update after the meeting:** the pace table, the tracker's `status` column, and the running raise data pack.

## 4. Diagnostic — if X is off pace, do Y

Work top-down. The first row that's red is your actual problem; the ones below it are usually symptoms.

| If this is red | The real problem is probably | Do this, this week |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Creators sourced** | Nobody is doing the work, or the filters are too tight | Loosen the GMV floor to $500. Add Lane 4 categories. If it's a capacity problem, hire the VA today — this is not a founder task. |
| **DM reply rate <15%** | The opener is about the brand, not about her | Re-read the first line of 10 sent DMs. If any leads with "Orelli" instead of her own words, that's the bug. Quote her `struggle_evidence` verbatim. Check you're not sending links in touch 1. |
| **Reply → signed <30%** | The offer isn't clearing the four negatives | Lead with the two things creators actually value: 20%-locked-for-life and free product off the first run. Then push more volume into **Lane A paid** ($150 flat) — money resolves this objection instantly. |
| **Signed → posted <45%** | Onboarding logistics, not willingness | This is almost always fixable. Are codes issued within the hour? Did she get her personal one-pager, or a folder? **Offer to write her first three lines** — this single intervention recovers a large share of non-posters. Tighten the bounty deadline to 7 days. |
| **Posted → repeat <40%** | She doesn't know repeat posting is expected or paid | Message every single-post creator personally with her actual numbers ("your video did 14K, here's what #2 usually does"). Make the milestone ladder visible. Consider a 3-post bonus. |
| **CTR <0.5%** | The CTA is buried or absent | Is the link comment actually **pinned**? Is she saying "search Orelli" out loud? Audit 10 live posts — expect to find several with no pinned comment at all. |
| **CVR <1.0%** ⛔ | **The page, not the creators** | **Stop adding creators.** Fix the page: is the 2-pack default-selected? Is the ship date reassuring rather than alarming? Is the refund promise above the fold? Add the WTP survey's own respondents as social proof. Re-test for a week before scaling. |
| **Bundle take <40%** | Page defaults and creator language | Default-select the 2-pack. Reframe as "one for home, one for your bag." Add the per-bottle price. Brief creators to say "I got two." |
| **Preorders red but everything above is green** | Traffic is fine, the offer isn't | Genuinely re-examine whether $30 four months early is the ask. Fallback: add a free waitlist path beside the paid preorder so the traffic isn't wasted. |

## 5. The four gates — each is a real stop

### ⛔ GATE A — end of W1 (Aug 9): can we open at all?
- [ ] Preorder page live, tested, ship-date + refund language on it
- [ ] **Legal has reviewed preorder terms and the competitor-comparison assets**
- [ ] **Payment processor has been told about the preorder model and hasn't objected**
- [ ] Affiliate app live, codes and links generating
- [ ] Asset Pack complete and phone-tested
- [ ] All five creator docs finalized
- [ ] Tracker built with the full schema

**Fail → delay Phase 1 by a week.** Do not start outreach without the pack. A creator who signs and gets nothing for four days is a creator who never posts.

### ⛔ GATE B — end of W3 (Aug 23): 25 creators signed
- [ ] 25 signed agreements, compliance acknowledged
- [ ] At least 8 from Lane A (paid proven sellers)
- [ ] All 25 onboarded with code, link, one-pager, assigned angle

**Fail → push the preorder open date by one week.** Opening to 8 creators wastes the launch moment and produces a CVR read too noisy to act on.

### ⛔ GATE C — end of W5 (Sep 6): CVR ≥1%
**The most important gate.** You now have ~6,000 clicks — enough to trust.

- **CVR ≥1.5%** → green. Scale hard, spend the remaining outreach budget.
- **CVR 1.0–1.5%** → proceed, but fix the page in parallel. Every point of CVR is worth more than ten creators.
- **CVR <1.0%** → **stop recruiting.** One week of page work. Re-read at W6. Adding creators to a page that doesn't convert burns roster goodwill you need in December.

### ⛔ GATE D — W9 (Oct 4): extend or close?
- **≥300 preorders** → close on schedule, bank the scarcity moment, roll into launch prep.
- **150–300** → extend to Nov 1 with the same roster. Marginal cost is near zero; creators are already onboarded.
- **<150** → close, and re-frame the raise around CVR + CAC + roster rather than volume (see proposal §2.1). Do not extend a program that isn't working — you'll burn the roster before December, and that roster is worth more than the preorders.

## 6. The parallel gate that isn't ours — but blocks everything

**Taste gate: must pass by Oct 15.** Owned outside this program (master strategy §11), but this program is fully exposed to it.

| Check | When | If it fails |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Formulation round 1 acceptable | By W4 | Second round; no impact on preorder yet |
| Blind panel ≥80% "would buy again" | By W7 | ⚠️ Warn creators the timeline may move |
| **Final go/no-go** | **Oct 15** | **Refund every preorder in full, personal note from Brooks, keep the roster intact for a later launch** |

**Standing rule: preorder cash sits in a separate account and is not spent before Oct 15.** Cap preorders at **2,000 units** — the number you can refund without insolvency.

If the gate fails, the roster is still the asset. Handle the refund personally and well and most creators will stay; handle it badly and you lose the distribution asset that was the real point of the whole exercise.

## 9. Standing rules — the things that quietly decide this

1. **Reply to inbound creator DMs within 4 hours.** Reply speed predicts close rate at this tier more than anything in the offer.
2. **Code + link + one-pager within one hour of signing.** Never "I'll send it tomorrow."
3. **Review every creator's first post before promoting it.** ~20 minutes each. Cheapest insurance in the program.
4. **Message the top 3 creators personally, every week.** They will produce a disproportionate share of the revenue and they are the ones you most need in December.
5. **One tracker, one owner.** A creator DM'd by two lanes is a creator lost.
6. **Never spend preorder cash before Oct 15.**
7. **Post count is the metric to maximize.** This window is hit-driven — every additional post is another lottery ticket on a topic the algorithm already rewards.
