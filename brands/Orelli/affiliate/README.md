## The three constraints that shape everything

1. **No samples ship.** So no creator can claim to have tried it — `16 CFR §255.1` requires an endorser be a bona fide user. Every video is an **announcement, not a testimonial.** Doc `03` is the enforcement.
2. **TikTok Shop can't process a preorder** (fulfillment SLA + OTC category eligibility). So TikTok Shop is the **sourcing pond**, not the checkout. Money runs through Shopify. Roster migrates to TikTok Shop at T-0.
3. **$29.99 × 20% commission is thin** (~17% contribution bundle-forward, ~11% on a single unit). Preorder runs bundle-forward with a 2-pack hero, for a capped window only.
