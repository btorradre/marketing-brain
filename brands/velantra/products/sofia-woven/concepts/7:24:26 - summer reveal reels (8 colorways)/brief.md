## Why the reference works
Silent luxury unboxing. The gift box does the status work: a bag presented as an object worth
gifting to yourself. Four slow drifting shots (box reveal, hardware macro, box wide, window
beauty) mimic the first five minutes of ownership. One quiet confidence line of text. No pitch,
pure desire.

curtain daylight. Macro beat celebrates the leather flap edge + white stitching + weave (our
"hardware" — the bag has none, which is the point). On-screen line:
"Structured, hand woven, and made for summer." (Didot, white, upper third)
