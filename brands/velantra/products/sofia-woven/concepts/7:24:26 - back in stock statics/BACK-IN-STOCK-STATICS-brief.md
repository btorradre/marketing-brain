# Velantra — "Back In Stock" BOF Static Pack

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Swipe:** Vestirsi "Back In Stock" static (Meta, US, running 99 days — Apr 17 → present, 162 ads in rotation)
**Funnel position:** Bottom of funnel. Retargeting — viewed product / added to cart / past visitors.
**Format:** static JPG in two placements — **9:16** (1080x1920, Stories/Reels) and **4:5** (1080x1350, feed).
**Deliverables:** 10 ads x 2 ratios = 20 files. 8 x The Sofia Woven Tote (every live colorway) + 2 x The Eleanor Weekender.

> The 4:5 is a **recompose, not a crop**. A centre-crop of the 9:16 zooms so far that the headline
> collides with the handles, so the 4:5 keeps the plate's full composition and widens the draped
> backdrop by fanning out its own edge columns, feathered into the plate. Product scale and type
> rhythm stay consistent between the two ratios.

---

## 1. Why the swipe works

The Vestirsi ad is a **restock-scarcity** play, not a discovery play. Every element earns its place:

| Element | Job |
|---|---|
| Dark chocolate draped-fabric backdrop | Kills the "dropshipper on white cyclorama" read. Draped textile = editorial, expensive, tactile. Also makes a straw/tan bag pop hard in a feed of bright white product shots. |
| Bag dead-center, front-on, nothing else in frame | Zero cognitive load. The buyer already knows the product — this is a reminder, not an education. |
| "Back In Stock" in a high-contrast Didone serif | The entire hook is 3 words. It says *you already wanted this and you couldn't have it.* It converts prior interest into urgency without a discount doing the work. |
| One-line subhead | Names the exact item so the ad matches the product page the buyer already saw. Congruence = cheap clicks. |
| Tiny brand mark, low in frame | Brand confidence signal. No badge clutter, no "50% OFF" starburst — the restraint IS the positioning. |
| "(again)" in the body copy | The highest-leverage two words in the ad. It's social proof and scarcity in one parenthetical: it sold out before, other people bought it, it will happen again. |

**The mechanic we're replicating:** prior-interest → scarcity → light offer. NOT discount-led. The discount is the tiebreaker, never the hook.

**Why one ad per colorway:** Meta already knows which colorway each retargeting audience viewed. A person who spent 90 seconds on Sunny Yellow should see Sunny Yellow back in stock, not a generic tote. This is the cheapest congruence win available at BOF.

## 2. Layout system (locked — measured 1:1 off the swipe)

| Element | 9:16 (1080x1920) | 4:5 (1080x1350) |
|---|---|---|
| Backdrop | Deep chocolate brown matte cotton, draped, soft vertical folds, edge vignette | same, widened |
| Headline | "Back In Stock" — Didot Regular, white, 69px (cap height 49px), +3px tracking, centered, cap-top y=299 | cap-top y=210 |
| Subhead | "The [Name] in [Colorway]" — Helvetica Neue Light, white, 27px, +1.6px tracking, centered, cap-top y=380 | cap-top y=267 |
| Product | Centered, front-on, eye level, middle band only | same |
| Brand mark | VELANTRA wordmark, white, 226 x 30px, centered, top y=1610 | top y=1132 |

Type **size** is identical in both ratios (same 1080 canvas width); only the vertical rhythm changes.

The swipe places its wordmark at y=1521. Ours sits at 1610 because GPT Image 2 does not land the bag
at an identical scale on every plate — Lightning Orange runs to y≈1575 and would have put white type
on straw. One uniform position clears all ten and keeps the set typographically identical, which
matters more for a campaign pack than matching the swipe to the pixel.

Headline and subhead are **identical across all 10** except the colorway name. Typography is composited in post, never model-generated — that is why the type is pixel-identical across the set and the spelling can never drift.

## 5. Testing notes

- **Ship all 10 as one ad set**, one ad per colorway, and let Meta allocate. Do not pre-judge colorways — Lady Pink and Sunny Yellow historically over-index versus what the merchandising instinct says.
- **Colorway-matched retargeting** is the upside case: if the catalog audience can be split by viewed-variant, pair each ad to its own colorway audience. That is where this format beats a generic DPA.
- **Do not add badges.** No "SALE", no starbursts, no price stamps. The restraint is the mechanism — the moment it looks like a discount ad it stops reading as a restock.
- **First iteration to try if CTR is soft:** swap the headline to `Back In Stock` → `Restocked` or `It's Back`, keeping everything else fixed. Type layer is a separate script, so a full re-render of all 10 is a one-command change.

## 7. Frame-QA record (mandatory pass — all 10 cleared)

Fresh-context auditor, plates magnified per bag region against the matching canonical reference.

**Construction clean on all 10 first time:** flap reads as one seamless sheet on every Sofia (no split,
no floating tabs, no gap, only the 2 handle slots), 2 belts, 2 rolled handles, zero metal hardware,
no front detailing duplicated on any side or back face. Colorways correct — natural sandy tan straw on
all 8 with only the leather carrying the colorway, **Caban Black correctly renders as tan straw + black
leather, not an all-black bag**. No stray text, watermark, hands, props, or second bag in any frame.

**One regeneration:** `eleanor-light-chocolate` failed on the first pass — only ONE handle rendered
(rear handle absent), plus invented embossed floral tooling across the cognac flap and a rightward
shift. This is the exact failure mode the `velantra-weekender` skill flags for GPT i2i. The prompt now
permanently pins both handles and bans decorative stamping; the regenerated plate re-audited **PASS**
(two separate parallel rolled tubes anchored into the leather band, plain grain leather, centred to
within 0.26% of frame width).

Noted and accepted, not defects: Sky Blue and Caban Black show an asymmetric belt arrangement rather
than the symmetric X. **Both match their own reference photographs exactly**, so they are faithful to
the real product — they just read slightly differently from the other six in a side-by-side.
