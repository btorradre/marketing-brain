## Shared DNA across all 4 (the Nuamore playbook)
1. Same product, 4 sophistication angles: social proof (celebrity/viral) → identity flex (street interview) → rational value (luxury b-roll) → urgency (clearance). This is a full-funnel angle ladder for ONE hero product.
2. Every ad ends in a concrete offer: 50% off + scarcity reason (waitlist / sellouts / last units).
3. Price anchoring shown on-screen ($278 → $129) rather than only spoken.
4. Luxury dupe positioning is always implicit ("Is that a Patek?", "looks like money") — never says "dupe."
5. 9:16, bold white captions, all under 50s, hook inside first 2 seconds.
