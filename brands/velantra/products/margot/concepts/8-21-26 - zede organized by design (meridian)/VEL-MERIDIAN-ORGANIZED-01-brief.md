# VEL-MERIDIAN-ORGANIZED, five finished ads

**Product:** The Meridian Leather Tote
**Reference:** Zede Paris, Pont-des-Arts Backpack, "Stop rummaging"
**Format:** 15.3 seconds, vertical 1080x1920, one voiceover over a music bed
**Scenes:** five creator-led sets, pixel-seeded from the reference ad's own frames
**Status:** SHIPPED. Five finished MP4s, animated through Omni, ElevenLabs voiceover.

Every ad mirrors the reference: the same four beats, the same rolling three-line caption
stack, the same black SHOP NOW card, the same wordmark close.

---

## 1. The five cuts

Each cut has its own creator, room, colorway, props, script and voice. The four beats, the
timings and the caption treatment are held constant across all five, so the structure is the
constant and everything a viewer would read as repetition is gone.

| Cut | Set | Colorway | Room and moment | Creator | Voice | Voiceover |
|-----|-----|----------|-----------------|---------|-------|-----------|
| ORG-01 | C | Midnight Black | Walnut desk, laptop behind | Dark shoulder-length hair, black knit, grey trousers | Claire | Stop rummaging. This bag is organized by design. |
| ORG-02 | B | Brown | Marble kitchen counter, coffee mug | Dark hair tied back, rust knit, indigo jeans | Cassidy | Stop digging. This bag zips down the middle. |
| ORG-03 | D | Coffee Brown | Oak hall bench by the front door, keys and wallet | Light brown hair loose, cream cardigan, dark denim | Brenda | Your keys are buried in there. In this bag they have their own side. |
| ORG-04 | A | Taupe | Walnut table and windowsill | Blonde, dark green tee and skirt | Bella | Everything in your bag is touching everything else. This one is split down the middle. |
| ORG-05 | E | Cream | Linen bedspread by a window | Blonde low bun, pale blue shirt, off-white trousers | Clara | Your laptop is sitting right on your keys. In this bag they sit on separate sides. |

ORG-01 carries the reference line word for word.

**The pairing is deliberate.** ORG-01 never names the zip, so it took black, the colorway whose
interior reads darkest. ORG-02 names the zip outright and took brown, which shows it most
clearly. ORG-03 says keys, and its scene has actual keys and a card wallet sitting on the table
while she packs.

**Caption cards**, revealed a phrase at a time in a rolling three-line stack in the lower third,
the phrase being spoken bright and its neighbours dimmed:

- ORG-01: STOP RUMMAGING / THIS / BAG IS / ORGANIZED BY / DESIGN
- ORG-02: STOP DIGGING / THIS BAG / ZIPS DOWN / THE MIDDLE
- ORG-03: YOUR KEYS / ARE BURIED / IN THERE / IN THIS BAG / THEY HAVE / THEIR OWN SIDE
- ORG-04: EVERYTHING IN YOUR BAG / IS TOUCHING / EVERYTHING ELSE / THIS ONE IS SPLIT / DOWN THE MIDDLE
- ORG-05: YOUR LAPTOP / IS SITTING RIGHT / ON YOUR KEYS / IN THIS BAG / THEY SIT ON / SEPARATE SIDES

Every card is cut to the word timings of its own read, so the type lands with the voice rather
than on a fixed grid. The cards sit in the lower third so they never cover the hand or the zip
during the demo.

## 3. How it was made

The reference is not a studio product ad. It is one creator in a real home, her hand packing the
open bag on a sunlit windowsill, then the same woman walking a corridor with it. So every scene
here is pixel-seeded from the reference ad's own frames: the reference frame goes in as the
scene, that colorway's own real product photograph goes in as the product, and the prompt keeps
the room, the daylight, the camera and the phone-camera grain while changing only the bag, the
surface and the wardrobe. Realism is inherited from real footage rather than invented from a
description.

Twenty keyframes at six credits each, a hundred and twenty in total. Every frame was checked
against the real product for the rolled top handles, the centre turn-lock plate, both side belt
buckles, the silver feet, single-colour leather and no logos anywhere.

**Animation.** The two beats that carry motion, the hand packing and the walk, are Google Omni
image-to-video from the approved keyframe, ten seconds each, trimmed to the beat. The two
bookend beats stay Ken Burns from the still, because hero-product beats never go to a video
engine (measured product mutation) and the reference shoots both of those as locked static
shots anyway. All ten Omni clips were checked frame by frame for construction drift: rolled
handles, centre turn-lock, both side buckles, silver feet, single-colour leather, no logos.
None mutated, so no re-rolls were spent. Omni's native audio is discarded; our own voiceover
and music bed are laid over the cut.

Voiceover is ElevenLabs v3 on the Creative setting, one voice per cut, each read generated inside
a longer passage and sliced out on its own word timings so the delivery is not clipped. All five
reads were run back through speech to text and every one matches its script word for word. Type,
end cards and the wordmark are burned in post, so no engine ever had to spell anything.

## 4. What the copy does and does not claim

The mechanism is the zip pocket that runs down the middle and splits the interior into two
halves. That is verified against the interior photography on the live page, not assumed. No
measurements appear anywhere, because the product page publishes none. No competitor is named,
no price is quoted and no proof is cited. The pile of a messy bag is only ever described in the
voice, never shown as another bag on screen.

ORG-01 is the reference line held verbatim on instruction. It reads for any bag on the market,
which is the known trade for matching the reference exactly. ORG-02 through ORG-05 each anchor to
the zip, so the five-cut test also reads the swap test itself.
