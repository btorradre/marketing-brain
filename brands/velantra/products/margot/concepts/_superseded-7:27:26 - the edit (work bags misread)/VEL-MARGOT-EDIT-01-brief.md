## 3. The script (LOCKED after fact-check — two price corrections from Brooks's draft, nothing else changed)

*Commas and periods only. All numbers as words.*

> These are the best work bags on the internet right now, at every single price point. I ranked all five on what you actually get for your money.
>
> Now if money isn't an object, we start at number five. The Row's Marlo tote, four thousand nine hundred dollars. It is stunning, it had me in an absolute chokehold. Matte grained calfskin, quiet, perfect. You are also paying four thousand nine hundred dollars for a plain leather tote.
>
> Number four. Polene's Cyme, six hundred and thirty. Sculpted Italian calf leather, instantly recognizable. It is a sculpture first though. One open space, and the folds are what you are paying for.
>
> Number three, and this one I actually love. Cuyana's System tote, three hundred and fifty eight. Italian leather, built for work. The system is the catch though. The laptop sleeve is sold separately, and it is a hundred and eighteen dollars on its own.
>
> Number two, if you want the one everyone trusts. Longchamp's Le Pliage, a hundred and eighty. It weighs nothing and it folds flat. It is also nylon, and it does not hold a shape.
>
> So every single one of these is a beautiful bag. The only question is how much of what you are paying for is actually the bag.
>
> Which brings me to number one, and it almost feels illegal. Structured leather with a fine embossed grain, a belt strap and a silver turn lock. It stands up on its own, it fits your laptop and your planner, and it goes with everything you wear to work. Would you believe me if I told you this one is under a hundred dollars? This is the Margot.
>
> Comment MARGOT and I will send it straight to your DMs.

**The rubric trick, work-bag edition:** every competitor gets genuine praise plus one honest limitation, and every limitation is a *what-you-get-for-the-money-at-work* limitation — pure price (Marlo), sculpture over function (Cyme), the add-on tax (Cuyana), no structure (Le Pliage). She never calls a bag bad. The scorecard is authored so only the Margot passes all four: structured, functional, complete out of the box, under a hundred dollars.
