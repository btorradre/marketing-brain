## 2. Product truth — the Margot (new product, read before generating any b-roll)

Structured leather work bag, $99.99, six neutrals — **Burgundy is hero.** Looks far more expensive than it costs; that gap is the whole ad.

- **Open top, NO flap, no lid.** Single most common AI failure — regenerate any frame with an invented flap.
- Two slim flat top handles (carried by these only — the detachable shoulder strap never appears).
- A slim belt strap runs across the front through two polished-silver **keepers**, its two thin silver-tipped **clasp plates** meeting in a shallow V.
- One small SQUARE polished-silver **turn-lock** sits just above the V. Exactly one — AI sometimes grows extras.
- Leather is a fine **embossed swirling grain** — NOT the smooth round "pebble" grain most totes have. AI drifts to generic pebble constantly; check every keyframe against the zoomed product photo.
- Interior: soft TAN lining, center zip pocket.

**Regenerate on sight:** any flap, pebble-grain leather, extra/mutated hardware, merged straps, stretched handles.
