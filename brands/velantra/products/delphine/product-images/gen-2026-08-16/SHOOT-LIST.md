## The shoot — about 15 minutes

Phone is fine. 4K, portrait or square, HDR off if you can.

**Setup:** a large sheet of white or bone paper or foamboard, curving up the wall behind so there is no
horizon line. Bag near a big window, window to one side. One white board or sheet on the shadow side to
bounce light back. No overhead lights on — mixed colour temperature is what makes phone shots hard to match.

**Shots — each one still, camera at the height noted, bag filling ~80% of frame:**

| # | Shot | Camera | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Front elevation, flap closed | dead level with bag mid-height | straps fastened, handles standing up |
| 2 | Three-quarter, flap closed | level, bag rotated ~35° | must show one full side gusset for depth |
| 3 | Side profile | level | shows the 14 cm depth and the side buckle |
| 4 | Back panel | level | plain back, for completeness |
| 5 | Open, flap folded back | ~40° above | the money shot — show the tan interior and the slip pocket |
| 6 | Closure macro | ~30cm away | the oval plate, a staple and the turn post all in frame |
| 7 | Base | angled from below-front | shows the four gold feet, settles that question for good |
| 8 | In hand / on arm | natural | one is enough, for scale |

**Do all 8 for one colourway.** I can recolour to the other two from those, which is the one part of the
pipeline that has worked reliably all day.
