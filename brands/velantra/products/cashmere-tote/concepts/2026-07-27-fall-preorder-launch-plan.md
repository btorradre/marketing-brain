## 1. Canonical silhouette spec (write-once, paste into every image prompt)

- **Body (CORRECTED 7/27, Brooks):** wide east-west structured shopper in a **fine wool-cashmere-blend felt — smooth, soft, densely brushed, plush like a luxury cashmere coat, fine even oatmeal melange heather, short soft nap. NEVER coarse, matted, cracked, or veiny.** Rectangular face; from the side the profile tapers — narrow 18 cm base spreading wider toward the open top. Top edge dips gently at center. Bag is roughly twice as wide as tall (50w × 26h).
- **Handles:** two slim rolled top handles. The grip crown is wrapped in **soft supple smooth fine-grain leather (gentle sheen, never thick/stiff/heavily pebbled)**; the handle columns below are wrapped in the body felt and continue down the front face as stitched vertical straps to the base.
- **Signature front belt (CORRECTED 7/27, Brooks):** one **slim half-inch (~1.3 cm) wide** soft-leather strap wrapping the full front at mid-height, threaded through matching slim felt loops. Both strap ends hang free past the sides with rounded tips, each finished with a round gold grommet/eyelet scaled to the slim strap.
- **Side detail:** on each gusset the belt forms a **V shape converging at a round gold ring/disc** at center — the single most recognizable angle of this bag.
- **Top/interior:** fully open top, no zip. Interior is the same felt tone, soft-structured; gold hardware visible at the side-belt anchors. (LP lines it in calfskin — ours TBD by supplier; do not claim calfskin lining.)
- **Hardware:** aged gold/brass throughout. **No logos, no embossing** on the front band (LP embosses "Loro Piana" there — ours stays clean unless Brooks wants a blind Velantra emboss).
- **Dimensions:** 19.7 × 10.2 × 7.1 in / **50 × 26 × 18 cm** · handle drop **16 cm / 6.3 in**.
