# v2 — SITUATIONAL AWARENESS PASS, 2026-08-22

Brooks caught two frames that "a real person would not do." Auditing every beat found the same class of problem in three more. **Five fixes across all three timelines.** Files in `exports/` are now v2; the v1 renders are in `exports/_v1-superseded/`.

## What he caught

1. **Groceries in the bag** (kale, a baguette, a jar) under "I filled it all the way up" — while the next line lists "my laptop, a water bottle and a sweater." Wrong load for this avatar, and it contradicts the script two seconds later.
2. **The bag on the floor of a coffee shop** for the product intro. Nobody sets a $120 wool bag on a cafe floor.

## What the full audit found

The capacity trio was showing **aftermath instead of action**, and all three traced to one mistake: I used a blanket **2.0s in-point on every 10s Omni clip** to dodge late-frame drift. In these clips the action happens in the first ~1.5s, so 2.0s lands after it.

| Line | 2.0s in-point showed | Corrected in-point shows |
|---|---|---|
| "My laptop" | laptop already inside, hand placing a wallet | the laptop in its sleeve going IN (0.3s) |
| "a water bottle" | hands resting on the bag, no bottle in frame at all | a stainless bottle in hand, dropping in (0.5s) |
| "and a sweater all go in" | hand pressing a flat knit down | the knit being lowered in (0.3s) |

**The clips were right. The cuts were wrong.** This slipped through because I QA'd the library on a contact sheet sampled at t=3s, which is not the frame the edit actually uses.

|---|---|---|---|

## Two standing tests, from here on

- **Load test.** Would THIS avatar put THAT in the bag on THIS occasion, and does it match what the VO says is inside?
- **Placement test.** Would a real owner set the bag down THERE? Her own floor, a chair, a bench, a counter, a car seat = yes. A public floor = never.

Plus: **pick the in-point on the frame where the action happens.** Never reuse one in-point across a shot list, and verify on the composed timeline frame, not a source contact sheet.

Saved as `feedback_broll_situational_plausibility` and `feedback_broll_inpoint_shows_action_not_aftermath`.
