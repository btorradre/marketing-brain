## PRODUCTION LAWS IN FORCE

- **Engines:** creator-on-camera segments = Seedance 2.5 lipsync. ALL b-roll and the full VO variants = Omni. No exceptions.
- **Segments:** fixed 6s segments, every segment seeded from the SAME approved creator keyframe (never chained). One-creator element lock: creator, wardrobe, room, light locked across all segments of a variant.
- **VO:** ElevenLabs v3, one continuous full-length take, cut to picture afterward. Never per-line renders, never atempo.
- **Text on screen:** Seedance cannot spell. Every caption/overlay ("Colette," "Velantra," price) is a post overlay via the house caption tool.
- **Product truth QA on every generated frame:** brushed wool body, leather-wrapped handles, belt runs across the FRONT/BACK faces only, each belt end hangs FREE with its own gold cap, side gusset is CLEAN wool (any strap/hardware on the side panel = artifact, regenerate). Aged gold hardware. No logos anywhere. Stands upright. Open top. No shoulder carry ever (6.3" handle drop) — hand or forearm carry only.
- **TOF opening:** frame 1 is the creator, not the product. Bag enters frame at the end of the hook line.
- **Colors:** Caramel is the hero unit on camera. Espresso appears only in the colors beat (library is undershot on espresso — generate the 2-up, don't pull a defect still).
- **Existing b-roll library:** `broll/library-2026-08/stills` is usable EXCEPT VEL-COL-009 (duplicate bag), VEL-COL-012 (leash merge), VEL-COL-090 (cool grey body), VEL-COL-018 (missing disc cap), and every shoulder-carry still.

## 50-SECOND CUTS (locked for these boards)

### Script 1 — "The perfect fall bag" (~150 words)

Okay, if you're looking for the perfect fall bag, I need to show you this.

Every fall it's the same thing. My summer bags look wrong the second the weather turns, and my leather bags just sit on top of everything I wear.

This is the Colette from Velantra. It's clearly inspired by the Loro Piana tote, but they sell it for about a hundred and twenty dollars. It's brushed wool, so it's cashmere soft, and it goes with the coat, the sweaters, denim, everything I own from September on. And it stands up on its own. Open top, my laptop slides in with room to spare, real leather handles.

I've carried it every day for two weeks and haven't switched bags once. People keep asking if it's designer. There's no logo anywhere on it. Caramel or espresso, and they're doing a pre order right now.

Go look at Velantra. The link is right there.

### Script 2 — "The bag that finally got it right" (~155 words)

Okay, if you've spent years trying to find the right bag, I need to show you something.

Every bag I've bought got one thing right and everything else wrong. The pretty ones collapsed the second I set them down, and the roomy ones had no shape at all.

This is the one that finally got it right. The Colette from Velantra. It's obviously inspired by the Loro Piana wool tote, except that one is thousands of dollars and this is about a hundred and twenty. Soft brushed wool, but with actual structure. It stands upright packed full or half empty. Open top, laptop fits with room left over, real leather handles, the belt across the front with the gold details.

I dumped my whole life in it and set it down. It just stood there holding its shape. People always ask where it's from. No logo anywhere. That's exactly why it reads expensive.

Go look at Velantra. Link is right there.

## AI-VO VARIANTS (3 per script = 6 ads, Omni b-roll only, no talking head)

Same 50s scripts read as VO. ElevenLabs v3, one continuous take per voice. Three voices per script = the 3 variants: (V1) bright late-20s US female, quick and casual; (V2) warm lower-register 40s female, unhurried; (V3) dry, almost deadpan 30s female. Majority-coverage caption overlays burned in.

**B-roll sequence (both scripts share this spine; order shifts to match each script's lines):**

1. **Hook (0–6s):** POV hands lifting the Colette off an entryway bench toward camera / unzipping nothing — it's open-top, so: hands parting the open top toward the lens. Fall coat rack in background.
2. **Problem (6–12s):** closet pan of wrong-season bags; stiff leather tote dropped on a fall outfit flat-lay, it doesn't sit right. (Script 2: slouchy tote collapsing in slow motion when set down.)
3. **Reveal (12–18s):** slow orbit of the Colette standing alone on the bench in window light. Overlay: "the Colette · $119.99".
4. **Texture (18–24s):** fingers pressing the brushed wool nap; slow slide along the front belt to a gold cap. Medium close only.
5. **Capacity (24–30s):** top-down pack-out — laptop, wallet, bottle, sweater — then the hands-off stand.
6. **Life (30–38s):** forearm carry crossing a street in a camel coat; passenger-seat drop; café counter set-down. Real footage preferred here — crawl TikTok for genuine fall street-style arm-carry clips and gate them; generate via Omni only what the crawler can't find.
7. **Social proof (38–44s):** front/back spin showing no logo; the 2-up caramel + espresso.
8. **CTA (44–50s):** bag by the front door, keys dropping in the tray beside it, overlay "VELANTRA" + link callout.

**Word-sync law applies:** every b-roll clip must show the exact thing the VO line is saying at that moment — audit line by line before assembly.
