## The angle

**Golden nugget:** the quiet pleasure of getting in before a thing is everywhere. She is not an early adopter and does not want to be one, which is exactly why her doing it here carries weight. The pre-order stops being a delay to apologise for and becomes the reason to act.

**Verdict she leads with:** I don't usually pre-order anything, and I did this one.

**The one physical payoff:** the spec run, delivered as a rapid list, because the length of the list is what justifies the exception she made.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** it is the only ad in the family that makes the pre-order the subject rather than the tail. It also has the shortest sentences and the fastest cutting rhythm, twelve clauses in 35 seconds.

**Why this one matters commercially:** it is the only script in the ten that turns the ship date into a benefit rather than a condition. Worth testing first.

**Swap test:** passes. The list is entirely ours.
**Six-month test:** **This is the one ad in the family with an expiry.** It is written for the pre-order window and it dies the day units ship. That is deliberate and disclosed rather than accidental. Every other ad in the family runs indefinitely.

## Voiceover

> I don't usually pre-order anything, and I did this one. This is the Colette from Velantra. Brushed wool body with a cashmere feel, structured so it stands up on its own, a leather belt across the front finishing in two aged gold discs, leather wrapped handles, no logo anywhere. Nineteen point seven inches across, and it fits a thirteen inch laptop. Caramel or espresso. It's a hundred and nineteen right now, it ships in early October, and mine comes from the first run. That's the only reason I did it early.

## Self-audit

**Six-month test:** **fails by design, and this is the only one that does.** The script is explicitly about the pre-order window and it becomes untrue the moment stock lands. Flagged rather than hidden. Every other ad in the family is durable.

**Swap test:** passes. Eight consecutive Colette-specific facts.

**Compliance:** live price stated as current ("a hundred and nineteen right now") with no discount claimed, no compare-at implied. Ship date stated twice. Decision register throughout, and "mine comes from the first run" is true of a placed pre-order. No fibre claim, no lining claim, no origin claim, no competitor.

> **Optional proof line, needs Brooks's sign-off before use.** As of this morning 136 pre-orders are placed (85 Caramel, 51 Espresso), pulled from live Shopify inventory. A line such as *"and it's already a hundred and thirty something orders in"* is true and sourced, but it is a soft number that moves daily and it has not been used publicly before. **Not written into the script.** If approved, it slots into beat 13 and the VO is regenerated.

**Retirement:** pause on the day the first run ships. The other nine ads in the family need the October VO swap; this one needs turning off.
