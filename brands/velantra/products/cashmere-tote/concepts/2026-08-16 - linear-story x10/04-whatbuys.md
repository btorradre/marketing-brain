## The angle

**Golden nugget:** the real discrepancy between what a bag costs and what it actually is. She isn't looking for cheap, she's looking to know what she is paying for, itemised, before she commits. This is the brand's value thesis carried by one woman doing an inventory.

**Verdict she leads with:** I wanted to know what you actually get for a hundred and nineteen dollars.

**The one physical payoff:** the macro run. Every component named out loud lands on its own frame.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** longest of the ten at 39.9s and the most component-dense. It is the spec walkthrough of the family, but told as a buyer's checklist rather than a feature list.

> **Register warning.** This ad sits closest to the banned territory. It states our price and our components and stops there. **No comparison to any other bag at any price, named or unnamed.** No "you'd pay X elsewhere". No "designer". The argument is made entirely by the length of our own list.

**Swap test:** passes. Nine of our own physical facts in a row.
**Six-month test:** passes.

## Voiceover

> I wanted to know what you actually get for a hundred and nineteen dollars before I ordered anything. This is the Colette from Velantra. The body is brushed wool with a cashmere feel, and it's structured, so it holds its shape instead of slumping. The handles are wrapped in leather where your hand sits. The belt across the front is leather, and it ends in two aged gold discs. It's nineteen point seven inches across and it fits a thirteen inch laptop. No logo anywhere. Caramel or espresso. That's the whole list, and that's what a hundred and nineteen gets you. They ship the first run in early October.

## Edit notes

- Beats 3 to 10 are a **metronomic macro run**: eight components, eight cuts, 2–3.6s each, every one landing on its own clause. Keep the rhythm even. The regularity is the argument, because it makes the list feel long.
- Beat 7 gets a fractionally longer hold than its neighbours. It is the only ornament on the bag.
- Beat 11 is the single colorway break in the family. **One beat only.** Beat 12 is back on Caramel.
- Beats 1, 12 and 13 are one location, one light, one wardrobe. Shoot as a set.
- Longest ad in the family. If it needs shortening for a placement, cut beat 10 (`No logo anywhere`) first, since Ad 03 owns that argument in full.
