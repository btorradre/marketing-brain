## The angle

**Golden nugget:** she is at the age where being read as *trying* is worse than being read as plain. A logo announces what you paid, in both directions. A blank front announces nothing, and that silence is the thing she is actually buying.

**Verdict she leads with:** the thing I kept coming back to is that there's nothing written on it.

**The one physical payoff:** the slow turn around the whole exterior finding no mark anywhere.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** this is the only ad in the family built on an absence. Everything else stacks what the bag has. This one stacks what it doesn't, and then cashes that into the only decoration it does have, which is the belt and the two gold discs.

**Swap test:** passes hard. Most bags at this price are covered in hardware branding. The belt curving down into two round gold disc caps is a Colette shape.
**Six-month test:** passes.

## Voiceover

> The thing I kept coming back to is that there's nothing written on it. No logo on the front, no logo on the side, nothing stamped into the leather. This is the Colette from Velantra. What's on the front is a leather belt straight across, and the ends curve down and finish in two round gold caps, and that's the whole decoration. Oatmeal wool body, leather wrapped handles, aged gold hardware. Nobody can tell you what it cost by looking at it. That was the point for me. A hundred and nineteen, and they ship the first run in early October.

## Edit notes

- Beats 2, 3 and 4 are a rapid-fire trio, 1.6–2.3s each, hard cuts. Three claims of absence in five seconds. Do not slow them down.
- Beat 7 is the payoff and it needs the full 4.4s. It is the only decoration on the bag and it has to land as a reward for four beats of nothing.
- Beats 6 and 4 pull from the same library clip. **Use two different sections of it** so the repeat is not visible.
- **Sweep the whole cut for stray brand marks** before delivery. This is the one ad where a logo anywhere in frame, on a coffee cup, a shop window, a shoe, kills the argument.
- Register check: "nobody can tell you what it cost" is about the bag's discretion. It is never played as a sneer at anyone who paid more. If a take reads smug, use another.
