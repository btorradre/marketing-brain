## The angle

**Golden nugget:** she has been buying bags for the version of herself who carries a phone and a card, not the version who actually leaves the house. The honesty in the hook is the hook. It is the shortest ad in the family and the most direct.

**Verdict she leads with:** almost every tote I looked at turned out to be too small once I was honest about what I actually carry.

**The one physical payoff:** the laptop lying flat across the width rather than standing up against a side, which is the physical difference 19.7 inches buys.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** it is built on one number and one geometric consequence of that number. Ad 02 measures before ordering; this one is about the self-deception that makes measuring necessary in the first place.

**Swap test:** passes. Lying flat instead of standing on edge is a direct consequence of our width.
**Six-month test:** passes.

## Voiceover

> Almost every tote I looked at turned out to be too small once I was honest about what I actually carry. This is the Colette from Velantra, and it's nineteen point seven inches across. That's wide enough that a thirteen inch laptop lies flat inside it instead of standing up against the side, and there's seven inches of depth behind it, so the sweater and the bottle and the lunch all go in on top of it. It's structured brushed wool, so full or empty the front stays flat. A hundred and nineteen, and they ship the first run in early October.
