## The angle

**Golden nugget:** the bag you actually keep is decided by the part that touches you, not the part that photographs. Every bag looks fine in a photo. The one you're still carrying in two years is the one whose handle doesn't cut into your hand at the supermarket.

**Verdict she leads with:** here's the part of a bag nobody photographs and everybody feels.

**The one physical payoff:** the two-tone junction. Felt coming up out of the body, leather taking over exactly where the hand closes.

**Why it isn't the reference ad again:** this is the most granular ad in the family and the only one built on a single component. It is also the strongest craft argument the Colette has, because the two-tone handle is a construction decision, not a finish.

**Swap test:** passes completely. The felt-into-leather two-tone handle is the most Colette-specific detail on the bag.
**Six-month test:** passes.

## Voiceover

> Here's the part of a bag nobody photographs and everybody feels, which is the handle. This is the Colette from Velantra. The handles are two tone. That detail is what decided it for me. A hundred and nineteen, and they ship the first run in early October.

## Edit notes

- **Nine of twelve beats are macro.** This is the one ad in the family that breaks the creator-led rule, and it does it on purpose, because the argument is tactile and a wide shot cannot make it. Dana still opens and closes the ad so the first-person frame holds.
- Beat 6 is the payoff and gets the longest hold in the ad at 4.8s. The junction has to be unmistakable. If the chosen library section does not clearly show felt becoming leather, use a different section rather than a different clip.
- Beats 5 and 6 must **travel in the same direction** (up the handle). If the library sections run opposite ways, reverse one in the edit.
- Beats 2, 7 and 9 all show a hand or forearm. **Face-free crop is mandatory on all three** and the sleeve should read as the rust cardigan. If a library clip shows a clearly different sleeve colour, crop tighter.
- Watch the macro clips for the Omni macro-mutation signature. If a handle changes proportion mid-clip, trim earlier.
