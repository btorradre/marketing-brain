# Product Visual Reference Model — Colette Wool Tote (Velantra)

Built purely from the supplied product images (canonical 3/4 hero, front, side v4, interior top-down, espresso hero, on-model street shot). Nothing below is taken from a PDP or spec sheet. Where the images do not settle a detail it is listed under Uncertainties and is off-limits for generation.

## What the images establish

**Construction / silhouette**
- East-west carry-all tote. Clearly wider than tall; from the front roughly 2:1 width to height. Deep, boxy footprint (the side view shows a broad flat gusset, roughly a third of the width).
- Softly structured: it stands up on its own with a flat base, but the walls are fabric, not rigid. Top edge is straight, mouth is a wide open oval when viewed from above.
- Symmetric front and back faces. Side gussets are a single clean panel with one vertical centre seam. Base is a flat rectangle with a stitched panel outline visible from inside.

**Materials / surface**
- Body, straps, base and interior are all one material: oatmeal-greige brushed wool felt with visible short fibres and a mottled, slightly heathered surface. Matte, no sheen, no knit ribs, no weave.
- Trim is smooth, thin, supple leather: warm cognac tan on the Caramel colorway, dark espresso brown on the Espresso colorway. Only the trim changes between colorways; the felt body is identical.
- Interior: the same felt continues inside. No printed lining, no visible pockets, no lining pattern. A stitched rectangle marks the base panel.

**Handles**
- Two top handles, one on the front face and one on the back. Each rises from two wide felt straps that continue down the face of the bag.
- Handle grip section is rolled and wrapped in leather (cognac or espresso); the leather wrap covers the top arc only, the lower portions of the handle loop are felt. Handle drop is short: the bag hangs at hip height on a forearm, and shoulder carry is not something the images show.
- No shoulder strap, no crossbody strap, no strap attachment rings.

**Front / back face straps and belt**
- Two wide vertical felt straps run the full height of each face, from the top rim to the base, aligned with the handle bases.
- A slim horizontal leather belt, about half an inch wide, crosses the face roughly one-third down from the top, passing through the felt straps (there is a felt loop at each strap where the belt threads through). The belt ends extend beyond the outer straps and curve outward and downward, hanging free, each finished with a small round aged-gold disc cap. The belt runs across the FACE only; nothing attaches to the side gussets.

**Closure**
- The mouth is open; no flap, no zipper, no drawstring. The interior image shows one small felt tab with a gold snap stud at the top centre of the front inner rim and one on the back inner rim: a single centre snap tab that can hold the mouth closed. From the outside, closed, this reads as the two rims pinched together at the centre. The side view shows the tab as a small felt tongue at the top of the gusset seam.

**Hardware**
- Aged gold (warm, slightly matte) only: four disc caps on the belt ends and the two small snap studs. No other metal anywhere.

**Branding**
- No logo, no embossing, no plaque, no lettering, no monogram anywhere inside or out. Generation must never add one.

**Scale**
- On the model the bag spans roughly from her hip to past her opposite thigh. It is a large everyday bag, laptop-scale, not a mini.

## Uncertainties (do not generate around these)
- Exact interior pockets or a laptop sleeve: not visible, so no interior shots that depend on organised compartments. Contents simply sit inside the felt walls.
- Whether the belt is functional or decorative: never show it being unbuckled or adjusted (there is no buckle).
- Bag when empty and slouched: images only show it standing; keep it standing or lightly softened.
- Back face: not photographed but the construction is symmetric; back-view shots reuse the front construction exactly.
- Base underside: never shown; avoid bottom-up shots.
- Weight and drape on a shoulder: the drop is short; carry by hand or forearm only.
