## GOLDEN NUGGET

The motive is not "I want a nice bag." It is: **she wants to be read as the woman with taste, without
having to say what she paid or wear someone else's logo to prove it.** The no-logo, all-leather,
aged-brass Vivienne lets her be discerning instead of branded. Every headline below leads with that,
never with "a good deal."

---

## LAW-FORCED DEVIATIONS FROM THE REFERENCES

Mirror fidelity is the default. These five beats deviate, and only because a house law forces it.

1. **Ref A's title card says "Mulberry Bayswater Dupe."** Competitor naming is banned outside the
   ranked-roundup carve-out. Every A headline carries a verdict instead of a competitor name.
   "Birkin-inspired" is also out, because baked overlay text IS a generation prompt and that phrase is
   banned in prompts.
2. **Ref A's "look at all that room!" is a capacity claim.** Not cleared. Replaced with the cleared
   wording from PRODUCT-TRUTH §11: "Fits far more than a top handle bag usually does."
3. **Interior is TODO.** Neither the source photos nor the three real TikToks ever show inside the bag.
   Interior beats are shot **packed and in deep shadow** so no lining colour, fabric, or pocket is ever
   asserted. This mirrors what Ref B's own footage actually shows. **See the open question at the bottom.**
4. **Both references use a black bag.** Black's strap treatment is unconfirmed (tonal vs contrast).
   Chocolate is the confirmed hero and is used throughout.
5. **Pre-order law.** Every variant carries "Ships October" on the end card and the product ID by 0:06.
   Ref B's macro beat 4 swaps the reference's camera for our brass closure so the product stays on screen.

inside the bag, so every interior beat here is rendered as packed contents over deep shadow, which asserts
nothing. If the factory sample comes back with a cream twill lining, beats A7, A8 and all of Ad B need
a re-roll. Confirming it now is cheaper than re-rolling later.

Fixed from the first pass: colorway drift (frames were coming back chestnut and oxblood, now locked

### v3, 2026-08-27 — product consistency fix

Brooks flagged that the bag looked different in every frame. He was right, and the cause was the seeding:
v2 anchored every prompt on the burgundy TikTok photo and passed the approved Chocolate three-quarter only
as a colour hint, so the engine kept re-inventing the bag's character frame to frame.

v3 drops the burgundy seeds entirely. The approved Chocolate three-quarter is now the SINGLE product
reference in all 17 prompts, with a locked parts list read directly off that image: the two-tone split,
the one-piece flap with its two round handle cutouts, the plaited braid along the flap's rear edge, and
the brass count of ONE oval, ONE knurled post, TWO parallel-bar staples, TWO strap end plates. The TikTok
frame is passed as a scene reference only, with an explicit instruction to take nothing about the bag
from it.

**Seeding rule note:** the skill's rule says always i2i from a real photograph, never a previous
generation. Brooks designated this approved generated master as the reference for this product, which
supersedes it for this run.
