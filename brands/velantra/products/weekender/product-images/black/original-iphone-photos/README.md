# Original Black Weekender photos (retired from the PDP gallery 2026-08-09)

These 5 images were the Black colorway's live gallery from launch until 2026-08-09. They are
REAL PHOTOGRAPHS of the physical black bag (shot on iPhone, in a home and a hotel room), not
generated images. They were retired from the PDP because they did not match the editorial studio
look shared by Light Chocolate, Army Green and Dark Chocolate, so Black read as a different
product line in the gallery.

They remain the SUPREME ground truth for the black colorway's material and colour. Wire them as
the i2i reference for any future Black generation.

- black-01.png  front view, all black leather, gold hardware  <- primary colour/material reference
- black-02.png  three-quarter view
- black-03.png  open, showing the CARAMEL TAN leather interior  <- interior truth
- black-04.png  carried at the hip (scale reference against a person)
- black-05.png  gold hardware macro against black leather

Product truth for Black: ALL smooth black leather exterior (NO canvas panel, unlike the other
colorways), caramel tan leather interior, warm brass gold hardware.
