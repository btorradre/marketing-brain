# Founder's Creed, The Eleanor Weekender. Script Draft V1

**Swipe source:** Vestirsi "Woven Collection" manifesto (TrendTrack vestirsi-beiVGU, 20s)
**Story type:** Founder's creed. A first-person belief manifesto, not an origin narrative. No events, no timeline. The founder states a standard as law, rejects the category default, defines the ideal, names his values, then reveals the brand as the embodiment of the belief. The launch rides the belief's momentum.
**Format:** ~30-34s vertical. Male founder VO (Brooks register), never on camera. Word-by-word karaoke overlay. Two visual registers alternating every 1-2s: craft-proof macros and editorial carry frames.
**Golden nugget (ours):** A bag too precious to actually carry has failed at its job. She wants permission to bring the beautiful bag AND use it hard. The Weekender sells relief from babying.

## VO Script V1 (~101 words, single seamless ElevenLabs track)

The bag you travel with should earn every trip you take.

I grew up sailing. Canvas, brass, and good leather were never meant to stay new. They were meant to get better.

So I do not think women need another bag that is too nice to use. I think they need one that can take the trip.

Three days of clothing. Slides into the overhead bin. Keeps its shape packed full or half empty.

Reinforced handles. Solid brass. Leather that picks up character with every trip. Built for decades, not seasons.

That has always been the idea behind Velantra.

Introducing the Eleanor Weekender. Discover yours.

## Production guardrails (from velantra-weekender skill, non-negotiable)

- Hand or forearm carry only. The handles cannot reach a shoulder and Seedance invents a strap if staged.
- Bag never opened, unfastened, or manipulated on camera. Open-packed state appears only across hard cuts, sourced from the validated broll look (`brands/velantra/products/weekender/broll/Open_bag_packed_for_weekend_202607111429.mp4`).
- "Weekender" never as native engine text, post overlay only. Karaoke overlay is post anyway.
- Both clasp plates gold, pin the right plate explicitly (silver-plate attractor).
- Photoreal block + reference-refusal preamble in every i2i prompt, 3 variants per scene, frame-QA subagent pass before animating.
- No "Birkin," no origin claims, no competitor names, no em dashes in any prompt or overlay.
- Founder VO only, founder never on screen.
