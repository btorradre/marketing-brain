### The aesthetic spec, non-negotiable

Shot on an iPhone. That means, literally:

- **Handheld with micro-shake.** No gimbal glide, no dolly, no crane. If a move looks operated, it is wrong.
- **Practical light only.** Hotel lamp and window for the room beats, terminal fluorescents and daylight for the gate beats. No fill, no key, no bounce.
- **iPhone-native depth.** Shallow on the macro inserts, deep everywhere else. Never cinema-camera bokeh.
- **Slightly imperfect framing.** Head a little off centre, a beat of focus hunt on the inserts, the odd tilt. Ref B is full of this and it is why it reads real.
- **No grade.** Warm interiors, cool terminals, whatever the phone gives.
- 1080x1920.

### ⚠️ The one line I changed, and why

Brooks's line: *"everyone has seen Haaland with all of these Birkin bags, but the problem is they're all thousands of dollars."*

**"They're all thousands of dollars" is a competitor price comparison and it quotes a Hermès price.** Both are banned, and the ban is specific rather than general: the Birkin clearance says the phrase is legal as a shape mechanism *"never as a comparison, never with a Hermes price, never ranked against anything"* ([[feedback_velantra_birkin_inspired_mechanism]], [[feedback_velantra_no_competitor_comparisons]]).

**What I wrote instead, which lands the same beat:** the gap gets resolved by stating **our own price, early and flat**, and the audience does the subtraction itself. This is exactly what the men's VOC recommended on 8/02 (*"We simply state our own spec and our own price"*), and it is what the Brad Pitt reference itself does, since it never prices the thing it is dismissing.

> "Everybody's seen him with these bags. This is the Eleanor Weekender from Velantra, and it's about a hundred and sixty dollars."

Same relief, no banned comparison, and it pulls the price forward to 0:06 where Brooks wants the problem solved. **If you want your exact line, say so and I will write it, but it fails the law gate as it stands.**

## Production laws that bind this build

1. **Storyboard first.** Keyframes on the Cutroom board, Brooks approves there, and only then does anything animate ([[feedback_ad_engine_storyboard_first]]).
2. **F04 and F07 never go to Seedance.** The open-bag mechanism has failed on 2.0 and 2.5 even from validated keyframes. GPT Image 2 i2i stills, hard cut either side.
3. **Every prompt carries the identity block, the closure-hardware truth regardless of crop, and a per-shot relational scale anchor.** Dimensions alone render this bag as a handbag.
4. **Motion is Omni.** VO ad, no lipsync required ([[feedback_broll_engine_rule_omni_vs_seedance]]).
5. **Hand or forearm carry only.** The rolled top handles cannot reach a shoulder. Never a shoulder carry, never the loose interior strap.
6. **Every generation wires a still from `product-references/real-product-2026-08-08/`.** QA every frame with explicit counts: one knurled post, one oval keyhole plate on the flap tab and never on the band, two staples of two bars each, two upright handles, one eyelet per side face, caramel leather interior.
7. **"Birkin" is spoken and captioned. Never typed into a generation prompt** — the kie claims-grep hard-fails.
8. **Situational plausibility.** He would put the bag on the carry-on handle and on a hotel bed. He would not put it on a terminal floor ([[feedback_broll_situational_plausibility]]).
9. **No Ken Burns fill.** F02, F03, F06, F08, F09, F10 and F11 are real motion or the beat gets cut.
