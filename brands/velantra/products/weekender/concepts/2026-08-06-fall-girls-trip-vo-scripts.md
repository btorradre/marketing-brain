## Production notes

- **Do not stage the bag being opened, unfastened, or manipulated on camera.** Closure-interaction law, third strike. Open-bag beats are generated as already-open frames with GPT Image 2 i2i and cut to across hard cuts, never animated open.
- **Hand or forearm carry only.** The rolled top handles cannot reach a shoulder and the engine invents a strap to bridge it.
- **Overlay the product name in post.** Seedance cannot spell "Weekender."
- Verbatim identity block + photoreal block into every frame prompt. Three variants per scene, then pick.
- **VO:** ElevenLabs v3, stability 0.0, one seamless full-length take, sliced to the beats afterward. Never per-clip.
- Register check: brand and offer are third person ("they're running a sale", "it's on sale"), her own decision is first person ("why I bought it"). No "we", no "our".
- **Colorway note:** light chocolate fronts this cut. Both colorways are already fall tones, which is why the season peg costs us nothing in new product photography.
