## Reference autopsy

**Visual grammar.** Sixteen beats, roughly 3.5s per visual. Every beat is the same construction: a full-bleed still (a brand's Instagram post screen-recorded with the like count showing, or a clean model/product shot) with the creator's talking head as an **opaque rectangular PiP bottom-right, about 45% of frame width, inset from the right and bottom edges**. She is chest-up in a brown off-the-shoulder top against a plain dark room, gesturing. Captions sit dead centre of the frame, white with a black outline, three to eight words. Beat 1 is a 2x2 grid of four bags with the title on the seam. The last beat is "if you're after a classic black."

**Script grammar.** Title hook → self-intro ("if you're new here, my name is Annelise") → brand 1 with a colour beat and a hardware beat → brand 2 with a capacity beat and a "perfect plane travel bag" beat on a Rimowa → brand 3 with a context beat → brand 4 with social proof ("literally viral", "everywhere on my Pinterest feed") and two "so so oversized" beats → brand 5, the classic black. ~150 words, 55s.

**Why it works.** She never sells one bag; she curates, so the viewer trusts her. The stills do the desire work and she does the permission work. Our version keeps the curation voice ("I found it") but every slot is the Weekender, which is [[feedback_sell_our_product_not_the_category]] applied to a listicle format.

## Production recipe (as run)

1. VO: ElevenLabs Woman Over 40, three takes, STT-gate, pick the take that says Velantra and Weekender right. Save the word timestamps for captions.
2. Creator: upload the approved keyframe → HeyGen photo avatar → poll to `completed` → `POST /v3/videos` with `avatar_id` + `audio_asset_id` + `engine: avatar_v`, 9:16 1080p. Verify head-region motion delta 7-10.
3. Bed: cut the 16 library visuals to the STT beat times. Stills get a slow 4% push-in, clips play straight. Scale 720p clips to 1080x1920.
4. Composite: bed → creator plate as an opaque rectangle at the geometry above → Pillow caption cards on Scribe word timestamps (max 6 words, flush at terminal punctuation) → music bed ducked -18dB under the voice.
5. QC: brand and product names correct on playback, ship date in the black caption, price matches the live page on ship day, no logo visible anywhere, hand carry in every beat, one continuous VO, zero black frames.

Reusable code: `concepts/8-21-26-birkin-greenscreen/production/` (`build_captions.py`, `build_caption_video.py`, `assemble.py`). Swap the PiP block for the inset rectangle and move the caption MarginV to mid-frame.
