Material & care
Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. If it gets wet, let it dry away from heat.
Keep out of prolonged sun and damp; both shift the colour of leather.
Store upright, out of direct light, with the bag empty.
Natural marks in the hide are left in deliberately. The colour deepens with wear.
