Shipping policy
Shipping Policy
Thank you for visiting and shopping at Velantra. Below are the terms and conditions that constitute our Shipping Policy.
Processing Time
All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders are not processed on weekends or holidays. If we are experiencing a high volume of orders, processing times may be slightly delayed. In such cases, we will notify you via email.
Shipping Times
Please allow 7-10 business days for delivery after your order has been processed. Shipping times can vary depending on your location, but we strive to deliver within this window.
Shipping Rates & Carriers
Free shipping on all orders over $75. Orders under $75 ship at a flat $4.99 (Standard Shipping). U.S. orders ship from our U.S. fulfillment center by Standard Shipping; US Express Shipping is available at checkout for $6.99. The carrier may vary depending on your location.
Shipping Confirmation & Tracking
Once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can track your order directly from this email to monitor its delivery status.
Delivery Delays
While we aim for timely deliveries, please note that shipping times are estimates. Delays can sometimes occur due to unforeseen circumstances such as customs clearance or inclement weather. We are not responsible for shipping delays beyond our control but will assist in tracking and resolving any issues.
Shipping Destinations
We ship to addresses within the United States and to the international destinations offered at checkout (including Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Singapore, and Hong Kong). International orders ship free. International delivery times vary by destination and customs processing, so please allow additional time beyond our domestic window. We are unable to ship to P.O. boxes or APO/FPO addresses at this time.
Customs, Duties, and Taxes
For international orders, Velantra is not responsible for customs fees, duties, or taxes applied to your order. Any such fees imposed during or after shipping are the responsibility of the customer.
Lost or Stolen Packages
Velantra is not liable for any lost or stolen packages confirmed to be delivered to the address entered for an order. Upon inquiry, we will confirm the delivery to the address provided, date of delivery, tracking information, and shipping carrier information for the customer to investigate.
