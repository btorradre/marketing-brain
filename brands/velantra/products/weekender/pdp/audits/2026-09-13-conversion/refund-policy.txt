Velantra Return, Refund & Warranty Policy
Effective July 2026
If something isn't right with your order, we'll make it right. Email us at
customerservice@velantrafashion.com
and we'll respond within one business day. Every request on this page — cancellations, returns, refunds, warranty replacements — is handled directly by our team, and eligible refunds go straight back to your original payment method. You never need to involve your bank to get your money back.
Our Two-Year Warranty — Free Replacement
Every Velantra bag is covered by a
two-year warranty
from the day your order is delivered. If your bag develops a defect in materials or construction — stitching comes undone, a clasp or zipper fails, a strap or handle separates, the lining tears —
we will send you a free replacement
. No repair fees, no shipping charges, no fine print.
To make a warranty claim, email
customerservice@velantrafashion.com
with your order number, a short description of the issue, and 2–3 photos. We'll review your claim within 2 business days and ship your replacement free of charge.
The warranty doesn't cover normal cosmetic wear and patina, damage from misuse or accidents, or items modified or repaired by a third party. Full details are on our
Two-Year Warranty page
.
Order Processing & Cancellations
We move quickly: orders are sent to our fulfillment center shortly after they're placed — often within the hour — so they can get to you as fast as possible. Because of this,
contact us within 1 hour of placing your order
to cancel it, and we'll cancel and issue a full refund.
After that window, your order has usually already entered fulfillment and can no longer be intercepted. You're still fully protected: once it arrives, you can return it unused within 30 days for a full refund of the product price.
Changing or Removing Items From an Order
Ordered more than one item and changed your mind about part of it? Email us with your order number and the item you'd like removed, and we'll refund that item to your original payment method. If the full order has already shipped, we'll handle the removed item as a standard return.
If Your Order Is Delayed
Delivery estimates are estimates, and occasionally an order takes longer than expected. If your order is delayed, we'll email you proactively with the updated timeline. If your order is significantly delayed and you'd rather not wait, email us —
we'll issue you a full refund
. You don't need to wait for the package to arrive, and you never need to open a bank dispute. A shipping delay will never cost you your money.
30-Day Returns & Exchanges
You can request a return or exchange
within 30 days of delivery
for unused items in their original packaging with all tags attached. Email
customerservice@velantrafashion.com
with your order number and we'll send you a return authorization and simple instructions.
Items must be unused, with all tags attached and original packaging included.
Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless we made an error with your order. Exchanges are always free: when you exchange for a different color or style, we ship the replacement at no charge.
We may decline returns for items that arrive worn, washed, or otherwise not in original condition.
Where to Send Your Return
Email us first at
customerservice@velantrafashion.com
so we can issue your return authorization. Once you have it, send your bag to:
Velantra Returns
4805 McKinney Ave, Apt 413
Dallas, TX 75205
United States
Please write your order number on a note inside the package so we can match it to your account the day it arrives. We recommend using a tracked shipping service, since we can only refund returns we actually receive.
Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items
If your order arrives damaged, defective, or isn't what you ordered, email us clear photos of the item, packaging, and shipping label within
7 days
of delivery. We'll make it right with your choice of a
free replacement
or a
full refund
— at no cost to you.
Non-Returnable Items
Gift cards
Items marked final sale at the time of purchase
Items that are used, damaged after delivery, or missing tags and original packaging
Orders confirmed delivered to the address provided, where the package was lost or stolen after delivery (see our
Shipping Policy
)
Refund Processing
Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method. Cancellation, item-removal, and delay refunds are issued as soon as we confirm them with you; return refunds are issued once your return is received and inspected. Please allow
5–10 business days
for the credit to appear on your statement, depending on your bank. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless we made an error with your order.
A Note on Chargebacks
If you're ever unhappy with an order, please email us before opening a dispute with your bank. We resolve refund requests in days, while a bank chargeback typically takes 30–90 days to complete — and once a dispute is opened, the card network freezes the transaction, which means we're no longer able to refund you directly until the dispute is closed. Contacting us first is always the fastest way to get your money back.
