THE RHYTHM OF MAKING

Each piece follows a measured process of cutting, stitching, and finishing.

Over 15 days, the leather is shaped with close attention to material, structure, and detail.

Day 01-03 Leather Selection & Cutting Texture often says more than words. Full-grain leather is sourced from tanneries certified Gold by the Leather Working Group (LWG). Heavy surface coatings are avoided, preserving the leather’s natural grain, supple texture, and individual character. Each hide is carefully selected and precisely cut so the panels come together in a balanced, consistent composition.
Day 04-08 Thread Selection & Stitching Each well-placed stitch gives the form its strength. Selected to meet exacting standards for texture, strength, flexibility, and finish, the thread gives each seam a clean, precise expression. Carefully controlled spacing and tension support the bag’s structure while refining its overall feel.
Day 09-13 Edge Paint & Finishing Refinement is visible in every edge. Specially selected edge paint creates a fine, subtly textured finish with a light and refined appearance. Built up in multiple thin layers, it gives the edges quiet depth without making them feel heavy. Its flexibility and adhesion allow the finish to sit naturally with the leather, adding definition to the silhouette and detail of the bag.
Day 14-15+ Final Quality Inspection Nothing is finished until every detail is in place. Before packaging, every bag is reviewed for silhouette, panel alignment, stitch consistency, edge finish, leather condition, and hardware function. Only pieces that meet the required standards are approved for release.

Client Advisor

Hi, how can we help?

How long until I receive my piece?

Every ARFORTI is scheduled into production the moment your order is confirmed: the atelier's making typically takes 15–20 days, and once it passes final inspection it is dispatched within 1 business day. Standard shipping (free worldwide) arrives approximately 4–6 business days after dispatch; express (approx. $30), 1–3 business days. From order to delivery, usually three to four weeks. See the Shipping Policy.

How can I follow my piece's progress?

Sign in to your ARFORTI account to see every step of your piece — from cutting and edge-painting to saddle stitching. Every moment of its making is shown to you, openly.

Can I cancel or change my order?

Within 24 hours of confirmation, you may cancel in full, free of charge; to change colour or style, write to your advisor within the same window. Beyond 24 hours the hide enters an irreversible production schedule and can no longer be altered — but once your piece is delivered, you still have the 30-day return service.

Where do you ship, and what does it cost?

We ship to most countries and regions worldwide. Standard shipping is free worldwide (no minimum); express is approximately $30 (the amount shown at checkout prevails).

What if the piece I love is sold out?

Join the waitlist on the product page and leave your email. The moment that style reopens for production or there is any news, we will write to you first.
