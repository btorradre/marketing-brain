# Velantra — Quiet Luxury

A standalone Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme built around ARFORTI's typography, restrained spacing, editorial homepage and split product-page layout, with Velantra's own catalog and newly generated media.

[Preview the installed draft](https://velantrafashion.com/?preview_theme_id=151389143105) · [Open the theme editor](https://admin.shopify.com/store/uzdgxy-sb/themes/151389143105/editor)

The current unpublished draft is **151389143105**, “Velantra — Detail Film Cleanup · Sep 6,” cloned from live theme **151364960321**. The latest update removes the reported surface-line artifacts from all seven handbag detail films, including the shared Weekender/Eleanor film and the homepage Vivienne film. All 68 shots retain their order and exact cut timing. Production sources and delivery evidence are in [the cleanup project](video/edit/surface-cleanup/README.md).

The authorized catalog changes rename Weekender colors on both listings: **Light Chocolate → Cognac** and **Dark Chocolate → Espresso**. Army Green and Black retain their names.

The homepage now features models carrying the **Vivienne, Weekender and Meridian**, with editable product/image blocks and links to the photographed color. The repeated wide Vivienne image is hidden. Native compare-at prices appear across product pages and cards, and can be managed through Shopify product pricing. The desktop and mobile navigation contains All bags, Accessories, Track your order and Contact, with a separate editable menu assigned to this draft. All bags opens Handbags and Accessories opens the five-product Accessories collection. PDP media now uses a horizontal gallery on desktop and mobile, retaining zoom, color filtering and arrow/swipe controls.

## Storefront

The theme includes editable header/footer groups, homepage sections, 12 handbag templates, five accessory templates and a default product template, collections, search, cart drawer and cart page, contact form, regular pages, blog/article pages and a 404 page. Product pages retain native variants and inventory behavior, theme-wide preorder notices, image zoom, color-aware galleries, accordions and optional app blocks.

Cormorant Garamond and Montserrat are bundled with their open font licenses. Colors, layout spacing, fonts, page sections, media and product presentation are customizable in Shopify's editor. The studio image mapping belongs to this theme; turning it off restores original catalog imagery in the preview.

The three-second desktop and mobile hero clips are original Google Omni outputs, with no video editing or re-encoding. The product studio images use Velantra product references. Source silhouettes, materials, colors, hardware and useful shot types were reviewed against the originals. Product detail macros retain their close crops; model and demonstration views preserve their intended context. ARFORTI photography, video and theme code are not included in the delivery.
