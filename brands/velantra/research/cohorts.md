# Velantra persona and angle cohorts

**Use this when choosing who an ad speaks to.** There are 19 cohort cards: 17 grounded in direct category-comment evidence to varying degrees, plus two exploratory cards for European fall-trip styling and celebrity/screen-style aspiration. Each card explains the desired identity, buying situation, objections, source language and distinct messaging routes.

**Core motive [analyst inference]: look like the version of yourself you have in mind, while feeling good about the choice and its cost.** The important variation is what that version looks like: quietly polished, creatively dressed, professionally composed, ready for a trip, or pleased with a personal discovery.

This guide comes from a sequential reading of the entire [100-page report](voc/2026-09-11-instagram-angle-database/report/Velantra-Voice-of-Customer-100-Pages.pdf) and the full underlying text of every one of its **603 unique quoted comments**. The larger database contains 16,437 comments; this is not a claim that all 16,437 received a manual review. [Review notes and audit](voc/2026-09-11-instagram-angle-database/cohort-review/manual-review-notes.md) document the decisions. [Every report quote](voc/2026-09-11-instagram-angle-database/cohort-review/quote-cohort-ledger.md) has a review disposition and source ID.

The persona descriptions below are **constructed creative targets**, not biographies of commenters. Do not assume age, income, location, relationship status or purchasing history beyond a particular comment's own words. These cohorts overlap and can change with the purchase. They are messaging groups, not platform targeting lists or measured market segments. Source comments are research language, not Velantra testimonials.

## How to use the cards when writing ads

Choose **one primary cohort + one angle + one exact bag/color + one verified reason to believe**. A secondary cohort can refine the situation, but should not introduce a second competing desire. For example, C01 quiet polish with a C02 budget constraint is coherent. Combining C04's wish to avoid the obvious choice with a claim that everyone has the bag requires a different rationale.

The shared positioning is affordable luxury. An angle is the specific reason to pay attention, such as keeping the admired look while choosing a different price. A founder video, celebrity-style breakdown or ranked comparison is a presentation format. A restock is a purchase trigger. None automatically creates a distinct persona.

The message directions below are **analyst-written premises**, not customer quotations, finished hooks or approved product claims. Lead fashion creative with appearance, styling and enjoyment; use capacity, materials, value and convenience to support that desire. Check the [product registry](../product-skills/registry.json) and the exact product's records before writing specifications. The product matches here are starting hypotheses based on the report's product chapters.

Evidence labels describe this selected review only. “Grounded” means direct relevant statements support the motive; it does not mean a statistically validated segment, verified identity or proven conversion response. Multiple replies under one post are not independent replications. The Instagram and TikTok versions of the same judgment concept are particularly dependent.

## C01. The old-money / quiet-polish aspirer

**Constructed persona:** She wants an elegant, composed appearance. Restrained styling, a considered shape and an outfit that feels finished matter more than a prominent name. Some members need a lower price; others can afford designer and simply prefer this look.

**Golden nugget [inferred]:** Feel established in her taste before anyone knows what she spent. Her interest is in the visual impression, not necessarily inherited wealth or belonging to a particular class.

Q035 is a direct aesthetic preference. Q043 joins classic styling with value and low familiarity in one purchase. Q534 expresses a desired identity in a style interview; it is not specifically a handbag purchase. Q024 and Q311 add explicit budget friction in the quiet-luxury conversation.

**Angle routes:** Old-money styling at a price she can justify; elegant shape with restrained branding; a classic bag styled with clothing she already likes. European-inspired everyday dressing is a possible styling treatment, without claiming European origin or a rule followed by all European women.

**Boundary:** Q034 and Q066 show that visible logos can also feel stylish. Q430 even describes a quiet-luxury favorite as performative. Avoid treating restraint as moral superiority or proof of wealth. If the attraction is specifically a famous silhouette rather than understated polish, choose C02.

## Buying conditions that cut across cohorts

Use these to refine a cohort brief. They usually explain what must be resolved before purchase, rather than who the person wants to become.

|---|---|---|
| Unfamiliar-brand trust | Q011 questions low-price quality; Q159 waits for reviews; Q468 cannot distinguish the legitimate website | Clear identity, accurate product details, genuine evidence and an unambiguous buying destination |
| Delivery and availability | Q253-Q261 describe stock/waitlist frustration; Q524 reports a missing order; Q527 likes the bag but dislikes the wait | The actual availability and delivery expectation, without using someone else's complaint as a Velantra performance claim |
| Physical fit and carrying comfort | Q005, Q009, Q130-Q140, Q325-Q333, Q597-Q603 identify different sizes, loads and carrying preferences | Measurements, an actual fit demonstration and an appropriately scoped use case |
| Material and construction | Q033 asks for presentation appropriate to price; Q254 asks about conflicting material descriptions; Q460 questions hardware | Precise SKU facts and close-up evidence; no quality-equivalence shortcut |

## Evidence and maintenance

The review preserves useful contradictions instead of assigning every comment to a buyer. Generic praise, link requests, seller promotion, unrelated product questions, insults, copied creator lines and speculative industry claims have separate dispositions in the ledger. No motive was inferred simply because the parent creator used an angle keyword.

Short quotes above are exact contiguous excerpts of the full stored comments. Spelling is preserved. Each anchor has the original comment ID, creator context, report page and source link. Instagram links identify the comment; TikTok links identify the parent post and the ledger retains the comment ID. Link availability can change; the archived text remains in the database. The original PDF's comment timestamps, engagement snapshots and automatic transcript limits still apply.

To update this guide, add direct evidence to the existing cohort ID, preserve contradictory cases and record the new source/date. Create a new cohort only when it has a distinct desired identity or purchase decision, not merely a new platform, season, product or creative format. Validate exploratory cards through buyer interviews or open-ended purchase reasons before describing them as established customer groups.
