GETTING STARTED


Boilerplate

Velantra is a coastal luxury handbag brand for the woman who lets her taste speak louder than any logo. Led by its hero piece, the Straw Birkin, Velantra designs structured, heritage-quality bags that carry the ease of a seaside summer and the craftsmanship of a bag built to last decades, not seasons. Every piece is made from natural materials, hand-woven straw, full-grain leather, and solid brass, with no visible branding, because the details do the talking. Rooted in the quiet-luxury rhythm of the coast, the ferry line, the farmers' market, the harbor-side lunch, Velantra is the bag people stop to ask you about. Backed by a two-year warranty and lifetime repairs, it is designed to be discovered, carried, and passed down.

Manufacturing / Made Where

Reality: Designed in the U.S. by founder Brooks Orradre; handcrafted overseas (China) by our trusted manufacturing partners.
Approved public phrasing: "Designed in the U.S., handcrafted by skilled artisans overseas."
Do not claim: New York atelier, Italian craftsmanship, made in Paris or Italy, or any US/EU manufacture (illegal country-of-origin misrepresentation and PR risk). Delete these from the site, kit, emails, and popups. If a reporter asks directly, confirm production is overseas.


Key Media Messaging

1. The summer bag of 2026. The Straw Birkin is the coastal, quiet-luxury successor to last year's viral "Boatkin" moment, the Birkin silhouette in hand-woven straw.
2. Quiet luxury, no logo. Heritage materials and construction with zero visible branding. Status you feel, not status you advertise.
3. Built to last decades, not seasons. Two-year warranty plus lifetime repairs. An investment piece, not a trend buy.
4. The bag people stop to ask you about. Designed as a conversation piece and word-of-mouth discovery, not a billboard.
5. Coastal by design. Made for the harbor, the market, the ferry, the patio. Effortless from beach to dinner.


What Makes Velantra / The Product Unique

The shape everyone wants, without the tax. The Straw Birkin captures the coveted Birkin silhouette in hand-woven straw with leather trim, no logo, no four-figure price, no waitlist games.
Structured straw solves the straw-bag problem. Most straw bags are flimsy and beach-only; the Straw Birkin holds its shape, works in the city, and carries a laptop and water bottle.
Natural materials, no logo. Hand-woven straw, full-grain leather, solid brass. Nothing to advertise the brand for you.
A full color run means collectibility. Buyers rarely stop at one; the color range drives the "which one is mine?" loop.
Warranty plus lifetime repairs. Rare at this price tier and central to the "decades, not seasons" promise.
Direct-to-consumer, so designer-grade materials at an accessible price.
