# Velantra customer-language database refresh

Scope: Instagram first, with TikTok triangulation. Preserve all 167 unique supplied references (164 Instagram, one TikTok, one TrendTrack, one X), their original notes and duplicates. Retrieve public post metadata, comments and available Reel transcripts using Apify. Expand from seed creators and angle-specific search terms. Keep original exports and run identifiers.

## Positioning and angle taxonomy

Affordable luxury is the brand-level value position. Evaluate distinct motives and entry points: new-brand discovery; founder/value origin; designer-spend rejection; old-money aesthetics; European everyday styling; European fall-trip styling; outfit elevation; social judgment/approval and resistance; industry markup skepticism; quality/material discernment; logo independence; seasonal woven-bag desire; work-bag polish; travel/large-bag style; gifting; and stock/newness/social-proof purchase triggers.

Research covers the requested designer, conspiracy, and seasonal themes despite older local copy rules. Findings are not preapproved ad claims. No asset production, storyboard, or editing work is in scope. Fashion applications lead with aspiration, with practical reasons supporting the desired look.

## Collection waves

1. All supplied Instagram links: post metadata and up to 100 comments per link.
2. All supplied Instagram links: available audio transcripts through the Reel actor; static posts and missing audio remain marked.
3. Supplied TikTok: metadata, available subtitles and up to 200 comments.
4. Instagram keyword search: ten angle-related terms, up to 25 results per term.
5. Seed creator expansion: 15 relevant accounts, up to 18 Reels each.
6. TikTok discovery: eight corresponding queries, up to 15 posts each.
7. Select relevant new posts across themes and creators for comments and transcript enrichment. Do not select only high-engagement winners.

## Evidence rules

- Original seed notes are hypotheses/reference intentions, not factual summaries of the linked content.
- Creator captions and transcripts are creator language. Viewer comments are category-audience language; purchase and Velantra-customer status are unverified unless the source proves them.
- Separate handbag, adjacent fashion, and unrelated-category material. Keep cross-category creative references but exclude them from handbag-specific claims.
- Deduplicate by platform comment ID and post shortcode; preserve collection-wave memberships. Flag repeated text and likely link-request/emoji traffic. Do not equate engagement with a motive or conversion.
- Automatic theme tags are retrieval aids. Curated claims require direct excerpts, context and opposing evidence. Comment counts are sample counts, not market prevalence.
- Price, production, heritage, quality parity, market popularity, gender beliefs and industry wrongdoing are not established by social-media rhetoric.
- Preserve exact quote text in the database. Report excerpts are short, clearly marked and trace to IDs and source URLs. Historical research remains a separate cohort.
