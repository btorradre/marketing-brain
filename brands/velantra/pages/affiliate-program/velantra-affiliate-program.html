Who We Are

A mother-and-son handbag house.

Velantra started a year and a half ago with a simple idea between a son and his mom: professional women deserve structured, heritage-quality bags without the four-figure markup. She's still our ideal customer — and our toughest critic — to this day.

Our first bag sold out almost immediately. Then came the Boat Tote, our summer workhorse. Now the Straw Tote — hand-woven seagrass, made in limited batches — has become the bag strangers stop people on the street to ask about.

Along the way we carved out a niche we love: coastal quiet luxury. Every bag is designed in California, refined obsessively, and built to last decades, not seasons.

Questions

The details, plainly.

How do I actually get paid?
You'll get a personal discount code and tracking link. Every order placed with either one is credited to you, and we pay out your 15% commission on a monthly basis. No minimum sales threshold to join.
Is the bag really free? What's the catch?
Really free, and there isn't one. You pick the bag and colorway, we ship it to you, and it's yours whether or not your first video takes off. We'd rather you create from a place of actually loving the product.
How much content am I committing to?
There's no rigid quota. We ask that you share the bag authentically within a few weeks of receiving it, and we'll work out a rhythm together from there. Creators who post consistently and perform well are the ones we bring onto retainers.
Can the 15% go higher?
Yes. 15% is the starting point, not the ceiling. If your content performs, we're open to negotiating a higher rate — and for our best creators, a full monthly retainer as an official Velantra brand partner.
When will my bag ship?
Most bags ship right away. The Straw Tote is hand-woven in limited batches, so certain colorways occasionally go into production queues — if yours is one of them, we'll tell you upfront and you can wait or pick another.
Who do I talk to if I have questions?
The founder. Email us or DM us on Instagram and Brooks will get back to you personally — usually same-day.
