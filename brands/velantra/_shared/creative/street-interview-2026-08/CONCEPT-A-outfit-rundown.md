# CONCEPT A — "What are you wearing?"
### Velantra Colette Wool Tote · 26 seconds · 9:16 · five versions

Burned on screen for the first 3 seconds: **"What are you wearing? — {City} edition"**

A stranger with a mic stops a woman on the sidewalk and asks what she's wearing. She runs down her outfit piece by piece. Everything is good clothing with no logos on it. The last thing she names is the bag.

**She opens mid-answer.** No name, no hello, no "I'm from New York." Frame one is already the first item. The title card carries the city — she never says it, and no viewer ever needed to know her name.

---

## The workflow

**Step 1.** Generate her starting frame with GPT Image 2. One frame per woman. Three variants, pick the best.
**Step 2.** Animate that frame with Seedance 2.5 as the first frame. One pass, whole ad, audio included.

That's it. No keyframe per shot, no stitching, no voiceover laid on top.

---

## The script

26 seconds · 5 shots · 4 cuts. Every line is sized to its beat. **Never add words to a beat and never stretch a beat to fit a line.** If a line won't fit, cut the line.

**Beats 3, 4 and 5 are identical in all five versions.**

| Beat | Window | Line |
|---|---|---|
| **3** | 00:09–14 | "I don't really wear logos, I just want it to feel expensive." |
| **4** | 00:14–20 | "And the bag is the Colette, it's Velantra, they just did it for fall." |
| **5** | 00:20–26 | "It's the only thing I've carried all month. It's on pre-order right now, get yours below." |

**The Colette is a genuine pre-order** — it ships early October, so beat 5 is accurate as written. If that changes before you generate, the line changes with it.

**Beats 1 and 2 are the only lines that change per woman.** Beat 1 is the first frame of the ad — she is already mid-answer, naming a garment. There is no greeting and no name.

## Non-negotiables

- The only handbag brand named anywhere is Velantra. Everything else named is clothing or footwear.
- No competitor is named, implied or criticised. Nothing we don't make is ever the problem.
- She never speaks as the brand. "It's Velantra", "they just did it for fall". Never "our", never "we".
- The interviewer never speaks and is never seen. Only a forearm and a mic at the right edge of frame.
- The question is a burned title card in post, first 3 seconds only. It is never spoken audio.
- The Colette hangs from its two short top handles, in the hand or on the forearm. **It can never go over a shoulder** — the handle drop is 16cm and physically cannot reach.
- No music in the generation. No on-screen text in the generation.
- Any frame that reads as CGI, a product render or a catalogue photo is a regenerate, not a ship.

# STEP 2 — Animate it

**Seedance 2.5.** Upload the approved starting frame and set it as `first_frame_url`.

```
model:          bytedance/seedance-2-5
first_frame_url: <the approved starting frame>
duration:        26
aspect_ratio:    9:16
resolution:      720p
generate_audio:  true
```

Three things that silently ruin a pass:
1. The runtime in FORMAT, the beat windows, and `duration` must all be **26**. Mismatch makes it compress and the delivery slides off the timeline.
2. Never write "add", "remove", "change" or "replace" — those reroute the job into edit mode and lock the duration. Write "lifts", "turns", "pulls".
3. Never negate an object. "No table" produces a table. Say what IS there.

The five prompts below are complete. Copy the one that matches your frame.

# Check before it ships

Pull frames at 3 to 4 per second, not 1.

1. Exactly 5 cuts. Cuts every two seconds means the timeline was ignored.
2. Same face either side of every cut. Check the frame immediately before and immediately after each one.
3. Wardrobe and earrings identical across every cut. Earrings vanish first.
4. Dialogue lands inside its beat window. Over half a second of drift means the beat was overfull.
5. Lip sync holds. If it goes soft, cut words — never add duration.
6. The bag: right colorway, one belt strap, gold hardware, no logos, open top, **no shoulder strap**.
7. The mic and forearm are still there and haven't changed shape or position.
8. Photoreal — sensor noise, blown highlights, imperfect focus, real creases. Clean, even and tack-sharp means it's a render. Reject it.
9. Transcribe it. Under-filled beats grow speech that isn't in the script.

10. The CTA in beat 5 is intact and audible. If pre-order has ended by the time this ships, the line is wrong and the pass has to be rerun.

**If it comes back wrong,** fix with the cheapest tool: trim in post, then patch the audio, then regenerate the starting frame, then rerun. A rerun is 1,638 credits, so if a take is 95% there, patch it. Change one variable per retake — a retake reliably fixes what you named and breaks something else.
