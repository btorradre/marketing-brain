# QA checklist — Ingrid B-roll keyframes

Judge in this order: 1) photoreal, 2) product truth, 3) composition. A frame that nails the bag but reads as CGI is a REJECT.

## 1. Photoreal (fail on sight)
- FAIL: uniform polished surfaces with no creases or wear, clean even lighting with no blown highlights and no noise, perfect centered framing, tack sharp everywhere, spotless surfaces, a background that reads as a staged set or 3D render.
- PASS needs: available light with uneven exposure, believable sensor grain or softness, lived-in setting, slightly imperfect framing.

## 2. Product truth — Ingrid (oversized veg-tan top-handle carryall)
- Body: heavily creased, rumpled, glossy antiqued veg-tan leather. A smooth, uniform, un-creased surface = FAIL. Clearly wider than tall, oversized, softly structured, deep triangular side gussets, stands upright.
- Colorways, one per frame: Bordeaux = dark chocolate/oxblood body with CONTRASTING light whiskey-tan belt straps and tan edge piping. Cognac = burnished whiskey tan all over, matching belts/piping. Black = glossy black all over, black belts/piping. Bordeaux with dark belts (no contrast) = FAIL. Any colorway mix in one frame = FAIL.
- Front: sculpted fold-over front panel with hand-stitched curved edge, handles passing through shaped cutouts.
- CLOSURE TRUTH IS PER COLORWAY (the gallery image is the law, each colorway differs):
  - BORDEAUX: TWO separate light whiskey-tan straps, each through its own raised brass staple loop, each ending in its own angled brushed-gold clasp plate, and a SMALL brass turn lock at top center BETWEEN the two plates (three metal elements in a row). Straps fused into one belt with a single central plate = FAIL on bordeaux only.
  - BLACK: ONE slim black belt across the front through brass staple loops left and right, closing at exactly ONE central brushed-gold Kelly-style clasp plate with a gold DIAMOND-shaped turn lock on it. This single-central-plate layout is CORRECT for black. Two side-by-side clasp plates on black = FAIL. 
  - COGNAC: ONE cognac belt across the front through brass staple loops, closing at exactly ONE central brushed-gold Kelly-style clasp plate with a gold RING-shaped turn lock. A double row of decorative braided stitching along the curved top edge is CORRECT for cognac (it is on the real product, not an invention). Two side-by-side clasp plates on cognac = FAIL.
- Oversized/invented extra hardware beyond the colorway's layout = FAIL.
- Handles: two rolled round leather handles. One handle, flat handles, or a shoulder strap anywhere = FAIL.
- Base: darker leather corner caps + small metal feet.
- Hardware: brushed antique brass GOLD only. Silver or chrome hardware = FAIL. Engraved/lettered hardware plates = FAIL.
- Open shots: mouth gapes open, handles fall outward, straps hang loose. Interior = natural caramel tan suede-feel flesh side, UNLINED. Fabric lining, interior zipper, logo tag = FAIL.
- No logos, embossed text, or lettering anywhere on the bag.

## 3. General
- No readable text, signage or logos anywhere. No other branded bags. Faces must be fully out of frame or hidden. Hands, if present, look natural (five fingers, plausible pose, real skin texture).
- Vertical composition, bag prominent and true to its colorway.
