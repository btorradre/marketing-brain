# VEL-FOUNDER-TOF-01 — "Waiting on the Brand" — Brand-Level Founder Story (TOF)

**Status:** THE single TOF concept for the founder-story family (Brooks, 2026-07-23 pm). Brand as a whole, runs to the collection page (Brooks to confirm final URL). The per-product founder scripts in `products/straw-birkin/concepts/7:23:26 - founder story designer inflation/` and `products/weekender/concepts/7:23:26 - founder story designer inflation/` remain as MOF retargeting only — their TOF sections are superseded by this brief.
**Format:** Founder phenomenon-explainer / mini-VSL, 9:16. Full cut ~80s (agitation beat + self-intro added 2026-07-23 pm; standard founder-VSL length for cold), tight cut ~55s (map below). Skeleton adapted from the Bellaucci Heart Necklace founder ad (teardown in the STRAW-01 brief §1).
**Architecture:** 9 locked hooks → agitation bridge → ONE singular body. Hook is the only variable (H9 additionally swaps one agitation clause). THREE ad sets by wound: H1-H3 (exclusivity/permission), H4-H8 (price-gap), H9 (slow living / AI-era authenticity), so the winning WOUND reads cleanly.
**Emotional gateway:** designer inflation — she feels taken advantage of by the new price points. Identity sold: the smart-money woman who chooses not to spend frivolously. North star positioning (internal only): the timelessness of the great American heritage brands.

## 3. Laws (all locked 2026-07-23)

- **Language (avatar-sourced, Brooks's mom):** never "scam/scammed"; she feels TAKEN ADVANTAGE OF. Never priced-out/bitter/can't-afford framing. Never "tired of X" structure. "Affordable luxury" internal only. Designer talk category-level, no brand ever named (no Chanel, no Hermes, no Birkin, no L.L.Bean — positive comparisons are still comparisons).
- **Identity-first:** after the hook the ad stays on HER. Products appear only in beat 6, as roles, unnamed.
- **"Boatkin"** = the WSJ trend bag only, never applied to our products (the Weekender is never called a boatkin).
- **Truth-locks before mix:** the WSJ anecdote (publication, summer, price seen) and the maker claims (met manufacturers, samples) must be lockable as literally true. "The bags keep selling out" verified at run time.
- **Origin claims:** heritage language stays aesthetic ("timeless", "hand down"), never implies American manufacture. Sign-off "Founded in Newport Beach" ONLY if used — ⚠️ unresolved conflict: produced brand films + About letter say "Founded in Nantucket". One answer must hold everywhere before this and the films run simultaneously.
- **No personification, no muses. Commas and periods only, no em dashes anywhere (VO or prompts).**
