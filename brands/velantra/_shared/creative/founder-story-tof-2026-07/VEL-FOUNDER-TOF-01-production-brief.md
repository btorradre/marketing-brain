## 2. A-cam — filming checklist for Brooks

**Setting:** one warm neutral interior, kept identical across every take. Living-room energy like the Bellaucci reference: seated, textured background (sofa, wall art fine — zero visible brands/logos anywhere), or a coastal porch if light is controllable. Nothing that reads office or studio.
**Light:** big soft window at ~45° to your face, no overhead lights on. Golden-hour-adjacent if natural.
**Camera:** iPhone REAR camera, vertical 9:16, 4K 30fps, tripod or stable prop at eye level, ~1-1.5m away. Frame: head to mid-chest, eyes on the upper-third line, slightly off-center. Do not change framing between takes.
**Audio:** lav mic if available; otherwise quiet room, camera within 1.5m, and run a Voice Memos backup in a shirt pocket. Record 10s of room tone before wrapping.
**Wardrobe:** plain quality basics in light neutrals (white, cream, navy). No logos, no patterns, no jewelry that catches light.
**Eyeline:** hooks, promise, and close DIRECT to lens. Origin and maker beats can sit a few degrees off-lens (interview feel) — pick one and stay consistent.

**Delivery notes per beat:** Hook = level, unhurried, almost amused. Origin = warm storyteller, smile on "my mom". The refusal line gets a small shrug — she's smart, not angry. Maker = lean in, matter-of-fact competence. Promise = slowest lines in the ad, let the pauses sit ("One promise." full stop, breath, then the promise). Close = quiet, certain, near-smile on "waiting on the brand."

**Record list (one session):**
1. Full body read (agitation → close, starting "And somewhere along the way..."), 3 clean takes minimum. Do not stop for small stumbles mid-take; restart the sentence and keep rolling — we cut around it. Also read the H9 agitation first-clause variant once ("And somewhere in all that rush... stopped feeling good too.").
2. All 9 hooks, direct to lens, 2 takes each. Leave 1s of silence before and after every line.
3. Tight-cut body read (compressed lines from concept brief §2), 2 takes.
4. OPTIONAL but high-value real inserts (beats AI for authenticity, costs nothing): (a) your hands going through leather/straw swatches or samples on a table, 20s; (b) hands setting a folded generic broadsheet newspaper on a kitchen table next to coffee, 15s. No real mastheads on camera.
5. Room tone, 10s.
