# QA checklist — Colette + Margot B-roll keyframes

Judge in this order: 1) photoreal, 2) product truth, 3) composition. A frame that nails the bag but reads as CGI is a REJECT.

## 1. Photoreal (fail on sight)
- FAIL: uniform polished surfaces with no creases or wear, clean even lighting with no blown highlights and no noise, perfect centered framing, tack sharp everywhere, spotless surfaces, a background that reads as a staged set or 3D render.
- PASS needs: available light with uneven exposure, believable sensor grain or softness, lived-in setting, slightly imperfect framing.

## 2. Product truth — Colette (oatmeal felt tote)
- Body: oatmeal greige brushed wool FELT (visible fibre softness), never smooth canvas or leather. Clearly wider than tall (about 2:1 front ratio), stands upright, softly structured.
- Front: slim leather belt (cognac for Caramel, dark brown for Espresso) crossing horizontally THROUGH two vertical felt straps. Belt ends curve OUTWARD and downward, each capped with a small round aged GOLD disc. No belt = FAIL. Straight belt ends with no discs = FAIL.
- Handles: two rolled handles, leather-wrapped grip over felt, short drop.
- Top: fully open. Any flap, zipper, or closure on the mouth = FAIL.
- Hardware: aged gold only. Silver anywhere = FAIL. Logos or lettering on bag = FAIL.

## 3. Product truth — Margot (structured leather work tote)
- Body: single-color grained/embossed leather (burgundy, black, or tan brown), wider than tall, top rim about as wide as the base, stands upright, never slouching, never stretched tall or pinched.
- Front: two slim belts hanging OPEN through slim silver keepers, ending in two angled SILVER clasp plates meeting in a shallow V. One small square SILVER turn-lock plate top center. Missing V plates or missing turn-lock = FAIL. Belts fused into one strap = FAIL.
- Top: OPEN, no flap covering the mouth, no zipper across the top. A full front flap = FAIL.
- Hardware: polished silver ONLY. Gold or brass hardware = FAIL.
- A shoulder strap visible anywhere = FAIL.
- Open shots: tan suede-feel interior; a center zip divider is correct. A zipper closing the bag mouth itself = FAIL.

## 4. Duo scenes
- Both bags present, each obeying its own truth block. Any feature bleed (gold on Margot, silver on Colette, leather Colette body, felt Margot) = FAIL.

## 5. General
- No readable text, signage or logos anywhere. No other branded bags. Faces must be fully out of frame or hidden. Hands, if present, look natural (five fingers, plausible pose, real skin texture).
- Vertical composition, bag prominent and true to its colorway (Caramel vs Espresso trim, Burgundy vs Black vs Brown body).
