## About the role

Velantra is a fast-growing accessible-luxury handbag brand (velantrafashion.com). We are hiring a social media manager to fully own our Instagram: creating and publishing 2 to 3 posts per day, every day, using AI image and video tools.

This is not a generic social media job. Almost everything we post is AI-generated, and our bags have very specific construction details that AI models get wrong constantly. The core skill of this role is making AI content that looks completely real and matches our product references exactly. If you cannot tell the difference between an AI image that looks real and one that looks AI-glossy, or you do not naturally zoom in to check details against a reference photo, this role is not for you.

You get a full AI suite, paid for by us. You never pay for a credit.

## What you'll do

- Create and publish 2 to 3 Instagram posts per day, 7 days a week, from a batched calendar that is built and approved in advance
- Short Reels: styling clips, what-fits-inside, colorway reveals, packing and unpacking, simple product motion. Most run 5 to 15 seconds
- Simple statics: product and lifestyle stills, colorway grids, new-arrival and back-in-stock announcements, quote and text cards
- AI image generation from our product photos as references, for both feed stills and Reel frames
- Write captions in our brand voice. Quiet, understated, confident, zero hype. We give you a full voice guide
- Check every asset against the product reference before it goes anywhere. Our bags have locked design details that must never be wrong
- Light community management: reply to comments, flag important DMs, keep the tone consistent
- Keep a simple content calendar so anyone can see what is scheduled and what shipped

## What the work is actually like

The volume sounds heavy and it is not, because the content is simple by design and the work is batched. Most posts are one image and a caption. Reels are short and are built from stills we help you generate. The tools, prompts, and product references are already set up. What we are paying for is consistency and detail: hitting 2 to 3 posts a day every day, and never letting a wrong bag go out because you were moving fast.

## The rules that are not negotiable

These are the ones that get content rejected, so read them before you apply.

- The bag in your image must match the reference gallery exactly. Same construction, same hardware, same stitching, nothing added, nothing missing
- Our bags are logo-free. No logo, no brand text, no monogram anywhere on the product
- Never name or compare us to another brand in a caption, a comment, or on-screen text
- Never state or imply where the bags are made
- Nothing that looks like a glossy 3D render. If it does not read as a real photograph, it does not go out
- We do not offer any pay-later or installment plans, so those badges and phrases never appear in a post

None of this is difficult once you have read it. It just requires actually reading it, and checking your own work before you send it. The people who have not worked out in roles like this were not the slow ones, they were the ones whose posts came back needing the same corrections every week.

## You're a good fit if

- You have proven hands-on experience with current AI image tools (GPT image models, Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana or similar) and image-to-video tools (Kling, Seedance, Veo, Runway or similar), and you can show us work you personally made
- You have obsessive attention to detail. You will be given reference photos and your output has to match them precisely. This is the number one skill we are hiring for
- You know Instagram natively: Reels, feed aesthetics, Stories, scheduling tools, what a good hook looks like in the first second
- You write clean, natural English. Captions are yours to write
- You can hold a daily posting rhythm without being chased
- Bonus: a fashion or luxury aesthetic sense, and basic CapCut or Canva for text overlays and simple cuts

## How to apply

Applications missing any of these four items are deleted unread.

1. Start your application with the word CARAMEL. This is how we filter for people who read instructions
2. Link 3 to 6 examples of realistic AI content you have personally generated, images or video. Tell us which tools you used for each
3. In 2 or 3 sentences: how do you make AI-generated people and products look real instead of fake? Tell us what you actually do differently
4. Your timezone and your daily availability. If you have run a brand's Instagram before, link the account
