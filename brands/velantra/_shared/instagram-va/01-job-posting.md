## Instagram Content Creator & Account Manager — AI-Generated Content (Fashion Brand)

**Part-time, remote. Long-term role.**

Velantra is a fast-growing accessible-luxury handbag brand (velantrafashion.com). We're hiring a VA to fully own our Instagram: creating and publishing **3 posts per day**, every day, using AI image and video tools.

This is not a generic social media job. Almost all of our content is AI-generated, and our bags have very specific construction details that AI models get wrong constantly. The core skill of this role is **making AI content that (a) looks completely real and (b) matches our product references exactly**. If you can't tell the difference between an AI image that looks real and one that looks "AI-glossy," or you don't naturally zoom in to check details against a reference photo, this role isn't for you.

### What you'll do

- Generate photorealistic AI images and short videos of our handbags (product shots, lifestyle scenes, model/UGC-style content) working from our product photos as references
- Check every single asset against the product reference before it goes anywhere — our bags have locked design details that must never be wrong
- Write captions in our brand voice (quiet luxury, understated, zero hype) — we'll give you a full voice guide
- Schedule and publish 3 posts/day (feed + Reels mix) and keep a simple content calendar
- Light community management: reply to comments, flag important DMs
- Work from a batching workflow — content is created and approved in advance, not scrambled day-of

### Requirements

- Proven hands-on experience with current AI image tools (e.g. GPT image models, Midjourney, Flux, Nano Banana) and image-to-video tools (e.g. Kling, Seedance, Veo, Runway) — you must show us AI work you've made
- **Obsessive attention to detail.** You will be given reference photos and your output must match them precisely. This is the #1 skill we're hiring for.
- Strong written English for captions
- Comfortable with Instagram natively: Reels, feed aesthetics, Stories, scheduling tools
- Reliable daily availability (posts go out 7 days/week; creation is batched)
- Bonus: fashion/luxury aesthetic sense, basic CapCut/Canva skills for text overlays
