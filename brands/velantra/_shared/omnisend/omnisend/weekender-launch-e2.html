A Closer Look

Built like a handbag.
Packs like a suitcase.

The Weekender's silhouette borrows from the most recognizable structured bag of the last four decades — scaled for travel. Here's what that means up close.

One flap. One motion.

Unfasten the two belted straps, fold the leather flap back in a single piece, and the whole mouth of the bag opens wide for packing. One twist of the gold turn-lock closes it again. Full-grain leather with burnished edges and visible stitching — it will darken and gain character with every trip.

Room, and a place for the small things.

Natural cotton-canvas lining with a leather slip pocket on the back wall — phone, keys, passport. The heavyweight canvas body holds its structure whether it's stuffed for a long weekend or half-empty on the way home.

One size. The right size.

18" wide, 14.5" tall, 7" deep — two to three days of clothing, a toiletry kit, and a book. It sits upright in the trunk, stacks on top of your rolling carry-on, and slides into the overhead bin. One bag, any weekend.

A note from our founder

"I designed the Weekender because I was tired of choosing between a bag that could handle a trip and a bag I actually wanted to be seen carrying. This one is both."

— Brooks, Founder
