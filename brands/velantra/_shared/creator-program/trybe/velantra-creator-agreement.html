Velantra Creator Program

What You're Opting Into

Welcome to the Velantra Creator Program. This page explains, in plain language, how you earn with us and what we ask in return. By accepting in Trybe, you're agreeing to the terms below.

Your Commission
15% of every sale you bring in

Every video you post carries your unique tracked link or code from Trybe. When someone buys through it, you earn 15% of that sale. There's no cap — the more your content sells, the more you make.

How Earning Works

  • Attribution. Sales are tracked through your unique Trybe link or code. Always use it — untracked sales can't be credited to you.
  • No cap, no minimum. You earn 15% on every attributed sale, whether your video drives two orders or two hundred.
  • Payouts. Commissions are calculated on completed (non-refunded) orders and paid out through Trybe on the platform's standard payout schedule.
  • Returns. If an order is refunded or charged back, the commission on that order is reversed.

The Retainer Path

Commission is the starting point, not the ceiling. Creators whose videos consistently perform are offered a fixed monthly retainer on top of, or in place of, commission-only terms.

Step 1

Start on commission

Post following the brief, use your tracked link, earn 15% per sale.

Step 2

We watch performance

Views, engagement, and attributed sales across your videos — reviewed regularly.

Step 3

Retainer offer

Consistent performers get a guaranteed monthly rate for an agreed number of videos.

Retainers are offered at Velantra's discretion based on performance and are agreed separately in writing. Being in the program doesn't guarantee a retainer — strong, consistent content is what earns one.

What We Ask From You

  • Follow the creative brief. Formats, angles, and the do/don't list live in the Brief & Angles document inside Trybe.
  • Original content. Everything you submit is your own work, featuring you, filmed for this program.
  • Honest disclosure. Follow FTC guidelines: use #ad or your platform's paid-partnership label on every post.
  • Accurate claims only. Stick to the product facts in the brief. Don't mention where products are made, don't name or compare to other brands, and don't invent discounts or shipping promises.
  • Keep it live. Leave posted videos up for at least 30 days.

Content Usage

  • You keep ownership of your content.
  • By submitting through the program, you grant Velantra the right to repost your videos on our brand channels and to use them in paid advertising (including allowlisting / Spark Ads with your authorization).
  • We'll always credit your handle on organic reposts.

Leaving the Program

  • Either side can end participation at any time, no penalty.
  • Commissions you've already earned on attributed sales still get paid.
  • Usage rights on content already submitted continue per the section above.

The Short Version

Post great videos following the brief, use your tracked link, earn 15% of every sale you drive — and if your content performs, we'll come to you with a monthly retainer. That's the deal.
