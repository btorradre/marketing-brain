# Trybe Creative Angles — paste-ready (8)

One angle per Trybe "Creative angles" slot. Title on the first line, "what to make" description below.

Angles 1–4 work for every bag. Angles 5–8 are bag-specific — the bag is named in the description so creators self-select.

---

**1. Most Requested**
Make a hold-to-camera video presenting the bag as the one your audience keeps asking about. Show it front-on, turn it to a side profile, then lean the handles toward the lens and trace the stitching. Works with any bag in the line-up. Opener: "Okay this is the most requested bag in my closet right now."

**2. Macro Cold Open**
Make a craftsmanship video that opens on an extreme close-up of the weave, grain or stitching — your voice talking before your face appears — then cut to you lifting the bag and close on "it costs nothing like it looks." Works with any bag, strongest on the Sofia's weave and the Margot's leather grain.

**3. On the Body**
Make an honest on-body review showing how the bag actually wears: in the crook of your arm, the silhouette from the side, and how it reads with your outfit. Casual, direct, "quick honest review" energy. Works with any bag.

**4. The Reveal**
Make an unboxing with a genuine first reaction. Part the tissue paper, lift the bag out for the first time, show the interior, and hug it to your chest on the last line. A real gasp beats any script. Works with any bag.

**5. Does It Actually Fit? — Sofia or Camille**
Make a practicality demo that answers the number-one question. Hands only: slide the belts open and fold the flap all the way over, then drop in your sunglasses, phone, and wallet on camera — then cut to you holding the closed bag with a playful verdict.

**6. The Boatkin of Summer '26 — Sofia only**
Make a trend video positioning this as the new "Boatkin" of summer 2026 — the structured straw bag everyone's hunting for. Open on a close-up of the bag, rant that every straw bag you wanted this summer was floppy, fragile, or sold out, squeeze the side to prove it holds firm, then style it on your forearm and close with urgency. Opener: "Guys, I think I found the Boatkin of 2026."

**7. The Three-Day Test — Eleanor only**
Make a packing demo that proves the weekender actually holds a trip. Overhead shot, pack three days of real clothes into it in real time so the volume reads, push it down once and keep going, then fasten the belts and lift it into a side profile so it still looks structured when full. Opener: "I'm packing three days into this bag and I'm not checking a thing."

**8. Desk to Dinner — Margot only**
Make a one-bag-two-lives video. Slide your laptop in and let the turn-lock close on camera in a daylight work outfit, walk with it on the shoulder with a close-up on the leather grain, then cut to evening in a different outfit with the same bag. The sell is that it never looks like a work bag. Opener: "This is the only bag I own that fits a laptop and still looks like this."
